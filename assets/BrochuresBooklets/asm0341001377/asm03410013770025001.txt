CURRENCY: The monetary unit is the Baht (or Tical), which is currently quoted between 20 and 25 to the U. S. dollar on the open market. The official rate is approximately 10 to 1 (9.827).
HISTORY: Before the war, Siam was the only independent nation in southeastern Asia. Although their country has experienced numerous invasions and frequent changes of dynasty during its 3000 years of history, the Siamese have succeeded in maintaining their independence.
The Portuguese were the first Europeans to arrive on the scene. They came in the 17th century to establish trade relations and were followed by the British, then the French.
Prior to 1932, Siam was an absolute monarchy. But in 1932, a bloodless revolution ushered in a limited monarchy with a new Constitution, under which the King ruled together with an Assembly of People’s Representatives.
By 1940, the Japanese succeeded in achieving a predominant influence in Siam. Declaration of war on January 25,1942, on the U. S. and Great Britain was made against the will of the people, and on August 16,1945, it was declared null and void.
June 12, 1940, Pan American inaugurated regular fortnightly mail service between the United States and Auckland, New Zealand. Passenger service inaugurated one month later.
