HOTEL ACCOMMODATIONS : Pan American will make every effort to obtain advance hotel accommodations for the account of through passengers at overnight stops. The rates for hotels are listed under a description of the facilities available at each of the terminals en route.
SHIPPING SOUVENIRS—DISPATCHING RADIOGRAMS AND CABLES: Traffic Representatives at any station en route will be glad to assist you in shipping souvenirs and dispatching radiograms and cables.
BEFORE YOU GET OFF: Frequently, in disemplaning at overnight stops, passengers leave coats, hats, brief cases and other articles in the seats or baggage racks. Obviously, Pan American cannot be responsible for the safety of such unchecked articles. Therefore, it is requested that you have in your possession all your personal belongings before getting off the Clipper.
