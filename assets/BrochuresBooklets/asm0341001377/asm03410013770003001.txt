Your Clipper Trip
PAN AMERICAN WORLD AIRWAYS TRANS-PACIFIC AND ASIATIC SERVICES
The following information has been prepared to help make your trip by Clipper more interesting and enjoyable. Whether this is your first overseas flight, or whether you are a regular Clipper passenger, Pan American wants this to be a trip you will always remember with pleasure.
AT YOUR SERVICE: Your purser or stewardess will be glad to bring you a game kit, playing cards, stationery, ink, magazines or newspapers. If you wish information on the Clipper’s position or speed, if you’d like to have your seat adjusted, or if you’d like a blanket or extra pillow, you have only to ask.
NO TIPPING, PLEASE: Pan American employees take a genuine pride in their share of our Company’s reputation for service and do not accept gratuities.
COMPLIMENTARY MEALS SERVED ALOFT: Dietetically planned meals will be served during normal meal hours. Coffee, tea, and cooling drinks are available at any time.
HOTELS AND GROUND TRANSPORTATION EXTRA: As is customary on all United States air carriers, the price of your ticket does not include the cost of your hotel accommodations or transportation to and from the airport.
SMOKING: You may smoke if you wish, except during take-offs and landings or while emplaning or disemplaning. Because most passengers object, it is requested that you do not smoke cigars or pipes while aboard.
October, 1927, Pan American established the first international air service under the American flag from Key West, Florida, to Havana, Cuba.
