HONG KONG, “Riviera of the Orient,” lies at the mouth of the Pearl River, on the southeast coast of China. A British Crown Colony of 390 square miles, Hong Kong includes the island of Hong Kong, Stonecutters’ Island, the Kowloon Peninsula, and the New Territories (leased from China for 99 years, starting July 1, 1898). The Colony’s population is roughly a million-and-a-half persons.
A free port, with import duties imposed only on liquors, tobaccos, perfumed spirits and certain oils, Hong Kong provides the finest deepwater harbor between Shanghai and Indo-China.
