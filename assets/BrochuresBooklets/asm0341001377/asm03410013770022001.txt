CURRENCY: The Hong Kong Dollar is the monetary unit in Hong Kong. The official rate of exchange is 3.97 Hong Kong Dollars to 1 U. S. Dollar, but can be higher (as much as 5 to 1).
A BRIEF HISTORY OF HONG KONG: Before the British settled in Hong Kong, the island and its surrounding waters and inlets were a stronghold of pirates. In 1627 the British East India Company began trade with Canton, but it was not until after the Opium War (1839-42) that England was successful in negotiating a satisfactory trade agreement with China. During this war the English used the harbor of Hong Kong as a naval base and established a settlement on the island’s barren shore. In 1842 the island was ceded to Great Britain as one of the terms of the Treaty of Nanking, and from that time on it grew steadily in population until it became the great port it is today. As a result of the Second Opium War in 1860 Britain obtained the possessions of Kowloon and Stonecutters’ Island, and in 1898 she was granted a ninety-nine-year lease by the Chinese Government for the lands known as the New Territories. Immediately after the Pearl Harbor bombing on December 7, 1941, the Japanese attacked the colony of Hong Kong by land, and on December 25th, when the water supply came under Japanese control, Hong Kong surrendered. Since the end of the war, Hong Kong has gradually reassumed her previous importance as a world port.
April 21, 1937, Pan American began the first flight between San Francisco and Hong Kong, China.
