To continue the education of all our employees in the subject matter contained in the booklet, we would prefer to use the Clipper as the media of circulation. It would be our intent to run the articles in a series (as planned by the other Divisions), attempting to associate the articles as printed with other matters current at that time.
In this fashion, we would avoid the cost of separate booklets for all employees and probably would achieve a greater degree of readership.
