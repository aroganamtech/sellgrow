If you’re conscious of your pennies and you want to save on your fare, Pan American’s popular Rainbow Service is the answer. The Douglas Super-6 and new DC-7B clippers* of this tourist-fare service were designed for your comfort and—all important—to maintain the high standard of dependability of the World’s Most Experienced Airline. Excellent complimentary meals are served in flight.
If you desire the comforts of your own living room, fly on one of Pan American’s luxury Boeing Super Stratocruisers . . . you have a choice of two services—“The President” or the extra fare “President Special.” You’ll relax in air-conditioned comfort in your large lounge chair which adjusts for reading or sleeping. There’s no extra charge for the delicious full-course meals, in-between snacks or cocktails. If you wish, wander down to the lower lounge to chat with your fellow passengers over refreshments—served to you by an attending steward who is there to see that your every wish is realized. On the “President Special,” Sleeperette* Service is featured —and on both services, at a slight surcharge, bed-size berth accommodations are available.
