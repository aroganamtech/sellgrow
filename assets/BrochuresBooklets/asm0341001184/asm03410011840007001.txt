Epidaurus . . . About 92 miles southwest of Athens; is noted for its Sanctuary of Asclépios, ancient health resort. During July, the “Epidaurus Festival” takes place with excellent productions of classical Greek drama in this ancient theater. Also of interest are the Tholos, the Stadium and a well-preserved, open-air theater with remarkable acoustics. Hotels include the newly built Amphitryon and the Bourtzi.
