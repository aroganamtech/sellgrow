Travel with Children? Why not?
Why travel with children unless you absolutely have to? There are lots of reasons well-traveled parents take their children with them even on holiday to foreign lands. Probably the most important is the sense of security that comes in knowing their child is with them.
Then there is the expense of leaving them at home—of finding obliging relatives to take over—or hiring a competent housekeeper. Even camp is no solution since camp fees and equipment can often exceed the cost of a complete trip abroad for a child. Especially when you consider his air fare (free or 10% under two; 50% from two to 12) and minimum or no charge for hotel accommodations.
By taking your school-age child with you, you will enjoy the experience of seeing the world through his eyes and you’ll be surprised how much more you will see as a result. In all probability he’ll know more about the history and culture of the land you visit than you will. And he’ll quickly pick up foreign words and phrases.
