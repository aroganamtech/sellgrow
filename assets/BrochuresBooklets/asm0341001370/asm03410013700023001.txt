Pan Am has a fine, newly revised guide to educational opportunities abroad. “New Horizons in Education” gives detailed data on principal universities abroad including summer sessions. It covers such subjects as how to enroll, credits earned, living costs and student life.
For the younger set, the book also covers details on many boarding schools and day schools abroad. This unique guide is available from either Pan Am or your local bookstore. Hard cover $6.95, paperback $3.95.
Going Abroad to Live?
Most of your questions about taking your children to a foreign country to live will be answered in “New Horizons Living Abroad.”
It is an 832-page guide to living conditions in 90 countries. A must—whether you’re going for a year or even a few months. $4.95 at Pan Am or your bookstore.
All of these books, as well as New Horizons World Guide may be ordered direct. Send check or money order to Pan Am, P.O. Box PAA, Jamaica, N. Y. 11430.
