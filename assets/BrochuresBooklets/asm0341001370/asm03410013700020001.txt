If you are taking your children to Europe, you will need more daytime clothing and less sportswear. A child does not look well on city streets attired in dungarees and tennis shoes. And little girls should wear skirts or dresses on city streets. Children’s clothing can be comfortable and attractive without being restrictive.
European boys under 12 most often wear short pants with knee length or short socks, depending on the weather. So if your boy likes shorts, he can be properly attired in them, even on the Champs Elysee. But with the shorts, a jacket and tie or in hot weather, shirt with collar.
In the European countryside or at resorts, your children can clamber into their favorite shorts and old shirts and feel “right at home.” And incidentally, don’t rush out and buy an all new wardrobe for junior. Take careful stock of what he has, add to it only the things you absolutely can’t do without.
