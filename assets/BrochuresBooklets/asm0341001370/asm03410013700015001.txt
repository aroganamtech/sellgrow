For a child, the case should contain:
□	several jars of junior food
□	a spoon
□	cleansing tissues
□	a sweater
□	a few cookies or crackers in a plastic bag (especially handy to and from the airport or wherever such items are not available)
□	damp washcloth in plastic bag (for sticky hands and face)
□	a favorite toy or book
Incidentally, Pan Am’s popular overnight bag makes an ideal case for baby or child. You may purchase a Pan Am bag for $1.00 at any Pan Am office or if you travel First Class President Special you will, receive a complimentary one.
Lavatories aboard Pan Am Jet Clippers are fully stocked with Kleenex, paper hand towels, individually wrapped guest soaps, toilet seat covers and Wash N’ Dry. The latter is a tiny package containing a paper towel moisturized with a harmless, mild astringent. You’ll find them just right for mopping up little faces and hands after meals—or just general clean up. Particularly if you forget the washcloth!
