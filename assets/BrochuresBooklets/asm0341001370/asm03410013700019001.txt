Avoid materials that need starching and ironing. If you are going to a hot climate, knitted cotton fabrics, such as tee shirt material, are ideal.
Keep the baby’s or child’s clothing as simple and functional as possible. On the beach in Jamaica, he may look adorable in that ruffled, starched sunsuit, but how long will it stay that way? A simple tee shirt and gay plastic-lined pants would be better. For an older child, a tee shirt and shorts.
Even in resort areas it is advisable to take at least one “dress-up” outfit for your older child. A jacket and tie for the boy, an attractive dress with perhaps a matching sweater for the girl.
