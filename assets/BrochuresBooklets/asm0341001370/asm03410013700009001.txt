Special fares for tiny travelers
If your child is under two years of age he will travel at 10% of the normal adult fare and in the following areas he will enjoy travel absolutely free: to and from the U.S. and Haiti; Nassau, Bahamas; Dominican Republic; Puerto Rico; the Virgin Islands; Jamaica and Mexico: (and from Seattle to Alaska).
Even though you may not have to pay anything for your “under two’s” Jet Clipper flight, always inform the Pan Am office when you make your reservation that you will be traveling with a baby. He must have a ticket issued to him even though it will be marked “free.”
If your child is two to 12 years of age, he will travel at 50% of the normal adult fare. If you are traveling with two children under two years of age, only one may go free or at 10%; the other travels at half fare.
The “under two” child is not assigned a seat (other than your lap) but most often there will be an extra seat on the Pan Am Jet Clipper and the check-in counter will try to assign your seat next to an empty seat or row of seats. If not, the stewardess will reseat you after your Clipper is airborne.
