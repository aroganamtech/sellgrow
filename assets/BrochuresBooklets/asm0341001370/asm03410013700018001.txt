Do not take too much clothing. It just becomes a burden. Most hotels ’round the world have good laundry facilities and one can always rely on the hotel maid to do hand laundry. And you’ll be surprised how much more people will do for you when there’s a child involved. You may not be able to get 4-hour laundry service on a Sunday, but chances are, your baby will!
Weather permitting, confine yourself to knits—
especially the synthetic fabrics, since they are easy to care for, dry quickly and remain neat and fresh.
