Don’t forget my diapers!
It is a good idea to pack a separate small traveling case for each baby or child. The older child will enjoy carrying his own case. All the things he will need enroute can be put into the case except for medicines, which you should pack securely with your own things or keep in your purse. It is a good idea to bring a small bottle of baby aspirin plus whatever medicines your baby takes regularly and prescriptions for same, never pack these in a case where he might get hold of them.
A traveling case for a baby should contain:
		
bottles of formula		a light blanket
several jars of baby food		a rattle or cuddly toy
a spoon		small bottles of baby lotion, baby powder, cream, etc.
disposable diapers		cleansing tissues
a bib		a soft washcloth
a sweater		
Disposable diapers are kept aboard the aircraft just in case you forget yours. If you bring regular cloth diapers, the stewardess will give you a moisture-proof bag for soiled diapers.
