You and baby are never alone
If you are a mother traveling alone with a baby or
children, just let Pan Am know and we’ll see that
you get all the help possible every step of your trip. . . ®
There are equipped nurseries or Clipper Passenger
Lounges in Pan Am’s major air terminals, where
you may rest with your little one until departure
time. Special service representatives will assist you
to and from the Jet Clipper or carry your infant for
you. And in flight, the stewardess will be happy to
stay with your baby while you take time to “freshen
up.” You will also be helped through customs.
In fact, traveling alone with baby means “extra special” attention all the way.
