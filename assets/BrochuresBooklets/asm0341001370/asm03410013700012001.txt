If your baby is taking regular pasteurized milk, it is still wise to pack a few bottles, although the stewardess can replenish them easily since milk is always carried aboard a Jet Clipper. If necessary, the stewardess can sterilize the bottle and nipples for you. (Be sure to check on the new pre-sterilized disposable bottles—a boon at home or away!)
The stewardess will also refrigerate the bottles for you although you may find it more convenient to store them icy cold in an insulated bag.
When you think your baby is getting hungry, ask the stewardess to heat the bottle for you. If this occurs right after boarding the Jet Clipper, wait until you are airborne, then ask the stewardess to heat the bottle.
