You can order whatever you want in baby food, in advance, from Pan Am. But don’t forget, because only small rations of baby food are kept aboard the aircraft. Or you may wish to bring your own. The stewardess will heat the baby food for you. Do bring his own spoon. If you forget, the stewardess can sterilize a regular spoon. If your baby is on table foods, he may be able to eat some of the regular meal that will be served. Even so, it is wise to bring a jar or two of junior food or order it, in advance from Pan Am.
If your off-spring is out of the “baby” stage, you can order a child’s menu in advance from Pan Am (except on flights to Caribbean and South America). It consists of frankfurters or hamburgers, french fried potatoes, ice cream and cake and milk. The stewardess will be happy to serve your child’s dinner first.
Fruit juices, crackers or cookies are available for “in-between” snacks.
If your child’s birthday should happen to fall on the day you take your Jet Clipper flight, you may order, without charge, a birthday cake. It, too, must be ordered in advance—preferably when you make your Pan Am reservations.
NOTE: On Pan Am’s Thrift Class Jet Clipper flights to San Juan and Hawaii check first about the availability of bassinets, children’s meals and birthday cakes, since it is not always possible to provide these items on Thrift flights.
