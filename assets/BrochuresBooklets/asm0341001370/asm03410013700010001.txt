Bassinets are so comfy!
If you are traveling with an infant, a bassinet will be reserved for you on originating flights when you make your Pan Am reservations. If at all possible, you will be assigned to a seat directly behind the bulkhead (a floor-to-ceiling partition) which is equipped with fixtures for a bassinet. The bassinet can be installed by the stewardess in just a few seconds. Since there are a limited number of bulkhead bassinets available on the aircraft, they are reserved on a “first-come, first-served” basis. In the case that
