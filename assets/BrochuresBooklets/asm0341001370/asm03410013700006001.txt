In Europe, child care is a highly-regarded profession and well-trained children’s nurses are readily available at very little cost. These women differ completely from the American “baby sitter,” and you’ll have to prepare yourself for the shock of seeing your “sitter” do everything but sit.
Europe abounds with excellent doctors and hospitals (some of the finest in the world) so be assured that in case of emergency, you’ll have no trouble getting the very best care.
Bermuda, Nassau and selected Caribbean islands are also wonderful places to take children for here are natural playgrounds with all the water activities so dear to any child’s heart. Bermuda and various Caribbean islands not only specialize in child care but in infant care and many hotels are participating in Pan Am’s special “take along the baby” island vacation plan. Under this plan, all the equipment necessary to make a baby happy is provided free of charge by the hotel—cribs, high chairs, hot plates in the room for heating formula. In addition, the hotels have American brand baby food on hand as well as American brand prepared formula and canned milk.
