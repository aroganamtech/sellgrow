Babies, Baggage & Birth Certificates
If your baby is traveling free or at 10% fare, he will not be entitled to baggage allowance. However, you will be permitted to carry a portable bassinet, or carry cot plus baby food for consumption inflight, free. Any other of baby’s belongings will be charged to your baggage allowance.
If he is traveling at 50% fare, he will have the same baggage allowance as an adult (44 lbs. for Rainbow Economy or Thrift; 66 lbs. for First Class President Special).
A child, no matter how young, will need essentially the same credentials as an adult in entering a foreign country. For many areas of the world, he will need a passport, for others, a birth certificate. He will need a s/nallpox vaccination to get back into the United States. All of these matters are best taken care of when you handle your own. Your local Pan Am office will give you specific information on credentials and vaccination certificates.
Information on credentials, plus what to wear, see and do, is contained in Pan Am’s handy purse-size “Woman’s Way” booklets. Ask for “Woman’s Way to See Europe,” “Woman’s Way to See Hawaii
