Children on a European holiday
Hints for miniature wardrobes
Clothing for your infant or child will depend on the weather you will encounter and the nature of your trip. Wardrobes will vary widely depending on whether you are taking your baby on vacation in Bermuda or to visit relatives in Europe. The best way to begin planning your offspring’s wardrobe is to consult New Horizons World Guide, Pan Am’s 640-page complete guide to travel anywhere in the world." The Guide will give you the high, low and average temperatures for each month of the year for almost every city in the world. From these charts, you will know what to expect and can plan accordingly.
