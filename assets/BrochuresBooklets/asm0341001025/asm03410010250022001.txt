AUSTRALIA
Brisbane. Varying from 20 miles to 150 miles off the coast is the Great Barrier Reef, largest coral reef in the world—1,200 miles long—and in many ways a perfect vacation area, especially in winter. Quiet green islands, brilliant coral, tropical fish, thousands of coves and beaches, transport and accommodations in plenty. Dedicated skin-divers should not miss it, neither should collectors of exotic sea shells. The islands of the Whitsunday group are very beautiful: Brampton, Hayman, Lindeman, South Mole—reached from Mackay. However, the others to north and south have their advantages—in particular Green Island, with its underwater observatory, and Dunk Island, both reached from Cairns. Most economical way to see the reef is by taking one of the package tours offered by several Australian companies, or a cruise from Mackay at about A$30 a day. A-5 day cruise costs A$260.00.

TASMANIA
CAPITAL
Hobart, population 164,530. Tasmania, 140 miles south of the manland, is the “apple isle,” the smallest of the Australian states and the most verdant. It is a beautiful little island, with its rugged mountains, irregular coastline, orchard-lined valleys, lonely alpine lakes, and colorful past. Pan Am’s World Rent-a-Car is at 223 Liverpool Street, Hobart (tel: 345877), and at Wrest Point Hotel-Casino, Sandy Bay Road, Hobart (tel: 2501122). At Launceston it is 918388).
