Peaks rise thousands of feet straight out of the sea, cascades crash down sheer slopes, dark pine forests loom in the distance—all combining to create the world of Norway and the mountain kings. Much of this—perhaps New Zealand’s grandest scenic treasury—can be reached by road, motor launch, or air. THC Milford, at the head of Milford Sound and run by the Tourist Hotel Corporation, takes 80 guests and is a center for exploration (from US$37 double). A 2-hour motor launch trip (NZ$4.50) visits the 540-foot Bown Falls, Stirling Falls, Mitre Peak, Mount Pembroke, and other spectacles. And the launches are welcomed by grampuses—large creatures like porpoises, which surface and follow the boats. Milford Fjord is the startling and magnificent of the fjords, dramatic in its rugged grandeur.
