SPORTS
You’ve come to the right place. Every Australian is a great sportsman. He lives for strenuous activity in the great outdoors. There are water sports in abundance, of course—some of the world’s best swimming, surfing, skin-diving, and big-game fishing, and no lack of facilities since all the state capitals are coastal. Most towns have a racetrack—some have several. Tennis is a passion; there are lovingly kept greens for golf and bowls. You can ride, sail, and watch four kinds of football and cricket.
NEW SOUTH WALES
CAPITAL
Sydney, population 3,121,750. The oldest colony (1788), now the; most populous state on the continent, yet only a tenth of its area. New South Wales in many ways leads Australia. Other states might deny it proudly, but to visitors Sydney will appear the most lively and forward-looking city. Certainly the nightlife here, the theater, music, and industry are far advanced.
AIRPORT INFORMATIONS
Sydney’s Kingsford Smith Airport is 6 miles from the city center. A taxi into town costs A$5. There is a Pan Am’s World Rent-a-Car desk at the airport (tel: 669 5796).
