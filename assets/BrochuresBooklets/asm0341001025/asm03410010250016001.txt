SOUTH AUSTRALIA
CAPITAL
Adelaide, population 900,379. Apart from the sheep and cattle country near Adelaide, most of this state is barren, fiercely hot land of ancient desert and dry salt lakes. Adelaide itself is a gracious city inlaid with gardens and churches. Until recently, it had the reputation of being quiet and strait-laced; now it is awakening to the possibilities of tourism and a livelier pace. Every two years in March a festival of the arts is held—the Edinburgh Festival of Australia. And it helps that this is the wine capital of Australia. Its climate is dry. bright, and warm most of the year. Pan Am’s World Rent-a-Car is at 30 Mophett Street 9tel: 512856) and 28 Hindley Street (tel: 513411).
