HOW TO GET THERE
Fly Pan Am to Auckland International Airport from New York via San Francisco, and from Los Angeles via Honolulu. Flying time from New York is 18/2 hours, from San Francisco 13 hours, from Los Angeles 12/1 hours, from Honolulu 8/1 hours. Also fly Pan Am from Sydney to Auckland; flying time 2/1 hours. Time in New Zealand: GMT+12. Daylight Savings Time is observed.
REQUIREMENTS FOR ENTRY AND CUSTOMS REGULATIONS
Passport, except for residents of Australia who are British subjects; visa not required of British subjects or British Commonwealth citizens, but non-Europeans must have temporary entry endorsements on their passports. US citizens do not need visas for a stay of 30 days or less. Most other nationals do, unless they hold a forward booking for within 20 days. Currency; no restrictions, except that import of NZ currency is prohibited. Maori artifacts may not be exported. Pistols and revolvers may not be imported, and a license must be obtained for other firearms. Narcotics are prohibited. Duty-free allowance: 200 cigarettes or 50 cigars or ?2 pound of tobacco; one-fifth US gallon of liquor and one-fifth of wine.
