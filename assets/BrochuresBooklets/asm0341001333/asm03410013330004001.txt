BUT FIRST...
ARE YOU ELIGIBLE?
First things first: are you eligible? Check yourself against the following list of qualifications for probationary Stewardess . . .
1.	Age 21 thru 26 at the time employed by Pan American.
2.	Height: 5'3" to 5'9" (1.60 to 1.75 meters).
3.	Weight: 105 to 140 pounds (48 to 63 kg.), in proportion to height.
4.	Marital Status: Single.
5.	Education: High School graduate (two years of college preferred).
6.	Languages: Applicants must be fluent in English and have a reasonable knowledge of one of the following languages: French, Spanish, Italian, German, Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Turkish, Russian, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic, Hindustani or Greek.
7.	Must be a citizen of the United States or able to obtain an immigration visa to the United States.
8.	Must be able to obtain and maintain passport, visas and U.S. resident status, and be able to travel freely and perform duties as assigned while in all lands served by Pan American.
9.	Must be in excellent health—able, in fact, to pass a flight medical examination and receive various inoculations. Vision must be at least 20/40 in one eye and no worse than 20/100 in the other eye. Eyeglasses are not permitted while on duty. (Contact lenses are permitted only in special cases.)
10.	Must have good posture and appearance.
11.	Must be of good moral character.
12.	Must have a pleasant speaking voice and habitually use good English.
13.	Must have a pleasing personality, poise, tact and diplomacy—and possess a genuine liking for people.
14.	Must be willing, adaptable and prepared for hard work.
15.	Must be able to swim.
16.	Although Stewardesses are normally based in the U.S., applicants must be willing to accept assignments outside the continental United States should the need arise.
HOW DO YOU APPLY? Now, if you feel that you meet Pan Americans requirements, the next question is, “How do you apply?” Depending on whether you are located in the Eastern or Western part of the United States, you should write directly to either the Employment Office, Pan American World Airways, Hangar 14, JFK International Airport, Jamaica, New York 11430 or the Employment Office, Pan American World Airways, International Airport, San Francisco, California 94128. Applicants who are in the New York City or San Francisco area are invited to apply in person to either of the above offices any weekday morning.
If you are in the Miami area, you are invited to write or visit the Employment Supervisor, Pan American Building, N.W. 36th Street, Miami, Florida 33159. If you live outside the U.S.A., communicate with your nearest Pan Am office and ask when an interviewer will be in your area. You must pass a check of qualifications— height, weight, languages, etc. — before being interviewed. Travel and other personal expenses involved in being interviewed are paid by the applicant. Once accepted, you’ll undergo an intensive training course at Pan Am’s International Stewardess College in Miami.
WHAT TRAINING DO YOU RECEIVE? Training takes five weeks and includes extensive classroom work in a variety of subjects—plus actual training under in-flight conditions in a specially built replica of a Clipper cabin interior, called a “mock-up.” The subjects you’ll study are:
•	Crew conduct
•	Personal grooming
•	First aid
•	Customer relations
•	Baby-care
•	Passenger service
•	U.S. Customs Regulations
•	Currency conversion
•	Aircraft documentation
•	Aircraft familiarization
•	Emergency equipment and procedures
•	In-flight food preparation, meal service and beverage service (conducted in airplane mock-up)
•	Theory of flight, navigation, communications
