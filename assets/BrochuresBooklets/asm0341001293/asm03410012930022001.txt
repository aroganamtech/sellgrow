International Requirements and Agreements
Awake already?
That’s good, because breakfast will be served soon, and we’ll be landing in Paris a little later. Meanwhile, I can tell you something about the arrangements made between our government, and that of France, permitting us to make this trip across the ocean and put down at the airport just outside Paris.
To operate an international airline, it isn’t enough just to have airplanes, flight crews, ground crews, fuel, oil and the other physical things that go to make up scheduled air transportation. You need something else that is basic to international air transportation, and that is the permission to land at your destination.
Each nation has sovereignty over the land within its boundaries. Most of the land is owned by citizens of the country, but the government has final authority where the rights and welfare of all the people of the country are concerned, or in other words, it exercises sovereignty.
