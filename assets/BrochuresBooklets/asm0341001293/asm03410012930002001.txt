This map is based on the polar azimuthal projection — it shows approximately what you would see from a point in space directly above the North Pole, or what you would see looking down at the North Pole on a globe, with land areas below the equator added.
The route, wide blue line, is that of the round-the-world trip described in this book. For simplicity, most other routes of the Flying Clippers are not indicated, but if they were, they would link up most of the cities whose names are shown. The thin blue lines are alternates for travel round the world.
Air line mileage of this round-the-world route is 20,700 miles. If flown without any stopovers, by Jet Clipper, the journey could be completed in 36 hours flying time.
