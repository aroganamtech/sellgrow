“A lot of girls want to be stewardesses, and a lot of young men want to be stewards. There are plenty of applications. But a lot of people just aren’t eligible. They’re very fine people, but just don’t fit in for stewards and stewardesses. So even though we have all these applications, we sometimes have to advertise in the papers, and sometimes the Flight Service Superintendent has to go out on recruiting trips. If you know anyone who wants to be a steward or stewardess, there are plenty of openings. And it’s no disgrace if you are not selected — if we picked someone who was not suitable such a person would just be unhappy and quit.
“People are hired for steward and stewardess jobs in groups, and put into classes for training.
“At Kennedy Airport in New York, at Miami, and at San Francisco, these classes are in session just about all the time. Training has to be continuous like this, so we’ll have new stewardesses to fill the jobs when the girls get married, or resign for other reasons.
“I trained at New York. In the training department there we have a Clipper cabin mockup, with real airplane seats, a galley, all the equipment used by cabin attendants and a public address system. The stewards and stewardesses in training start as “passengers” They sit in the seats, and the instructors serve them. Then we change around. The people in training do the serving and the instructors criticize.
“We have lectures and demonstrations, too. And we have very thorough instruction as to what our duties will be in case there is an emergency. Fortunately, most of us never have an occasion to carry out these emergency duties, but we’re always ready. We have refresher courses in them constantly, all the time that we are employed.
“If we pass the cabin attendants instruction course, we’re assigned to flight duty — the first time
out just to watch, and do what the purser tells us to do, and then with a regular assignment.

“Will I quit and get married? I suppose so, most do.
“To a pilot or a passenger? Not very likely, although it does happen sometimes. The Captains and the business men passengers generally are married already, and I guess the junior pilots already have girl friends. Anyway, the stewardesses don’t marry pilots or passengers any more frequently than nurses marry doctors or patients, and probably less frequently.
