Flying in the United States, or Europe and some other places, there are more aids to air navigation provided on the ground. But to a large extent, those aids to air navigation are there to control the traffic. More airplanes are flying on the airway, and it is necessary to keep them separated from each other. Out here, with less traffic, it’s easier for the few of us to look out for each other.
“The way things are now, airplanes can go anywhere — so long as there is a place to land when they get there. And fast, too. The schedules for Jet Clipper service around the world call for a little over 60 hours elapsed time, about 40 hours in the air. So it is possible for an air passenger to circle the globe in less than three days. Actually, a passenger will take two weeks, or a month, or even more, for a trip around the world, with most of his time available for whatever it is he wants to do at various points en route.
