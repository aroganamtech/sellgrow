Clipper Services
We chose to make the first leg of our trip around Aviation’s One World, the flight from New York to Paris, via First Class President Special Service, and change at Paris to Rainbow Economy Service for the flight to Rome and Beirut.
There are two basic types of service in international air transportation, with varying degrees of comfort and luxury, and with different fare levels: First Class President Special Service and Rainbow Economy Service. We selected the First Class President Special Service for the trans-Atlantic flight of this book. There are also President Special Services on other routes. The President Special Service — for which an extra fare is charged — features fewer passengers, extra cabin attendants and complimentary cocktails and hors d’oeuvres.
Two different services are combined on one airplane. The seating arrangement is called “dual configuration.”
Where dual configuration is used on the 707 Jet Clippers, the Rainbow Service passengers, occupy the rear part of the passenger cabin, and there is a bulkhead separating the section aft from the first class section forward.
Another term used in connection with the different classes of air line service is “high density seating.” In Rainbow Economy class there are more seats in a given area than in First Class President Special — this is high density seating. In economy class, the seating arrangement enables still more passengers to occupy a given area.
In one of the photographs two side by side seats are illustrated. Note the roominess. In the other photograph, a grouping of three seats is illustrated — not quite so much room, but still a comfortable arrangement, and one especially suitable when three are travelling together.
