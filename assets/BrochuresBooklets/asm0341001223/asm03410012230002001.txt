SURF AND SWIMMING
Hawaii offers literally hundreds of inviting beaches, from the golden sands of famed Waikiki (with unexcelled swimming, canoeing and surf riding) to secluded, “undiscovered" shores, perfect for rest and romance.
TENNIS AT ITS BEST. Flower-bordered tennis courts, Hawaii’s equable climate, sports-loving companions — with such a perfect combination, "champ" or novice, you're assured of “net results.”
FISHING AND HUNTING
Fighting fish abound in Kona waters — tuna, marlin, swordfish, and crevally. Many record catches are made. Ducks, pheasants, wild turkeys, and mountain goats make Hawaii a “happy hunting ground."
HIKING AND RIDING
Inviting vistas lure you on through fern forests, fcoa trees, past stupendous waterfalls and rainbow valleys. On horseback you can explore mighty Halea-kala crater, world's largest dormant volcano.
GOLF AT ITS MOST PICTURESQUE
Imagine driving your old golf balls into the active crater of Kilauea volcano! That's just one of the amazing things you can do in Hawaii, where Sport is King.
