The palace contains a wonderful art gallery and an interesting collection of historic English furniture. Its hundreds of square feet of windows look out on the most glorious gardens in England. There are geometric boxwood mazes for you to wander through, quaint walled Elizabethan flower beds and graceful groupings of beautiful trees and shrubs. Hampton Court is another all-day sight. From April to September you may take a daily riverboat up the Thames between Westminster and Hampton Court through Richmond.
