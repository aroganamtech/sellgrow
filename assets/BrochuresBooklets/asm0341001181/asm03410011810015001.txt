Wales ... You should try to get to Wales, which has at least a a hundred old castles. North Wales is the best. The cities of the south are grim, bleak coal-mining centers not unlike our own. Go to Llangollen for the annual Eisteddfod, which is a festival for musicians and dancers from all over the world. But it is the magnificent Welsh singing such as you heard in “How Green Was My Valle’'” which rules the occasion. Llangollen is in Denbighshire where flows the River Dee.
