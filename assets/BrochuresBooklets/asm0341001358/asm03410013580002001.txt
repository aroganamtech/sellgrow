LEVEL 1
Arriving via DOMESTIC flight, departing via INTERNATIONAL
flight: Your baggage will be transferred automatically, and you should go to the International Departure area for your flight.
Arriving via DOMESTIC flight, departing via DOMESTIC flight:
Baggage will be transferred without any additional effort on your part. And you should go to your specific departure gate for your flight.
Arriving via INTERNATIONAL flight, departing via DOMESTIC
flight: Proceed to baggage claim area for your flight (it will be found on Level 1) and go through U.S. Immigration and U.S. Customs clearance. When this has been done, Pan Am will transfer your baggage to your Pan Am domestic flight. You will go to the elevator in the WEST wing, and take it to Level 3; from there, you go to the specific departure gate for your flight.
When transferring to another airline, outside the Pan Am Woridport: If you are arriving via a Pan Am international flight, you must clear U.S. Immigration and Customs before proceeding to the shuttle bus platform at the NORTH end of the Woridport on Level 1. If you have come in on a domestic flight, you may claim your baggage immediately and go immediately to the bus area.
Please use the pictures to help guide you through Pan Am’s Woridport.
