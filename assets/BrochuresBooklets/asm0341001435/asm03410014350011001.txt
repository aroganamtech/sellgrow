CLIPPER HALL
The Home of Pan Am’s History
The proud home of Pan American’s history— Clipper Hall—is located in the Pan Am Building Ticket office in New York. The memorabilia displayed in Clipper Hall symbolize the origin and development of Pan American World Airways... a story identical with that of international air travel.
Collected as a result of a world-wide treasure hunt, the historic items found in Clipper Hall offer living testimony to aviation’s fabled beginnings. Here are famous relics such as General Charles A. Lindbergh’s camera and drafting instruments with which he photographed and charted routes and courses for Pan Am; the airplane engine and propeller from the Fokker F-7...Pan Am’s original, tri-motored aircraft; scale models of many aircraft flown throughout Pan Am’s history; a world map used by Juan T. Trippe, aviation pioneer, founder of Pan Am and now Honorary Chairman; a Mayan stone urn discovered by workers preparing a landing strip in Latin America...and many other important landmarks from days gone by.
