With cruising speeds upto633 milesperhour —10% faster than the 707—passengers will be able to save from 30 minutes to an hour on Pan Am’s long distance routes. The greater capacity, speed, efficiency and fuel economy on the 747’s forecast increased comfort and service for passengers.
Through its order of 33 Boeing 747’s at an estimated cost of $765,000,000, Pan Am is again first with a new generation of aircraft...as it was first with so many of the planes that made aviation history.
And the supersonic age of air transportation is closer than ever before. Pan Am’s supersonic airline of tomorrow will be able to streak across the skies at speeds up to and above 2,000 miles per hour. Within the next few years you will be able to fly at Mach 2 (1,450 MPH)—twice the speed of sound—aboard Pan Am’s new Concordes. You can also expect to ride at Mach 3 (about 2,000 MPH) in a new American built SST (Supersonic Transport). Pan Am engineers, designers and technicians will have a hand in aviation’s destiny. The Clipper 747’s, SST’s and Concordes will meet the same high standards of performance and dependability that Pan Am always imposes upon all aircraft added to its fleet.
