BOEING 707—Developed jointly by Pan Am and Boeing, this jet aircraft can fly non-stop between New York/Buenos Aires or London/Los Angeles.
BOEING 321C JET FREIGHTER—Another major breakthrough for Pan Am...the first all-cargo Jet Clipper. It can carry over 40 tons of cargo at speeds of 575 MPH.
BOEING 747—Pan Am will be the first to fly this new aircraft. 10% faster than the 707, the 747 will mark a new era of luxury and spaciousness for air travelers.
