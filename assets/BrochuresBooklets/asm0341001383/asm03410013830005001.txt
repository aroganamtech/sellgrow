n Important Announcement For Clipper Club Members
You may have heard or read that Pan Am has announced the sale to United Airlines of our Pacific Division, including the Orient and South Pacific. This will allow us to better concentrate and focus our efforts on providing you with the finest in airline service to Europe, Africa, the Middle East, South America, the Caribbean, and within the United States including Hawaii.
Pan Am and United have agreed that all Clipper Club memberships will be honored for whatever the duration of their term in Clubs that
are currently operated by Pan Am in the Pacific as Clipper Clubs. United will continue the operation of Pan Am Clipper Clubs in Auckland, Hong Kong, Manila, Melbourne, Seoul, Sydney and Tokyo, and your Pan Am Clipper Club membership card will still offer you entry to the finest network of airport lounges in the Pacific Basin.
So, in renewing now, you can continue to enjoy the benefits of Clipper Club membership in more locations throughout the world than any other airline can offer.
