If you can tear yourself away from famed Waikiki’s sun and surf, explore the rest of the Isle of Oahu beyond Honolulu . . . along highways lined with eucalyptus and hibiscus . . . jungle fields of sugar cane and pineapples, and occasionally a water buffalo plowing a taro patch. Don’t miss Pearl Harbor and Schofield Barracks, Kaneohe’s fantastic coral gardens, and the amazing Blow Hole where surf makes like a Roman Candle. The scenery’s spectacular, especially Nuuanu Pali from the Koolau range, Diamond Head and along Tantalus Skyline Drive.
Honolulu boasts a volcanic crater in midtown . . . Aloha Tower for viewing the harbor ... a church built of coral . . . Iolani Palace, and the world’s finest Polynesian collection in Bishop Museum. Shop for clothes and native crafts, porcelain and jade . . . sample the native and continental dishes, the Chinese and Japanese delicacies. After dark, enjoy the gaiety of the suave and cosmopolitan hotels and night spots ... or the haunting atmosphere at an informal luau under a sprawling hala tree.
