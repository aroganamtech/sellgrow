1st DAY— CLIPPER TO THE ISLANDS. Overnight flight from Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle or Portland.
2nd DAY—HONOLULU. Arrive in the morning to an exciting lei welcome. You will be transported to your hotel. Balance of day free — shopping, sports, swimming and sun bathing — a good start on your Hawaiian holiday.
3rd DAY—OAHU. An all-day scenic motor trip around this fascinating island. For full details see SIGHTSEEING DRIVE No. 1 on page 6.
4th DAY—PEARL HARBOR YACHT TRIP. Morning free. Leave by special conveyance at 12:15 P.M. to board your yacht for a 20 mile cruise through Pearl Harbor. Pass “Battleship Row” with its sunken ships. Observe the peacetime operations of this famous base. Return to the hotel in the late afternoon.
Sth DAY—MOUNT TANTALUS TRIP. An awe-inspiring panoramic drive for a close look at the wonderful Hawaiian tropics. See SIGHTSEEING DRIVE No. 2 on page 6.
€5th DAY—WAIKIKI. This tropical beach resort is made for vacation fun, with endless things to do, both day and night. To be sure you don’t miss anything, visit the Hawaiian Visitors’ Bureau. They will be happy to give you complete information on things to see, to do, and special events which may be scheduled during your stay.
7th DAY—MAUI, THE “VALLEY ISLE”. This side trip is a short flight, with a splendid air view of the city of Honolulu, Diamond Head and Koko Head Craters on the way. From the airport, motordrive to the 10,000-foot summit of Mt. Haleakala and the brink of a dormant crater that is considered the largest in the world. Trip includes a delightful luncheon, and a visit to Iao Valley, Yosemite of the Pacific and site of the “Needle”, an amazing freak of nature. Late afternoon we check into the lovely Maui Palms Hotel, where there is time for a swim before dinner. Overnight at Maui Palms.
8th DAY—HAWAII. After breakfast, board the airliner for the one hour flight to Hilo. On arrival, drive through Hilo to Rainbow Falls and to the orchid gardens and packing plants, then on to Hawaii National Park visiting a forest of giant fern trees, blossom-covered Ohia trees, the Lava Tube, Kilauea Iki and Halemaumau craters, steam cracks and sulphur banks. Luncheon at the world-famous Volcano House.
