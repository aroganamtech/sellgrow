CIRCLE DRIVE OF OAHU ISLAND
Leave your hotel in the morning by limousine. Drive through downtown Honolulu, passing the Arts, Civic and Territorial buildings and the statue of King Kamehameha. Visit Iolani Palace, the former royal palace of the Hawaiian kingdom, containing the only Throne Room in America. Beyond the city, through beautiful tropical country, the highway enters Nuuanu Valley to famous Nuuanu Pali, an observation point on a high cliff, with a breathtaking view. Descending to the coast, the highway skirts the sea. You’ll see papaya and banana plantations, ancient Hawaiian fishponds. A stop is made at the Mormon Temple at Laie — interesting for its exquisite beauty. But just before coming to the temple, you will see a Samoan village at the entrance — a photographer’s paradise. There’s a steady succession of sights. Fishermen bringing in octopi and rainbow-colored fish; a busy mill that in season transforms swaying cane into sugar; mile upon mile of lush countryside. Beyond Schofield Barracks, there is a panoramic skyway view of Pearl Harbor. Return in the late afternoon to Honolulu and Waikiki.

MOUNT TANTALUS DRIVE
This half-day scenic drive starts from your hotel and climbs the slopes of tropical Mount Tantalus. From this height you will get a never-to-be-forgotten view of Honolulu, from Pearl Harbor to Diamond Head. You’ll be treated to the rare and delicious Macadamia nuts which grow on this mountainside. A stop is made at the Punchbowl, a 500-foot high extinct volcano crater that rises sharply almost in downtown Honolulu. You will see the exotic night-blooming cereus growing on the walls of the Punahou School. ISLAND OF OAHU
1st DAY— CLIPPER TO THE ISLANDS. Overnight flight from Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle or Portland.
2nd DAY—HONOLULU . Arrive in the morning and receive a traditional lei welcome. You will be transported to your hotel. Balance of day free — shopping, sports, swimming and sun bathing — a good start on your Hawaiian holiday.
3rd DAY—OAHU. An all-day scenic motor trip around this fascinating island. For full details see SIGHTSEEING DRIVE No. 1.
4th DAY—PEARL HARBOR YACHT TRIP. Morning free. Leave by special conveyance at 12:15 P.M. to board your yacht for a 20 mile cruise through Pearl Harbor. Pass “Battleship Row” with its sunken ships. Observe the peacetime operations of this famous base. Return to the hotel in the late afternoon.

5th DAY— MOUNT TANTALUS TRIP. An awe-inspiring panoramic drive for a close look at the wonderful Hawaiian tropics. See SIGHTSEEING DRIVE No. 2.
6th & 7th DAY — WAIKIKI. Days for rest and relaxation, for fun and shopping, for dancing, and doing all the things which Hawaii, and particularly Waikiki, offer. Leave by overnight flight on the 7th day.
8th DAY— MAINLAND ARRIVAL at Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle or Portland.
