2nd DAY—HONOLULU. Arrive in the morning to a traditional Hawaiian lei welcome. You will be transported to your hotel. Balance of day free.
3rd DAY—OAHU. An all-day scenic motor trip around this fascinating island. See SIGHTSEEING DRIVE No. 1.
4th DAY—PEARL HARBOR YACHT TRIP. Morning free. Leave atl2:15P.M.byspecial conveyance to board your yacht for a 20 mile cruise through Pearl Harbor. Pass “Battleship Row” with its sunken ships. Return late afternoon.
5th & 6th DAY — WAIKIKI. Days for rest and relaxation, for fun, shopping, dancing, and all the things which Hawaii, and particularly Waikiki, offer.
T'th DAY— MOUNT TANTALUS TRIP. An awe-inspiring panoramic drive for a close look at the wonderful Hawaiian tropics. See SIGHTSEEING DRIVE No. 2 on page 6.
8th & 8th DAY—WAIKIKI. This tropical beach resort is made for vacation fun, with endless things to do, both day and night. To be sure you don’t miss anything, visit the Hawaiian Visitors’ Bureau.
lOth DAY — HAWAII — KONA. Take off from Honolulu to Kona. Check in at lovely Kona Inn or Kona Palms for leisure time until lunch. Early afternoon drive to the reconstructed heiau (temple) of King Kamehameha the Great, and the City of Refuge — then to Kealakekua Bay, visiting the strange painted church of St. Benedict, a large Buddhist Temple, the Royal Slide, St. Michael’s Coral Grotto, Chapel of St. Peter by the Sea, Iveaulou Bay, Keei Battlefield, and Hikiau Heiau. Late afternoon return to the delightful village of Kailua—Kona Inn or Kona Palms for dinner and night.
11th DAY—KILAUEA CRATER AND HILO. Early start after breakfast and drive first to visit the 1950 Lava Flow, Mark Twain’s monkey-pod tree, to Punaluu Black Sand Beach. Next comes the Lava Desert and then Hawaii National Park with its great Kilauea Crater and nearby wonders of Halemaumau Fire Pit, Twin Craters, Chain of Craters, Thurston Lava Tube and the gigantic Tree Fern Forest. Lunch at Volcano House. In the afternoon visit the orchid nurseries and packing plant, Rehabilitation Center (lauhala weaving crafts), Rainbow Falls and the City of Hilo. Dinner and overnight at Hilo or Naniloa Hotel.
12th DAY—KAUAI, The Garden Isle. After breakfast fly from Hilo to Kauai via Honolulu, passing en route Maui, Molokai and Lanai. On arrival proceed to Kauai Inn for a delicious buffet lunch. In the afternoon drive through Ana-hola, Kapaa, Hanapepe Valley to Waimea Canyon, the Grand Canyon of the Pacific. On the return visit Nawiliwili Harbor,

13th DAY—FERN GROTTO and HANALEI. After breakfast drive to Wailua River for motor-boat excursion to the Fern Grotto through a world of rare jungle beauty. The Evergreen Cave (fern grotto) presents a fantastic scene with thousands of ferns growing downward from its roof. Drive north through vast pineapple fields to primitive Hanalei Valley. Picnic lunch. Visits are made to the dry cave, where ancient chiefs held court, and the wet cave, allegedly dug by the Menehunes. Return in the late afternoon to airport for the short flight back to Honolulu.
