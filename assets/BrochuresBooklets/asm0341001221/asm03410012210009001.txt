1st DAY — CLIPPER TO THE ISLANDS. Overnight flight from Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle or Portland.
2nd DAY—HONOLULU. Arrive in the morning and receive an Hawaiian lei welcome. You will be transported to your hotel. Balance of day free — shopping, sports, swimming and sun bathing — a good start on your Hawaiian holiday.
3rd DAY — OAHU. An all-day scenic motor trip around this fascinating island. For full details see SIGHTSEEING DRIVE No. 1 on page 6.
4th DAY—PEARL HARBOR YACHT TRIP. Morning free. Leave by special conveyance at 12:15 P.M. to board your yacht for a 20 mile cruise through Pearl-Harbor. Pass “Battleship Row” with its sunken ships. Observe the peacetime operations of this famous base. Return in the late afternoon.
5th DAY and ©th DAY— WAIKIKI. Days for rest and relaxation, for fun, shopping, dancing, and all the things which Hawaii, and particularly Waikiki, offer.
7th DAY — MOUNT TANTALUS TRIP. An awe-inspiring panoramic drive for a close look at the wonderful Hawaiian tropics. See SIGHTSEEING DRIVE No. 2 on page 6.
8th DAY and 9th DAY - WAIKIKI. Free for rest and relaxation or for swimming, surfboard riding, shopping and other activities for which this resort is famous.
lOth DAY—MAUI. The first of six days of exciting adventure and exploration. Leave Honolulu Airport for departure to Maui. Morning excursion to the summit of Haleakala, one of the world’s largest extinct volcanoes. Return to Haleakala Mountain Lodge for your luncheon. In the afternoon, motor to beautiful Iao Valley, historic Hawaiian battleground. Late afternoon check into the lovely Maui Palms Hotel in time for a swim before dinner. Overnight at Maui Palms.
11th DAY—HAWAII. After breakfast, board the airliner for the one hour flight to Hilo. On arrival, drive through Hilo to Rainbow Falls and to the orchid gardens and packing plants, then on to Hawaii National Park visiting Kilauea Volcano, the Lava Tube, Tree Fern Forest and Halemaumau, main fire pit of the volcano. Steam cracks and sulphur banks are found here. Luncheon at the famous Volcano House. In the afternoon drive to Black Sand Beach at Punaluu, thence via Kailua to Kona Inn or Kona Palms for dinner and the night. Hawaii, of course, is the “big Island” and it’s still growing. You can see how Nature does the job with volcanoes —- especially at Kilauea Crater.

12th DAY — KONA — KEALAKEKUA. All morning motor trip through the Kona district visiting Honaunau (the City of Refuge), the Coffee Mill, the plantation country and the little church of St. Benedict with its painted mural walls. Return to Kona for lunch. Afternoon and evening are free for sightseeing, sports and fun on your own. Deep-sea fishing is good here.

Ith DAY — KONA COAST CRUISE. After breakfast, stroll or drive to Kailua Pier to board the motor vessel “Captain Cook” for a 14-mile cruise in the sheltered waters of leeward Hawaii to Kealakekua Bay and historic monument to Captain Cook. Transfer to the glass-bottom boat “Coral Queen” for a view of the most beautiful coral gardens in the world. Step ashore at the very spot where Captain Cook fell. Return to Kailua to enjoy an excellent buffet lunch. In the late afternoon board the plane from Kona and proceed via Honolulu to Lihue Airport on Kauai. Arrive at lovely Coco Palm Lodge or Kauai Inn for dinner and overnight.
14th DAY — KAUAI. After breakfast, begin the picturesque tour by motorboat up the Wailua River to the Evergreen Cave, a fantastic scene with thousands of ferns growing downward from its roof. Return to hotel for lunch. In the early afternoon drive to Hanalei Valley. Visits are made to the Dry Cave where ancient chiefs held their court, and the Wet Cave, allegedly dug by the Menehunes (elves). Return to Coco Palm Lodge or Kauai Inn for dinner and the night.

15th DAY —WAIMEA CANYON. After breakf ast visit Nawiliwili Harbor, Alakoko Fish Pond, Kuhio Park and beautiful Poipu Beach. A stop is made at the famed Spouting Horn—a salt water geyser exploding sea water high into the air. Then move on to the western side of the island enjoying a picnic lunch en route. From there to Waimea Canyon, the Grand Canyon of the Pacific. The long climbing road leads to Ivokee and Kalalau Lookout for one of the Island’s most spectacular views. In the late afternoon return to Lihue Airport to board the airliner back to Honolulu.
16th DAY to 21 st DAY—WAIKIKI. This tropical beach
resort is made for vacation fun, with endless things to do both day and night. To be sure you don’t miss anything, visit the Flawaii Visitors’ Bureau. They will give you complete information on things to see, things to do, and special events scheduled during your stay. Leave by overnight flight on the 21st day.
22nd DAY — MAINLAND ARRIVAL at Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle or Portland.
