Nature’s at her technicolored best on Maui, Kauai, and Hawaii . . . and Molokai and Lanai, too. They’re spectacular, with vivid foliage, volcanoes and plantations. You’ll find jungles, forgotten villages and even a forest of 40-foot ferns . . . gorges, cascades and Akaka Falls, higher than Niagara. And wonderful beaches everywhere . . . beaches with black sand, sheltered and coral-free, and beaches where the sand barks under your feet.
On Maui, don’t miss Lao Needle, the 1200-foot granite monolith . . . and Haleakala, the world’s largest inactive crater. You’ll say your entire trip 8 was worth just one sunset here, 10,000 feet above the
sea. On Kauai, be sure to see the weird Haena Caves and Napali Cliffs . . . Hanalei Valley, "birthplace of Rainbows”, and Napoli Trail. ..and Waimea Canyon for the colors the mist and sunlight paint on the canyon walls.
And out on big Hawaii see seething, hissing Kilauea Crater, the ‘‘pit of eternal fire”. Here, too, are famed Mauna Loa, 2l/2 miles high, and Mauna Kea, where you can hunt wild boar or ski on tropical snow. At Hilo you’ll shop and send home orchids by Clipper. But be warned about the Kona Coast. It makes you forget clocks and calendars as you battle marlin twice your size or laze under the sun like a native in the days before Captain Cook.
