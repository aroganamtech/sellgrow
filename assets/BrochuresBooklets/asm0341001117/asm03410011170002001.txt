Sound Sleep in air-conditioned Comfort
Pan American’s new Clippers welcome you to pleasant, restful slumber aloft. Wide, curtained berths comfortably accommodate two people. Controlled ventilation, improved soundproofing and smooth flying above the weather—all add up to a perfect night’s sleep while your Clipper speeds you to your destination. Here is a truly delightful new travel experience.
