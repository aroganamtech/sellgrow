THE PEOPLE POINT OUT THE EDUCATIONAL VALUE OF HU SHIH S STATEMENT . . .
From a Rochester, New York high school teacher . . . “Your magnificent advertisement written by Dr. Hu Shih is truthful advertising which proves to our nation the work and sacrifice which lies ahead ... it is the students now in high school and college who will benefit.”
From the Department of Correction, City of New York . . . “We were very much struck by the significance of your advertisement containing a unique statement by Dr. Hu Shih ... we are now preparing the annual educational report for this department. Believing the statements by Professor Dewey and Dr. Hu Shih both significant and appropriate we would appreciate your kind permission to include them in our report.”
From another high school teacher . . . “You have happily combined advertising with public service ... I am enclosing one of the letters written by a second year English student after we had read and discussed your advertisement . . . It is still on my bulletin board and is still being talked about.”
