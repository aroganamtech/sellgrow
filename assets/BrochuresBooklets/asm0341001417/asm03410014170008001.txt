WHAT THE PEOPLE THINK . . .
A letter from Rockford, Illinois . . . “Concerning the statement signed by John Dewey . . . Congratulations to Pan American Airways for one of the most intelligent and interesting advertisements I have ever seen ... A contribution not only to the art of advertising but to the war efforts and the peace effort to follow.”
From a letter signed bij a Captain in the Army Air Forces ... “I have been interested in your series of statements which was begun by the words of Dr. Dewey. This idea is a very brilliant and valuable one and deserves the attention of all those who are looking upon the war as merely a preview to the ‘free world order’.”
From the AAA (U. S. Department of Agriculture) . . . “We wish to compliment you on your fine advertisement which appeared in Time, October 31, 1942. Would it be possible for us to get 100 copies of this for use in our county Triple A office? The illustration of the man behind the plow is very appropriate.”
From a New York City publisher ... “I wonder if there would be any chance of getting re-prints of the series of advertisements beginning with the first one containing a statement by John Dewey. These are outstanding and really revolutionary in bringing home to the people the meaning of the ‘people’s war’.”
From a teacher at a California college ... “I am wondering if you have re-prints of the statement by Dr. John Dewey and the statement which followed, by Dr. Hu Shih. If you have, I could use them to great benefit with our students who are training to become teachers.”
