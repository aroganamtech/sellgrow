THE PUBLIC RESPONDS
As you will see on the pages which follow, none of the statements published by Pan American carried any request for a reply. Nevertheless there was a response from people in every walk of life — members of the armed forces, farmers, college professors, high school teachers, Protestant Ministers, high officials in the Catholic Church, businessmen, mothers of fighting men, and members of women’s clubs and societies.
* * *
Excerpts from some of these letters begin on Page 6 of this booklet and are continued on pages 7, 12 and 13, 20 and 21, 26 and 27. The last page, in the back, lists some of the organizations by name from which letters were received.
In the excerpts themselves, the people speak.
Their statements show, we think, a greater public awareness of the problems facing our world in the future than some students of public opinion might have believed possible.
