WHAT THE PEOPLE SAID . . .
From a man in Jacksonville, Florida . . . “Permit me to congratulate the parties responsible for the lovely, timely, and highly significant advertisement featuring a statement by Archbishop Stritch . . . N. B. The contribution of Count Carlo Sforza has just come to my attention. The above applies to the Sforza statement also.”
FROM CANADA TO THE GULF . . .
From a man in Toronto, Canada . . . “Your advertisement with a statement by Dr. Stephen Leacock has attracted very favorable comment in Canada. . . A voluntary committee in charge of finding homes for orphans in Ontario has asked me to write to you to see if your company would grant permission for them to use Dr. Leacock’s message.”
From a Sunday school teacher in Tyler, Texas ... “I am to teach a course on The Church and America and another on the Global Gospel. I have been attracted to an advertisement signed by the Pan American Clippers in Life, May 3rd issue. I have clipped it for use in these courses.”
FROM SEAMEN AND BUSINESSMEN . . .
Written on the stationery of the U. S. O. . . . “Recently I returned from sea. When I read the statement by Dr. Karl T. Compton, that article made me very happy . . . Dr. Karl T. Compton has shown me what I had hoped, and still do hope may some day come true. He has shown me what the future of our country will be like. ... I am in the U. S. Merchant Marine, and shortly return to sea as one of the officers in the engineering department.”
