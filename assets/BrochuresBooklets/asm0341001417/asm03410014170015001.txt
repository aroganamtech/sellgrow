From a Methodist minister in California . . . "Please accept and convey to Pan American my personal appreciation for the two-page display in the November 23, 1942 issue of Life ... I speak for many who will fail to write. Thanks for helping us in our work!"
From a man in Texas . . . “The writer is a member of The Society of Friends (Quakers). Your advertisement quoting the Archbishop of Canterbury came like a breath of pure air in the terrible atmosphere of much of the present postwar activities."
From a Chaplain in the U. S. Naval Reserve . . . “The advertisement of the Archbishop of Canterbury has such a fine picture and text that I think it would be a most appropriate addition to the walls of my office in this U. S. Naval Training Station."
From a Professor of Psychology of the University of Texas ... “I was tremendously impressed by this statement" (Archbishop of Canterbury) “and very grateful to your organization for having made it available to the public.”
From a Chaplain in the U. S. Army Air Corps . . . “It would be a particular assistance to the six of us Chaplains at (deleted) Field to have copies of this advertisement” (Archbishop of Canterbury) “for use on our bulletin boards in order to bring it to the attention of the men . . . My sincere congratulations to you for having planned and used this material.”
From a man in West Virginia . . . “May I tell you how inspiring to me was your two-page devotion (it would not be fair to call it just an advertisement) entitled 'The Full Development of Personality.’
From the pastor of a church in Massachusetts . . . “You have a marvelous advertisement quoting the Archbishop of Canterbury ... We would like to distribute through our parish AT ONCE 1,500 copies of this Pan American advertisement."
