FORUM OF THE FUTURE
A series of announcements published in national magazines by Pan American World Airways during the period from August, 1942 through April, 1944.
Even before Pearl Harbor, Pan American World Airways found itself so deeply involved in providing over-ocean air transport for members of the armed services, for government officials and others on war missions, that no space for civilian passengers or for ordinary commercial air cargo was available aboard the Clippers.
Under these conditions, it was asked, “Why shouldn’t the company advertising also make a contribution toward winning the peace to come? . . . Why couldn’t various leaders of world thought help to clarify public thinking by defining the kind of world we Americans are fighting to create?”
Thus, the “Forum of the Future’’ came into being, with the opening statement signed by lohn Dewey, at the end of August, 1942.
From the beginning of the series to date, twelve statements have appeared, all with their main emphasis on what the post-war world will, or might at least, be made to hold for the common man.
