AIRPORT INFORMATION
Kai Tak Airport is in Kowloon, across the harbor from the city center. Airport bus to Hong Kong Island costs HK$2. Taxis are available, with fares to downtown Kowloon averaging about HK$5, and HK$20-30 via tunnel to Hong Kong Island, a 20-minute ride.
There is an airport departure tax of HK$15. The airport has a duty-free shop, but all of Hong Kong is duty free. Kai Tak Airport also has a hotel reservations counter.
ACCOMMODATIONS
Hong Kong boasts some of the most luxurious hotels in the Far East. There are moderately priced hotels on both sides of the harbor and a number of inexpensive guest houses, mostly in Kowloon. For young people there is the International Youth Hostel at 90 Hung To Road, Kowloon, and for budget-minded travelers of all ages two YMCAs in Kowloon and YWCAs both in Hong Kong Island and in Kowloon.
