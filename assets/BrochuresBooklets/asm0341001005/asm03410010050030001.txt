In the underground mausoleum here there are carved marble thrones. Ten miles from Peking is the beautiful 12th-century Luouchiao (Marco Polo bridge), made of white marble with carved, animal-topped balustrades. The city Loyang has a quaint old section with narrow streets and some very interesting Han Dynasty graves containing early paintings. Chufoo, close to Tsinan, capital of Shantung province, was the birthplace of Confucius. It has a lovely temple and grounds, and the philosopher’s grave a wood partly surrounded by earth walls.
