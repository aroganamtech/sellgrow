Hong Kong
WHAT’S SPECIAL
Imagine the cultural and ethnic presence of the People’s Republic of China in a territory whose official language is English. Or think of the age-old traditions of the Far East contrasting with the commercial and banking activities of the West, and you’ll begin to realize what makes Hong Kong ayinique travel experience: unique because, in a dozen miles, you can pass from a rural People’s Republic of China with farmers tending small fields to startling forests of modern buildings; then, almost in their shadow, to a village of boats whose people are born afloat, go to school afloat, and shop, eat, and die afloat.
The first Europeans to sail into the area were Portuguese navigators whose voyages paved the way for the foundation of Macao, Portugal’s 16th-century beachhead on the Cantonese coast. Ry the late 1600s the lure of a flourishing trade with the Orient had also attracted the British, whose East India Company kept up a lively commerce throughout the 18th century in the area, based in Canton. But with the introduction of opium into the bartering scene, Sino-British relations degenerated rapidly.The famous “opium wars” that followed were settled by the 1842 Treaty of Nanking, which ceded Hong Kong to the British. Kowloon was added to the Crown possessions in 1860, and the New Territories and adjacent islands were leased from the People’s Republic of China in 1898.
COUNTRY BRIEFING
Size: Hong Kong, Kowloon, and the New Territories have a combined area of 403.7 square miles.
