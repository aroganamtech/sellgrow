HONG KONG
Population: 4,600,000
Capital: Hong Kong has no capital as such, though the Central District (formerly known as Victoria) is the downtown commercial area.
Climate: Mild, with heat and humidity rising as spring wears on, and a rainy spell in summer. Winters are chilly enough for an overcoat. Best season—autumn.
Weather in Hong Kong: Lat N22°; Alt 109 ft

HOW TO GET THERE
Fly to Kai Tak Airport, Hong Kong, via either of Pan Ain’s “Round the World” flights: westbound from Honolulu via Tokyo or Eastbound from New York via London and Delhi. Also nonstop westbound from San Francisco. Westbound, flying time from San Francisco 14% hours, Honolulu 13% hours, Tokyo 4% hours. Eastbound, flying time from New York is 21% hours, London 15 hours, Delhi 6% hours. Time in Hong Kong: GMT+8.
