Another recommended hotel is the Matsuya, Calcada de San Francisco (from US$20 double). The smaller but newer Sintra (from US$19 double) is close to the heart of the city. Nightclubs and casinos can be found in the city (the gambling is especially lively), and there is a choice of restaurants with excellent Oriental and Portuguese cuisine. This “special territory is under Portuguese administration” (as it is termed to avoid the People’s Republic of China’s displeasure) is 75 minutes by hydrofoil or 2/2-3 hours by ferry from Hong Kong. A passport (but no visa) is needed for most Western tourists. Those needing visas may obtain them at the Consulate-General in Hong Kong (14th floor of the Central Building at Pedder Street). The Government Information and Tourism Office at Government House is helpful in arranging tours to outlying Coloane and Taipa islands where there are pleasant small inns and beaches.
