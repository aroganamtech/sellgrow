Equator and International Date Shortly before you reach Canton Isiai will cross the equator an imaginary line earth midway between the poles Midway berween Canton Island and the Fijis on the South Pacific routes and two-thirds of the way between Honolulu and Wake Island on the Central Pacific Routes, the Clippers will pass over another imaginary line—the International Date Line. This line follows approximately the 180th Meridian on either $ide of which the date differs by one day. When you cross the International Date Line westbound you lose one- day from the week—eastbound, you gain a day; thus flying from Canton Island to the Fijis ot Honolulu to Wake Island you lose Saturday, Sunday, or any other day in the week concerned. This lost day is made up on the eastbound flight when you will have two Saturdays, two Sundays, etc.
