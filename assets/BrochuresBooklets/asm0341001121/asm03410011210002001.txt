For, as the Holy Father wrote:
** We have no doubt that this will be done in a special manner at the Grotto of Lourdes where there is such ardent devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary conceived without stain of sin.
But let this holy city of Borne be the first to give the example, this city which from the earliest Christian era worshipped the heavenly mother,
its patroness, with a special devotion. As all know, there are many sacred edifices here, in which she is exposed to the devotion of the Boman people; but the greatest without doubt is the Liberian Basilica.
Encyclical Letler of Pope Pius XII on the Marian Year, “Fulgens Corona Gloriae”, September 8, 1953

MARIAN SHRINES in the Western Hemisphere, as well as those in Europe, will be the scene of many Pilgrimages during 1954. Two of the most famous are conveniently reached by Pan American Clippers.
(Upper Photo) Just 4 miles north of Mexico City, a Clipper stop, is the national shrine of Our Lady of Guadalupe, patroness of Mexico, where the 16th century miraculous mantle of Juan Diego is displayed.
(Lower Photo) Our Lady of the Angels, Cartago—near San Jose, another Clipper stop — where the Patroness of Costa Rica appeared to the Indian slave girl.
