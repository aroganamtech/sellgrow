ROUND
SOUTH AMERICA
2 1 st day to 28th day	CUZCO — Inland trip by rail, overnighting at Puno on the shores of Lake Titicaca, to the one time capital of an Empire embracing one third of the continent. Your sightseeing in Cuzcc will include the city proper, an excursion to the citadel o Sachsahuaman, and a full day drive to Pisac for the Sunday market; the climax will be a full day trip by motor and horse back to the outstanding ruins of Macchu-Picchu. Return by rai to Arequipa.
29th day to 3 1 st day 32nd day to 34th day 35th day 36th day	LIMA — By plane to the capital of Peru and visit this beautifu city founded by Pizarro; also excursion over the famed Centra Highway to Chosica and the Incan ruins of Cajamarquilla. QUITO — Morning flight to the highland capital of Ecuadoi where you visit the Colonial gems of Quito and the famous Equatorial Monument. BALBOA — Leave by plane via Cali, Colombia, for the Cana Zone, visiting Panama City and the locks, crossing the Isthmus to Cristobal.
36th day	U. S. GATEWAY — Arrive by afternoon plane.
U. S. TRADE WITH LATIN AMERICA
The pleasure traveler AND THE BUSINESSMAN alike will find EXPRINTER SERVICE ever ready to care for all needs. Passport visas are no more a problem; detailed and authoritative information is furnished on any subject. The pleasure traveler will encounter many opportunities en route for some business angle which would not have occurred to him unless he had taken this trip. If the main purpose of the trip is from a business viewpoint, the need for advance reservations and occasional relaxation will be well met by a prearranged itinerary.
