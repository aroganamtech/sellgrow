ST. THOMAS AND ST. CROIX—Visit the two principal islands of the Virgin Islands. Time free for swimming and personal activities in these restful isles. Return to San Juan on the 7th day.
CIUDAD TRUJILLO —In one and a half hours to the capital of the Dominican Republic. Visit the city and the Cathedral containing the ashes of Columbus.
PORT-AU-PRINCE —In exactly one hour to the capital of Haiti, set in a beautiful bay. Visit the building markets, the lovely Cathedral, and the surrounding tropical hills at Petion-ville and Kenskoff.
MIAMI — Wing homeward to this U. S. Gateway.
Other interesting stop-overs can be arranged in the French Islands of Martinique and Guadeloupe, the British Leeward and Windward Islands, Barbados, Trinidad, Jamaica, and, of course, Cuba.
