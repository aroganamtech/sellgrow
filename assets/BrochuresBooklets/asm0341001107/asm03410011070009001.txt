MEXICO
CRUISE 101-9 DAYS IN MEXICO
Daily Departures
1st day MEXICO CITY — Arrive by Pan American World Airways. You will be met and transferred to your hotel.
During your holiday in Mexico, a comprehensive program of sightseeing will be furnished to include the following:
CITY —On one day you will visit many of the artistic and historic sites in Mexico City — the public buildings, the famous murals, the colorful markets, the boulevards, Chapultepec Park. PYRAMIDS — On another day motor over the Pan American highway to San Juan Teotihuacan, the “place where the gods reside” to view the Pyramids of the Sun and the Moon and the Temple of Quetzalcoatl. Stop briefly at Tenayuca and the Shrine of Guadalupe. Luncheon included.
FLOATING GARDENS —Sunday is the day for an excursion to world famous Xochimilco, the “Venice of Mexico." A box luncheon will be enjoyed aboard your boat while you go past the “Floating Gardens."
ORIZABA — A three day excursion to the scenic “Mexican Alps" into a region of flowers and orchards. Visit Cholula and Puebla and after sightseeing in this venerable colonial city, continue to Garci-Crespo, famous springs resort near Tehua-can. Travel over a spectacular mountain road into the lovely region of Orizaba visiting Cordoba and the garden spot of Fortin. Return will be over the thrilling mountain road to Mexico City.
TAXCO — A two day side trip visiting Cuernavaca, the famous resort of princes and notables, and Taxco, picturesque and hospitable little town cradled in the mountains. Overnight stop is at Taxco, with ample time to visit the famous tin and silversmiths before return to Mexico City.
9th day MEXICO CITY — Transfer to the terminal for departure to the U. S. A. by air.
