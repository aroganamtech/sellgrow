U. S. GATEWAY — Leave by plane for Panama. Arrive at the Canal Zone in the afternoon.
PANAMA CITY — Visit old Panama and the canal facilities, crossing the Isthmus to Cristobal.
LIMA — Afternoon flight to the capital of Peru. Visit the city and excursion by car to Chosica and the Inca ruins of Caja-marquilla.
AREQUIPA — Morning flight to the city of Eternal Sunshine at the foot of “El Misti.”
CUZCO — Inland trip by rail, overnighting at Puno, to the one time capital of an Empire embracing one third of the continent. Your sightseeing will include the city proper, an excursion to the citadel of Sachsahuaman, and a full day drive to Pisac for the Sunday Market; the climax will be a full day trip by rail-car and horseback to the outstanding ruins of Macchu-Picchu.
LAKE TITICACA — By train to Puno for the crossing of the highest navigable body of water in the world.
LA PAZ — Visit the capital of Bolivia, the highest in the world, at the foot of majestic Illimani. Return in the afternoon of the 1 4th day to Lima.
QUITO — Fly to the highland capital of Ecuador where you will visit the colonial gems of Quito and the famous Equatorial Monument.
BOGOTA — Morning flight via scenic Cali. Visit the capital of Colombia and the Quinta Bolivar, nearby Monserrate for the panorama, and Tequendama Falls.
MEDELLIN — Morning flight. Visit this attractive city and excursion into the luxuriant Aburra Valley and its coffee “fincas." U. S. GATEWAY — Homeward bound via Balboa or Barran-quilla — your choice, spending one night in either city.
