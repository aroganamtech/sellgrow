To Be Right, Travel Light
On the average air trip you’re allowed to carry 66 lbs. free. Over that, there’s a nominal charge. But considering that, besides the clothes you're wearing, it’s perfectly permissible to “weigh in” carrying your overcoat, umbrella, a book or two and pocket camera, it’s amazing how much you’ll be able to include in that 66 lbs. On an air-sea trip forwarding of heavier baggage can be arranged.
A canvas duffle bag can be packed in your suitcase going and will take care of the souvenirs when you’re coming back. For side trips you’ll find a canvas overnight bag convenient. Aside from these, your main piece of luggage can be whatever you want, but a Pullman case with rigid sides, measuring approximately 30" x 18" x 9", is preferred. This type can be locked and strapped for greatest protection.
