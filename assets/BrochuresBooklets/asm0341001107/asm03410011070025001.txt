Departures every Second Friday
U. S. GATEWAY —Wing over the Caribbean or Central America to the Canal Zone via Pan American World Airways. BALBOA—Visit Panama City and the locks. Leave by Panagra plane via Cali, Colombia, and Guayaquil, Ecuador, for Lima.
LIMA —- Visit the beautiful capital of Peru, founded by Pizarro, and excursion over the famed Central Highway to Chosica and the Incan ruins of Cajamarquilla.
AREQUIPA — Morning flight to this beautiful Colonial city of Peru at the foot of El Misti.
CUZCO — Inland trip by rail, overnighting at Puno on the shores of Lake Titicaca, to the one time capital of an Empire embracing one third of a continent. Your sightseeing in Cuzco will include the city proper, an excursion to the citadel of Sachsahuaman, and a full day drive to Pisac for the Sunday market; the climax will be a full day trip by motor and horseback to the outstanding ruins of Macchu-Picchu. Return by rail to Arequipa.
SANTIAGO — Fly over the nitrate fields of Chile to Santiago. VINA DEL MAR — Visit Santiago, the capital of Chile, in a beautiful setting at the foot of the Andes, and the fashionable ocean resort of Vina del Mar, near Valparaiso.
CHILEAN AND ARGENTINE LAKES — Overland trip by rail, lake steamers and motor through the incomparable setting of mountains and lakes, visiting Puerto Varas, Peulla, in Chile, and Puerto Blest, Bariloche, in Argentina from where one crosses the great pampas northbound for Buenos Aires.
BUENOS AIRES—Visit the cosmopolitan capital of Argentina, including an excursion to the picturesque boating resort of El Tigre (in summer) or to the shrine of Lujan (in winter). Sail on Friday in T.E.L. ARGENTINA, BRAZIL, or URUGUAY of the Moore-McCormack Lines, crossing the Rio de la Plata for a brief call at Montevideo, Uruguay.
SANTOS — Sightseeing tour of this great Brazilian coffee port and its beautiful beaches, with an excursion to Guaruja Island. RIO DE JANEIRO—You will drive around this most beautiful city in the world, thrill to the cable ascent of Sugar Loaf Mountain, and ascend Corcovado.
PORT OF SPAIN —Call at the island of Trinidad. Excursion over the famed Saddle Back Drive and to Maracas Beach. NEW YORK — Due to arrive.
