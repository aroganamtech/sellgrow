LIMA—Visit the beautiful capital of Peru, founded by Pizarro, and excursion over the famed Central Highway to Chosica and the Incan ruins of Cajamarquilla.
AREQUIPA — Morning flight to this beautiful Colonial city of Peru at the foot of El Misti.
CUZCO — Inland trip by rail, overnighting at Puno on the shores of Lake Titicaca, to the one time capital of an Empire embracing one third of a continent. Your sightseeing in Cuzco will include the city proper, an excursion to the citadel of Sachsahuaman, and a full day trip by motor and horseback to the outstanding ruins of Macchu-Picchu. Return by rail to Arequipa.
SANTIAGO — Fly over the nitrate fields of Chile to Santiago. Visit the capital of Chile, in a beautiful setting at the foot of the Andes, and the fashionable ocean resort of Vina del Mar, near Valparaiso.
CHILEAN AND ARGENTINE LAKES — Overland trip by rail, lake steamers, and motor through the incomparable setting of mountains and lakes, visiting Puerto Varas, Peulla, Bariloche, and Osorno.
WINGS ACROSS THE ANDES — Over the lofty Andes and the Pampas to Buenos Aires.
BUENOS AIRES — Visit the cosmopolitan capital of Argentina, including an excursion to the picturesque boating resort of El Tigre (in summer) or to the shrine of Lujan (in winter). Sail on Wednesday in the DEL SUD, DEL NORTE, or DEL MAR of the Delta Line.
SANTOS — Excursion by rail from this great Brazilian port to the progressive city of Sao Paulo, where sightseeing will be provided, including the Butantan Snake Farm. Leave by night train for Rio de Janeiro.

CURACAO — Drive around Willemstad, seat of Government of the Netherlands West Indies, a shopper's paradise.
