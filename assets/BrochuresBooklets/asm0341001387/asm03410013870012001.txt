Annual or Three-Year Membership.
For only $65 for an Annual Membership or $175 for a Three-Year Membership, plus a $25 initiation fee, you can join this very special association of world travelers. And you'll also get two attractive silver-tone luggage tags embossed with your name and membership number, along with your silver-tone membership card.
Lifetime Membership.
Invest $650 plus a $25 initiation fee just once. And receive a Lifetime Membership in the world's most exclusive airline Club. A membership that entitles you to special privileges and Club extras for the rest of your life. Among them, you'll receive a handsome, engraved brass wall plaque. You'll also receive two attractive gold-tone luggage tags embossed with your name and membership number, and a special gold-tone Lifetime Membership card.
Senior Citizen Lifetime Membership.
Invest $390 plus a $25 initiation fee just once. You will receive all of the above lifetime benefits at this special money-saving rate. Applicant must be 60 years of age or over. Copy of a photo ID containing date of birth must be attached to application.
