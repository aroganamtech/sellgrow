2ND DAY. SATURDAY, DEC. 23:
This morning you will see the City of Jerusalem. Your motorcoach will take you to the Mount of Olives and the outer walls of the Old City. Then on foot you will enter this sacred city to visit the site of the Temple of Solomon where now stands the Mosque of Omar, the Via Dolorosa (Way of the Cross), the Church of the Holy Sepulchre (in which is the Tomb of Christ and Calvary). Lunch will be at a restaurant in the New City.
The afternoon will be at leisure to revisit those places of special interest to you. Return in the evening by motorcoach to your hotel.
3RD DAY. CHRISTMAS EVE,
DEC. 24:
This afternoon, you will drive by motorcoach to the city of Bethlehem, where you will visit the Church of the Nativity. Dinner will be at a restaurant in Bethlehem. After dinner you will be free to do whatever you wish until the motorcoach will return you to your hotel about an hour after midnight. You may wish to return to the Church of the Nativity for the Midnight Service or just wander around the hills of Bethlehem, to gaze and think.
4TH DAY. CHRISTMAS DAY,
DEC. 25:
Today is free to spend as you feel appropriate. You may wish to arrange on your own to return to Bethlehem, spend the day in Jerusalem or just relax and celebrate the holiday at leisure.
5TH DAY. TUESDAY, DEC. 26:
Today is at leisure to shop, relax, or perhaps arrange an optional tour to Nazareth and Galilee. Dinner this evening at your hotel.
6TH DAY. WEDNESDAY, DEC. 27:
Transfer to Lod Airport for your flight to Rome or Athens. (Dec. 9 departure returns to the USA from Tel Aviv arriving the same day.)
2ND DAY. GOOD FRIDAY,
APR. 13:
Early this morning you will be driven by motorcoach to the gates of the City of Jerusalem. On the way your guide will point out the Palace of King Herod, take you to visit the Garden of Gethsemane and the Valley of Kidron from whence you can see the Golden Gate. Then you will enter the Old City on foot and follow the Via Dolorosa (Way of the Cross) to the Church of the Holy Sepulchre which covers Calvary and the garden of the Tomb of Christ. You will then have an early lunch in the New City, so that you may have this Good Friday afternoon at leisure in Jerusalem. You will return by motorcoach to your hotel in the evening.
3RD DAY. SATURDAY, APR. 14:
Lunch at your hotel. Late this afternoon, you will be driven to the Gates of Jerusalem, where you will be free to spend the evening as you feel appropriate. You may wish to go to the Church of the Holy Sepulchre where ceremonies are held throughout the evening in preparation for Easter, or you may wish to just wander through the streets of Jerusalem. Shortly after midnight, your motorcoach will take you back to your hotel.
4TH DAY. EASTER SUNDAY,
APR. 15:
Mid-morning you will be driven again to the gates of the Old City of Jerusalem to spend the early part of the day celebrating Easter as you feel appropriate. Your motorcoach will return you to your hotel at about 2:00 o’clock in the afternoon. Dinner at your hotel.
5TH DAY. MONDAY, APR. 16:
Today is at leisure. Perhaps arrange an optional tour to Nazareth and Galilee, or a visit to Emmaus where Christ met the two disciples and broke bread with them after the resurrection. Dinner this evening at your hotel.
6TH DAY. TUESDAY, APR. 17:
Transfer to Lod Airport for your flight to Rome or Athens. (Mar. 30 departure returns to the USA from Tel Aviv arriving the same day.)
