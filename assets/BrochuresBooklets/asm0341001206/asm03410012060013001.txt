1ST DAY. SATURDAY:
USA.
Evening departure on your Pan Am Jet Clipper flight to Vienna.
2ND DAY. SUNDAY:
VIENNA.
Morning arrival. Assistance and transfer to your hotel. Balance of day free.
3RD DAY. MONDAY:
VIENNA.
Morning city sightseeing tour. Afternoon free.
Your morning tour will show you the principal landmarks of imperial and modern Vienna. You will see the Hofburg Palace, drive along the famous Ring-strasse, and then continue to the Palace of Schonbrunn for a visit.
4TH AND 5TH DAYS.
VIENNA.
These two days free to shop, sightsee, relax, go to theatre or concerts or revisit at leisure the museums and palaces of Vienna.
Budapest at night
6TH DAY. THURSDAY: VIENNA/BUDAPEST.
Morning transfer to the train station to board the express train to Budapest. Meeting and assistance upon arrival and transfer to your hotel. Dinner this evening with wine and Gypsy music at a typical Hungarian restaurant.
Today’s train ride takes you swiftly and comfortably across the border of Hungary to Budapest, a short ride away. This evening’s welcome dinner completes the day’s transition from beautiful, dignified Vienna to the laughter and nostalgia of Budapest.
7TH AND 8TH DAYS.
BUDAPEST.
Friday morning sightseeing tour of Budapest. Saturday at leisure.
Today’s four will show you the highlights of Budapest, with its Parliament buildings, the Matthias Church, the palaces, and the modern monuments that make it unique.
9TH DAY. SUNDAY: BUDAPEST/USA.
Transfer to the airport for your Pan Am flight back to the USA, arriving the same day.
