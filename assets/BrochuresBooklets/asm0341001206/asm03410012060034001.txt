USEFUL INFORMATION:
Tour Prices are per person based on sharing twin-bedded or double-bedded room with private bath or shower at a first class hotel for the first night. The balance of accommodations are provided in twin-bedded rooms without bath or shower, at country guest houses a full day’s drive from the city of arrival. Guesthouse accommodations are included in the program in order to comply with IATA regulations qualifying the Group Inclusive Tour Air Tariff (GIT).
These accommodations are generally below the level to which U.S. tourists are accustomed, but they may be used at any time during the effectiveness of the guesthouse voucher. Single rooms, when available, are at supplements shown.
Optional collision insurance is recommended. In lieu of optional collision insurance, the rental company may require a deposit to cover the cost of the deductible collision amount at the time of delivery. Gasoline, garage fees, tolls, etc., are not included in the price. Minimum renting age is over 21 for Italy, over 18 for Germany, over 24 but under 65 years for England.
Note: Italy requires an international drivers license which is obtainable from AAA.
