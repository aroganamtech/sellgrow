Your morning city tour introduces you to the Eternal City, where memories of the past abound. Drive along the Via Veneto, lined with shops, hotels and
Interior of St. Peter's, Rome
charming out-door cafes to Piazza Bar-berini, featuring the Fountain of Triton, designed by Bernini in 1640. Then to the Piazza di Spagna to see the Fontana dell Barcaccia also by Bernini. Behind it, sloping gently upwards is the famed 200-year-old flight of 137 Spanish Steps, dominated at the top by the Church of Trinita dei Monti. Proceed via the Piazza del Popolo, with its baroque churches and Egyptian obelisk, to Piazza del Pantheon. Here stands one of Rome’s best preserved monuments of antiquity. The Pantheon was built in 27 B.C., and dedicated to the seven planetary divinities. The majestic interior is especially spectacular because of its dome that rises 142 feet tapering to a small circular opening from which it receives its only interior light.
After your visit, drive through Trastevere, and then across the Vittorio Emanuele Bridge with Castel Sant’Angelo on your right, entering Vatican City for a tour of St. Peter’s.
