7TH DAY. THURSDAY:
ATHENS.
Morning sightseeing tour of the city of Athens and a visit to the Acropolis. Afternoon at leisure. Dinner this evening at your hotel.
Your morning tour will show you the Parliament Buildings and the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier where the famed Evzone Guards stand ’round the clock. Then on past the former Royal Palace and the ruins of the Temple of Zeus near the arch
built in the time of Roman occupation. Then on up to the Acropolis where you will see the magnificent Parthenon, the Temple of Nike and the porch of the Caryatids, just to mention a few of the sights.
8TH DAY. FRIDAY:
CAPE SOUNION.
Afternoon drive by motorcoach to Cape Sounion, the southernmost tip of the Attic Peninsula, on which stands the ancient temple of Poseidon. Dinner at your hotel in the evening.

Transfer to the airport for your connecting flight to your Pan Am Jet Clipper arriving USA the same day.
