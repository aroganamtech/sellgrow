Paris by night
Continue to the Etoile, twelve avenues radiating like spokes from the central circle, dominated by the Arc de Triomphe, erected by Napoleon in honor of his victorious army; some say the very symbol of France.
But the real marvel of Paris, for many, is the Eiffel Tower, this strange monument of steel lacework. For years it was compared to a giraffe that weighed 15 million pounds and whose head soared a thousand feet high. Today, one can not conceive of its not being there.
More highlights seen on your tour are the Invalides, Rodin Gardens, the Grand and Petit Palais and the Church of Sainte Marie Madeleine.
11TH TO 15TH DAYS:
PARIS.
Five days at leisure to shop, relax, take independent excursions, or explore the city on your own.
Everybody who visits Paris loves it for a different reason, and rightly so. Paris has the knack of making you feel happy wherever you go, whatever you do. For some, Paris means Montmartre where you’ll find the celebrated all-white church, and the Place du Tertre, which puts the city on the map as a painter’s paradise.
For lovers of nightlife, there’s Pigalle with its cabarets, cafes, and a brand of striptease art born long before the permissive age.
