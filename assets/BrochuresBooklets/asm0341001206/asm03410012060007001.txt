1ST DAY. SATURDAY:
USA.
Board your Pan Am Jet Clipper for your flight to London. (Day flight tour members arrive in London the same evening.)
2ND DAY. SUNDAY:
LONDON.
Assistance on arrival and transfer to your hotel.
This afternoon you could begin to study the theatre guides to organize your plans for show going. The excitement of the London Theatre scene beckons you. Choose the shows you want to see and the days and times you wish to see them. Sunday is the only day the theatres are dark, so this is the day to map your strategy.
4TH TO 8TH DAYS.
LONDON.
Five days free to explore London on your own during the day and go to the theatre in the evening.
So much to do and see in London: The sights of historic England from the Tower of London to the new Coventry Cathedral (a day’s excursion to the north country); the great restaurants that dot the city, and of course, the shows. Your host in London will help you build your week around the four shows you want to see.
9TH DAY. SUNDAY: LONDON/USA.
Transfer to the airport for your Pan Am Jet Clipper flight home, arriving the same day.
DAY FLIGHT OPTION: For New York departures, you have an opportunity to spend an additional night in London for only a few dollars more. You’ll leave in the comfort of Pan Am’s Jet Clipper flight 100 at 10:00 a.m. from John F. Kennedy Airport, arriving in London the very same evening. You’ll travel in style and after a good night’s sleep at your hotel, be refreshed and ready to explore London the following morning.
