Highlights of your morning tour are the Houses of Parliament, a complex of elaborate spires, towers and crenella-tions; “Big Ben”, the 320 foot clock tower, symbol of London ringing out the hour. You will visit Westminster Abbey, a fine example of early English architecture, to delight in the monuments to the great and the famous. Then there’s Trafalgar Square with its landmark column (flanked by four vast lions) of the famous Lord Nelson, and not to be outdone is Piccadilly Circus, where the leading shopping and theatre avenues all merge around the statue of Eros.
13TH AND 14TH DAYS.
LONDON.
Two days at leisure for relaxing or exploring the city on your own.
London is more than the formidable Tower with its Crown Jewels. It is St. Paul’s Cathedral, the greatest achievement of Christopher Wren, the Rosetta Stone at the British Museum, Chamber of Horrors at Madame Tussaud’s Waxworks and the Zoo at lovely Regent’s Park.
These days are yours to see the sights, or take an excursion to Windsor Castle, home of English royalty since the time of Henry I.
