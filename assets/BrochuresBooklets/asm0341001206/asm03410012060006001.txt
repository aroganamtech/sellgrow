3RD DAY. MONDAY: LONDON. Morning sightseeing tour of London’s highlights. Afternoon at leisure.
Your morning panoramic sightseeing tour of London shows you the Houses of Parliament, Big Ben, Trafalgar Square and Piccadilly Circus, plus a visit to Westminster Abbey, just to name a few of London’s highlights you will see.
4TH TO 8TH DAYS. LONDON.
Five days free to explore London on your own.
For centuries London was the center of the civilized world. To many it still is.
Once you arrive, you’ll have to agree there is something special about it. There’s a feeling about the city. Of being alive.
Of the noble heritage of poets and kings, of statesmen and warriors.
It won’t take you long to discover that London is a vast and sprawling city. Yet each of her parts is as unique and exciting as the next. Whichever direction you go, London is alive and waiting. The British Museum, home of the Magna Carta and the Rosetta Stone, or the National Gallery, London’s foremost collection of masterworks, will captivate you.
Do try a pub-crawl through the East End —definitely another village, where you’ll hear some of the « quickest wit in London.
And there are lots of different excursions you can make outside London. Here are a few suggestions: The famous universities of Oxford (with its dreaming spires) and Cambridge (where the undergraduates still pole their punts on the slow-running river). Or take a trip into Shakespeare country with its lovely old English timbered houses and thatched roofs. Brighton is a must, too.
