6TH DAY. WEDNESDAY:
ROME/ATHENS.
Transfer to the airport for your flight to Athens. Meeting and assistance upon arrival and transfer to your hotel. Dinner at the hotel this evening.
7TH DAY. THURSDAY:
ATHENS.
Morning sightseeing tour of the highlights of Athens with a visit to the Acropolis.
Your morning tour will show you the Parliament Building and the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier where the famed Evzone Guards stand ’round the clock. Then on past the former Royal Palace and the ruins of the Temple of Zeus near the arch built in the time of Roman occupation. Then on up to the Acropolis where you will see the magnificent Parthenon, the Temple of Nike and the porch of the Caryatids, just to mention a few of the sights you will see.
8TH DAY. FRIDAY:
ATHENS.
This day is free for shopping, optional excursions or relaxation. Lunch or dinner at your hotel.
Today you may wish to arrange a tour to Delphi, or perhaps a one day cruise to the Islands of Hydra, Poros or Aegina.
9TH DAY. SATURDAY: ATHENS/CAIRO.
Transfer to the airport for your flight to Cairo. Upon arrival you will be met and transferred to your hotel.
1OTH DAY. SUNDAY:
CAIRO.
Early morning excursion to Giza where stand the Great Pyramids and the Sphinx, which you will visit.
Today’s sightseeing tour is outside the bustling city of Cairo to the most famous of the ancient monuments of the world, the Pyramids. You will be able to enter the Great Pyramid of Cheops and climb to the King’s burial chamber. From here you will be able to walk down the hill to the alabaster temple where the Egyptian priests performed their complex ceremonies of embalming the royal dead. Then to the marvelous Sphinx who stares out across the desert.
11TH AND 12TH DAYS.
CAIRO.
These two days are free to arrange optional excursions to suit your special interests.
There is so much to see in Egypt, that these days can be filled with optional excursions to some of the wonders that are here: The Great Bazaar of Cairo, the City of the Dead, the alabaster mosque of Mahomet Ali, or the great Egyptian Museum which is an absolute must. Perhaps you may wish to arrange a day trip to Luxor.
13TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: CAIRO/JERUSALEM.
Transfer to the airport for your flights to Tel Aviv. Upon arrival, you will be met and driven from Lod Airport to Jerusalem and your hotel. Balance of the day is free.
14TH DAY. THURSDAY: JERUSALEM.
Morning tour of the area around the Old City of Jerusalem with a visit into the Old City itself.
Today’s tour will show you the Mount of Olives, the Valley of Kidron and the venerable walls of Jerusalem. You will then enter the Holy City itself and visit some of the places that make Jerusalem a city of pilgrimage to Christian, Jew and Moslem alike. After your morning tour, the afternoon is free to return to those places that are of special interest to you.
15TH DAY. FRIDAY: JERUSALEM/HAIFA.
Drive this morning through the Valley of Armageddon to your Haifa hotel overlooking the harbor. Afternoon free.
Haifa, in the north of Israel, is located within easy distance of Galilee. While you are in Haifa, you will want to arrange some optional sightseeing, perhaps to Nazareth or Tiberias, or in the city of Haifa itself which is located on Mount Carmel.
16TH DAY. SATURDAY:
HAIFA.
Today is free to relax or explore this fascinating and historic part of the world.
17TH DAY. SUNDAY:
HAIFA/USA.
Morning transfer from Haifa to Lod Airport at Tel Aviv for'your connection to your Pan Am Jet Clipper flight to the USA arriving the same day.
