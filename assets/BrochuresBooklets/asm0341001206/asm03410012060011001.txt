Fiori, Signoria and Michelangelo Squares, Santa Croce Church and the Pitti Palace.
Florence has everything for visitors with a sense of the beautiful. Every square and ancient building is a work of art: The Piazza del Duomo, the Baptistry; Giotti’s historic Bell Tower; the Church of Santa Croce where you’ll find the tombs of Michelangelo and Machiavelli; and that most famous of bridges spanning the Arno — the Ponte Vecchio.
5TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: FLORENCE/ROME.
Transfer by private motorcoach to Rome. Balance of day at leisure.
6TH TO 8TH DAYS.
ROME.
Morning sightseeing tour followed by two and a half days to explore on your own.
Your morning city tour will take you to the Piazza’s Barberini, di Spagna and del Popolo, and visits to the Pantheon and St. Peter’s.
There is always something to do or see in Rome. Shopping the smart boutiques and stores at the foot of the Spanish Steps, or just wandering through the old streets, you will always find a surprise to make a memory. But wherever you go, you’ll find yourself surrounded by the ever-present sense of history and timelessness which is the true atmosphere of Rome.
