LOCATION . .. Except for a small area south of Bulgaria on the European continent, Turkey lies in Asia, occupying the peninsula of Asia Minor, across the Black Sea from Russia and across the Mediterranean from Egypt. Istanbul is 843 airmiles from Rome.
CHARACTERISTICS... Turkey is a wonderful vacation spot which is rapidly growing more popular with cosmopolites who are discovering for themselves its wonderful beaches, splendid climate, classic examples of Byzantine architecture, and an atmosphere of sophistication. The Turkish people are extremely cordial and international-minded. If you are looking for something different with the exotic atmosphere of the East, you'll like Istanbul.
POPULATION . . . 20,934,670 (1,000,020 in Istanbul).
SIZE. . . 294,416 square miles, 10 per cent larger than Texas.
CAPITAL ... Ankara, population 286,781.
GOVERNMENT ... A Republic with a National Assembly elected by universal suffrage. The President is elected by the Assembly.
HOW TO GET THERE... By Clipper from New York to Istanbul, 20'/2 hours, to Ankara 211A hours routings via Frankfurt, Paris, London, and Rome. By ship, about 15 days via Italy, Egypt and/or Greece.
