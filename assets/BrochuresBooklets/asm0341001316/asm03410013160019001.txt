No warehousing! Less capital tied up in stock! Newer merchandise! Less chance of overbuying! All reasons why more and more people ship cargo via Pan Am—to destinations all over the world.
Clippers carry airmail on every flight
62% of the U. S. airmail to territories and countries overseas flies Pan Am. In a year’s time this nets the Government a profit of about $6,000,-000 from stamps. Pan Am receives an average of 52 cents per ton-mile on air mail—compared to an average of 68 cents per ton-mile on passenger traffic.
