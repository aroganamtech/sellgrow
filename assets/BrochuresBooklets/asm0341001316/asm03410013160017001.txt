GUIDED MISSILES. Now Pan American is using its
long experience in the Caribbean to operate a guided missile test range under contract to the U. S. Air Force. Pan Am is responsible for the planning and operation of the range in Cape Canaveral, Florida. This covers launching, testing and tracking of ballistic bombs and long-range guided missiles. Pan Am and its principal subcontractor employ more than 6,000 people on this project.
AIRLIFT IN THE EVENT OF EMERGENCY.
Our entire fleet of Clippers and flight crews are instantly available for military traffic to any part of the world. The cost to the Government of maintaining these aircraft and the flight crews would amount to $83,000,000 a year. In a special airlift, Pan Am carried 260,000 pounds of food, clothing and drugs from charitable organizations in the U.S. to Hungarian refugee camps. As another example, Pan Am brought from Australia to the United States some 30 members of the Hungarian and Rumanian Olympic teams who elected to come to America rather than return to their native lands.
GOVERNMENT
SERVICE. Today, in Afghanistan, Pakistan, Turkey and Thailand, Pan Am personnel are training personnel of national-flag airlines to assure technical and administrative efficiency. Fees earned by Pan American under its several contracts with the U.S. Government serve to reduce the company’s subsidy needs and thus, in effect, are turned back to the U.S. Treasury.
