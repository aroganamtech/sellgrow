Thousands of small businesses . . . great new businesses and new careers have been created by an industry which was itself new in the twenties.
The designing and building of plane engines opened up one vast new field which alone employs thousands. And engines are only one example of many vital parts which are completed in their own factories and delivered to the aircraft builders for
installation in airliners. Propellers, Sperry gyroscopes, and seats are a few more examples of these vital parts which are manufactured in their own individual plants. The fuselage is practically the only part of the plane which is made by the aircraft builders. It is constructed, insulated and decorated in a vast operation that requires many people.
