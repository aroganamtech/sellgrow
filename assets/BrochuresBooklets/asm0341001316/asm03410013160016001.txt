GOVERNMENT SERVICE
AIRLIFT IN THE EVENT OF EMERGENCY
GUIDED MISSILES
In the past, Pan American has been called on in emergencies. During World War II, over half of the military services assigned by the Government to the U.S. air transport industry were entrusted to us. Pan Am furnished 25% of the contract air transport service—developed, constructed or improved 50 airports—trained about 5,000 navigators and other airmen—rendered extensive aircraft modification services.
In June 1950, within 24 hours after a request was received from the Department of Defense, Pan Am aircraft were carrying needed supplies for Korea across the Pacific. Pan Am, with its own aircraft and those of 4 subcontractors, furnished about 25% of the total airlift furnished by civilian operators during the Korean emergency. It stands ready to perform such services again—if needed. Meanwhile. ..
