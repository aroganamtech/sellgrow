SOUTH AMERICA
You—and people in many other lands—are Pan American World Airways. Nearly one quarter of Pan Am’s employees are stationed overseas. More than 75% of these employees are local people trained by Pan Am. Their job, like yours, is vital to the successful operation of our world-wide transport network.
Pan Am’s task is a tremendous one: to link all six inhabited continents with scheduled air service. In this task, we face more competition than any other airline in existence—almost 70 competitors at last count, most of them foreign-flag carriers with far lower wage rates than our own.
