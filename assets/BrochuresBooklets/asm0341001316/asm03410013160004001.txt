By “tourists” we mean passengers who travel for pleasure rather than on business. They help make friends for our country abroad. Their tourist dollars enable foreign countries to pay for badly needed American exports and help reduce the need for foreign aid. The amount of these tourist dollars now exceeds one-and-a-half billion, and this year more of the Americans who spend them are going abroad by air than by sea.
