Last year, for example, a new high of nearly a billion and a half dollars was spent by tourists traveling from the United States to Europe and other countries. Every tourist dollar spent abroad helps to pay for U.S. machinery, pharmaceuticals, fountain pens, aircraft and a thousand other U.S. manufactures. The more U.S. goods sold abroad, the greater is employment here at home.
