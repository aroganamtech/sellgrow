... Pan American’s New Electronics System for faster, more efficient

Needless to add, service to the public is vastly improved, telephone waiting time greatly reduced and “lost” calls virtually eliminated. PAT is an example of alert airline management which keeps pace on the ground with advances in the air.
How It Works. Reservations clerk simply inserts a destination plate into the keyset (below). . . pushes buttons for the requested month and day . . . pulls a lever.
An electrical impulse races to the master reservations board (above). . .“searches” the slot for the requested date and destination . . .“bounces” back to the ticket office to tell whether or not the flight is open.
