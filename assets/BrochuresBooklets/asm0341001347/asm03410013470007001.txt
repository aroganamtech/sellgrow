Friday
Flight departs JFK 7:00 p.m. Check in at Pan Am counter then proceed to First Class Lounge one hour prior to your jet clipper evening flight direct to London. “Theatre in the Air” is available and dinner will be served on board.
Saturday
Breakfast will be served aloft prior to your morning arrival at Heathrow Airport, London. Afternoon free.

Sunday

Following sightseeing, etc. transfer back to Dublin. Reserved accommodations at Jury’s Hotel, evening dinner and cabaret.

Early morning departure from Dublin. Motor coach transfer to Shannon Airport, arriving early enough for shopping at duty free shop. Departing Shannon Airport on Pan Am’s flight 79 at 2:45 p.m., arriving in New York 2:45 p.m.
