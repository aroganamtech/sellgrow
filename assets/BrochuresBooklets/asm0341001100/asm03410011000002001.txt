LOCATION . . . Colombia lies in the northwest corner of South America at the northern end of the Andes, the only country on the continent which fronts on both the Pacific and Atlantic oceans.
CHARACTERISTICS . . . Colombia is perhaps the most purely Spanish of all South American countries, a characteristic which is reflected in its architecture. Bogotá, the capital, is frequently called the “Athens of America” because it is a cultural center.
The principal thoroughfare, Carrera 7a, stretches for about 85 blocks. It is a study in colorful contrasts with women in smart French frocks and Indians wearing ponchos. The hotels, clubs and theaters are very fine. Orchids grow in wild profusion in Colombia and are one of the sights of Medellin. Colombia is a country of infinite variety.
POPULATION ... Estimated 11,537,605, the country has about as many inhabitants as New York, Philadelphia and St. Louis combined.
SIZE . . . 439,830 square miles, slightly larger than Texas and California combined.
CAPITAL ... Bogotá, with a population of 643,187, is near the size of Milwaukee.
GOVERNMENT ... A republic governed by a President.
