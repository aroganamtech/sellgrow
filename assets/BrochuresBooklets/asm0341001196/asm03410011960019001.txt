GREAT BRITAIN
magnificent views and beautiful areas for climbing and walking. The business and tourist center of Caernarvon is here, as is Caernarvon Castle, official seat of the Prince of Wales. To the northwest are the Menai Straits, Anglesey, and towering masses of rock in wild, barren countryside.
Northern Ireland
The beautiful countryside of Northern Ireland makes it a lovely place for a vacation; the unspoiled scenery of the countryside contrasts starkly with the current political unrest. In a quieter time, the visitor would do well to spend his days relaxing and walking or playing golf at one of the many well-known golf courses.
