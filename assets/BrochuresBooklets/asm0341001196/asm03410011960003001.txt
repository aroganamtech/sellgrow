Great Britain
WHAT’S SPECIAL
Great Britain—England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland—combines a past unequaled in its political, economic, and cultural greatness with a present that provides a variety of attractions for visitors from all over the world. This densely populated and amazingly compact country provides a feast of history and beauty in its monuments, cathedrals, artistic treasures and ceremonial grandeur, but Britian is not only a museum of the past; there is a viable and lively present-day culture, which still offers the best in drama, art, and music.
The joys of the British countryside are world-famous for the sheer beauty of the natural landscape and for the charm of the villages sprinkling it, as well as for the majestic public monuments to be found throughout the country. It is a place where the North American traveler, especially, will feel at home; the common language and the cultural and social heritage derived from the mother country will provide a basis for the visitor to see the real differences between the cultures. Britian is today seeking a new world role for itself, and perhaps the key to its success will lie in its traditional sense of the importance of human rights and tolerance, its dipolmatic skills, inventive genius, and dry, self-effacing sense of humor.
