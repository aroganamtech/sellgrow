HOW TO GET THERE
Fly Pan Am direct to London, Heathrow Airport, from the following cities: Detroit via Washington; Seattle; Los Angeles; Honolulu via San Francisco; Houston via New York; Berlin via Hamburg. Flying time from Detroit is 8 hours, Washington 6/1 hours, Seattle 9 hours, Los Angeles 10 hours,Honolulu 15 hours, San Francisco 10 hours, Houston 9/1 hours, New York 6/4 hours, Berlin 2 hours, Hamburg hours.
London is also served each day by Pan Am’s “Round the World” flights eastbound and westbound. On westbound flights flying time from Tokyo is 22h hours, Hong Kong 17*2 hours, Delhi lOh hours, Frankfurt IJ2 hours. Time in Great Britain: GMT+1. Daylight saving time observed.
REQUIREMENTS FOR ENTRY AND CUSTOMS REGULATIONS
A valid passport; visas not required for US or Canadian citizens. Smallpox and cholera vaccinations required if coming from an infected area. Duty-free allowance: 1 pound tobacco in any form (% pound if coming from a European country); 1 bottle liquor and 1 bottle wine; % pint perfume. You can bring in an unlimited amount of foreign currency, but you may officially take out not more than you declared upon entry. Note: British customs checkpoints offer a choice of two entry gates— green, “nothing to declare” (subject to spot checks), and red for those
