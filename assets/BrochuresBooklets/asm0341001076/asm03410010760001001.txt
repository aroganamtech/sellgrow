To the eastward are the Windward and Leeward Islands. These include the French islands of Guadeloupe and Martinique. On the latter, the Empress Josephine, first wife of Napoleon Bonaparte, was born.
Trinidad, lying a short way off the South American mainland, is becoming increasingly important as a source of oil. Almost every visitor to the island makes certain to see the seemingly-inexhaustible asphalt lake. Here is the place to hear strange and fascinating melodies of the Calypso singers, the "voice of Trinidad.”

BERMUDA
Bermuda, only a few hours from New York via Flying Clippers, is a Gulf Stream paradise cut off from the hustle and bustle of larger lands. In this cool, idyllic Arcady, Bermudians have learned to embrace the leisurely delights of -civilization, their pure air touched only by the scent of myriad semi-tropic flowers, the wafted tang of sea breezes and the delicious fragrance of cedar. The romance of perpetual June lingers here 365 days a year; Bermuda has been called "a fairy garland dropped in a rainbow sea.”
You can cycle over lovely coral roads to pure white sand beaches, or take a leisurely horse and buggy ride past vine-covered stone fences on your way to an afternoon of unexcelled sailing.
