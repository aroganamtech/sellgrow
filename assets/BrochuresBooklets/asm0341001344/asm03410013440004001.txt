During the winter months of early 1967, New York's Summer Festival Queen Nancy Davison traveled the continent to stimulate interest in visiting New York City, particularly among those groups that planned to travel to North America for a visit to Expo 67 at Montreal, Canada this summer.
Her tour took her to Oslo, Stockholm, Helsinki, Copenhagen, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt, Berlin, Paris, Milan, Turin and Rome.
In each of those cities, Summer Festival Queen Nancy bore the best wishes of the people of the City of New York and the greeting of His Honor the Mayor, John V. Lindsay.
Her arrival and activities attracted widespread attention in the press, and a sample of the newspaper accounts is included here.
In addition to these efforts, which were sponsored and carried out under the supervision of the New York Convention and Visitors Bureau with the cooperation of Pan Am, Pan Am itself does much to promote New York as a destination to be visited by the peoples of the world.
In the past year, Pan Am spent nearly $5.5 million throughout Europe and other parts of the world in promoting travel to the United States. Some $1.6 million was focused on travel to New York City.
In addition to this advertising effort, which included newspapers, magazines, sales promotion materials, outdoor advertising signs and radio and television, Pan Am maintains 272 sales offices in 13 countries, states and territories that actively distribute materials and promote travel to New York.
In addition, Pan Am has 177 general sales agencies and 5,884 travel agents that encourage the residents of other lands to visit New York on their trips to the continental United States.
