



Pan Am’s cargo operations at John F. Kennedy International airport provide another economic stimulus to the City of New York.
The company’s $8.5 million fully automated cargo terminal has doubled the capacity of that airport to handle cargo. This new facility not only provides jobs for hundreds of workers, but will enable the airport to absorb the projected increases in the importance of air freight, which are forecast at nine times current levels by 1976.
As a citizen of the city, Pan Am contributes to many local charities and worthy causes. Through the payroll deduction method, the company’s employees are consistent and generous contributors to the Greater New York Fund, and other civic, health and welfare efforts.
As an employer, Pan Am is proud to be among the leaders in the hiring of paraplegics and other similarly handicapped citizens.
Pan Am is proud of New York. It is proud to be an active member of the community, with a substantial responsibility in helping New York in its role as gateway to the hemisphere.
