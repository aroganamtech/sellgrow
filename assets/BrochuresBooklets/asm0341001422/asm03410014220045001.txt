During World War II, over half of the military services assigned by the United States Government to the air transport industry were entrusted to Pan American. It furnished 25% of the contract air transport service--developed, constructed or improved 50 airports --trained about 5, 000 navigators and other airmen--and rendered extensive aircraft modification services. We have quoted above what Presidents Roosevelt and Truman have said as to the value of this service.
In June 1950, within 24 hours after a request was received from the Department of Defense, Pan American aircraft were carrying needed supplies for Korea across the Pacific. Pan American, with its own aircraft and those of 4 sub-contractors, furnished approximately 25% of the total airlift furnished by civilian operators during the Korean emergency. It stands ready to perform such services again--if needed.
Pan American is presently utilizing its long experience in the Caribbean to operate a guided missile test range under contract to the Air Force.
Pan American's subsidy for rendering these essential services compares with the sum of $44,454, 000 disbursed in 1954 in connection with the Government's price-support program for peanuts.
