HOW DID PAN AMERICAN COME INTO EXISTENCE AND WHAT HAS IT ACHIEVED?
Pan American was created in 1927* shortly after General Charles A. Lindbergh's solo flight from New York to Paris, Its founders were twelve World War I pilots* each of whom subscribed $25, 000„ The initial organization consisted of two aircraft, some twenty experienced employees, and a 90-mile route from Key West, Florida to Havana, Cuba,. Its 27-year growth to approximately 125 large transport aircraft and some 18, 000
employees has been accomplished, in accordance with American tradition, by private capital under United States Government regulation.
Pan American was the first airline to carry the American flag
into Latin America, first across the Pacific, first across the Atlantic, and first around the world. Its transpacific air service, initiated in 1935, was the first transocean passenger service in the world. Although its total volume of business places it among the four or five largest airlines, it is surpassed in this respect by at least two other American carriers; the
government-owned French company, Air France, claims to be the "largest" airline, apparently on the basis of its route mileage.
During World War II, Pan American rendered service to the Government which President Roosevelt characterized as having an importance that "cannot be overestimated" and which President Truman stated "could not have been equalled by any other Allied agency, " In the post-war period it has taken the lead in the introduction of low fare service on overseas and international routes-~thereby placing foreign, travel within the pocketbook of the average man, stimulating international goodwill, and helping to shrink the "dollar gap",
