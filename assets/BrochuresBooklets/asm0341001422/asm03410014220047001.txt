HOW MUCH DOES PAN AMERICAN’S SUBSIDY REALLY COST THE GOVERNMENT?
Of the $18, 7Z8, 000 subsidy estimated for Pan American by the Civil Aeronautics Board for the fiscal year 1955, some $9, 000, 000 would immediately return to the Government in income taxes.
For the same period the Government will realize a net profit of approximately $6, 000, 000 on U. S. mail carried by Pan American; this represents the difference between the revenues received from the sale of postage stamps to the public for this mail and a full allocation of related costs.
The above two items substantially offset the subsidy estimated for the fiscal year 1955„
Any remaining subsidy balance would be more than offset if Pan American were paid for carrying U„ S. mail at the same rates paid foreign-flag carriers, which rates are fixed by Postal Administrations of the world as members of the Universal Postal Union.
