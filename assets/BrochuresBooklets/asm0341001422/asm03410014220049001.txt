CONSIDERING THE AMOUNT OF SERVICE RENDERED, DOES PAN AMERICAN RECEIVE A DISPROPORTIONATE SHARE OF THE TOTAL AIR TRANSPORT SUBSIDY?
The Civil Aeronautics Board estimates that of the total subsidy for the fiscal year 1955 for international, overseas and territorial air carriers--$28, 070, 000 excluding intra-Alaskan and intra-Hawaiian operations and international stub-end operations of domestic carriers--Pan American will receive $18, 728, 000 or 67% of the total. However,
Pan American will render approximately 67% of the service performed by this group of carriers, so that its share of the subsidy will be the same as its proportion of the service rendered. Pan American has much more than its proportionate share of the long, thin national interest routes--such as those to the South Pacific, South Africa, and up to the door of the Iron Curtain in Finland and Austria.
