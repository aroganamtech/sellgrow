WHO OWNS PAN AMERICAN?
Pan American is owned by the public. It has 29, 000 shareholders of record plus an estimated 1 1, 000 shareholders whose stock is held for them by brokers. The average stock holding is 152 shares. At the current market price, this represents a value of some $2, 600 per shareholder.
*
Fifty-three percent of the stockholders own less than 100 shares. It is not believed that any stockholder, including those whose stock is held by brokers, owns more than 2% of the Company's stock. (See Chart No. 2. )
Pan American's 40, 000 stockholders are located in each of the 48 states of the United States. (See Chart No. 3 showing distribution of stockholders by states. )
