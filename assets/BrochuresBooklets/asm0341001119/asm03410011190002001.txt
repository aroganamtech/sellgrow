If Scandinavia is the “old country” to you, you already know its charm — it has an appeal like no other land on earth. But if you’re looking to Denmark, Norway, Sweden, and Finland for a holiday . . . ah, you’ve a surprise treat in store.
These Lands of sunlit nights are lands of enchantment, where scenery was sculptured by a master hand — from the steep fjords, towering mountains and tumbling waterfalls of Norway, to the islands, the waterways and modern cities of Sweden. From the picture-book towns, prosperous cities and fertile plains of Denmark, to the lakes and parks and fine modern cities of Finland. Everywhere a scene you’ll want to capture with your camera.
But Scandinavia is more than this. Wherever you go you’ll find good cooking, warmhearted people, party-making hospitality and such a round of gaiety that you’ll hate to leave when the time comes to say Farvel. And remember: your dollar buys much, much more in Scandinavia and Finland. You can travel here at bargain prices.
