Accommodations
Rome is a busy place year-round, so it’s a good idea to make hotel reservations in advance. Hotels in the deluxe class include the Ambasciatori, Bernini Bristol, Excelsior and Eden — all on or near the fashionable Via Veneto. The Cavalieri Hilton atop Monte Mario is ten minutes from the heart of the city. In the first class range are the centrally-located Michelangelo, Napoleon and Metropole. The Claridge, Hermitage, Residence Palace and Ritz are in residential sections.
Rome also has many pleasant and inexpensive pensioni, where rates range from US $14 to $40 daily with all services, meals and taxes included.
The Bellavista Milton, Delle Legazioni, Sitea, Scalinata di Spagna, Suisse, Tea, Texas,
Villa Borghese and Waldorf are among the most popular. There are also several youth hostels and six campsites within easy reach of the city.
