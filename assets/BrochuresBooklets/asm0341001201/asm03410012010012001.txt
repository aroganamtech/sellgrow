Shopping
What: Rome’s best buys are antiques, ceramics, gifts, gloves, jewerly, knitwear, lace and linen, leather goods, men’s clothing and hats, religious articles, silks, silver, straw goods, watches and women’s dresses.
When: Store hours are 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. and 3:30 to 7:30 p.m. Where: The main shopping center is formed by Via Condotti, Via Frattina,
Piazza di Spagna and Via Sistina.
You might try John Bernard Ritter for antiques, Moccia for ceramics and Myricae for general gifts. Gucci specializes in leather; Bellini in lace and linen, Landsman in gloves.
For the latest in men’s fashions, go to G. Battistoni,
Cucci, Angelo, Palazzi, Piattelli ...and for hats, to Barbisio or Fabrizi- Ladies couturier fashions can be found in the shops of such famed designers as Emilio Pucci, Fabiani and Valentino.
