On Your Own
While you can’t cover Rome completely without a taxi or bus, we suggest you do as much as possible on foot, using the street map in this folder. You might ride to a central area — Vatican City, the Spanish Steps, the Colosseum — then get out and walk. It’s even more fun discovering things on your own with the Pan Am Tours on Tape cassette, “Rome Walking Tour.” When you drop it into a portable tape player, the voice of an expert guide will take you around the major sights...fill you in on their history, good restaurants and shops along the way. This tape is available for US $2.95 through your Pan Am Travel Agent or Pan Am office.
On Tour
If pressed for time, take one of the conducted bus tours available at any tourist office. These are easily booked by the concierge at your hotel.
Or contact American Express (Tel. 688751), CIT (Tel. 479041), Thos. Cook & Son (Tel. 479441). The average cost of a half-day tour is 2,500 lire (US $4.30), including entrance fees, tips and guide.

Monuments: Among the most notable landmarks in Rome are the Baths of Caracalla, the Theatre of Pompey, where Julius Caesar was murdered, and Castel Sant-Angelo (Hadrian’s Tomb). Others include the Roman Forum, Colosseum, Pantheon and Piazza di Spagna. The catacombs of St. Callixtus and St. Sebastian along the old Appian Way are also worth a trip.	1
