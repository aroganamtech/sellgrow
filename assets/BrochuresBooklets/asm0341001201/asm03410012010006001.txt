Welcome to Rome (Leonardo da Vinci) Airport
Passing Customs
There are no currency restrictions. Visitors from non-European countries may bring in 400 cigarettes or 1 lb. of tobacco, 1 bottle of liquor or 2 bottles of wine. Visitors from European countries may bring in 200 cigarettes or Vi lb. of tobacco, 14 bottle of liquor and 1 bottle of wine. One still and one movie camera, five rolls and two reels of film are allowed duty-free. It’s best to doublecheck before departure, as customs regulations may change. When you leave Italy, there’s an air departure tax of 1000 lire (US$1.75).
Changing Currency
If you didn’t bring a foreign currency packet, you’ll want to change your money or ■travelers checks into Italian lire at one of the airport exchange counters. The lira is valued at 580 to US $1. Travelers Checks are honored everywhere in Rome.

To Town
Leonardo da Vinci Airport is on the Mediterranean coast,
22 miles from Rome. Airport buses make the trip frequently. Charge is 800 lire (US $1.40). The same trip in a taxi costs about 6000 lire (US $14.) plus tip. Rental cars are also available.
What To Tip
If you take a taxi into Rome, the driver should get 15% of the fare. The minimum tip is 100 lire and there’ll be a surcharge of 150 lire after 10 p.m. Tip the station porter 130 lire per bag; hotel porter 100 lire minimum. The chambermaid gets 100 lire a day or 500 a week. Doorman gets 100 lire for hoiling a taxi, breakfast waiter 100. An additional service charge will be included in your hotel bill.
