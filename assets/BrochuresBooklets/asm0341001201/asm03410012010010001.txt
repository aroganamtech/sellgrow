Side Trips
Tivoli (Hadrian’s Villa) and the fountains of the Villa D’Este are only a half-hour drive from Rome. The halfday tour to both costs about US $6.
Storybook Frascati, the ruins of Ostia Antica, and the fortress of Bracciano are also within easy reach of the city. Those planning to take side trips on their own, using cars or public transportation, will find the directions and background information in Tourist Pathways Through the Province of Rome most helpful.
Cultural Events
Music: Opera, at its height during the winter season, is a gala occasion in Rome. Summer visitors will enjoy the open-air opera at the Baths of Caracalla and Basilica of Massenzio in the Roman Forum during July and August. There are also excellent recitals, orchestral and chamber concerts throughout the Fall and Winter.
Theater: Stage performances begin at 9 p.m. and, except for an occasional visiting company, are in Italian.
Ballet is often in conjunction with opera companies at the Rome Opera and Baths of Caracalla.
