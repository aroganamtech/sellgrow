Personally Yours helps make the most out of your time in France!
Personally Yours is a remarkable new customized travel planning service offered FREE to Avis Rent A Car customers in France. Whether you are fascinated by French folklore or enraptured by the castles and palaces in France, Personally Yours gives a complete touring itinerary individually tailored to your own special interests.
Your Personally Yours Customized Itinerary:
Just tell us which two categories you are most interested in out of the 8 categories in France. We enter this information into our computer along with data on the length of your visit. You receive a customized itinerary and a detailed guide to popular attractions in your selected categories.
The itinerary also suggests outstanding inns, hotels and restaurants en route.
See the special attractions and features only the natives would know about.
Choose from the 8 interest areas
in France:
•	Highlights of France: includes all the most popular sights in the country.
•	Undiscovered France: with its "insider’s insight” is a pleasant surprise, especially if you are revisiting the country.
•	History of France: probes the background and the past to what you will see en route.
•	Castles and Palaces: includes the region’s most interesting and historic buildings.
•	Antiques: features the country’s best antique shopping prepared by experts.
•	Parks and Gardens: is a must for nature lovers and gardeners.
•	Museums: includes some of the world’s most famous museums.
•	Shopping and Crafts: provides all the information necessary to find those “buys” France is noted for.
Here’s how to get a Free Personally Yours Computer-Generated Itinerary:
Personally Yours is offered FREE to you when you rent from Avis while visiting France. Contact yourtravel agent or call Avis Reservations Office at 1-800-331-1084.
After providing the reservation information, simply request a Personally Yours itinerary. You will need to provide the following information:
•	Your name, address & telephone number.
•	Name of accompanying traveler, where applicable. (Itinerary can be personalized with two travelers' names.)

•	Specify one or two interests of your choice in France.
