BOOKLETS GIVE STEP BY STEP DETAILS ON GROUP TRAVEL
The mysteries of "going places and seeing things the togetherness way through group travel" are explained in twin booklets produced by Pan American Airways for group organizers and travel agents.
The two volumes present in easy-to-understand simplified terms all details on organizing, promoting, financing and ticketing groups.
Written for both the group organizer who may have no previous travel experience and for the travel agent who needs specific processing information, the Pan American booklets range from planning strategy to an analysis of the rules.
Although focusing on the facts and figures of low-fare affinity group travel, the booklets also discuss other forms of group movements — common interest groups, overseas conventions and incentive programs.
One of the booklets, "1963-1964 World Wide Group Fares," shows step by step exactly how to arrange group travel via Pan American and includes a suggested speech for club and organisation meetings. An array of promotional aids, fare and flight frequency charts, a "how to apply" section and an application blank are included.
The second volume, "Pan Am's 'How to' Book of Group Travel" gives specifics on program planning and covers such topics as "How to Start Your Group on a Trip Abroad," and "How to Set Up an Overseas Convention," and "How to Use, Plan and Organize andIncentive Travel Program."
