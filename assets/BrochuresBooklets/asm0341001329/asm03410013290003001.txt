C^HE story of the clippers is as American as Bunker Hill. In the colorful, lusty mid-1800’s, when the United States was feeling its first surge of growth, the clipper ships, carrying the wealth of the Orient in their holds, brought fame and fortune to the bustling, brawling seaports of America. Swiftly their speedy hulls flashed down the ocean lanes of the world. Amoy, Foochow, Hong Kong, Ceylon, Calcutta — names with a savor as rich as the produce they gave — knew well the character of these ships and the men who sailed them.
