This challenge of speed was being met. On the drawing boards of one of the greatest shipbuilders of the time, a strangely beautiful ship was being planned. Shortly, the shipyards of John W. Griffiths were ringing to the sound of adz and saw. One of the most beautiful things ever produced in America was taking form — a clipper ship! On her maiden voyage, this ship, the "Rainbow”, broke all speed records to Canton and return, and was immediately proclaimed the fastest ship afloat. Soon other clippers were sliding down the ways in New York, Boston, Baltimore, and other Atlantic ports.
Among the most famous of these were such romantic names as "Westward Ho”, "Dauntless”, "Queen of the Pacific”, "Flying Cloud”, and "Sovereign of the Seas.”
