If you’re looking for a place that’s delightfully detached from the world, you’ll find it in Bermuda. It’s the peaceful land that lulls time to sleep and bids you to relax and enjoy the leisurely life.
It’s spring the year round in Bermuda, with spectacular flowers blooming everywhere to make up twenty-two square miles of semi-tropical beauty. You’ll stretch out lazily on bright coral sands, and picnic in secluded rock-sheltered coves. You’ll sail on turquoise sun-speckled waters . . . play golf at some of the immaculate courses that sprawl over the island. You’ll spend balmy evenings dancing on moon-washed patios overlooking a silver sea. You’ll do the million things this exciting playland offers.
Sightseeing? There’s the Aquarium, ages-old Leamington Caves, fascinating Devil’s Hole, and the intriguing Marine Gardens seen through glass-bottom boats. As a change from taxis, you can travel on bicycle . . . the way everybody goes everywhere in Bermuda ... or hire a quaint horse-drawn carriage.
Browse through Hamilton’s waterfront shops and buy British cashmere, tweeds and antiques . . . and up to a gallon of your favorite liquor.
If you’re out of the U. S. for more than twelve days, you may bring back $500 worth of souvenirs or gifts, duty free ... $200 for a lesser period.
And remember, no passports or visas are required. . . you’ll need nothing more than proof of your U. S. citizenship for vour romantic Bermuda vacation.
