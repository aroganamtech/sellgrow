WHEREAS
The advent of Trans-Pacific air travel was inaugurated on November 22, 1935 by the flying boat "China Clipper," and
Pan American Airways and the China Clipper established San Francisco as a major gateway for international flight routes; and
Pan American Airways thus carried on the tradition of San Francisco as the Gateway to the Orient; and
WIEiÆAS
Ever since the historic flight of the "China Clipper" Pan American Airways has provided San Francisco with excellent air service to 8j5 lands and 12k cities of the world; and
WHEREAS
From a handful of pioneers from the days of 1935 Fan American Airways in San Francisco has grown to a total of 2,600 men and women providing an annual payroll of more than thirty million dollars in the Bay Area; and
The pioneering spirit of the Pan American Airways family is as strong today as in 1935?
NOW, THEREFORE, I, John F. Shelley, Mayor of the City and County of
San Francisco, do hereby designate Monday, November 22, 1965 as "China Clipper Day" and urge the people of San Francisco to join with me in honoring Pan American Airways for their accomplishments in pioneering world wide aviation.
IN WITNESS VSFREOF I have hereunto set my hand and caused the seal of the City and County of San Francisco to be affixed this fifteenth day of November, nineteen hundred and sixty-five*
