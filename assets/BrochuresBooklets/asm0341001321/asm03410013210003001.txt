
The year 1965 produced an interesting contrast in the history of flight. Our astronauts established records which seem to assure our continued leadership in outer space. And on November 22, the press, radio, television and many civic and political leaders noted the 30th Anniversary of the inaugural flight of the China Clipper—Pan American World Airways' pioneering flight across the Pacific.
This publication contains some examples of the recognition accorded the China Clipper 30th Anniversary. We of Pan Am are proud of this continued expression of public awareness of our pioneer accomplishments in commercial aviation and wish to share it with you.
