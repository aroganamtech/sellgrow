Pan Am, however, feels that the time has come to talk a bit about flight dysrhythmia, and various ways that its airplanes can be used to make travel a little more restful, a little easier, for you, the passenger. Our tone will be light, (with the help of R. H. Sutherland), our illustrations (by Henry Syverson) will, we hope, be entertaining. The suggestions will be many, including a few that may startle you. We would welcome yours; after all, you are the ones with the world’s most experiences.
