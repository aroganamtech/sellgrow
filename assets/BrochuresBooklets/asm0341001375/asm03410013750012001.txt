From these facts can be drawn the conclusion that, once you leave your very own mattress, your rest and sleep aren’t going to have the same quality—until you get back to it. Thus, you should obviously try to pick up as much rest as you can, before your flight, and during your trip, to give your rest cycle the best chance to keep up with the changes that are taking place.
A good way to begin your routine is to start well before the scheduled time of your trip—say, a day or two before. (This won’t do much good if you’re reading this in a Pan Am airplane, passing through another time zone every hour, but keep it in mind for next time.)
