It seems safe to say, at this point in time, that jet travel, by itself, doesn’t upset these clocks. What CAN upset them is the combination of ultrafast travel and the human tendency to use this factor to continue activities in defiance of what his (or her) body is set up to handle. Although everyone’s pattern of life, and his life processes, are as individual as his fingerprints, everyone shares the need for adequate rest, even though everyone has his own prescription for getting the most out of that life cycle.
