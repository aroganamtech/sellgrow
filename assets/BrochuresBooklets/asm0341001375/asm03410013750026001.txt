As travel by the world’s peoples continues to grow— and as the world gets smaller and smaller in terms of travel time—today’s students of dysrhythmia may find themselves out of work, victims of the human race’s incredible ability to adapt to changes in the ways of doing things. In an amazingly short time, travel has come from a foot-flattening, kidney-shocking, mind-blanking, soot-filled ordeal, to the present simple business of walking onto a jet airplane for a few minutes or hours (with occasional delays and rare detours) . Lean back and relax, let your clocks adjust themselves. They’ve been doing it since the first set of clocks accompanied the man on his trip to the other side of that hill.
