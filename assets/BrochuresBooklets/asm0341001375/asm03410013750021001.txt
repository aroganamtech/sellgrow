PILLOWS AND BLANKETS: One of the best ways to set your mind at rest on an airplane, after the seat has been reclined, is to	put a pillow behind your head.
There’s nothing orthopedic about this, actually; the pillow is a psychological device. When the average person lies down on his bed at home, his head contacts a pillow. If his head hits a pillow when he leans back in an airplane, there’s a touch of friendly security for the mind to work on.
On the other hand, the blanket has a definite physiological use. When you fall asleep, your body temperature tends to go down, but more significantly, if your life is on a pretty regular schedule, the clock that operates your temperature will turn it down when your regular sleeping time comes around.	Covering yourself with
a blanket will trap body heat and give you a warm feeling during the times your personal thermostat is set on “low.”
