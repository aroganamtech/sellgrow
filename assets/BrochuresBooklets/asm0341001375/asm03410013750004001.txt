“Dysrhythmia” is the word used to describe the real, potential, and imaginary upsets of the body’s biological clocks caused by moving, at jet speeds, across time zones. Research into dysrhythmia is at an early stage—about the same stage as the research into that phenomenon known as “sleep”—but many doctors and scientists in allied fields, aided by such organizations as Pan Am, are probing the outer edges of the situation.
