First Minute: Follow the signs right to your flight.
The moment your car enters our three-lane, horseshoe-shaped roadway, a computer-operated sign system guides you to within one hundred feet of your flight.
The signs display your flight number and destination, along with a color-coded symbol.
Now supposing that the symbol for your flight is a red triangle. Well, all you do is follow that red triangle from your car right to your plane.
The key to our new sign system is its ability to direct you to the check-in position as close as possible to your departure gate.
After all, you’re here to fly, not to walk.
