Second Minute: While you check out of your cab, a skycap takes your bags to our check-m counter.
Curbside is about twenty-five feet from your check-in counter.
So twelve steps ought to do it.
At the counter, our agent reports your name, flight number and destination to “Panacheck,” our check-in computer.
Your luggage is quickly ticketed and automatically dispatched to your plane by way of the world’s first completely automated baggage handling system.
In about a minute and a half after check-in, your bags arrive at your plane.
Not a hand will have touched your bags along the way!
By far the fastest and most error-free in the industry, Pan Am’s new baggage handling system is equipped to handle more than 5,000 pieces of luggage an hour —about 156,000 a day.
Now that we’ve given you an idea of how you’d check-in arriving by cab, we’d like you to know how much easier we’ve made life for people who arrive by car.
This terminal’s got something no other terminal in the world’s got.
Short-term, roof-top parking.
With spaces for 400 cars. And special baggage check-in facilities right there.
