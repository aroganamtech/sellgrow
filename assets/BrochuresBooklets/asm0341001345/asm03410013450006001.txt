Fourth Minute: W; check you in, check you out and assign you a seat on the plane.
This is the last stop you’ll have to make before boarding your flight: the boarding lounge.
When you present your ticket to the flight attendant, he’ll ask, “Smoking or No Smoking?” and “Window or Aisle?”
Then he’ll assign you a seat and give you a boarding pass.
If you had more time, you could relax in comfort in our spacious boarding lounge while awaiting your flight announcement.
Our boarding lounges have separate sections for first class and economy passengers. And both sections are bright and modern.
Remember, if you didn’t have time to report to the check-in counter, you can check your bags right here at the boarding gate.
