The worlds most experienced airline’s terminal.
One of the great places to spend an hour when youVe got an hour to spend.
You can have a suit pressed at a valet service. Or take a refreshing shower in one of our rest rooms.
You can buy duty-free liquor and cigarettes. Or shop at a fashion boutique where you can also buy duty-free perfumes and cameras, when traveling abroad.
You can have a drink standing up at the African “Bush” Bar. Or have a drink sitting down at the Scandinavian “Fjord” Lounge.
You can wander through a gourmet shop examining the exotic foods. Or browse in a bookstore for food for thought.
You can pick up a tube of toothpaste at the drug store. Or send a dozen roses from a flower shop. Or cash a check at First National City Bank.
You can read magazines at the newsstand. Or curl up with a paperback thriller in our lounge.
You can have a leisurely sit-down lunch or dinner at the Window Restaurant. And watch the jets take off and land at the same time.
You can have coffee and pastry at the Viennese Pastry Shop. Or, Mexican and Spanish food at La Cocina.
You can have French food at the Brasserie. Or Mediterranean food at the Eastern Market. Or you can have American food at the State Side Restaurant.
(We call our complex of restaurants the International Food Bazaar.)
What you can’t do while waiting for a flight at Pan Am’s new terminal is get bored.
