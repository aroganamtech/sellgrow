About This Booklet
People who fly scheduled airlines seem to be endlessly curious about the industry, and people who operate airlines are just as curious about their passengers.
The Air Transport Association of America, representing most U.S. scheduled airlines, has attempted to satisfy the curiosity of its members by studying what passengers know and do not know about the industry. This brief booklet attempts, in turn, to satisfy passengers’ curiosity by setting forth some points on which there is confusion.
