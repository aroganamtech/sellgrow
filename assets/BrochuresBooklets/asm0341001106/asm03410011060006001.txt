MEXICO CITY, MEXICO
The distinguished Reforma has emerged from a million dollar "New Look” program to take its place as one of Latin America’s most modern and most beautiful hotels. Located on the fashionable Paseo de la Reforma, close to business and sightseeing centers of interest, the Reforma offers 250 newly decorated rooms and suites, all with private bath and shower; handsome interiors and gracious multi-lingual service. Le Chantedair is one of the city’s loveliest settings for dining, dancing and entertainment.
