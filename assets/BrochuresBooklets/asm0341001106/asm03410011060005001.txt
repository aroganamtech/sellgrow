HAVANA, CUBA
Situated on a bluff overlooking the blue Gulf of Mexico and picturesque Havana, the Hotel Nacional is a unique landmark of Cuba. On its handsome 15-acre grounds, originally the site of a fortress, ancient cannon still face to the sea. Everything is here for your enjoyment. Two Olympic-size outdoor pools . . . Cabana Sun Club ... La Arboleda for distinctive dining and dancing ... a new Casino, which also features outstanding entertainment . . . fine shops, card rooms, ball rooms and private function rooms. All guest rooms have outside exposure, lovely views, large private baths, and many have private terraces. Air conditioned rooms available.
