4Hotel Oarrera
One of the finest and most modern hotels in the western hemisphere, the Carrera is situated in the center of this colorful capital city. A spectacular outdoor rooftop pool and cabana club, in view of the surrounding mountains; the Robinson Crusoe Bar, festive night club and restaurants are among the Carrera’s many brilliant attractions. Guest rooms are spacious, all with private bath. A favorable rate of exchange and an ideal year ’round climate make Santiago an irresistible stop-over on any South American journey.
