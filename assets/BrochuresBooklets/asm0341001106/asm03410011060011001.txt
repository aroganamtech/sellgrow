Living is ail pleasure in an IHC Hotel. Guest rooms are spacious and immaculate and all have modern private bath. Every IHC Hotel makes the maximum of its setting, with large windows and patios providing handsome views of the city or surrounding countryside, and many have outdoor terraces, pools and other scenic features.
Many guest rooms were designed to be attractive sitting rooms by day, converting to comfortable sleeping quarters at night. Spacious suites and apartments are also available, many with private patios.
Although ultra-modern in design and fastidious in accommodations and service, IHC Hotels do not come under the luxury classification in rates. Their purpose is to provide superior accommodations and facilities for the practical traveler who desires to live pleasantly in a foreign land without paying resort rates.
