BELEM, PARA BRASIL
Built in the opulent days of the Amazon rubber boom, the Grande with its imposing architecture reflects the rich tastes of that romantic era. An extensive program of redecoration has modernized the hotel, retaining its colorful and exotic flavor. Tile baths, comfortable beds and tasteful furnishings add to the enjoyment of your stay. An attractive restaurant serves an international menu and the air-conditioned bar is a popular rendezvous for guests.
