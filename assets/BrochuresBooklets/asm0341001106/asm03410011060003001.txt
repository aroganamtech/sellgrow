Completely modern, completely air-conditioned, the splendid new Del Lago forms a striking contrast to the tropical landscape of Lake Maracaibo’s west shore. Beautiful sun terraces, the outdoor dining patio and sparkling pool and cabana club provide daytime relaxation; starlight dancing is a nightly feature. The glass-walled dining room, smart Mara Bar and a gallery of shops with European and South American merchandise are among the features that have characterized the Del Lago as one of the most exciting new hotels in the western hemisphere. All guest rooms are convertible living room-bedrooms with modern tile bath-and-shower combinations.
