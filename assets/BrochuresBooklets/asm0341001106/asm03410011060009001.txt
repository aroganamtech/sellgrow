MONTEVIDEO, URUGUAY
Air-conditioned throughout, beautifully new and modern, the Victoria Plaza towers 22 stories above the Plaza Independencia, providing a lovëly view of the tree-lined avenues and colorful harbor of Montevideo. 400 handsomely decorated rooms, all with private bath, spacious suites, smart restaurants served from stainless steel kitchens, and a dramatic night club and cocktail lounge add variety to life in this cosmopolitan city. One of the world's finest hotels!
