Tl Orel ïamanaco
CARACAS, VENEZUELA
One of the most spectacular sights in South America is the beautiful new Tamanaco, situated 3000 feet above sea level, overlooking the lovely valley of Caracas. Spacious, modern interiors . . . sun terraces and patios ... convertible living room-bedrooms and dramatic restaurants offer the traveler an unforgettable visit in the "land of perpetual Spring/’ Complete resort facilities indudecabanas, swimming pool, tennis, 9-hole pitch and putt golf, riding, dancing, entertainment and every opportunity for scenic enjoyment and relaxation. Businessmen particularly appreciate the Tamanaco’s excellent conference and convention facilities.
