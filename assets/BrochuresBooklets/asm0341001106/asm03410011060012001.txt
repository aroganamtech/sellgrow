In Latin America, every mealtime is an occasion to relax and be festive, and IHC Hotels outdo themselves in the culinary departments. Delicious international cuisine, featuring American favorites, continental dishes and native specialties, is prepared in spotless kitchens and the fine service and beautiful dining rooms add to the gaiety of each mealtime occasion. Wine cellars are stored with the choice beverages of many nations.
You needn’t stray beyond the confines of your IHC Hotel to enjoy an exciting vacation. Distinctive night clubs in many of the hotels offer music for dancing and entertainment; chic cocktail lounges, beautiful restaurants and shops with foreign treasures provide many fascinating ways to while away the hours. Several IHC Hotels have their own swimming pools and resort facilities and there are always nearby sightseeing, shopping and sports facilities to complete the perfect holiday.
