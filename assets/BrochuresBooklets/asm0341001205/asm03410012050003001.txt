Give yourself
A small one’s enough to give you the break of a lifetime. After all, haven’t you been wanting to visit Europe “someday” for years? Then rationalize. There are so many reasons why “someday” is a great big beautiful “NOW.”
Just yesterday, it seems, it took a matter of months to complete a “grand tour” of maybe 20 cities in Europe. NOW you can do the whole bit in an ordinary two or three-week vacation.
Just yesterday, you needed weeks in which to book passage, write or wire for reservations, receive confirmations, all subject to change. NOW all this confusion and uncertainty can be eliminated by the split-second pushing of a few buttons.
Just yesterday, you needed thousands of transportation dollars in your grand tour budget. NOW you can fly to all 20 of these same cities, and back, for a few hundred dollars. (Even that doesn’t have to be in cash!)
NOW you can make your “someday” dreams come true for little more than you’d spend on the usual mountain or seashore vacation. And you can go to the mountains (or the shore) any day.
Besides, you can dream about “someday” forever, without its ever coming true. NOW is different. It’s here. To be lived. To be remembered through all the “somedays” forever. Go NOW. And dream about going again “someday.” This way, you’ll be sure.
Even NOW, though, nobody’s going to force a round-trip ticket into your hand. Nobody’s going to stow you aboard a Europe-bound Jet Clipper. The first move in Europe’s direction is still up to you.
What’s involved? Nothing more than a visit or a phone call to your nearest Pan Am office. Or to any Pan Am-Authorized Travel Agent.
Don’t be shy. Give yourself that little nudge. Aim your footsteps through that doorway bright with travel banners. Lift that receiver. It’s the only way to get started. And you have to get started before you can go. Just one little nudge lets this be your year for Europe. Yours.
