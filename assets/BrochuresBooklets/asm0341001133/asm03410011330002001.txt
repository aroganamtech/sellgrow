VENICE Motor via the Brennej Pass through the glorious scenery of the mighty Dolomite Mountains to Cortina and on to Venice—the "Pearl of the Adriatic.” Morning sightseeing includes the Doge’s Palace, St. Mark’s Cathedral and a gondola ride on the Grand Canal.
FLORENCE Drive via Padua and Bologna over the Apen-nine Mountains to Florence—the "Cradle of the Renaissance.” Our sightseeing tour includes the Medici Chapels, the Cathedral, Ponte Vecchio, the Pitti Palace and Uffizi Gallery of art masterpieces followed by a drive to the old Etruscan city of Fiesole for its fine view of Florence. A day of leisure, shopping for bargains in silks and leather goods.
ROME Motor via the medieval hill towns of Perugia and Assisi to Rome—the "Eternal City.” Two days of sightseeing includes the Pantheon, the Roman Forum, the Colosseum, the Vatican Museum, the Sistine Chapel, St. Peter’s Cathedral and the Catacombs. One day at leisure.
PISA Drive via Siena, an important Italian art center, to Pisa. At Pisa visit to the Baptistry, the Cathedral and the famous Leaning Tower.
NICE Motor via Genoa and the Italian and French Rivi-eras to Nice—fashionable Mediterranean resort. Morning at leisure. Afternoon excursion to Monte Carlo.
AVIGNON Drive via Aix-en-Provence to Avignon containing the ancient Palace of the Popes.
VICHY Via Valence and St. Etienne to Vichy, famous for its mineral springs.
PARIS To Fontainebleau to visit the castle and continue to Paris—world center of art and fashion. One day city tour including Notre Dame Cathedral, Napoleon’s Tomb, Eiffel Tower, Arc de Triomphe, Latin Quarter, and Montmartre. Afternoon excursion to Versailles Palace and gardens. Four days at leisure.

HEIDELBERG- Drive to Heidelberg of Student Prince BADEN-BADEN fame. Our sightseeing tour includes the University and the old Castle. Lunch is enjoyed at one of the student inns. Motor to Badden-Baden, popular resort.
INTERLAKEN Motor via Basle to Interlaken.
LUCERNE Over the snows of Susten Pass, the most modern Alpine highway in Europe, affording thrilling views of the Alps, to Lucerne, charmingly situated on the Lake of Four Cantons. One day at leisure to see the Lion of Lucerne Monument, the historic bridges and the fascinating shops.
INNSBRUCK Via the Duchy of Lichtenstein and the Arl-berg Pass to Innsbruck, Austria. Our sightseeing tour includes the old part of the city, Court Church, Ambras Castle and Berg Isel.
