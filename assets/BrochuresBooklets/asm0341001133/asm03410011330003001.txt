How to Book
Send deposit with name and address to the agent who sent this book to you. Indicate tour number requested.
DEPOSIT: For reservations, the following deposit per person is required: $200.00.
BALANCE OF PAYMENT: Balance of payment is due six weeks before departure.
REFUND: Refund in full of all payments if tour member cancels six weeks or more before departure.
PASSPORT: Apply at once to your nearest U. S. Passport Office or Clerk of Federal Court for Passport Application Form. Proof of citizenship such as old passport, birth certificate, baptismal certificate or affidavit of birth; two photos, siz 3 x 3; identifying U. S. witness and passport fee of $10.00 are required. Naturalized citizens require Citizenship Papers.
SMALLPOX VACCINATION: The U. S. Health Dept, for re-entry to this country, requires a smallpox vaccination certificate of each passenger, issued within three years and indicating reaction or immunity.
CLOTHING HINTS: Limit of one suitcase, size limit 10 x 15 x 28. A small overnight bag to be cared for by the passenger may also be taken. Specific needs will vary according to individual tastes. For the most part you can adapt your usual Spring and Summer wardrobes for use aboard. Select non-crushable and easily laundered materials like nylon. Aboard ship formal attire is required only in first class. In Europe formal attire is not required. Include comfortable walking shoes, light topcoat or raincoat, bathing suit, sun glasses, soap, kleenex and toilet kit. Don’t bring an electric razor or traveling iron unless adapted to European voltages and fittings. "Limit your luggage—and you limit your cares.”
MAIL AND CABLES: Complete list of hotels provided.
