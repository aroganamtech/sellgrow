EATING AND ENTERTAINMENT
In addition to the first-rate restaurants in the Breidenbacher Hof and Park Hotel, other restaurants are excellent, though expensive: Bateau Ivre (Kurze Strasse 11—tel: 328688), with French cuisine, Midlers und Fest (Königsallee 14—tel: 328458), Walliser Stuben (Adersstrasse 46—tel: 374757) for Swiss food, and La Belle Epoque (Karl-Arnold-Platz 5—tel: 434848). Less expensive are: Csikbs, Hungarian (Andreasstrasse 9—tel: 329771); Hemesath (Königsallee 48—tel: 326359); Käsekiste (K8-Center—tel: 327540); Schneider-Wibbel-Stuben (Schneider-Wibbel-Gasse 5—tel: 365604). Popular regional dishes can be sampled in the beer taverns of the old town, the best of which are Der Schlüssel (Bolkerstrasse 45); Schiffchen (Hafenstrasse 5); and Zum Urigen (Bergerstrasse 1). Nightspot include: Pferdestall (Schneider-Wibbel-Gasse 8); Salome (Bahnstrasse 18); Die Pille (Liefergasse 11) and the bohemian Fatty’s Atelier (Hunsrückenstrasse 13).
WHAT TO SEE
A fashion-conscious city, Düsseldorfs most elegant shops are on the Königsallee (KO), the Berliner Allee, and Schadowstrasse. In the Old Town (Altstadt) overlooking the Rhine are the tower, the solitary survival from the 13th-century castle, and the twisted belfry of the Gothic St Lambert’s Church. The poet Heine birth place is Bolkerstrasse 53, and the picturesque Schneider-Wibbel-Gasse has a Glockenspiel which performs at 11, 3,6, and 9pm. The city is dotted with small lakes, and the most attractive garden is the Hof garten, where the Goethe Museum has a collection of the writer’s manuscripts and first editions. Jägerhof Castle, a former hunting lodge, has superb collections of Meissen porcelain and 20th-century paintings (Klee, Picasso, Chagall, Ernst). The Fine Arts Museum displays sculpture and German Expressionist paintings, and the adjoining Hetjens Museum covers 7,000 years of world ceramic art. Benrath Castle, 6 miles outside town, is a beautiful rococo building in a fine park.
COLOGNE (KÖLN)
This is the capital of the Rhineland, 30 miles south of Düsseldorf. It is noted for remnants of antiquity ... a 3rd century tower, the ruins of a palace (beneath the new town hall), a Dionysus mosaic floor, a gate, a precious collection of glass housed in the Roman Museum and a mausoleum in surburban Weiden.
