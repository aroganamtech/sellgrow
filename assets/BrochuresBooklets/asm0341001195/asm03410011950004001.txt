HOW TO GET THERE
Fly Pan Am direct to Frankfurt-am-Main Airport from Detroit via Washington, D.C. and London and nonstop from New York. Flying time from Detroit, 9h hours, Washington 8 hours, London 1*2 hours, New York under 712 hours. Also from Zurich to Berlin, flying time 111 hours and frequent local service between Hamburg, Stuttgart, Fankfurt, Nuremberg and Munich. Time in Germany: GMT + 1
REQUIREMENTS FOR ENTRY AND CUSTOMS REGULATIONS
A passport is the only requirement. There are no currency restrictions. Duty-free allowance: 200 cigarettes if arriving from a European country, 300 cigarettes if arriving from a Common Market country, 400 cigarettes if from elsewhere; 1 liter liquor, 1.5 liters liquor if arriving from a Common Market country; 2 liters wine, 3 liters wine if arriving from a Common Market country; 50 grams perfume; gifts worth up to 100DM, or 460DM if purchased in a Common Market country.

ACCOMMODATIONS
All cities and towns have a V erkehrsamt or Verkehrsverein to assist tourists in finding accommodations. Inexpensive accommodations may be found in pensions and guesthouses. A room and breakfast at a simple inn costs from US$6. For luxury try a “Castle Hotel” $10.
USEFUL INFORMATION
Banks: Hours are 8:30-12:30 and 2-4, with the following exceptions: Thursdays until 6pm; Friday afternoons l:30-3:30pm. Exchange offices at main railway stations are open daily until late at night. There are also exchange offices at airports and in many city centers.
Mail: Stamps can be bought from post offices, vending machines, and stations. Mailboxes are yellow.
