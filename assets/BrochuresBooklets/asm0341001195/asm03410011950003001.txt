Germany
WHAT’S SPECIAL
The landscape of Germany is varied and beautiful—the Black Forest and the Rhineland are worth visiting in themselves—and are filled with fascinating architecture in the shape of picture-book castles, palaces, and churches. Its people are industrious and hard-working, but they know how to enjoy themselves—whether playing or listening to the music that abounds in even the smallest town or relaxing in the easy conviviality of the local Bierstube.
