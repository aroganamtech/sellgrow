GERMANY
Telephone Tips: There is direct self-dial service with Germany and to many cities in the US and Europe Public booths and at cafes and restaurants. Insert two 10-pfennig coins, dial, and speak. Useful numbers:
Newspapers: Many foreign newspapers are available.
Tipping: Restaurants: service charge of 10$ included in checks, and 1-3DM can be left in addition; taxis 3$; porters, IDM per bag; hotel porters, 1DM; chambermaids, nothing except for exceptional service; cloakroom attendants, 50 pfennigs.
Electricity: 220 volts 50 cycles AC.
Laundry: Quick and efficient, usually takes 2 days, but 24-hour services are available. Major hotels have an express service (laundering a shirt from 3DM; dry-cleaning a suit from 13DM).
Hairdressing: Major hotels have salons (cost of a shampoo and set, from 28DM; man’s haircut from 17DM; tip 10$).
Photography: All film and excellent equipment is readily available. Black and white film costs from 4.95DM and takes 24 hours to be developed; color film costs from 10.95DM takes 2-5 days.
Clubs: Rotary, Jaycees, Lions in many cities.
Babysitting: Some hotels have services.
Rest Rooms: Public facilities are available costing 20-50 pfennigs. Women, Damen; men, Herren.
Health: Many doctors and dentists speak English. Imported pharmaceutical goods and toiletries are available.
TRANSPORTATION
All towns have good bus services. Hamburg, Frankfurt, and Munich also have streetcars. There are subways (U-Bahn) in Frankfurt, Munich, Hamburg, Dusseldorf, Cologne, Bonn, and Hanover, and suburban lines (S-Bahn) in Hamburg, Munich, and Dusseldorf. Payment is by cash or ticket. Taxis are readily available on the street or by phone, and they all have meters. Luggage is charged at 50 pfennigs per bag, and there is an ádditional night fare. Pan Am’s World Rent-a-Car offices at Berlin, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Munich, and Stuttgart airports; Battonstrasse 5, Frankfurt (tel: 290066); Hamburg (tel: 326655); Seitzstrasse 9-11, Munich (tel: 223333); Giesserwall, and Herzogstrasse 6C, Stuttgart (tel: 623849). City peak traffic hours are 7-9am and 4-7pm. Long-distance trains, with sleepers and restaurant cars, are excellent, particularly the express and the deluxe Trans Europe trains. The Eurailpass is accepted in Germany.
