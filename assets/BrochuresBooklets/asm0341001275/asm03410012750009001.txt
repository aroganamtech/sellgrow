FLYING CLIPPER SHIPS
THIS great transportation system makes possible the fastest, most luxurious travel known.
Giant airliners—largest in the world—provide unequalled comfort. Effortless speed of 117 miles an hour permits you to spend each night in a well-appointed hotel, "ashore.” Traveling only in daylight hours you miss none of the scenic beauties.
Cabins larger than Pullman compartments—fitted with soft, roomy chairs, broad aisleways, wide, clear windows—mark the final word in luxury. The smooth, scarcely perceptible motion of the plane is a welcome innovation. And clearance of customs is but a matter of minutes—instead of hours—for the air traveler.
Long, unbroken journeys become short, interesting flights-—a new city, often a different country, every two or three hours. Not only its veteran crew—but 2,000 trained men along the airways—have a part in the smooth, swift flight of your airliner.
