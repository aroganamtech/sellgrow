IN 3 DAYS
DOWN the western aerial highway to South America . . . leaving the United States at Brownsville . . . far-off tempting lands within hours of your home. . . .
A five hours’ air journey to Mexico City . . . On the second day: Vera Cruz . . . vistas of interior Mexico from Gulf to Pacific . . . Guatemala, filled with historic treasures . . . Salvador by mid-afternoon . . . Next day: Western Honduras . . . great banana plantations, along the range of smoking volcanoes . . . Managua, Nicaragua between two lakes . . . changing panoramas to San Jose, exquisite Capital of Costa Rica . . . overland to Panama City . . . across the canal, spectacular flight, to Cristobal on the Atlantic for your third dinner after Brownsville. . . .
Two weeks—You can "cruise” through Central America, leisurely moving from country to country . . . home by way of Jamaica and Cuba if you choose ... six days of actual travel . . . 6,000 miles seeing 10 countries intimately . . . not just glimpses of water fronts ... 8 travel-free days and 14 nights to spend as you please ... or stop over anywhere as long as you wish. . . .
