ROLLING down to Rio” . . . just seven leisurely days aboard a veritable magic carpet . . . via this sea-level route ... feasting the senses upon kaleidoscopic scenes of a dozen different countries . . . endless, amazing vistas . . . nooks and corners of a world never before on travel maps. . . .
South from Miami and the West Indies to San Juan . . . down the old Spanish Main —-haunts of Cap’n Kidd and his swashbuckling buccaneers . . . Virgin Islands . . . St. John’s, Antigua . . . Guadeloupe . . . Martinique . . . curious Saba Island where people live in the bottom of a volcano . . . raging Mt. Pelee . . . lovely Port Castries, St. Lucia . . . Port of Spain. Trinidad, meeting place of all the world . . . the incredible Guianas, fighting back the jungle . . . bits of Old England, Holland, France . . . across the equator and the 500-mile mouth of the mighty Amazon at Para . . . panoramic coast of vast Brazil . . . moving history of civilization . . . unparalleled scenic splendor . . . fittingly climaxed by glamorous Rio de Janeiro . . . most beautiful city . . . Sugar Loaf . . . the harbor . . . magnificent public buildings, boulevards . . . unlimited diversions in this dazzling capital. . .
Hardly a day more to Santos, mighty coffee port . . . dynamic south Brazil . . . Uruguay, the "purple land,” Montevideo . . . watering place, world renowned beaches, casinos . . . then Buenos Aires . . . third greatest city of the New World . . . true "Paris of the West” sophisticated, smart, that "some day” goal of every true traveler . . . capital of great and interesting Argentina!
