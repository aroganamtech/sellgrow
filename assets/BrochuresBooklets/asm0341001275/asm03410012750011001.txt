SEVEN hours ... to travel 3,000 years! . . . —Glorious ruins of the vanished Maya, Americans before Rome was founded . . . massive temples rising from jungle depths . . . Chichen Itza, last of the great Maya Capitals . . . crumbling walls of once-great cities . . . grim temples atop massive pyramids . . . stately Temple of the Thousand Columns where magnificent religious festivals were held for the great Feathered Serpent . . . strange carvings, spectacular architecture, striking vistas. . . .
Once beyond reach of all but the hardiest traveler . . « now within seven leisurely hours of Miami by airliner . . . stopover at Havana if you wish ... on across the Yucatan Straits to comely Merida . . . escorted to the Maya Ruins . . . stay as long as you like . . . then return to Miami . . . or home again by airliner through Mexico City and Brownsville . . . also reach Merida in one and one-half days from Brownsville . . . return same way or via Cuba—Miami.
We especially recommend this trip for a two weeks’ vacation ... or more ... an unusual holiday in one of the most interesting bits of the world today ... a travel adventure of a lifetime.
