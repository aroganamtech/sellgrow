CUBA . . . Haiti . . . Santo Domingo . . . Puerto Rico . . . Alluring names! . . . visions of tropic sunlight filtering through swaying palms . . . seductive music drenched in silvery moonlight . . . tropic sea beating rhythmic lullabies on glistening coral strands. ...
A two weeks’ "cruise” in two days . . . surrounded by every comfort, alert steward at your elbow . . . above Cuba’s patterned fields, Santiago, Guantanamo . . . brief aerial passage to mystic Haiti . . . palm-covered mountains rising from the sea . . . throbbing native market places . . . flamboyant foliage . . . Citadel of Christophe, the Black Emperor . . . eerie customs . . . mystic air . .. on,to Santo Domingo, tomb of Columbus, mementos of his life in his favorite land ... a few minutes more to San Juan, Puerto Rico . . . curious blend of ancient Spanish, modern American . . . moss-grown fortresses, centuries-old streets . . . Ponce de Leon’s well and burial place . . . ancient hand-paved highways over haystack mountains . . . sports, too: horse racing, fishing, boating, golf on the edge of the sea. . . .
Panorama . . . Teeming with color, life, romance . . . filled with age-old travel treasures.
