WHERE earth-bound travelers must spend a week, aerial voyagers take only two brief days. . . .
Across Cuba . . . restful night in old Jamaica, time to wander about Kingston ... six fleeting hours to span the Caribbean in giant flying boats . . . luncheon in Colombia, on the South American continent . . . four hours more to Cristobal, at the cross-roads of the Western World.
Panama Canal, engineering wonder of the world . . . Gatun Lake . . . Culebra Cut . . . across the Isthmus by air in 20 minutes . . . both oceans in view at the same time . . . American Balboa . . . foreign Panama City . . . French, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, Indian, English shops, old-world treasures . . . ruins of Panama City, destroyed by the pirate Morgan . . . villages of San Bias Indians . . . curios of the jungle . . . fishing, jungle hunting, bathing, motoring, smart social gatherings. . . .
Two weeks’ vacation . . . allows eight days, ten nights in Panama . . . Or reserve two days on the way—for the air trip up the Magdalena River from Baranquilla to Bogota, brilliant capital of Colombia, set in the Northern Andes. . . .
