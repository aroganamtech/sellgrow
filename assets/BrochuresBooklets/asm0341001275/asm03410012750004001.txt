
Miami .... Conventional American . . . skyscrapers, traffic lights, bustle.
Magic.........Two smooth hours aboard a giant
Clipper Ship of the air . . . over verdant keys, the Indigo Gulf Stream . . . then a coastline, a medley of colors . . . panoramas. . Fascinatingly foreign . . . colorful, exciting.
Sparkling capital of modern pleasure seekers . . . splendid hotels, beautiful theatres, hospitable people . . . narrow-balconied streets reminiscent of the Conquistadores . . . quaint old-world bazaars . . . broad boulevards, shady parks redolent with vivid blossoms . . . the great Sea Wall, the Prado, Tropical Gardens ... La Playa, glistening beach, translucent waters . . . the Casino to woo the Goddess of Chance . . . magnificent Jockey Club, Oriental Park to cheer on racing thoroughbreds . . . beyond, delightful Cuban countryside . . . across the storied harbor, grim old Morro Castle. . . .
Two Hours! . . . the time you would spend on a bridge session, at a movie ... or trying to think of a place to go! One day from the North!
