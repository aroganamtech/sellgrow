Bed Time Aloft
If you are flying at night, you may want to sleep. The picture opposite shows how the seats are converted into berths.
There are three different types of overnight flying services on the Clippers. If we reserve and pay for a berth, we are taking advantage of the most comfortable and luxurious kind of flying at night. The Flying Clippers also have the Sleeperette, which is simply a reclining chair that tips back and has a foot rest which comes out from under the seat. Look at the picture at the bottom of the page.
On the Rainbow, which is the inexpensive tourist type service, we will sit up all night. The seat goes back so that we can recline. On these
Rainbow Super DC-6 planes there is not room for berths or Sleeperettes, because they carry more passengers than the deluxe Flying Clippers. Of course, you pay much less for your ticket on this service.
If you choose to travel on the Rainbow to save money, you don’t mind sleeping in the chair, because it takes only about 13 hours to reach our destination anyway.
THINGS TO DO by yourself \ with your parents9 or friends
1. Using paper plates, make clock faces showing what time it is in New York, London, and Tokyo, when it is 9:00 p.m. in your town. To help you with this look at a globe or map that shows time zones around the world or consult an encyclopedia or atlas.
2. Organize a “spotters” club with your friends and parents. Collect pictures or models of different planes to help you identify them as they fly overhead.
THINGS TO DO in school
1.	Measure the distance from New York to London on a globe or a polar projection type map (shows great circle routes). Find out how many hours it would take for the trip if your airliner went 300 m.p.h., 400 m.p.h., 500 m.p.h.
2.	Make a movie or television show illustrating the highlights of your trip from New York to London. You may want to include such scenes as boarding the plane, mealtime, visiting the crew, and bedtime. Invite another class to see your show.
