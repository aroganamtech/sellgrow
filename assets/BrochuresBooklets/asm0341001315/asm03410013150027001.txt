SUPER-6 The Super-6 operates across the Atlantic as the Rainbow Service. This is the low-cost tourist service, and is available to Lonjdon, Paris, Frankfurt and Rome.
Manufacturer of the Super-6 is the Douglas Aircraft Co. of Santa Monica, Calif. It is designated as the DC6-R. Its wing span is 117 feet 7 inches; length 105 feet 7 inches; height 28 feet 8 inches; gross weight 107,000 pounds; passenger capacity 80 (for tourist class service). Power plant consists of four Pratt and Whitney engines, each of 2,500 h.p. Top speed 360 m.p.h. Cruising speed 313 m.p.h. Operating range 4,000 miles. Normal operating altitude 22,500 feet.
“STRATO” CLIPPER The “Strato” Clipper operates on the Atlantic route to London and Frankfurt, Germany, also on the Pacific flying from San Francisco to the Philippines, New Zealand and Australia. From Miami the “Strato” Clippers fly to cities along the east coast of South America.
Manufacturer of the “Strato” Clipper is the Roeing Manufacturing Company of Seattle. It is designated as the B-377. Its wing span is 141 feet 3 inches; length 110 feet 4 inches; height 38 feet 3 inches; gross weight 148,000 pounds; passenger capacity 60 (on the President) or 51 (on the President Special). Power plant consists of four Pratt and Whitney Wasps, each of 3,500 h.p. Top speed 375 m.p.h. Cruising speed 300 to 340 m.p.h. Operating range 4,200 miles. Normal operating altitude 15,000 to 25,000 feet.
CONSTELLATION TYPE CLIPPER The Constellation Type Clipper operates on the routes to Rome, Italy, and to Johannesburg, South Africa, also on certain connecting routes in the Middle East and the Orient.
Manufacturer of the Constellation Type Clipper is the Lockheed Aircraft Corporation of Burbank, Calif. It is designated as the Lockheed Constellation. Its wing span is 123 feet; length 95 feet 3 inches; height 23 feet 8 inches; gross weight 96,000 pounds; passenger capacity 49. Power plant consists of four Wright Cyclone engines of 2,200 h.p. each. Top speed 350 m.p.h. Cruising speed 300 m.p.h. Operating range 3,500 miles. Normal operating altitude 18,000 feet.
PAN AGRA DC-6 The Panagra DC-6 is operated by the affiliated company Pan American-Grace Airways on its service down the west coast of South America to Chile and across to Argentina. The luxury service on which this airplane is operated is called El Inter-Americano. Panagra also operates a tourist service over the same route called El Pacifico, with DC6-B airplanes similar to the Super-6 described elsewhere on this page.
Manufacturer of the Panagra DC-6 is the Douglas Aircraft Co. of Santa Monica, Calif. It is designated as the DC-6. Its wing span is 117 feet 6 inches; length 101 feet 6 inches;height 28 feet 5 inches; gross weight 93,200 pounds; passenger capacity 52. Power plant consists of four Pratt and Whitney engines of 2,100 h.p. each. Top speed is 360 m.p.h. Cruising speed 310 m.p.h. Operating range 3,600 miles. Normal operating altitude 18,000 feet.
NOTE: Passenger capacity on any large airplane will vary with different interior arrangements, as for luxury service as compared with regular or tourist service. The number of passengers carried also may vary with the length of the route and the length of the flight in hours, which is determined by wind and weather. On a long flight where a large fuel load is required, it may be necessary to carry fewer passengers than the number of seats available.
