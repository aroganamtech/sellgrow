1ST DAY.
USA/HONG KONG.
Depart aboard your Pan Am Jet Clipper flight bound for Hong Kong.
2ND DAY.
HONG KONG.
You’ve crossed the International Date Line, so it’s the next evening when you arrive. You will be met by your Pan Am’s World Representative outside the Customs area and transferred to your hotel. Get a good night’s sleep for tomorrow begins your activities.
3RD DAY.
HONG KONG.
Morning at leisure. Afternoon sightseeing tour. Welcome dinner in the evening.
The morning is yours to scout for some of the wonderful bargains in Hong Kong. Hong Kong offers the best excuses in the
world for spending money. Its stalls and stores sell everything, and at bargain prices; its thousands of restaurants offer billions of cheap, delicious meals. What’s more, its natural setting is almost dis-tractingly beautiful: Islands studded with deep green forests, bays lined with golden beaches, high cool scenic peaks.
In the afternoon your tour will show you around Hong Kong Island. Up to Victoria Peak to gaze out over all Hong Kong, then on to the commercial center, the banking area, Susie Wong’s Wanchai district and Happy Valley. In the evening a special welcome dinner will be held at your hotel where you’ll have the opportunity to get acquainted with the other people who will share your exciting experience of seeing China.
4TH DAY.
HONG KONG.
Pre-China breakfast briefing. Remainder of the day at leisure.
This morning you have breakfast with your group for a pre-China briefing.
