How much does Pan American’s subsidy really cost the Government?
Of the $26,388,000 subsidy originally estimated for Pan American for the fiscal year 1955, which it now appears will be reduced, some $12,000,000 would immediately return to the Government in income taxes.
The difference between the amount which will be paid to Pan American for carrying the United States mail and what the Government would have to pay a foreign carrier for doing this amounts to another $6,625,000.
These two figures alone reduce the net cost of the subsidy as originally estimated to $7,763,000.
Other items to be taken into account in considering the true cost of the subsidy to the Government are the Post Office Department’s profit on the U.S. mail handled by Pan American, estimated at $16,100,000, and the transportation tax collected by Pan American as agent for the Government, amounting to $4,362,000.
