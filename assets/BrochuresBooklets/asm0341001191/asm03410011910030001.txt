MADAME ARTHUR - 75 bis Rue des Martyrs. ORN. 48.27. The sexes get pretty confused up here. The waiters are dressed like women and so are most of the entertainers. It’s the sort of place that makes you glad you’re normal.
CARROUSEL -	40 Rue du Colisée -
ELY. 51.85. Female impersonators are featured in a clever show, with beautiful costumes and some rough and subtle humor.
LE MONOCLE - 60 Bd. Edgar-Quinet -DAN. 41-30. A bit of Greenwich Village ; masculine-looking women dancing with feminine-looking women. Music is on the corny side, but it’s a possibility if you like that sort of thing.

TOUR D’ARGENT - 15 Quai de la Tournelle - ODE. 23-32. (Closed Mondays.) Knoivn as the restaurant with the view, the Tour d’Argent looks out on the Seine and Notre-Dame Cathedral. The specialty is pressed duck, We never cared for it much. Our preferences are the superb Tour d’Argent potage, the quenelles de brochet, and the noisettes de veau. The crêpes suzette and soufflés are among the best in Paris.
