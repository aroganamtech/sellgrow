AUBERGE DU VERT-GALANT - 42 Quai des Orfèvres. DAN. 83-68. We suggest you try the jambon en croûte sauce madère, épinards en branche, soufflé de barbue Vert-Galant, coquille St-Jacques, and tournedos sauté Alexandre. An excellent restaurant and wine cellar.

PETIT BEDON - 38 Rue de Pergolese -PAS. 23-66. (Closed Mondays). Formerly a neighborhood bistro of the elegant 16th arrondissement, this redecorated grande salle feeds its patrons well. A specialty is the pork tenderloin. Order your table in advance.
AUBERGE DU PÈRE LOUIS - 17 Rue de Ponthieu. ELY. 19-39. A small but nicely furnished restaurant whose specialty is chicken broiled over charcoal. Other tasty dishes are sole meunière and foie gras with truffles.
