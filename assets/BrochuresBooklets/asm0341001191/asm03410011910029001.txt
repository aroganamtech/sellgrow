THE BLUE NOTE - 27 Rue d’Artois -BAL. 18-92. Piano music, an occasional celebrity and jam sessions.
SCANDIA CLUB - 35 Rue Gay Lussac -ODE. 99-47 (Closed Mondays.) A smorgasbord of entertainment and food. Reasonable prices and young entertainment. Also serves supper.
FRANC PINOT - 1 Quai de Bourbon -ODE. 46-98. One of the most interesting caves in Paris, located on the Tie St-Louis. A three-piece combo plays for after-dinner dancing. You have to see it to believe it. Franc Pinot is a club, but you can use this book as a temporary pass.
A LA ROMANCE - 11 Rue Monsieur-le-Prince. ODE. 57-27. Open all night. A good place for a nightcap.
SLIGHTLY WEIRD
SUZY SOLIDOR -	4 Rue Balzac -
BAL. 21-95. One of France’s great artists plays hostess in her club where, between songs and poems, you can stare at a roomful of paintings with all the same subject— Suzy Solidor.
