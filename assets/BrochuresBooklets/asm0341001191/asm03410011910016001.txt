Many people believe that after their third or fourth ride they will overcome their fear of riding in Paris taxis. This is not necessarily true. Parisians who have been riding in cabs for years not only still ride on the floor, but most of them put the back seat over their heads.
NIGHTCLUBS
The legend that Paris is one of the most sinful cities in the world has been passed on from generation to generation. Although it has always been considered a wicked place, the soldiers of World War I brought home such a fanciful legend of Paris and French women that the city could not live up to it.
Unfortunately, as the tales grow stronger the sins grow weaker. In 1947, the city’s Municipal Council closed all the bawdy houses and drove most of the sorority members into the streets. Almost everyone, including the woman council member who proposed the bill, have admitted they made a mistake, and the authorities now realize they did not wipe out prostitution by padlocking the doors of the institutions.
