LIBERTY’S BAR (Chez Tonton) - 5 Place Blanche. TRI. 87-42. See Cabarets. Loud and noisy and a lot of fun. Champagne is insisted upon after 10,30.
AU MOUTON DE PANURGE - 17 Rue de Choiseul. RIC. 78-49. Au Mouton de Panurge (La Cuisine et l’Esprit français) is a Paris restaurant that would never get by the Boston censors. Based on the Rabelaisian theory that laughter is the right of man, Au Mouton de Panurge has carried out the writer’s spirit in its decor, menu and specialties. The specialties include a lamb dish called mouton Braguibus, la bombe crottée bergère (ice cream sprinkled with chocolate), le philtre d’amour (coffee), and le gros-dodo.
