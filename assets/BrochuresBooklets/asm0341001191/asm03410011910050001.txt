PATACHOU - 13 Rue du Mont-Cenis. MON. 30-46. (Closed Mondays.) One of the most popular places on the Butte Montmartre. Lady Patachou is mistress of ceremonies, when she is not appearing in a theater or show. Her specialty is singing risqué French salle de garde songs.
LA BROCHE D’OR - 5 Rue Bernard-Pa-lissy. LIT. 55.16. (Closed Sundays.) On the Left Bank. Most of the good things are charcoal broiled over a large spit served by cute looking waitresses. Lots of candles of that sort of thing.
COPENHAGUE -	142 Champs-Elysées.
ELY. 20-41. Good for lunch, tea or dinner. Specialties include smoerrebroed, whale steak and of course Danish pastries.
CHEZ HONORINE ET RAFFATIN -16 Bid. Saint-Germain des Prés. ODE. 22-21 (Closed Sundays.) A fantastic assortment of hors-d’œuvre, baby lamb, suckling pigs and chicken are the specialties. The desserts are also excellent. It’s always very crowded ; imperative to reserve a table.
