LASSERRE - 17 Av. Franklin-Roosevelt. ELY. 53-43. (Closed Sundays. Check during August to make sure they’re not closed.) One of the most elegant Paris restaurants. M. Lasserre recently built an upstairs room with a sliding roof, which invites outdoor dining in the summertime. The specialties are poularde à la Lasserre, casserolette de filets de sole, ris de veau Lasserre, and soufflé au Cointreau. There is soft piano music, and you must telephone in advance if you wish to dine upstairs.
PLAZA-ATHÉNÉE - 25 Av. Montaigne. BAL. 43-30. This fine hotel also has a fine dining room, and in summer time a very comfortable garden. There are many excellent dishes and wines to choose from. The soufflé de homard Plaza-Athênêe and mousse de turbot Montaigne are special treats. The Plaza is preferable for lunch but the dining room is crowded in the evening.
GEORGE-V - 31 Av. George-V. BAL. 35-30. - French gourmets will tell you the George-V Hotel food is excellent. There is a plat du jour every day and a choice of many exotic dishes. In the summer you can dine outside. The George V has a great cave as well.
