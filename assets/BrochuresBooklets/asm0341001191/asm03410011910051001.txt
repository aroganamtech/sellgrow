INEXPENSIVE PLACES
($2 to $4:00)
CHEZ MAXIME - 68 Av. des Ternes -ETO. 03-07. (Closed Tuesdays and in July.) One of the better low-priced restaurants. Coq an vin, sole meunière and langouste Neubourg are a few of the dishes.
LE GALLIERA - 6 Av. de New-York. PAS. 98-21. Located on the quai, with the emphasis in the summer on outdoor dining by the Seine. The specialties are Angevine and Chinese and include escalope de veau à la crème, brochet au beurre blanc, and poularde à l’Angevine.
CAVEAU MONTPENSIER - 15 Rue Mont-pensier. RIC. 82-96. (Closed Sundays.) A colorful restaurant just off the Palais-Royal. The wines are good.
CHEZ GEORGES - 34 Rue Mazarine. DAN. 69-49. (Closed Mondays. Check to make sure they’re open in August.) Nourisher of poets and artists. The turbot béarnaise and shashlik are good here, and so always is the plat du jour.
