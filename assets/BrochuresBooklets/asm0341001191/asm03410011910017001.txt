The police have also cleaned up most of the shows, and the only difference between a floor show in New York and one in Paris is that the girls in Paris wear nothing above the waist.
Most nightclubs start their shows around 11:30 at night. Except in the Pigalle area, there are no cover charges in Paris nightclubs. One bottle of champagne for four persons is the cover, the minimum—but not the maximum. In the first-rate clubs, champagne is currently selling for $13, plus a service charge. In order to discourage cognac and whisky sales, these drinks are priced almost as high as champagne.
