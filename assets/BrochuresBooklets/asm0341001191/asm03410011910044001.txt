CHEZ GAFNER - 14 Rue Dauphine. DAN. 49-81. (Closed Sundays.) Specializes in canard flambé aux ananas, made in front of the customer. The coquilles St.-Jacques, poulet au curry, and mousse au chocolat are also appetizing dishes.
A LA GRILLE - 50 Rue Montorgueil. CEN. 24-64. (Closed Sundays and in August.) One of the top bistros we’ve found. Escargots, oysters, rognons flambés and bécasses flambées are the specialties. If you’re undecided about wine, try the Beaujolais of the house or the Sancerre.
CHEZ JOSEPH - 56 Rue Pierre-Charron. ELY. 63-25. (Closed Sundays.) An old and famous Paris restaurant. The service is impeccable and the food is wonderfully prepared.
