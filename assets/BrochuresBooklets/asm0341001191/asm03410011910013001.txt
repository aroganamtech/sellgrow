THEATER SEATS
How and where to obtain theater seats has always been a challenge to the out-of-town visitor. Fortunately, there are many agencies in Paris which handle seats for all types of entertainment. And the charge for this service is not much more than the taxi fare to the theater.
The American Express Company has a ticket agency on the main floor of its offices on Rue Auber. Reservations can be made for every kind of show in town except the Opera. There is a 15 per cent charge. Unless a musical or play is doing exceptionally well, two or three days’ notice is sufficient to obtain seats. Nightclub reservations can be made through this agency also.
Thos. Cook & Son also has ticket agencies at its two offices, at the Place de la Madeleine and in the Rue Scribe. The fee is 20 per cent and seats are available for everything but the Opera.
There are ticket agencies at the Crillon, Ambassador, Louvre, George V, Plaza-Athenee, Ritz, Claridge, Grand Hotel, Scribe and the St. Lazare railroad terminal. The service charge varies from 12 to 15 per cent.
