INTRODUCTION
This is not a credit card book. It is a secret list of Paris restaurants and night clubs handed down from one generation to another by the gourmets and bon vivants in our illustrious family. It was Charles the Bouillabaisse in the 12th Century who first started keeping the list. The male members of the family were each given a copy on their 21 st birthday.
