Lido has a fast, well-paced, professional production which can hold its own with any in New York. Reservations should be made in advance. Dinner is served at 9:30 and the show goes on at 11.
MOULIN ROUGE - Place Blanche -MON. 00-19. The entertainment policy changes frequently. At the time of this writing the Moulin Rouge, which is famed in movies, song and story, puts on a big show with name acts. There is also dancing. It is large and noisy and may remind you of the Roseland in New York or the Palladium in Hollywood.
THE PIGALLE FLESHPOTS
The main features of the Pigalle fleshpots are large displays of bosoms. They are frankly tourist traps, but if you’ve had enough wine you may enjoy them.
Tourists looking for more degrading entertainment will have no trouble finding it. Almost every Pigalle doorman is an agent of sin.
EVE - 7 Place Pigalle. TRU. 37-96. A club which presents a bra-less burlesque show. The girls work the bar between acts. It’s better to visit this one without the wife.
