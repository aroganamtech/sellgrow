JEWISH RESTAURANTS
LE SPORTIF - 24 Rue VieMe-du-Temple. ARC. 09-44. (Closed Tuesdays.) Right in the heart of the immigrant Jewish section. Features “bouillon knedlach” gefulte fish, and honeycakes.
RESTAURANT FLAMBAUM - 37 Rue du Faubourg-Montmartre. PRO. 70-93. (Closed Mondays.) Jewish delicacies are featured at Flambaum’s. Chicken soup and matzo balls, roast chicken and chopped chicken liver are usually ordered by the show people who eat there.
RUSSIAN RESTAURANTS
DINARZADE -	16 Rue de la Tour.
TRO. 05-05. One of the most luxurious Russian wining and dining places. The prices are as high as the Eiffel Tower. Caviar, sturgeon and shashlik, and Siberian soufflé are available at a price. Singers and violinists take over after dinner. In summer you must dine in the Dinarzade garden
NOVY - 6 Rue Faustin-Hélie. TRO. 02-33. (Closed July and August.) Exotic Russian food, gypsy music and an Eastern atmosphere. Victor Novsky, formerly of the Czar’s navy, sings Russian army songs and his wife, Nadine Tcharova, gypsy ballads.
