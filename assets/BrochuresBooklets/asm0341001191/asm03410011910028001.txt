CHEZ MA COUSINE - 12 Rue Norvins -MON. 49-35. Some excellent entertainers make this Montmartre nightclub a favorite.
LE SEXY - 68 Rue Pierre-Charron -BAL. 25-18. Strip tease, strip tease, strip tease and more strip tease with an occasional straight act.
LE GRISBI - 22 Rue Quentin-Bauchart -BAL. 08-51. Small show, big bosomed girls.
BARS WITH MUSIC
GORDON HEATH’S BAR - 6 bis Rue de l’Abbaye (across from the Saint-Germain Church). ODE. 27-77. Heath is a tall man with a deep voice and a satchel full of folk songs. He sings to the breathless young American set and has acquired a loyal following among the French. There is a candlelight concert at midnight when requests are made, and candles blown out one by one after each song. A good place for a midnight “fine.”
MARS CLUB - 6 Rue Robert-Estienne. -ELY. 47-99. Someone can always be found at the piano. The entertainment is mostly American, and visiting musicians like to drop in informally.
