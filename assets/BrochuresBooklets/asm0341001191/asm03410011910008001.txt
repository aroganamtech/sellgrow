TIPPING
In restaurants, tipping is not a difficult problem. The head waiter does not have to be tipped, nor does the captain (unless he makes a special dish for you). The service in most restaurants is added to the check. It wavers between 12 per cent and 15 per cent. If the service is not on the check (always ask if le service est compris?), pay between 12 per cent and 15 per cent, anyway.
You do not, repeat, do not, have to leave something extra on the plate.
The sommelier, poor man, does not get a share of the waiter’s loot, so you should tip him extra, depending on how many times he lifts your wine bottle. The least you can get by with is 100 francs.
Nightclubs almost, always add a 15 per cent tip to the check. You do not, repeat, do not, have to leave any more. You also do not have to tip the head waiter. You’ll get a good table anyway (we think).
Usherettes are miserably paid and depend on tips to put their children through school. Twenty to fifty francs in the movies and 100 francs in the theater will take care of them.
