ANDROUET -	41 Rue d’Amsterdam.
TRI. 26-90. (Closed Sundays.) A unique restaurant, specializing in dishes made with cheese. The soupe gratinée (onion soup made with cognac or calvados), fondue (melted cheese and kirsch) and parmesan soufflé are all unusual dishes. Androuet has one of the best wine cellars in Paris. If you are in doubt, the waiters will suggest what to drink with dinner.
CHEZ DOUMEL - 8 Av. de New-York. PAS. 93-36. Specializes in bouillabaisse, fish soup, and grillades. The steaks are very impressive.
TANGAGE -	68 Rue de Ponthieu -
ELY. 93-34. (Closed Sundays and in August.) This is the foreign correspondents’ favorite hangout. Different specialties every day.
TAVERNE NICOLAS FLAMEL - 51 Rue de Montmorency. ARC. 07-11. (Closed Sundays and August.) The poularde Nicolas Flamel and filets de sole au champagne are famous, and the soufflé aux fruits maison is unbeatable.
