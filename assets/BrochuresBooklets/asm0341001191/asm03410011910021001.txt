CLUB DU VIEUX COLOMBIER - 21 Rue du Vieux-Colombier. LIT. 30-09. Hot and always ear-splitting. Another of the student cellars, with the kids raising the roof and the adults sitting in discomfort to watch them do it.
LE CAMÉLÉON - 57 Rue St-André-des-Arts. DAN. 64-40. (Closed Sundays.) Where enthusiasts of modern jazz gather to listen, to dance and to smoke cigarettes.
LE CHAT QUI PECHE - 4 Rue de la Huchette. DAN. 23-06. Man, dig this one.
TROIS MAILLETZ - 56 Rue Galande. ODE.00-79. The joint jumps after 10,30. It’s hot but the jazz is cool.
SPANISH AND SOUTH AMERICAN MUSIC
LA PUERTA DEL SOL - 52 Rue Pierre-Charron. ELY. 35-30. This one is built like a bull ring. The tables are the stands and the ring itself is used for rhumba and samba dancing. The orchestra and authentic Spanish entertainment are good.
LA CABANE CUBAINE - 42 Rue Fontaine. TRI. 16-58. A Cuban dance orchestra and South American acts. Suitable for rhumba addicts.
