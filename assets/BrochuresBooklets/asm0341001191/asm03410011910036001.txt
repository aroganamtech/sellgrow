GASTRONOMIC PALACES
(BETWEEN $5 and $9)
LA BOULE D’OR - 15 Place d’Aligre. DID. 93-70. (Closed Fridays and in August.) It is located near the Bastille on the Place d’Aligre, in one of the oldest buildings in the area. The slant of the roof, and the walls, have no visible effect on the food. It would be difficult to match the quiche Lorraine, cervelas truffé en broche, or côte de veau au paprika. Other dishes worth trying are the tournedos Rossini, the côte de bœuf marchand de vin, and the turbot grillé The wines and cheeses are first rate.
AUBERGE DE LA TRUITE - 30 Faubourg Saint-Honoré. AN J. 12-86. (Closed Sundays.) The specialties are from Normandy. M. Dorin has a mouth-watering sole Normande, a delicious caneton à la Béarnaise, and a soufflé Normand.
ALLARD - 41 Rue Saint-André-des-Arts. DAN. 48-23. (Closed Sundays, and in August.) The favorite bistro of French actors, playwrights and writers. It’s difficult to find better food in Paris.
