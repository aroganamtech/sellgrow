EL CATALAN - 16 Rue des Grands-Augustins. DAN. 46-07. (Closed August and Mondays During Summer.) A good restaurant with a Spanish accent, where you can have flamenco singing and dancing with your paella valenciana. Telephone for a table.
AU COCHON D’OR - 31 Rue du Jour. CEN. 38-31. (Closed Sundays, Mondays, and in August. Serves lunch only.) You must taste M. Saundier’s Beaujolais and try his grilled specialties.
RESTAURANT PAULINE - 5 Rue Villedo. RIC. 45-98. (Closed Sundays ¿an in August). Excellent bistro favored by politicians and people in the know.
CHEZ MAX -19 Rue Castellane. ANJ. 33-81. (Closed Sundays and in August.) Max does unbelievable things with lobster. The lobster omelette is one of the specialties. This is really a fine bistro.
