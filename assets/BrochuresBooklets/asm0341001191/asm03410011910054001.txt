RECH - 62 Av. des Ternes. ETO. 38-89. (Closed May 1 to September 1.) Large red lobsters, fresh salmon and tasty oysters are the attractions here. There are also juicy escargots. The restaurant is open until two in the morning and is suitable for supper as well as dinner.
PRUNIER - 9 Rue Duphot. OPE. 11-40. (The restaurant at Rue Duphot is closed Mondays and in July and August, but there is another Prunier at 16 Av. Victor-Hugo that is open all year.) These famous sea-food restaurants feature filets de sole pochés (with white wine, oysters, and mushrooms), marmite dieppoise (a fish soup), and lobster prepared with champagne and truffles. You can also get a good steak, cooked with sauce Bechamel and oysters.
MÉDITERRANÉE - 2 Place de l’Odeon. DAN. 46-75. An old standby of the fish-lovers, both Parisians and tourists. Specialties include gigot de mer, homard aux aromates, and bouillabaisse.
