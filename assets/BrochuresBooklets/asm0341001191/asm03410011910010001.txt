The George V Bar is a watering spot for show-business people. In summer, the favorite drink is a Pêche Champagne, made with fresh crushed peaches and champagne.
The California Bar is the favorite of newspaper men, who make it unofficial headquarters for exchanging tall stories and long gripes. Paul and Jean tend bar, and know how to make a large percentage of American drinks.
Most mixed drinks average between 50 cents and one dollar but anything with whisky in it ranges between $1.25 and $ 2.00. The difficulty in importing bourbon and scotch is the reason for the high price scale.
THEATERS
There are over forty legitimate theaters in the city, but many are closed in July and August. The productions are in French, and you must have a command of the language to appreciate them. Musicals are easy to follow, even if you don’t understand the language.
