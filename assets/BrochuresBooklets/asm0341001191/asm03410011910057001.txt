Paella is a favorite Spanish dish, made with rice, chicken, bacon, moules, langoustines and vegetables, cooked in a large frying pan. The soup, Spanish sausage and cheese are also worth trying. A paella takes 20 minutes to make, so it should, if possible, be ordered in advance.
CHEZ JOSÉ - 32 Avenue de Chatillon. LEC 89-13. (Closed Thursdays.) One of the best Franco-Spanish restaurants in Paris. The Favorite dish is paella. Another specialty is poulet sauté ibérique.
BARCELONE - 9 Rue Geoffroy-Marie. TAI. 47-66. (Closed Monday midday and during August.) One of the better Spanish restaurants. M. Félix Ferrer would have you try his calamares fritos, calamares en su tinta, con arroz pollo, and paella valenciana.
LA VENTA - 33 Rue Guenegaud. DAN.69-83. (Closed Sundays and in August.) Besides authentic Spanish dishes, this Left Bank restaurant has music and floor show to match. Closed for lunch, but popular for supper.
