FLAMBERGE - 12 Av. Rapp. SOL. 91-37. (Closed Sundays and Mondays, and August 1 to September 15.) One of the really great restaurants of Paris. It boasts only six tables and everything is cooked in front of you. The sole and lamb steak are great specialties.
CHEZ L’AMI LOUIS - 32 Rue Yert-Bois -TUR. 77-48. (Closed Tuesdays and in August.) The best frogs’ legs you’ll find in Paris. This beaten-down restaurant also serves delicious game dishes and superb wines. The best dishes include the Cha-teaubriant, truite, rognons de veau flambés and bécasse à la mode.
