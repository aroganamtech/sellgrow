RESTAURANT PRAGA - 2 Rue de l’Arc-de-Triomphe. ETO. 11.41. A Russian restaurant with French prices. Caviar, blinis, borschtand all that sort of thing.
YAR - 7 Rue Robert-Estienne. BAL. 31-21. All Russian dishes, including caviar, are available.
LE MOSCOU - 7 Rue Jean-Giraudoux. PAS. 21-25. (Closed Tuesdays and in August.) A Russian bistro where eighty per cent of the clientele are exiles from the homeland.
NITCHEVO - 64 Rue de Longchamp. PAS. 82-08. (Closed Tuesdays and in August.) Very inexpensive.
FRANCO-CZECH
CHEZ LOUIS -14 Rue Lincoln. ELY. 36-14. (Closed Saturday evenings, Sundays, and in July.) Louis Brzicky specializes in Czech and Viennese dishes. If you come on Monday or Thursday you can order his famous “chicken in the pot.” Other Louis specialties are roast goose with sweet-and-sour cabbage, beef goulash and wiener schnitzel.
