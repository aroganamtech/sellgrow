LUXURY RESTAURANTS
($6 to $12)
BERKELEY - 7 Av. Matignon. BAL. 47-79. The Berkeley is a beautiful restaurant just off the Champs-Elysées. It has booths decked with flowers and appeals to the best instincts of the famed and wealthy. The specialties are steak au poivre, rognons de veau flambés and gateau pistache maison.
LE CABARET - 4 Av. Franklin-Roosevelt. ELY. 18-52. Monsieur Rabu, the famous owner of the Cabaret, died, but the restaurant still carries on his best traditions. The specialties are filets de sole Cabaret, poularde à la française, and navarin aux pommes.
TAILLEVENT - 15 Rue Lamennais -ELY. 39-94. (Closed Sundays.) This restaurant, just off the Champs-Elysées and the Rue Washington, has an elegant atmosphere and an amazing list of great wines. The dishes we’ve tried and liked are the quenelles de brochet, steak Beaulieu and médaillon de ris de veau.
CHEZ PIERRE - Place Gaillon. OPE. 87-04. (Closed Sundays.) Traditionally a fine menu. Pierre boasts a steady clientele who know food and wines. Omelette Richmonde, poissonnêe Nantaise, filets de sole maison and steak au poivre are the best.
