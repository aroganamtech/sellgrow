FOUQUET’S - 99 Av. des Champs-Elysées and 48 Av. George-V. BAL. 59-54. Among the famous names of Paris restaurants. The clientele is made up of people from show business. We recommend the canard sauvage (wild duck), bécasse flambée (flaming woodcock), pilaff de volaille and timbale de langouste au porto.
PAVILLON DE L’ÉLYSÉE - Carré Marigny, Champs-Elysées. ANJ. 29-60. (Closed Sundays.) French politicians and men of letters and the arts gather here to dine in the beautiful garden or in the hallowed dining room. It still is considered one of the first-class restaurants of Paris. Cassoulet de homard is a specialty.
ROTISSERIE PÉRIGOURDINE - 2 Place Saint-Michel. DAN. 70-54. (Closed Fridays) They call it the “Temple of Gourmets.” The specialties are brioche de foie gras frais, omelette aux truffes, pigeon aux petits pois primeurs and rognons flambés Saint-Michel.
LUCAS CARTON - 9 Place de la Madeleine ANJ. 22-90. One of the most important restaurants in the city. A fashionable and expensive house which maintains the highest cooking standards.
