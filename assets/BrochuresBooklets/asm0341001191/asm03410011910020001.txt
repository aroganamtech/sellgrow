L’INDIFFERENT - 26 Rue Fontaine -TRI. 27-93. Some violent entertainment is in store. The nudes are limited in number but make up for it in quality.
LES NATURISTES - 1 Place Pigalle -TRU. 13-26. Next to Eve’s and with the same type of program. Les Naturistes shows nature in the raw. The girls can’t dance, but who cares? Champagne is brought without request to the table, but you can see as much as you want from the bar.
LE PIGALL’S - 77 Rue Pigalle TRI. 53-38
Same thing as above.
FOLIES PIGALLE - Place Pigalle -TRU. 25-56. The last time we were there a girl was doing a striptease on a horse.
JUST JAZZ
LE CLUB SAINT-GERMAIN-DES-PRÉS - 13 Rue Saint-Benoît. LIT. 81-84. One of the famous Left Bank caves that at one time was supposed to have been haunted by existentialists. There may not be any existentialists left, but you’ll see some fancy jitterbugging by French students. And you’ll either be driven out into the street, or right on to the floor by the loud jazz music.
