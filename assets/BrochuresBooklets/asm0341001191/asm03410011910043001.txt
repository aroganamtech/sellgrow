RESTAURANT DES ARTISTES - 11 Rue Lepic. MON. 15-82. (Closed Mondays and in August.) This one is run by a professor from the Cordon Bleu who also gives instructions and recipes to his clients on request. Professor Pierre Mangelette can whip up the lightest turbot soufflé you’ve ever lasted.
CLOSERIE DES LILAS - 171 Bld du Montparnasse. DAN. 70-50. Specialties here are the loup à la goulette, tripes en chemise, carré d’agneau.
RESTAURANT QUASIMODO - 42 Quai d’Orléans. ODE. 63-60. (Closed Mondays.) Located in a picturesque setting behind Notre-Dame. A bonne cuisine and excellent wines. We recommend coq au Chambertin, quenelles de brochet Nantua, poulet à la crème, bécasse flambée à l’armagnac, canard à l’orange and crêpes Suzette.
LE BOSSU - 27 Quai de Bourbon -ODE. 53-30. (Closed Saturdays and in August.) Notable for its delicious soufflés. The omelettes and coquilles St-Jacques are also a specialty. An off-the-beat restaurant worth trying for lunch or dinner.
