Coq au Riesling, filet de bœuf en chemise, rognonnade de veau à VAlsacienne, and gigot d’agneau Gueb-willer are some of the specialties, and two delicious desserts are the sorbet framboise and the pavé Alsacien, a cake with a solid chocolate filling.
PARIS EST (Gare de l’Est) - 4 Rue de Strasbourg. NOR. 81-63. (Closed Sundays.) Some of the finest food in Europe is served in railroad stations, and the Gare de l’Est is among the best known. Specialties include langouste soufflée, rable de lièvre, and the terrine.
CHEZ BENOIT - 20 Rue St-Martin -ARC. 25-76. (Closed Sundays and in August.) An established restaurant for 35 years. The specialties are Lyonnais and we have a hunch you’ll like M. Benoit’s escargots made with white-wine sauce, the gratin de crevettes, and the steak au poivre. There’s sawdust on the floor and lots of good wine stacked in the cellar.
LA BOURGOGNE - 6 Avenue Bosquet. SEG. 97-39. (Closed Sundays.) This is truly a great family restaurant, known and respected by the French gourmets. Everything is good but the plat du jour is usually an experience. Also great wines. Reserve or else !
