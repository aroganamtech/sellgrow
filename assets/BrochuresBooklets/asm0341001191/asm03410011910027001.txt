LAPIN AGILE - 4 Rue des Saules -MON. 85-87. (Closed Mondays.) Once the exclusive fraternity house of sous-Zess poets, artists, and writers, this leaning, rickety building serves brandied cherries and the best of French folk music. Frede, the former owner, is dead, but his son Paul carries on the Lapin Agile tradition. The room is filled with paintings by many well-known artists. The benches are hard and the room is dark, and if you’re lucky you may catch some of the romance of Montmartre’s golden era of nonsense.
CRAZY HORSE SALOON -12 Av. George-V. RAL. 69-69. The emphasis here is on strip-tease. Some of the best practitioners in town put on a show that lasts from 11,30 to 2:00, in a Wild West setting as the French imagine it. Best intimate show in Paris.
MILORD L’ARSOUILLE - 5 Rue Beaujolais. CEN. 88-14. A typical French nightclub. Knowledge of the language helps. The entertainment is clever and fast.
LA VILLA D’ESTE - 4 Rue Arsene-Hous-saye. ELY. 78-44. (Closed July and August. Open daily for dinner; teadancing Saturdays and Sundays. Features top-notch French singers such as Juliette Greco and Georges Ulmer, and a Cuban band.
