LE PORT DU SALUT - 163 bis Rue St-Jacques. ODE. 32-03. A lively, clever show. The fun starts at 6:00 p.m. and goes on until 3:00 in the morning.
L’ÉTABLE - 7 Carrefour de l’Odéon -ODE. 55-01. One of the newer developments in the St-Germain-des-Prês area : a stable ivhere you can eat well and hear the younger singers. It’s a good idea to phone for a table.
LA BOULE BLANCHE - 33 Rue Vavin. DAN. 78-98. The Left Bank’s answer to Pigalle, La Boule Blanche features, in a more intimate atmosphere, nudes who are just as luscious, and dance just as badly, as any on the Right Bank. There is no nonsense here about dark suits or black ties. You don’t need to know any French for this one.
L’ÉCLUSE - 15 Quai des Grands-Augustins. (Closed Mondays.) A small, but very small, smoky French club with guitar playing and very fast, witty French acts. For long-haired women and long-haired men.
