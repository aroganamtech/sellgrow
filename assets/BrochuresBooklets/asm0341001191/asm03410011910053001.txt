CAVIATERIA - 32 bis Av. George V-BAL. 62-49. Caviar and vodka are featured but not to the exclusion of other fare. Piano music and friendly company.
CALAVADOS - 40 Av. Pierre-ler-de-Serbie - ELY. 27-28. A good restaurant in the high-rent district which serves luncheon, dinner and supper and stays open all night. At supper you can get scrambled eggs, onion soup and thick, juicy steaks.
BRASSERIE LIPP - 151 Bid. St-Germain. LIT. 53-91. (Closed Mondays and in August.) A well-known Left Bank hangout with excellent beer and a choice of tasty dishes, including a famed choucroute garnie.
SEA-FOOD RESTAURANTS
AUX MERVEILLES DES MERS - 128 Bld de Clichy. LAB. 47.08. (Closed May 15 to September 1. ) Sea-food enthusiasts ought to like this one. Bouillabaisse is a specialty of the house, as is the langouste du chef. For people who like their trout fresh, the waitress will catch a fish from a pond in the middle of the room andbroil it.
