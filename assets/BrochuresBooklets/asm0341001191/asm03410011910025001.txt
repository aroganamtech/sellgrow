EL DJAZAIR - 27 Rue de la Huchette -ODE. 96-97. The home of the belly dancer, North African atmosphere, a navel battle every evening. You can have cous-cous on the side.
LE JOCKEY - 127 Bid. Montparnasse -DAN. 48-93. Helzapoppin atmosphere. Everyone is supposed to get in the act. It used to be the gay place of the twenties.
DINNER DANCING
MAXIM’S - 3 Rue Royale. - ANJ. 27-94. Quiet dancing, good food, fantastic prices, and the most beautiful ambiance in Paris. Friday night is dress night, and the world’s most beautiful women can be seen in desperate competition.
DINARZADE - 16 Rue de la Tour -TRI. 05-05. One of the most luxurious Russian wining and dining places. Besides dance music, Dinarzade also has a small show with Russian and French singers.
PAVILLON DE L’ÉLYSÉE - Carré Marigny, Champs-Elysées. ANJ. 85-10. Traditionally one of the fine restaurant-cabarets. An orchestra plays for dancing, and four or five acts supply the entertainment.
