CHEZ DOUCET - 4 Rue Marbeuf. ELY. 85-92. (Closed Saturdays and in August) Another restaurant in the Champs-Elysées area which serves excellent food at reasonable prices. The cuisine is plain and the service is fast.
MILLE COLONNES - 20 Rue de la Gaieté - DAN. 68-69. This is a good place to know about when you’re really counting your sous.
AFTER THEATER
ELYSÉES-MATIGNON - 2 Av. Matignon BAL. 73-13. (Closed in August.) The club downstairs, reserved for the theatrical world, is always overflowing with celebrities. If you can’t get in there you can eat upstairs.
RELAIS-PLAZA -	23 Av. Montaigne.
