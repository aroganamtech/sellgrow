LE RiGHI -	11 Rue La Tremoille.
BAL. 46-51. (Closed Saturdays and July.) ] e Righi is owned and run by three brothers. Their specialties are bouillabaisse, soupe de poissons, scampi, sole meunière, fritto misto, pasta asciutto, and tagliatelle bolognèse. The three brothers make their own pasta, and we think it’s among the tastiest in all Paris.
LA STRESA - 7 Rue Chambiges. ELY. 49-82. (Closed Tuesdays and Sundays during August.) This is a good Italian restaurant. The ravioli and canelloni and, of course, the spaghetti, ore house standbys.
FLORENCE -	23 Rue de Ponthieu.
ELY. 66-66. (Closed during August.) Many people consider the Florence the top Italian restaurant in town. There is no doubt it should get the highest rating.
