LA TRUITE VAGABONDE - 17 Rue des Batignolles. EUR. 77-80. (Closed Mondays and in August.) We recommend, naturally, the trout, which you can choose from a large fish bowl before sitting down. All the fish dishes here are tops. A very good medium-priced restaurant.
RELAIS BISSON - 37 Quai des Grands-Augustins. DAN. 71-80. (Closed Sundays.) Considered one of the great restaurants of Paris. Very expensive but well worth it. The loup is exceptional. Reserve in advance.
FRANCO-ITALIAN RESTAURANTS
SAN FRANCISCO - 1 Rue Mirabeau. MIR. 75-44. (Closed Mondays and in July and August.) It would be difficult to find a better Italian restaurant in Paris. Their pizza, ravioli, and cote de volaille, plus the spaghetti, are bywords among Paris gourmets. The Italian and French wines are the best, but prices are high.
