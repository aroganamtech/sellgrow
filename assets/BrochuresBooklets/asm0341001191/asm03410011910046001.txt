CHOPE DANTON - 4 Carrefour de l’Odèon. DAN. 67-76. (Closed Mondays and in August.) Another Left Bank restaurant popular with students and the American colony in Paris. The specialties are côte de veau à la crème, coquille St-Jacques maison, and jambon à la crème.
RESTAURANT LUCIEN - 12 Rue Surcouf. INV. 46-93. (Closed Thursdays, August.) An eight-table bistro serving food at reasonable prices. The owner was formerly maître d’hôtel at Jean Casanave. Specialties include pepper steak, terrine de volaille, rognons de veau à l’Armagnac, and the soufflé of the house.
