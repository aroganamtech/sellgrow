CHEZ CHATAIGNIER - 75 Rue du Cherche-Midi. LIT. 82-74. (Closed Sundays and Mondays, and July 1-August 15.) The house of Châtaignier has many delicious dishes. Among them are homard à l’Armoricaine, canard à l’orange, brochet beurre blanc Nantais, and the soufflé maison. Reserve in advance.
FABIEN - 28 Rue Duret. PAS. 74-94. (Closed Tuesdays and in August.) Compares with the finest Paris restaurants. Among the delicacies are the blinis with smoked salmon, toast à la moelle (marrow on toast), omelette Basquaise, and the petite marmite. The duck with peaches is also worth your attention.
COCHON D’OR - 192 Av. Jean-Jaurès. NOR. 23-13, BOT. 39-81. Located across the street from the Paris slaughterhouses, the Cochon d’Or seems to have the best steaks in town. As a matter of fact, we don’t think you’ll find better grilled meat in any restaurant in France. Cochon d’Or has a fixed-price menu which includes a terrine maison, grillade Cochon d’Or, pommes soufflées, fraise melba or tarte, and choice of a half bottle of Muscadet or Beaujolais.
