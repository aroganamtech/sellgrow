CARROLL’S —	36 Rue de Ponthieu.
ELY. 46-60. Chi-chi spot in the Champs-Elysées area. Gets a big play from the café society crowd, who come late and stay until early in the morning. Carroll’s features one or two excellent singers and a small but reliable orchestra.
JIMMY’S CLUE - 4 Rue Huyghens DAN. 27-92. (Closed Sundays and Mondays.) One of the smartest boîtes in Paris. Jimmy’s, at one time a watering place for the beautiful and the damned, still gets the best people in town. The room is small, the rhumba orchestra among the best, and the atmosphere reminiscent of El Morocco.
MACUMRA - 12 Rue	Sainte-Anne
RIC. 97-86. Strictly for late-nighters who like the sound of a Brazilian orchestra. Where the smart people go when they’re not smart any more.
