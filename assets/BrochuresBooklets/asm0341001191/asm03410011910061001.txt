LES HALLES
After the ball is over and after the boites are closed, Paris tradition demands that you visit Les Halles, the great central market, for onion soup. No one knows who started the custom or how long it has been in existence, but generation after generation of revelers has descended on the market at three or four in the morning to finish off the evening.
The first public market was constructed in 1183 by good King Philippe-Auguste and consisted of wooden sheds and a long wall that was named by the populace « Les Halles». In 1852, Napoleon III ordered the building of the present structure and there has been little change in the Halles since.
