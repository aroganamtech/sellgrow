LA GUITARE - 14 Rue Hautefeuille. DAN. 41-11. A bar with Seville atmosphere, featuring—naturally—flamenco music and dancing.
LA CANNE A SUCRE - 4 Rue Ste-Beuve. LIT. 37-17. (Closed Sundays.) West Indian music and entertainment, specializing in Calypso.
THE GYPSY CABARETS
MONSEIGNEUR - 94 Rue d’Amsterdam» TRI. 25-35. (Closed Sundays.) If you have the kind of girl who is melted by luxury, flowers, and music slathered in old-world atmosphere, take her to Monseigneur. The main entertainment is a thirty-piece string orchestra that fills the tiny jewel box with Russian and gypsy melodies. Friday night is dress night. This is one of the most popular clubs in town, for residents and tourists alike.
LES GRANDS-SEIGNEURS -	6 Rue
Daunou. OPE. 62-91. Also known as Ciro’s, this plush Russian palace is bountifully filled with silver goblets, wine-colored walls, velvet drapes, and impressive-looking champagne buckets. Gypsy violinists play at your table between dances and entertainment. Your date will love it.
