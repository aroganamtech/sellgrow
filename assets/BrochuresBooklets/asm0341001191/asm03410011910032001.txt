LAPÉROUSE - 51 Quai des Grands-Augustins. DAN. 68-04. (Better check to make sure they’re not closed Sundays during the summer.) We’ve gotten into many arguments on the merits of Lapérouse. Some friends claim it is the best restaurant in Paris, others that the cuisine and service are just fair. The many times we’ve eaten there we’ve found the food uneven. Sometimes it has been wonderful, other times it has been indifferent. The specialties worth trying are foie gras, médaillon de veau Orloff, canard Nantais de Colette and soufflé Lapérouse. There are private dining rooms in case you wish to be alone.
LE GRAND VÉFOTJR - 17 Rue de Beaujolais. RIC. 58-97. (Closed Sundays.) No restaurant list of Paris would be complete without this one. Many Frenchmen claim it is the best restaurant in France. Don’t miss the croûte Landaise or the lamproie Bordelaise (river eel), or the poularde Grand Véfour.
