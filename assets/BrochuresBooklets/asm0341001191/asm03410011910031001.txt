MAXIM’S - 3 Rue Royale. ANJ. 27-94. A visit to Paris is certainly not complete without a visit to Maxim’s. The restaurant has changed little in its fifty-odd years, and the romance and glamour of the “Merry Widow” operetta still prevail. Albert, the discerning headwaiter still stands at the door and decides who sits where. Friday night is dress night, and no one gets in without a black tie or evening clothes. If you call for a reservation, sound important.
RITZ HOTEL - 15 Place Vendome -OPE. 28-30. The Ritz Hotel dining room is famous for lunch. Luncheon at the Ritz is an event, and something to save up for. The headwaiter, is in charge of seating arrangements, and a call to him is necessary if you expect to get a table. The Ritz has an excellent prix-fixe luncheon. It also has one of the great wine cellars of France. You can expect to trip over several ex-kings, counts and dukes while trying to find your table.
L’ESCARGOT - 38 Rue Montorgueil -CEN. 83-51. (Closed Mondays and in August.) For 150 years this restaurant, located in Les Halles quarter, has been serving gourmets and wine lovers of the world.
