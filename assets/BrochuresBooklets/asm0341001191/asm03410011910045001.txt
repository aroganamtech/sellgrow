AU CLOCHER DU VILLAGE - 8 bis Rue Verderet, Auteuil. AUT. 35-87. (Closed Tuesdays and in August.) Located at the Eglise d’Auteuil, this is one of the cleanest bistros we’ve visited. It also serves some wonderful dishes. The specialties are bouchée à la reine, saucisson chaud en croûte, quenelles de brochet sauce Chablis, and coq au vin. Whatever you eat, be sure to save some room for the mille-feuilles.
CHEZ ALBERT - 122 Av. du Maine -SUF. 47-62. (Closed Mondays and in August.) One of those small bistros run by a husband-and-wife team. Albert, who has worked at the Ritz, the Crillon, and other Paris hotels, does all the work m the kitchen and then comes out to see if the patrons are satisfied with his creations. Among the specialties are escargots, filets de sole, quenelles au Chablis, and coq au vin.
