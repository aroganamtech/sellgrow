PHARAMOND - 24 Rue de la Grande-Truanderie. GUT. 06-72. (Closed on Sundays in July. Check whether they’re closed in August.) Among the Normandy specialties you should try here are the tripes à la mode de Caen. Pharamond is also proud of its steak Chateaubriand and its pommes soufflées.
CAMILLE RENAULT - 60, Rue de la République, - Puteaux (near Neuilly). LON. 01-30. (Closed Monday-August). This one is owned by a chef known as Big Boy. When he’s not in the kitchen making delicious things he is off buying art which he hangs in the restaurant. A family style restaurant, but what a family.
AUBERGE ANNE DE BEAUJEU - 8 Av. Franklin-Roosevelt. ELY. 91-20, 92-54. The best Beaujolais in Paris can be found here. M. Trompier, the director, is secretary of the Compagnons of Beaujolais and refuses to serve any other type of wine. The specialties are also from Beaujolais. Our preference is the entrecôte à la moelle comme en Beaujolais.
