LE PETIT COLOMBIER - 42 Rue des Acacias. ETO. 28-54. (Closed in August.) A comfortable, reasonably priced restaurant serving an excellent continental cuisine. The dishes include filet de sole, langouste thermidor, pilaff de volaille au curry, and the escalope des gourmets. The proprietor is a chevalier in the Tastevin (Brotherhood of Wine Tasters) and serves a very good Tavel Rosé and Vosne Romanée.
NUIT SAINT-JEAN - 29 Rue Surcouf -INV. 61-49. (Closed Sundays and in August). Our vote for the best charcoal-broiled steaks in town. For 800 francs the patron gets a thick, juicy tournedos with a delicious béarnaise sauce and tasty pommes dauphine. Other specialties are the croque-monsieur and terrine de canard truffé.
MADAME ALBERT - 124 Rue de Grenelle. INV. 77-23. (Closed Sundays.) A small restaurant near the Invalides that serves a splendid poularde sauce suprême, raviolis maison, and a fine roast duck.
