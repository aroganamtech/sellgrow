Durand, at 4 Place de la Madeleine, sells some concert tickets without service charge.
At no agency should one pay more than 20 per cent above the price of the ticket.
Less expensive seats at the Opera are available only to those who are willing to stand in line at the box office.
Many theaters in Paris close during the months of July and August, so it is advisable to look at a Paris paper to see which houses are open.
TAXIS
The Paris taxi is one of the oldest instruments of destruction in France. Some taxis, believed to have been built before the invention of gunpowder, can still be seen on Paris streets, and despite revolutions, wars and gas rationing they have managed to contribute as much bewilderment and unhappiness as any transportation in the land.
The tourist, on first entering the walls of the city, is amazed to see so many taxis on the streets.
He is led into thinking that, because there are so many cabs in Paris (11,000), his transportation problems will be easily solved. This delusion is inevitably shattered within the first 24 hours.
