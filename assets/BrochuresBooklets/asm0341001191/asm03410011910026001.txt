PRÉ CATELAN - Bois de Boulogne. AUT. 05-60. This is Paris’s favorite outdoor dining and dancing restaurant. During July and August the Pré Catelan is one of the pleasantest places to spend an evening. The Café de Paris orchestra plays there during the summer months.
PAVILLON D ’ARMEN ON VILLE - Bois de Boulogne. SAB. 26.00. Another picturesque restaurant and outdoor nightclub, with Chinese lanterns lighting up a large and well-kept dance floor. The orchestra is usually first rate.
RIGHT BANK SHOWS
L’AMIRAL -	4 Rue Arsène-Houssaye.
BAL. 56-66. Off the Champs-Elysées, a step from the Arc de Triomphe. L’Amiral usually has an intimate revue, and you need a fair amount of French to follow the zany proceedings.
LE LIBERTY’S (Chez Tonton) - 5 Place Blanche. TRI. 87-42. (Closed Mondays.) The nearest thing to an entertainment marathon in Paris. The show is a madhouse of costumed waiters, impersonators, singers, musicians and comedians. Loud and noisy and a lot of fun. Champagne is insisted on after 10:30.
