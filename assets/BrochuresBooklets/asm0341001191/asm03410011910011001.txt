THE OPÉRA - Place de l’Opéra. OPE. 15-59 - Seats can be booked a week in advance. The Paris Opéra is one of the most famous in the world. With few exceptions, the productions are presented in French. Its famed ballet troupe performs on Wednesday evenings.
THE OPÉRA-COMIQUE - 5 Rue Favart. RIC. 72-00. Gives the less spectacular operas and operettas, well staged and exciting to see. Seats should be booked three or four days in advance.
COMÉDIE-FRANCAISE (Salle Richelieu) -Palais-Royal. RIC. 22-70. The French national theater’s famed repertoires are given here and feature the great classic plays. The Salle Luxembourg, at Place de l’Odèon (DAN. 58-13,1, often presents modern works.
PALAIS DE CHAILLOT - Place du Troca-déro. PAS.81-15. Once the meeting hall of the United Nations, the theater of the Palais de Chaillot is at present used for concerts and theatrical productions.
SALLE PLEYEL - 252 Rue du Faubourg-Saint-Honoré. CAR. 88-73. The city’s best-known concert hall. The Salle Pleyel features world-famous artists and symphony orchestras.
