Who Are You?
Statistics given in this report are drawn from the replies of the 30,928 people who responded.
Men in the group outnumber women about 9 to 1. But this is a fortunate thing, ladies, because the answers to a number of the survey questions, subjected to feminine evaluation, could well mean a shopping spree for a couturier dress in fashionable, fabulous Paris.
It’s a solid section of the citizenry too — 48% of the respondents are from 30 to 50 years of age — with a slightly lower percentage over 50. Eighty-seven percent are married, and 65% of these are the parents pf children. Occupations cover a broad range from manufacturing and professions to dairy farmers and horticulturists — with a healthy segment of over 9000 who are company presidents, officers, or managers. As the expression goes, it is a Good Group.
