On the matter of how, the answers were gratifying in one case, almost embarrassing in another.
For instance, — we said “What means of travel do you propose to use in going to your dream destination?” You can’t argue with an IBM Tabulator; when the answers were toted up, 48% said they would go by air, 33% said they would use a combination of boat and air! America is becoming air-minded!
All of this was no surprise to us, of course. In 1934 the airlines, for the first time, exceeded surface transport in number of overseas passengers carried. Today, the ratio is in excess of seven to three, air versus surface.
It was when we asked ‘‘What transportation company will you use?” that we began turning a pleasant shade of pink. As a matter of courtesy, we will not mention names of our contemporaries — but besides being designated No. 1 carrier, Pan American was the choice over the next most popular airline by a ratio of almost 8 to 1, over the most-mentioned steamship company by a ratio of 13 to 1.
