AIR TRAVEL OPPORTUNITIES —AND THE FUTURE
What Are the Reasons for Plane Popularity?
What do people like best about air travel for international trips? You probably have your own opinions — see how they compare with others. Respondents were asked to check three. Percentages given represent the votes of those replying, and show that overseas travel by air is favored on many counts.
MORE TIME TO SPEND AT DESTINATION — 95%
This is the great dividend of air travel over long distances. While traveling itself is fun, especially on luxurious Clippers, the real reason for going anyplace — especially a strange land — is the enjoyment of the place itself. While slower travelers chew up precious days en route, the air traveler does more, sees more, “on location” — often with no greater living expense, and with no longer time away from home.
MORE CHOICE OF DEPARTURE TIME —51%
This point was discussed on an earlier page. It is an often-overlooked important advantage of air travel.
