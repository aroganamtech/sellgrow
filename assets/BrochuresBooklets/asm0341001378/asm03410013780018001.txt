Entirely aside from the benefit to the individual, the greatest advantage to a company is that it does away with the summertime chaos when workers and key people crowd a complete organization vacation schedule into the few short weeks of July and August. Changing from tradition is of course a management matter — but the ends gained make it well worth considering. Sometimes splitting vacations between the two seasons is the easiest way of introducing the idea — this move alone will do much to relieve the business of the concentrated summer illness known as persona absentia.
International Air Travel as a Business Incentive
A toaster, a washing machine, a 24-inch TV — all these have their appeal as an incentive to sales or work performance. But in our selfish view nothing quite compares with a Caribbean beach and a Calypso band, or a fast run-in on an outer wave toward the beach at Waikiki, for spurring a man on.
But what did our opinion pollsters have to say on the matter? Only 13% of those replying were with companies using incentive programs — but over 58% considered international air travel a stimulating incentive.
Possibly 13% is a good showing for the usage of travel in an incentive program. Clearly, though, more companies should be brought to realize the universal appeal of travel to colorful places —- to men, to women, and to the all-important wives. Not only the immediate appeal, but the long-term benefits.
