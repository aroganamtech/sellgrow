IT’S A NEW AMERICAN CUSTOM
You can see, from the Family Fare Table opposite, why more and more family groups are happily tramping the halls of the Louvre, making friends with the Little Mermaid in Copenhagen, singing songs with the gondoliers of Venice, these days. And as later pages show, there are many far places that easily can be covered within the time limits of midyear school vacations. In a later question, 68% of the married respondents revealed that they have in mind taking their family to their dream destination. What a wonderful thing Pan American’s Family Fare is for those with Europe on their mind!
There’s another economy factor you should weigh. Even though Europe is at its busiest and gayest during the winter months, with everything at its natural best, the fact remains that hotel rates and living costs are reduced down to Thrift Season level. Hotel reservations are easier to get, too. Everything’s better — and at a bargain!
IT’S ALWAYS SUMMER SOMEWHERE
Don’t forget another thing — we’re talking about the world, and weather-wise there’s the significant matter of the Equator. There’s a very special thrill in settling into your lush Clipper seat, knowing — but not quite believing — that within a matter of a few short hours you will not only have made a wonderful trade in scenery, but will be transposed into summer too!
