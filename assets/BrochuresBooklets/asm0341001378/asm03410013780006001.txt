How Long for Vacation?
One of the easiest means of self-deception is to hold to a belief just because it’s a thing you grew up with, or because a new set of facts just hasn’t come your way. To many people, the two-week vacation is an established thing, the accepted rule. But we wondered — haven’t things changed in the course of time — hasn’t the enlightened liberalization that introduced the "two-week vacation now been broadened to extend the period — and if so, how general is this trend?
So we asked — how long do you have for vacation? And this is the result:
2	weeks..............27%
3	weeks..............33%
4	weeks..............22%
Longer...............12%
NEW FREEDOM FOR FUN
As you’ll agree, this is interesting indeed. Two-week vacations are far from the rule. Four-week vacations are nearly as common, and three or four week periods combined add up to a smacking 55%.
We submit that it is proper to ask to what important use people will put this new-found and welcome time. And we may be forgiven for pointing out that the development of international air travel by Pan American has now reached a stage where an individual, or a family, can gain a second freedom — the freedom to forego too-familiar Camp Kilcare, or the trip in the family car, and see instead the streets and sights of old and lovely London, the happy face of Paris, or the scenes of Ali Baba.
