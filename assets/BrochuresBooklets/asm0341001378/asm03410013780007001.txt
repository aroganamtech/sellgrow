When Do You Take Vacation?
What’s the rule here? Is the Good Old Summertime the only time that’s good for vacation? Have people in any numbers learned that there’s no need to hibernate like bears — that a vacation in winter can do wonderful things for the soul and the spirit, and for the pocketbook as well?
The popularity poll on this question brought out some surprising things:
Of those responding
29% take vacation all in the summer
11% take vacation all in the winter
53% split vacation between summer and winter
At another point in the survey, we asked poll members when they would prefer to take their international dream trip. In votes cast, other seasons outweighed summer more than two to one!
WINTER AWAKENING
We think this is an eye-opener. People —- 64% — have awakened to the practical and pleasurable advantages of vacation-in-winter. And it’s a most sensible trend.
Let’s go back to the comment about the pocket-book. Practically everyone is working for you — to induce you to taste the extra sweetness of vacation at other times than summer. Take Pan American. From October 31st to April 1st you’re in a Have-Your-Cake-And-Eat-It-Too position. Between these dates, you can go to Europe, as a family, under the money-saving Family Fare Plan — using either First Class President Service or thrifty tourist Rainbow Service.
