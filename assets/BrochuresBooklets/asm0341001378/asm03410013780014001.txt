TRAVEL BONUS —
THE EXTRA CITY PRIVILEGE
In answering the question about where they wanted most to go, it was natural that, for many, the choice was difficult — how can one choose from such a selection of sugar-plums? But this does bring us to tell you about the wonderfully attractive Extra City privilege which Pan American offers —• a plan made possible by its great network of routes.
The Extra City privilege is a tremendous bonus. It means that in addition to going to your chosen destination, you can stop over in different cities and different countries, going and returning (even on One-Way trips) — without a penny of extra fare!
To demonstrate: Suppose you’re going to Rome (Round-trip fare $648.40). Your Extra City bonus would allow you to include: Glasgow • Copenhagen • Hamburg • Cologne/Dusseldorf • Frankfurt • Stuttgart • Zurich • Milan • Geneva • Nice • Paris • London • Dublin • Shannon. Or, by choosing another optional routing you could include: Shannon • Dublin • London • Amsterdam • Brussels • Paris • Zurich • Milan • Nice • Barcelona • Madrid • Lisbon • Azores • Bermuda.
Chances are, the Extra City privilege is new to you. In answering a later question, it was brought out that only 16% of the respondents had ever taken advantage of this feature — but 70% now plan to! Other combinations of Extra City routings equally exciting as the one given above, are shown in a special Extra City folder which you may ask for on the enclosed Request Card.
