WE TOOK A POLL FOR FACTS AND OPINIONS
Vou are a special friend of Pan American World Airways. At least, Pan American looks at it that way.
Last spring, you may remember, we held a Conference-by-Mail among a selected and widespread group of people. We wanted to explore your travel thinking — based on your past experience, and on your dreams. At the same time, we wanted to know whether knowledge about travel had caught up with the opportunities for dream-fulfillment that exist today.
This booklet is a report of the earlier survey. Since signatures were not called for, we have no way of knowing whether you were among those who replied. If you did, this report is a means of saying Thank You.
