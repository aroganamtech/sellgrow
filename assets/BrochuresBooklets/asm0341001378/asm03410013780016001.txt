We would never recommend dallying in the matter of travel reservations. All methods of transportation have peak periods — and it’s good to know that you’re all set well in advance, in any case. But there is one great advantage to world travel by air. You have far greater flexibility in planning—-instead of grappling with the problem of limited departures and accommodations. In the past year, for example, Pan American has made 6,821 transatlantic crossings. As many as 30 Clipper flights a day are in the air between the U. S. and Europe. Again though, don’t overlook the growing popularity of air travel —- make your reservations early!
THE BUSINESS ASPECT OF TRAVEL
Business and Pleasure CAN Mix
A great many men have discovered a very pleasant way to mix pleasure with business by substituting Clipper travel for slower methods.
It’s a secret that should be shared — and we did it by asking this question: “When you use air travel for international business trips, do you do so to (1) accomplish the trip quickly and return or (2) do you use the time gained to relax a little at destination?”
The vote on this question was about hfty-fifty. Now there’s nothing wrong with the first reason for using air travel. It’s the most wonderful device the world has ever known for speeding up business, for conserving valuable time, for extending business coverage in an era of expanding markets.
But there’s much to be said for the alternate answer to the question. The pressure of business being what it is, the adoption of air travel for international business trips gives the business traveler the chance to ease up a little, to rest and relax — away
