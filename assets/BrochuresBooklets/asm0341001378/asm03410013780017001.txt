The Important Matter off Company Vacation Policy
The vacation habits of individuals are, of course, in large part controlled by the vacation policies of the firms by which they are employed. The chances are good that you know more and more employed people who are taking winter vacations — and if your company has not yet come to see the winter vacation light, it might be helpful to know what the general trend is on the matter.
So in our original survey we asked whether companies employing our respondents encouraged winter vacations, or discouraged them. We also asked for individual opinions on the matter of company policy regarding winter vacations — did they personally favor the idea or not?
21% said their companies encouraged winter vacations
8% said that winter vacations were discouraged
52% said their companies had no policy on the matter
63% of the individuals approved the idea
