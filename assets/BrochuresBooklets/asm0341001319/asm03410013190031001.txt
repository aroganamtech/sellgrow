
Throughout its history, Pan Am has acted to help the average person travel the world. In our quest for lower fares we have also helped to develop the technologies which made the lower fares possible and have thus helped to create hundreds of thousands of jobs in this country and abroad. Pan Am has also made a significant contribution to the nation’s balance of payments.
We have built an important company and kept it flying even in trying times. We look to the past with pride—and look to the future more determined than ever to keep Pan Am in the forefront of world aviation.
WILLIAM T. SLAW ELL Chairman
Pan American World Airways, Inc.
