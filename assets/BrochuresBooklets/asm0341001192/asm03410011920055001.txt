Ballater, Aberdeenshire pop. 1,050 ... Delightful village on the River Dee in the “heart of the Highlands,” west of Aberdeen. Convenient to Balmoral Castle (gardens are open unless the Queen is in residence) and to Braemar. Loirston (B; closed winters), Tullich Lodge (B).
Balloch, Dumbartonshire (SW of Aberfoyle) pop. 1,800 ... On the “bonnie banks” of Loch Lomond. Lomond Castle (B) in nearby Arden.
Banchory, Kincardineshire (SW of Aberdeen) pop. 1,900 ... West of Aberdeen on the River Dee. Shooting, fishing, riding, tennis, golf. Lovely gardens and fine example of Jacobean architecture at nearby Crathes Castle. Tor-na-Coille (M), Raemoir (M).
Beattock, Dumfriesshire (SE of Glasgow) Regal, modernized mansion with trout-stocked lake, shooting preserve, riding, boating; golf course nearby. Auchen Castle (M).
Braemar, Aberdeenshire pop. 1,000 . .. Famous village on Royal Deeside where the Royal Family usually attends the Highland Gathering in September. Near Balmoral Castle, lnvercauld Arms (M; closed winters), Fife Anns (B).
Callander, Perthshire (NE of Aberfoyle) pop. 1,730 ... In a romantically scenic area described by Scott in The Lady of the Lake. Near the Trossachs, lochs Katrine, Lubnaig, Achray and Vennachar, and the Bracklinn and Leny falls. The hotel is a converted 17th-c. hunting lodge furnished with authentic antiques.
