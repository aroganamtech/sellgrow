American whiskies and cocktails are available in places frequented by Americans but it’s less expensive to drink gin, Scotch or Irish whisky, rum from the Commonwealth islands, or beer, in its infinite varieties, which is usually served at room temperature. Tap water is safe to drink all over the British Isles.
Holidays: Good Friday and Easter Monday Bank Holidays; Spring Bank Holiday on May 31 in 1971 (May 3 in Scotland); Summer Bank Holiday on August 30 in 1971 (August 2 in Scotland); Christmas Day; Boxing Day (first weekday after Christmas). New Year’s Day in Scotland only; March 17 and July 12 in Northern Ireland only.
Hotels: Good service and friendly atmosphere make British hotels a sheer delight, no matter how much or little they cost. Categories in this guide are as follows: De luxe hotels (D) are $20-$26 single, $29-$39 double, plus 15% for service. (Other hotels add 10%-15% optionally. When you check in, ask if rates include service charges.) Luxury hotels (L) are $12-$20 single, $18-$30 double. Moderate hotels (M) are $8-$ 15 single, $12-$ 18 double. Budget hotels (B) are $5-$ 10 single, $9-$ 12 double. All these rates are for rooms with private baths. Most British hotels, however, also have rooms without baths, which are considerably cheaper. Such bedrooms have lavatories with hot water but the tub and toilet are in a room down the hall. These public bathrooms are kept very clean.
