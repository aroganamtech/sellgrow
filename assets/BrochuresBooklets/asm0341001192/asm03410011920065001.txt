VI. More About Britain
Here are some good British books and magazines that are packed with detailed information.
London Man and London Woman—by Sir Francis Chichester and Sheila Chichester. Superbly detailed city maps in compact, easy-to-use form, plus lots of useful information.
This Month in London—official guide to entertainment, events and sightseeing . . .Hotels and Restaurants in Britain—official guide for prices, amenities; fine maps . . . In Britain—monthly calendar of coming events throughout all of Britain, articles on selected towns and regions. All published by British Tourist Authority.
What’s On—magazine. Weekly listing of London entertainment and events; at all newsstands.
Egon Ronay-BMC—Ronay’s annually revised paperback guide to hotels, inns, restaurants and pubs.
Good Food Guide—Raymond Postgate’s annually revised paperback gourmet guide.
Signpost—W. G. McMinnies. Delightful photographs and descriptions of hundreds of charming, usually rural, hotels and inns in Britain, Ireland and the islands; hard-cover, revised annually.
Shell Guides—sponsored by Shell Oil Co. Especially valuable for motorists.
Take Note—monthly magazine published by the Scottish Tourist Board. Detailed calendar of events in Scotland and well-illustrated articles on selected areas.
New Horizons World Guide—Now in its 18th annual edition, this highly regarded compendium of travel facts is indispensable. Sold by Pan Am.
