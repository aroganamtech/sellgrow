Chester, Cheshire (B-4) pop. 59,800 ... A medieval town on the River Dee, gloriously cluttered with timbered houses, ancient arcades (known as the “Rows” and used as shops), and remains of the old city walls. The soaring cathedral is notable for wood-carved scenes from medieval Bestiaries. Grosvenor (L), Blossoms (M), Queen (B).
Chichester, Sussex (C-5) pop. 20,280 . . . The Cathedral of the Holy Trinity is a remarkably fine combination of Norman and Early English workmanship. See also the Bishop’s Palace, St. Mary’s Hospital (13th century), the many fine Georgian homes, and the new Chichester Festival Theater. At Fishbourne, nearby, are magnificent 1st century Roman mosaics and other relics of the 100-room palace of King Cogidubnus. East of town is Arundel Castle, with a fine collection of 18th century furniture. Chichester Motel (M), Ship (M), Dolphin and Anchor (B).
Cirencester, Gloucestershire (C-5) pop. 12,670 .. . Foss Way, laid out by the Romans, still passes through this attractive Cotswold town. Valuable Roman relics are displayed in the Corinium Museum. The richly ornamented parish church in Market Place has an unusually fine fan-vaulted roof; and Coxwell Street’s stone houses are a photographer’s delight. King’s Head (M), Stratton House (B).
