Prestwick, Ayrshire pop. 12,560. . .International airport, great golf courses, sandy beaches and water sports; near to Robert Burns country. Prestwick is 30 miles southwest of Glasgow. Towans (L).
St. Andrews, Fife pop. 10,360 ... The Royal and Ancient Golf Club was established in 1754, and the Old Course has been delighting and infuriating golfers ever since. The University of St. Andrews, founded in 1410, has some handsome medieval buildings and an outstanding library of rare books and manuscripts. Explore the 13th-c. castle, complete with dungeon and spooky passages, and the gaunt ruins of the cathedral. St. Andrews is on the shore of the North Sea; sandy beach, fishing, sailing. South Queensferry, West Lothian (near Edinburgh) pop. 3,230 .. . Overlooks the impressive bridges spanning the Firth of Forth. Convenient not only to Edinburgh but to vast Linlithgow Palace and Elopetoun House, designed by Robert Adam. Forth Bridge Motel (M).
Stirling, Stirlingshire pop. 27,700... Massive castle was the “Key to the Highlands,” home of many Scottish kings from early in the 12th c. until England and Scotland were united in 1603. From the ramparts you can look out to seven battlefields, including Bannockburn where Robert the Bruce routed English pikemen and archers in 1314. Both Mary Queen of Scots (1543) and King James VI (1567) were crowned, while infants, in the Church of the Holy Rude.
