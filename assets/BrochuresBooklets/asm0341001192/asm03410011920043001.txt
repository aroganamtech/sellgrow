Manchester, Lancashire (B-3) pop. 644,450. .. Important industrial city. The cathedral and Chet-ham’s Hospital date from the 15th century, but most of the city’s buildings are Victorian or very modern. Piccadilly (L), Grand (M), Midland (M).
Newcastle-Upon-Tyne, Northumberland (C-2) pop. 260,750 . .. There is a 12th century castle keep, and a good museum of Roman relics in the Black Gate. Stephenson’s locomotive (1826) is in the Museum of Science and Industry. Go out and see Hadrian’s Wall, begun in about 121 a.d. and extending from the Tyne Estuary westward to the Solway Firth. Gosforth Park (L), Swallow (L), Royal Station (M), Royal Turk’s Head (M). At Gateshead-on-Tyne: Five Bridges (M).
Newquay, Cornwall (A-6) pop. 11,530 ... Old-time smugglers have been supplanted by surfers, and the sheltered sandy beaches of Newquay are now bright with bikinis. Spectacular cliffs, tennis, golf, fishing, boating and plenty of night life. Most hotels are open summer only. Atlantic (M), Bristol (M), Edgcumbe (M), Glendorgal (M), Headland (M), Kirbirnie (M), St. Rumons (M), Savoia (M), Tre-barwitli (M), Beachcroft (B).
Norwich, Norfolk (D-4) pop. 120,100 ... See 12th century Norman cathedral, the many medieval churches, quaint gabled houses, Maddermarket Theater (reopened after Oliver Cromwell’s demise in 1658), the Norman castle (with art gallery and museum). Maid’s Head (L), Royal (L), Castle (M), Lansdowne (M).
