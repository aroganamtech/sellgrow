IV. Touring Wales
Abergavenny, Monmouthshire (N of Cardiff, B-5) pop. 9,770 ... Resort town on the fish-filled River Usk. Tombs and effigies from the llth-c. castle are in the Priory Church. Excursions to Llan-fihangel Court; to Raglan Castle, home of the first steam engine (1663); to lovely Tintern Abbey; to Chepstow Castle. Angel (B).
Aberystwyth, Cardiganshire (B-4) pop. 9,920 .. . On Cardigan Bay, backed by low hills. A narrow-gauge railway runs to wildly scenic Devil’s Bridge with its dramatic gorge and waterfall. Aberystwyth is a university town, and also the home of the National Library of Wales. Belle Vue Royal (M).
Barmouth, Merioneth (B-4) pop. 2,270 . . . Seaside resort south of valiant old Harlech Castle. Inland are quaint Dolgelley, where nightly curfew is still rung, and rugged Cader Idris mountain, Min-Y-Mor (B).
Caernarvon, Caernarvonshire (B-4) pop. 9,170 . . . Dominated by the magnificent old castle (built about 1283-1322), where the first Prince of Wales was born and where Prince Charles was invested in 1969. Behind the town are the Snowdonia Mountains, and in front of it is the Menai Strait, which is spanned by a bridge, 9 miles north at Bangor, to Anglesey Island, ancient Druid stronghold. Prince of Wales (B), Royal (B).
