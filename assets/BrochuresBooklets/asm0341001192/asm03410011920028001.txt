Stores: The incomparable Harrods, Selfridges, Simpson’s, Fortnum and Mason’s, Dickins and Jones, John Lewis, Harvey Nichols, and Robinson and Cleaver’s are among London’s top department stores; Liberty’s for fashions, fabrics and furnishings; Foyle’s for books. Marvelous tailors along Savile Row. Wonderful shops along Regent and Bond Streets. British designers include Digby Morton, Hartnell, Hardy Amies, Worth and Charles Creed. Teen-agers go mad for Mod styles in shops on Carnaby Street, and on King’s Road in Chelsea. Marks and Spencer’s, in London and countrywide, is low on glamour but very high on quality clothing at rock bottom prices.
