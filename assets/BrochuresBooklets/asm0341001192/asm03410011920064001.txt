Stranraer, Wigtownshire pop. 9,365 ... At the head of Loch Ryan, and only 39 miles by car ferry from Larne in Northern Ireland. Near Stranraer is the great Lochinch mansion, its beautifully landscaped grounds including the Black and White lochs and the ruins of Castle Kennedy. George (L), North West Castle (L), Lochnaw Castle (L; 5 miles out).
Torridon, Ross and Cromarty (NW of Nairn) . . . Modernized hunting lodge in the remote wilds of northwestern Scotland. Loch fishing, shooting. Loch Torridon (M).
Troon, Ayrshire pop. 9,955... Just north of Prestwick Airport, this popular Firth of Clyde resort provides superb golf courses, fine beaches, boat excursions to Isle of Arran and the Kyles of Bute. Marine (L), Sun Court (M).
Trossachs, Perthshire (N of Aberfoyle) pop. 250 . . . A beautiful wooded region of lochs, mountains and glens made familiar by Scott’s The Lady of the Lake and Rob Roy. Trossachs (M; closed winters).
Turnberry, Ayrshire pop. 500 . . . Ailsa and Arran golf courses, tennis, swimming, and an aura of elegance in this smart resort. Nearby are Culzean and Killochan castles. Turnberry (L).
Ullapool, Ross and Cromarty (NW of Nairn) pop. 700 ... A very trim and tidy harbor where the herring fleet winters from October to March. Surrounded by spectacular scenery for hiking, fishing, shooting. Royal (M).
