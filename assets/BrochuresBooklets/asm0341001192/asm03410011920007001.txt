I. Useful Facts
The United Kingdom consists of the island of Great Britain (England, Scotland and Wales), six counties in Northern Ireland, and a host of snug little islands half-forgotten in the surrounding seas. All put together, they cover a land area of 94,215 square miles, which is smaller than the state of Oregon. Yet you couldn’t see all of Britain’s landmarks in a lifetime. From Hadrian’s Wall, begun in a.d. 122, to Jodrell Bank, the satellite tracking station, this small land is packed with places made familiar by history, art, literature and science.
