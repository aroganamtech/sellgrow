Arbroath, Angus pop. 19,680 . .. North Sea coastal town immortalized by Scott in The Antiquary. Dramatic red sandstone caves, pocked with cliffs, were a smugglers’ refuge. The abbey in High Street was founded by William the Lion, who was buried there in 1214; annual historical pageant. Celtic sculptured stones in the llth-c. church of St. Vigeans, and Drosten stone covered with Pictish inscriptions. Sea-fort h (M).
Auchterarder, Perthshire (N of Gleneagles) pop. 2,560 .. . Famous Gleneagles Golf Courses (King’s, Queen’s and Wee Course). Also tennis, lawn bowling, indoor pool, evening entertainment. Gleneagles (D; closed winters).
Aviemore, Inverness-shire pop. 900 . . . Exciting new sports center on the Spey with the Cairngorm Mountains for great skiing from December to June. Ski school, equipment for rent, shuttle-service buses to slopes. Ice skating and curling. In summer, pony-trekking through pine forests, sailing, canoeing, fishing, heated pool. Highland Wild Life Exhibition. Attractive shops and plenty of night life. A completely modern, year round fun place to stay while exploring the Highlands. Coylumbridge (M), High Range (M), Stratspey (M), Aviemore Chalets (B).
Ayr, Ayrshire pop. 45,300 . . .“Capital” of the Robert Burns country. The poet was born at Alloway, south of town via Tam O’Shanter’s route; museum next to Burns family cottage; famous Brig O’Doon. Fine beaches, golf and proximity to Prestwick Airport make Ayr a popular summer resort. Station (M).
