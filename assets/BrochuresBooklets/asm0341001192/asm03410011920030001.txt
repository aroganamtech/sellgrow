The Underground or Tube (subway) is remarkably quiet, comfortable and easy to use. Fares are the same as for buses (above). Local guides will tell you which “tube station” to aim for to reach your destination, and you can be anywhere in London within about 15 minutes.
Among the best of the many conducted bus tours around London are those operated by American Express, Thos. Cook & Son, Evan Evans, Frames’ and Universal Sightseeing. If you prefer a private conducted tour of your own design, a car and driver-guide can be hired through Take-A-Guide and London Couriers. If you want to join a conducted walking tour, with lots of intimate details on sights along the way, get in touch with Off-Beat Tours of London or Love London. Or do it on your own with Pan Am’s Tours on Tape covering both city sightseeing and the English countryside.
Thames launches are another way of viewing the city. They operate in summer all the way from Hampton Court Palace near Kingston, Surrey, up through the city to Greenwich, where the world’s longitude lines and standard time zones begin, although Britain is now an hour ahead of Greenwich Time, as in Western Europe.
