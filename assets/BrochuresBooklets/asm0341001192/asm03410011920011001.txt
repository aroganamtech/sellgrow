For extensive travel around the country, Hotels and Restaurants in Britain (75p, $1.80), put out by the British Tourist Authority, is a complete guide.
Information Centers: British Tourist Authority, 680 Fifth Avenue, New York, N.Y. 10019; other offices in Chicago, Los Angeles, Dallas, Toronto and Vancouver. In London, the BTA is at 64 St. James’s Street, S.W.l. Pan Arn’s London office is at 193/4 Piccadilly, W.l (Tel. The Scottish Tourist Information Center, 2 Rutland Place, West End, Edinburgh 1. Pan Am is at Prestwick Airport, and at 130 St. Vincent Street, Glasgow, C.l. (Tel. City 5744). Wales Tourist Board, 7 Park Place, Cardiff. Northern Ireland Tourist Board, 6 Royal Avenue, Belfast 1.
Language: English with regional variations.
Leaving Britain: No departure taxes. Pan Am passengers leaving the country from London can save time by checking out at Semley Place Air Terminal, off Buckingham Palace Road. Special Pan Am buses go directly to the airport for only 45p ($1.08). Miscellaneous: Wherever you are, your hotel will call a physician, should you need one, whom you will pay as at home. But even tourists, if necessary, can participate in the almost-free services of the National Health Scheme.
