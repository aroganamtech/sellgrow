Derby, Derbyshire (C-4) pop. 129,200 . .. Home of the Rolls-Royce, Royal Crown Derby Pottery (fine museum) and Derby cheese green-streaked with sage. Four miles from town is Kedleston Hall, Robert Adam’s greatest masterpiece from the majestic rotunda right down to the fire-irons, which he also designed. Pennine (M), Midland (M).
Dorking, Surrey (E of Guildford, C-5) pop. 23,200 ... A nice place to park your car if you don’t feel up to London traffic. Nearby are Polesden Lacey (great art collection), Albury Park House (fine art and furniture) and Leith Hill (Wedgwood china collection and luxuriant gardens). White Horse (B), Burford Bridge (M; at Boxhill).
Dover, Kent (D-5) pop. 36,000 . . . Smart Hovercraft now speed passengers and automobiles from Dover to Boulogne in only 35 minutes. The great Norman castle, on one of the White Cliffs of Dover, is built around a Roman lighthouse and a Saxon Church. Visit the museum in Maison Dieu Hall. White Cliffs (M).
Folkestone, Kent (D-5) pop. 43,100 ... A major port for over 1,000 years, and also a major resort with sports and entertainment. The surrounding scenery is dramatic with towering cliffs overlooking the English Channel. Burlington (M), Grand (M), Royal Oak Motel (B, in Newingreen, 6 miles inland).
Grantham, Lincolnshire (C-4) pop. 25,760 . .. The Church of St. Wulfram is considered one of the finest medieval churches in England, and there is a fascinating chained library in a small priest’s room.
