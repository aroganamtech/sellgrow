Shrewsbury, Shropshire (B-4) pop. 51,130... Shrewsbury Castle is 900 years old, and the whole town is enchanting with quaint old timbered houses, charming Georgian and Regency mansions, fine old bridges and churches. See Rowley’s Mansion, now a museum of Roman relics from nearby Viroconium. Drive over to medieval Ludlow and dine at the famous Feathers or AngC; see 13th century Stokesay Castle, with its vivid gardens; and the 11th century Much Wenlock Priory. Lion (B), Lord Hill (B), Prince Rupert (B).
Southampton, Hampshire (C-5) pop. 208,710 .. . Southampton is the departure and arrival point not only for ships circling the globe, but also for the new Hovercraft that go to the Isle of Wight. Skyway (M), Dolphin (B), Polygon (B), Royal (B).
Stoke-on-Trent, Staffordshire (B-4) pop. 263,910 ... Here are the Potteries, a federation of towns immortalized by Arnold Bennett in his Five Towns stories. From their kilns come the prized chinaware of Wedgwood, Spode, Royal Doulton, Minton and Copeland. North Stafford (M).
