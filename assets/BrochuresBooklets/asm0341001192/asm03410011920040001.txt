Sir Isaac Newton attended King’s School, which is still functioning. The Angel and Royal is reputedly where Richard III signed the Duke of Buckingham’s death warrant in 1483, and the George was enthusiastically recommended in Dickens’ Nicholas Nickleby. Nearby are Bel voir Castle (magnificent furnishings and paintings) and Belton House (rose gardens, Grinling Gibbons wood carvings). Angel and Royal (B), George (B).
Grasmere, Westmorland (NW of Ambleside, B3) pop. 1,000 . .. Lies on the lake amid lovely scenery. Wordsworth and his sister lived in Dove cottage and so, later, did Thomas de Quincey. The Dove is now a museum, and Wordsworth and Coleridge are buried in the St. Oswald churchyard. Grasmere Red Lion (M), Swan (M), Gold Rill (B).
Guildford, Surrey (C-5) pop. 54,100... A delightful place to stay, with good bus and train service into London. Stately homes nearby: Albury Park, Clandon Park, Loseley House, Polesden Lacey. Angel (M), White Horse (M).
Harrogate, Yorkshire (C-3) pop. 59,400 . . . The 88 mineral springs here make Harrogate the major health resort in northern England. The Hallé Music Festival is a popular July event. Sports include fishing and riding, tennis and golf, hiking in moors and dales. Excursions to Harewood House, Ripley Castle, Rud-ding Park, Newby Hall and moated Markenfield Hall. Cairn (M), Crown (M), Granby (M), Majestic (M), Old Swan (M), Prospect (M).
