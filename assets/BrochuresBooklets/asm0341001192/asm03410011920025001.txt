Night Life: Check with What’s On In London because new places are constantly popping up. Nightclubs really are clubs in Britain, but many are licensed to grant foreign visitors temporary memberships, valid for one year, for an average price of $2.52 each. Devoted revelers can save time and money by joining the Clubman’s Club (170a Finchley Road, London, N.W.3), which issues a sort of identification card for £1 ($2.40) that ensures automatic membership in a long list of London nightclubs, gambling clubs, drinking and dining clubs.
Nightclubs, long established and all in the West End, include the Astor, Churchill’s, Danny La Rue’s, Edmundo Ros, Embassy, Eve, Gargoyle, Murray’s Cabaret.
Major hotels, with orchestras for dancing, are the Dorchester, Grosvenor House, Hilton, May Fair, Royal Garden and Savoy (also has floor shows).
Restaurants with dancing and floorshows, all in the West End, are the Carousel, Celebrity, Latin Quarter, L’Hirondelle, Showboat and Hippodrome Theater Restaurant.
Gambling Clubs have taken London by storm. The Charlie Chester Casino is open 24 hours a day, with stakes from 12!^p (300) on up. More posh and expensive are the famous Crockford’s and the Victoria Sporting Club, both with excellent restaurants, and the Twenty-One Club, which also has dancing and a cabaret show.
