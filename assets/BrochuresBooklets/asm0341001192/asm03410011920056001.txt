Dalkeith, Midlothian (SE of Edinburgh) pop. 9,000 . . . Convenient to Edinburgh. Drive past Borth-wick Castle, refuge of Mary Queen of Scots and Bothwell in 1567. Motel Derry (B).
Dornoch, Sutherland (SW of John o’Groats) pop. 975 . .. Fine climate, sandy beaches, great fishing and excellent golf in this ancient town where Scotland’s last witch was executed in 1722. Elotels are open summers only. Royal Golf (M), Dornoch (M).
Dumfries, Dumfriesshire pop. 13,350 . . . Handsome border town on the River Nith where Robert the Bruce violently eliminated his rival for the Scottish throne in 1306. The house where Robert Burns died in 1796 is now a museum. Visit Caerlaverock Castle (8 miles south), besieged in 1300 and beautifully restored in early Renaissance style. Cairndale (M), Station (M).
Dundee, Angus (W of Carnoustie) pop. 185,230 . . . Seaport and industrial city fronting on the north bank of the Firth of Tay. Interesting old City churches are three parish churches under one roof. Impressive bridges span the Firth. Glamis Castle (12 miles north), where Macbeth supposedly murdered King Duncan, was girlhood home of Elizabeth, the Queen Mother. Angus (M).
Dunkeld, Perthshire pop. 1,000 . . . About midway between Perth and Pitlochry on the River Tay. 15th-c. cathedral evolved from Celtic church founded about a.d. 700. Enchanting old houses around the marketplace date from 17th c. Trout and salmon fishing. Cardney House (M), Dunkeld House (M).
