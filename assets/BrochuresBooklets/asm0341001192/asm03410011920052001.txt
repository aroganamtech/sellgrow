Bodnant Gardens (8 miles) are among the finest in the country. Also visit regal Penrhyn Castle near Bangor. Imperial (L), Craigside Hydro (M), Gogarth Abbey (M), Si. George’s (M).
Llangollen, Denbighshire (B-4) pop. 3,000 . . . Host of the International Music Eisteddfod held annually in the second week of July. Trout and salmon fishing in River Dee, spanned by 14th-c. bridge. Plas Newydd House, where celebrities were entertained by the eccentric “Ladies of Llangollen” (1778-1831); Chirk Castle (14th c.); Dinas Bran Castle (13th c.); Valle Crucis Abbey (founded 1200) and Eliseg’s Pillar (9th c.). Hand (M), Royal (M); Ruthin Castle (M; 15 miles out in Vale of Clwyd).
Swansea, Glamorganshire (B-5) pop. 170,400 . . . Industrial city on Gower Peninsula. Famous British Empire panels in Brangwyn Hall. Attractive seaside resort in suburban Mumbles. In Swansea: Dolphin (M), Dragon (M). At Mumbles: Osborne (L).
Tenby, Pembrokeshire (A-5) pop. 4,500 . . . An ancient town with modern resort amenities for golf, tennis, sailing, swimming in Carmarthen Bay. Good base for exploring the castle-strewn Pembroke coast. Hotels are dramatically situated along the cliff top, with steps down to sand beaches. Belgrave (L), Imperial (L).
