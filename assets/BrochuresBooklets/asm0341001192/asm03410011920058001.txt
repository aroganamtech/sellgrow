Elie, Fife (opposite North Berwick) pop. 930 ... Good swimming and fine golf in this quaint resort town on the Firth of Forth. Marine (M).
Falkirk, Sterlingshire (SE of Sterling) pop. 38,000 ... Important industrial center midway between Glasgow and Edinburgh. Metropolitan (M).
Fort William, Inverness-shire pop. 2,775 . . . The road through the Great Glen from Fort William to Inverness passes through some of the loveliest scenery in Europe. Southeast of Fort William is Ben Nevis (4,406 feet), highest mountain in Britain. Milton
(M)	, West End (M).
Gairloch, Ross and Cromarty (NW of Nairn) pop. 760 . . . Looking out toward the Hebrides, this west coast resort is reached by passing through wild Highland scenery of mountains and lochs, snug croft cottages. Just north of town are the Gardens of Inverewe where the moist Gulf Stream-warmed air supports an astonishing display of subtropical trees and plants. The hotel (golf, tennis, swimming, boating, sea and loch fishing) faces impressive sunsets. Gairloch (M).
