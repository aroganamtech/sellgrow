II. London
London is “a collection of villages” that have grown together over the last 2,000 years, retaining their old names and being further identified by postal districts. Hotels listed here by area are among the most comfortable and convenient but there are many others. See price codes, page 8. Advance reservations are advisable but to find a room on the spot, telephone 629-5414; or 935-2555; or 734-3059. Figures in parentheses are postal zones.
