Ladybank, Fife pop. 1,250 . .. Golf, trout fishing, sophisticated accommodations in modernized country mansion. Fernie Castle (L).
Largs, Ayrshire (W of Glasgow) pop. 8,900 . . . All kinds of summer holiday resort facilities, including yachting and steamer excursions on the Firth of Clyde. Marine end Curlinghall (M).
Lochearnhead, Perthshire (N of Aberfoyle) pop. 650 . . . Lively sports center on Loch Earn, with water skiing, sailing, squash, tennis, swimming, golf (7 miles away). Lochearnhead (L).
Melrose, Roxburghshire pop. 2,140 .. . Romantic setting for so many of Sir Walter Scott’s tales. Three miles away is Abbotsford, where Scott lived from 1812 until his death in 1832. The medieval style house is superbly furnished with relics of the Border warfare days that inspired so many of Scott’s books. Melrose Abbey, founded in 1136, is in pho-togenically lovely ruins. The abbey church contains the heart of Robert the Bruce and tombs of famous Scots; magnificent carvings and windows. West of town is Darnick Tower with memories of Border raids. George and Abbotsford (B), Waverley Castle (B).
Nairn, Nairnshire pop. 5,000 . . . Booming resort town known for its mild dry climate, fine fishing and swimming, excellent golf courses. Newton (L), Golf View (L; closed winters).
