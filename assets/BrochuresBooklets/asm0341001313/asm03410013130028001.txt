ales of the teeming cities of the East and the fascinating countries that surround them have fired the imagination of the western world since the days of Marco Polo. And you’ll soon see why as you discover the many strange and wondrous sights that await you in the Orient.
There’s mysterious, age-old India, land of mosques and exotic bazaars . . . glittering pomp and weird ceremony . . . fakirs and Maharajahs. Siam, fantastic with its bizarre temples and statues and ornate-costumed dancing girls; and Pakistan . . . land of rivers. On no account miss Singapore at Malaya’s tip and Hong Kong on China’s Coast . . . British Crown Colonies to be sure, but none-the-less rife with Oriental adventure and expression. And you can count on a friendly welcome to the tropical Philippines . . . with their coral beaches and grass houses.
No visit to the Orient is complete without an excursion to Japan, with its contrast of bustling Tokyo and silent Shinto shrines . . . its ageless lake reflection of serene Fujiyama . . . the awesome testimony of Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Japan is indeed a land where culture, custom, art and architecture still shine through the rubble of war.
