You’ll never forget your life aloft. It will be as much a part of your vacation thrill as any sight you’ll see. Within the comforting quiet of your sound-proofed, air-conditioned Clipper cabin, you’ll lounge to your heart’s content in a cloud-soft reclining chair. You’ll get long-remembered attention from cordial cabin attendants, ever at hand. They are ready to bring you, on a moment’s notice, your favorite magazine or refreshment, a pillow or blanket. You’ll enjoy delicious, full-course dinners served hot from a gleaming galley ... at no extra charge of course. On some flights, there’s even a gift of an orchid for each lady, as well as champagne for all.
As you relax you’ll revel in the magic of a far-off world slipping swiftly by below . . . and relax you will for the Clippers fly high above worrisome weather, and the air is silky smooth.
If you’re travelling aboard a Double-Decked “Strato” Clipper you can enjoy conversation and cocktails with your fellow passengers in an intimate lower lounge.
At bedtime a flick of the finger tilts your seat ’way back for rest. On many flights you’ll be provided as a matter of course with the easy-chair comfort of Sleeperette service. Exclusive with Pan American, Sleeperettes convert to dreamy stretchout beds at night. Full-length berths are also available on most flights — there’s a slight extra charge.
