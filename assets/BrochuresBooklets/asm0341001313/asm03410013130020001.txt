Mexico
Guatemala
here’s much of old Spain in Mexico, yet its charm and color is strictly a native product.
It’s a land of Aztec ruins and gay fiestas . . . tamales and tequila . . . mountains, deserts and balmy beaches. You’ll know sun and fun at every turn in Mexico — from luxurious Acapulco to memorable Yucatan. Make Mexico City for sure — her nightclubs offer up-to-date diversion . . . her hotels range from swank sophistication to quiet informality . . . her bullfights are the best.
Guatemala is a tiny tourist paradise with a delightfully different atmosphere. It is relatively untouched by the outside world . . . its pleasures are simple — but irresistible. You’ll spend most of your time just looking and your eyes will be enchanted. Lake Atitlan, ringed by volcanoes and bright-colored Indian villages, is a sight to treasure always . . . and Chiehieastenango on market days is utterly fascinating.
True to old Spanish tradition, Mexicans love the thrill of watching the daring movements of a toreador.
