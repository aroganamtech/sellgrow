ome say that Brazil’s capital city of Rio de Janeiro is the most exciting vacation playground in the world. It’s without a doubt a top contender for the title . . . but then, so is all of South America. Rollicking Rio is the Paris of Latin America. Its Carnival, its Copacabana Beach, its beauty and climate attract fun-worshippers from every land and you’ll find it as unpredictable as tomorrow’s news. Of course if you’d rather get away from it all you can do that too, for the fishing in the Chilean-Argentine Lake Region is excellent.
When you’re not eating steak you’ll be doing the Tango or going to the races in busy Buenos Aires. A modern, jubilant, sophisticated city, “B.A.” is monumental proof of Argentine progress and if you’re a true cosmopolite you won’t dare pass it by. If you’ve got time and pesos to spare you’ll go to the play-town of Mar Del Plata too, for a whirl at the Casino (the largest in the world) and a frolic on Bristol or St. James Beach.
A group of Buenos Aires folk dancers demonstrate that Argentina is a land of true Spanish music and gaiety.

Cattle ranching is a top industry in Uruguay . . . and “gauchos”— not cowboys — round up the herds.
The beautiful Lake Regions of South America are a fisherman’s delight—for trout and salmon are specialties.
