even thousand years of civilization and conquest stand recorded in the Middle Eastern countries. Visit Turkey, Iraq, Syria, Lebanon, Jordan and Egypt, and you’ll find vivid vestiges of centuries-old cultures. The crumbling walls of Babylon, the Roman aqueduct, the fabulous palaces of Sultans, the fortresses of the Crusades ... all give silent testimony to the wealth of ancient, long-decayed empires. And you’ll be sure to tour Israel, the land that marks the foundation of two great universal religions.
Two of the seven wonders of the world — the Sphinx and Pyramids — stand on the sands of Cizeh, Egypt, and still arouse speculation as to how they were constructed.

Egyptian guides travel on camels — in the age-old tradition of the desert inhabitant.
Ramili Hisari Citadel in Istanbul, Turkey, was built by Mohammed II on the Bosporus Straits and is now the site of Robert College.
