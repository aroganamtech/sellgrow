A bustling seaport on Portugal’s west coast it is a continental crossroad where you’ll see faces from the world around, hear a dozen different languages along its teeming streets. Nearby is Estoril, a sun-drenched, coastal fun-spot where you’ll see Kings and nobles at the famed International Casino. You’ll meet no friendlier people anywhere than the smiling Portuguese and you’ll long remember sights which abound wherever you go.
