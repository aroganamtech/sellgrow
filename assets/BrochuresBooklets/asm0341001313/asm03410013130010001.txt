r your vacation days never seem long enough, take a ^ trip through Norway, Sweden and Finland, lands of the Midnight Sun. These northerly neighbors know little darkness from April to mid-August, so you’ll have to keep close check on your watch to know when it’s time for bed.
The cities will intrigue you with their cosmopolitan flavor and you’ll marvel at their cleanliness. Sweden’s Stockholm is a fascinating city of canals, bridges and smorgasbord. In Finland you’ll visit Helsinki, the “White City of the North.” And you’ll discover why Copenhagen, the capital of Denmark, is called the “Paris of the North.”
A tour of Central Europe might well take you through the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Germany, Austria and Switzerland. But you’ll need lots of time to enjoy all these wonderful countries. If your days are limited, you’ll do best to make two or three your goal for now. Let the rest wait . . . for you’ll be back!
Whatever you do, you’ll be sure to include Germany’s Old World mementos . . . Cologne Cathedral, Heidelberg Castle, the birthplaces of Wagner and Goethe, and the medieval Bavarian villages. And it’s just a short hop over to Switzerland. High and handsome, crisp and cool Switzerland — thanks to its fine hospitality and magnificent scenery — long ago became one of the most popular vacation spots in Europe. You’ll want to go to fashionable St. Moritz for sure . . . and a trip to Switzerland without seeing the majestic Matterhorn is unthinkable.
The famous Little Mermaid on Lange-Unie — a favorite promenade in Copenhagen—waits for the return of Danish fishing vessels.
