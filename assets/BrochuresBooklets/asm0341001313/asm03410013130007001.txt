From The U. S. A.
THE PRESIDENT SPECIAL
Extra-deluxe, extra-fare non-stop “Strato” Clipper service to London. Lower lounge for cocktails. Seven-course dinner, with champagne. Orchids and perfume for the ladies. Your choice of Sleeperette chair-lounge or full-length berth for night-time comfort. Limited passenger list. Extra attendants.
THE PRESIDENT
Double-Decked “Strato” Clipper service to Shannon, Glasgow and London. Same as The President Special but without the extra fare and attendants, the champagne and Sleeperettes.
THE RAINBOW
Low-fare Clipper tourist service to Shannon, Glasgow and London. (Service thru to Rome beginning 4/1/53.) Swift, pressurized Super-6 Clippers. Transatlantic fares as much as one third less. Delicious meals served.
From Points East
Within Europe, board The President or The Rainbow westbound at Brussels, Amsterdam, Frankfurter Cologne/Düsseldorf (Rainbow only).
From the Orient and the Middle East specify PAA’s Asia Express from points between Hong Kong and London. (Luxuriously-appointed Super-6 Clippers on this run.)
From South America
Take Pan Am’s luxurious Super-6 Clippers connecting at New York with The Rainbow or The President flights to Shannon, Glasgow and London. Or fly to London via Africa in sleek Constellation aircraft of Pan American’s affiliate, Panair do Brasil.
