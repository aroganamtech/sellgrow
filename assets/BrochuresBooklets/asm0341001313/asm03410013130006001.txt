THE
Isles
NGLAND, IRELAND, SCOTLAND AND WALES . . . four A7' historic lands of pageantry that will delight you with their greenness, graciousness and age-old traditions. They’re dignified ladies, a bit on the quiet side, but their charm is irresistible.
In Ireland you’ll visit Dublin — and see the homes of some of Erin’s great men of literature. And don’t miss Trinity College and the Wellington Monument — tallest obelisk in the world. And everywhere in Erin soft-throated Irish wit will lighten your heart with laughter.
London-town is England’s pride and you’ll find a match for every mood within its boundaries. There’s gay night life for the man about town and colorful tradition for lovers of yester-year. And you’ll be charmed with the quaint villages and beautiful scenery of England’s historic countryside.
It’s cottages, castles and village choirs in Wales . . . while north in Bonnie Scotland you’ll walk hand in hand with history and legend, and thrill to the sight of kilted bagpipers.
At Shottery is the cottage of Anne Hathaway, Shakespeare’s wife. This still-familiar architecture . . . with its low, thatched roof and small-paned windows . . . was common in 17th century English dwellings.
Windsor Castle, built by William the Conqueror, and for centuries the residence of monarchs, bears a mark of each reign upon its interiors.
