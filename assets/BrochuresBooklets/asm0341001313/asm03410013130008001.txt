ROM THE CHATEAU COUNTRY
Normandy to the Pyrenees . .
to the Alps . . . from . France is enchanting, debonair and above all romantic. It’s the land where gracious living is the rule and life seems to the visitor like one grand perpetual vacation.
And Paris is the warm heart of France. Eternally young, full of fashion, fun and fascination, she’s the darling of dreamers . . . philosophers and gourmets . . . poets and painters. You’ll be tempted to stay in Paris forever but all of France is equally fabulous and you’ll want to see Brittany and Riviera, too.
“Tres gai” Riviera ... as luxurious and relaxing as Paris is exciting . . . and equally irresistible. Moonlit beaches, glittering resorts, famous people from every country, glamorous night life, lazy days under the sun ... all wrapped up into a neat, gilded package and tied with the endless string of good French food and hospitality.
The beautifully-detailed carving and statuary on the huge Arc de Triomphe in Paris depict famous events in the history of France — and mark the final resting place of France’s unknown warrior.
