HOW TO GET THERE
From North America EL INTER AMERICANO
From Miami to Panama, Guayaquil, Lima, Santiago and Buenos Aires. Regular fare. Spacious Club lounge. Special food and bar service. Sleeper berths available between Miami and Lima at extra charge. For extra-fare, extra-luxury service ask about special “Fiesta Lounge” flights of El Inter Americano.
EL PACIFICO
DC-6B Tourist-fare service from Miami to Panama, Guayaquil, Lima, La Paz, Santiago and Buenos Aires.
El Inter Americano and El Pacifico are operated between Miami and Panama by Pan American World Airways with Pan American-Grace Airways aircraft. No change of plane at Panama. From Panama South all flights are operated by Panagra. From New Orleans, Houston and Los Angeles, other direct Pan American services enable you to join these flights at Panama.
From Europe
If you wish to visit North America, too, Pan American’s frequent flights to New York plus connections with El Inter Americano or El Pacifico provide wonderful service. Or, take affiliate AVIANCA’s El Colombiano.
