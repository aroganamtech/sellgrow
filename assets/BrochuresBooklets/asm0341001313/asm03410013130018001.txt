ar from home, but reverently loyal to their British heritage, Bermuda and the Bahamas offer peaceful sanctuary from the cares of the everyday world. You’ll never really be sure how leisurely life can be until you’ve visited these quiet, unhurried Atlantic islands. Beguiling by day, bewitching by night, their semi-tropical beauty and gracious charm will linger with you long after you’ve left.
The romantic islands of the Caribbean sprawl comfortably in a long, lazy semi-circle from the mouth of the Gulf of Mexico to the coast of South America. Cuba is the largest, and its capital city of Havana is one of the gayest. Haiti, land of Voodoo, and the Dominican Bepublic where Columbus lies buried share the island of Hispaniola. Then there’s Jamaica, the tiny picturesque Virgin Islands, and fabulous, polyglot Trinidad with its calypso singers and ring-nosed women. There’s mystery and adventure of every sort in the Caribbean and you’ll find excitement everywhere.
You’ll enjoy the sunny white sands and rocky hideaways of Bermuda . . . the land of relaxation.
