ourists are no longer endangered by cannibals in the Fiji Islands, so have no fear. Instead, throughout the Pacific Isles you’ll find peaceful thatched-hut villages, palm-shaded beaches and thriving sugar-cane and pineapple plantations. Viti Levu, the largest island in the group, will be your headquarters and most of your loafing and sightseeing will be done there.
New Zealand and Australia are active, uncrowded, outdoor countries where you can find scenery to match your every mood. There are rugged mountains and fertile bush lands, spectacular fjords and waterfalls, sun-drenched beaches and crystal-clear lakes. The cities are cosmopolitan, the wild “outback” fascinating and life as uncomplicated as any you’ll find.
