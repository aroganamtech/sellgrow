ll the good things you’ve ever heard about the Hawaiian Islands will only partially prepare you for the precious hours you’ll spend there. A delightful blend of exotic flowers, sparkling surf and happy, carefree Hawaiian natives make this mid-Pacific oasis read like a page from a fairy tale. You’ll no doubt make Honolulu and nearby Waikiki beach on the isle of Oahu your first stops but be sure to store up memories of as many other islands as you can. There are the volcanoes of Hawaii, the largest island; bamboo forests on Maui; white beaches of Kauai . . . a thousand fabulous sights that make for a luxurious holiday.
