he most impressive sight anywhere — and everywhere -— along South America’s west coast is the majestic Andes. Towering high in the sky they serve as a magnificent backdrop for the seaside countries of Chile, Peru, Ecuador and Colombia.
Chile is first, last and always a land of breathtaking beauty where sports take precedence over almost everything else. Cradled as it is between the Andes and the South Pacific you can angle for deep-sea dolphin or tuna in the morning and schuss the nine-mile ski run at Portillo in the afternoon. Santiago, Vina Del Mar, Valparaiso and the Chilean Lake country will all compete for your favor and you’ll linger ’till the very last minute in order not to disappoint them — or yourself.
Inca ruins, alert-eared llamas, antique silver and bargain prices are all part of the scene you’ll grow to love in Peru. And you’ll relive again and again the smart times you had in Lima . . . your stroll through the strangely silent Indian market at Huancayo . . . your first view of Lake Titicaca . . . the warm and gracious welcome you get from the chic and very polite Peruvians.
