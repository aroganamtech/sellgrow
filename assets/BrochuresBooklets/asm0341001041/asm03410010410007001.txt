There are probably three reasons why the Caribbean has developed into a year-round favorite with travelers. First of all, Caribbean temperatures vary less than 10-12 degrees between summer and winter. Second, during the summer months there’s a better choice of accommodations and rates are greatly reduced. And third, the Caribbean’s so easy to reach since Pan Am Clippers can whisk you in hours to wherever you want to go along the Spanish Main and permit you to hop from place to place quickly and comfortably after you get there.
VISITING THE CARIBBEAN is like traveling round the world. Jamaica and Barbados, for example, are British in both custom and character, while Dutchspeaking Curacao is a miniature Holland, complete with narrow buildings, gabled roofs and bridges. Cuba and Puerto Rico and the Dominican Republic are as Spanish as language and tradition can make them, while Trinidad gives you a king-size sample of India and the Orient, including Hindu temples, Moslem mosques and women in saris.

CARIBBEAN CIRCLE TRIP
Best way to see the Caribbean — Mexico and Central America, too — is one of the popular Clipper CIRCLE TRIPS which you can start any time since they are in effect all year long. On these CIRCLE TRIPS, which can be taken in either direction, you’re able to see much more for less money because they all offer the money-saving advantages of Round Trip fares and some permit even greater savings. Several of the most popular CIRCLE TRIPS are shown below. However, these only suggest the scores of combinations possible on a Clipper CIRCLE TRIP.
from the EAST COAST
. . . start from New York, return via Miami ... or vice versa. Or complete the circle and return via Central America, Mexico and West Coast. The New York-San Juan-Miami all-first-class CIRCLE TRIP is popular winter, summer or any time. Cost is well under $200 — little more than the direct Round Trip flight to Miami. On another CIRCLE TRIP (for little more than $200, with a 30-day limit) you can include San Juan, Dominican Republic, Port-au-Prince, Kingston, Montego Bay, Havana, Miami and New York. And on still another CIRCLE TRIP (about $360) you can even include South America’s Columbia and Venezuela . . . stopping off at San Juan, Dominican Republic, Haiti, Curacao, other islands, Caracas, Maracaibo, Barranquilla, Trinidad, Kingston, Montego Bay, Havana, Miami and New York.
from the MIDWEST and GULF
. . . use the Houston, New Orleans or Brownsville Gateways. In some cases you can include Mexico or Central America or return via the East Coast. On a CIRCLE TRIP for about $315 you might start from Chicago, include Houston, Mexico City, Merida, Havana, Camaguey, Montego Bay, Kingston, Port-au-Prince, Ciudad Trujillo, San Juan and then either Miami or New York on your way back to Chicago. And on another CIRCLE TRIP (approximately $375) you can leave New Orleans and include Merida, Havana, Camaguey, Montego Bay, Kingston, Port-au-Prince, Ciudad Trujillo, San Juan, Windward and Leeward Islands, Port of Spain. Also, local Pan Am stops, such as Caracas en route to Panama, and then any stops en route to Guatemala and Merida as you return to New Orleans.
from the WEST COAST
. . . your Caribbean vacation can include Mexico, Central America and return via the East Coast. Typical of the trips available is a CIRCLE TRIP for just over $300 from Los Angeles or about $25 more from San Francisco, which includes Mexico, Guatemala, Yucatan area, Cuba and Florida.
