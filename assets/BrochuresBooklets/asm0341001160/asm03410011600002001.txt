Flying in air-conditioned comfort, your transatlantic flight is sheer luxury . . . the perfect beginning for your European holiday. And though you’re cruising along at nearly six miles a minute, there’s never a sensation of speed. Also, because Clippers fly “above the weather”, in the untroubled upper air, you’ll enjoy a smoothness and comfort found in no other method of travel.
