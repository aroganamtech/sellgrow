You don’t even need to leave Capetown to see beautiful scenery. This view is from the gallery of the Prime Minister’s residence, left to the country by Cecil Rhodes.
This peaceful setting is at the Wilderness Hotel near the town of George in the Mossel Bay region east of Capetown.

Near Eshowe in Zululand about 100 miles northwest of Durban, tourists watch native women dressing each other’s hair.
There’s a great variety of strange tribal customs and dress among the African natives. Note this Zulu bead-work and the necklaces which often take the form of love messages.
Overlooking the Valley of a Thousand Hills, the Rob Roy, pictured here is one of several fine hotels at Durban, center for vacation activity in Natal Province.
The characteristic African accommodations for tourists, called Rondavels, are very handy for sightseeing around the Keurbooms River mouth and Plettenberg Bay on the Garden drive from Capetown to Durban.
This view is of the flower market in the Capetown Plaza with the City Hall in the background.
A trip through the huge Kruger National Park is an unforgettable experience for travelers to South Africa. Excellent camping facilities are available at several points in the park.
The white sands and surf of beautiful Hout Bay are seen here from a local residence, on the Atlantic about 14 miles south of Capetown.
