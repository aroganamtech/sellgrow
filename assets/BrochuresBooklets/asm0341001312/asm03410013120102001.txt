LOCATION . . . Antigua is one of the Leeward Islands, a part of the Lesser Antilles, southeast of Puerto Rico, 1,692 miles from New York.
CHARACTERISTICS . . . Antigua, “Where there is Sunshine Every Day of the Year,” has some of the best swimming in the West Indies, also three golf courses completed or in building, fishing, tennis, sailing. There is no “night life,” although some of the bars remain open until midnight.
POPULATION... 42,000, of which approximately 1,500 are white, the remaining number colored.
SIZE . . . The island is 108 square miles, about 16 miles across at any point, with headlands extending out all around the island forming bays and beautiful Horse Shoe beaches.
CAPITAL ... St. John’s, with about 12,000 population.
GOVERNMENT . . . Antigua is the capital of the Leeward Islands, where the Governor is in residence. There is also an Administrator to the Island. It is a British Crown Colony, having its own Executive Council and Legislative Council.
HOW TO GET THERE ... By Pan American Clipper, St. John’s is only 3 hours flying time from San Juan via the Virgin Islands; 13 hours (elapsed time) from New York. From Miami 5 hours non-stop to San Juan,
then 3 more hours to St. John’s. By ship, about 7 days

ACCOMMODATIONS . . . Antigua Beach Hotel... 5 miles from St. John’s with its own beach, tennis court, small golf course. Accommodates 40. Rates: $6 to $9 United States. American plan.
Kensington Hotel... In St. John’s. Old world patio and Colonial house. French cuisine. Accommodates 15. Rates: $5 United States. American plan.
Turtle Gate ... In St. John’s. Luncheons and dinners served on patio. Accommodates several. Rates: $7.00 United States. American plan.
Happy Acres Hotel. . . One mile from St. John’s. Accommodates 15. Rates: $4.50 United States. American plan.
There is also the Mill Reef Club with tennis courts, fishing, boats, numerous beaches, goif course under construction. Admission by proposal of members only.
BANKS . . . Barclays Bank and Royal Bank of Canada maintain branches in St. John’s.
CALENDAR OF HOLIDAYS . . . Usual English holidays are .observed, including New Year’s, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Princess Elizabeth’s Birthday (April 21st), Whit Monday, Empire Day (May 24th), King’s Birthday (June 14th), Emancipation Day (August 1st), Christmas, Boxing Day (December 26th). Early closing days—Thursdays and Saturdays.
CIGARETTES AND TOBACCO . . . American cigarettes are scarce, so tourists should bring with them their own supply, paying duty at the customs. English cigarettes and pipe tobacco are available.
CLIMATE .. . Rainy season September and October. Other times clear, dry, with light Trade Winds from the East, making insect life almost non-existent. Summer and winter delightful.
COMMON COURTESIES AND LOCAL CUSTOMS . . . Negroes are referred to as “Coloured People”. Ladies do not wear shorts in St. John’s while shopping. Men wear coats at dinner.
COMMUNICATIONS... Radio telephone to United States
from New York or Boston.
and Canada during working hours. Cable open 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Sunday mornings.
CURRENCY . . . British West Indian dollar valued at 4 shillings and 2 pence (British currency) or about 60c United States. United States currency and checks must be converted as needed into West Indian currency or English silver.
DOCUMENTS REQUIRED . . . Proof of United States citizenship, such as old passport, birth certificate, driver’s license.
DRUG STORES . . . Miss O’Neal’s, Watts, Harpers carry everything.
FAUNA . . . Dove, pigeon, some migratory duck, yellow breasts, blackbirds. Reef fish, tarpon and bone fish.
FLORA . . . Red lilies, roses, hibiscus, bougainvillea, poin-settia, mahogany, coconuts, royals, cabbage palms, palmetto, fig, banana, plantain, avocado, citrus, papaya, mangoes in season.
GAMBLING . . . Pari-mutuel betting at two race meets a year. No gambling casinos.
LAUNDRY AND DRY CLEANING . . . Native labor always available for washing. Dry cleaning limited.
LIQUOR .. . Gin, whisky, Barbados rum very reasonable.
MEDICAL FACILITIES . . . Hospital and English and Canadian trained doctors.
MOTION PICTURES . . . There is one movie house in St. John’s.
MUSIC . . . Steel bands playing Calypso, and local dance orchestras, with small groups playing local instruments, singing native improvisations.
NIGHT CLUBS—CABARETS . . . No night clubs. Dancing at hotels and Saturdays and holidays.
PAN AMERICAN’S OFFICE ... 44 High Street (Tel. 241).
RELIGION . . . Antigua is Anglican (Church of England) but also has Catholic, Methodist, Seventh Day Adventist churches.
RESTAURANTS... Turtle Gate, Kensington and hotels serve luncheons and dinners.
