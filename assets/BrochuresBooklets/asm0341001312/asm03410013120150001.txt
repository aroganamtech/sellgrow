NEW HORIZONS
COLOMBIA (Cont.)
THEATRES • • • There are several large theatres in Bogota. The Colon is the opera and concert house. The Colombia which seats two thousand people and has a ballroom and a cabaret. The Municipal specializes in musical comedies. The Colombia is new and elaborate and worth visiting.
TIME • • • Same as United States Eastern Standard Time. TIPPING • • • Usually 10 percent, no more. Tip only waiters, porters, hat check or cloak room attendants. No tipping for taxis. A small tourist tax will be added to your hotel bill.
TRANSPORTATION • • • There are many taxi fleets and cars for hire.
WATER • • • The water is good to drink. But bottled water is always a safe bet.
WHAT TO BUY . • . Alligator shoes and bags are good buys here. But best of all are emeralds, for Bogota is the world center for the best emeralds. (Bauer and Kling are two leading jewelry shops.) There is the usual handwrought silver, Indian rugs and blankets. Unusual are the figurines sculptured from crude rubber and tinted.
WHAT TO WEAR . . . For Bogota, light weight wool suits and dresses, sweaters and skirts and a topcoat. A fur jacket can be used. For the coastal cities, lighter weight clothes, of course. A raincoat and overshoes are recommended wherever you go. Men need a topcoat and light wool suits for Bogota. Sports clothes for the coastal cities. Slacks and sport jackets may be worn.
In Barranquilla, your individual terrace overlooking the pool is a favorite attraction of Hotel Del Prado’s charming suites.
Cali, about an hour by air from Bogota, is a beautiful city with many parks and buildings of colonial interest.
