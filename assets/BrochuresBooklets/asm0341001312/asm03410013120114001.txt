LOCATION . . . The Netherlands overseas territory of Curasao consists of the islands of Curasao, Aruba and Bonaire off the coast of Venezuela and a group of small islands at the north end of the Leeward Islands 500 miles away. The official name of this territory is now The Netherlands Antilles.
CHARACTERISTICS . . . Curasao has the quaint fairy-tale charm of The Netherlands itself. The gabled houses, spotless streets, the bridges are all part of the illusion. This is a wonderful spot to buy perfumes and other luxury items. It is also a delightful spot to relax and let the world go by.
POPULATION . . . Approximately 165,000, of which 100,000 live on the island of Curasao. Aruba has about 55,000 inhabitants.
SIZE . . . Curasao is 36 miles long and 2y2 to 7 miles wide. Aruba is 19 miles long and about 5 miles wide,
CAPITAL... Willemstad, population about 45,000.
GOVERNMENT . . . Formerly a Dutch colony, Curasao is now an overseas territory, an integral part of the Kingdom of the Netherlands but with complete freedom in its internal affairs since 1950.
HOW TO GET THERE ... By Pan American Clipper, non-stop flights from New York, 8]/2 hours, or service from Miami, about 7y2 hours elapsed time. By ship about 4 days.
WILLEMSTAD
ACCOMMODATIONS ... In Willemstad, the capital of Curaçao, there are several fine hotels. Favorite with most tourists is the Americano, which faces the harbor and the pontoon bridge, rates about $12 per day up, single; Piscadera Bay Club, which offers seabathing, $8 and up per day; The Park Hotel, rates from $6.50 per day; Trianon Hotel, $8 and up. All hotels are American plan.
ARTS .. . The Curaçao Museum, housed in a restored building of old Dutch architecture contains beautiful paintings, furniture, decorations and other antiques of past centuries as well as exhibits of recent history of the island. Specimens of all trees and plants found on the island grow in its spacious gardens. There are also regular exhibitions from all parts of the world.
BANKS . .. Maduro & Curiel’s Bank, Hollandsche Bank Unie, Edwards, Henriques & Co.’s Bank, and the Curaçao Bank which is thé government owned bank of issue.
CIGARETTES, TOBACCO . . . All United States brands at same price as at home.
CLIMATE . . . Curaçao is famed for its truly Caribbean climate. The sun shines almost continually and cool breezes prevail. The ideal time to visit Curaçao is the end of November to May. The rainfall (22 inches per year) scant, with short showers.
, CLUBS ... Club de Gezellingheid (one of the oldest buildings in Willemstad; Van Engelen Club, Kwiek Club; Piscadera Bay Club with guest rooms. Cards may be arranged.
COMMUNICATIONS . . . Telephone and telegraph to the United States. For further information apply to offices of Lands-radio and Telefoondienst: Wilhelminaplein (CWZ Building), Handelskade 18, Hato Airport and All America Cables & Radio, Inc., Handelskade 26.
CURRENCY .. . The monetary unit is the guilder, worth about 53 cents.
CUSTOMS REGULATIONS FOR UNITED STATES CITIZENS AND DOCUMENTS REQUIRED .. . Birth certificate or naturalization papers. No passport, no visa. Vaccination certificate, not more than 3 years old. Health certificate issued within 30 days prior to start of journey.
DRUG STORES . . . Modern as at home. Soda fountains.
FOOD . . . Creole food is the specialty of the island, you’ll also have Dutch, Spanish, Oriental and American food.
LANGUAGE . . . Dutch, Spanish and English. Also dialects.
LAUNDRY AND DRY CLEANING ... in Willemstad facilities are modern.
LIQUOR . . . Curasao is the famous native liqueur. There is also Holland gin, Holland beer and imported whiskies to be had at more reasonable prices than at home.
MEDICAL FACILITIES . .. The St. Elizabeth and the Green Cross Hospitals are excellent.
MOTION PICTURES .. . Modern movie theatres.
NIGHT CLUBS AND CABARETS . . . Club Chobolobo, Chobolo Estate, Schottegat Road, Piscadera Bay Club and others.
PAN AMERICAN’S OFFICE . . . S.E.L. Maduro & Sons are agents for Pan American in Willemstad.
RELIGION . .. Protestant, Roman Catholic churches and Jewish synagogues (one of them is the oldest in America).
RESTAURANTS ... Avila Hotel for wonderful food. The Lido for quick lunches. The Trianon Hotel for good Dutch cuisine in old Curasao setting, the Oriental restaurant and Far East for Chinese and American dishes. Piscadera Bay Club for luncheon and dinner in a Seabreeze, the Old Dutch Tavern for Dutch and Indonesian food in a 17th century Dutch setting, the Club Chobolobo for cocktails and dinner dancing. Other good ones are the West End, the Chunking, the Hotel Americano, and Restaurant Hato Airport.
SHOPS AND STORES . .. Curasao, because of its low import duty of 3.3% on nearly all goods, is one of the greatest shopping centers. See next page for what to buy.
SIGHTSEEING .. . Willemstad with its gabled roofs and narrow buildings, looks like the Netherlands. There are many ancient buildings to be seen in the city itself. You should go to see the oldest Protestant Church on the Islands, built in 1769. It is located on the square right back of Government House. The Synagogue dates back to 1732.
