LOCATION . . . Ecuador on the northwest coast of South America between Colombia and Peru, occupies a corner of land where the Andes splits into two ranges. Located on the equator, the country has a climate which varies greatly between the uplands and the plains. Quito is 534 hours by air from Panama.
CHARACTERISTICS ... In Quito the sun rises at six o’clock every morning because the capital is on the equator. This city is the second highest capital in the world, its altitude is 9,000 feet. It is always cool here but quaintly beautiful. The city is filled with wonderful Spanish Colonial architecture and art. Its streets are steep and colorful. The country descends from the grandeur of the Andes to the tropical city of Guayaquil, on the Rio Guayas, where hyacinths float on the river. For wonderful scenery, a taste of ancient civilization, a touch of deep-sea fishing, try Ecuador where North Americans are extremely welcome.
POPULATION ... 3,362,000, fewer than Chicago.
SIZE . . . 275,936 square miles. Small among South American countries, Ecuador is larger than Texas.
CAPITAL . . . Quito, a city of 225,000, slightly smaller than Richmond, Virginia.
GOVERNMENT ... A republic with a President, Senate and Chamber of Deputies.
HOW TO GET THERE ... By Panagra’s de luxe through-plane service, “El Interamericano,” Guayaquil is only 8 hours from Miami; by connecting airline 14y2 hours from New York. Quito is less than two hours from Guayaquil. By ship, about 7 days from New York to Guayaquil.
QUITO
ACCOMMODATIONS • • • The Cordillera in El Batan, near the airport, is tops for Quito. There is a magnificent view and excellent food. Rates about $8.00 per day single, American plan. The Colon is pension-type hotel, a favorite with Americans. The Embajador is also very good, all rooms with bath. Good food. The Majestic and Savoy are commercial hotels downtown.
ARTS • • • Quito, during colonial times, was the art center of South America. There are churches and museums containing century-old paintings and sculpture. The magnificent churches are museums in themselves.
BANKS • • • Travel offices and hotels cash traveler’s checks. Free exchange is allowed to tourists.
CALENDAR OF HOLIDAYS .. . Independence Day, May 24th; Labor Day, May 1; Political Transformation Day, June 5th; Bolivar’s Birthday, July 24th; Independence Day, August 10; All Saints Day, November 2; Anniversary of Cuenca, November 3; Christmas and Twelfth Night. Holy Week, with Maundy Thursday and Good Friday. And the carnival for three days preceding Lent.
CLIMATE • • • Quito is on the equator and the sun rises exactly at six o’clock every morning. It is called the City of Eternal Spring, but it is apt to be a little colder than spring as we think of it. Temperatures range from 50° to 65°. Quito is the second highest capital in the world topped only by La Paz. Quito’s altitude, 9,000 feet.
CLUBS • • • American Club; Pichincha Club for bridge and cards; Jockey Club; for golf and tennis, see sports.
COMMON COURTESIES AND LOCAL CUSTOMS • . . Afternoon tea is a ritual in Ecuador. Between 5:30 and 7:30 everyone stops for tea.
COMMUNICATIONS ... All America Cables and Radio, Inc. Telephones, Radio International.
CURRENCY • • • The monetary unit of Ecuador is the sucre, worth about six cents.
CUSTOM REGULATIONS FOR UNITED STATES CITIZENS AND DOCUMENTS REQUIRED . . . Tourist card $1, valid for 90 days, issued by consuls and certain PAA and Panagra offices on presentation of identification and proof of paid transportation beyond Ecuador. Health and vaccination certificate.
DRUG STORES . . . There are modern drug stores where most United States preparations are sold. However, bring your own prescriptions filled with you.
FOOD . • • Food is well spiced and fairly exotic. Try locro, a stew soup of green corn, potatoes and cheese. Or llapingacho, eggs in the saddle, or in other words, on beefsteak. Humitas are tamales much more delicate than the Mexican type. The food at the Cordillera is international and very good.
GAMBLING . . . There is a National Lottery at Guayaquil and a casino at Playas.
LANGUAGE . . . English is understood in the tourist centers and better hotels and shops.
LAUNDRY AND DRY CLEANING FACILITIES ... La
Quimica is one of several good dry cleaners.
LIQUOR • • • Pilsener. Danesa and La Victoria are two native beers which are famous throughout South America.
MEDICAL FACILITIES . . . Clinica Ayora, Clinica Mo-derna and Clinica Quito are modern hospitals and have English-speaking doctors.
MOTION PICTURES ■ • • The Bolivar is the newest theatre. But there are several others.
NIGHT CLUBS AND CABARETS ... Quito is not noted for its night life, but there are interesting spots for the curious. Fiesta night club has good orchestra and food. Also Boris Club. Dancing at the Embajador on Saturday nights.
