LOCATION. .. Barbados is the most eastern of the Windward Islands.
CHARACTERISTICS . . . The flying fishes play around Barbados just as they do around Mandalay. It’s one of the specialties of this colorful West Indian Island where the natives speak with a British accent. Their rum is marvelous and so is their weather which is summer the year round. It boasts some of the finest bathing beaches in the world with waters which never rise above 77 degrees. The perfect spot for a relaxing and unusual vacation.
POPULATION... Estimated 199,000, the population of Jacksonville, Florida.
SIZE . . . Barbados is triangular in shape and is 21 miles long by 14 miles across the widest part.
CAPITAL... Bridgetown, estimated population 15,000.
GOVERNMENT... Barbados is a British colony.
HOW TO GET THERE ... By Pan American’s de luxe “El Presidente” non-stop service from New York to Port of Spain (10 hours), then U/3 hours to Barbados via British West Indies Airways. By ship about 9 days from Boston.
ACCOMMODATIONS . . . There are many fine hotels, residential clubs and guest houses where you can stay in Barbados. Among those right on the sea with their own private beaches are the Aquatic Club, Ocean View, Hastings, Edgewater, the Royal-on-the Sea, the Crane and the Powell Spring, others which are excellent but not on the ocean are the Marine, the Windsor and the Enmore. Rates are from about $8 per day up (including meals). Among the residential clubs are the Colony, Four Winds, Crane House Club, Kingsley Residential Club, Sam Lord's Castle, and the Paradise Beach Club.
ARTS. . . The Barbados Museum contains a collection of historical archeological natural history exhibits. It is one mile outside of Bridgetown.
BALLET . . . None.
BANKS . . . The Royal Bank of Canada, the Canadian Bank of Commerce and Barclay’s Bank.
CALENDAR OF HOLIDAYS. . . New Year’s Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Empire Day (May 24), King’s Birthday (celebrated in June), Whit Monday, First Monday in August, First Monday in October, November 9, Christmas, Boxing Day (December 26).
CIGARETTES AND TOBACCO . . . American cigarettes are expensive. Local and English brands are good.
CLIMATE. . . Summer all the time. There is very little variation in temperature from month to month and between day and evening and the constant sea breezes make Barbados an ideal place to visit the year around.
CLUBS . . . There are many clubs in Barbados, including the Savannah, the Bridgetown, Royal Barbados Yacht Club, Aquatic Club, and the Paradise Beach Club. Cards may be arranged. For golf, see sports.
COMMON COURTESIES AND LOCAL CUSTOMS . . . It is very British (sometimes called “Little England”), very formal and very conservative. No shorts or backless dresses except on the beaches.
COMMUNICATIONS. . . Cable and wireless, West Indies Limited, the Western Union Telegraph and Trans-Pacific telephone service all over the world.
CURRENCY . . . The British West Indies dollar worth about 60 cents in United States currency.
CUSTOMS REGULATIONS AND DOCUMENTS REQUIRED BY UNITED STATES CITIZENS ... No passports or visas necessary for any visit up to six months. But you should have some identification such as a birth certificate. Fifty cigarettes may be brought in duty-free as well as used sports equipment, personal clothes and jewelry. Visitors must declare the amount of money they are bringing in.
DRUG STORES . . . Any number of modern ones.
FOOD . . . The specialty of Barbados is the flying fish. This is sort of a salt water relative of the perch and is delicious. The greens and vegetables are excellent.
GAMBLING. . . Horse racing. Three race meets yearly.
LANGUAGE . . . English, of course.
LAUNDRY AND DRY CLEANING FACILITIES. . . Very good.
LIQUOR. . . Barbados rum, of course. All other imported whiskies at about the same prices as at home. Barbados Rum Punches and Swizzles are famous and so are the lime squashes and gin slings.
MEDICAL FACILITIES. . . The General Hospital is modern and excellent and there are fine doctors and dentists available. There is no malaria in Barbados.
MOTION PICTURES . . . There are seven cinemas showing the latest pictures daily.
MUSIC. . . Calypso singers and Barbadian bands play native and popular music.
NIGHT CLUBS AND CABARETS. . . Club Morgan for dinner or supper and dancing; the big hotels have dancing.
RELIGION ... All denominations except Presbyterian.
RESTAURANTS ... As in most of the West Indies, Barbados has very few restaurants. The hotel dining rooms and the clubs all have superb food. However, there is Goddard's Bar and Restaurant, the Manhattan Club and Restaurant and the Women's Self-Help Association Restaurant. Chinese restaurants are China Doll and the Green Dragon.
SHOPS AND STORES. . . Cave Shepherd and Co., Ltd., Da Costa & Co., Ltd., C. F. Harrison Co., Dominica Hand-Crafts Co., William Fogarty, Ltd., Alleyne Arthur & Co., Manning & Co. and Plantations, Ltd. are among the many fine stores here. Also T. R. Evans.
