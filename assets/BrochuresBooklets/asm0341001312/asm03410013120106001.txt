LOCATION ... St. Lucia, the largest of the Windward Islands, lies south of Martinique and is directly in the path of the trade winds on the route of the sailing clippers of long ago.
CHARACTERISTICS . . . This delightful island, one of the Lesser Antilles, is French in heritage, British in character. The towns of Castries and Soufriere have a quiet charm which appeals to the visitor who wants to get away from regular tourist attractions. There is splendid bathing on uncluttered white sand beaches, excellent fishing, some magnificent scenery and a combination of mountains and sea which is irresistible. Winter months are dry and sunny but not excessively hot. An ideal spot for a different vacation.
POPULATION ... About 70,000, of which 24,000 live in Castries.
SIZE ... 27 miles long and about 14 miles wide, the island has an area of 223 square miles and a coast line of 150 miles.
CAPITAL . . . Castries, one of the most beautiful harbors in the world with its beautiful green hills on each side covered with flamingo trees, alemada vines, hibiscus and other tropical shrubs. Hotels and residences are located on the sides of the hill overlooking the harbor.
GOVERNMENT... A British colony.
HOW TO GET THERE ... By Pan American clipper from New York non-stop to San Juan, Puerto Rico, 6i/3 hours flying time; from Miami, 5 hours; then 5 hours from San Juan via the Island stops to Vieux Fort which is 45 miles by scenic road from Castries. By way of Trinidad, Pan American’s de luxe “Strato” Clipper, “El Presi-dente” flies non-stop to Port of Spain in 9 hours from New York; then by British West Indian Airways, about two hours to Castries.
CASTRIES
ACCOMMODATIONS • • • Hotel Antoine is located high on a hill overlooking the capital and the harbor. Rates are about $3.50 and up. The Hotel Villa is also on the hill, but closer to the center of the town. This is a newer hotel with excellent food.
BANKS • • • Barclay’s Bank.
CALENDAR OF HOLIDAYS New Year’s Day; Good Friday; Empire Day; King’s Birthday; Whit Monday; Christmas; Boxing Day and several other local celebrations.
CIGARETTES • • • English cigarettes, but no American. Take your own.
CLIMATE • • • Climate is generally mild. Winter season is long and dry with temperatures between 70 and 80 degrees.
CLUBS • • ■ Pigeon Island Beach Club caters specially to tourists. It stands on a charming little bathing beach, has a good bar and meals are served to order. The Vigie Club is private but cards may be arranged upon proper introduction. This is a beach club and night club too. Lawn Tennis Club, cards needed. Luna Park Club.
COMMON COURTESIES AND LOCAL CUSTOMS Tea at four o’clock is a ritual here.
COMMUNICATIONS • • • There is cable and wireless service, West Indies Lts. at Castries. Inter-island phone service. Air mail to the United States and Europe.
CUSTOMS, REGULATIONS AND DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR UNITED STATES CITIZENS Regulations are the same as for other British West Indies Islands. Proof of citizenship required.
CURRENCY • • • British West Indies dollar worth about 60 cents in United States currency.
DRUG STORES • • • There are modern chemists’ shops in Castries.
FAUNA • • • Doves, pigeons, migratory ducks—-other tropical birds. Game fish.
FLORA • • • Giant ferns, flamingo trees, wonderful flowering shrubs, tropical plants, trees and palms. Beautiful flowers.
FOOD • • • Lobsters are delicious. There are no larger ones in the world. Fish and tropical fruits are plentiful. All meats must be imported. Hotels serve international cuisine.
GAMBLING • • • There is a race track. Gambling is illegal, but occurs at some private clubs.
LAUNDRY AND DRY CLEANING FACILITIES Laundry is good and reasonable. There are no dry cleaning establishments.
LANGUAGE • • • English is the native language. Some French is spoken, and also a French patois.
LIQUOR • • • Barbados and Trinidad rums are the popular drinks. English and Canadian whiskies are obtainable at less cost than at home.
MEDICAL FACILITIES • • • Hospital at Castries is staffed with English Government doctors. There are dentists, too.
MUSIC • • • Simple native music only.
MOTION PICTURES • • • Castries, Soufriere and Vieux Fort each have a motion picture theatre.
NIGHT CLUBS AND CABARETS • • ■ Night life is gay and clubs are open all night. Spots include the Vigie Club, Palm Beach Club, Luna Park Club and other smaller bistros.
RELIGION • • • Majority of natives are Catholic. There are Anglican and Methodist churches.
RESTAURANTS • • • There are very few restaurants. Most people eat at hotels and clubs.
SHOPS AND STORES • • • All stores close daily at noon. Dry goods and grocery stores are chains. Leading shops include: Peter & Co., Minville & Chastanet and J. Q. Charles.
