LOCATION . . . The Virgin Islands of the United States consist of the islands of St. Thomas, St. Croix, St. John and about 50 nearby islets. St. Thomas is only 75 miles east of San Juan, Puerto Rico.
CHARACTERISTICS . . . These perfectly delightful islands which the United States acquired by purchase from Denmark are still unspoiled by too many tourists and offer wonderful vacation attractions. As they are a free port, it is possible to purchase all sorts of wonderful things at a fraction of their cost in the United States. Too, there is a six-weeks divorce law which has attracted a number of people. There are new luxury hotels and the older ones are small but charming. The Virgin Islands have a wonderful climate, a charm all their own and you are on home territory.
POPULATION ... 26,654 inhabitants, about half of whom live on St. Thomas.
SIZE ... 133 square miles. St. Thomas has an area of only 32 square miles.
CAPITAL... Charlotte Amalie with 11,463 of the 13,811 inhabitants on St. Thomas.
GOVERNMENT . . . The islands are governed by a legislative assembly made up of locally elected municipal councils; and a governor appointed by the President.
HOW TO GET THERE ... By Pan American Clipper only 30 minutes from Puerto Rico. About 7 hours (flying time) from New York with stopover in Puerto Rico.
CHARLOTTE AMALIE
ACCOMMODATIONS ... There are a number of excellent hotels (rates, American plan, single and double): Bluebeard's Castle, $16 and $32, overlooks the town and the sea; the new Flamboyant Hotel, $16 and $32 up, 2 miles from town overlooking sea, has own pool and beach; Tradewinds Hotel, overlooking Brewer’s beach and the sea; and the Caribbean Hotel overlooking Lindberg Beach, both $8.50 and $15 up; the new Virgin Isle Hotel, $14 single, $24 double and up with breakfast, is the last word in luxury hotels, has a beautiful pool and two cork tennis courts, dancing nightly to well-known bands, terrace for each room. Downtown is the famous Hotel 1829, American plan, $9 single, $18 double, and the Grand Hotel, European plan, $4 and $6 up. There are also many excellent guest houses such as Smith's Fancy, Higgins' Gate, Harbour View, Miller Manor and Villa Olga ranging up from $6 European and $8 American plan.
ARTS • • • There is an Art Center with exhibitions of works of the natives and others.
BANKS • • • The Virgin Island National Bank is the only one. This has a branch in St. Croix.
CALENDAR OF HOLIDAYS.. . Besides all the ones we observe at home, the Islands' have a few of their own. In St. Thomas they are: March 31, Transfer Day (Day the Islands were taken over from the Danes); July 25, Supplication Day and two Thanksgivings, one, the same as at home, the other, October 25 in Thanksgiving for the Island’s survival of hurricanes.
CIGARETTES AND TOBACCO . . . American cigarettes are only 12 cents a pack.
CLIMATE • • • Not the usual tropical rainy seasons, but occasional sun showers. The temperature averages about 80 degrees. Constant Trade Winds keep the humidity down.
CLUBS • • • The only private club is the Estate Contant Club.
COMMON COURTESIES AND LOCAL CUSTOMS.. . Brief shorts are frowned upon. English walking shorts are accepted for men and women during day but ladies more appreciated in female attire.
COMMUNICATIONS ... All American Cables and Radio, West Indian Cable and Wireless, Ltd. Air mail. United States Post Office. Telephone service to the States.
CURRENCY . . . United States money is used.
CUSTOMS REGULATIONS AND DOCUMENTS REQUIRED . . . No passport required of United States citizens.
DRUG STORES . . . Two, well stocked with United States products.
FAUNA... No wild life here.
FLORA. . . Magnificently colored hibiscus, oleanders, bougainvillea and orchids grow here.
FOOD... In some places they serve native Creole dishes, but for the main part the hotels and the restaurants specialize in typical American food, charcoal broiled steaks and seafood.
GAMBLING. . . There is horse racing at a small track in St. Thomas and the newest fad in St. Croix is donkey cart races, under the auspices of the St. Croix Jonkey Club. The big event is the Great West Indian Steeplechase.
LANGUAGE . . . English, of course.
LAUNDRY AND DRY CLEANING FACILITIES... There are several good laundries and dry cleaning places.
LIQUOR. . . Any brand of liquor you want at the lowest prices: Scotch for $2.25 a fifth and up, excellent cognac for $3, rum for about 70 cents a bottle.
MEDICAL FACILITIES.. . There is a government owned hospital with a capable staff.
MOTION PICTURES. . . Center Theatre on Dronningens Gade (Queen Street) is air-conditioned and shows American films.
NIGHT CLUBS AND CABARETS .. . There are numerous night spots as well as the restaurants in the leading hotels. Some of the better known are . . . the supper room at the Caribbean Hotel, popular for dinner and dancing to Calypso songs and good American music; the Hideaway, very popular among the all-nighters; the Ransom House is good for food and drinks and of course the new Virgin Isle Hotel where there will be New York “name” bands playing for dancing every night.
