LOCATION . . . Lebanon occupies a small strip of land at the east end of the Mediterranean, bordered by Israel on the south and by Syria on the north and east.
CHARACTERISTICS . . . This is a wonderful little country which within its boundaries has some of the most magnificent ruins in the world; the famous “Cedars of Lebanon,” modern cities and villages which turn the clock back hundreds of years. There are wonderful beaches within a short distance of high mountains on which skiing is excellent. It is the Biblical “Land of Milk and Honey” with orange groves, banana plantations and olive groves. With wonderful wild flowers and crystal clear rivers gushing from grottos. It is a wonderful mingling of the old and the new, and off the beaten track. ’
POPULATION... 1,186,145; the country has a slightly greater population than Detroit.
SIZE . . . About 120 miles long and varying from 30 to 35 miles in width, the total area is 3,600 square miles.
CAPITAL . . . Beirut, with a population of about 340,000, which is slightly larger than Rochester, N. Y.
GOVERNMENT... an independent republic.
HOW TO GET THERE ... By Pan American Clipper one-stop service from New York to Beirut, only 24Vi hours. By ship about 14 to 21 days depending on the steamship line.
BEIRUT
ACCOMMODATIONS . . . Best hotels are the St. George, the Bristol, and the Normandy. Rates run about $6 per day, single. The St. George is large, modern and has a beach for bathing. It is on a point and is surrounded by water on three sides.
ARTS . . . The National Art Museum and the Museum of the American University both house excellent collections of art and antiquities.
BALLET . . . Visiting French troupes.
BANKS . . . Banque Nationale; Banque Zilkha; Bank de Syrie et du Liban, Bank of Iran and Middle East, Arab Bank, Ltd., Banco di Roma, Bank Zilkha, Thos. Cook & Son.
CALENDAR OF HOLIDAYS . . . There are 23 legal holidays in the year. The principal ones are: New Year, January 1st and 2nd; Christmas Day, December 25th; Easter; Independence Day, November 22nd.
CIGARETTES AND TOBACCO . . . American brands are available at about double their cost at home. Native cigarettes are excellent. You may bring in 100 cigarettes or about Yi lb. of tobacco.
CLIMATE . . . The climate is almost as varied as ours. There are four distinct seasons but it is almost always cold in the mountains and usually quite warm near the sea. April through November is the best time to visit Lebanon.
CLUBS ... St. George’s Club, Rotary Club, Syro-Lebanese Club, Masonic Lodge. A tourist would have to be accompanied by a member to make use of any of these clubs.
CURRENCY . . . The Lebanese pound is the monetary unit. The established rate is $1 United States equals 3.50 Lebanese pounds. However, there is an open market for the exchange of money which fluctuates daily. Currency of any denomination is exchanged on this market openly and legally.
COMMON COURTESIES AND LOCAL CUSTOMS . . . The Lebanese are extremely hospitable. A guest is a sacred trust. Even in the poorest homes the Lebanese offer gifts and hospitality.
COMMUNICATIONS . . . Telegraph service, air-mail.
CUSTOMS REGULATIONS AND DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR UNITED STATES CITIZENS . . . Passports and visa required. Vaccination certificate for re-entry to the United States.
DRUG STORES . . . There is one drug store in Beirut which has a soda fountain and is similar to those at home. The majority are chemists’ shops.
FAUNA . . . Principally, the mule, Arab horse and cattle in general as well as poultry, migratory and local birds. Fish of various forms common to the Mediterranean.
FLORA. . . Lebanese flora is similar to that of Southern Europe. It is rich in all kinds of flowers and the Cedars of Lebanon are outstanding.
FOOD... Barbecued meats; rice dishes and wonderful dishes cooked in grape leaves, similar to Syrian foods, are native here. A majority of tourist restaurants serve Continental food of good quality and variety.
GAMBLING . . . There is an excellent race track in Beirut. There are casinos at the smart resorts.
LANGUAGE... French, Arabic and English. You will have no difficulty when using only English in Lebanon.
LAUNDRY AND DRY CLEANING . . . Laundry is excellent and cheap. Dry cleaning establishments are modern.
LIQUOR. . . Arrack is the native drink, but drink it cautiously. At the better restaurants and hotels you can get cocktails and most of the European liquors. A great variety of American liquors available at very reasonable prices.
MEDICAL FACILITIES . . . There are excellent hospitals, among them the Hospital of the American University; St. Joseph’s Hospital with English speaking doctors.
MOTION PICTURES . . . There are many motion picture theatres and mostly American films are shown. These theatres include: Dunia, Roxy, Capitole, Rivoli, Metropole.
MUSIC . . . There is no national symphony, but there are concerts presented by The Lebanese Academy of Arts.
NIGHT CLUBS . . . There are two principal night clubs: The Lido and the Kit Kat. The Kit Kat has a gambling casino for roulette and baccarat. These night clubs have nightly floor shows which last approximately 2 hours.
PAN AMERICAN’S OFFICE... Parliament Square (Tel. 96-60).
RELIGION . .. There are churches of all denominations, and Beirut has two resident Cardinals.
