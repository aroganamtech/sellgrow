ONLY THE CLIPPERS HAVE MADE 50,000 ATLANTIC CROSSINGS a record no other airline ran match!
On Wednesday, June 29, a giant 60-ton "Super-7" Clipper carrying 71 passengers touched down at Orly Field, Paris.
This marked the 50,000th time a Flying Clipper* of Pan American World Airways had made the Atlantic crossing. No other airline has approached this record.
Think about this unmatched record next time you plan a
trans-Atlantic trip. It is mighty reassuring to know you're on the one airline that leads the world in over-ocean experience.
More people fly Pan American than any other airline . . . across the Atlantic . . . across the Pacific . . . throughout Latin America . . . and 'Round the World. It has earned the highest compliment experienced air travelers can give— "Pan American knows how to run an airline."
