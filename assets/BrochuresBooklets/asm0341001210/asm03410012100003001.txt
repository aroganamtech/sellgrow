Trip by Flying Clipp
Outline of Procedure

7

The following outline will enable the class to prepare talks or papers. It may also be used as a guide during each lesson.
OUR TRIP TO EUROPE
1.	We start our vacation. A joint class discussion led by the committee.
a.	Scenery on way to New York
b.	Mode of travel
c.	Important towns en route
d.	Points of interest
2.	New York—A description
3.	La Guardia Airport.
a.	Description.
b.	Ticket office and waiting room.
c.	Control tower.
4.	Our Ship, the Flying Clipper America.
Description of the Boeing Strato-cruiser, the Flying Clipper which is to fly us over the Atlantic.
a.	Size
b.	Fuselage, wings
c.	Engines, propellers
d.	Automatic devices for flying
e.	Preparations for trip (safety precautions)
f.	Captain and crew
5.	We take to the air.
Display pictures taken from the air.
a.	The land as we leave
b.	Interior of plane
6.	Arrival at Gander Field, Newfoundland.
a.	Fueling and check up
b.	Length of stay
c.	Weather conditions
7.	We fly the Atlantic.
a.	Description of the mechanics of flight
b.	The weather
8.	Arrival at Shannon Airport, Ireland.
EUROPE
Each group may use this outline in the preparatory work.
1.	General description of country.
a.	Land—mountains, rivers and coastline
b.	Location
c.	Climate and vegetation
d.	Natural resources
2.	The People.
a.	Race
b.	Language
c.	Customs and dress
d.	Standard of living
e.	Children
3.	A short history of the Country
a.	National heroes of the past
b.	Famous living people
c.	Statesmen
4.	Industries
a.	Farming
b.	Mining
c.	Manufacturing
d.	Industrial arts and crafts
5.	Cities
a.	Population
b.	Relative size
c.	War damage—new buildings
d.	Public buildings—famous landmarks, schools, churches, theatres, museums, business.
e.	Homes.
f.	Transportation facilities.
6.	Art, architecture, music, literature, culture, folk-lore and dance.
SIGHT-SEEING TRIP IN EACH COUNTRY
This should be imaginative with each member of the group participating.
1.	Mode of travel used on trip
2.	Description of weather
3.	Tell about a point of interest
4.	Tell about the people seen, their dress, language, etc.
5.	Describe parks, flowers, trees, birds and animals
