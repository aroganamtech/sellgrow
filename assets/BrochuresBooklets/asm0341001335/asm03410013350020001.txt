IF YOU DON’T HAVE A CAR THAT FLIES AND WE MEAN LITERALLY SPROUTS WINGS HERE’S THE NEXT BEST THING
Want to add something new to your next automobile or trailer-camper vacation? Try combining localized airplane flights with your ground travel.
Primarily your trip is by car, pickup camper, travel trainer or some other sort of motor home. Local airplane sightseeing flights, in light planes, are secondary parts of your trip, restricted to selected places best seen from the air.
An example may clarify this new concept in traveling. My wife and I recently took a three-week’s vacation trip by car through northern California. We covered about 1,500 miles and saw a host of memorable sights. We drove through the delightfully different delta country between San Francisco and Sacramento, saw the imposing state capitol building, went up and over the lovely Sierra Nevada Mountains to Lake Tahoe, and there did a little gambling. Of course, we lost.
All along the way we camped out, in state parks, national parks, and at monument camp grounds.
The localized airplane flights were added fillips to our trip. We took three local flights: the first over the San Francisco Bay area at the beginning of the trek, a second one over Lake Tahoe a few days later, and the third in the Shasta Lake region another week or so after the second flight.
We chose those three places because they were logically best. Flying into San Francisco is acknowledged by air travelers to be one of the greatest such sights in the world.
