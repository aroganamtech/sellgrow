Maximum warranty recovery is dependent on the awareness and cooperation of all maintenance personnel. We all know the definition of warranty or do we?
Well, if any of us are doubtful, here briefly are the mechanics of the word warranty.
During the manufacture of any piece of equipment, some form of inspection (Quality Control) is applied. The cost of inspection, of course, is included in the overall labor cost, so obviously the purchaser pays for it in the selling price.
Applying this knowledge to the airline industry is academic.
One 707 aircraft costing $5,000,000 with an estimated 5% of the purchase price representing a type of warranty fund, means (a kickback if you will) on defective parts up to $250,000 for the purchaser.
To further emphasize warranty a fleet of 25 Boeing 747’s each costing approximately $20,-000,000 and the initial spares for this fleet estimated at $70,000,000, we have $570,000,000 total expenditure.
Based on the 5% concept the 747 fleet has a warranty potential of $28,500,000 during the next three years.
