“THE MASCULINE ART”
When you say the word ART most men picture a stuffy gallery filled with paintings and clay sculpture. They identify artists as prissy feminine types, clothed in brightly colored smocks with berets on their heads and brush in hand. Well, I must admit I was also guilty to some extent of type casting artists, until just recently.
Abstract and junk metal art or Object d’ Art is by no means for the prissy or feminine type. As a matter of fact anyone with average strength, knowledge of arc and oxygen acetylene welding and cutting procedures, a few simple hand tools and a large amount of mechanical creativity, is well qualified to begin a most interesting and rewarding hobby.
