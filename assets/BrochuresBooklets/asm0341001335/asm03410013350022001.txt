Continuing south we could see the Bayshore Freeway with its crawling cars, then the San Francisco International Airport with its many wings, loading-unloading concourses, and jets waiting to take off.
We continued down-bay to Stanford University at Palo Alto where the Hoover Memorial Tower sticks up above the spacious campus, and where we could see the world’s largest linear accelerator in which atomic age scientists can send a 20-billion volt beam down the two-mile long “barrel” to explore subnuclear particles.
Then back to San Carlos ... a truly wonder-filled tour, just under an hour, with the cost of the flight based on $28 an hour.
At Lake Tahoe we arranged for a similar flight with the Bridgeford Flying Service, again in a four-place Cessna. We passed over the casinos at Stateline—Harrah’s, Harvey’s, Barney’s, and the Sahara—glittering in the sun like a city center in Shangri-la.
