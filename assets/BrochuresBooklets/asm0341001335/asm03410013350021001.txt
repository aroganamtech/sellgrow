Three flights during the three weeks seemed to be a nice number to take, and would also fit into our budget.
All flight arrangements were simple and similar. To see the Bay area, we drove to nearby San Carlos Airport and arranged with Westbay Aviation for an hour’s air tour. We flew in a four-place Cessna, with the pilot in the left-hand seat, my wife in the right-hand seat, and me in the rear, the better to snap pictures through either the right or left side windows.
The pilot made a circuitous route, across the expansive Bay, past the University of Califor-nit’s campus at Berkeley with its authentic fields at extreme ends and its tall Campanile tower centered among the many buildings.
