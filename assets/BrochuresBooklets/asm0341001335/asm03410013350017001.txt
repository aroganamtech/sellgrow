His first creation was entitled “The Epitaph of a Bull” and since that time he has created almost an entire zoo of his own.
His animals have been placed in their natural habitat around his home. There’s “Albert the Alligator” with water gushing from his jaws, and “Big Bowzer” who greets you at the driveway, the Duck Family out for a stroll, and Cheetah the Monkey appropriately swinging in his tree.
Since his beginning in 1968, Dick has created over two hundred different works ranging from the animal variety to some very original wall designs.
Many of his creations are on view at the Capon Gallery on Long Island. He has donated all profits from the sale of his works to various charities stating that, if it were a business instead of a hobby he would lose all interest in it.
So the next time you feel embarrassed to purchase that paint by number Picasso kit for your two-year-old son, so you can help him finish it, why not visit your local Junk Yard and visualize your first creation in the Masculine Art.
