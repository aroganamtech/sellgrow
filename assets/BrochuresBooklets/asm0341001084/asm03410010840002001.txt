MONTERREY
Situated six hundred miles from Mexico City, Monterrey has many attractions for the visitor: thrilling rodeos, for instance, and health giving hot springs. Here, too, the dramatically modern La Purisima Church, contrasts with the centuries-old architecture of Old Spain surrounding it. The shelter of the verdant Mountains of the Saddle gives Monterrey its mild and dry climate... perfect resort weather the year around!
ACAPULCO
A huge oval bay set at the foot of towering mountains. . . miles of magnificent white beach fringed with palms . . .clear azure water rich with marlin and sail-fish and fascinating undersea life for the observer-there’s all this and much more resort fun to be enjoyed in sophisticated, magnificent Acapulco.

HAVANA, CUBA
Exotic Queen City of the Caribbean, the sun always shines and the fun never stops in gay Havana. There’s a wide choice of pleasure for every taste in this big metropolis.
