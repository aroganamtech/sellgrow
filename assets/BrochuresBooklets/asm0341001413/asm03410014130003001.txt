NOTES
1	HANDLING CHARGE: There is a $2.00 handling charge per Airwaybill covering each shipment leaving the U. S.
2	TELEGRAPHIC—RADIO OR CABLE COST: In accordance with Company’s regulations when shipments involve radio, telegraphic or cable expenses, the Company will assume one-way radio, telegraphic or cable expense and the shipper will be charged for one-way portion. Any additional messages due to cancellations of space reserved or corrections for which the Company is not responsible will be borne by the shipper.
3	LIMITS OF SIZE AND WEIGHT OF PACKAGES:
Single packages are acceptable for AIR EXPRESS transportation when they do not exceed 5 feet in length or 15 feet in length and girth combined, or 15 cubic feet in volume. The maximum weight for a single package will be 75 lbs. Packages heavier, larger or having a greater cubical volume may be accepted for transportation only by special arrangement. To determine the volume of a package multiply the length by height by width.
4	VALUATION ZONE: The above Air Express transportation rate table has a zone or key letter in front of each tariff (A. B. C. D.). These key letters are for reference to tables II, III and IV on following page.
TOTAL CHARGES AIR EXPRESS SERVICE
Railway Express Agenev tariffs are additional to Pan American Airways tariffs.
Charges for air transportation beyond Miami or Brownsville are the total of the following: (1) transportation rate (weight x pound rate); (2) valuation charge;
(3)	handling charge (constant at $2.00 per Airwaybill);
(4)	telegraphic charge if necessary.
TOTAL CHARGES: transportation plus handling plus valuation plus telegram. For further details communicate with any Company office or any Express Agent named herein.
