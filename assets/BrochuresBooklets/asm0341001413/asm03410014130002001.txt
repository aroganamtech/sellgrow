Speed trade—be ahead of your competitor—keep your capital hard at work and increase your profits—use P.A.A. Express service, the only fast, through Express service from here to there.
P.A.A. Express service makes available for merchandise the fast, dependable schedules which for nearly 4 years have carried the United States foreign airmail and passengers with clock-like regularity. You and your consignees are saved time and money because of its simplified routine, which eliminates practically all of the paperwork requirements that exporters up to now, have been forced to contend with. Immediate customs clearance at destination is an important added advantage in developing your foreign trade.
By simplifying shipping, documentation and clearance requirements, the paperwork and handling procedure on the ground has been speeded up in proportion to the air speed of the service; likewise these simplifications of export routine make this International Air Express service as simple for you to use as domestic Express service.
The surprisingly low tariffs coupled with the frequent and speedy schedules, make P.A.A. Express service available not only to the occasional shipper who, due to an emergency, is in need of the fastest transportation available, but as well to the farsighted business man who wishes to save that precious element TIME (and its twin, MONEY) and to expand his business.

INFORMATION FOR SHIPPERS
INFORMATION FOR SHIPPERS
1	WHEN ORIGINATING SHIPMENTS, shippers should communicate with the nearest office of the Company or one of our Express agents.
2	COMPLETE ADDRESS of consignee, as well as return address of shipper should appear on each package being shipped.
3	OUR AIRWAYBILL is the only document necessary, except when shipments to certain countries exceed $100.00 in value (Brazil—$45.00, Colombia—$50.00). Consular certification of the Airwaybill is not required. Copies of it serve as waybill, commercial invoice, customs declaration, shipper’s receipt. The shipper’s signature is required on the Airwaybill as it serves as a commercial invoice and because on the back is printed the Company’s contract of carriage which the shipper accepts when he signs the Airwaybill.
4	CERTIFICATION OF DOCUMENTS—see Nos. 3 and 8.
5	CLAIMS must be made in writing within ten days after delivery at destination, or in cases of non-arrival or non-delivery at destination, within sixty days after acceptance of shipment.
6	CONSIGNMENT on the Airwaybill will be to the Company for the account of the ultimate consignee at airway destination, except in the case of shipments destined to Brazil when consignment will be made to ultimate consignee in care of the Company at airway destination. See No. 17.
7	THE COMPANY’S CONTRACT OF CARRIAGE is
printed on the back of the Airwaybill, copies of which are available at any office of the Company or its Express agents.
8	CONSULAR INVOICES are required only in certain cases. See No. 3 above and consult the nearest Express agent for information as to when a consular invoice is required.
9	C. O. D. SERVICE will be announced when available.
10	CUSTOMS CLEARANCE is effected by the consignee with the assistance of our airway representatives who will notify consignees of the arrival of shipments.
11	CUSTOMS VALUE must be established by the shipper and must appear on the Airwaybill over the shipper’s signature.
12	DOCUMENTS—see Nos. 3 and 8 and consult nearest Express agent.
13	DOMESTIC EXPRESS—see No. 22.
14	DUTIES are for the account of the consignee, and are assessed upon the Customs Value established by the shipper.
15	EXPRESS COLLECT SERVICE will be announced when available.
16	FRAGILE articles must be so marked and must be packed acceptable to Railway Express Agency, Inc.
17	IMPORT SHIPMENTS arriving at Miami or Brownsville destined for consignees in this country are dis-
patched North in bond (when duties exceed $15.00) to the United States Customs Bonded Warehouse nearest to the point of destination by use of “IMMEDIATE TRANSPORTATION ENTRY.” Consignees are advised by us when shipment is dispatched and copies of the necessary customs forms are sent with letter of advice.
18	COMPANY’S ASSUMPTION OF LIABILITY is
fully stated in its contract of carriage appearing on the back of the Airwaybill. (See No. 3.) In no event shall the Company’s liability exceed the amount of the declared value of each shipment.
19	THE LIMITS OF SIZE, WEIGHT, VALUE are explained in the Company’s tariffs as printed on next page.
20	MARKING AND PACKING REQUIREMENTS are
as for Railway Express Agency service.
21	PROHIBITED ARTICLES are as follows:
(a)	Letters, postcards (representing correspondence from one party to another) or any other matter which under postal laws and regulations may be transported only as mail.
(b)	Arms, ammunition, explosives, corrosives, inflammable or volatile liquids or materials, or all such articles as are liable to endanger aircraft, persons or property.
(c)	Live stock except by special arrangements.
(d)	Articles, the importation of which is prohibited in the country of destination or the transport of which is prohibited in or above any of the countries traversed in flight.
22	RECEIVING STATIONS—Railway Express Agency, in each city where we have an Express Agent, has equipped one of its receiving offices to act as a Receiving Station for the acceptance of shipments, for dispatch to the Company’s offices at Miami or Brownsville, Texas via Railway Express Agency service.
23	SCHEDULES are subject to change without notice; our Express Agents are supplied with all current schedules.
24	SEALED shipments must comply with the regulations of Railway Express Agency.
25	SHIPMENTS are accepted at receiving stations subject to space having been reserved through an office of the Company or one of its Express Agents.
26	WHEN SHIPMENTS ARE UNCLAIMED OR UNDELIVERED twenty days after arrival at destination, the shipper will be requested to instruct us as to disposition of shipment. The Company’s contract of carriage contains regulations affecting the shipper in such cases.
27	SHIPPER’S RECEIPT is signed as accepted for transportation by the Company and mailed to the shipper upon delivery of shipment to us at Miami or Brownsville by Railway Express Agency.
28	TARIFFS are explained in the tables of tariffs printed on the following pages. Railway Express Agency tariffs from point of origin to Miami or Brownsville are additional to AIR EXPRESS tariffs.
