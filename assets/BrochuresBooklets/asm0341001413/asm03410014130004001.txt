SPECIAL NOTES TABLES II—III—IV
1	All packages must have a declared valuation stated on the airwaybill over the shipper’s signature. Valuation charges are assessed on all Express shipments irrespective of and in addition to the transportation and handling charges. The valuation charge varies according to the length of the haul and the declared value of the goods.
2	ASSESSMENT OF DUTIES ON DECLARED VALUE. Duties will be assessed upon the value declared by the shipper for customs purposes. The declared value appears upon the face of the airwaybill inasmuch as the airwaybill is a substitute for a commercial invoice.
3	VALUATION CHARGES TABLE II: Before accepting “ordinary” Express shipments valued over $5000.00 it will be necessary to request special rate from and advise the Company’s Express Office in New York the total value and the airway points involved.
4	SHIPPERS MAY VALUE THEIR TRANSPORTATION COSTS as well as the value of the merchandise by payment of the valuation charge for the total amount. However, the value for customs purposes must be shown and marked on airwaybill as a separate item in the “declared value column.”
