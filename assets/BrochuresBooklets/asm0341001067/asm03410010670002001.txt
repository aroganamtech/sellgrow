SIMPLE TO USE
AS DOMESTIC EXPRESS
There is no red tape to Pan American Air Express. No mass of documents to be filled out. No costly handling charges. No delays in customs clearance.
"Via Pan American,” you can dispatch your merchandise to Latin America as simply as you would ship a parcel by railway express within the United States. One single document, the AIR-WAYBILL, is the only form required. This form alone, carrying the shipper’s signature, serves as a commercial and consular invoice, bill of lading, declaration, shippers’ and consignee’s receipt. No certification of documents is required on ordinary shipments. Sent by air express, your shipments get preferred handling. Special arrangements have been made to expedite their passage through the customs of the various countries served by Pan American.
With the careful handling accorded along the airways, only simple wrapping is necessary, the same as that required for railway express shipments.
Simplified rates and valuation charges, together with the elimination of handling charges, make this international air express service especially economical to use. Fast air mail schedules insure speedy transit. Careful handling assures the arrival in perfect condition and its speedy delivery. Shipments up to 18,/ x 30" x 60" can be carried on all planes. On the main trunk lines much larger pieces may be shipped by air. On packages of extraordinary bulk and weight, our agent nearest you will gladly arrange the space reservation.
Your import shipments from southern countries, will be dispatched by the fastest available means, upon their arrival at either of our northern terminals. Consignees are advised when shipments are dispatched and copies of necessary customs forms are sent with the letter of notification.

Pan American Air Express is as near as your telephone.
Recognized agents familiar with all routes and schedules of the Pan American Airways System, are located in principal cities throughout the country. The agent nearest you will be glad to supply all necessary information, make reservations and arrange your shipments.
Simply get in touch with the nearest of these offices. Tell them what you wish to ship and when. They will provide you with a schedule for your shipment, when it should leave your office and when it will arrive at its destination. They will be glad to arrange shipment by railway or air express, as you desire, to Miami, Florida, or Brownsville, Texas, where it will be turned over there to Pan American Airways Express Department.
