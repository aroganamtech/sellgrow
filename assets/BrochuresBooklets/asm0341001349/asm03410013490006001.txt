A trip abroad is one project ideally suited for group activity. Exciting to think about. . . exciting to talk about. It stirs individual members . . . captures their imagination. One indication of travel’s great appeal as a group activity is the way business firms are offering it, with remarkable results, as part of their incentive programs for their workers and salesmen, their dealers, distributors and others.

In fact, travel has proved so effective as a group activity that more and more companies and organizations are using it as an incentive to spur their employees or members to greater accomplishment.
