“Off-Season Rates” and Accommodations
If your group chooses to travel during the “off season” to any particular destination — they can usually make substantial savings. They can get a wider choice of accommodations at the better hotels, for lower rates. They can escape the tourist-season crowds, and get a better selection and better prices on things they buy to bring back home.
Naturally the “Off-Seasons” vary throughout the world. For South America and the Caribbean, for instance, it occurs during our summer. For Europe, it’s November 1st to March 31st approximately.
Special Tours at Bargain Rates
This is another way your group can save substantially. Most tours include round-trip air transportation abroad, hotel accommodations, sightseeing arrangements and sometimes entertainment. Tours can be specifically designed — or adapted from existing tour schedules to meet your group’s requirements. Pan American has cooperated with many tour operators in designing a wide variety of especially-priced “Pan Am Holiday” cruises to literally anywhere in the world.
Like all special travel arrangements, prices on the typical tours described in this booklet are subject to normal rate changes which may increase or decrease their cost. Consider the prices quoted as reasonable estimates — to help your group gain a good idea of where they want to go and what they can afford. In all cases, your Pan American Representative will furnish up-to-date rates and other current information for any trip you have in mind.
