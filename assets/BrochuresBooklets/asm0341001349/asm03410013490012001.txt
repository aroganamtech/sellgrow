To get any group to act, someone has to get the ball rolling. And in this case it might as well be you because, as explained on Page 11, it may mean a free Clipper ticket for you. And don’t hesitate because your own travel experience is limited. For here’s all you have to do.
See Your Pan American Representative
Get in touch with your nearest PAA office (see list on Page 19) or any authorized Pan American Travel Agent. You’ll have experienced counsel right from the start at no extra cost. Besides, PAA Representatives have many Pan American aids — booklets, folders, movies, posters — to help organize group travel.
Start With a Plan
Plan a trip to interest most of your members. Your PAA Representative has many “special tours” available, some at sizeable savings and one may serve your purpose. If not, he can adapt one or work up a special Itinerary to meet your group’s requirements.
Make Copies off Your Itinerary
Make plenty of copies of your Itinerary for use in promoting your tour. Show dates you’ll leave and return, countries you’ll visit and time spent in each, cost, etc. Your PAA Representative can furnish timetables, fares, schedules, etc.
