
As 17 Million
Clipper Passengers Have Proved
Whether your group decides to go first class or tourist, all Clippers are designed for complete comfort and enjoyment. And the Clipper passage itself is certain to be one of the most enjoyable experiences of the trip abroad. The adjustable reclining Clipper lounge seats are so-o comfortable. It’s easy to read, smoke, relax, talk and play cards together — and there’s plenty of space to walk around. Pleasant cabin attendants bring books, magazines, snacks — or a pillow and a blanket. And outside the windows the sky-view is wonderful. So are the meals — and there’s no tipping.
The cabin is air-conditioned in the summer, comfortably heated in the winter. Most Clippers are pressurized too, so there’s no “elevator” sensation when your Clipper climbs after take-off, or descends for a landing. Time and distance go so fast, and you and your group have so much fun together — that you’re there before you know it. And since you’ll have your camera along, you’ll come home with an album of snapshots to relive your trip for years to come.
