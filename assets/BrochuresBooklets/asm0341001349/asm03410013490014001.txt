Tell the Cost — Explain the Savings
If yours is a Pan Am Holiday or other cost-inclusive tour, point out the savings. On other arrangements, explain the 10% saving on Round Trip tickets. If you’re going by Rainbow, explain the saving over first class President service. If you’ll travel in an “off-season”, explain how the group saves -— how they escape peak tourist crowds and enjoy better selections, better prices . . . even on their take-home purchases.
Suggest How to Handle Financial End
No doubt the financial side is up to your Ways and Means Committee but offer some suggestions. For example, each member might pay all or part of his or her expenses. Or the group can help out with surplus funds, if you have any, or with fund-raising activities. Mention how your members can “save up” . . . start a Travel Savings Account . . . collect “travel dues” at each meeting. Some employee groups often arrange to have savings withheld from pay and others are fortunate enough to receive subsidies from the Company Recreation Program.
Explain Travel “On Credit”, Too
Most banks loan money for vacation trips, just as they do for purchasing cars and paying bills. And members of employee groups can often borrow from the Company credit union and repay at their leisure. Most convenient method of all, of course, is the World-Wide Plan by which you Go Now and Pay Later. Your members would pay as little as 10% down, with the balance spread over as long a period as 20 months.
