Prof essional Organizations	They attend international Conclaves and Meetings — exchange ideas and information with noted foreign authorities — visit the great libraries, museums, schools and universities noted in their field. They, too, often can deduct their travel expenses from income tax.
Educational Groups	Teacher groups, student groups, or combinations of both often stay for a full summer course at some famous foreign university. Or travel for educational credit to one or many international destinations. Pan American, through its Educational Department, has arranged many tours for such groups.
Civio Groups	Chambers of Commerce and other Civic associations study civic projects of all types — and the extent and administration of recreation and other public facilities in cities all over the world.
Grange sind 4-PE Groups	They attend world-famous Fairs, and Cattle Shows, and visit model Poultry and Dairy Farms. They travel to regions all over the world to observe at firsthand new or unusual agricultural methods.
Labor Union Groups	Such groups attend foreign labor meetings and Conventions — meet and exchange ideas with prominent foreign labor leaders — observe working and living conditions in and around plants and factories abroad.
Social Groups	Many social groups -— Garden Clubs, Art Clubs, Music Clubs, Literary Clubs and others — find travel together broadening and exciting. They visit Italy, where the Renaissance began, Germany, birthplace of so many great composers, Holland, flower capital of the world . . . and of course, England and the Shakespeare country, to mention but a few possible destinations.
