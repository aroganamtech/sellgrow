
Prepare Your Presentation
Your PA A Representative will probably suggest at least one movie of the places you'll visit and help you arrange to run it. Pan American’s fine travel films in brilliant natural color and sound run about half an hour each.
He can furnish posters, too, folders, booklets, etc., on your destinations. Also special material ... on conventions, for example, and pilgrimages, sports, and special events, as well as study opportunities abroad, international exhibits, fairs, festivals, etc. Use what you think might help you.
Make Your Presentation
Arouse enthusiasm by presenting your suggestion at a meeting of all members. Enthusiasm is contagious — so give your presentation all the vigor and excitement it merits. Distribute copies of your suggested Itinerary and the literature secured from your PAA Representative. Then explain the idea as a group effort that stems from the common interests of the members themselves.
Explain You Can Travel Any Time
Emphasize the freedom of traveling abroad by Clipper — how your group can go any time they want to and attend any special events they wish. If yours is an employee group, it may wish to take advantage of slack periods or shut-downs regardless of season. Or your group may decide to head for Europe between November and March, when Europe’s less crowded and accommodations cost less. Or they can enjoy parallel conditions during off-seasons to other destinations.
