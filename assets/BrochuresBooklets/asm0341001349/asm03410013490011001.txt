FREE FARE
For Tour Conductor
Pan American offers free transportation to any group member who organizes and conducts a group of 15 or more persons on a Clipper trip abroad ... half-fare for conducting groups of 10 to 14 persons. Sometimes group conductors book passage direct with Pan American and make all the travel arrangements themselves. But usually they find it easier and more successful to work with an authorized Pan American agent who is experienced in handling such details and can often help the conductor adapt a “pre-packaged” tour for which hotel accommodations, sightseeing and even entertainment have already been arranged. Whoever elects or is appointed to serve as Group Conductor can learn how to qualify for the free transportation from any PA A Representative.
