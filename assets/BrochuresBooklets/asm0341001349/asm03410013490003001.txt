Most groups are formed for some special purpose. People have a mutual interest — something they can do more effectively together — and they enjoy doing it together. The same reasoning applies to travel. You get more out of it — have more fun when you go with a group of congenial companions.
Group travel offers other advantages, too. Often groups can command lower rates for hotel accommodations and sight-seeing. And if a member of the group acts as tour conductor, Pan American offers him (or her) free or half-fare transportation — depending on the size of the group.
If an air trip abroad for your group sounds like too ambitious a project — you won’t think so when you’ve finished this booklet. It tells you just what to do to make your group’s travel dreams come true. It suggests where to go — when to go — how to go. It tells how Pan American helps you at every step of the way — how to plan — how much it will cost — even to suggestions for your Ways and Means Committee.
All over the country the group travel idea has started organizations on the go. Every year, Pan American takes more groups to more places all over the globe. Group travel is fast becoming today’s most popular way to go places, to see things, and enjoy them — together.
