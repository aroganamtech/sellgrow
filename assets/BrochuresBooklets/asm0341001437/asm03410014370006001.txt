We think you’ll learn to love the super soft chair you now occupy. In fact it’s so comfortable you’ll probably want to take it with you when you leave. Sorry we can’t arrange it. But while you’re aboard, please look upon it as your very own. And though our cabin attendants can’t supply you with knitting, or with your favorite slippers, they’ll be glad to bring writing materials, the latest available periodicals and a seat table, for reading or writing comfort. Cozy? What’s more, you’ll find a small light above your head, which focuses directly on your book or writing paper, so you needn’t worry about disturbing your fellow passengers at night.
Another feature, exclusive with Pan American, is Sleeperette service offered on many flights. (Perhaps on this one.) A Sleeperette seat converts at night into a restful, bed-length lounge for perfect slumber. On some flights, both luxurious full-length berths and Sleeperettes are offered, so everybody sleeps.
An important part of your chair is the seat belt, or lap strap, if you will. It’s designed to remind you to remain seated when we’re taking off or landing. An electric sign at the front of the cabin will notify you when it should be fastened — though as a matter of fact, lots of passengers leave their seat belts fastened loosely at all times.
The Inside Story
The interior of your Double-Decked “Strato” Clipper is one place where something has been done about the weather. An air-conditioning system changes the air completely every ninety seconds. And no matter at what altitude you may be flying, there’ll be no altitude fatigue, for your Clipper’s cabin is pressurized, keeping atmospheric pressure at comfortable sea-level height.
