Quartet In Close Harmony
There’s no more perfect sample of coordinated teamwork than that offered by the crew which guides your Double-Decked “Strato” Clipper on her passage. We thought you’d like to meet them, so here goes:
In command is the Captain. He has averaged over 1,500,000 miles in the air, which adds up to plenty of experience. He was a capable pilot before he joined Pan American, but to qualify for a Captain’s rating with us, he put in five more years of training and study.
Next in line is the First Officer. He relieves the Captain at the controls and usually is a rated Captain himself. One of his major duties is to inspect the ship thoroughly before take-off, to make certain all is in readiness and that all cargo has been stowed properly.
The Second Officer is also a qualified pilot. He acts as navigator, and is the man who keeps track of the plane’s position while in flight. As a “stargazer” he has no equal, but it’s duty, rather than beauty, which accounts for his interest in astronomy.
With the wealth of modern radio gear aboard a Clipper the First and Second Officers are able to keep in constant contact with ground stations, ships at sea and other aircraft. They also keep the skipper supplied with the latest weather reports and news flashes for the passengers.
