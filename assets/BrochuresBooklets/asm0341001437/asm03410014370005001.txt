The Flight Engineer is responsible for the supervision of the four mighty 3500 H.P. engines which are whisking you through the skies. He also checks the huge amounts of fuel which are taken aboard. His display of panel instruments, bewildering to the uninitiated, tells him at a glance how the aircraft is performing mechanically.
At Your Service
We’re rather proud of our cabin attendants. And, we believe, with good cause. Naturally, they’ve been well trained, but that extra deftness with which they serve you comes from a combination of experience on many such trips as this, plus a definite liking for people.
They take a real pride in seeing to it that your trip is comfortable and we’ll be surprised if you don’t find them anticipating your wants in a good many cases. Of course, they can’t be expected to read your thoughts every time, so we’ve provided a little call button for you to use. Just press it and one of the Attendants will be at your side in a twinkling.
