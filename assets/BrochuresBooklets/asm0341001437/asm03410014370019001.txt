A good B-I-G — Y-A-W-N! It helps equalize air pressure in the ear passages. Or perhaps chewing a stick of gum provided by the stewardess will do the trick.
As a rule, over-water flying is much smoother than ocean travel or flying over land. Especially up “above the weather” where your Double-Decked “Strato” Clipper cruises. However, we may occasionally encounter strong rising and descending air currents which the plane will follow — just as a ship on the ocean follows the swells and valleys of water. If we do, and you begin to experience any discomfort, just ring for the stewardess. She’ll be glad to offer every possible assistance. Perhaps the new remedy Dramma-mine, about which you’ve probably read, will turn the trick. Every Clipper now has it aboard.
Since Clipper trips are designed for business or pleasure and not as entomological expeditions, all pests of the insect variety are eradicated by the use of “Bug Bombs”. The reason? To prevent the spread of plant pests and communicable diseases. The spray may be a trifle annoying for a moment or so, but the bugs will be much more upset than you. And at any rate, government regulations require it.
