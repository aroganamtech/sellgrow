Matter of fact, it’s possible you won’t leave the plane at all, since a Double-Decked “Strato” Clipper’s hull form makes her very seaworthy. There's a Lot of Thought
In a Rubber Yacht
The best brains in Air-Sea Rescue engineering designed the safety equipment aboard every Clipper. Some really Superman-type stuff has resulted from lessons learned during the war, when fliers made so many water landings it almost got to be habit-forming.
The rubber life rafts are outstanding examples of careful design. They have adjustable sides to shield you from sun and spray. Ample water and food supplies are carried and one of the three rafts has a self-powered radio transmitter with a 2000 mile range. Fishing tackle is provided, too. Resort equipment, wouldn’t you say?
