If your pride and joy is a member of the diaper brigade, we’re prepared for him. We have Baby Kits with disposable diapers and practically everything else you’ll need. Yes, even bassinettes. Of course, we don’t know his formula, but if you’ve brought along a few bottles, we’ll be glad to heat them to the proper temperature at times coinciding with his schedule. Wrist tested, too.
We’re sorry to say that with all the other passengers to look after, we can’t hold the young prince or princess for you, or change diapers, or take over at feeding time. However, he’s perfectly welcome to use the adjoining seat, if the space has not been sold. On the other hand, if you’re sharing a seat with baby, remember that seat belts accommodate only one person. So fasten the belt only around yourself. The baby will enjoy your arms more, anyway.
The Invisible Man
In case you’ve been informed that yours was the last available seat, yet when boarding the plane find the seat next to you unoccupied, please be reassured. As far as we know, there are no ghost riders in the sky. Nor is it a case of bad management. You see, aircraft are licensed to carry a certain amount of weight. And if head winds call for more fuel, or there’s a particularly important batch of mail or cargo to be taken aboard, we have to “make the weight”. And to stay within it, it may be necessary to keep a few seats vacant.
