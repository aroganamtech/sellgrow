A Bite Between Neals
Aboard a Clipper, raiding the refrigerator is quite all right. And the stewardess will even do the raiding for you. Just let her know your needs. Bouillon, sandwiches, cookies, fresh fruit — coffee, hot chocolate, tea or fruit juice. Any time, day or night. And, of course, there’s no charge.
The Land Of Nod
The Sandman has an easy job aboard a Clipper. There’s something in the soothing drone of the engines and the wealth of fresh air that induces sleep even in the most determined insomniacs. Adjust your Sleeperette, snuggle into the pillow and blankets we’ll provide and before you know it, you’ll . . . be . . .you’ll . . . mmmmmmmm. Or if you have a berth, the crisp whiteness of the sheets will be invitation enough to slumber. Whether your accommodation is berth or Sleeperette, it’s a good idea to leave your seat belt fastened loosely, then we won’t have to wake you in case flight conditions get a bit bumpy.
