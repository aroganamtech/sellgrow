Incidentally, the lounge seats only 14 people, and there may be many more than that aboard who’d like to visit it. So...we know you’ll understand.
Mealtime aboard a Clipper is really something. Perhaps it’s the altitude, or all the fresh air, that sends appetites soaring. Or may we flatter ourselves that it’s the tempting food? Prepared on the ground by our own chefs, meals are cooked aloft and served piping hot (or crisp and cool, according to the season), in full course fashion. And, of course, we’ll pick up the check.
Anyone’s liable to pop a button or separate a seam now and then. And the stewardess has a complete sewing kit for such minor repairs. She’ll be glad to do it for you, or you can handle things yourself.
