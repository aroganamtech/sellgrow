Lest You Forget
Please try to remember to take all your belongings with you when you leave the plane. Else your spare spectacles are liable to be winging away over Patagonia before you miss them. If you do leave something behind, notify your nearest Pan American Office and we’ll do our best to see that your property is returned as soon as possible. Though we really can’t be responsible for lost personal articles unless checked with us.
