When Inflation Is Fashionable
There’s a nylon life jacket in the back of the seat ahead of you. Slip it on like a vest, adjusting the straps so they’re snug, but not tight. Tie the top two strings. Cross the bottom at the front and tie around your waist. The life jacket is easy to inflate . . . but wait! Don’t inflate inside the plane, or your suddenly balloon-like proportions will interfere with your movements. Now fasten your seat belt tightly, and await instructions.
