From detailed weather maps, compiled just prior to departure, the Captain was able to plot all the different types of weather conditions likely to be encountered during the flight. And he charted his course accordingly. He took on the proper amount of fuel to meet conditions. And he will fly above bad weather or circle around it. He’ll do everything in his power to make your trip smooth, comfortable and swift.
Of course, weather reports are received constantly en route, too. And before you reach the halfway mark a reanalysis of weather conditions has been radioed to the Captain. These specific reports will determine the Clipper’s course from halfway point to final destination.
