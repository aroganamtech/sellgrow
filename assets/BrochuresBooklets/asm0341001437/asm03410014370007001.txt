You’ll find an ashtray built into the arm of your chair, and you’re welcome to smoke any time we’re aloft except when the No Smoking sign flashes on. But please don’t smoke in your berth. And if you should be stretching your legs outside the aircraft while we’re on the ground, safety regulations call for a distance of fifty feet between ship and smoker.
Just one more thought. No one seems to object to cigarette smoke, but you’d be surprised how many people feel squeamish about the fumes from pipes and cigars. They’d appreciate it if you’d limit yourself to cigarettes.
Cup Of Coffee?
Speaking of cigarettes always makes us think of coffee, the two go so well together. And if you’d like a cup of coffee with your cigarette, don’t hesitate to buzz an attendant and ask for one. It’s always ready and we’re glad to serve you.
