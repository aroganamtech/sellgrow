Well, Here We Are!
The wheels kiss the runway smoothly, the Clipper taxies up in front of the terminal and the drone of the engines dies away.
But don’t be in too much of a hurry to deplane. Chances are, there’ll be some formalities. Perhaps some official of the government in whose territory you have just landed will be coming aboard to welcome you and look over your travel documents. The stewardess or purser will advise you when all is in readiness for you to debark.
