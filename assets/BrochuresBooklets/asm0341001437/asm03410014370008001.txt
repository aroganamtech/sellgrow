Camera-Conscious?
Hope you brought your camera, for you’ll see a multitude of photogenic sights at which to level your lens. And here’s a tip for in-flight photography. Folks who should know say the best way to avoid unwanted reflections is to hold your camera up close to the window, and at right angles to the glass. Since international camera regulations vary, it might be wise to check with the Purser, or personnel at the airport, before you begin taking pictures.
