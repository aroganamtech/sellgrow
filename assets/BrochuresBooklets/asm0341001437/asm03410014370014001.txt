Let's Take A "For Instance"
Naturally, your Clipper Captain will be the first to know about any trouble. And just as naturally, the first thing he will do is radio to alert all agencies that an emergency has been declared. Next, since we believe in letting our passengers know what’s going on, he will inform you even though the situation is far from serious. Just to show you how it would be handled, let’s make believe you have just been told that we’re about to make a water landing.
Aye, Aye, Sir!
The main and most important thing you can do is to obey instructions. All Clipper crews have practiced what to do many times, and your complete cooperation will help make a difficult job easier. You can begin by removing sharp articles such as jewelry, pens, pencils, etc. from your clothing. Gentlemen, loosen your neckties. Ladies, those high heels are rough on rafts. Better slip out of them.
