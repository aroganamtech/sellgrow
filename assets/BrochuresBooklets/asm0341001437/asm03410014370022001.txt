For one thing, this is the biggest and most powerful commercial plane ever constructed, with 40% more power than any other airliner. The four mighty engines combine more horsepower than the largest locomotive ever built.
You knew at first sight that this was a BIG plane, but perhaps the graceful lines deceived you as to its actual size. From wing tip to wing tip she measures 141 feet, 3 inches — from nose to tail, 110 feet, 4 inches. And from airport runway to the top of the tail it’s more than 38 feet.
As far as performance figures go, your Double-Decked “Strato” Clipper was evolved from the famous B-50 of the Army Air Force. And you’ll remember one of those flew non-stop around the world. The Clipper’s cruising speed is approximately 340 m.p.h. and she has an operating range of 4500 miles. Usually she cruises at an altitude between 15,000 and 25,000 feet, but she can get up to 33,000 feet if necessary. And she climbs 1,100 feet per minute.
For your greater comfort, Clippers are completely soundproofed with fiberglass insulation, and multiple heat sources can keep cabin temperatures at 70° even if outside temperatures drop as low as 67° below Zero. Windows are double paned, with warm air circulating between panes to prevent frosting and fogging. And the air-conditioning system actually breathes through the walls of the cabin, changing the air completely every 90 seconds.
Truly, this is the finest, fastest and most comfortable plane in use anywhere in the world today.
