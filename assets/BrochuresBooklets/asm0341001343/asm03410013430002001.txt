PAN AM ORDERS GIANT 747 FOR NEW ERA OF FLIGHT


■ They laughed when Orville and Wilbur Wright sat down to build a mechanical bird: “If God wanted people to fly, He’d have given them wings.” All the guffaws didn't really bug the Wright brothers very much, 1903 being a very big year for laughing at airplane inventors. But the Wrights were annoyed with the contention that only angels, sparrows and butterflies
were entitled to rise above it all and taste the freedom of the skies. That idea really bugged them. So they built themselves an airplane and started something big. From then on whenever somebody said that he could build a bigger, faster or higherflying plane, there was very little laughter.
