The 747 Superjet is not only bigger than the 707 jet but represents a whole new generation of subsonic aircraft. Statistics tell the story: the 707 has a passenger capacity of 146, a cruising altitude of 35,000 ft., and a speed of 575 mph, while the 747 has a passenger capacity of 490, a cruising altitude of 40,000 ft., and a speed of 625 mph. The Superjet will cut flight time between New York and London from six and a half hours to roughly six hours.
