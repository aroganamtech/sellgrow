SAVINGS ON COMMODITY RATES
Up to 50% savings are available on commodity rates. Ask nearest PAA Office or PAA Clipper Cargo Agent.
RATES—Rates are per pound (or fraction), or per 250 cubic inches (or fraction), whichever is greater.
Rate 1—Applies to shipments of less than 100 lbs. Minimum charge varies with points served.
Rate 2—Reduced rate, applies to shipments of 100 lbs. and over.
DIRECTIONAL RATES—Directional rates apply wherever two sets of rates under Rate 1 and Rate 2 are shown. The first listed Rates 1 and Rates 2 apply from (origin) point at the top of column to (destination) point at left of table. Last listed Rates 1 and Rates 2 apply from (origin) point at left of table to (destination) point at top of column.
*—Rate applies via Atlantic Service only, t—Rate applies via Pacific Service only.
C—To determine rates between points marked “C,” combine rates according to routing desired.
NS—No service.
