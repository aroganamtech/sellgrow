HOW TO READ SCHEDULES
Figures appearing at the left of station columns should be READ DOWN while those at right should be READ UP.
THE. 24-HOUR CLOCK IS USED IN THIS TIMETABLE—Times shown between 00:01 (1 minute after midnight) and 12:00 (noon) are a. m. Times shown from 12:01 to 24:00 are p. m. To convert p. m. times to 12-hour clock, subtract 12 hours.
TIME ZONES—Arrival and Departure Times—Times shown in these tables are Meridian Times, in degrees west or east of Greenwich; 15° difference in longitude equals one hour change in time. Meridian time applicable to each station is shown opposite the station name.

PST—Pacific Standard Time (120th Meridian).

PASSENGER INFORMATION
BAGGAGE—Free weight allowance on all Pacific services, 66 pounds (30 kilos); Round-the-World, 88 pounds (40 kilos). Excess baggage charges—x/z of 1% of the normal adult one-way fare per pound (1% per kilo). In addition to their free baggage weight passengers may carry only the following articles: pocket camera, binoculars, umbrella, books and magazines for use during flight; all other articles will be weighed in as baggage.
CANCELLATION & SERVICE CHARGES—If your plans change please cancel your reservation promptly to avoid disappointing others. Failure to cancel will result in a service charge of 25% of the one-way fare to destination to which space has been reserved, or to the first stopover of more than six hours duration shown on ticket. However, such service charge will not exceed $50.00 U. S. Currency.
CHILDREN—One child under two years of age and not occupying a seat will be charged 10% of regular fare when accompanying full fare passenger. Children two to twelve will be charged half fare.
EQUIPMENT —On its Pacific Services, Pan American operates Double-decked “Strato” Clippers . . . also DC-4 type Clippers equipped with exclusive “Sleeperette” chair-lounge seating.
FARES—All fares quoted herein apply from airport to airport and are subject to change without notice. They do not inchide the cost of applicable ticket taxes, ground transportation, or hotel and ground meal expense.
IN-FLIGHT SERVICE—Each Clipper flying the Pacific carries a Purser and Steward or Stewardess to serve you while in flight.
MEALS—Delicious hot meals and light refreshments are served aloft without charge.
OVERNIGHT EXPENSES—Accommodations are provided at company expense at scheduled overnight stops for passengers holding through reservations on the same continuous flight.
PASSPORTS AND VISAS—Passengers must have all valid documents such as permits, passports and/or other certificates, required by the laws of the countries from, into or over which they are to fly. Consult any Pan American office or agency and consuls of the various countries for information.
PERSONAL MAIL—Passengers expecting personal mail, cables or telegrams addressed to them c/o Pan American in various cities en-route are requested to call for them at the Pan American Office.
RESERVATIONS AND PAYMENT—All reservations must be made in advance through authorized travel agents or any office of Pan American World Airways. Deposit or full fare is payable upon confirmation of reservations. In case of deposit, full fare is payable 15 days prior to scheduled departure date.
STOPOVERS—Stopovers may be arranged at scheduled stops at the time you purchase your tickets, except in certain instances.
THROUGH TICKETS AND CONNECTIONS—Any Pan American office or accredited travel agent will gladly assist you in arranging the details of your trip, including connecting transportation to any part of the world.
All fares, rates, charges and schedules shown herein are subject to Official Passenger and Cargo Tariffs and Official Schedules.
