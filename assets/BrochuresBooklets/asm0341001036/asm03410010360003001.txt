Many of the well-known hotels and "guest houses" for which Bermuda is justly famous have remained open throughout the war. Others are now being redecorated and will be open for visitors in the near future. As before the war visitors are assured a wide choice in type and cost of accommodations.
For those who wish to complete their arrangements in advance, all-expense tours of varying length are available.
