NO GOVERNMENT FORMALITIES
All war-time travel restrictions have been removed. No passports, visas, or priorities are required. Just pack your bag and step aboard your waiting Clipper. And don’t forget your golf clubs, fishing tackle, racket, or what-you-will—space is available for excess baggage.
