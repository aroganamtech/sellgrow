Old Spanish cannon still stand guard on the battlements facing the sea at the romantic old port in Campeche. On the bay of the same name, the haunt long ago of pirates who preyed on the vessels.

Campeche was founded in 1540 as a city, and still preserves many reminders of the days of the buccanners, the windjammers and the galleons laden with precious cargo . . .
In addition to its historic and archeologic interest, Campeche is also noted for the variety of fishing in its waters . . Fishing both commercial and sports, and it is the headquarters for expeditions into some of the choicest big-game hunting areas of Mexico . . .
