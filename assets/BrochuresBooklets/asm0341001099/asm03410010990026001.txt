For fun and for health, Tehuacan, in the state of Puebla, and about 75 miles from the city of Puebla, is one of Mexico's favorite resorts. Mineral springs provide enjoyable bathing, with the additional benefit of medicinal qualities. But you don't have to get sick to enjoy this lovely town, with the imposing snow-covered peak of Orizaba in the distance. Relaxation is the keynote of Tehuacan, which has been a mecca for Mexico City residents even long before de Spanish Conquistadores arrived. Several good hotels have been built here, and excellent food is always available.
