You can take an adventurous boat ride up or down the Chiapas River, pass through high-vaulted gorges, and admire the endless variety of tropical vegetation ... You can take camping equipment along for an overnight stay, or you can plan on a day-long picnic ... And there are the ruins of Bonampak, in the recently explored region of the Lacandon Indian tribes, with fresco paintings which have been marvelled at by thousands of visitors.
High above Tuxtla Gutierrez, at an altitude of about 7,000 feet is the fascinating little town of San Cristobal Las Casas, where the greatest variety of native costumes can be seen when the members of a dozen or more tribes gather at the market place from their native pueblos.
