Out beyond the costal waters, the Pacific teems with marlin, sailfish, barracuda, tuna, and giant rayfish. Smaller fish abound in nearby lagoons, secluded inlets and small bays --yellowtails, pompano, mackerel, red snapper, corbina, snook, pargo. Many sheltered coves may be found where the fisherman can cast into the water and get abundant strikes.
Acapulco offers an array of fine beaches, including some for the morning hours and some for the afternoon. Caleta and Coletilla are the favored spots in the morning, and Los Hornos in thé afternoon. And of course, water skiing is one of the big attractions in the placid wafers of the bay ... and there’s the thrill of watching the divers from the high cliffs at La Quebrada, making their death defying leaps over the rocks ...
Many of the excellent hotels also offer bungalow accommodations, with or without housekeeping facilities, so you can enjoy a holiday here in the way you like best. Trained maids and other house servants are always available at reasonable rates ...
For sightseeing, there are boats of all sizes available to take you for cruises along the shore or out into the bay, from where you can get a fine view of the many residences atop the imposing cliffs. And there are glass-bottomed boats so you can watch the marine life in comfort... You’ll enjoy a stroll to the fishermen’s wharf when they bring the catches in during the afternoon, and you II want to look at Fort San Diego, the bastion which protected Acapulco from pirates of long ago .. .
