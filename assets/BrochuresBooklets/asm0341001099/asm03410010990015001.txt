Cuernavaca is less than an hour away over a broad express highway, a thrilling ride of constantly changing eye-filling panoramas, with vegetation running the range from tropical to northern. In this soothing semi-tropical climate you'll find outdoor swimming pools at every hotel and at most residences. You have expert hotel and restaurant men, and top notch night club entertainers, in this setting of repose far --and yet so near-- from the bustle of metropolitan Mexico City. You’ll tour the city, with dazzling bou-gainville vines and bushes meeting your gaze on every side, and you’ll want to stroll for a long time in the famous Borda Gardens, once the summer and week-end palace of Maximilian and Carlota ... Nearby is the newly built resort area around Lake Tequesqui-tengo, where you can fish boat swim and enjoy all aquatic sports any time of the year .. . You can make a side trip to the ancient village of Tepoztlan, which retains its customs, mood and pace that prevailed 400 years ago ... You'll explore the fascinating caves of Cacahuamilpa and soak up health in the sulphur springs swimming pools of Cuautla and Tehuixtla . . . and back in Cuernavaca for the moonlight hours, dining and dancing at resort hotels where world-renowned chefs have found a haven of steady appreciation among thousands of tourists . . .
Cuernavaca also offers for your sight-seeing pleasure the original Palace of Cortez, with the large Diego Rivera murals on the veranda that looks out toward the ring of mountains . . . also the Municipal Garden with its replica of the statute of the famous little boy in Brussels .. . the Pyramid of Teopanzolco and the Salto San Antonio, a lovely waterfall with a tunnel under the rocks so you can stop and rest and have lunch if you've made the mile walk and feel like stretching out a while.
