You’ll be intrigued by the pink-tinge stones that form the facades of the beautifully arcaded buildings in the wind -ing streets.. . the great wall of Campeche and the citadel that looms over the waters . . . its handsome cathedral ... You'll have the feeling of being in a fortress, for the city was provided with special safeguards against invasion from the sea.
The balmy air perfumed with the fragance of thousands of tropical flowering plants will help you relax as you explore the intriguing town built on a series of huge subterranean caves which had been excavated by the early Mayas and used by them as catacombs. Most of the built-up city is up on the main hill which is at the edge of the turquoise waters of the Gulf.
A few miles from the town proper is Lerma, a popular bathing and fishing resort, further down the coast are other relatively unexplored seaside play areas such as Sybo-playa, Altunchen and Champoton. On the Merida highway northeast of Campeche are the famed grottoes, Up the coast to Progreso and beyond are many other beaches suitable for all types of water sports. Among these are Bahia Bonita, San Crisanto, Telchac, Bellamar, Playa de San Miguel, and Dzilam Bravo.
