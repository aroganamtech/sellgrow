If the inner man finds deep satisfaction, there are also many things of the spirit and for never-to-be-forgotten excursions. Puebla is known as "The City of the Angeles'.' Also as the Rome of America, for its great cathedral with a collection of superb old paintings, and for its many churches, some 60 in all. Among the most impressive, along with the main cathedral, is the Santo Domingo church and the Rosay Chapel, built around and old Dominican monastery . .. and the State Museum . . . the public market.. . the "Secret Convent" ... and the many shops where the renowned Spanish Talavera dinnerware is made . . .
Nearby is the village of Cholula with its still-unexcavated pyramid and its numerous churches (160), including the famed Ca-pilla de los Reyes with 64 columns and 49 domes ... another brief excursion takes you to the ancient town of Tlaxcala, the Ocotlan Sanctuary, the San Francisco church, and the ruins of San Esteban Tizatlan . .. At Tlaxcala and Santa Ana you’ll find some fine hand-wover woolen fabrics and rugs you’ll treasure forever... along with the beautiful China Poblana costumes you’ll find irresistible ...
