Sparkling Guadalajara, sometimes known as "The Pearl of the West" is a city you'll always treasure among the memories of your Mexican holiday ... Mexico’s second largest city, it is the Metropolis of the Pacific region, and renowned as one of the most hospitable cities in all the world, and boasting one of the finest climates . . .
The pleasant quality of the city and its inhabitants is reflected in all the splendid accommodations for visitors, ranging from the best restaurants to ultra-modern hotels and recreation facilities ... under the bluest of blue skies the year round.
