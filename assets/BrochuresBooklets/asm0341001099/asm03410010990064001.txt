Although basically an important industrial city, Monterrey has made a special effort to build itself also as a tourist city and the result is an array of the finest accommodations in lodgings, eating places, bars and night clubs. Local sightseeing includes the main cathedral, the Roble basilica, the city hall, the Bishop's Palace on a hill overlooking the city, which is dominated in another direction by the famous Sadi-dleback Mountain towering in the distances, the country club, and the big brewery, which makes a feature of tourist tours of the plant, with ample refreshments on the house . ..
Side trips include the Chipinque Mesa, the Huasteca Canyon, and the Garcia caves, all rewarding for the lover of nature. The caves are among the largest in America, and include several ponds and underground streams. Lights and steps have been installed to make the going easier. A de-lighful horseback ride leads to the caves from the spot where the automobile road ends . . .
