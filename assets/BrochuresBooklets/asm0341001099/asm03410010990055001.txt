Merida, a city known for its cleanliness and hospitality, offers the vacationer excellent resort factlities, including sparkling swimming pools and fine hotels, and structures reminiscent of Old Spain.
The markets of Merida are noted for their beauty and extreme cleanliness. They offer gold and silver filigree, henequén articles, objects made o f coral, hand-embroidered blouses and skirts, alligator bags, alligator hide shoes, turtle shell and mother-of-pearl jewelry . . .
Merida is only a 30-minute automobile ride from the coast, so you may also enjoy a variety of seaside recreation .. .
Of course, your visit to Merida will not be complete without a visit to the famous ruins of Uxmal and Chichen-ltza. You can make a comfortable base in Merida, which offers ultramodern hotel accommodations, complete with the finest cuisine, bar service and with swimming pools on the hotel grounds.
You’ll want to look on the Cenotes, the underground water caves, formed into pools of water that filled a natural formation after flowing in from an underground river. One of the best known is the cavern at Mucuyche, fifty feet deep and containing crystalline formations of great beauty, amid which the Ton and other curious birds make their nests . . .
