Mysterious hieroglyphics cover many of the structures .. . brilliant mosaic walls in honor of the queens and kings who reigned long before Europeans touched the hospitable soil of Mexico . .. And on the way to Mitla you'll pass more eld churches ... the Tlacolula market... the giant cypress tree at fhe point known as Santa Maria del Tule, a tree which measures about 150 feet around the trunk and 150 feet high, and estimated to be at least 400 years old . ..
Oaxaca state is also famous historically as the home of two great Mexicans --Benito Juarez and Porfirio Diaz... and of the legendary church bell which was tried for heresy hundreds of years ago and condemned to be struck forever . . .
Near the main square of Oaxaca City is the Juarez market, where gaily-dressed vendors offer you a great variety of things to eat drink and wear. You will see on display many of the handicrafts of pottery, jewelry and fabrics for which the region is famous . .. you can see these things being made at various workshops . .. And on Saturday nights you’ll want to witness the old Indian Feather Dance which is re-enacted as it was done, in full costume, by the pre Conquest tribes . . .
Nearby San Felipe village will intrigue you for a halfday holiday... at your hotel you can enjoy swimming, tennis, golf and other modern sports . .. While you relax in the cool of the fragant evenings, you'll hear the haunting melodies of the marimbas at the serenata of the plaza . . .
