Puerto Vallarta snuggles up against a mountain range covered with tropical vegetation: bananas, cocoanut palms, papaya, chicle trees, and tobacco plantations. Along one side are the bay and sea wall, just a step away from your hotel, and in the other direction the picturesque village climbs upwards, multi-colored roof tiles dotting the verdant hillsides.
In this semi-landlocked harbor with placid emeraid waters, endless hours of fun and relaxation await -- water and land recreation, surf bathing, shore and bay fishing, and out beyond the 30-mile bay all kinds of deepsea game fishing . . . There’s hunting in the hills around the town and bay. .. horseback riding through jungle trails dotted with cascades and waterfalls spilling into the sea ... picnics . . . boatrides ... dancing at night on the moonlight pavilion . . . and budgetwise, you’ll find Puerto Vallarta way below most other places with comparable facilities ...
