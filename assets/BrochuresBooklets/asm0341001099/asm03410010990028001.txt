Modern steamers linking Veracruz with all the world have taken the place of galleons of old, but ancient buildings still recall the Spanish Main.
Mexico city with its tropical pace will engulf you. Mardi Gras comes but once a year, but somehow the gaiety of that season lingers through the perfumed nights of this historic spot...
History romance and glamor are in every stone of the city’s buildings and walks ... The tang of the Seven Seas and a touch of the Old World and the Orient. You can't miss it as you relax with a mint-julep or savor a superb seafood meal at a sideawalk cafe under the outdoor colonnades ... And you can dream of the days gone by when pirates of the Spanish Main, including the dreaded Lauren-cillo, used to make his headquarters here ...
