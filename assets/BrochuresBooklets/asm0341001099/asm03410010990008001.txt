The great world metropolis of Mexico City is an experience of a lifetime. You'll be caught in its magic whirl as soon as you set foot on the historic ground now graced by one of the largest and most striking airports in the world. A gem of modern functional architecture ... Taxis await to take you to town. Today Mexico City boasts an array of magnificent hotels, reflecting the very finest in accommodations and service. There are at least a dozen hostelries that are classed as luxury types. Here, in ultramodern buildings designed for your every comfort, your dream vacation gets into full swing. All offer prompt and complete room service. As soon as you're in the mood, there is the array of attractions that only a world capital like Mexico City --Paris of the Americas-- can bring you.
