Your own private hammock awaits you, along with a glorious year-round spring climate and refreshing breezes .. . in picturesque, tidy, Spanish-Mayan Merida, capital of the state of Yucatan. The peninsula that juts out into the Gulf of Mexico towards Cuba . . . In this world'famous "White City", of sparkling whiteness and hundreds of windmills, you’ll find Spanish-Moorish and ancient Mayan architecture merged to create a lovely and unique landscape . . .
Yucatan is only a few hours away by plane and is completely different in ethnics and economics from the rest of Mexico. The winters are those of the tropical Caribbean, six months of endless springtime and completely dry weather. The summer season, on into October, is marked by torrential rains which seep down to form underground rivers and wells that are characteristic of this area.
