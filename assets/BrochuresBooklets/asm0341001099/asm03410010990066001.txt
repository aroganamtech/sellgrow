The resort and oil port of Tampico is the metropolis of a vast region along the Gulf Coast of Mexico, known as the Huasteca , which includes some of the finest fishing and hunting regions in the Western Hemisphere. Tampico is a short flight from the U.S. border via Pan American world Airways planes, from there you can plan a satisfying vacation inland or up the coast.. .
North of Tampico are the famous series of passes or inlets, already known to anglers all over the U.S., and fish from all over the Atlantic coast seek shelter in these protected areas.
Tampico on the Gulf of Mexico is an important port and oil center but of greater interest to the vacationer are the marvelous fishing grounds up and down the coast, and the beaches, the flowering jungles, and hunting.
