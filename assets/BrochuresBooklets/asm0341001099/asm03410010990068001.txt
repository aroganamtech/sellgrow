La Barra beach is located near Tuxpan, the Veracruz town, a short distance from Tampico. The Tuxpan River, like the Panu co, offer boating, swimming and fishing, as well as a casino-type yatch club and attractive resort hotels ... The beach is covered with all kinds of shells and driftwood, souvenirs of your Tampico visit that you will treasure ...
Farther down the coost is Tecolutla, becoming more popular as a resort every day. .. It is right on the Gulf and boasts superb hotel accommodations .. . Nearby is a great vanillagrowing region, peopled by the Totonac Indians who still reenact the famous Volador or Flying Men ceremony and dance in exotic costumes, which alone is worth the trip here ... Near Papantla, the Volador city, is El Tajin, an ancient Totonac pyramid and other ruins, with fascinating sculptures and carvings.
