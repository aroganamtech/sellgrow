A 50-mile paved road from Merida takes you to the ruins of Uxmal comfortably through the facilities of daily excursions organized by a local agency . . . The tour may be made independently by bus, also, if you prefer . .. One full day here will give you a pretty good picture of the life that once thrived here . . . There are the House of the Magician, a pyramid within a pyramid . . . the House of the Nuns . . . The Governor's House . . . the House of the Turtles . . . Temple of the Prophet. . . House of the Doves ... a ball court. .. The Temple of the Fertility Symbols . . . Many hours of enchanting browsing among the precision-built structures and the exquisite art work of the vanished Mayans . . .
Land of the living past--Yucatan-a few short hours from Mexico City by CMA plane. Here are famed Chi-chen-ltza and Uxmal with their ancient temples and other splendors of the Mayas.
