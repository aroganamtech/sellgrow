Guanajuato
The hills and mountains of Guanajuato have been famed for centuries and they still retain their ancient charm. The tourist easily understands how San Miguel de Allende became an art colony.
One of the world’s famous art colonies is located at San Miguel Allende, a fascinating hilly cobblestone-street town located a short drive from Queretaro.
