The entrancing state of Michoacan, a gem of native beauty, is easily reached from Mexico City or Guadalajara, and is one of the most rewarding excursions to be made . . .
The gardens of Morelia, the state capital, are famous everywhere, and besides there are the museums, the cathedral, the school of fine arts, and the dignified University of San Nicolas, one of the oldest schools of higher learning in the Western Hemisphere ... also the old Colonia aqueduct, the government palace built in 1732, the San Francisco church completed in 1610, the Carmelite church of the 16th century, the municipal palace, the home of General Jose Maria Morelos y Pavon, one of the Independence leaders, and the fascinating state museum . ..
Morelia is a city of flowers and orchards and many birds, of varied hue and song, and in the setting are several excellent hotels . ..
