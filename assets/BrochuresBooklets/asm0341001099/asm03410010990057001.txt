Best known of the areas near Merida is Chichen Itza, which dates to about 400 A.D., and which later joined Uxmal and Mayapan to form a league of three tribal nations .. . During the period, shortly prior to the arrival of the Spaniards, many of the Mayas fled to Guatemala . . . The cities were deserted and soon engulfed by the jungles, which in recent years have be giving back these hidden treasures ..
Like Chichen-ltza, Uxmal contains many imposing buildings in various states of ruin, including the House of the Governor, the Vestal Virgins House of the Doves, House of Turtles and others.
