A big thrill for all Oaxaca visitors is the Santo Domingo church and the monastery adjoining. The building is imposing from the outside, but surprise after surprise await youas you gaze at the dazzling decorations inside,many made of pure gold leaf... then you'll visit the archeology museum, teeming with precious objects found during excavations of the old Zapotec and Mixtec pyramids and burial grounds. . . jewelry of solid gold, of amber and obsidian, jade, crystal, pearls . . . They are there by the hundreds, and of course, the best known of the collections is the fabulous assortment known as the Monte Alban jewels .. .
After you see this collection you’ll want to make the trip to the site of the Monte Alban and Mitla ruins . ..
