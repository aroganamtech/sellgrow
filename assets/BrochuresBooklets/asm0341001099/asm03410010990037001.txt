A ring of mountains provide an eye-filling setting for the man-made beauty of Queretaro, within easy reach of Mexico City by excellent highways and railways.
A fascinating Colonial city in the heartland of Central Mexico, Queretaro has the charm of the Conquistador days along with the comforts of good hotels, swimming pools, tennis and pingpong courts . ..
