The flavor of Colonial Mexico is retained in many public buildings set amid luxuriant trees, flowers and shrubs, bringing back storybook memories of gay cavalier and lovely ladies.
Fifty miles beyond, on the rood to Acapulco, is quaint and fascinating Taxco, which the government has maintained as a Colonial monument. Because of that, none of its original charm has faded as modern conveniences have been added. It is one of the world famous art colonies, a joy and a delight for painters, sculptors and photographers, and for all those who enjoy its restful beauty, its cobblestone-covered hilly streets, reminiscent of a Renaissance village of Old Italy . . .
