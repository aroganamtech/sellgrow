You'll spend joyous hours along the broad paved streets lined with intriguing structures ... The Cathedral, for example, built the latter part of the 16th century, with simplicity of style, yet majestic in total effect. Inside there are several life-size figures of Christ, a marble floor, and a series of fourteen polychrome paintings ... The Museo Yucateco, with a cross section of early Mayan art. Figures of the God Chac-Mool, the jaguar, monoliths, columns with hieroglyphs, many depicting Kukulcan, the plumed serpent of the Mayas, weapons, ornaments of jade and gold . . .
