The boulevard along the waterfront, lined with rustling palm trees, is one of many attractions in the tropical Gulf port of Veracruz where pirates once held sway.
A trip to the Isla de los Sacrificios is an enjoyable excursion at Veracruz. This "island of the Sacrifices" can be reached by boat and is said to have been used for exotic religious rites by the natives before the Spanish Conquest ... You’ll want to visit the old San Juan de Ulua Castle, the ocean bastion that has guarded the coast for centuries and which served as a dungeon for a long time. Another worthwhile excursion in the Veracruz vicinity is a drive to Cempoala, where Toltecs built pyramids about 500 A. D . . .
After you are wrapped in the spell of Veracruz for a few days, you’ll begin to understand why Cortez decided to burn his ships and remain on this beautiful shore of Mexico . . .
