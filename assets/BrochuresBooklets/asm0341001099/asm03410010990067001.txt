A great place for fishermen and a major Mexican port, Tampico on the Glorious Gulf draws tourists the year around
The result is year-round fabulous sport, the fish literally fighting for any bait cast into the water by the surf anglers. Besides, there is deepsea game fishing farther out in the Gulf, equal to that of Veracruz, Acapulco, Mazatlan and Puerto Vallarta.
"Like eating in Tampico" has become a byword in Mexico, for the abundance of fresh and varied seafood and the produce of one of the richest agricultural zones in the country are available here at the fine restaurant and hotel dinning rooms .. . along with a steady supply of wild game from the vast forests and jungles of the Huasteca region ...
Ships from all over the world dock at Tampico’s harbor, and the city is the center of Mexico’s great oil production and refining industry . . . but for the tourist, life here is lazy, and there are plenty of places to indulge your yen for relaxation and recreation . .. Excellent beaches are found all along the coast line, and two favorites close to Tampico are La Barra and Miramar .. .
Lagoons north, south and west of Tampico provide a great variety of fishing. In the Gulf the famous Tampico tarpon abound, averaging 70 to 80 pounds, and fighters from the word "go". Anglers from all over the United States and Mexico converge here each year for the annual tarpon rodeo . . . In
