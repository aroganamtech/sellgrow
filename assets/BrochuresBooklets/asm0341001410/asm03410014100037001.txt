Pan American pioneered the world’s first tourist-class service between New York and San Juan in 1948, and the service was soon extended to South America, to Bermuda, and finally to Atlantic and Pacific points. Since air tourist fares are well within the reach of the average person, he can now spend his two or three weeks’ vacation in the Old World countries of Western Europe, in the New World countries of Latin America, or in Hawaii. By 1954 he will be able to fly to the Far East on Tourist services. 555 The Pan American story is still being written. The opportunities for developing with the Company are greater than ever. Today, jet Clippers are under construction for delivery in 1956 capable of carrying 75 passengers at cruising speeds of 500 miles per hour and at altitudes of 42,000 feet. When these new jet airliners go into service, they will cut travel time nearly in half on the routes on which they are operated.
