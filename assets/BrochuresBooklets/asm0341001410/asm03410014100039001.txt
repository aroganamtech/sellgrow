Communications energizes the nerve system of the entire Pan American body. Pan American has the only round-the-world radio-telephone system which permits contact between pilot and ground radio operators by voice. Pan American now has constant twenty-four hour communication with all its vast fleet of Clippers in the air. On the ground, Communications links the world-wide network of Pan American’s offices and stations. 5 J 5 Dispatch . . . Dispatch plans and monitors flight in conjunction with the captains of the Clippers. This section calculates the amount of fuel to load for any given flight, how many passengers and how much cargo can be carried in relation to fuel and weather conditions. It suggests the route and stops of the flight, keeps a watch on all flights, mapping their courses and keeping the captains advised of the wind and
