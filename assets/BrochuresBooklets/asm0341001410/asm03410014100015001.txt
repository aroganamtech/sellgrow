He can help you to help yourself. 5 5 5 Hours of Work — Regular work schedules for ground personnel are generally based on a standard work day and work week but at times your duties may call for irregular schedules. Our flights must operate 24 hours per day, seven days per week. So in staffing the flights working hours must vary. Government regulations, Company policy and Union agreements cover the precise terms of pay for extra hours of work.
5 5 5 Your Pay — You will have opportunity to grow with your job. As you grow in service and usefulness you will be rewarded accordingly. 555 The Company seeks to keep compensation, all factors considered, commensurate with airline industry levels. Rates for many jobs are fixed through agreements with Unions which our employees have chosen. Wage and salary administration for non-contract and management personnel is based upon up-to-date standard principles of job evaluation. 5 5 5 You will receive your pay check at regular intervals determined by your job.
