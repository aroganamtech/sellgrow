Pan American’s maintenance base in Miami is the largest privately owned one in the world. Engines are completely taken down and rebuilt here in an assembly line procedure which can be compared favorably with the modern factory. Other large bases are located in San Francisco and New York City. 555 Maintenance of an aircraft includes not just the power plants but thousands of delicate instruments, electronic and mechanical devices, and servicing of this type of equipment requires the highest of skills and training. 5 5 5 Medical. . . Doctors and nurses are employed at the Company’s main bases and at some other stations to conduct the periodic physical examinations for flight crew members, give the necessary inoculations to employees before they leave for foreign travel and treat employees taken ill or injured on the job. 555 Office and Clerical Services . . . Office and Clerical Services enable the other departments to function.
