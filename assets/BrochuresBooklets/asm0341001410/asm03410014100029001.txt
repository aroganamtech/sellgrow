Service Insignia — Length of service in Pan American determines the type of Service Pin to which you will be entitled. We trust that you will wear yours with pride. Upon employment you will receive a silver insignia. After three years you will receive a gold one. Thereafter, stars are added for each five years of service. 5 5 5 Job Security — We are as interested in the security of your job as we are in the future success of the Company. The two go together. If we all do our part well, a successful future is assured for the Company. In turn, the Company’s success is the best protection for the job security of all of us. 5 5 5 Promotion Opportunity — Promotion from within is Pan American’s policy. If a promotion opportunity occurs for which you are fitted and
have adequate seniority, you’ll be promoted into the job. You can help yourself by extending your knowledge and skill so you will always be ready for that next higher job.
