They assist in the employment of all personnel, in the establishing of training programs as required and in matters pertaining to promotion. They maintain all personnel records and advise management concerning wage and salary administration, job evaluation and classification. It is their concern to assist in the conduct of benefit plans and social and recreational activities. It is their concern also to negotiate and interpret union agreements and to ensure that the administration of these contracts is a true expression of the Company’s personnel policy. 5 5 5 Public Relations . . . Public Relations promotes and preserves the Company’s good name with the public. Public Relations assists sales in promoting the use of the Company’s services and counsels all departments as to probable public reaction to future programs. It is through this department that all information about the Company is funneled to the press. 5 5 5 Scheduling . . . Scheduling plans the

movements of the crews and aircraft to the right places at the right time. It is the duty of scheduling to see to it that each flight has a full crew complement, both operating and service. 5 5 5 Services of Supply . . .
