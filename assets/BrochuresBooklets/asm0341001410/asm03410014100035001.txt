Pan American Airways Africa, Ltd., operated the first Military Transport Airlift under the U. S. flag abroad. Clippers rushed military cargoes to combat areas overseas. Air bases were built on remote islands in the North and South Atlantic, and at strategic points in Africa and Asia. The Hump was successfully flown, linking beleaguered China with her allies. 5 5 5 After World War II, there remained the task of girdling the globe with scheduled air transportation. Again, the honor fell to an American plane, with an American crew, when PAA’s Clipper America took off eastbound from New York on June 17, 1947, to inaugurate the first round-the-world civil air service. 5 5 5 After the war, also, there remained the task of making long-distance international air travel a reality for the average man.
