Pan American has purposely made its qualifications so high that none but the best will ever handle the controls of a Clipper. 5 5 5 Flight Service and Commissary . . . Flight service sees to the passenger’s needs. A flight service attendant is a combination maitre d’hotel, cook, bartender, baby sitter, diplomat and host to the passengers aboard any Pan American flight. Flight attendants also handle the multiple paper work necessary for an international flight. 5 5 5 Commissary cooks the food and supplies the beverages for the passengers.
