In general ground employees earn two weeks’ paid vacation annually — three
weeks’ after ten years of service. Rules covering vacation schedules are established by your Union agreement or Company policy bulletins. Preference as to choice of vacation time depends upon seniority and upon the workload. Discuss your preference with your supervisor. 5 5 5 Holidays — Holidays vary according to the country where you are employed, but in the United States they will be New Year’s Day, Washington’s Birthday, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas. 5 5 5 Travel Opportunities — After you have been with us a year, you are allowed — except during peak seasons — an 85% discount for vacation travel along any of Pan American’s world wide routes . . . even all the way around the world. This travel privilege is extended to your dependents as your years with PAA increase. Your supervisor will be happy to explain the details. 5 5 5 At almost any time after your probationary period, you and your dependents are entitled to 50% discount on Pan American fares.
