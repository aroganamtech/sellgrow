Our Ways of Work. . . Pan American policies toward employees are very definite and of long standing. Our beliefs can be summarized as follows: 1. We believe people to be our greatest asset. 2. Most individuals want to contribute in the best way they can to a worthwhile, constructive enterprise. 3. Personnel practices, particularly those affecting promotion and discharge, must be fair and equitable to all concerned, including other employees, stockholders and customers. 4. All matters affecting employee relations should be fully and freely explained. The employees’ points of view and attitudes should be constantly known to management. 5.
