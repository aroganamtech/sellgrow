Services of Supply is a global quartermaster corps with a responsibility as varied and widespread as similar services in the Armed Forces. 5 5 5 Station Traffic . . . Station Traffic personnel take care of the passengers and cargo on the ground, weigh in the passenger’s baggage, check his tickets and travel documents, assign him a seat on the Clipper and see to his wants and needs before take-off. Passenger service representatives take care of the passenger on arrival at his destination. They see him through immigration and customs formalities, help him with problems encountered in a new country and assist in forward travel plans.
The Cargo group handles everything from heavy machinery to ladies’ hats, and their “passengers” range from canaries to elephants. 5 5 5 Traffic and Sales . . . Traffic and Sales sells the Company’s services and sets the prices at which they will be sold.
