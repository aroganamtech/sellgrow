These may vary in some parts of the world but here are the most important of them which apply most uniformly: 5 ? ? Your Supervisor — From the day of your arrival when he introduces you to your associates and helps you learn your duties, it is the job of your supervisor to be your friend and counselor. Go to him with your questions or your problems. You are both on the same team. It is his business to assist you to become a competent and happy worker. 5 5 5 Your supervisor can help you not only on business questions, but perhaps on personal problems, as he is your first point of contact. At the larger locations, for example, he
can guide you, if needed, to the Accounting Office regarding any questions on pay, to the Medical Office in the event of sickness or accident, to the Credit Union if you require some personal financing or to the Industrial Relations Department or
Personnel Office if you want to inquire about promotion or transfer opportunities.
