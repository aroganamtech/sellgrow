Employee Benefits
One of the primary rewards derived from working for Pan Am, beyond the satisfaction that comes from performing a necessary service in a thoroughly professional way, is travel throughout the world.
While all employees do not in the course of performing their assigned duties for Pan Am have the opportunity for travel, each employee and any member of the employee’s immediate family qualify for travel to any location along the Pan Am route system at substantial discounts.
Pan Am offers its employees a broad range of travel benefits. An employee qualifies for travel at a discount after three months with the Company, with the benefits increasing as length of service with Pan Am grows. After only three months, for example, an employee may travel at 80 per cent discount, and after a year can travel with a wife or parent at up to 90 per cent discount. Long time employees qualify for unlimited free travel, while retired employees are eligible for unlimited travel at nominal cost.
In addition, through reciprocal arrangements with other airlines, Pan Am employees qualify for reduced fares on other carriers, with little or no restrictions on the frequency of use.
Hotels and other travel oriented businesses frequently offer attractive discount savings.
Pan Am also offers an employee stock purchase plan in which shares of Pan Am stock are available for purchase through payroll deductions at prices below market levels.
The Company has a well rounded Group Insurance program including liberal health, disability, accident and life insurance benefits. Also available is an excellent retirement plan.
Each new employee should explore the full details of these benefits upon starting his career at Pan Am.
