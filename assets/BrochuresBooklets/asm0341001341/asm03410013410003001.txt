Foreword
A career in air transportation is uniquely suited to our times. For air transport is people oriented; that is, it is responsive to the desires and needs of individuals.
It does not deal in faceless statistics. It does not work with impersonal masses. It does not shuffle insensitive commodities. It deals with people, face to face; warm, anxious, happy, troubled, jovial, nervous, jocular, hard pressed people; each different, each with a unique dream or desire, each with an individual need to be served.
That doesn’t make our job easy. But easy jobs aren’t rewarding. And working with people, in a vital and helpful way, is the most rewarding kind of work you can get.
If you want a job that challenges your abilities, taxes your resources, but stimulates your growth and development, then a career in air transport is for you.
We cannot promise a comfortable routine, for our job is in finding new and better ways to serve our customers. And these new ways are always being developed and refined. But we can promise that you will not be stifled and that your rewards will come not only in the response of the persons you serve, but from a company that is founded upon the principles of innovation, public service, and pioneering effort.
NAJEEB E. HALABY PRESIDENT
