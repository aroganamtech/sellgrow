A Word About Pan Am
Pan Am is the leader in an industry that is undergoing its second major revolution in a decade.
A dozen years ago, Pan Am led the airlines of the world in the transition into the first successful jet powered passenger transport to operate on the major trade routes of the world. Now, in the decade of the Seventies, Pan Am is again the leader of the air transport industry in introducing the wide-bodied, comfortable and efficient air transport vehicle of the future: the 747.
This era, which will be followed by expansion into supersonic air transport, poses a vast opportunity to Pan Am and its employees.
There are broad horizons in administration, financial and economic planning, sales management and marketing, cus-
tomer services, flight line ground operations, aircraft maintenance, and data processing.
Pan Am is the world’s most experienced airline and has unceasingly served the public and the nation’s interest since 1927. Today, the company’s airline routes extend over 81,000 miles, serve 94 cities in 80 lands from 16 cities in thé continental United States, one in Alaska, two in Hawaii, and six locations in U.S. territories and possessions.
In addition to its airline operations, Pan Am operates airports and heliports; markets and services business jet aircraft; develops and operates hotels throughout the world; and contributes essential support services to the United States space exploration program.
