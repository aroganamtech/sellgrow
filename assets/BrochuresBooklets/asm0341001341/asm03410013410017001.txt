Airport Customer Service
A desire for direct, person-to-person contact with the traveling public is the mark of an Airport Customer Service representative, whose work entails the critical task of processing passengers, baggage, cargo and mail through terminals and onto and off aircraft.
To expedite this process, well defined and standardized procedures have been devised to carry out this processing in an efficient and expeditious manner. These procedures cover the review and processing of passenger documents, tickets, cargo, air waybills, and in providing information about travel and shipping to the public.
To prepare Passenger and Cargo Customer Service representatives for their assignments as well as to keep them abreast of developments in their areas, Pan Am maintains a variety of schools that develop and sustain proficiency in customer service techniques.
Applicants for a position in this field are considered if they have two years of college or equivalent experience, fluency in a foreign language, and certain other related skills.
Advancements based upon a combination of ability and seniority is provided through a series of ascending levels of responsibility which brings commensurate increases in pay. Supervisory and management positions in this field are filled by advancement.
Some opportunities exist for especially well qualified candidates, preferably those who have completed their military obligations. Such applicants should be knowledgeable in one or more foreign languages, personable, mature and well groomed, display a demonstrated ability for continuous productive work, sound health and a desire to render personal service of a high calibre.
