Management Associate Program
A select number of qualified candidates will be invited each year to enter Pan Am’s Management Associate Program. This is a broad-based program designed to give an applicant a full and well rounded view of the entire corporation in preparation for jobs of greater responsibility in higher levels of management.
Initially, all applicants will be given a twelve week orientation course exposing the full range of Pan Am’s activities, what responsibilities these job categories entail, and the type and kind of work that is performed by the several departments within the Company.
Following this three month introductory session, a four
week project assignment will be made in the applicant’s area of career interest. A description of these opportunities, i.e. Sales, Airport Customer Service, Ground Operations, etc., is described in detail on subsequent pages.
Following this specific project assignment, the Management Associate will be assigned to his area of chosen interest and may well receive additional, more specialized training in that area.
Additionally, it should be noted that the Management Associate Program should be considered as a flexible one which will be administered to meet individual needs. It will be altered according to the dictates of experience.
