Other Opportunities
In addition to the specific programs outlined in this booklet, Pan Am offers many other rewarding career opportunities to college graduates.
The fascinating business of operating an international airline requires management talent and specialists in many areas. To meet the challenges of rapid growth and intensive competition, tomorrow’s corporate leaders are being trained today. Many departments—purchasing, advertising, communications, supply, engineering, and personnel, to name a few—offer challenging careers to qualified men and women.
Pan Am offers liberal employee benefits which include worldwide travel at substantial discounts, comprehensive insurance and retirement coverage, a tuition refund program, as well as a stock purchase plan.
As an Equal Opportunity Employer, Pan Am actively seeks qualified candidates from all population groups.
When writing to Pan Am, enclose a resume of your education, employment experience, military obligations or service record, and extra-curricular activities, and mail it to the Director, Personnel Administration, Pan American World Airways, Pan Am Building, New York, N.Y. 10017.
