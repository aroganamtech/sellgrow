Data Reduction Services
Pan Am’s Data Reduction Services are conducted in a department where all data processing activities in the Company are centralized. The operations of this department include the functions of revenue and expense accounting, inventory, payroll, sales, flight and operational statistics, engineering studies, and passenger, cargo and hotel reservations.
To ensure the economic and effective development and performance of electric information processing, close coordination is maintained with all Company offices and de-
partments requiring Data Reduction Services.
To qualify for this field, candidates should have a specialized knowledge in economics, mathematics, statistics, or accounting. It is also desired that these candidates have some basic knowledge in the capabilities and programming of electronic data processing equipment. After a training period, qualified candidates are eligible to advance to the more challenging fields of system design, system analysis, and operations research.
