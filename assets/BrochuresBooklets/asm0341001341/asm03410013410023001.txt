Finance and Economie Planning
Pan Am has a variety of openings from time to time in the Finance and Economic Planning areas for men and women with MBA’s or other advanced degrees or equivalent background gained through working experience. Assignment areas include:
FINANCIAL ANALYSTS deal with corporate financial matters including capital budgeting and planning, facilities financing, special financial projects, or treasury work.
BUDGET ANALYSTS prepare and analyze annual Corporate and Departmental Budgets and evaluate variable budget parameters.
ROUTE ANALYSTS study current economic results and proposed new routes and schedules, forecast traffic and make profitability studies within assigned geographic areas.
ECONOMIC ANALYSTS are involved with flight costing, fleet analysis, long-range profit planning, and special economics studies.
SYSTEMS ANALYSTS develop management information
systems, perform operations research, and devise related systems applications.
ROUTE PROCEEDINGS ANALYSTS deal with the development of financial and traffic exhibits, testimony and rebuttals pertinent to route proceedings before the Civil Aeronautics Board.
Each of these positions offer exceptional challenge and give a broad perspective into all aspects of airline management. Analysts are regularly promoted to management assignments — often across departmental lines.
There are no formal training programs; all positions involve immediate productive assignments. Most positions are located at the Company Headquarters, Pan Am Building, New York City.
Requirements include the ability to make analytical judgments using quantitative disciplines; the ability to make articulate oral and written presentations; and the motivation to contribute to the goals of the organization through creative research and imaginative problem solving.
