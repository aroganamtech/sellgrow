Airport Services
Pan Am has personnel assigned to some 130 airport locations in the United States and overseas with a responsibility for directing aircraft movements and replenishing supplies for Pan Am Clippers while they are on the ground.
Intelligent, well educated, neat and mature candidates may qualify for these positions, which begin at first-level supervisor. An aptitude for general management skills and recognized leadership qualities are necessary. Military obligations must be completed.
A trainee begins an on-the-job training program under the tutelage of a supervisor. In addition, he receives classroom instruction in a variety of courses that will assist him in the handling of an aircraft on the ground and in the manage-
merit of related ramp and loading operations.
Following appointment as a supervisor, a person’s advancement will depend upon his effectiveness in performing his job and in his demonstrated ability to perform in positions of greater responsibility as the opportunities present themselves.
Ability to speak a foreign language is a valued asset, and an applicant must be willing to relocate, initially to a city within the United States that is served by Pan Am, and later for possible overseas assignment.
Ground Operations Supervisors engage in shift work, and satisfactory completion of a company physical examination is required.
