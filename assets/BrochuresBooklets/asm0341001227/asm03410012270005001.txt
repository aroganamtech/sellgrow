1ST DAY. SATURDAY: MAINLAND/HONOLULU. Depart by Pan Am Jet Clipper for Honolulu.
Receive the traditional lei greeting on arrival and transfer to your Waikiki hotel. There you can settle down and start to become Hawaiian.
2ND DAY. SUNDAY: HONOLULU.
Welcome get-together and briefing. City tour.
Enjoy a special briefing where your Pan Am’s World Host will explain the Islands, its people and the many things to do during your stay. Depart on the city tour where you’ll see Iolani Palace, Punchbowl, University of Hawaii and many other sights.
3RD DAY. MONDAY: HONOLULU.
Free day.
Revel in Honolulu. Get to know this exciting city.
4TH DAY. TUESDAY: HONOLULU/MAUI. Morning flight to Maui. Sightseeing on arrival.
Evening free.
On arrival, you begin touring the pretty Iao Valley, and on through the ancient capital of the Islands, Lahaina, also an old whaling port. Arrive and check-in at your hotel.
5TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: MAUI.
At leisure.
Enjoy your last day in Maui doing whatever you choose: golfing, shopping or relaxing.
6TH DAY. THURSDAY:
Morning flight to Kauai. Sightseeing on arrival.
Evening free.
You’ll be picked up at your hotel and island-hop over to Kauai. On arrival, tour the Wailua River area including a cruise on the river to Fern Grotto. A stop at Opaekaa Falls will also be made. After the tour, you will be taken to your Kauai hotel.
7TH DAY. FRIDAY: KAUAI.
Leisure day.
At leisure in Kauai—the enchanted island. Perhaps a visit to Waimea Canyon—the Grand Canyon of the Pacific. Maybe just relax and enjoy the sunshine.
8TH DAY. SATURDAY: KAUAI/MAINLAND.
Depart for home.
Transfer to the airport for the beginning of your journey home. Aloha.
