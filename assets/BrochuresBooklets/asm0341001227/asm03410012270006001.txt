1ST DAY. MONDAY: MAINLAND/HONOLULU.
Depart by Pan Am Jet Clipper for Honolulu.
On arrival, you will receive the traditional flower lei greeting and be transferred to your hotel.
2ND DAY. TUESDAY: HONOLULU.
Welcome get-together briefing. City tour.
Enjoy a special briefing where your Pan Am’s World Host will explain the Islands and its people. Depart on the city tour where you’ll see such sights as Iolani Palace, Punchbowl and the University of Hawaii.
3RD AND 4TH DAYS. HONOLULU.
Two days to enjoy Waikiki at your leisure.
5TH DAY. FRIDAY: HONOLULU/KAUAI.
Morning departure to Kauai.
On arrival, Hawaii for a Song passengers will sightsee the Wailua River area, including a cruise to Fern Grotto. After the tour, you’ll be taken to the hotel. Roadrunner passengers pick up their self-drive car.
6TH AND 7TH DAYS. KAUAI.
Two days at leisure.
8TH DAY. MONDAY: KAUAI/KONA.
Morning departure to Kona. Song passengers are transferred to their hotel. Roadrunner passengers pick up their self-drive car.
HOTELS: All with private bath or shower.
