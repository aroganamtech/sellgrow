1ST DAY. MONDAY:
MAINLAND/HONOLULU.
Depart by Pan Am Jet Clipper for Honolulu.
On arrival, you will receive the traditional lei greeting and be transferred to your hotel.
2ND DAY. TUESDAY:
HONOLULU.
Welcome get-together and briefing. City tour.
Enjoy a special briefing where your Pan Am’s World Host will explain the Islands, its people and the many things to do during your stay. Depart on the city tour where you’ll see Iolani Palace, Punchbowl, University of Hawaii and many other sights.
3RD AND 4TH DAYS.
HONOLULU.
Free.
Two days to enjoy Waikiki at your leisure.
5TH DAY. FRIDAY:
HONOLULU/NEIGHBOR ISLAND.
Transfer to the airport for your departure to one of the neighbor Islands.
HAWAII—“The Big Island”—One may ski from the slopes of one of the world’s highest volcanic peaks or swim on the same day.
KAUAI—“The Garden Island”—The greenest of the Islands.
See Kauai’s own natural phenomena, Waimea Canyon. Enjoy the peacefulness of this quiet Island.
MAUI—“The Valley Island”—Visit the old town of Lahaina; visit Haleakala National Park—a vast and dormant volcanic crater.
On arrival at your choice of Island, pick up your Islander U-Drive car and take yourself to your hotel.
6TH AND 7TH DAYS.
NEIGHBOR ISLAND.
Two days to explore on your own.
Use your car to explore and enjoy the Island of your choice.
8TH DAY. MONDAY:
NEIGHBOR ISLAND/MAINLAND.
Depart for home.
Return your car at the airport. Board Hawaiian Airlines to Honolulu to connect with your Pan Am flight home.
