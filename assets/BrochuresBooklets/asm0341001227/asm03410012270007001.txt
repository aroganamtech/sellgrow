1ST DAY. MONDAY: MAINLAND/HONOLULU. Depart by Pan Am Jet Clipper for Honolulu.
On arrival, receive the traditional flower lei greeting. Transfer to hotel.
2ND THROUGH 4TH DAYS. HONOLULU. Tuesday welcome get-together and briefing plus city tour.
At a special briefing your Pan Am’s World Host will explain the Islands, its people and the many things to do during your stay. Depart on the city tour. Balance of stay at leisure.
5TH THROUGH 7TH DAYS. HONOLULU/KAUAI.
Friday morning departure. Sightseeing on arrival.
Transfer to the airport for your flight to Kauai. On arrival, travel to the Wailua River area and cruise to the Fern Grotto. Then be taken to your hotel. Remainder of your stay free.
8TH AND 9TH DAYS. KAUAI/KONA.
Monday morning departure.
Transfer to the airport for your flight to Kona. On arrival, transfer to your hotel. Time to enjoy this quaint fishing village.
10TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: KONA/HILO.
Morning departure. Sightseeing en route.
Depart Kona for your cross-island trip to Hilo via the spectacular Hawaii Volcanoes National Park. Arrive Hilo at your hotel in the late afternoon.
11TH THROUGH 13TH DAYS. HILO/MAUI. Thursday morning departure.
Fly up to beautiful Maui. Transfer directly to your hotel. Make use of your train ticket during your free time and ride the 1890-style railroad into Lahaina and back.
14TH DAY. SUNDAY: MAUI/MAINLAND.
Depart for home.
Back to earth. From Maui to Honolulu to home.
