1ST DAY. SATURDAY/MONDAY: MAINLAND/HONOLULU.
Depart by Pan Am Jet Clipper for Honolulu.
On arrival, you will receive the traditional flower lei greeting. Transfer to your hotel.
2ND DAY. SUNDAY/TUESDAY: HONOLULU.
Welcome get-together and briefing.
City tour.
You’ll enjoy a special briefing where your Pan Am’s World Host will explain the Islands, its people and the many things to do during your stay. After this introduction to Hawaii, you will be taken on your city tour where you’ll see Iolani Palace, the only Royal Palace on American soil; Punchbowl, extinct volcano crater which is now the site of the National Memorial Cemetery of the Pacific; the University of Hawaii; and the Waioli Tea Room.
3RD THROUGH 7TH DAYS. HONOLULU.
Five free days to discover Waikiki.
The Island is yours to do as you please. Swim in the beautiful Pacific. Relax on the sandy beaches. Shop at the famed International Market Place. Visit Sea Life Park, the Polynesian Cultural Center, the Kodak Hula Show. Cruise to Pearl Harbor. In the evening, experience a Hawaiian luau—a native floor show. It’s all up to you. Your Pan Am’s World Host will be glad to assist you in arranging any optional activity.
8TH DAY. SATURDAY/MONDAY: HONOLULU/MAINLAND.
Transfer to the airport for your return flight.
It’s “aloha” time as all good things must come to an end. However, no one ever leaves Hawaii for good. Come back soon!
