But you will discover something seldom-realized, in the Islands of Hawaii — the real thing outdoes anticipation. The foliage is greener, the flowers brighter, the air softer and sweeter-smelling than you’d ever imagined. Here are the sun-bright tropical beaches, the surf-boards and outriggers, here are the graceful brownskinned dancers with hibiscus in their hair, here are the tall palms, the soft moonlight and liquid music in the air.
Don’t hurry — you can’t. But there’s much to see, and do, on these many islands. You’ll make friends with a Waikiki “surfer” to learn the trick of racing a wave-crest on a surfboard or in an outrigger canoe.
