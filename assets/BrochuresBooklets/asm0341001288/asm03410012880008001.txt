The Great Barrier Reef is Australia’s mightiest sight-to-remember. This great belt of living coral, 1200 miles long, is a marine museum —- a delight for fishermen, a kind of heaven for naturalists. Bring strong resistance with you — you will want to stay, as everyone does.
You wouldn’t expect to find towering fjords, glaciers and Alpine scenery, and all-year skiing on an island in the South Pacific — but you will. You wouldn’t expect to see cities, towns and gardens exactly like those in England, Ireland and Scotland, below the equator — but you will. And in New Zealand!
This is an astonishing country that you should see when you’re “down under”. Besides towering mountain ranges, there are live volcanos, hot springs and geysers as at the resort of Rotorua. Whether you’re the fresh or salt variety of fisherman, New Zealand guarantees a maximum catch in pleasure. Zane Grey deserted fiction in describing its superb deep-sea fishing. Twelve-pound Rainbow are not uncommon in the upper reaches of the Waikato River, in the Lake Taupo district — anytime during the season, from November 1 to May 14.
Australia is an out-of-doors country—and everyone goes to the beaches. You'll see crack life-saving teams in action in regular drills, find plenty of spots for lazy sun-bathing.
Your New Zealand fish stories will be fabulous — but true! The angling is fine — both for deep-sea giants and for fresh-water game fish of amazing size . . . Rainbows, for instance, of twelve pounds or more.
