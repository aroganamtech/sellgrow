Look for special comfort when you go places by Clipper. Pan American Clippers are among the safest and most dependable of all 4-engine airliners — and of all the global air systems, Pan American is the most experienced. On top of this is the fact that flying over water is the smoothest flying of all — in all seasons. Smoother than any kind of transportation you’ve ever known, including your own car.
You’ll like the pampering you get, too. Clipper Stewardesses will bring you magazines, stationery, games, a between-meal snack, a pillow or blanket. Pursers serve you too, and at mealtime double as chefs and expert bartenders.
All Clippers are large and roomy. You can move around, chat with fellow passengers. On double-decked Clippers there’s the lower-deck lounge, as intimate, as enjoyable as a select club. You’ll forget you are in the air at all.
And don’t forget — all this luxury, all this service, comes within the price of your ticket. There’s no extra charge for meals, no tipping — even before-meal cocktails are served with the compliments of the house.
The lower deck lounge in the newest Clippers is tops for relaxation. IPs a nicely-decorated sky club — congenial meeting place for a toast to your Clipper voyage.
When lights are low and the song of the engines brings your head to nodding, you'll find sweet comfort for sweet dreams. Whether your seat is the stretch-out reclining type, or a Sleeperette*, as shown in the picture, or converts to a full-length berth as in double-decked Clippers, you’re sure of a good night's rest.
Meals aloft are full-course and oh-so-good. These, and between-meal snacks, are served without extra charge. Tour Clipper flies so steadily, so smoothly, that eating is easy and pleasant.
