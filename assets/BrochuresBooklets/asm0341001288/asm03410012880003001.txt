¡Here’s the best part — and it comes first! Experienced air travelers will tell you that flying is fun because it’s always fresh and interesting. The sky road is never like too-familiar highways. There’s always something new to see — things that you never see from the surface of land or sea. You sit in serene blissful comfort and watch the world turn slowly beneath you — until, in hours or minutes, there you are looking down on some wonderfully exciting place. Yesterday you touched this spot with your finger on a globe — and here it is, real and twice as beautiful as you hoped it would be.
