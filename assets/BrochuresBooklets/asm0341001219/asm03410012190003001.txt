HOLIDAY PAA-101	1 WEEK AND A HALF
THE ISLANDS of OAHU
and HAWAII
1 »t Day — CLIPPER TO THE islands. Overnight flight from Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle or Portland.
2nd Day — HONOLULU. Arrive in the morning. You will be transported to your hotel. Balance of day free — shopping, sports, swimming and sun bathing — a good start on your Hawaiian holiday.
3rd Day — OAHU. An all-day scenic motor trip around this fascinating island. For full details see SIGHTSEEING TRIP No. 1.
4th Day — koko HEAD — KAILUA. Lava beds, craters, Diamond Head, and spectacular ocean scenery highlight this interesting half-day drive. For complete details see SIGHTSEEING TRIP No. 2. Balance of day for fun on your own in Honolulu.
5th Day — WAIKIKI. Free for rest and relaxation or for swimming, surfboard riding, shopping and other activities for which this resort is famous.
6th Day —Hawaii, the “Big Island”. A short delightful morning flight from Honolulu to Ililo, on Hawaii Island. On the way, there is an excellent air view of the islands of Molokai, Lanai and Maui. Motor to Rainbow Falls, visit an orchid Garden and packing plants, then on to Hawaii National Park visiting the Tree Fern Forest, Lava Tube, Twin Craters and the great fire pit of Iiilauea Volcano. Lunch is served at the world-famous Volcano House. In the afternoon, drive via the Black Sand Beach and Mark Twain’s giant monkey pod tree to Kona Inn for dinner and the night.
7th Day — kona — kealakekua. All-morning motor trip through the Kona district, visit Honaunau (the City of Refuge), the Coffee Mill and the plantation country, and the little church of St. Benedict with its painted mural walls. Return to Kona Inn for lunch. The afternoon and evening are free for sightseeing, sports and entertainment on your own. Deep-sea fishing is good here.
8th Day — kona coast cruise. After breakfast, stroll or drive to Kailua Pier to board the motor vessel “CAPTAIN COOK” for a 14 mile cruise in the sheltered waters of leeward Hawaii to Kealakekua Bay and historic monument to Captain Cook. Transfer to the glass-bottom boat “CORAL QUEEN” for a view of the most beautiful coral gardens in the world. Step ashore at the very spot where Captain Cook fell. Return to Kailua to enjoy Kona Inn’s famous buffet lunch. Afternoon free until flight departure to Honolulu.
9th Day — ALOHA TO THE islands. Homeward bound by Clipper. Leave by overnight flight for Los Angeles, or San Francisco, arriving on the 10th day.
notes Passengers destined for Portland or Seattle depart Honolulu by overnight flight on the 10th day, arriving on the mainland on the morning of the 11th day, allowing an additional day in Hawaii at extra cost.

HOLIDAY PAA-104	2 WEEKS
THE ISLANDS OF OAHU,
MAUI and HAWAII
1 St Day — CLIPPER TO THE islands. Overnight flight from Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle or Portland.
2nd Day — HONOLULU. Arrive in the morning. You will be transported to your hotel. Balance of day free — shopping, sports, swimming and sun bathing — a good start on your Hawaiian holiday.
3rd Day — OAHU. An all-day scenic motor trip around this fascinating island. For full details see SIGHTSEEING TRIP No. 1.
4th Day —PEARL HARBOR CRUISE. Morning free. Leave Hotel by special bus at 12:15 P.M. for Pier 12 where we board the 85 fool yacht “ADVENTURE” for a 20 mile cruise through Pearl Harbor. Pass “Battleship Row” with its sunken ships. Observe the peacetime operations of this famous base. Return to the hotel in the late afternoon. (This trip limited to U. S. Citizens.)
5th Day and 6th Day —WAIKIKI. Days for rest and relaxation, for fun, shopping, dancing, and all the things which Hawaii, and particularly Waikiki, offer.
7th Day—MOUNT TANTALUS TRIP. An awe-inspiring panoramic drive for a close look at the wonderful Hawaiian tropics. See SIGHTSEEING TRIP No. 3.
8th Day and 9th Day — WAIKIKI. This tropical beach resort is made for vacation fun, with endless things to do, both day and night. To be sure you don’t miss anything, visit the Hawaiian Visitors’ Bureau. They will be happy to give you complete information on things to see, things do do, and special events which may be scheduled during your stay.
1 Oth Day— MAUI, the “Valley Isle”. This air side trip is a short flight, with a splendid air view of the city of Honolulu, Diamond Head and Koko Head Craters on the way. From the airport, motor drive to the 10,000-foot summit of Mt. Haleakala and the brink of a dormant crater that is considered the largest in the world. Trip includes a delightful luncheon, and a visit to Iao Valley, Yosemite of the Pacific and site of the “Needle”, an amazing freak of nature. Return to Maui airport for flight to Hilo on Hawaii Island. Dinner and the night at either the Naniloa or Hilo Hotel.
1 1 th Day — HAWAII. Motor to Rainbow Falls, visit an orchid Garden and packing plants, then on to Hawaii National Park visiting a forest of giant fern trees, blossom-covered Ohia trees, the Lava Tube, Kilauea Iki and Halemaumau craters, steam cracks and sulphur banks. Luncheon at the world-famous Volcano House. From here via Black Sand Beach, forests and jungles to Kona Inn for dinner and the nighl.
12th Day —KONA —KEALAKEKUA. All-morning motor trip through the Kona district, visit Honaunau (the City of Refuge), the Coffee Mill and the plantation country, and the little church of St, Benedict with its painted mural walls. Return to Kona Inn for lunch. The afternoon and evening are free for sightseeing, sports, and entertainment on your own. Deep-sea fishing is good here.
13th Day —kona. Morning at leisure to enjoy Kona Inn’s salt water swimming pool. After luncheon depart from Kona airport on a mid-afternoon flight for Honolulu.
14th Day — ALOHA TO THE islands. Homeward bound. Leave by overnight flight.
15th Day — mainland arrival at Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle or Portland.

8th Day and 9th Day—Waikiki. Free for rest and relaxation or for swimming, surfboard riding, shopping and other activities for which this resort is famous.
loth Day—kauai. The first of five days of exciting adventure and exploration. Leave Honolulu Airport in mid-morning for 45-minute flight to Kauai Island. Motor to delightful Kauai Inn for luncheon. In the afternoon visit Wailua, Opaikaa Falls, Hanalei Valley and Beach, Wainiha Beach and the Haena Wet and Dry Caves where ancient chiefs held court. Return to Kauai Inn for dinner and the night.
11th Day — WAIMEA CANYON — KALALAU. First Stop this day is Poipu Beach. Visit Spouting Horn — the famous salt water geyser, then on to Waimea Canyon, the “Grand Canyon” of the Pacific. Continue to Kalalau Lookout for a spectacular view of the island. Picnic lunch at Kalalau. Return to Kauai Inn for dinner and the night.
12th Day — MAUI. After breakfast transfer to Lihue Airport for departure to Maui, via Honolulu. Morning excursion to the summit of Haleakala, one of the world’s largest extinct volcanoes. Return to Puulani Ranch for your luncheon. In the afternoon, motor to beautiful Iao Valley, historic Hawaiian battleground. Leave by plane for Hilo, on Hawaii Island, arriving in later afternoon. Motor to Naniloa Hotel on the shores of Hilo Bay for dinner and the night.
13th Day —Hawaii. Motor to Rainbow Falls, visit an orchid Garden and packing plants, then on to Hawaii National Park visiting Kilauea Volcano, the Lava Tube, Tree Fern Forest and Halemaumau, main fire pit of the volcano. Steam cracks and sulphur banks are found here. Luncheon at the famous Volcano House. In the afternoon drive to Black Sand Beach at Punaluu, thence via Kailua to Kona Inn for dinner and the night.
14th Day—KONA—KEALAKEKUA. All-inorning motor trip through the Kona district, visiting Honaunau (the City of Refuge), the Coffee Mill and the plantation country, and the little church of St. Benedict with its painted mural walls. Return to Kona Inn for lunch. Afternoon and evening are free for sightseeing, sports and entertainment on your own. Deep-sea fishing is good here.
15th Day—KONA coast cruise. After breakfast, stroll or drive to Kailua Pier to board the motor vessel “CAPTAIN COOK” for a 14 mile cruise in the sheltered waters of leeward Hawaii to Kealakekua Bay and historic monument to Captain Cook. Transfer to the glass-bottom boat “CORAL QUEEN” for a view of the most beautiful coral gardens in the world. Step ashore at the very spot where Captain Cook fell. Return to Kailua to enjoy Kona Inn’s famous buffet lunch. Afternoon free until flight departure to Honolulu.
16th Day to 20th Day — waikiki. This tropical beach resort is made for vacation fun, with endless things to do both day and night. To be sure you don’t miss anything, visit the Hawaiian Visitors’ Bureau. They will give you complete information on things to see, things to do, and special events scheduled during your stay.
21st Day —ALOHA TO THE islands. Homeward bound. Leave by overnight flight.
