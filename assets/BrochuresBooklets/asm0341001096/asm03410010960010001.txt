Brasilia. . . The new capital of Brazil, an architectural and city planning wonder, is rising on a high plateau 580 miles northwest of Rio de Janeiro. Started in May 1957, it was inaugurated in April 1960. Among hotels are the beautiful Brasilia Palace and Nacional; rates $7 to $13 single. Advance reservations advisable.
SOURCES OF FURTHER INFORMATION ... The local tourist commission office at Rua Mexico 104 in the Brazilian Press Association Building has folders, maps, lists of events. The Brazil Herald is an English-language newspaper. The Pan American office is at Avenida Presidente Wilson, No. 165A (Tel. 52-8070) in Rio. At Rua Sao Luiz 29 (Tel. 36-0191) in Sao Paulo. In New York the Brazilian Government Trade Bureau is at 551 Fifth Avenue.
