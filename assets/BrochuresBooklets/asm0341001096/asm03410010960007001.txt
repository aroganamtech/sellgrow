There’s golf at the Sâo Paulo Golf Club (card necessary), underwater fishing at Ubatuba near Santos, numerous tennis clubs (cards necessary). Hunting is excellent in the interior: boar, alligator, jaguar, wild buffalo, tapir; game fish and game birds. Conducted trips are organized by Safari Ltda. of Rio de Janeiro.
