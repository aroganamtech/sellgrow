.Your memorandum May 9> 1955
As you know, we originally requested 200 of the original booklets and these were distributed on a very careful basis to our key personnel. You will be interested to know that we have received many favorable comments*
With regard making a broader distribution to the employees I feel, as does Mr# Clarence Young, that this would not be widely read. We do have the same opinion, however, that if we were to break the facts down in smaller pieces, it would be read with considerable interest* Our present thinking is to set up a by-line in our Clipper of "Know Your Airline11 and over a period of a number of issues, possibly six or more, cover all of the facts included in the booklet. As a matter of fact, we might even continue this by-line after we complete the publishing of the information now available*
