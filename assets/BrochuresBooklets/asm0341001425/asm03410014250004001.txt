reference: Your May 9 memorandum
We have considered the condensed version of Facts and Figures attached to your referenced memo. As a pamphlet, and with the appropriate charts included, we believe it would be as effective as the larger original version.
We do have some reservations as to readership interest in such material when it is presented as a package, so far as the over «.all employee group is concerned. Our experience to date indicates that when strictly Company information is offered in ''sandwich” form with other material of general interest it is more widely read. For this reason, we are currently studying the possibility of presenting the contents of the booklet as a series of articles in our '’Clipper".
We believe that it can be covered in not more than six issues. Each article would cover a related area and include the appropriate charts. The entire series would appear in consecutive issues, and be tied together by some simple format device or other means of identification.
This method of treatment not only avoids the extra cost of thirty or forty cents per employee, but, we believe, will actually be read by more employees than if presented in pamphlet form. For these reasons we do not desire to order a supply of the booklets for this Division.
