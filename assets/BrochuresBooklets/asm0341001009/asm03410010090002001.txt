AIRPORT INFORMATION
Tokyo Airport
American citizens must have passport, visa and smallpox vaccination certificate unless staying less than 72 hours, when a visa is not necessary. The ordinary tourist visa is valid for 60 days. There is an excellent duty-free shop at the airport. The terminal building is on seven floors with incoming passengers on the first floor and outgoing passengers on the second. In the arrivals lobby on the first floor there is the Tourist Information Center for Japan and the Tokyo Metropolitan Information Service. There are also automatic telephones connected directly to the major hotels. Just pick up the receiver and you are put through immediately. On the second floor there are a coffee shop, cocktail bar and dining room and on the third floor, a hotel for transit passengers. You can bring into Japan duty-free: 400 cigarettes or 100 cigars or 1 lb tobacco; 2oz perfume and three bottles of liquor.
GETTING INTO TOWN
Tokyo International Airport is 11 miles from the center of the city.
Taxis
The fare into the city should come to about Y1,200. There is no need to tip taxi drivers and some may even refuse to accept a tip.
Monorail
The monorail runs from the airport to Hamamatsucho Station from about 6am—11 pm. The fare is Y190 and the journey only takes 15 minutes. But there is hardly any room for baggage so it is mainly used by people going to the airport to meet or see-off visitors.
Buses
Buses run every 30 minutes to the central area. The fare is Y120.
