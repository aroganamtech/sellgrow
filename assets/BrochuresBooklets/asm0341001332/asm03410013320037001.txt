Pan Am is more than an airline. Its subsidiary, Intercontinental Hotels Corporation, manages or operates one of the world's most extensive hotel groups. Inter-Continental consists of 80 hotels in 48 countries. Of this total, the Corporation owns five hotels, leases 20, manages 33, and franchises 22. These hotels have approximately 29,000 rooms and employ 36,000 people. Since 1970, the company has been adding two to four hotels a year.
Also, for more than 50 years Pan Am has provided management and technical assistance to aviation interests worldwide and has been a major contractor in support of the U.S. Space Program. In 1979,
Pan Am commenced consolidation of all of its worldwide management and technical service concerns in a wholly owned subsidiary, Pan Am World Services, Inc. consisting of the Technical and Base Support Services Division which supplies management, operations, maintenance and other support services for government and private industry; the International Services Division which supplies management, operations and maintenance services at overseas locations, with major emphasis on aviation-related programs; the General Aviation Services Division which supplies management, operations and maintenance for metropolitan airports and heliports, and is engaged in consulting on airport management operations; and the Engineering and Design Department which provides services in the field of airport and space facility design, computer applications and technical documentation.
A total of 5,529 employees are assigned to these divisions.
