Aerovias Centrales - a Pan Am subsidiary 1932-1935.
AOA - American Overseas Airlines, Inc. which Pan Am acquired in 1950.
CNAC - China National Aviation Corporation - Pan Am owned 45% interest 1933-1945 and 20% interest 1946-1949.
Panagra - Pan American-Grace Airways, Inc. - a Par. Am subsidiary 1929-1966.
* These cities are served only by cargo flights at the present time.
Pan Am had service from Port Washington, New York from 1937 before moving to La Guardia Airport, N.Y. in 1940.
