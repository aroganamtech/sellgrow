Dividends of 10 cents a share were paid in each quarter from March 1, 1973, and were raised to 12^ cents per share on June 4, 1974.
On December 7, 1976, E. F. Dolansky was appointed President and Chief Operating Officer, after serving three years as Executive Vice President. Previously, he had been Vice President-Finance, a post he held when he joined the airline in 1962. He was a member of the Board of Directors from 1964.
On June 22, 1977 National inaugurated nonstop flights from Miami to Paris -- the first U.S.-flag service between Florida and the French capital. President Carter on January 27, 1978 signed the order granting National routes between Miami, Tampa and New Orleans, and Paris, Amsterdam and Frankfurt. The new European authority signaled a further international expansion and concluded the Transatlantic Route Proceeding begun four years earlier.
National launched the most comprehensive pattern of service ever from the Southeast to Europe on April 30, 1978. Included were 15 weekly nonstop flights to London, Paris, Frankfurt and Amsterdam.
The schedules included the first nonstop flights from Miami to Frankfurt and Amsterdam, plus the first nonstop flights to Europe from Tampa and direct European service from New Orleans. The first nonstop flights from New Orleans to Europe began July 2, 1978. National also received authorization and commenced service between New York and Amsterdam on December 13, 1978.
