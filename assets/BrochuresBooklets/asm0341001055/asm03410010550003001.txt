Give yourself a push...
. . . and it can be you on that tropical island.
Make it come true. That dream of you on a tropical island. Just catch a Pan Am jet to the Caribbean. It’s much easier and less expensive than you may think.
Nowhere will you find a lusher atmosphere. A kinder climate. Such cool, crystal-clear waters. Or powder-fine beaches. Or so many different ways to enjoy yourself.
Feeling lazy? Go ahead. Beachcomb the day long. The sun never tires. For a bit of action, swim out. Look down deep. Nobody knows how many Spanish galleons lie on the bottom. You might find one. It happens. For these are the waters of the Jolly Roger. Of pirates bold and galleons laden with gold.
Want real adventure? How about shooting the rapids on a bamboo raft. Or hunting alligators? Exciting!
At the end of the day, be deliciously private. Relax on a star-lit terrace over a Planter’s Punch and watch the moon rise dripping from the sea.
Or kick up your heels. Night life is an utter whirl. Bright. Brilliant. Bouncing. There are roguish Calypso songs, steel bands and Bongo drums that will set your feet tapping. Limbo dances. Rum, rhumba and roulette. Carnivals galore with mummers and madcap pantomimes, costume balls, parties.
Be a bon vivant. Order flying fish for dinner. Eat oysters picked off a tree. Sample dozens of taste-tingling native dishes. Stay in the castle of a scoundrelly — but tasteful — buccaneer.
If sports are your game, you’ll find your hands full. Of fishing rods, tennis rackets, golf clubs, sailboat tillers.
The shopping bargains will make your head spin. You’ll be picking up things you never thought you could afford.
