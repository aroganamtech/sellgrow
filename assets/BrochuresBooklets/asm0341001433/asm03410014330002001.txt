Tonight's meeting was designed for the purpose of providing our Miami Management Team members direct contact with members of the executive level of our Management Team. We were fortunate in being able to schedule Mr. James 0. Leet,
Executive Vice President-General Manager, as our main speaker.
Mr. Leet joined Pan Am in 1943 as a junior airport manager under an executive training program. He has served in various airport manager and district sales manager capacities in Pan Am before joining Irish Airlines as Vice President in 1959.
In 1967, he joined former Northeast Airlines as Senior Vice President and became President of the airline in 1968. He rejoined Pan Am in 1970 as Senior Vice President-Marketing and was recently named General Manager. In his capacity as Executive Vice President-General Manager, Leet now supervises the operational arm of Pan Am along with the Marketing side of the airline.
At the conclusion of his update on Pan Am, Mr. Leet will answer questions from the membership. This "cracker-barrel" session is your opportunity to clarify any questions that Mr. Leet may not have covered in his speech.
Mr. Ben Atkins, Managing Director, Miami and Mr0 Jesse Houston, Staff Vice President-Miami Base, will also be available for any questions related to the Miami functions.
