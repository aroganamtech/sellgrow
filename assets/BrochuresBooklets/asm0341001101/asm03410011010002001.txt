Mexico
WHAT’S SPECIAL
Mexico offers the delights of long beaches, a temperate climate, beautiful countryside, and charming people. It’s a land of striking differences. Here you will find luxurious resort areas—such as Acapulco, with its famous cliff-divers—and, not far away, picturesque Indian villages with simple adobe dwellings. Grand colonial mansions are juxtaposed with modern skyscrapers. And you will discover a rich archaeological heritage in the ancient stone pyramids dating back to the splendor of the pre-Hispanic civilizations. There is, of course, Mexico City itself, the oldest city in North America, situated on the site of an Aztec community discovered by the great Cortfes in 1519, and today a great modern capital city as sophisticated as any in Europe.
Mexico is a forward-looking and progressive country, and the instinctive charm of its people and their culture is as welcoming today as always.
