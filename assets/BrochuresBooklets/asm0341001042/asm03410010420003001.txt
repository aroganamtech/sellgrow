CAPE HAITIAN Extension Tour
One of the most fascinating excursions you can make in the West Indies (and in the world, for that matter . . .) is to Cape Haitian; you visit the Citadelle of King Henri Christophe who was a leader in Haiti’s struggle for independence against Napoleon’s France, the Palace of Sans Souci, etc.
The trip from Port au Prince is made by air or by car and the excursion takes two or three days, depending on time available. Your sales agent will be glad to submit programs and rates.

TRAVEL DOCUMENTS
United States citizens do not need passports for trips to the Caribbean, but they must carry proof of their nationality such as a birth certificate, expired passport or naturalization certificate. Tourist cards for the Dominican Republic and Venezuela should be secured prior to departure.
TAXES
The U.S. Transportation Tax is applicable on certain domestic air portions, and is payable with the air ticket; your sales agent will give further details. Haiti Tourist card costs $1.00 (payable in Haiti); Cuba Tax is $2.50 (payable in Cuba). Fee for tourist card for the Dominican Republic is $2.14 (payable prior to arrival). Nassau landing tax is $1.70 (payable in Nassau on departure).
HEALTH REQULATIONS
Travelers who leave United States territory should be in possession of a smallpox vaccination certificate, not older than three years. (This does not apply to trips to Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands).
