1st DAY — ARRIVE SAN JUAN
From New York or Miami on Pan American World Airways Clipper: you will be met at the airport and transferred by car to your hotel.
2nd and 3rd DAYS — SAN JUAN
You will enjoy luxurious living at your hotel—swimming in the pool or relaxing at the private beach, with social activities. During your stay two sightseeing trips are provided: one interesting morning trip visiting all important points in San Juan, and also a delightful afternoon drive to El Yunque, the Caribbean National Forest.
4th and 5th DAYS — CIUDAD TRUJILLO An hour’s Clipper flight to the Capital of the Dominican Republic. A sightseeing trip of the city, oldest walled city of Santo Domingo, etc. Your hotel has a fine swimming pool and there is dancing under the stars.
6th and 7th DAYS — PORT-AU-PRINCE
About one hour’s comfortable flight across the island of San Domingo to the Capital of Haiti. You stay in Port-au-Prince, or in the suburb of Petionville, overlooking the city and the bay. Make a sightseeing trip of Port-au-Prince, visiting the Presidential Palace, colorful native market, etc. Also a drive into the cool mountains at Kenscoff at 5,000 ft. elevation, with magnificent panorama.
8th DAY — KINQSTON
A short flight takes you to lovely Jamaica; continue from the airport by car to your hotel. That afternoon you make a tour of Kingston with a visit to the beautiful Hope Botanical Gardens. Extra time available for some shopping and for an afternoon swim in the hotel’s pool.
9th, 10th, and 11th DAYS — OCHO RIOS • THE NORTH SHORE • MONTEQO BAY
These three days are spent at the resort of your choice on
Jamaica’s glamorous North Shore . . . Travel by car from Kingston for the scenic drive through the mountains and valleys to Ocho Rios to stay at a popular resort hotel, continuing to Montego Bay on your day of departure from Jamaica... or make the trip from Kingston via Ocho Rios to Montego Bay in one day, to stay at a famous bay view hotel. Whatever resort you choose, you will enjoy the magnificent beaches, the sports, the fun and entertainment of your Jamaica holiday.
12th DAY — HAVANA
From Montego Bay by swift airliner to Havana. Stay at a leading hotel.
13th DAY — HAVANA
Sightseeing of the city and the charming suburbs. In the evening you will be taken on the famous night trip, visiting the Tropicana Night Club to enjoy music, dancing and entertainment, and to native sidewalk cafes.
14th DAY — HAVANA • MIAMI • NEW YORK Enjoy most of the day as you please, swimming in the pool of the hotel, or revisiting the shops, and then you will be transferred to the airport for the short flight to Miami and on to New York with Eastern Airlines or National Airlines or to other city of your destination, or direct to New York via Cubana or National Airlines.
