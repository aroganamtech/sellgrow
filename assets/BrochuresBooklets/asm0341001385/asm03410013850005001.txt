When you reserve a seat on a Pan Am flight that offers Clipper Class service, you’ll travel in a separate section of the aircraft reserved exclusively for Clipper Class passengers. This exclusivity results in a serene environment that is conducive to relaxing without interruption from passengers in other sections of the aircraft.
Your Clipper Class seat has been designed for maximum comfort, and it’s wider than most business seats found in comparable service available elsewhere. That’s important. And we’ve removed the middle seat in Clipper Class so you’ll never be more than one seat away from the aisle.
