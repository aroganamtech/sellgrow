A great deal of our contact with the public is over the telephone. Every time you send your voice over the wires to a customer, you do something for your airline’s public relations. You either build them up or tear them down. Let’s let our telephone voice speak well for our company.
Answer promptly .
When the telephone rings, assume the call is from a busy customer who may go elsewhere if kept waiting. Get to the phone quickly and answer pleasantly.
Don’t be a man of mystery . . .
Start the conversation by promptly identifying the company, your department and yourself, as: “Pan
American World Airways, Traffic Department, Mr.-----
speaking.” It saves both your time and his.
Avoid the vanishing act , , .
Try never to leave a customer hanging on the line. If you must go away to get information, tell him how long you’ll be gone—and stick to it. Better still, take his name and number and offer to call back.
