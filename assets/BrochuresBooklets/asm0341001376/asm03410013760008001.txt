A well fed passenger . . .
... is a happy passenger. And PAA’s Clipper passengers are among the best-fed in the world. Let’s keep them happy by showing every consideration for their comfort while in the air. Many other conveniences—blankets, reading material, between - meal snacks, etc.—are provided aboard every Clipper ... all for the passenger’s comfort. While he’s aboard a Clipper, every passenger is our guest. Let’s treat him as such.
