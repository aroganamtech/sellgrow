Left behind at the airport . .
. . . is the plight of the passenger who missed his connecting reservation. A passenger’s trip doesn’t always end when he steps down from a Clipper. An important part of Pan American service is making continuing reservations by boat, train or airline. The Clipper passenger is still our guest until he makes his connection and is on his way again. Let’s make every effort to make his trip a smooth one, with no enforced “layovers.”
