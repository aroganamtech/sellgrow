TEAMWORK
It takes teamwork of thousands of men and women to keep Pan American Clippers flying the airways. To do the job efficiently, we must be able to work together and get along smoothly with one another. The best lubricant for smoothing out everyday contacts is considerate behavior toward others . . . our fellow workers as well as our patrons.
Constructive suggestions . . .
. . . make a GOOD airline BETTER. A knocker lowers the morale of any group. If you don’t like the way something is being done, don’t just criticize. Try to work out a better plan, then bring it to the attention of those who can do something about it. Progress and improvement are based on constructive ideas.
