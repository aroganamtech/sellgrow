Arguments lose friends . . .
When a customer is mistaken, never contradict him or make him feel foolish. Why win an argument and lose a friend? Let him feel his error is perfectly natural and give him every opportunity to “save saves friends.
Be alert . . .
It’s very easy to agree with a passenger who’s griping about inconveniences he encountered at some other Pan American station. But how many of us actually try to understand what caused the inconvenience? Be sympathetic. Try to find out the specific causes for complaints.
You can do your organization a great service by finding out in detail the reasons for complaints and passing them on to the proper officials. Let’s improve our service by being more alert to the CAUSES rather than the RESULTS of complaints. Don’t pass the buck!
