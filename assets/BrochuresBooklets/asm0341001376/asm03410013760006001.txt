Delayed flights ...
. . . are hard on the disposition — particularly for a passenger who may be tired, hungry and impatient to finish his journey. With the latest instruments we are rapidly overcoming obstacles—like weather—which used to disrupt schedules. But if there is a slight delay, remember that a courteous explanation of it can be your best insurance against a dissatisfied passenger.
The best laid plans . . .
... of mice and men oft go astray. And so does the best-cared-for baggage. A passenger who Clippers happily southward while his baggage wings its way merrily northward is apt to be a bit unhappy. His case merits your utmost in tact, courtesy and efficiency. Some people think a great deal of baggage flys the air-lanes continuously, shunted from city to city and plane to plane till the end of time.
Your care and attentiveness in handling baggage and personal effects can go far toward dispelling such a myth.
