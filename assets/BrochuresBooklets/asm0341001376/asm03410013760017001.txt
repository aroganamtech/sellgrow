PATIENCE
Some travelers can’t seem to make up their minds which plane to take, when to leave or whether to leave at all. They may be merely confused or lack information. Be careful not to show impatience. Try to help them clarify their problems and reach a decision. Here, again, tact and patience make friends while brusqueness makes enemies.

SMILES
A smile is the most becoming thing you can wear. It never goes out of style, never wears out. And, if you haven’t tried one recently, you’ll find it reacts inside you and actually helps you be pleasant and good-natured. Better still, it’s contagious. It wins smiles in return and spreads faster than a backyard rumor. Inside an organization, a smile aids efficiency. And in dealing with customers, it has no equal as a good public relations builder.
