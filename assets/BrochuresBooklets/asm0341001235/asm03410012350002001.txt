
Another city in Pan Am’s California service.
Another city in our growth across the Sunbelt.
It is with great pleasure that we welcome Palm Springs to the roster of great American cities now being served by Pan Am.
On March 1 st, we inaugurate daily service between Houston, Phoenix and Palm Springs, providing the only direct service between Houston and Palm Springs and adding to the growing Pan Am network of flights across the Sunbelt.
The Palm Springs Story.
Flourishing oasis in a golden desert. Mecca of presidents, political figures, movie greats, business executives and sports celebrities. Golf capital of the world, where you can come to watch the championship competitions or work on your game at some of the finest, most beautiful courses you’ll ever play. A virtual paradise for tennis professionals and enthusiasts of every level.
The place for some of the best spas in the country. It’s all here. In fashionable, elegant Palm Springs.
Getting you there in Style.
We’ll give you our best. And with Pan Am, that means you’ll receive the kind of care and thoughtful attention traditionally devoted to our passengers throughout our years of service.
