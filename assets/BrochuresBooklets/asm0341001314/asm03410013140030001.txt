We Make the Best Planes BETTER
Because airplanes look very much alike on the outside, you may feel that they are alike. This is not necessarily so. In adopting any airplane, Pan American has given its engineers and technical experts the assignment of making it more than a good airplane, of making it a better airplane. This has resulted in a Pan American partnership with aircraft manufacturers, out of which has come greater comfort for passengers, higher operating efficiency, and utmost dependability in the planes that Pan American flies.
In the development of the Double-Decked "Strato" Clipper alone, Pan American modifications fill a book an inch thick, and represent an expenditure of $200,000 in research and time of engineers, pilots, and maintenance men in refining the airplane.
In the course of this work, a Pan American engineer, working with the Sperry Gyroscope Company, developed the amazing device which is shown on the opposite page. This is the Engine Analyzer, another "first" of PAA as equipment for its Clippers. The Engine Analyzer is a tattle-tale of trouble before it starts. In flight, or on the ground, it tells exactly how all the plane’s power plants are operating, right down to any individual spark plug of the 224 found in the engines of Double-Decked “Strato" Clippers. Besides serving as a positive probing eye into the most inaccessible inner workings of engines, assuring maximum operating efficiency at all times, the Engine Analyzer speeds your trip by simplifying mechanical inspections at trip stops.
This Engine Analyzer is typical of the advanced aircraft engineering which has distinguished Pan American as an airline, and earned for it a reputation for comfortable, fast, safe and dependable international air service.
