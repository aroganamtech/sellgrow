Clipper Passengers Are All Gourmets
Pan American Clippers will spoil you for other types of travel — particularly at mealtime. And there's a special reason. PAA has pioneered an entirely new and wonderful method of food service. All meals are prepared and cooked in great central kitchens, such as the one in New York, the one in London, Paris or San Francisco. Skilled chefs supervise menus, test for taste, and at just the right moment, when it's perfectly done and flavor is best, the food is quick-frozen. Meals are then flown to distributing points throughout Pan American's world system, and used to supply Clippers.
On board your Clipper is a shining ship's galley, shown at the left, equipped with special electric ovens. You don't have to wait for your full-course meal, because using this fast modern method passengers are served at the rate of two meals a minute. Most important, you can always be sure that wherever you go in the world by Clipper, meals will be unvaryingly good and food quality always the same high standard. On first-class flights there's no dinner check, and on tourist service flights the wholesome food is optional and very inexpensive.. To make meals a further highlight event there’s no tipping, for this or any other service on board the Clipper. Your only wish will be that all your trips could be made by Clipper.
