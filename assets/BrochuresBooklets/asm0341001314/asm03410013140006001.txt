FIRST CLASS Cabin
for FIRST CLASS Comfort
What is life like, aboard a Pan American Clipper? All Clippers are designed and appointed with your travel enjoyment in mind. This picture was taken in a Double-Decked “Strato” Clipper, largest and most luxurious of the fleet. The seat you sit in is a reclining lounge chair, adjustable to any comfort-angle. You can read, smoke, relax. There’s room to walk around. It’s a club, or a fine hotel — you’ll find it hard to believe you’re in the air at all.
Further contributing to this comfortable feeling, is the fact that the cabin is pressurized and air conditioned so that you experience no discomfort in ascending or descending, at any level. The result is the most enjoyable air ride you’ve ever had.
