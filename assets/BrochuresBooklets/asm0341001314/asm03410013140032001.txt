The girl in the Tunis blue uniform, wearing the golden half wing that is the badge of a Clipper Stewardess, is a very remarkable person. She is attractive, brainy, personable — these things she was born with. She is from 2\ to 26 years old, and has passed a strict company physical examination. She must have two years of college or two years of business experience, and be able to speak at least one foreign language fluently. But she is also highly trained by PAA — just to make your trip more pleasant. Clipper Stewardess training includes a month of intensive classwork, from which she emerges as something of an authority on as many as 20 subjects, from aerodynamics and customs regulations to the feeding of small children and the mixing of drinks. She learns about food and food service in the company's own flight kitchens.
There's another personality aboard every Clipper who has your comfort at heart. It's the Clipper Steward who serves as genial partner to Clipper Stewardesses in filling your every wish as a world traveler.
Wherever you go by Clipper, passenger service is one of the many Pan American standards that never changes. It's a thing that makes you glad you went by air, glad you went by Clipper.
