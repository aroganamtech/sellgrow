Pan American’s Meteorology Division has learned how to do something about the weather, by knowing what the weather is doing. First of all, international Weather Stations blanket the world. Weather Ships at sea, hundreds of airliners in flight, are all a part of a great world weather reporting system.
Twenty-four hours a day, up-to-the-second information is funneled, by radio and Teletype, to flight and weather experts at Pan American terminal points in all parts of the world. With this precise data, meteorologists plot weather maps which serve as a basis for the first step in any Clipper flight, preparation of the Flight Plan — to take advantage of the very best winds, the very best weather.
