You’ll Enjoy Being a V. I. P.
How important can you feel? You'll never know until you've traveled by Clipper. Purser and Stewardesses make your comfort their full-time job. Magazines, games, stationery, pillow or blanket, something to eat between meals? — all you have to do is ask. On “The President Special" to Europe, and “El Présidente Especial" to South America, there's champagne, orchids, Lanvin's Arpège, and breakfast-in-bed, just to make you feel extra-special.
