CLIPPER WINGS
Make the World a Neighborhood
This is the story of Pan American
—The World's Most Experienced Airline — as told in short text and big pictures. It is not a history book, albeit Pan American has an illustrious history. It is a photographic presentation of a great world-wide airline system, the greatest inter-continental air service in the world. From a short 90-mile thread stretching between Key West and Cuba, Pan American World Airways System has grown to reach all of the six continents, and eighty countries and colonies around the globe.
To make a camera acquaintance with the System of the Flying Clippers, to see how these famous airliners are flown, to meet the people who fly them, to go behind the scenes to learn how dependability records are made and maintained, spend a few minutes with the pages that follow.
