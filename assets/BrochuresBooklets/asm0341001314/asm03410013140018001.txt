Your Spirits Go Up
When You Come DOWNSTAIRS
(Photo by Ewing Xrainin for 7own & Country Magazine)
In Double-Decked “Strato” Clippers there's a spiral staircase to something new in air travel pleasure. Below the main cabin is the lower-deck Lounge. The curtains are gay, the walls are mirrored, the lights are soft — it's a perfect set-up for easy relaxation, for good companionship. For those who wish it, there's a neat service bar for cocktails and tall drinks, with the Purser serving as a master mixer.
