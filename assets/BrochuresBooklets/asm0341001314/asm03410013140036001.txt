Workhorse with WINGS
Few people fully realize the tremendous growth of air shipping. The day when people looked upon air shipping as an emergency service only has passed. Today the use of Clipper Cargo is so general and so steady that Pan American operates an all-cargo fleet, in addition to its regular planes. Some of these Cargo Clippers can carry over eleven tons of goods in a single load. In ten years Clipper Cargo volume has grown from 1,185,000 pounds to over 48,000,000 pounds (24,000 tons) per year.
There are few things that are not being shipped by air today. Cargo manifests include everything from pharmaceuticals, machinery and textiles to livestock, orchids and tropical fish.
Clipper Cargo has completely altered the concept of international distribution of goods and in a single decade air shipping has opened up the entire world as a market for manufacturers and distributors. With increasing use, rates have gone down too — so that in many cases, in addition to its many other benefits and advantages, air shipping costs no more and often less than surface forwarding. Weight and size seldom present any problem. A new hotel in the Caribbean had all its equipment arid furniture Clipper-shipped — 22 plane loads in all, at a cost lower than any other shipping method.
Clipper Cargo has also played an important part in strengthening the economies of the free countries of the world. By increasing the accessability to the world's supply of raw materials and by providing the means to export finished products to a far larger market than ever before, Clipper Cargo has enlisted to help the cause of freedom.
