The Province oí Córdoba is rich in beauty and history. One can travel through the “hills” for days over fine roads, eminently suitable for motoring, feast their eyes on the ever changing colours of the hills; wind around hair-pin bends; ride, or walk, as the fancy may be suited. And, breathe health laden air that brings joy into life.
This section of the country has become an all year resort, its altitude being especially beneficial and its scenic attractions and outdoor sports of interest to all.
In every part of the hills can be found comfortable accommodation. With the opening of the new motor road, the City
of Córdoba can be reached by car in one day, and arrangements for hiring cars may be done by phoning to 34 (Defensa) 2743. A few hours later the traveler can be in the heart of the hills, whose scenic beauty is unsurpassed as rippling mountain streams and waterfalls unfold to the view.
