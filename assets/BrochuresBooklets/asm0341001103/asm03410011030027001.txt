AVENIDA 9 DE JULIO is a new thoroughfare cut through the heart of the city by demolishing entire city blocks. Lanes are formed by gardens with fountains separating them, and parking places with capacity of about 2.000 automobiles are built underground, while trees have been transplanted to line the sides. Facing this avenue is where casa Ricciardi have their exhibition of jewels.
CENTRAL POST OFFICE, situated at Av. Leandro N. Alem and Sarmiento. In the Stranger’s Hall in the building, foreign newspapers are displayed, together with information of interest to tourists. There are also facilities for letter writing.
THE MUNICIPAL MUSEUM OF COLONIAL ART, Suipacha 1422. Beautiful private collection including old mansion, typical of colonial days; hand carved doors, etc.
NATIONAL SCHOOL-MUSEUM OF THE BOCA, in calle Pedro Mendoza 1835. Paintings donated by Benito Quinquela Martin, who very ably depicts life on the water front.
PLAZA DE MAYO, entitled Plaza Mayor by Juan de Garay, which name was changed in 1806, to commemorate the Victory over Grab Beresford, may be entitled the centre of Buenos Aires. From it the whole city and suburbs radiate in the form of a semicircle.
PLAZA SAN MARTIN, This lovely park is situated at the North end of calle Florida and is famed for the splendid residences which face it. In the centre of the plaza is a monument to the memory of General San Martin.
CALLE FLORIDA, runs from Rivadavia to Plaza San Martin. It is the most popular street in Buenos Aires and is lined by fashionable shops of all kinds. During most of the day motor traffic is stopped and this street becomes a meeting place for the strollers and shoppers.
