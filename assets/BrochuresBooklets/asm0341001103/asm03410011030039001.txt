OF ARGENTINA
BAHIA BLANCA, with a population of 124.563, is one of the principal ports of Argentina. Situated on the left bank of the Naposta River, it serves a large area for the shipment of grains and wool. 397 miles from Buenos Aires, the port is served by the Southern Railway, and the Rosario-Puerto Belgrano Railway.
CORDOBA, Capital of the province of the same name, has 320.000 inhabitants. It stands at an altitute of 1.440 feet, 432 miles from Buenos Aires. The district is renowned for its beauty and the city for its buildings, for in point of age, Córdoba comes next to Lima. The National Observatory is here and makes Córdoba the Argentine Greenwich. It is the gateway to the sierras (hills), lakes and waterfalls of the hill district.
LA PLATA, Capital of the Province of Buenos Aires, dates from 1882. It is 35 miles from Buenos Aires and can be reached by road, rail or river. Here are located some of the big meat packing plants and oil refinery. La Plata is Argentina’s “model city”. It contains wide streets and imposing buildings. The population is 267.644. The La Plata’s museum, famous for its collection of extinct animals, is open daily to the public. It treasures are largely ethnological and include skulls, mummies and prehistoric implements used by man. There are zoological, botanical, geological, mineralogical, paleantological and archae-logical sections. The museum is one of the finest in the world and attracts savants and students from all nations.
