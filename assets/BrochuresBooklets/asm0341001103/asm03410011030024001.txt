THE CABILDO, facing Casa Rosada, formerly a seat of Government used by the councillors of the Viceroy, was erected in 1711. Converted into a museum in 1940, it contains paintings, documents, furniture, etc.
THE NAVAL MUSEUM at the corner of calles Florida and Córdoba, contains models of Argentine Naval vessels and pictures ol historical interest.
THE NATIONAL HISTORY MUSEUM is situated in Parque Leza-ma, with an entrance from Calle Defensa 1660. It contains valuable exhibits of the time of the dictator Rosas.
THE NATIONAL LIBRARY in calle México 566, founded in 1810, has occupied its present site since 1902. About 200,000 volumes and 10,000 manuscripts are catalogued.
