IN BUENOS AIRES
SAN IGNACIO CHURCH is situated at the corner of calles Bolivar and Alsina. San Ignacio is said to be the only Church in Buenos Aires which has conserved in part the architecture of colonial times.
SANTO DOMINGO CHURCH, situated at the corner of Defensa and Belgrano, founded in 1756, as from a British stand-point the most interesting of all the Churches in Buenos Aires.
LA MERCED CHURCH at the corner of Cangallo and Re-conquista.
SAN FRANCISCO CHURCH is at the corner of Alsina and Defensa and belongs to the Franciscan Monastery. It is remarkable for the richness of its internal decorations.
HOLY CROSS CHURCH, situated in calle Estados Unidos 3150, is a splendid structure of Gothic architecture.
ST. JOHN’S FRO-CATHEDRAL is situated in calle 25 de Mayo 282.
SAINT ANDREW’S CHURCH, situated in calle Belgrano 575. THE AMERICAN CHURCH is situated in Corrientes 718.
The General Post Office is situated in Sarmiento and Av. L. N. Alem. Other Branches — Av. de Mayo 757 — Callao 124 — Presidente Perón Station — Plaza Constitución Station — Plaza Once Station.
The “Cía. Internacional de Radio S. A.” have their Offices at Av. Roque Sáenz Peña 540, where they have soundproof booths for the convenience of the passerby who wishes to make an international call. Telephone guides of most of the Countries may be consulted there. Overseas or long distance calls can also be made from every telephone of the United River Plate Telephone Company.
