Incorporated by Pan American-Grace Airways and Pan American World Airways to fly the West Coast route between Buenos Aires and the United States, the Douglas DC-6 represents the ultimate in passenger comfort and convenience.
On the extra-fast, extra-fine “El Inter-Americano” service, these ships reach Miami slightly over 19 hours after taking off from Buenos Aires. En route, this service stops only at Santiago, Chile; Lima, Peru, and Balboa, Canal Zone. Connections to all cities on the South American West Coast are possible from any of these points or directly from Buenos Aires by means of other Panagra flights.
Still pioneering the latest type aircraft after 20 years in the international aviation field, Pan American World Airways will shortly incorporate these new Clippers to offer passengers added comforts and facilities.
