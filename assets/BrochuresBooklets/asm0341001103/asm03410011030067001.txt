Southward from Buenos Aires, thirty nine hours by train, is the famous lake district of Patagonia, with its beautiful Lake Nahuel Huapi, fast becoming known as a rival to the Canadian Rockies and the Swiss Alps. The National Parks Board control the district and are leaving no stone unturned to make it more and more attractive to the visitor.
Trout and salmon fishing can be indulged in the many lakes and streams that abound throughout the territory. In winter, skiing and toboganning appeal to those who, more and more, are taking up these winter sports.
No visitor to Argentina should miss the opportunity to visit this beautiful district. Transportation is by fast and comfortable trains over the Southern Railway.
Night trains leave Plaza Constitución Station five times a week for Bariloche.
