OF INTEREST IN
BUENOS AIRES
HE PRESIDENTIAL PALACE on the east side of the Plaza
de Mayo, called because of its traditional colour “La Casa Rosada”, is the official residence of the President. It is notable for its statuary, the rich furnishings of its halls and for its libraries.
THE CATHEDRAL mother church of all the Metropolis is located on the north of Plaza de Mayo and flanked by the residence of the Archbishop. The tomb of General San Martin, within one
of the chapels which is guarded always by soldiers in the red and blue uniform which the hero’s grenadiers wore in the battle.
CONGRESS HALL at the west end of Avenida de Mayo, of great size and in Greco-Roman architecture, is the seat of the legislature. It contains the Senate and the Chamber of Deputies.
THE NATIONAL GALLERY in the Avenida Alvear is housed in a former water supply pumping plant so skillfully adapted as to rank with the best modern organized galleries.
THE MITRE MUSEUM and Library in calle San Martin 336 preserves intact the household of Grab Bartolomé Mitre a former President of the Republic and founder of the newspaper La Nación. The manuscripts, documents and printed works are of real value and constitute an unique record of Argentine development.
THE MUNICIPAL MUSEUM Avda. Quintana 81 contains interesting exhibits of former days.
THE NATURAL HISTORY MUSEUM is situated in the Parque
Centenario, entrance from Avda. Patricias Argentinas 560. It houses palaentological, zoological, mineralogical sections and marine specimens.
