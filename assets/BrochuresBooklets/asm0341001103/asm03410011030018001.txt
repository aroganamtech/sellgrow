Capital of the Argentine Republic, has a population of 3.000.371 and is the largest City below the equator. The Municipality of Euenos Aires is a Federal District.
Adding to the population of the Federal District that of the surrounding suburbs, the total population of Greater Buenos Aires is 4.408.373.
This great city dominates the country commercially, socially, culturally and politically.
The City of Buenos Aires has been rightly described as the most progressive in the world to-day. Modern, cosmopolitan, it is famed for its beautiful parks, wide avenues, exceptional cleanliness and general air of prosperity. Its inhabitants are served by five subway systems, bus lines, tramcars, “micro” motor busses, called “colectivos” and comfortable and modern taxis.
Up to date office buildings, theatres, hotels and appart-ments with central heating, air conditioning, and all modern conveniences are no novelty. Amongst the modern service flats, we may mention the Salverda building situated at Talcahua-no 1253.
The remarkable change in the face of Buenos Aires in the last two decades is invariably commented upon by visitors and business men who have not been in the Argentine for some time. Each week sees some new improvement and the City Fathers are fully alive to the necessity of providing beautiful open spaces. Perhaps it is for this reason, with its wide boulevards, open air cafés, and general brightness of aspect that the City has been termed the “Paris of South America”.
