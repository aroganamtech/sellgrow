FOOT-BALL, Is one of the most popular sports in Argentina. Some very exciting matches may he seen on Sundays and holidays throughout the season, which lasts from March to November inclusive. During the summer months, matches are played sometimes at night with artificial light. The River Plate Stadium situated at Av. Centenario and Ramsay, is the largest in South America, and seats some 150.000 spectators. This Club won the 1947 championship.
Other important foot-ball clubs are the following:
Boca Juniors, situated at Brandsen and Del Valle Iberlucea. Huracán, Avda. Alcorta 2750.
Independiente, Alsina and Alte. Cordero (Avellaneda).
San Lorenzo, Avda. La Plata 1674.
POLO, The smart Argentine sport par excellence may be seen at its best from October to December at the polo grounds opposite the Argentine Hippodrome. This field is probably the only one in the world practically in the heart of a big city.
BOXING AND WRESTLING, Exhibitions are frequently given in Luna Park, situated at Corrientes 99.
GOLF, There are many excellent golf courses around Buenos Aires. None is public, but however, guest privileges are extended if properly introduced by a member.
Some of the good clubs are:
Golf Club Argentino - Golf (Central Arg. Railway). Ituzaingo - Ituzaingo (Western Railway).
Jockey Club - San Isidro (Central Arg. Railway).
Ranelagh Golf Club - Ranelagh (Southern Railway).
San Andrés - San Andrés (Central Arg. Railway).
Swift Golf Club - B. M. Gonnet (Southern Railway).
TENNIS, There are no public tennis courts around Buenos Aires, but admission to the numerous private clubs can easily be arranged through members.
