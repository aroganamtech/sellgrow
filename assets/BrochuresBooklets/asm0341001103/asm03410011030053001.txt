Mendoza is the vineyard of the Argentine Republic. In fact, it has the four largest wineries in the world, all within a few minutes of the centre of town and every one of them worth a visit.
It is an interesting, friendly Province, and well worth breaking your trip to visit. It is scenically beautiful and historically important. Within a few hours of the city itself are several interesting winter resorts, such as Villavicencio and Potrerillos.
