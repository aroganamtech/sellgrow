THE PALERMO PARKS, with their splendid avenues where the welthiest citizens have their magnificent palaces with large and beautiful gardens. Here is where most of the Nations have their Embassies and residences. These avenues are horded by well kept gardens and the famous “Rose Garden”, containing thousands of rose trees of all types and descriptions.
BALNEARIO MUNICIPAL (Municipal bathing beach) is a magnificent engineering feat, all being constructed on reclaimed land. It has, in addition to other appointments, an open air theatre, games, gardens and public music. The drive along the Balneario river front runs the whole length of the sea wall.
