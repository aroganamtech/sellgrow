The name of Tigre does not imply all that this district means. The Tigre itself is one of a number of rivers that flow through the Paraná Delta, which is also intercepted by artificial canals.
It is a favourite resort not only of tourists, but also of the population of Buenos Aires and its environs. At week-ends the rivers are gay with all kind of craft, from the small skiff and the canoe to great launches and yachts of luxurious proportions. Here, at the town of Tigre itself, on both banks of the river, are situated many yacht and rowing clubs. Almost every Saturday and Sunday during the spring and summer there is a regatta in progress.
The Tigre is easily reached from Buenos Aires, the distance being only I714 miles. Arrangements for hiring private cars may be done by phoning to 34 (Defensa) 2743. On the Central Argentine Railway there are two routes, both by fast electric trains. From the Tigre, the visitor can either by private launch or many of the various public launches that ply the rivers, spend a day among beautiful sylvan surroundings. Numerous “recreos” or hotels are to be found along the waterways where excellent food can be obtained, and should the visitor so desire, accommodation to stay for so long as he may desire. The scenery is varied and continually changing, the banks being gay with flowers or flowering trees, while all along can be seen the fruit trees, apples, pears, oranges, lemons, plums, etc.
The waters of the Delta are a paradise for the fisherman. Fish abound in plenty from the fighting Dorado, the Surubi, a fish that attains to 20 to 30 kilos, the Boga, another fighter, the Tararira which will give the keenest of fishermen plenty of action, in addition to many other species.
