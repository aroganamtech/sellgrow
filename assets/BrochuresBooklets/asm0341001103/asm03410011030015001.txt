The Republic of Argentina is rapidly becoming a tourist Paradise. It has so much to offer, so great a variety of scenery, combined with a temperature climate and facilities of transportation that compare with any in the world.
With an area of 1.086.340 square miles and a population of 16.107.870 it extends from the sub-tropical lowlands of the North to the Straits of Magellan, characterised by the fertile pampas of the East, the arid Patagonian plain of the South, and the table-lands of the West, which rise to the Andes mountains, separating Argentina from Chile.
Argentina is a country with a tremendous future. Literally, the “surface has not yet been scratched”. No other country in the world has more fertile soil, soil that in various parts of the country is capable of producing everything from tropical fruits to hard wheat. Metals abound. The mining industry is only at the beginning of its era. There are ample supplies of oil, distributed from north to south of the country. And above all, it produces the finest of cattle, the beef products of which are recognised, particularly in European markets, as outstanding in their quality.
Visitors to Argentina have plenty to see and occupy their time. From the City of Buenos Aires radiation can be made in all directions. To the sea-side resorts of the South. The glorious Lake district, rivalling the Swiss Lakes and Alps. The wonderful bracing climate of the Córdoba Hills. Mendoza, with its vineyards, situated in the foothills of the mighty Andes, the falls of Iguazú, rivalling Niágara, just to mention a few of the attractions.
A tour of Argentina is well worth while. And, a feature of the “pull” exercised by the country, is the fact that tourists invariably make a return visit. There is so much to see and do.
