The map shown above will answer your question. You’re a long way from home in miles, but only 30-odd hours away by Pan American or Panagra. This is where the two great routes meet, so that if you came to Argentina by one route, you might find it worthwhile to return home via the other, and thereby get to know both sides of this vastly interesting continent. The distance is about the same, and the same standards of safety and service are in force along both routes.
You can fly from Buenos Aires to any point on PAA’s (East Coast) route any day of the week. Also, you can leave for any point on Panagra’s (West Coast) route any day of the week.
On the Panagra route you can get to Córdoba, Mendoza, Tucumán and Salta, within Argentina, in a matter of a very few hours. Chile’s wonderful capital, and the picturesque La Paz (the roof of the world) are also within a few hours flight on the Panagra route.
If you have your mind set on thrilling Rio de Janeiro, Montevideo, the friendly capital of Uruguay, or the Iguazu Falls, you can reach them all within a day’s flight or less from Buenos Aires.
