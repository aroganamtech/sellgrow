The Province of Mendoza is bound up with the history ol Argentina. Over the snow clad mountains of the Andes started the army of General San Martin for the liberation of Chile. The whole province is a veritable garden, dominated by the Andes in the background. Equitable in its climate, it is a real beauty spot of Argentina.
On the top of the Cerro de Gloria mountain; is erected the magnificent monument to the memory of the Army of the Andes. The work of Juan Ferrary, an Uruguayan sculptor, it is considered one of the finest monuments in the world.
