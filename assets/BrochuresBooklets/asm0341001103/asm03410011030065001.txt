The Province of Tucumdn is rich in historical association.
The district was one of the earliest parts of Argentina to be settled. The growth of sugar cane was initiated in 1646 and since that date the Province has become the centre of the sugar industry, which owes its present position to Bishop Colombres, who, in 1821, put it on a sound commercial basis.
Now, annually, over four million tons of sugar cane are delivered to the factories for refining.
*
The whole Province abounds in vegetation. Grape Fruit, Avocada Pears, Oranges, grow in profusion, the soil and climate being eminently suitable for their propagation. The nature of the terrain is such that new vistas rapidly unfold between vale and hill. The climate all the year is equitable.
