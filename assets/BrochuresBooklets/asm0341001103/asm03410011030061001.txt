UJUty
Jujuy stands in the mountains towards the Bolivian Frontiet at an altitude of 4,000 feet. It is a picturesque Colonial town, with a warm but healthy climate, set among fascinating scenery ranging from snow-capped mountains to tropical valleys, and the cacti plants of Humahuaca desert, quaint, red-roofed houses, colorful Indians and their llamas.
