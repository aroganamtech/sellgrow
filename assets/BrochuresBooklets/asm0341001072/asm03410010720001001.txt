Buenos Aires’ celebrated Plaza de Mayo is on the site of the original settlement laid out in 1580 by Juan de Garay. Mendoza, "the Valley Forge of South American Independence,” was where Liberator General José de San Martin trained his famous Army of the Andes.
Members of the crack San Martin Mounted Grenadiers stand perpetual guard over the General’s tomb in the great Cathedral of Buenos Aires.

Delightful resorts dot the Cordoba Hills area, in central Argentina. Skiing, best in July, is popular at numerous mountain spots in the Andes, where cool alpine lakes invite refreshing relaxation.
Mar del Plata, on the Atlantic, is (in some respects) "Argentina’s Atlantic City.”. Sports are popular everywhere. Iguazu Falls, on the Brazilian border, outdoes our own Niagara Falls.
Pleasant steamer excursions can be made up the broad Rio de la Plata, and tributary rivers.

Religious festivals are celebrated throughout the nation, with the usual pageantry.

Buenos Aires is the largest city in South America, and the center of Argentine social and economic life. Beautiful parks and stately buildings dot this city, famed for its operas and theaters, business activities, night life, etc.
