WINGS ACROSS THE SOUTHERN BORDER
SWIFTLY and daily winging across the border from four convenient gateway points, Pan American Airways, and its affiliated Compania Mexicana de Avia-cion, link every principal city of the United States and Canada with every one of Mexico—plus those of all other Latin American nations—by superb operations coordinated for through service with the north-south schedules of leading U. S. domestic airlines.
These four strategic over-land and over-water routes of the Flying Clipper Ships, which initiated aerial service between these countries 15 years ago, have long since proved their inestimable value as arteries of inter-
national travel, trade and communication. Today they have taken on added significance, for in keeping the Capitals and production centers of these allied lands within a maximum of a day from each other, the most expeditious transport of war-essential men and materials is assured.
From Canada, the United States and Mexico over— through the international gateways of Brownsville, Los Angeles, Laredo and Miami—the distinguished service and dependability of world-wide and world-famous Pan American Airways are available for time-saving trips and shipments of every kind.
For further information consult any Pan American Office or the Office of any Agent for Pan American Airways.
