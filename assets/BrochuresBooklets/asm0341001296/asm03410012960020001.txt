The importance of a strong and vigorous civil airline is not lost upon the Russians. They’re using their airline, Aeroflot, as a key weapon in their all-out campaign for international economic supremacy.
Aeroflot is now a powerful organization, using brand new pure jet and turbo-prop aircraft.
It has been regularly operating a passenger jet—the TU-104—for several years. The plane now flies into the West—to Copenhagen, Brussels and Amsterdam. The Aeroflot leaders have also begun service to London and have expressed interest in flying to New York.
