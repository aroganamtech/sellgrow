Passengers won’t be the only beneficiaries of the new age of speed that the jets will bring.
The letter writer and the Post Office will share in this boon, too.
Air mail has grown from a $159,000 business to one grossing $18,000,000 annually. Initially, most of the revenue from the air mail stamps went to aid the development of the young airlines. Now, about 89 per cent of the revenue of the air mail stamp goes to the Government.
A Post Office spokesman, in fact, recently said that airmail is the only major mail service that returned a profit!
The advent of jets will also point up the growing importance of the airport.
