The Airlines' New Responsibility
Aircraft powered by jet propulsion are embarking on a new mission—a mission of service to the public.
The Civil Jet Age will be an Age which will see many benefits for the passenger, the commerce and the postal service.
But, more than that, the inauguration of the new jet aircraft will provide for an enormous contribution to the national defense.
This booklet describes in brief not only some of the benefits that will flow from this newest of Ages, but also points out some of the problems facing the scheduled airlines today as they prepare to assume this new, and all-important, responsibility.
STUART G. TIPTON, President The Air Transport Association of America
Washington, D. C.
