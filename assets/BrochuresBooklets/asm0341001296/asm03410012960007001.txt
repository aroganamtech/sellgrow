Climbing easily at 345 miles per hour, the jet will reach 10,000 feet in seven minutes and will take only 30 minutes to reach normal cruising altitude—30,000 to 40,000 feet.
Levelling off, there will be an absence of sensation. Cruising along at about 600 miles per hour, with practically no vibration, passengers will find it easy to balance a coin on end. Your after-dinner coffee will be a smooth sea, no ripples will mar its face.
The sheer speed of the jet alone demonstrates the ever-expanding usefulness, and service, of commercial flight. Measured in terms of one-hour-in-the-air, each advance in airliners has widened the business, cultural and recreational circle of any given city.
When the jets come in, they will join their fellow airliners flying over a vast network of highways in the sky.
