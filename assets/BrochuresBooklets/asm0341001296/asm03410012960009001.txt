The system now in use, though in need of modernization, is safe. So safe, in fact, that it is now 22 times as safe to fly on a scheduled airliner as it is to travel by auto! For the past six years, the fatality rate in air travel has been less than one per 100 million passenger miles, or a safety rate of 99.9974%.
The airlines, of course, pay their fair share for the use of the system operated by the Civil Aeronautics Administration. Last year, the domestic airlines paid over $25 million in fuel taxes —an amount equal to the industry’s net profit for the year.
Because airlines, government agencies and the electronic industry have cooperated so well, the system has been steadily, and noticeably, improved. Increasing efficiency and safety is, and will be, the result.
