Looking beyond the benefits that the jet age will bring to U.S. citizens, the commerce and the postal service, there is an all-important aspect to the jet transition—the national defense.
The commercial jet transports—all 304 of those now on order plus subsequent additions to the airlines’ fleet—are counted upon to be a major factor in military planning.
The Civil Reserve Air Fleet (CRAF) is designed for “minute man” service in a national emergency and now includes 314 long-range first-line planes with an estimated value of $550 million.
