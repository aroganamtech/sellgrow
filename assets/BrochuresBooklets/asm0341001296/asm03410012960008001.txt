The network is the Air Traffic Control System and comprises 158,000 miles of Federally-owned and operated airways.
Generally, airways are 10 miles wide. Planes using the ATC system fly the centerline of an electronic path, leaving plenty of leeway on both sides. In addition, there is separation of 1,000 feet above and 1,000 feet below. Ample space in front and in back is made possible by the regulated time intervals at which planes are “fed” into the system.
The major problem of the day for all users of the airspace is simply this: We’re running out of this valuable, natural resource.
Great chunks of sky are reserved for missile testing, national preserves, TV towers and military defense areas.
