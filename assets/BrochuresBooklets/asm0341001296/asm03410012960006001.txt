The smallest of the pure jets will be over 124 feet long. The longest will be more than 150 feet from nose to tail. The average distance from wing tip to wing tip: 135 feet, longer than the distance covered by the first flight of the Wright Brothers at Kitty Hawk.
What will it be like to fly in these new machines? Jet cruising altitudes will be six to eight miles high—above most weather. Outside, the temperature will be 50 degrees below zero but in the cabin a comfortable climate will prevail. Though you’re 35,000 feet above the earth, the cabin will be pressurized to 5,000 feet.
