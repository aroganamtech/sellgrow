Chapter 19
OUR PASSENGER SERVICES
By the time you have completed your basic training in our classrooms, and in the Jet Clipper “mock-up”, you will be so familiar with our passenger services that you will be able to recite them in your sleep, and performing these services will become second-nature. Here is a preview of what we have to offer in the way of different classes of air travel.
FIRST CLASS "PRESIDENT SPECIAL" SERVICE Seating
Four abreast, two on each side of the aisle. The seats are far apart, giving the passenger enough leg room to stretch out comfortably regardless of his height. These Jet Clipper seats are the most comfortable and luxurious ever offered to the air traveler. They are upholstered with fluffy foam rubber, in nylon fabric. Each seat reclines fully. Unique tables, fastened to the forward seat, can be adjusted to suit the individual traveler’s requirements.
The service is presented and executed with all the éclat that exemplifies the most renowned international dining rooms. An elaborate cocktail—aperitif—canape service precedes the meal. The large integral seat tables then are set with large continental style tablecloths and napkins, the finest china, silverware, centerpiece with floral decoration, and French crystal stemware.
The first of a multi-course dinner is the Continental Hors d’oeuvres course, featuring a selection of delicacies including fresh Iranian caviar, Pâté de Foi Gras, and smoked salmon, served at the passengers’ tables from the rolling Clipper buffet.
Dining Service
Pan Am’s First Class “President Special” Service always has set the highest standard for fine dining in the air, and, in the over-ocean Jets, it has become the ultimate in dining elegance.
