BASIC DIVISIONAL FUNCTIONS
Each operating Division of Pan American Airways is organized into departments, with certain specific responsibilities and duties. We can group all of these departments, generally, into those which—
PRODUCE air transportation . . . OPERATIONS .
perform ASSISTING functions
. . . STAFF SERVICES . . .
All Division departments are under the administrative control of the Division Executive Vice-President, who reports to Pan Am’s President, and is responsible for his Division’s activities.
The Executive Vice-President is aided by a Division Manager and/or other Executive Officers or Special Assistants. He is further assisted by specialists and superintendents who head the various Division departments.
