INTRODUCTION
. . . Welcome to membership in a great and vital organization—the Pan American Airways System. Naturally, you’ll want to get off to the best possible start . . . and to help you do just that, we have prepared this Indoctrination course. It’s basic purpose is to give you a sense of relationship to your company . . . and to show you where, and how, your job fits into the over-all Pan American picture.
