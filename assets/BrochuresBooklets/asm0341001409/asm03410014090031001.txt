COMMISSARY-—They’re in charge of the specially prepared Pan American food, which they load aboard the plane along with the buffet and galley equipment. Commissary is also responsible for headrest covers, blankets, first-aid kits.
PASSENGER SERVICE — They offer a friendly smile and a welcoming hand to our customers. They help passengers embark and disembark from the plane . . . assist them in clearing customs . . . checking health certificates . . . and other similar governmental formal-ities.
PASSENGER TRAFFIC — These are the people who check-in our passengers . . . make sure their tickets and passports are in order. They also weigh-in passenger baggage. Others make up general declarations, prepare seat charts and give weight and balance close-out figures to Dispatch.
