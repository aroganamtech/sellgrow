0930! A half-hour before departure and the passengers are coming on board.
The Purser is at the entrance to greet and welcome our guests. Pm assigned to the lounge, asking each one for his seat number and name, and assisting them to their seats. Susan, the other stewardess, is down the aisle, assisting them also.
Well, they’re all seated. By the way, you’d better go to your seat, too . . . the “Fasten Seat Belt—No Smoking” signs are on. Now, I’ll check their seat belts, help out wherever I’m needed.
“Good morning, ladies and gentlemen. This is Pan American’s Jet Clipper service, Flight 100 to London. Captain Johnson and all of us are proud to welcome you aboard. Our estimated flight time from New York to London will be six hours and five minutes. We will be flying at an altitude of 31,000 feet. In just a little while the Captain will give you a complete report on our flight plan and route.”
That was the announcement over the public address system by our Purser. Now, since we have quite a few Swedish passengers who are going on to Sweden from London, he’ll make the same announcement in Swedish. If this were a flight to Frankfurt or Rome, he or one of us would make the announcement in German or Italian.
Now we have the life jacket and oxygen mask demonstrations. The Purser will speak while we go through the demonstration. These are required by law for every airplane that flies over water and above 25,000 foot altitude.
I’ll come back to you when they’re all seated and comfortable.
