GUIDED MISSILES RANGE DIVISION (GMRD)
Another Pan American component is the Guided Missiles Range Division (GMRD). Pan Am is the prime contractor to the United States Air Force to administer the facilities at the Guided Missiles Test Center at Cape Canaveral, Florida and at Fort Huachuca, Arizona.
TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (TAP)
We also have technical assistance teams, under contract with the Agency for International Development (AID) to help friendly nations to develop their resources and improve their working and living conditions through the exchange of technical knowledge and skills.
Our teams are working with the national airlines of Pakistan and Afghanistan.
INTERCONTINENTAL HOTELS CORPORATION (IHC)
We are also in the hotel business. In 1946, Intercontinental Hotels Corporation (IHC) was organized as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Pan Am to help develop hotel accommodations in areas of the world where the lack of such facilities created an obstacle to travel.
Today, IHC is the world’s largest overseas hotel corporation. We now operate 15 hotels in Latin America, the Caribbean, Australia and Lebanon, and are building hotels in the following countries: Austria, West Germany, Switzerland, Ireland, India, Pakistan, Ivory Coast, Jordan and Singapore.
IHC’s headquarters is in New York City.
U. S. SALES
Although we don’t carry passengers within the United States, with the exception of Hawaii and Alaska, Pan Am is a United States flag carrier. As a result, a sizable part of our total passenger and cargo revenue originates in the United States.
Because of this, we have District Sales Offices in the United States acting as a sales organization for both Pan Am Divisions.
