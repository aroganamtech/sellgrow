Tummy Flattener: Lie flat on floor (while boning up on French verbs, for instance). Raise arms at right angle to body. Now rise slowly to sitting position, keeping legs flat on floor. 10 times.
Waistline and rib cage: With head up, arms on hips, feet apart, bend forward at waist. Rotate upper torso, stretching to right, then back and to the left side. Make eight circles, slowly.
Thigh and Hips: Stand with arms outstretched, palms against wall. Drop head, bend right knee close to head. Raise head, swing leg back and high. Repeat with left leg. 10 times each leg.
Thigh and Hips: Lie on floor on your side, arms before you for balance. Raise left leg high as possible, keep knees straight. Do 12 times; repeat with right leg.
Calves and Thighs: Stand on tiptoes; grasp back of chair, one hand on hip. Bend knees and lower body slowly to squatting position. Return to starting position. Do 10 times.
