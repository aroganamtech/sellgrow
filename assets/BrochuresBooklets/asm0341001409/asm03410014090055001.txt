PAN AMERICAN SPELLS COURTESY
Our business brings us into contact with people from many nations, from many parts of the world. Just as you want them to respect your country, your customs, your language—it’s your job to respect theirs. Every Pan American customer is a Pan Am guest — and is entitled to the best service and most courteous attention, regardless of race, creed or color.
Since our customers may use many of our routes, at many different times, our standards of courtesy must be uniformly high at every office and airport in the System . . . and on every flight. Courtesy is the one medium of exchange that is accepted gladly all over the world. It reflects the good will and “working togetherness” that has won us our enviable position.
ACTIONS SPEAK LOUDER
Many travelers today are still somewhat anxious about flying—especially over large bodies of water. Even those who aren’t anxious like to feel secure— like to know that everything on their flight is in per-
fect working order. All travelers—anxious or otherwise—will form their impressions of a flight from the behavior of the airline personnel with whom they come in contact.
If you get flustered and irritable ... or “fly off the handle” . . . you’ll convey the impression of an organization that can’t be relied on. You’ll heighten passenger anxiety.
On the other hand, a calm, assured manner inspires confidence. Anxiety and frustration can largely be overcome by good communication . . . courteous treatment . . . considerate behavior.
Remember—even though you won't find it on the ticket, or in the tariff . .. SERVICE is a part of every Pan American sale . . . and providing that service is everyone’s responsibility. A vital, important responsibility . . . because . . . business goes where it’s wanted and stays where it’s well treated.
