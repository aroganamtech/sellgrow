This is a cockpit showing the seating positions for a typical flight. There are slight variations in positioning, depending on the type of airplane, and the length of the flight.
The Captain is in absolute command. The First Officer is the co-pilot, and is the Captain’s right-hand man. The Flight Engineer is a specialist trained to know the airplane in terms of performance, engines, mechanical and electrical systems.
The Second Officer serves as the Navigator. He monitors the route, in keeping with the flight plan. He also monitors changes in headings required by weather conditions, etc.
The Stewardess is a member of the Flight Service Crew, NOT the Flight Crew. Of course, her main duties are to assist the passengers — helping them to enjoy their flight.
While at the controls, the Captain frequently makes announcements about the weather,the altitude, the landmarks below, arrival time, etc., a form of good customer relations, relieving the boredom of a long flight.
Destination reached, the end of another routine flight. Maintenance and Fleet Service have taken over. Soon, the Clipper will be ready for another flight and another Flight Crew.
