Here are some of the functions performed by our people at the airports to bring about this careful element of timing. These functions are handled by specialized groups or, sometimes, are combined and performed by a few people. However, the results are the same . . . because the way an airport job is done is based on Pan American experience and our high standards of performance.
MAINTENANCE — Our mechanics are men who know all about the engines and other working parts of our aircraft. These specialists make certain that our equipment is in perfect order, before every take-off ... in keeping with strict Pan American standards of operation for all of our flights.
AIRCRAFT FLEET SERVICEMEN —they clean the interior and exterior of the plane. Following the plan of weights and balance, they also load cargo, mail and baggage into the plane; and handle the ramp for passenger loading and unloading.
CARGO TRAFFIC — Specialists in servicing our cargo customers. They prepare, when necessary, cargo forms and air waybills. They’re also responsible for clearing cargo through customs if such facilities are located at the airport.
