OPERATIONS
The Operations Department is responsible for the efficient, safe and economical production of all seat and ton miles of “lift” — as required on the Division’s routes. To carry out this function properly, Operations is charged with the administration, development, maintenance, safety and efficiency of all flight and ground activities, in accordance with established Pan Am procedures and policies, and Division and government regulations.
Its responsibilities cover all operational activities, both inflight and on the ground—and its basic aim is to give maximum service to the needs of all of Pan Am’s customers. Sections within the Operations Department include Flight Operations (Chief Pilot and Flight Crews) . . . Communications . . . Maintenance . . . Aircraft Scheduling . . . Crew Scheduling . . . Route Scheduling . . . Meteorology . . . Dispatch . . . and Ground Operations.
