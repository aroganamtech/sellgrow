HIS STAFF
Assisting the President as aides and advisors are the Vice Presidents and other Officers who head the many departments of the Staff Components and the Services Components, better known as the System General Offices.
The Staff Components consist of the President and those executives who advise and assist him in the formulation and development of the company’s planning, organization, coordination, control and leadership, and in the appraisal of its performance.
The Services Components coordinate the specialized services within their respective areas, throughout the company.
The Operating Components are responsible and accountable for the successful conduct of all operating service functions within the framework of the company’s overall policies and programs.
The Pan American Organization is not really as complicated as it might seem at first glance . . . even though it has thousands of parts which must be organized into a smoothly interlocking pattern. Actually, the really important thing isn’t how the lines run up or down ... or who reports to whom, and why. It’s how we work together . . . how everyone fits in. The President and his officers couldn’t get along without us . . . and we couldn’t get along without them. It’s each of us doing what we know best that makes the organization work.
