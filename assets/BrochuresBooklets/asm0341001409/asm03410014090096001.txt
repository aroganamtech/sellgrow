UNIVERSAL AIR TRAVEL PLAN (UATP) ... a “charge account” system universally accepted by almost all scheduled airlines.
USEFUL LOAD... the crew and passengers, oil and fuel, ballast, other than emergency, ordnance or portable equipment.
VALIDATE .. . to stamp or write on the passenger ticket an indication that the passenger ticket has been officially issued by the carrier.
VALIDATION ... by validation is meant the indication on the ticket by a stamp of endorsement that the passenger has accepted the reservation as indicated on the ticket and that the ticket has been purchased and the reservation therefore is firm.
VHF . . . Very High Frequency, pertaining to radio signals.
VISA.. . an endorsement made on a passport by the proper authorities, denoting that it has been examined, and that the bearer may proceed.
VORTEX GENERATORS . . . rows of thin metal fins (look like playing cards stacked on end) on the top of
the 707 wings. They improve the flow of air over the wing and increase the effectiveness of the control surface on the trailing edge.
WEIGHT AND BALANCE MANIFEST ... the form usually required in determining the amount and distribution of loads in an airplane, in relation to its flight plan, runways, atmospheric pressure, temperature and many other factors.
WEIGHT EMPTY. . . the empty weight of the airplane used in the determination of operating weights.
WING . . . that part of an airplane which extends out from the fuselage and which provides the “lift”, being usually thought of as the whole area extending from wingtip to wingtip, on both sides of the fuselage.
YAW . . . the action which occurs when the nose of the airplane turns right or left.
ZERO MERIDIAN . .. Greenwich, the point from which the equator longitude is counted, both east and west. (See also Meridian, Ante Meridian, Post Meridian.)
ZONE TIME ... synonymous with Standard Time.
