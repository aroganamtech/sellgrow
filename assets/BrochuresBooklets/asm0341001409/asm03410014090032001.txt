FLIGHT SERVICE — Pursers, Stewards and/or Stewardesses, make our passengers comfortable during a flight. Before each flight, they check the cabin, see that safety equipment is aboard, familiarize themselves with food to be served, and make certain that special dietary foods (previously requested by passengers) are available.
DISPATCH—Many vital functions are coordinated here. Scheduling and briefing of flight crews . . . figuring of weight and balance manifests . .. reports on the weather . . . keeping in contact with the plane during its flight . . . are some of the activities of the Dispatch Office.
FLIGHT CREW — This usually consists of the Captain, the First Officer, the Second Officer and the Flight Engineer. The Captain is in complete charge of the plane and is assisted by his First Officer, second in command. The Second Officer is the Navigator who is also a qualified pilot. He plots the course of the plane and coordinates the route through air-to-ground contact. The Flight Engineer checks the functioning of all mechanical equipment both in the air and on the ground.
