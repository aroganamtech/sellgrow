The resulting figure is known as the WEIGHT EMPTY EQUIPPED. Next, we subtract this WEIGHT EMPTY EQUIPPED from the GROSS TAKE-OFF WEIGHT, as previously established. The remaining figure is known as the ALLOWABLE USEFUL LOAD (AUL). In other words, the ALLOWABLE USEFUL LOAD is the actual weight which can safely be added to our empty plane.
