Chapter 6
AIRPORT FUNCTIONS
An explanation of the functions
of Pan American employees who work at airports all over the world.
If an international airline like Pan American Airways is to fulfill its mission, a dramatic event must happen hundreds of times each day, in airports all over the globe.
This is the moment of departure or the moment of arrival of a Pan American Clipper. Wherever you work; whatever job you perform-—everything you do leads to these important moments. It probably seems a fairly simple thing to our customers. They buy their tickets or arrange for cargo shipments. Passengers and cargo arrive at the airport. The plane is there . . . ready . . . waiting for them.
But, actually it takes the combined knowledge and working effort of over 21,000 Pan American people to put one passenger ... or one piece of cargo into the air!
At airports all over the world, the careful skills of Pan American people are blended into one of the most exciting elements of airline operations . . . the element of timing.
