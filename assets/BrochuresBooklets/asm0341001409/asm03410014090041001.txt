Chapter 9
PAN AM COMPONENT STRUCTURES
A brief explanation of the structure and functions of Pan Am's Divisions
by
Each of Pan Am’s Divisions functions within the framework of certain general rules and policies set down our Head Office.
Since each of our Divisions operates in different parts of the world, with differing problems of geography, international law, etc., they each face different problems. So, in order to fulfill its mission successfully each Division has set up its own organization, keyed to its own needs.
More important, however, is the fact that most basic functions are similar. In many respects, each Division’s organization resembles that of Pan Am’s System General Office . . . and many System General Office functions have their counterparts in the Divisions.
So, even though the “organizational framework” of your Division may be different from the other, remember that basically, the departmental functions of both our Divisions are the same.
