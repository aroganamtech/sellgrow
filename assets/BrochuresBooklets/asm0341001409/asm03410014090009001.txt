THE FUTURE
Also at this very moment, on the threshold of the Jet Age, plans of aircraft for the next stage of flight — the supersonic commercial airliner — are on the drawing boards.
Within your lifetime, within the span of your career with Pan Am, the whole world may really be as accessible as the nearest town. And, as in the past, all that happens in the future of the company—will depend upon the girls who type the letters, maintenance men, weather men, pilots and navigators, engineers, accountants, porters and baggage men, people in Reservations, Scheduling, Purchasing and Flight Service.
The air transportation business is constantly changing, creating real challenges, demanding flexibility and adaptability, depending upon the acquired experience of more than 23,000 employees to keep Pan Am in its position of leadership.
