GROOMING, POSTURE AND WEIGHT
Naturally, when you were chosen by our panel of interviewers, your grooming, your health and the way in which you carried yourself all played an important role in the decision. But we thought that you would appreciate some tips on how to keep your general health and appearance in top condition.
EXERCISE
The most important way to maintain your health, of course, is to get the proper amount of rest and the proper amount of the right exercise. Plenty of good, sound sleep will give you the rest that you need. Good, wholesome exercise will keep you trim and feeling “on top of the world”.
The duties that you will perform on the Clippers will, of course, provide you with exercise that will be well distributed throughout your body—walking, stretching, bending. In addition, between assignments, and especially on “lay-overs”, swimming and good, long walks in the sunshine and fresh air (and you will do plenty of that sightseeing), are wonderful tonics for body tone, health and spirits.
However, if you feel that you would like to do some simple exercises to supplement these, the following are recommended. They can be done in the privacy of your room, and take only minutes of your time:
Waist: Kneel, with knees apart and arms outstretched at shoulders. Keeping head up, swing upper body around to left as far as you can. Then swing to right, twisting hard. 10 times.
Bosom: Stand, feet apart, hands clasped behind. Bend forward from waist, keeping back and legs straight. Stretch hands away from body as you bounce from waist. Repeat 5 times.
Legs: This you can do any time you’re telephoning. Point right toe as you stretch right leg hard. Without bending knee, raise and lower leg quickly ten times. Repeat with left leg.

Tummy: Let your favorite records spin as you do this. Lie on your back, on the floor. With legs straight, feet together, raise legs 12 inches off floor. Count to 5; lower legs. Do 5 times.
