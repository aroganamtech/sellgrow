This is one of our Jet Clippers. Let’s take a look at the men who fly these machines, worth $6,000,000, and carrying up to 168 passengers in Economy Class service.
Here are the men who fly our Clippers. Pan American has trained and developed the finest, most experienced crews in the air transport business, guiding our planes over thousands of miles with an outstanding record of safety and efficiency.
Through careful methods of selection, they are screened and tested. Once they pass the tests, they must pass rigid medical examinations given by Pan Am and the Federal Aviation Agency.
Then comes a period of intensive training. Many hours are spent in the classroom before entering the cockpit. Each member of a Pan Am crew is one of the most highly-trained men in the world, and he must return at least twice a year for refresher training.
“Flights” are made in Link Trainers and in Flight Simulators which allow practice of emergency situations economically and safely.
-Training in emergency procedures such as “ditching” is thorough. In this session, the uninflated life raft is thrown out the door. It automatically becomes inflated by the line running from it to the plane. Every member of the flight crew undergoes this kind of “wet drill” at least once a year.
