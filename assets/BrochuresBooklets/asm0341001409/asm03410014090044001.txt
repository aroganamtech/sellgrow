DIVISION TRAFFIC and SALES
The Traffic and Sales Department is responsible for all traffic, sales, service and advertising activities—and for the functional supervision of all Traffic and Sales offices in the Division. In other words—it is charged with the sale and efficient utilization of the seat and ton miles of “lift” produced by Operations. Traffic/ Sales responsibilities include, among other things, the sale of company air transport service, development and placement of advertising and sales promotion programs, development of fares and rates to help increase revenue, and the supervision of travel agent and interline activities within the Division.
Sections within the Traffic/Sales Department include Passenger Traffic . . . Cargo Traffic . . . Passenger and Clipper Cargo Sales . . . Passenger Service . . . Flight Service . . . Reservations . . . Schedules . . . Advertising ... and Sales and Service Training.
STAFF SERVICES
To help perform assisting functions, and quite similar to the System General Offices are the Staff Services, which include Services of Supply . . . Economic Planning . . . Legal . . . Public Relations . . . Medical . . . Industrial Relations . . . and Accounting Departments.
