JET AGE ACCESSORIES
Don’t you agree that our ensemble is flattering? Of course, it must also be complemented by the right accessories, otherwise it means nothing. Here are Pan Am’s requirements along these lines:
Nylons
A subdued, light color. This beautifies and gives a natural good look to the legs.
Jewelry
On duty you may wear one simple ring, one chain and cross, or a religious medal of conservative size, if the chain is of such length that the medal or cross do not show at the neck. Official Pan Am earrings are the only type permitted. A watch is also recommended, and it is a good idea to keep it set at Greenwich Time, so that you may keep your passengers informed of the correct time anywhere in the world, if they should ask (and they do). Remember the chapter “Time Conversions”, in which you read about how to tell Greenwich Time from the Pan Am System Time Table?
