You know, I can’t really tell you what it’s really like being a Pan Am stewardess, because it means different things to different people.
One thing I can say . . . it’s glamorous. It’s glamorous and it’s very, very interesting. But it also calls for plenty of plain hard work, and once in a while it does upset your social calendar. One time, you’re home on a weekend, other times you’re home for several days, and then you’re off again. You simply can’t plan any dates months in advance. But it has plenty of compensations. You do get to meet many interesting people and get to see so many different and fascinating things and places.
Take my last few weeks, for instance. Three weeks ago, I flew to Bangkok, Thailand. We had a two day layover there, so two other girls and I visited the Temple of the Reclining Buddha and took a boat trip on the Cha Phya River. On the way back, I picked up a beautiful piece of Neilloware. The week before last, I was in Portugal and Rome.
Yes, the rewards are many, if you’re quick to adapt to new situations, and enjoy meeting and helping people of different ways of life.
Well, here we are at Pan Am’s departure building . . . didn’t take long, did it?
Let me check in my bags, then we’ll go to the Briefing Room, where the day really begins.
First, I check my mailbox. Oh, good! My vacation schedule has been approved! You know, that’s another one of the advantages . . . one month’s vacation and 90 percent off the regular published fare to anywhere in the world! This time, I’m going to travel ’round South America. I can hardly wait!
