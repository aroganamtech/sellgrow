NOTE TO: JTT
You will recall my having mentioned the preparation of an elementary "Bible” to be used by our people - particularly in the grate mots - in acquainting appropriate pertont with tome essential facte about our Company. We had in mind particularly tome of the teeming ignorance and improper allegationa which appeared in the Appropriationt Hearingt last spring» and which may appear again in the tame quarter during the next month or two.
At you may alto recall» Mr. Rut tell Adams suggested something along this line after the subject had been raised by tome of hit friends in Washington.
Mr. Friendly very kindly dictated the original draft and Mr. Ferguson hat been supplying the requisite statistics and charts.
We are now preparing a limited number of inexpensive copies from the text attached hereto.
The three of us request your advice as to whether you find anything unsuitable or inappropriate in the text and charts.
John C. Leslie
cc: Vice Pres. Friendly, Legal, NY Mr. R. G. Ferguson, Treat., NY
