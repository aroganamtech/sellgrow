... And why Florida needs the Pan Am Connection
The importance of superb, efficient, competitive air service between Florida and London isn’t limited to providing ease of travel for the people of Florida.
It is also a pipeline of tourist and business dollars into the Florida economy from Europe.
Nearly two-thirds of the traffic on National’s Miami-London route originates in Europe.
Europeans are eager to come to Florida, which is becoming known as the “New Spain,” or new vacation spot, for Europeans.
Pan Am’s proven marketing skills and strong identity as an international airline in Europe mean that we can promote Florida effectively in Europe. With the Heathrow connection, we can deliver these visitors with a minimum of inconvenience.
No other carrier can enhance Florida’s tourism and business potential as well as Pan Am, working with the people from National who built this service in the first place.
No other carrier can offer so much to the Florida traveler.
Also, the new Pan Am will be an airline with very strong Florida ties. We got our start here. So did National. We are staking much of our future in Florida, and are planning steps to make Miami a truly international aviation crossroads. We are giving Tampa the international service it has been denied for too long.
By combining National’s domestic Florida routes with Pan Am’s system, we are tying in millions of Floridians with the Pan Am worldwide network. And a key part of this system is the feed into Miami, and in the case of European and beyond service, travel to London and Heathrow Airport.
Florida’s air travel future looks exciting—with Pan Am Miami-London (Heathrow) service.
