What’s Best for Florida? The Key Question
In giving its approval to the merger ot Pan American World Airways and National Airlines, the Civil Aeronautics Board said the combined airline would continue to operate National’s Miami-London route while at the same time, the CAB will hold a separate hearing on which airline should serve it on a permanent basis.
It might not come to that—President Carter, who now has the merger before him for review, could decide to include the Miami-London route in the merger package and give it to Pan Am on a permanent basis. But if it does come to a hearing, the combined Pan Am/National will be a vigorous contender.
Several airlines already have applied for the Miami-London route. No doubt, each will be able to show how the route will help their operations and be good for them.
But that is not the key question. Floridians must decide what is good for Florida.
To make that decision, we urge Floridians to compare what Pan Am/National can offer with the Miami-London route as the heart of its Florida-Europe service with any other applicant.
A close comparison, based not on a particular airline’s self-- interest, but on the benefits offered to Florida, makes it clear that continued operation of this important route by the combined Pan Am/National is best for Florida.
