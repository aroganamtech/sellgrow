The Sunshine Connection
Why Pan Am/National should retain the Miami-London route—
Heathrow i s the London airport Floridians want to fly into. Only Pan Am/National can continue Heathrow service; any new U.S.-flag carrier would have to land at less-convenient Gatwick Airport.
Florida travelers can expect superior European service.
More Europeans, both inbound tourists and
business travelers, will visit Florida.
Pan Am’s single-carrier world connections beyond London are unparalleled among U.S.-flag airlines.
National employees who pioneered the route will be able to continue to develop European service through the merged Pan Am/National.
Miami-London i s the key to Pan Am’s ambitious Florida-Europe plans.
No Other U.S. airline can offer Floridians so much.
The Merged carrier will bring new visitors to Miami as a result of new Latin America-Miami-Europe stopover fares.
