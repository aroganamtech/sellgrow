Why Pan Am/National needs the Miami-London Connection
The merged carrier has ambitious air travel plans tor Florida, not just for European service, but for extensive Latin American, Pacific, African and U.S. connections to its global system.
But clearly, the heart of its European-Florida plans is the Miami-London (Fieathrow) route.
This is the linchpin of the European “Sunshine Connection” built by the dedicated people of National Airlines, who will become part of the merged carrier. It was their vision, their hard work and their faith that forged such strong ties between Florida and Europe. It seems only right that they be able to continue strengthening the Florida economy and making European travel more convenient for Florida and Florida-bound air travelers.
Equally important, the Miami-London route has been the principal profit route for the European service pioneered by National.
It has enabled National to expand service to other European cities—and it will continue to anchor further development plans by the combined Pan Am and National.
With the Miami-London operation, the merged Pan Am and National will be able to step up competition with foreign airlines and improve service between Florida and Europe.
Without Miami-London, there is a gaping hole in our European-Florida travel plans. By making the most of London connections throughout our global system, we can be the most efficient and productive—and therefore give you the most for your travel dollar.
