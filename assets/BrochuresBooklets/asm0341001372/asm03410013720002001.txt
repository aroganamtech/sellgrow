Astro Photographers now makes available high quality glossy photographs of the aircraft that made America’s commercial aviation history.
Included are the Fokker F-7 which flew this country’s first scheduled international service between Key West and Havana, the pioneer Ford “Tin Goose”, the famous “China Clipper” and “Yankee Clipper” flying boats, and today’s high-speed jets.
These are offered in both the large 8" x 10" professional size and the smaller 4" x 5" collector size. They can prove invaluable for the serious collector, student and aviation enthusiast.
