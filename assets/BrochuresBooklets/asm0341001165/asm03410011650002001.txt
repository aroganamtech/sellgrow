NOW the whole family can fly to EUROPE on Pan Am’s new FAMILY FARE PLAN
Here’s the biggest travel news since Pan Am pioneered low-cost tourist fares. Now wives can go along with their husbands on that trip to Europe. Even entire families can fly abroad during Thrift Season for little more than the parents alone would pay in summer. Routine business trips can turn into family holidays in Europe. Parents can really enjoy vacations abroad, sharing them with the children.
You travel during “Thrift Season”
. . . when Europe’s uncrowded, less expensive and offers better choice of accommodations. The FAMILY FARE PLAN applies on trans-Atlantic travel starting after Oct. 31st and ending before April 1st. While half the fun’s traveling together, you don’t have to, except to your first stopover.
How the plan works
You save on the fares for your wife and your children 12-25 years of age. The Plan doesn’t apply to younger children, since those under 12 always travel at half fare on Pan American . . . and an infant under 2, of course, pays only 10%.
Go via Rainbow or President service. On The Rainbow, you pay the low Thrift Season Round Trip Tourist fare for yourself and save $200 on each of the other Family Fares. On The President, you pay the Thrift Season Round Trip First Class fare and save $300 on each of the other Family Fares. On one-way trips, each fare saving is $130 on The Rainbow...% 150 on The President.
What’s more, you can finance the trip on Pan Am’s Pay Later Plan . . . pay 10% down and take up to 20 months to pay. See other side for detailed Round Trip savings.
