PLAN A TRIP
With the preliminary “look-see” completed, select ail imaginary destination and draw up an itinerary for a trip to the place chosen.
Do this step by step as follows:
1.	Find your destination.
Look on the map (center spread) for the city you have selected as your imaginary destination. Suppose you have selected Damascus which is in Syria. Find it on the map, and draw a circle around the name so you can find it again easily.
2.	Find the “Gateway City.”
Now find the “Gateway City” from which you will leave the United States on your journey to Damascus. Let us assume you want to leave from New York. Circle New York on the Map.
3.	Lay out your route.
Trace the direct route from New York to London, Brussels, Istanbul and Damascus.

Now what is that time of 22:40 for leaving Istanbul? If you have read the section “How to Use the Time Table” carefully you know already that Pan American time tables use the 24-hour clock. If you wish to convert 22:40 to the equivalent p.m. time, subtract 12 hours — it is the same as 10:40 p.m.
Eastern Standard Time of 75° West Time, Shannon and London are 0° time, Istanbul and Damascus have 30° East Time. The difference between New York and Damascus is 7 hours — Damascus is 7 hours ahead of New York — so from your total of 62 hours and 10 minutes, subtract 7. The true elapsed time is 55 hours and 10 minutes.
In working other examples, determine difference in time by consulting a time zone map (a big atlas will have one) or use the Pan American World Airways time disc.
To find the flying time add up the time the airplane spends on the ground when it stops, and subtract this from the total for elapsed time.
