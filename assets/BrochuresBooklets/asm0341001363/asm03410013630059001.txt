AFGHANISTAN
Jeshyn/Afghan Independence Celebration. National, international fairs. Local industry, handicrafts; national dances, sporting events. Kabul. Three days in last week of August.
Pashtunistan Day Celebration. Tribal dances, wrestling, sporting events. September 1.
Buzkashi. Famous Afghan equestrian game requiring superb team horsemanship. Object is to carry a beheaded calf to the goal. In provinces of Kunduz, Baghlan, Mazar-i-Sharif, Samagan, every Friday. October-April.
Kingfs Birthday Celebrations. Usually a Buzkashi game is held on this occasion. October 14.
BHUTAN
Tongsa Tsechu. Mask dances. January. Bhutanese New Year Celebrations. Archery, songs, dances, “Doigor" games. February.
Puna Domchhen. Lamas' mask dances performed in Punakha, the ancient Capital. February.
Paro Tsechu Festival. Mask dances. Paro Dzong. April, May.
King7s Birthday Festivities. May.
Saga Dawa. Religious ceremonies commemorate Lord Buddha's birth, death, attainment of Nirvana. June.
Thimphu Domchhen. Mask dances performed. Thimphu. September, October.
BRUNEI
Royal Malay Regiment's Birthday Celebrations. Parade, display of equipment, vehicles and aircraft of regiment. Brunei. May 31.
H.M. Queen Elizabeth II Birthday Celebrations. Parade. June 10.
H.H. The Sultan Birthday Celebrations.
Colorful decorations, illuminations, stage shows, sports, Funfair, lantern processions, parade. Lasts one week from July 15.
Constitutional Day Celebrations. Parade. September 29.
BURMA
Independence Day Celebration. Exhibitions, cultural shows, fireworks, dances, Burmese boat races. In Rangoon and large towns. Week beginning January 4. Water Festival and New Year. Nationwide celebrations. April 12-16.
May Day. Exhibitions of Burmese products, fireworks, cultural shows in Rangoon. May 1.
Buddha Day. Commemorates life of Buddha. Offerings of water at Shwe-da-gon pagoda in Rangoon and elsewhere. May 19.
Waso Festival. Buddhist Lent begins with pilgrimages to pagodas. July 17. Ngapali Beach. Beautiful natural beach open September to April.
Thatinkyut Festival. End of Buddhist Lent. Pilgrimages, nationwide festival of lights. October 13-15.
Thasaungdaing Festival. Colorful lights, decorations in Rangoon.
November 11-13.

CAMBODIA
Tet Festival. Chinese and Vietnamese New Year. Celebrations in the pagodas and in the streets. Genies parade, unicorns dance. Date varies. Usually January or February.
Chaul Chenam Festival. Cambodian New Year. Illuminations in Phnom Penh. Boxing matches. Chhoung plays, Ram Vong dances, other events, social entertainments in the villages.
April 13-15.
Kathen Festivals. Folk dances, music, songs, blessing stanzas, theatrical shows. One a year at every monastery during October-November.
Water Festival. Colorful boat racing, lights display at Royal Palace and public gardens, fireworks on Tonle Sap River and at Royal Palace, illuminated boat parade. Phnom Penh. November. Independence Day. Lights display, traditional and modern dances, songs, music, sports. Phnom Penh and towns. November 9.
CEYLON
National Heroes Day. Historical pageants. At Colombo. January 1.
Duruthu Perahera. Colorful procession at Kelaniya Temple, 5-1/2 miles from Colombo. Full Moon Day, January. Skin-Diving and Spear-Fishing Season. South and Southwest Coast, especially at Hikkaduwa. January-April. Independence Commemoration Day. Games, folk dances, pageants, parades, processions. February 4.
Sinhalese and Tamil New Year. Domestic festival with much merrymaking, particularly in villages. April.
May Day. Workers processions. May 1. Milad-Un-Nabi. Muslims celebrate birth of Prophet Mohamed. May.
Wesak. Commemorates birth, enlightenment, and death of Buddha. Most colorful Buddhist celebration of year. Festival of Lights. Full Moon Day, May. Poson. Prince Mahinda brought Buddhism to Ceylon in 247 B.C. Processions carry his statue; annual pilgrimage to Anuradhapura, Mihintale. Full Moon Day, June.
Dewinuwara. 10-day festival to honor God Vishnu, with processions of dancers, elephants, acrobats. Carnival. Bazaar. At Dondra. July-August.
Esala Perahera. Considered Asia's greatest pageant. Elephants, drummers, dancers, whip-crackers, temple dignitaries take part in procession at Dalada Mali-gawa (Temple of the Tooth), Kandy. Lasts for 10 consecutive nights. July-August.
Vel Festival. Procession, with music and dancing, carrying statue of the Hindu god. Colombo. July-August.
Deepavali Festival. Festival of Lights celebrated by all Hindus. November. Christmas Season. Series of gala nights in Colombo hotels. Christian celebrations. December 1-31.
Idu'l Fitr. Moslem festival of joy marking end of Ramazan, month of fasting. December.
Sanghamitta Day. Commemorates planting at Anuradhapura of Sacred Bo Tree—believed to be world's oldest authenticated tree, and a sapling of tree under which Buddha attained enlightenment. December.
