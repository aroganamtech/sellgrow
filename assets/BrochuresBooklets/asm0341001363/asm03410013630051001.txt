GUYANA
Republic Day. Festive occasion with parades, speeches. February 23.
Phagwah. Colorful Hindu festival, with throwing of red fluid on friends, exchange of gifts and feasting. Festival symbolizes end of winter and awakening of flowers, animals, etc., to herald coming of spring. In March.
Easter Festivities. Kite flying, dances and picnics in the country. April 11. Labour Day. Parade in Georgetown.
May 1.
Christmas and Boxing Day Festivities.
Carol singing, dances, parties throughout the country, steel-band tramps. December 25.

PARAGUAY
Festival of St. Blaze, patron saint of Paraguay. February 3.
Independence Days. Student and Army parades. May 14-15.
St. John's Night. Colorful, popular fiestas held at night at several city "barrios" (quarters), with barefooted "promisers" walking over bonfires, typical dances, fortune telling games. June 24.
Founding of Asuncion Celebrations. Asuncion. August 15.
Race Day. Columbus Day Celebrations. Also dedicated to Indians and Spanish who fused into Paraguayan "race". October 12.
All Saints Day. National holiday and religious festival. November 1.
Immaculate Conception of the Virgin of Caacupe Festivities. Include pilgrimages, religious celebrations, traditional fiestas. Caacupe. December 8.
PERU
Festival of the Virgin of the Candelaria.
Folk dance competition, religious procession. City of Puno. February 9.
Ancon Festival. Theatre, concerts, ballet, and songfest. Ancon. March. International Vintage Festival. Election and parade of queens and court. Ica province. March 8-16.
Holy Week Celebrations. Notable in Arequipa, Ayacucho, Cuzco and Tarma. April 4-11.
Inti-Raymi. Inca Festivity of the Sun, held in Sacsahuaman fortress at Cuzco. June 24.
Independence of Peru National Festival.
Celebrations, fairs, parades nationwide. July 28, 29.
Spring Fiesta. Election of queen, folkloric parades, songs, folk dance competition. Trujillo. September 23-30.
International Pacific Fair. Some 45 countries represented at trade and commerce fair. Lima. October.
Lord of Miracles Procession. Faithful in purple habits accompany image of Lima's patron saint. October 18,19, 28. Puno Week. Legend of Inca Empire's founders acted out; hundreds of boats sail on Lake Titicaca; song and dance contests, handicraft fairs. Puno. November 1-7.
Christmas Celebrations. Notable in Trujillo, Lima, and Liociapampa. December.
SURINAM
New Year's Celebrations. Fireworks. January 1.
Chinese New Year. Parties, fireworks, other celebrations. January or February—varying with Lunar Calendar. Carnival. Costume parties at clubs. Last Saturday before Lent. February 20. Queen Juliana's Birthday Celebration. Parades, other events. April 30.
Freedom Day. Celebrates basic freedoms of man, commemorates abolition of slavery and Surinam's status with Holland and Netherlands Antilles as equal partner in Kingdom of The Netherlands. Parades, Kotto-Missie shows. July 1. Konfriejarie/People's Fair. Week-long festivities. August-September.
Trade Fair of Surinam. Last week, September, first week, October.
National Chinese Holiday. Chinese Community celebrates with fireworks, dragon dances, other events. October 10. Diwalie. Hindu Festival of Light. November.
St. Nicholas Day. St. Nick joins parade, distributes gifts to children.
December 5, 6.

URUGUAY
Carnival. Traditional parade with Carnival Queen, huge decorated floats, giant dolls, merry troupes of masqueraders, grand pageantry in Montevideo. 4 days Mid-February.
Semana Criolla/Creole Rodeo Week.
Renowned Gaucho Rodeo with spectacular competitions, performances. Horse Show. Horse-breaking exhibitions. Folk Singing, Dancing. Open-air Barbecues. Other Events. Late March—early April. Livestock Fair. Similar to the Semana Criolla but on a smaller scale. Highlights livestock auctions. Montevideo. August.
