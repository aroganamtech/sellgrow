SAUDI ARABIA
Eid Al-Adha. Feast of The Sacrifice. Nearly one million Muslims from all over the world perform the Haj, or pilgrimage, to Mecca and the other holy places in Saudi Arabia. Date governed by Lunar Calendar.
Eid Al-Fitr. The Feast of the Breaking of the Fast. Muslim religious ceremonies follow Ramadan, the holy month of fasting. Date governed by Lunar Calendar.

TURKEY
Skiing Competitions. Bursa, Kayseri, Ankara, Erzurum, etc. January.
Anzac Day. Celebrations commemorating the Anniversary of Canakkale Naval Victory (Dardanelles). Canakkale. March 18.
Uludag Winter Festival. Ski competitions precede folk festivals, dances, and parades at Mount Olympus. First week in April.
Spring Festival. Centuries-old tradition celebrates coming of Spring. A special candy, believed to give youth and strength, is distributed free in the streets. Fair, folk dances. Manisa.
April 16-26.

Spring Festivities. In all cities and towns. May 1.
