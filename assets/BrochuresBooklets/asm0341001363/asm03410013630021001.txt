TOGO
Independence Day Celebrations. Parades, processions, colorful folk dances, games. Festivities in Lomé, towns and villages. April 27.
Agbogbo Zan. Ewe People's traditional festival commemorates their liberation from domination of Chief Agokoli. Representatives of Ewe People from Dahomey, Ghana, Nigeria and Togo gather for festivities. Nuatja. September.
Kpe So So. End of lunar year for Mina People. "Sacred forest" is searched for symbolic stone: a white stone means a good and happy year, a brown or black stone an evil year. Religious ceremonies honor ancestors. Anecho. A movable feast about September.
Yeke-Yeke. New Year celebrations of Mina People. Colorful ceremonies, festivities, folk dances. Anecho. A movable feast about September.
TUNISIA
Flower and Orange Festival. Nabeul. April.
Tunis International Fair. International exhibition of economic progress. Tunis. Every two years, April-May.
Dougga Festival. Classical plays at Roman ampitheatre. Dougga. June.
Rugs Fair at Kairouan. Exhibit of the well-known Kairouan rugs. June. Carthage Festival. Open air theatre at Antonius Therms. Folk dances, Andalusian music, theatre, jazz. Tunis. July. Hammamet Festival. Open air theatre. Same program as in Carthage Festival. Hammamet. July, August.
International Folklore Festival. August.
U.A.R. (EGYPT)
Sound and Light Shows. At the Pyramids and the Sphinx at Giza. At the Citadel in Cairo. Every night.
Moon Festival. Honors Egypt's ancient glories. Throughout Egypt. February. International Tennis Championships. Cairo and Alexandria. A number of the world's best players are invited. Late March.
Sham El Nessim and Spring Feast. Throughout Egypt. Late April or early May.
Anniversary of the Revolution. Celebrations in all Egypt. July 23.
Nile Flood Festival. Egypt's oldest traditional festival goes back to the Pharaohs' time. Agricultural, industrial progress displayed. Cairo. 2nd half, August. Ballet. Occasional performances. November through March.
The Hegira New Year, End of the Fasting Month (Ramadan), Bairam Feast, Islamic New Year are celebrated throughout Egypt. Dates vary, depending upon the Lunar calendar.
UGANDA
Labour Day Observations. May 1. Republic Day. Commemorates Uganda's becoming a Republic. Processions depict year's achievements. Military parades are highlight. Nationwide. September 8.
Independence Day. Celebrations throughout country with pageants, parades and traditional dances. October 9.
