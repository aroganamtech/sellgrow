ARGENTINA
International Folkloric Festival. Cos-quin. January 22-30.
"La Quebrada De Humahuaca" Carnival. Intoxicating "chicha" libations, street dancing, burning of Devil effigy. Jujuy. February.
Bacchus Festival of the Vineyards. Procession of regional floats. Election of Bacchanalian Queen. Mendoza. March. Fishing Festival. International trout and salmon fishing tournaments. Chubut Province. 7 days in March.
International Regatta Season. Tigre River, Buenos Aires. March.
National Cattle Show. Championships, prizes, exhibitions. One of the great shows of its kind. Buenos Aires. July. National Day of Argentina. Patriotic and traditional celebrations. July 9. Bariloche Snow Festival. Ski enthusiasts join in series of picturesque events ending winter season, ski championships. Bariloche. July-August.
Snow Festival and Ski Tournament. Special ski race known as "La Posta An-dina"—at night, the mountains are lit up by torches. Los Molles Vallecitos (Mendoza Province). July-August.
Dorado Fishing Season. International Fishing Tournament in National Park of Iguazu. August-November.
Fiesta Del Senor Y La Virgen Del Mill-agro. Religious festival in Salta. More than 100,000 are present for closing Grand Procession. September.
Polo Season Opens. Buenos Aires. September.
Arts Exhibitions. Buenos Aires. Septem-ber-November.
National Yachting Championship. Olivos, Buenos Aires. October.
International Fishing Contest. Trout and salmon. Bariloche. November.
"Day of Argentine Tradition". Dancing, singing, processions, horseback riding, camp-work, all in the Argentine tradition. November 10.
BOLIVIA
Folkloric music "Penas" every Friday and Saturday evening. In La Paz. Throughout year.
Religious festivities and native fairs.
Display of regional Indian costumes, music and dancing. In Lake Titicaca region. Usually once a week throughout year.
Fair of Alasitas. Display and sale of folkloric artcraft miniatures. La Paz.
January 24-31.
Carnival. Colorful and traditional festivities; demonstrations of "Diablada". Oruro. February 20, 21.
Ski Season Begins. La Paz. April 1.
La Paz Day. Civic and school parades. July 16.
Bolivian Independence Celebration.
School, civic and military parades and other celebrations throughout Bolivia. Early August.
BRAZIL
Nosso Senhor Do Bonfim (Our Lord of the Happy Ending) Festival. One of
most colorful of religious feasts lasts 10 days. Salvador, Bahia. January 20-30. Festival of Our Lady of Conception. On Itapoa beach, Salvador, Bahia.
February 2.
Carnival. (3 days preceding Ash Wednesday). World's biggest party includes a gigantic masquerade, public balls with dancing in the streets, organized parades of dancers (Escolas de Samba) and hundreds of private balls. Rio de Janeiro. February 21-23.
National Holiday. Celebrates Tiradentes (National hero), Discovery of Brazil and foundation of Brasilia. April 21.
San George's Day. Festivities in church of San George, Rio de Janeiro. April 24. Grand Sweepstake Horse Race. Sao Paulo. First Sunday in May.
Feast of Caravaggio. Pilgrimage to Church of Our Lady of Caravaggio. Far-roupilha, Rio Grande do Sul. 3rd and 4th weeks of May.
Feasts of Saints Anthony, John and Peter. Fireworks, paper lanterns, bonfires, Latin square dances. Countrywide.
June 13, 24,29.
Grand Sweepstake Horse Race in Rio de
Janeiro. 1st Sunday in August.
Our Lady of Glory Festival. Religious festivities. Outeiro da Gloria Church,
Rio de Janeiro. August 15.
Independence Day. National Holiday. Military parade. September 7.
Our Lady of Aparecida Feast. Pilgrimage to Aparecida do Norte, Sao Paulo, honoring patron saint of Brazil. Festival lasts for entire month. September 8.
Festival of Penha. Religious festivities in Church of Our Lady of Penha in Rio de Janeiro. October.
Our Lady of Nazareth (Feast of the Ci-rio). Greatest festival in Northern Brazil. Belem, Capital of Para. Festival lasts two weeks. October 13-27.
Christmas Celebrations. Elaborate pageants, songs, dances, folk plays, satirical drama. Entire country. December 25. Goddess Iemanja Ceremonies. Unusual Macumba ritual on Copacabana, Ipa-nema and Leblon beaches in Rio de Janeiro. December 31.
New Year's Eve. Carnival balls in Rio de Janeiro. December 31.
CHILE
Horse Racing. The Derby, Vina Del Mar, January. El Ensayo, Santiago, February.
Festival De La Cancion. Festival of Song. Vina Del Mar. February.
Penuelas Exposition. Sponsored by the Agricultural Society of the North. In Penuelas. February.
Rodeo. Los Andes. Late March. Vendimia. Vintage celebrations— dances, "ramadas", election of queen. Mainly in Aconcagua, Curico and Maule. April.
Semana Pomairina. Traditional festivities, famous for cider. Pomairina. Week in May.
Skiing Season. Central Region near Santiago and Southern Region. June-September.
Religious Holidays. St. Peter's Day. Image taken to sea and followed by fishermen in boats. Many places during June. Virgen de la Tirana, Iquique, in July. Virgen de Andacollo, Coquimbo province, December.
Agricultural and Artisan International Fair. Parque Cerillos. September. Independence Day. Celebrated with a Te Deum and popular festivities. September 18.
