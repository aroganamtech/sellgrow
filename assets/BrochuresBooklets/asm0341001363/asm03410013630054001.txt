AMERICAN SAMOA
Samoan Cricket Season. 50 to 100 barefoot contestants in each game. From November '70 to March, 1971. New season starts in November.
Easter. Visiting day between American Samoa and Western Samoa celebrated with sports competitions, other events. April 11.
Flag Day. Commemorates 1st American, 1st Samoan flag raising, constitutional government's establishment. Celebrated with boat racing, parades, singing, dancing; tug-of-war, basket-weaving, firemaking, coconut husking contests.
April 17.
White Sunday/Children's Day. Flower-garlanded children in white march singing to church. Recitations, pageants. Always the second Sunday in October. October 10.
Swarm of the Palolo. When moon and tide are right, palolo (the coral worm) emerge from reef to propagate. Samoans consider eggs a great delicacy—scoop them up in nets by light from gas lanterns. A carnival atmosphere sweeps the island as Samoans—and visitors—wade out to the reef. Late October or early November.
