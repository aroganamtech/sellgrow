On arrival in Kuala Lumpur, the capital of Malaysia, you will be transferred to your hotel. This afternoon’s sightseeing takes you to the .University of Malaya— Malaysia’s first university, along the Lake Gardens, passing the Houses of Parliament, National Monument and the National Mosque. This evening you must visit the “Tin Mine,” a simply fantastic disco situated right in your hotel. Tuesday you can explore on your own. Dinner, this evening, has been arranged in the exclusive Melaka Grill.
13TH THROUGH 15TH DAYS. KUALA LUMPUR/MANILA. Wednesday transfer to the airport for your flight to Manila. Afternoon and evening free. Thursday morning city sightseeing. Friday is free until the Fiesta Filipina dinner.
You’ll be intrigued by this cosmopolitan city on the Island of Luzon, one of 7,000 islands in the Philippines. Everywhere you go, you’ll see and hear “Mabuhay,” which means welcome—and they mean it.
Thursday morning you’ll visit Malacanang Palace, Santo Tomas,
Intramuros, the Spanish walled city and other interesting sights.
Friday is free; perhaps take an optional excursion to Lake Tagatay, but be back in time for your Fiesta Filipina dinner with cultural show.
16TH DAY. SATURDAY: MANILA/HONG KONG.
Transfer to the airport for your flight to Hong Kong. Day at leisure.
Back to Hong Kong on Saturday morning for one last look. Perhaps to have a final fitting at that marvelous Hong Kong tailor, or take another ride to the top of the Peak where you can have lunch or dinner. Tomorrow you are homeward bound, so make sure you have all those last minute gifts.
17TH DAY. SUNDAY:
HONG KONG/USA.
Transfer to the airport for your flight home. You recross the International Date Line, arriving in the USA Sunday morning.
