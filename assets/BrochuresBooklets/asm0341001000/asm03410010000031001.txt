1ST DAY. FRIDAY: USA/AUCKLAND.
Depart aboard your Pan Am Jet Clipper bound for Auckland.
Surf Carnival, Australia
2ND AND 3RD DAYS. AUCKLAND.
You cross the International Date Line, lose a day, arriving in Auckland on Sunday morning. Afternoon sightseeing, evening free.
Auckland, in the North Island, is New Zealand’s largest city situated on a narrow isthmus between the Pacific Ocean and Tasman Sea. The drive from the airport to your hotel takes you through parks, past beautifully kept houses with neat gardens and rolling tracts of pasture where sheep and horses graze. Quite unlike any other airport to city drive!
Sunday afternoon, your tour takes you across the Auckland Harbor Bridge to the seaside suburbs; climb to the summit of Mount Eden, an extinct volcano; have a wonderful view of the city!
4TH DAY. MONDAY: AUCKLAND/WAITOMO/ ROTORUA.
Morning departure for Rotorua, visit the Waitomo Glowworm Grotto en route. After lunch, continue to your hotel. Evening Maori dance concert.
Not far from Rotorua are the Waitomo Caves and their glowworm grottos. As
you descend the steps, the damp underground breeze cools your face. At the bottom is a great pool, eerily silent. Here you board a rowboat and glide to the dark corners where thousands of shimmering blue and gold glowworm webs wrap around limestone outcroppings.
This evening, attend a Maori dance concert and watch a Maori group give a performance of traditional songs and dances.
5TH DAY. TUESDAY:
ROTORUA/AUCKLAND.
Morning sightseeing. Afternoon departure for Auckland.
Today, visit Whakarewarewa Thermal Reserve, heart of Rotorua’s thermal wonderland where you’ll see geysers, craters and pools of boiling mud. Also visit the Maori Arts and Crafts Centre, Ohinemutu and Rainbow Trout Springs, where you can feed tame trout by hand.
This afternoon, back to Auckland. Tomorrow morning, bright and early you leave for Sydney.
