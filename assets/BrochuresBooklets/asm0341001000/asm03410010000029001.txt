10TH AND 11TH DAYS. AGRA/JAIPUR.
Wednesday free until departure for Jaipur. Dinner on arrival. Thursday morning sightseeing; afternoon free. Dinner at your hotel.
Wednesday is free, perhaps re-visit Agra Fort or take the interesting optional excursion to Fatephur Sikri which is 26 miles from Agra and was built in 1569. Your flight to Jaipur leaves early this evening; transfer to your hotel and join your friends for dinner. Thursday morning you visit Maharajah’s Palace, the Albert Museum, the Observatory, then drive through the bustling Pink City of Jaipur. The afternoon is free to wander around on your own or take a trip to Amber, the ancient capital of state until 1728.
To get to the fort, you have an exciting ride on an elephant. It’s quite safe.
12TH DAY. FRIDAY: JAIPUR/DELHI/MADRAS.
Free until departure back to Delhi with connection to Madras. Lunch at your hotel.
This morning you can go back to the Observatory or wander through the city until lunch time. In the afternoon you fly back to Delhi and on to Madras. Transfer to your hotel.
13TH DAY. SATURDAY:
MADRAS.
Morning city sightseeing. Afternoon free. Dinner at your hotel.
Madras is a fascinating city situated on the Bay of Bengal. It has temples, churches, a cathedral where the body of the apostle St. Thomas is said to be buried.
