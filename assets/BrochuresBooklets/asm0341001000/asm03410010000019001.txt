You’ll visit the old gold mining town of Arrowtown, the museum, the Coronet Peak Ski fields and return to your hotel by crossing Skippers River, once famous for gold mining. The evening is free. 9TH DAY. SUNDAY: QUEENSTOWN/CHRISTCHURCH. Morning departure by air. Weather permitting, your flight will stop at Mount Cook, giving you a few hours to spend on your own. Continue to Christchurch. Evening free.
Mount Cook thrusts its deep-blue glaciers and snow-capped rocks 12,349 feet skyward. The Maori tribesmen named it Aorangi, “the cloud piercer.” Christchurch is the largest city in the South Island and is known as “the most English city outside England.” In the summer time practically all the gardens are a mass of flowers, with every one competing for the “best garden” prize. Flowers even bloom in winter!
Sydney Harbor
10TH DAY. MONDAY: CHRISTCHURCH/SYDNEY. Morning city sightseeing of Christchurch. Afternoon flight across the Tasman Sea to Sydney. Assistance on arrival and transfer to your hotel. Evening free.
This morning you have a tour of Christchurch before leaving for Australia. You’ll have a comprehensive tour of the city and suburbs, drive up to Evans Pass and look down on Lyttleton Harbor with its many bays and inlets, to the Southern Alps and Canterbury Plains. On your arrival in Sydney, you will be met and transferred to your hotel.
