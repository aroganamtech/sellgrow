1ST DAY. MONDAY:
USA/HONG KONG.
Depart aboard your Pan Am Jet Clipper bound for Hong Kong.
2ND DAY. TUESDAY:
HONG KONG.
You’ve crossed the International Date Line, so it’s Tuesday evening when you arrive. Transfer to your hotel.
3RD AND 4TH DAYS.
HONG KONG.
Wednesday morning sightseeing of Kowloon and the New Territories. Thursday morning sightseeing of Hong Kong Island, followed by lunch.
Wednesday morning you cross the harbor and set off for Kowloon and the New Territories. You’ll drive through the industrial area to the old walled village
of Kam Tin; then reach Lok Ma Chau Police Post where you can look over into the People’s Republic of China. This afternoon would be a good time to shop.
Thursday morning’s tour takes you up to Victoria Peak to gaze out over all Hong Kong. You’ll see the commercial centre, the banking area, Susie Wong’s Wanchai district, Happy Valley, and on to Repulse Bay for lunch. The afternoon and evening are free.
