Colorful Sails, Philippines
6TH AND 7TH DAYS. AUCKLAND/SYDNEY.
Early morning flight on Wednesday to Sydney. Transfer to your hotel. Afternoon free. Thursday morning, sightseeing. Evening free.
Wednesday morning you leave for Sydney. The remainder of the day is free. Thursday morning, your tour will take you to the historic Rocks area near “Coathanger” Bridge, through Paddington, where the restored houses look as if the terraces have been made from iron-lace. Onto the magic of the Opera House. This afternoon, you can visit Taronga Park Zoo, where you can actually see those legendary koalas, walk along the winding paths to discover the Australian bird sanctuary and rare species enclosure. A lovely way to spend an afternoon. Evening is free.
8TH AND 9TH DAYS. SYDNEY/MELBOURNE.
Friday morning, fly to Melbourne. Saturday morning sightseeing. Remainder of time at leisure.
On arrival in Melbourne, you’ll be met and transferred to your hotel. Your hotel is within walking distance of Fitzroy Gardens. This afternoon, explore the exquisite boutiques or stroll along the bank of the River Yarra.
On Saturday morning, your city tour takes you past the stately government buildings and the sporting fields of the
1960 Olympics. You’ll see the many beautiful parks and gardens for which Melbourne is known. This afternoon and evening are free.
10TH DAY. SUNDAY: MELBOURNE/SINGAPORE. Morning departure for Singapore. Transfer to your hotel.
On arrival in Singapore, you’ll be met and transferred to your hotel. The impact of Singapore will be tremendous. Skyscrapers, trishaws, temples, hotels, a dozen nationalities celebrating their festivals. Singapore is a city of one-way streets. It is green, clean, beautiful and so busy!
