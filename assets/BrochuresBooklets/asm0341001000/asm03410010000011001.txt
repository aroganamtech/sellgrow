You are free to enjoy a cruise this evening or perhaps have dinner at the incredible Seafood Restaurant where you can practically catch your own fish, from enormous lobsters to the delectable crayfish.
9TH DAY. SATURDAY: BANGKOK/KUALA LUMPUR. Morning tour of the klongs. Afternoon flight to Kuala Lumpur. Transfer to your hotel.
Bright and early, a visit to the klongs and the floating market—Bangkok’s answer to the American supermarket— starts the day. Water taxis glide by as farmers hawk their produce, paddling their way from one waterway (or klong) to another. A stop will be made at the lovely Wat Arun (Temple of the Dawn), whose five towers rest on a series of terraces supported by statues of demons and angels.
Fly this afternoon to Kuala Lumpur, capital of Malaysia. You will have great fun exploring your hotel, one of the newest in Kuala Lumpur, plenty of restaurants to choose from, tiny shops to browse in and a luxurious cocktail lounge where you can enjoy a leisurely drink before dinner.
10TH DAY. SUNDAY:
KUALA LUMPUR/SINGAPORE. Morning tour of the city. Afternoon flight to Singapore. Transfer to your hotel.
Today you will see the National Mosque and for contrast the modern university; then a short drive into the Malaysian countryside to see a tin mining operation and a rubber plantation. This afternoon a short flight south takes you to Singapore, probably one of the most fascinating, exciting cities on your itinerary. Transfer to your hotel, then go up to the pool, the highest in Southeast Asia, and sip a Singapore Sling while watching the sun go down.
11TH DAY. MONDAY: SINGAPORE.
Morning free. Afternoon tour of the city.
Singapore, they say, is Instant Asia. You’ll agree when you see the trishaws and harbor . .. the temples, shrines and mosques where a dozen nationalities celebrate their countless festivals ... and the women wearing saris, sarongs and lacy kebavas. But Singapore has an unmistakable British accent, with Kip-lingesque touches like the Cricket Club and nostalgic Raffles Hotel. This after-
noon your tour will take you to all the highlights of Singapore and bring you back to your hotel in time to take a nap and then perhaps have dinner at Hin’s Heavenly Cookhouse.
12TH AND 13TH DAYS. SINGAPORE/MANILA.
Tuesday morning fly to Manila. Wednesday morning city tour. Afternoon and evening free.
This morning you are off to Manila, a happy and colorful city. On Wednesday you’ll visit Malacanang Palace, Santo Tomas, Intramuros, the Spanish walled city and other interesting sights. This afternoon you are free to browse in the many shops, take a tour to Lake Tagatay or perhaps Corregidor.
14TH DAY. THURSDAY: MANILA/HONG KONG.
Morning flight to Hong Kong. Transfer to your hotel.
On arrival you will be transferred to your hotel. This afternoon has been left free for you to relax or start a shopping spree. This evening why not take a harbor cruise or dine at one of Hong Kong’s wonderful restaurants.
15TH AND I6TH DAYS.
HONG KONG.
Friday morning tour of Kowloon and the New Territories. Remainder of time at leisure.
Friday morning you cross the harbor and set off for Kowloon and the New Territories. You’ll drive through the industrial area to the old walled village of Kam Tin; then reach Lok Ma Chau Police Post where you can look over into the People’s Republic of China.
This afternoon and tomorrow, Saturday, you can shop, go up to Victoria Peak and gaze out over all Hong Kong, wander up and down the ladder streets, take the ferry across the harbor to Kowloon side to the Ocean Terminal, a huge “shopping centre”. In fact, you can cram so much into these days you will feel you have been there weeks. A wonderful way to wind up your vacation.
17TH DAY. SUNDAY:
HONG KONG/USA.
Transfer to the airport for your flight home. You recross the International Date Line arriving in the USA Sunday morning.
