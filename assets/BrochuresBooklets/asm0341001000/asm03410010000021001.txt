1ST DAY. SATURDAY: USA/TOKYO.
Depart aboard your Pan Am Jet Clipper bound for Tokyo.
2ND DAY. SUNDAY:
TOKYO.
You’ve crossed the International Date Line, so it’s Sunday afternoon when you arrive. Transfer to your hotel. Evening is free.
3RD AND 4TH DAYS.
TOKYO.
Monday morning city sightseeing. Monday afternoon and all day Tuesday free.
The Imperial Palace Plaza is the first stop on your morning tour, followed by the National Diet Building, the beautiful Meiji Shrine Outer Garden and the Asakusa Amusement Centre. While at leisure, you might explore the Ginza district where some of Tokyo’s richest stores crowd amongst a bewildering variety of cafes, restaurants and china shops.
5TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: TOKYO/KYOTO.
To Kyoto by Bullet Train, Afternoon sightseeing.
Streak down to Kyoto this morning at 125 m.p.h. aboard one of Japan’s famed Bullet Trains. This afternoon, visit charming Kinkaku-ji in its lovely garden setting; Sanjusangen-do, with its thousand and one gilded statues of Kannon Bosatsu, the Goddess of Mercy, the vermilion lacquered Heian Shrine and the tranquil Kiyomizu Temple.
7TH DAY. FRIDAY: KYOTO/HONG KONG.
Transfer to Osaka for your flight to Hong Kong. Balance of the day at leisure.

6TH DAY. THURSDAY: KYOTO.
A free day to explore on your own.
Kyoto has become synonymous with the very spirit of Japan. The “Celestial City” was the centre of Japanese civilization for 10 centuries and was the Imperial capital until 1868. Its streets are a succession of mansions, temples and shrines, presided over by the Emperor’s ancient palace. Nijo Castle, another former residence of the Imperial Family, is a moated 17th-century structure complete with turrets and profusely decorated with wood sculpture, carvings and murals.
