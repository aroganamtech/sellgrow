1ST DAY. FRIDAY:
USA/HONG KONG.
Depart aboard your Pan Am Jet Clipper bound for Hong Kong.
2ND DAY. SATURDAY:
HONG KONG.
You’ve crossed the International Date Line, so it’s Saturday evening when you arrive. Transfer to your hotel.
3RD AND 4TH DAYS.
HONG KONG.
Sunday free. Monday morning sightseeing.
You have plenty of time on your own in Hong Kong. Take the ferry across the harbor to Kowloon, where the reflections from the jewelry-shop windows hit the streets with a ricochet of light. Buy a dozen or so custom-made shirts. The tailor keeps your measurements—you just re-order.
Monday morning’s tour drives you out as far as the border of the People’s Republic of China. En route you will pass through the New Territories settlement areas, visit an ancient walled village and see the farmlands of the ancient Hakkas. Afternoon and evening free.
5TH DAY. TUESDAY:
HONG KONG/BANGKOK.
Fly to Bangkok. Transfer to your hotel. Remainder of the day at leisure.
6TH THROUGH 8TH DAYS. BANGKOK/PATTAYA.
Wednesday morning sightseeing. Afternoon free. Thursday afternoon departure for Pattaya. Friday at leisure. Dinner while in Pattaya.
Wednesday you’ll see three of Bangkok’s most famous temples—Wat Trimitr, with its solid gold Buddha; Wat Benchamabopit, known as the Marble Temple, and Wat Po, with its magnificent reclining Buddha.
Thursday morning is free, then you leave by motorcoach for Pattaya, a beautiful beach resort. Your hotel has its own private beach, magnificent gardens and only a few minutes by boat from deserted islands where the water is so clear you can see the fish that tickle your toes. You can drive into the village or spend this time soaking up the sun and taking a well earned “breather.”
9TH AND 10TH DAYS. PATTAYA/SINGAPORE.
Saturday morning departure to Bangkok connecting with your flight to Singapore. Sunday morning sightseeing. Balance of the time at leisure.
Bright and early on Saturday morning you drive back to Bangkok and board your flight to Singapore. On arrival you’ll be met and transferred to your hotel. The balance of the day is free to explore on your own. Try and go down to Change Alley where you can bargain for anything, a gold watch to a length of batik. Sunday morning your tour takes you to all the highlights of this city: The Padang, Singapore’s waterfront; City Hall and Supreme Court and, for contrast, Haw Par Villa known as the Tiger Balm Gardens. The afternoon and evening are free.
11TH AND 12TH DAYS. SINGAPORE/KUALA LUMPUR. Monday morning departure for Kuala Lumpur. Afternoon sightseeing. Tuesday free. Dinner at the Melaka Grill.
