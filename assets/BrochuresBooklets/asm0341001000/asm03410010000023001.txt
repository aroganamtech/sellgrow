16TH DAY. SUNDAY: KUALA LUMPUR.
29TH DAY. SATURDAY: ROTORUA/USA.
Morning sightseeing. Afternoon and evening free.
This morning’s tour takes you to the University of Malaya, Malaysia’s first university, along the Lake Gardens, passing the Houses of Parliament, National Monument and the National Mosque. This afternoon try to visit the Selangor Pewter factory or the Handicraft Centre on Mountbatten Road. This evening you can dine at your hotel or at one of the small restaurants sampling satay, it’s truly delicious.
17TH AND 18TH DAYS.
KUALA LUMPUR/SINGAPORE. Monday morning flight to Singapore. Tuesday morning sightseeing. Remainder of time at leisure.
On arrival in Singapore you’ll be met and transferred to your hotel. The impact of Singapore after Malaysia will be tremendous. Skyscrapers, trishaws, temples, hotels, a dozen nationalities celebrating their festivals. Singapore is a city of one-way streets. It is green, clean, beautiful, and so busy. Tuesday morning your tour will take you to the highlights of this city: The bustling waterfront, City Hall, the Supreme Court, Raffles Hotel, and the Tiger Balm Gardens.
19TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: SINGAPORE/BALI.
Morning flight to Bali via Jakarta. Transfer to your hotel. Balance of day to relax.
20TH DAY. THURSDAY:
BALI.
Morning free. Afternoon sightseeing.
When you have time for only one stop in Indonesia, it must be Bali—an enchanting isle of temples, festivals, gods and volcanoes; a legend in itself. Spend the afternoon visiting the lovely Pura Kehen temples at Mengwi, followed by a performance of the Ketjak (Monkey Dance).
21ST DAY. FRIDAY: BALI/SYDNEY
Day free until evening departure by air for Sydney.
22ND THROUGH 24TH DAYS. SYDNEY.
Saturday morning arrival. Transfer to your hotel and relax for the rest of the day. Sightseeing on Sunday morning and Monday is free.
Sydney’s life centres around its massive harbor and the rugged steel arch bridge that spans it. An extraordinary addition to its skyline is the futuristic Opera House, which looks like a mass of 200-foot sails. Sunday morning you will drive through the city, visiting the historic Rocks area and the beautiful beaches. You have free time to explore on your own, shop for opals or sample the delicious Sydney rock oysters.
25TH DAY. TUESDAY:
SYDNEY/AUCKLAND.
Fly across the Tasman Sea to Auckland. Transfer to your hotel.
26TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: AUCKLAND.
Wednesday morning city sightseeing. Afternoon free.
Wednesday morning your tour takes you across the Auckland Harbor Bridge to the seaside suburbs; climb to the summit of Mount Eden, an extinct volcano, have a wonderful view of the city! This evening is free for you to discover one of Auckland’s many excellent restaurants.
27TH DAY. THURSDAY:
AUCKLAND/WAITOMO/
ROTORUA.
Morning departure for Rotorua, stopping to visit the Waitomo Glowworm Grotto en route. After lunch continue to your hotel. Evening free.
Not far from Rotorua are the Waitomo Caves and their glowworm grottos. As you descend the steps, the damp underground breeze cools your face. At the bottom is a great pool, eerily silent. Here you board a rowboat and glide to the dark corners where thousands of shimmering blue and gold glowworm webs wrap around limestone outcroppings.
28TH DAY. FRIDAY:
ROTORUA.
Morning sightseeing. Evening Maori dance concert.
Today visit Whakarewarewa Thermal Reserve, heart of Rotorua’s thermal wonderland where you’ll see geysers, craters and pools of boiling mud. Also, visit the Maori Arts and Crafts Centre, Ohinemutu and the Rainbow Trout Springs where you can feed tame trout by hand. This evening attend a Maori dance concert and watch a Maori group give a performance of traditional songs and dances.
Free until departure by coach for Auckland where you board your Pan Am Jet Clipper flight home. You recross the International Date Line, arriving in the USA early Saturday morning.
