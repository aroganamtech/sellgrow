SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA
OOUTHERN California has plenty to offer the airways ^traveler in the way of good food. This list hits the high spots. They’re popular—it’s a good idea to make reservations.
A BIT OF SWEDEN, 9051 Sunset Blvd. (BRadshaw 2-2800). Swedish meat balls a specialty. Dinner from $2, smorgasbord $1.50.
BARCLAY KITCHEN, 8438 W. Third. (WEbster 7143). The steak here is excellent. Good service too. Dinner from $2.25.
BEVERLY-WILSHIRE HOTEL, 9514 Wilshire Blvd. (BRadshaw 2-1800). Good food. Favored by movie folk. Dinner from $2.50.
BOB DALTON'S STEAK HOUSE, 1056 S. La Cienega. (CRestview 5-9704). Steak dinner from $1.95. Elbow Room Cocktail Lounge in conjunction. Open 5 p.m. to 1 a.m.
BRITTINGHAM'S RADIO CENTER RESTAURANT,
Columbia Sq. (Hollywood 3611). Patronized almost exclusively by radio and movie people. Dinner from $1.35. Closed Sunday.
BROWN DERBY, 9537 Wilshire Blvd. (CRestview 6-2311). 3377 Wilshire Blvd. (FItzroy 5151). 4500 Los Feliz Blvd. (OLympia 2913). 1628 N. Vine, Hollywood. (Hollywood 5151). Good food and lots of movie celebrities. Phone early for reservations at the Hollywood Derby.
CAPE COD ROOM (The Town House), Wilshire Blvd. at Commonwealth. (Exposition 1234). An authentic bit of New England. Features charcoal-broiled chops, chicken and fish. Breakfast from 45f, lunch from 75f, dinner from $2.50.
CHASEN'S, 9039 Beverly Blvd. (CRestview 1-2168). Celebrities who like superior food come here. Dinner from $3. Closed Monday.
COCK 'N' BULL, 9170 Sunset Blvd. (BRadshaw 2-1397). Famous for roast beef and New England dinner. Closed Monday.
CORONET, 7580 W. Sunset Blvd. (Hillside 5560). Italian-French food. The grenadine of beef with mushrooms is a favorite. Dinner from $1.50.
CRILLON, 8192 Sunset Blvd. (BRadshaw 2-3444). Music with your meal. Dinner from $2.25, Sunday buffet supper $5. Closed Monday.
DON THE BEACHCOMBER, 1727 N. McCadden Place. (Hollywood 3968). Tropical food with a wide array of tempting rum drinks. Dinner from $2.50. Closed Sunday.
EATON'S, 3550 Wilshire Blvd. (FEderal 0715). 8500 Burton Way. (BRadshaw 2-4241). 12010 Ventura Blvd. (STanley 7-1085). 1150 Colorado, Santa Anita. (RYan 1-6033). Famous for their melt-in-your-mouth fried chicken and hearty steaks.
IVAR HOUSE, 1737 Ivar Ave. (GRanite 2001). Nice service and good food. Dinner from $1.50.
LITTLE HUNGARY, 8917 Sunset Blvd. (CRestview 5-9263). If you have a hankering for Hungarian goulash, this is the place. Dinner from $2. Closed Monday.
LUCEY'S, 5444 Melrose Ave. (Hollywood 5166). Lots of motion picture and radio celebrities come here. Always crowded. We suggest their chicken, hunter style. Dinner from $2.50. Closed Sunday.
MELODY LANE, 744 S. Hill. (MAdison 4801). 5351 Wilshire Blvd. (WAlnut 4384). 6301 Hollywood Blvd. (GRanite 0819). 3800 Wilshire Blvd. (Exposition 6771). Four popular restaurants and sweet shops in convenient locations for dinner and after theatre. Dinner from $1.25.
