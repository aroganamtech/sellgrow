AIRWAYS
HOLIDAY GIFTS
TTERE are a few ideas gathered about town to help out on that annual headache—what to buy for Christmas gifts, and where. And for more suggestions, try the big department stores. Most of them have shopping services designed to help the person with the I-don’t-know-what-to-get malady.
ADRIAN'S PERFUME: The creator of feminine fashions has entered the perfume field with his Saint and Sinner. In attractive crystal bottles at $25 an ounce at J. J. Haggarty’s, 520 W. Seventh in Los Angeles.
ALLIGATOR HANDBAGS: An alligator is attractive only when his hide is made into a shoe or handbag—then he’s tops and makes a gift high up on milady’s preference list. Handbags in red and brown, with imported ones priced from $75, domestic bags from $37.50. At Wether by-Kay ser, 517 W. Seventh St. and 5416 Wilshire Blvd. in Los Angeles.
BLOUSES: The ones by De De Johnson of California are hits wherever they go. There’s one long-sleeved number in turquoise at $7.95 which would add a decorative note to any one’s Christmas tree. At the Nobby Knit Shops in Beverly Hills, Westwood and Palm Springs.
BRASS CANDLESTICKS: Antiques with round base and slim
stem at $26.50 the pair. At the Wayside Shop, Barker Brothers, Seventh and Figueroa in Los Angeles.
CAMERA CUE: A clever device, the Adel Color Slide Viewer, magnifies 35 mm. Kodachromes about five times. Electrically operated and selling for $18.50, it is an answer to the camera addict’s prayer. At Winter and Company, 525 W. Sixth St. in Los Angeles.
COMPACT: Lovely and large and light in weight, with a sterling silver basket weave design. Sells for $20 at The Broadway Department Store, Fifth and Broadway in Los Angeles.
CORONA D'ORO: An informal California-made glassware wrapped in gaily colored raffi-like material for patio or lanai dining. Several designs, and practical as well as pretty. One really useful item is a 32-ounce coffee bottle selling for $2.50 with matching glasses at 80^ each. At Coulter’s, 5600 Wilshire Blvd. in Los Angeles.
