THE BEST AFTER DARK
TVTIGHT clubs in Southern California are prepared to offer 1 the airways traveler anything and everything in the way of after-dark amusement. The accent is on smooth sophistication, with name bands, star acts and distinctive decor now common features of all top spots. If you’re a movie fan, you’ll know that spending an evening in a night club is one time-proved Way of rubbing elbows with Hollywood’s elite. Below are the more popular spots. Reservations are a good idea, sometimes a necessity, so phone numbers are included for your convenience.
BAR OF MUSIC, 7351 Beverly Blvd. (WEbster 7811). This is the bar that music made famous, hence the name. Felix de Cola and John Remon at twin pianos keep the customers asking for more. Also on hand are Paula Drake, Ernie Baron, Alice Tyrell and Dick Winslow and his orchestra. Open from 6 to 2. Dancing from 6:45. First show at 7:30. Minimum on Sturday only, $2.
BILTMORE BOWL, Biltmore Hotel, 515 S. Olive St. (Michigan 1011). When you’re downtown, this is one place you should visit. Popular with movie folk and socialites. The floor show is always good. Scintillating music is by Ted Straeter’s orchestra nightly except Sunday, from 8. Open at 6. Floor shows at 8 and 11:45. Dinner from $2. Cover $1, Saturday and holidays $1.50.
BUBLICHKI RUSSIAN CAFE, 8846 Sunset Blvd. (CRestview
5-9264). Da, the accent is on the Rahshahn here, and you’ll thoroughly enjoy it. Especially when they put dishes like zaktiska or filet mignon a la Stroganoff in front of you. Lilting Russian times by Jac Scholl’s gypsy band. Open from 6. Dinner from $2.50. Closed Tuesday.
CAFE CALIENTE, 20 Olvera St. (Mutual 2056). Entering this cafe is like crossing the Mexican border line. You’ll dance and eat by candlelight. The food is good, the wines excellent. Jose Moran’s tango-rhumba band plays for dancing from 6. Open from 5 to 12:30. Shows at 7:30 and 12. Dinner from $1. Closed Tuesday.

CASINO GARDENS, 2946 Ocean Front, Santa Monica (Santa Monica 6-5268). Headline bands for dancing at this romantic beach spot. Popular with younger crowd. No dinner, only sandwiches and drinks served. Dancing from 8 to 1. Admission $1.20. Closed Monday.
CASINO ROOM, Ambassador Hotel, 3400 Wilshire Blvd. (DRexel 7011). Gene Walsh’s smooth rhythms please afternoon dancers as well as the evening crowd. Open from 4:30 to 12:30. Closed Monday.
CHARLEY FOY'S SUPPER CLUB, 12915 Ventura Blvd., Cold-water Canyon (STate 4-3159). Joe Frisco entertains, which is a guarantee of hearty laughs. Food is definitely out of this world. Specialties include boneless squab cooked in wine sauce and thick fillets. Open 4 to 1. Shows, 9 and 11:30. Dinner from $2. Closed Tuesday.
CINEGRILL, Hollywood-Roosevelt Hotel, 7000 Hollywood Blvd. (Hollywood 2442). Photo murals of early silent movies on the walls are a constant reminder that you’re in the cinema capital. Victor Vincent provides music for dancing from 9 to 12:30 with Margaret Sherron vocalizing. Each day there is a chafing dish special. Entrees from $1.15. Cocktail dancing from 4 to 6 Saturday and Sunday.
CIRO’S, 8433 W. Sunset Blvd. (HEmpstead 2381). Motion picture biggies in the audience, ace acts and bands behind the footlights and a real culinary artist, Chef Gabriel in the kitchen. This month H. D. Hover presents sensational Enric Madriguera and his orchestra with Johnnie and Sandra Steele. Shows may be seen and dancing is permitted from the bar. Exceptional wine cellar. Dinner from 5:30. Open to 2. Floor shows at 9 and 12. Cover $1.50, Saturday $2. No cover at bar.
COCOANUT GROVE, Ambassador Hotel, 3400 Wilshire Blvd. (DRexel 7011). This world famous night spot has Freddy Martin for dancing from 8. Visiting celebs always to be glimpsed under the palms. Afternoon dancing Saturday. Cover $1, Saturday $1.50. Open to 1, Saturday to 1:30. Dinner from $2.25. Closed Monday.
EARL CARROLL'S THEATRE-RESTAURANT, 6330 Sunset
Blvd. (Hollywood 7101). Two spectacular shows on a revolving stage with outstanding singers, comedians and “The 60 most beautiful girls in the world.” The Sketchbook revue is well worth seeing, the cuisine worth sampling. Open at 7. Dancing begins at 8:30. Shows at 9:15 and 12. Cover $1.65 without dinner. Dinner from $3.30.
FLORENTINE GARDENS, 5955 Hollywood Blvd. (Hollywood 6311). Here you’ll find one of the best floor shows in town. The current attraction, Parisian Nights, goes on at 9:30 and 12. Open from 6:30 to 2. Cover $1.50 if you don’t want dinner. Dinner from $3.
GARDEN ROOM, The Town House, Wilshire Blvd. at Commonwealth (Exposition 1234). Dancing to the music of Chick Floyd and his orchestra every night but Tuesday from 7:30. Bunny Gravert furnishes band vocals and intermission piano numbers. Open at 6. Zebra Room for cocktails. Lunch from $1.50, dinner from $3.50. Cover $1, Saturday $1.50.
