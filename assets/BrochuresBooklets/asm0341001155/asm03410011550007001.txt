NETHERLANDS
The Netherlands — or Holland — is like a welcome mat spread out before you . . . her glowing tulips and busy canals reminding you of all the colorful stories and pictures you’ve seen of this storybook land. You’ll enjoy all the charm of her distinctive trademarks, too — the wooden shoes, the windmills, the brilliant native costumes. And who knows? Perhaps your hotel will be an age-old castle that’s been modernized to make your visit not only memorable but comfortable and convenient as well.
BELGIUM AND LUXEMBOURG
Belgium, with its gay cafes, masterworks of art and long stretches of beaches, is the perfect holiday land. Clean ... modern ... honest . . . and full of laughter and good fun. Brussels, with her chic Parisian attitude and industrious Germanic activity, is the heart and center of the country . . . the perfect “homebase” for all your visiting, not only in Belgium but nearby Luxembourg, too.
