Using the telephone
To find a telephone number not in the directory dial 142 if the number is in London, 192 for numbers elsewhere. To get the operator dial 100. For enquiries and operators for Austria, Belgium, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Luxembourg and Scandinavia dial 105, for France, Switzerland and outside Europe dial 108. International

Maps and Guide Books
A large number of maps and guide books are available, the two most useful being Nicholson's London Guide and Geography's A to Z London Atlas. Free maps of central London are available from London Transport Enquiry Offices.
The following books are also useful:
Visitor's London	Footloose In London
London Street Finder What's On In London.
Newspapers
If your hotel cannot get you the newspaper you want, particularly if it is a foreign language newspaper, try one of Soho's newsagents or go to Solosy, 53 Charing Cross Road.
All Night Post Office
The Post Office at Trafalgar Square is open all night. Usually crowded by the lower floor telephones, where telephone calls can be made to, and received from, abroad.
