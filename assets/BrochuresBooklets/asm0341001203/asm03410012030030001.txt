ENTERTAINMENTS
For top-class entertainment, London leads the world. She has some of the best theatres, night-clubs, discotheques and gaming-clubs
Day or night there is a rich variety of things to do and places to go. The night spots of Soho, theatres of the West End, concerts in the parks, whatever your tastes London can entertain you.
It would be impossible to list here everything that London has to offer, the choice is so wide, but we'll try to give you a few guidelines as to what's where.
Night-Spots
It has been said that- London has more to offer the pleasure-seeker than any other city in the world. When the sun goes down the city takes on a new and exciting lease of life and the streets are as bustling and busy as ever.
There are plenty of night-clubs and discotheques where dancing, good food and often an excellent cabaret are available. While many are 'members only', they welcome visitors from abroad. Subscriptions vary and it is wise to telephone in advance, even to book tables at nonmembership restaurants.
