If you've got problems:
Ring The Samaritans 626 9000 or St Martins-In -The-Fields Social Service Unit 5 St Martin's Place WC2 930 1732. Both will be able to help you, whatever the problem.
International Travellers Aid. Head Office, YWCA National Offices, 2 Weymouth Street W1 636 9722. To help any traveller in distress.
Cash:
Many banks have now been made available 24 hour cash dispensing machines which require a special card obtainable from the banks concerned. You should apply for one of these before you get stuck. If you have a cheque guarantee card you may find that some gaming clubs will cash a cheque for you if you are a member.
If you are a bonafide air traveller, you can cash a cheque at Barclays Bank at the air terminal in Buckingham Palace Road until 23.00 Mondays to Fridays.
Accommodation:
If you are temporarily stranded without accommodation over night you could try the Turkish Bath, if you're male, at 91 Jermyn Street 930 9957 which is open 24 hrs. You can have a turkish bath and sleep afterwards .in comfort for about £3.50.
You might prefer to eat and drink your way to dawn in one of the following restaurants or cafes:
Canton 11 Newport Place WC2 Cavendish Hotel Jermyn Street SW1 Dunkin' Donut Ludgate Circus EC4 Micks Cafe Fleet Street EC4.
Several pubs serve a light, early morning breakfast. These are usually located near Markets such as Covent Garden (Coach & Horses, Nag's Head), Smithfield (Victoria) or Billingsgate. Try Berwick Street Market, too. Very pleasant after that turkish bath.
