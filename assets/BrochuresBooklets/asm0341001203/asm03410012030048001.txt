AN EVENING OUT
Theatres
London's theatres are second to none, with an enormous choice of productions. Drama, comedy, farce, ballet, experimental theatre, multi-media, the list is endless and the quality high. Many theatres specialise, such as Covent Garden Opera House or the more avant garde Roudhouse, others are more general, such as the Theatre Royal.
Although centred in the West End, there are theatres scattered througout the city, including a complex of theatres, cinemas and art galleries on the Southbank. All provide excellent entertainment.
Tickets can be bought either from the theatres, through a ticket agent or from a travel agent. Chappells, 50 New Bond Street 493-2444 and Keith Prowse, 27a Shaftesbury Avenue W1, 437-2948, can get tickets for you. Your hotel ticket agency or receptionist will also be able to help.

Concerts
There are a number of good concert halls in London where a range of music can be found. The best known being the Royal Festival Hall on the Southbank which includes several halls, and the Albert Hall.
Royal Festival Hall
Southbank SE1. Forms the Southbank Arts Centre Orchestral and choral concerts.
Royal Albert Hall
Kensington Gore SW7 589 8212. Orchestral, choral and pop concerts.
Most theatres are closed on Sundays, but there are theatre clubs and societies which'you can join for a small fee. It is necessary for you to be a member for 48 hours before attending.

Open Air Music
Relaxing in the open air and sunshine while listening to good music in a London park is a pleasure not to be missed. The concerts are usually free, but for a small fee you can hire a deck-chair and listen in comfort. All sorts of music is played, including jazz, military and light orchestral. Here are a few easily reached places where you can spend a pleasant hour or so, although there are many others.
