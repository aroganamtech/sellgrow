Although, in accordance with the Law, you must wait 48 hours after you register before you can use the many facilities of the Club, membership to Overseas Visitors is free.
The Club is renowned throughout the World for its relaxed, but exciting, atmosphere, and the Casino on the First Floor offers 24 tables of Roulette,
Blackjack, Dice and Punto Banco, with a wide range of stakes.
Once a member, you can drop in any time —the Club is open from 2.00 pm until 4.00 am each day.
The superb Restaurant, seating 250, overlooks the main Casino Floor and specialises in international cuisine. Dinner is served from 6.30 pm until midnight and, thereafter, breakfast until 3.30 am.
On the Second Floor, for the less adventurous, is an enormous Card Room where Draw and Stud Poker and Kalooki are played.
Both floors are fully air-conditioned, contributing to the friendly and casual, ambience, though for gentlemen, jackets and ties are requested.
Make your trip to London a visit you won't forget by joining us. We look forward to welcoming you.
