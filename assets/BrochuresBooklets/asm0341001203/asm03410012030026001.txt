0 Daily Events
The following are special ceremonies which you can see every day, all year round. For individual daily events call the relevant Teletourist number (see our ‘May we help you?’section).
THE CHANGING OF THE QUEEN'S GUARD Buckingham Palace SW1 Ceremony lasts half hour. Not held in bad weather. If Royal standard is not flying then held at St James's Palace at 11.30.
THE CHANGING OF THE QUEEN'S LIFE GUARD Horse Guards Arch, Whitehall SW1. Ceremony lasts 20 minutes. CEREMONY OF THE KEYS Tower of London EC3. The Chief Warder, with an escort from the Brigade of Guards, locks the west middle tower and Byward tower gates with traditional ceremony.
