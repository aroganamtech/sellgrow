MEDICAL FACILITIES . . . There are modern hospitals in Panamá City, Colón and the Canal Zone. American trained, English-speaking doctors are available. Your hotel will help you if you need one. The Republic takes great pride in its high health standards.
MOTION PICTURES . . . First-run American pictures are shown at the Lux, Bella Vista and Central Theatres, which are the best. There’s also a new drive-in theatre. All pictures are in English with Spanish titles. There are showings also of Spanish, French and Italian films. Tropical is the most popular for Spanish-speaking pictures, made mostly in Mexico or Argentina. MUSIC . . . You’ll hear all kinds of music all the time you are in Panamá. The combination of native music and the influence of jazz has given an individual slant to the Panamanian type of Latin music with its several kinds of drums for El Tamborito, Panamá’s famous national dance.
NIGHT LIFE AND CAFES . . . Panamá City is a gay town. Most hotels have music and dancing. Especially nice is the Bella Vista Roof at El Panamá Hilton.
RESTAURANTS . . . One of Panamá City’s best restaurants is the Jardin el Rancho. Superb food, grand drinks are served under tropical stars, indoors when rains come. There’s good eating in the hotel dining rooms, both native and American cooking. O. K. Amigo and Pete’s Catalina are excellent places to dine. Meals in Panamá cost about the same as they do at home. Try Capri for Italian food.
