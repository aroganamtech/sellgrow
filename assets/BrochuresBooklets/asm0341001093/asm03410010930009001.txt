PANAMA

SPECTATOR SPORTS . . . Panama’s spectator sports include horse racing, baseball and the unique annual fishing rodeo that is becoming increasingly popular with the world’s best fishermen. Baseball holds first place in the interest of the Panamanians. Basketball is becoming very popular. Swimming races and golf tournaments draw crowds, too. Bullfighting at the Macarena ring during dry season.
SPORTS . . . The fishing is marvelous, both at sea and in the trout streams in the mountains. Exciting big game fishing for marlin, tuna, dolphin, pompano and mackerel can be done at various seasons of the year. Boats complete with bait, tackle, food and crew are available at reasonable rates on a twenty-four hour or less basis. The best trout streams can be found in Chiriqui, the central province of Panamá.
The interior of Panamá is a hunter’s wonderland. The mountains abound with big game and wild fowl. Jaguar, puma, tigrillo and ocelot cats, mule deer, tapir and wild hog are among the principal animals. The fowl include wild duck, turkey partridge, small quail. There are guides, horses, and all varieties of camping equipment for hire. It’s open season all year for both hunting and fishing. Panamá’s beaches are beautiful and the waters of the Pacific and Atlantic oceans offer wonderful swimming and sailing. The Panamá Golf Club in Panamá City has an exceptionally fine course available for tourists.
TIME . . . Same as United States Eastern Standard Time.
TIPPING . . . The ten per cent rule applies here on all tipping Tipping runs pretty much as it would at home.
