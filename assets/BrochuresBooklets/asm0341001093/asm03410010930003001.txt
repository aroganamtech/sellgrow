LOCATION . . . The Isthmus of Panamá is geographically situated between Costa Rica in Central America and Colombia in South America. At its narrowest point, 50 miles wide, the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans are united by the world-famous Panamá Canal.
CHARACTERISTICS . . . The Isthmus of Panamá is composed of two sections, the Republic of Panamá and the Panamá Canal Zone. The Zone is a strip of land leased to the United States by the Republic of Panamá. It stretches for five miles on either side of the Canal and bisects the Republic into two sections.
