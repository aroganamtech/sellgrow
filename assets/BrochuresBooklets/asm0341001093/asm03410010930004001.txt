PANAMA
POPULATION . . . Estimated at 1 million, slightly larger than Washington, D.C.
SIZE . . . 28,575 square miles, about half the size of the State of Washington.
CAPITAL . . . Panamá City, population 221,200.
GOVERNMENT . . . The Isthmus of Panamá is an independent Republic of 9 provinces, with the exception of the 10-mile-wide strip of land surrounding the Panamá Canal, land-leased to the United States and under its jurisdiction.
