AYH-12 CORONATION-Trip of the Year . . .
Leaves New York May 25 on The Rainbow, Pan American’s Clipper Tourist flight and returns to New York June 21. Total Cost: $690. Emergency Fund $30. Ireland, Scotland, England and Wales. Groups will land at London, pick up bicycles, and start hosteling, visiting Winchester, see a play at the Shakespeare Theater at Stratford-on-Avon, the Colleges of Oxford, and then to London for the Pageantry of the Coronation. North through the Lake Country of England to the rugged terrain of Scotland to get acquainted with the Bobby Burns Country. Edinburgh, Stirling, Killin, Glencoe, bloody meeting grounds of the Clans, Oban, Loch Lomond and Glasgow. Crossing the Irish Sea to Belfast, the group will then bicycle along the Antrim Coast of Ireland, through the sparkling green countryside of Drogheda, down to Dublin, and thence to Shannon for the homebound plane.
AYH-13 GREAT BRITAIN - Literary Special
Leaves New York June 15, June 29, July 13 and July 27 on The Rainbow, Pan American's Clipper Tourist flights, and returns to New York July 12, July 26, August 9 and August 23. Total Cost: $690. Emergency Fund: $30. England, Wales, Scotland, Ireland. An informal trip covering historical and literary highlights of the British Isles. After the group picks up bicycles at'London, it will travel through the fabled woods and countryside which have furnished the background for famous English novels from Scott to Shute; the sonnets of Shakespeare come to life in the forests and streams, and you live again the stirring plays of the great master at his theater in Stratford. The trip will cover visits to the homes of other great writers as well as the Cathedral of Winchester, Windsor Castle, the picturesque Wye Valley, Stonehenge, Dublin’s Abbey Theater and Book of Kells, Mellifont Abbey, Birnam Wood, and the castles of Edinburgh and Stirling. The itinerary will be much the same as that outlined for the Coronation trip.
AYH-14 FRANCE AND THE LOW COUNTRIES
Leaves New York June 15, June 29, July 13 and July 28 on The Rainbow, Pan American's Clipper Tourist flights, and returns to New York July 12, July 26, August 9 and August 23. Total Cost: $735. Emergency Fund $30. London, Belgium, Netherlands, Northern France and Paris. Groups will land at London to pick up bicycles, cycle to Dover, cross the English Channel to Ostend, Belgium, and its ancient cities of Ghent, Bruges, Antwerp, and Brussels; thence to Holland, The Hague, Amsterdam, and the rebuilt city of Rotterdam before visiting Mont St. Michel and the rolling countryside of Normandy and Brittany. Then to Tours, Blois and up to Loire Valley with the beautiful Chateaux, to Paris for the final week. Homebound flight takes off from London.
