Pan Am SERVICE is SUPERB
From take-off to touch-down, courteous cabin attendants pamper your every whim. Whatever you wish . . . food, drink, pillow, reading matter . . . you’ll find your desires anticipated.
The living is easy in your roomy Clipper cabin, designed for
stretch-out, stroll-about comfort. Far below, the world drifts by—
at 5 or 6 miles a minute. And it’s so smooth, because you’re
up above the weather. Your cabin is pressurized so you
never notice a change in altitude. Air-conditioned, too —
cool in summer, cozy in winter. Relax under soft lights,
flick your cigarette in the ashtray at your fingertips,
press the button that adjusts the angle of your
cloud-soft chair and lean back contentedly. Yes, indeed!
Clipper comfort is incomparable!
