RAINBOW Service. Pan Am’s pace-setting Clipper tourist service, the pioneer in bringing air travel within the reach of millions, is still renowned as a low-cost leader to everywhere. Rainbow flights are aboard the fastest, most modern aircraft in Pan Am’s fleet — giant Super-6 and Super-7 Clippers flown by the same veteran air-crews as premium-fare flights. Fine meals aloft, too, at no extra charge. Yet this famous service costs no more than ordinary tourist service.
