CHILE
SIZE . . . 286,396 square miles, a larger total area than Texas but most of the country is barely 100 miles wide.
CAPITAL . . . Santiago, the fourth largest city of South America with a population of approximately 1,800,000.
GOVERNMENT ... A Republic with a President and National Congress elected by universal suffrage.
HOW TO GET THERE . . . Panagra El Inter Americano Jet aircraft operate with no change of plane from New York and Miami to Santiago and Antafagasta. Convenient Jet connections from other U.S. Gateways. From New York to Santiago 14 hours; Miami 10 hours and 10 minutes. New York to Antafagasta 12 hours and 45 minutes. (New York to Panamá over the routes of National and Pan American). First Class and Economy service on all Panagra flights. By ship about 18 days from New York to Valparaiso and about 30 days from the west coast of the United States.

ACCOMMODATIONS . . . Hotels in Santiago are marvelous, have good food and smart European-style service. The Carrera, operated by Hilton Flotéis, is a 17-story hotel with roof-top swimming pool and garden. It is one of the finest on the continent. Rates are $9.00 single, $15.00 double. Other first class Hotels in Santiago are the Hotel Emperador, Críllon, Pan Americano and the new Santa Lucia Hotel. First class but not de luxe are the Lancaster, Kent and Ritz.
