CHILE
Puerto Varas, where you journey by launch to Enseñada across Lake Llanquihue (pronounced "Yankee way’’). In Puerto Varas there is the Gran Hotel and La Playa.
From Enseñada, you have an hour’s bus ride to Petrohue where a small steamer awaits you for a beautiful trip across Lake Todos los Santos. You arrive at Peulla and spend the night in a comfortable hotel patterned after a Swiss chalet.
While here you can take day excursions to Cabatue and Rio Blanco. You clear customs at Peulla and shortly after leaving by bus, you arrive at Casa Pangue, and enter Argentina.
If you enter Chile from Argentina, go by way of Bari-loche and enter the Lake Country at Peulla. Go by boat to Villarrica, where the scenery is as fine as the fishing or skiing. The trees, the forests, the fjords, the snow-capped mountains, the crystal lakes, the views, the fishing, the magnificence of the scenery will entrance you; the best hotel is the Antumalal. You should really have two weeks to devote to this lake trip. For more on the Lake Country, see Argentina.
Antofagasta . . . Antofagasta is unique—not a twig or a blade of grass; water piped from the Andes. Earth in the city square came as ballast from England. If you’re game for the fantastic trip to "Chuqui” (world’s largest open-pit copper mine), make arrangements through Panagra. Staff house at mine for visitors; good food and wine. The brand-new Hotel de Purismo is first rate.
