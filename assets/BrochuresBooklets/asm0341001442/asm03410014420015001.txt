(Population 74,870)
Largest island of the group, its 4,030 square miles are filled with endless variety . .warm weather, cold weather; tropical beaches, lush jungle growth; ginger bordered highways, age-old forests; lava wastes, fertile plantations; Christian churches, remains of ancient pagan temples; active volcanoes, snow-crowned mountains. Off the beaten track in Puna and Kona are many spots fairly steeped in Hawaiian tradition and history . . for here much remains of the old Polynesia.
