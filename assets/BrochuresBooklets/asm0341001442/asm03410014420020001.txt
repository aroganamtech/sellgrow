A new era in air transportation has arrived, borne on the wings of Pan American’s new double-decked “America”-class Clipper. Biggest commercial landplane in the world, this impressive newcomer is years ahead in design, engineering and performance. From the passenger’s viewpoint, comfort, speed and security have been combined to a degree undreamed of only a few months ago.
Built and perfected by the same manufacturer who produced the war-time B-17 and B-29 bombers, the “America”-class Clipper is the commercial version of the non-stop world-girding B-50 Superfort. This double-decked, luxury airliner will turn previously tiresome inter-continental journeys into brief flights of utmost comfort and convenience. The vast Atlantic and Pacific oceans will now be spanned in many hours less than ever before...the long flight between the United States and Rio de Janeiro or Buenos Aires will be cut by as much as half a day.
In 1949 alone, twenty of the “America”-class Clippers will be carrying the flag of the United States, establishing new records for speed and distance all over the world.
Pan American’s Round-the-World Route together with the network that serves 73 countries and colonies will come to know the majestic sight of these unrivalled double-decked champions of the skyways of tomorrow.
