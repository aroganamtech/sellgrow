
The great Panagra DC-6 flies high and fast along the far-flung South American routes of PAA and Panagra.
Completely air-conditioned and pressurized it gives its passengers a degree of luxury and comfort once thought impossible in an airplane.
When you choose this continent-spanning DC-6 for an overnight trip you may relax in a supremely comfortable reclining seat or sleep in one of .............■ _ ; : > ■ .
the eight bed-sized berths.
On daytime flights you will enjoy the leg and elbow room assured by the big armchairs only two-abreast on each side of a wide "promenade” aisle. Delicious meals will be served on your own individual table. And on certain flights, the exclusive Fiesta Lounge provides a rendezvous for greater sociability.
The luxury "Inter-Americano” DC-6 flight operates daily between Buenos Aires and Miami via the West Coast of South America.
