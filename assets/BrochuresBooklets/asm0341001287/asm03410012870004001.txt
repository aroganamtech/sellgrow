PAA’s great, fast, double-decked "Strato” Clipper establishes entirely new standards in long-range luxury and comfort.
You can smoke, read, write, play cards, take a walk, sleep in a wide, soft berth. You can go downstairs for a cocktail or other refreshments—converse in normal tones—move around almost as you would in your club or your home.
The roomy top deck accommodates seats, berths, dressing rooms and galley. Your individual seat is a reclining easy chair adjustable to any comfort angle. On designated flights, you have the added advantage of Sleeperette service, which gives you a fully reclining seat.
A spiral staircase leads to an attractive mirrored lounge and service bar on the lower deck, seating 14 passengers.
The pressurized, air-conditioned cabin provides complete comfort even at 25,000 feet altitude.
Decorations are beautiful. Meals are delicious. Service is smiling and efficient.
You will enjoy a new high in travel comfort on PAA’s magnificent double-decked ^Strato” Clipper, the world’s most luxurious airplane in regular passenger service.
