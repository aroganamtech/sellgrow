Inter- Continental King of the Airways
This advanced model of the famed Douglas DC-6 has been engineered sand improved for maximum speed, range, dependability and spaciousness.
Designed primarily for long-range flight, the.PAA Super-6 Clipper is almost certainly the fastest non-jet airplane in trans-ocean passenger service.
The Super-6 is super-comfortable. All seats are adjustable reclining. The cabin is heated, air-conditioned and pressurized.
When equipped with Sleeperette* service, there is luxurious space for stretching out and walking around. With standard two-abreast seats, and even with the economical high density tourist seating arrangement, there’s never a sense of over-crowding.
Cruising at 320 m.p.h., the 10,000 h.p. Super-6 Clipper is indeed today’s proud, high-flying king of the intercontinental airways.
