I Fast-Moving Master of Medium Distance
The speedy, graceful Convair Clipper is a world-wide favorite with pilots as well as with passengers.
Its big twin engines deliver power to spare for quick take-off and climb, and for fast cruising on shorter flights.
As responsive to controls as a well-trained race horse, its smooth maneuverability and sleek lines are those of a thoroughbred.
The Convair’s passengers find still other features of this trim, medium-range masterpiece to praise.
They like the large, 16 x 18 inch, full-visibility windows which reveal extra-wide vistas of the landscape below. They enjoy the luxurious comfort of the adjustable, reclining, deep-cushioned seats.
And of course they love the good food and friendly service standard with PAA the world over.
PAA’s Convair Clipper is a beauty to look at—a joy to travel in.
