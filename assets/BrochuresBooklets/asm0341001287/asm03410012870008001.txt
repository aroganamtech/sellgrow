THE MIAMI OVERHAUL BASE-WORLD'S
LARGEST AVIATION MAINTENANCE PLANT
Here the Clippers undergo the famous maintenance procedures which have helped to earn for PAA the "dependability championship of the world.”
On frequent and inflexible schedules, the units of the Clipper Fleet arrive at this vast aircraft maintenance base for complete routine check-up and servicing.
On equally rigid schedules, the giant aircraft land at Miami for major overhaul. Engines, instruments,; wings, control surfaces, landing gear and all other components are removed, inspected, serviced, and repaired or replaced. Improvements, suggested by PAA’s many years of operating experience, are made. When reassembled, the Clippers are actually better than new!
The Miami Overhaul Base occupies 50 acres of Miami’s huge International Airport. It is PAA’s own gigantic "factory”—by far the largest of its kind in the world—where dependability is built into the Clipper Fleet on a Check and Test assembly line that never stops.
