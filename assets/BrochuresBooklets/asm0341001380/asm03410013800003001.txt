Why We Have the Nerve To Make This Survey
AND WHY IT WILL BENEFIT YOU!
QUITE frankly, we hope to use the results of this survey to help us in our business of serving you. And, quite possibly, to help you in your business. By studying your answers to our questions, we may be able to gain valuable insight into how service can be improved. At the same time, you may point out to us how air travel can be made to appeal to more people. As a traveler — or prospective traveler — you stand to benefit from what we discover.
Because this survey can be important to all of us, we’re sure you realize the value of every reply. That’s why we hope you’ll be willing to spare the few moments it will take to answer our questions. If you can’t answer them all, we hope you’ll answer as many as you possibly can. There is no need to sign your name or identify yourself in any way. And you may find it to be fun.
