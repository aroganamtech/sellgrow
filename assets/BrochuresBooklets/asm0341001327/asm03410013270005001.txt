BOEING 377 STRATO CLIPPER — Another Pan Am giant step forward. A double-decked aircraft which featured a sleeperette service for all passengers and completely private staterooms.
LOCKHEED L-49 CONSTELLATION — Famous “Connies” scored another Pan Am “First" in 1947 . . . flew first scheduled round-the-world flight.
MARTIN M-130 — “China Clipper”, chosen by Pan Am to make a pioneering dream come true . . . the first scheduled flight across the Pacific.
BOEING B 314 — "Yankee Clipper" the last and largest of Pan Am’s fleet of flying boats.
