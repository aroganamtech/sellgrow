CLIPPER HALL
The Home of Pan Am’s History
The proud home of Pan American’s history — Clipper Hall — is located in Pan Am’s Jet Age Terminal at John F. Kennedy International Airport, New York. The memorabilia displayed in Clipper Hall symbolizes the origin and development of Pan American Airways... a story identical with that of international air travel.
Collected as a result of a world-wide treasure hunt, the historic items found in Clipper Hall offer living testimony to aviation’s now fabled beginnings. Here are famous relics such as General Charles A. Lindbergh’s camera and drafting instruments with which he used to photograph and layout routes and courses for Pan Am; the airplane engine and propeller from the Fokker F-7 ... Pan Am’s original, tri-motored aircraft; scale models of many aircraft flown throughout Pan Am’s history; a world map used by Juan T. Trippe, founder, aviation pioneer and still president of Pan Am; a Mayan stone urn discovered by workers preparing a landing strip in Latin America ... and many other important landmarks from days gone by.
