If Business Is Taking You to the United States...
... a little extra knowledge of business people and their ways will do much to ensure the success of your trip. Although basic business practices in the States are not wholly unlike those you may be accustomed to, business attitudes do differ. . . the pace of business Is different, business people are different, the business philosophy of North America may sometimes seem the opposite of your own.
So it is best to equip yourself with a working knowledge of what is proper and what is not before you board your plane. And that is what this booklet is about. It will give you an informal briefing on what to expect in U.S. business circles . . . what the business courtesies are, where the pitfalls lie, what Is acceptable in a business relationship and what should be avoided. . . how to be prepared for cordial and profitable business discussions with your counterparts in the States.
We hope this information may help you to a most satisfying and rewarding business and personal experience.
