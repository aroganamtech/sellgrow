WHAT YOU’LL NEED TO ENTER THE UNITED STATES
In general, visitors must possess unexpired visas and passports to enter the United States. U.S. visas are issued on the basis of reciprocity, but usually most U.S. visitors' visas are valid for four years, for multiple entries, and are issued free of charge. Apply by mail to the U.S. consulate nearest to you. A vaccination certificate against smallpox is required. Vaccination certifcates against cholera or yellow fever are required only if you are traveling from areas infected with those diseases.
MAKING APPOINTMENTS
Although it is not always wise to make “cold” business calls in the United States, you can do so with some success — provided the company you're contacting is large enough to have a multi-level hierarchy of executives. Usually someone will be free to see you. In the U.S.A., responsibility is often widely dispersed in a business organization. And if the individual you are primarily interested in seeing is not available, another executive may be willing to discuss your proposition — frequently with profitable results. Best idea is to establish contact ahead of time by mail —then telephone on arrival. But if occasions arise when your pre-arranged appointments are completed, by all means feel free to call or “drop in” on a firm without previous arrangement. You'll be surprised how friendly and cordial American businessmen are towards visitors from another land.
