TAKE ADVANTAGE OF PAN AM’S FREE MARKETING SERVICE
One of the most comprehensive private sources of free marketing information — both before your trip and during your stay in the United States — is available through your Pan Am office. Pan Ann's World-Wide Marketing Service can help you find markets for your goods or sources of supply, without cost or obligation. Among the essential international marketing aids it provides are:
Information: Latest and most reliable reports on marketing conditions direct from local sources — to give you marketing facts, help you understand American trade practices.
Transportation: World’s largest over-ocean fleet of passenger and cargo Jet Clippers® — to speed your goods and you safely to and from U.S. markets.
Follow-through: Most experienced in-the-field advisory help — on procedure, import/export regulations — every important detail of doing business.
There is no obligation involved in requesting the assistance of World-Wide Marketing Service — so do not hesitate to let it help you. Call or write your nearest Pan Am office and say you would like to talk with a marketing specialist. Include a brief description of the products you wish to buy or sell and the kinds of outlets or sources you are looking for. If you have any preferences in market areas, mention them . . . and whether you or your representatives are veterans in international marketing or have not yet done business abroad.
These facts will brief a Pan Am specialist on your particular operation, give him facts for an accurate analysis of your marketing goals. Then he can discuss these goals with you in detail.. .taking all such matters as financing, distribution, communications, shipping, etc. into consideration. Though WWMS does not, of course, act as export broker or business representative abroad, it will steer you to the individuals, organizations, firms and government agencies you need.
