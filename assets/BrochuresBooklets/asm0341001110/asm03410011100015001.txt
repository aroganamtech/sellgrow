1ST DAY. FRIDAY:
USA/SAN JOSE.
Board your Pan Am Jet Clipper for San Jose. Assistance and transfer to your hotel.
2ND AND 3RD DAY.
SAN JOSE.
Saturday morning sightseeing tour of the city. Afternoon at leisure. Sunday free.
Your sightseeing tour will include the University of Costa Rica, residential districts and markets. A visit to the Archaeological Museum will introduce you to the precious collection of gold artifacts and other pre-Columbian Indian relics. You will also see the ornate National Theatre.
4TH DAY. MONDAY:
V SAN JOSE/SAN SALVADOR.
Transfer to the airport for your flight to San Salvador. Assistance on arrival and transfer to your hotel.
5TH DAY. TUESDAY:
SAN SALVADOR.
Morning sightseeing tour. Balance of the day at leisure.
You will visit the residential areas, native markets, universities, historical buildings, plus interesting surroundings such as Los Planes and the famous Puerta del Diablo (Devil’s Door).
6TH DAY. WEDNESDAY:
SAN SALVADOR/GUATEMALA. Transfer to airport for flight to Guatemala. Assistance and transfer to hotel. Afternoon city sightseeing tour.
Your afternoon tour will show you the residential districts, the Arts and Crafts Market and the new University Complex. Then continuing on you’ll visit Minerva Park, where you’ll see a large relief map of the city; the main plaza with its National Palace and Cathedral; and the new Civic Center.
7TH THROUGH 9TH DAYS. GUATEMALA.
Early morning departure on Thursday for a full day excursion to Chichicastenango and Lake Atitlan. Following two days free.
Visit Chichicastenango and see the Indians bring their wares to market then continue on to Lake Atitlan. Return to your hotel in the early evening. (Day of tour will vary to coincide with Mayan Market day.)
10TH DAY. SUNDAY: GUATEMALA/USA.
Transfer to the airport for your Pan Am Jet Clipper flight home.
