5TH DAY. TUESDAY: COCOYOC/OAXACA.
Breakfast at hotel. Box lunch en route. Dinner at hotel.
Early morning departure. Drive through the towering summits of the Sierra Madre del Sur to the city of Oaxaca.
6TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: OAXACA/MITLA/MT. ALBAN/ OAXACA.
Breakfast at hotel. Tour to the ruins of Mitla and Mt. Alban. Sightseeing tour of Oaxaca. Lunch and dinner at hotel.
Visit the ruins of Mitla. This site is divided into five groups some of which are only partially explored. Continuing, visit the ruins of Mt. Alban, built centuries ago by the Zopotec Indians. The tombs of these ruins yielded a priceless treasure of urns, jewels and goblets. Lunch at hotel and city tour of Oaxaca.
7TH DAY. THURSDAY: OAXACA/TUXTLA GUTIERREZ. Breakfast at hotel. Lunch and sightseeing Tehuantepec. Dinner at hotel.
Continuing south, journey through the Isthmus of Tehuantepec. Lunch and sightseeing in Tehuantepec, one of the principal cities on the Isthmus. Early afternoon arrival in Tuxtla Gutiérrez. Time to relax.
8TH DAY. FRIDAY:
TUXTLA GUTIÉRREZ/
SAN CRISTÓBAL de las CASAS. Breakfast at hotel. Sightseeing en route of Sumidero Canyon. Lunch at hotel. Afternoon tour of San Cristóbal and surrounding area. Dinner at hotel.
Early morning departure, on route to San Cristóbal you visit Sumidero Canyon, 26 miles in length with walls rising to 6,000 feet. After lunch tour San Cristóbal. It is situated in the small beautiful valley of Hueyzacatlán.
9TH DAY. SATURDAY:
SAN CRISTÓBAL/LAKE ATITLAN. Breakfast at hotel, lunch en route. Sightseeing en route of Comitán and Totonicapan. Dinner at hotel.
Drive via Comitán. Cross the border at Ciudad Cuauhtémoc to Guatemala. Say goodbye to your Mexican tour escort and greet your tour escort for Guatemala. En route to Lake Atitlan visit the village of Totonicapan, famous for its glazed pottery.
10TH DAY. SUNDAY:
LAKE ATITLAN/CHICHICASTEN-ANGO/LAKE ATITLAN.
Breakfast at hotel. Full day tour to Chichicastenango including lunch. Dinner at hotel.
Chichicastenango is an Indian-colonial town in the Maya-Quiche highlands. Colorfully costumed Indians bring their wares to the marketplace. Return to your hotel in the early evening.
11TH DAY. MONDAY:
LAKE ATITLAN/IXIMCHE/ ANTIGUA.
Breakfast at hotel. Lunch en route.
Tour of Iximche. Dinner at hotel.
Iximche ruins is famous for its stelae. It was the fortress capital of the Cakchiquel Maya until the nation fell in 1524. Lunch en route and on to Antigua for overnight.
12TH DAY. TUESDAY: ANTIGUA/GUATEMALA.
Breakfast at hotel. Morning tour of Antigua. After lunch continue to Guatemala City.
This morning tour the “Most Noble and Loyal City of St. James of the Knights of Goathemala.” See the Plaza Real, Palace of the Captains General, and the monastery of San Francisco. After lunch your motorcoach continues to Guatemala City.
13TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: GUATEMALA/TIKAL/FLORES. Breakfast at hotel. Full day tour to Mayan ruins at Tikal including lunch. Dinner at hotel.
Transfer to the airport for an early flight to Tikal, the Mayan ruins located in the heart of the jungle. Today these ruins offer a fascinating view of America’s ancient civilizations. In the afternoon continue to Flores for overnight.
14TH DAY. THURSDAY: FLORES/GUATEMALA.
Breakfast at hotel, morning flight to Guatemala City. Afternoon free.
Early morning flight to Guatemala City. Transfer to hotel on arrival. Day free.
15TH DAY. FRIDAY: GUATEMALA/USA.
Board your Pan Am Jet Clipper flight in the morning for your return home, arriving in the afternoon.
