7TH DAY. THURSDAY: GUATEMALA/CHICHI-CASTENANGO/ LAKE ATITLAN. Morning departure by motorcoach. Lunch in Chichicastenango. Dinner and overnight in Lake Atitlan. Chichicastenango is an Indian-colonial town in the Maya-Quiche highlands. The colorfully costumed Indians bring their wares to the marketplace which is set up in front of the Dominican Santo Tomás Church built in 1540.
8TH DAY. FRIDAY:
LAKE ATITLAN/GUATEMALA. Breakfast at hotel. Lunch and sightseeing in Antigua on return drive to Guatemala City.
On your way back to Guatemala City you’ll stop for sightseeing and lunch in the “Most Noble and Loyal City of St. James of the Knights of Goathemala”, Antigua. You will see the Plaza Real, Palace of the Captains General, and the Monastery of San Francisco to name just a few of this city’s highlights.

9TH DAY. SATURDAY: GUATEMALA.
Day free to relax or shop.
10TH DAY. SUNDAY: GUATEMALA/MERIDA.
Transfer to the airport for your flight to Merida. Assistance on arrival and transfer to your hotel. Balance of the day at leisure.
11TH DAY. MONDAY:
MERIDA.
Morning sightseeing tour. Balance of the day at leisure.
On your tour you’ll see the main points of interest in Merida; the State Museum of Anthropology and History, the Monument to the Country, the Cathedral and other churches of the 17th-century, the two remaining gates to the colonial city of Merida and the municipal market.
12TH DAY. TUESDAY: MERIDA/UXMAL.
Morning departure. Sightseeing tour of Uxmal including lunch. Dinner and overnight at the hotel.
Founded about 700 A.D. it’s believed Uxmal was created as an aristocratic center of Mayan rule. Many of the buildings have been identified and excavated, and several restored.
13TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: UXMAL/CHICHEN-ITZA. Breakfast at hotel. Sightseeing tour of Chichen-Itza including lunch. Dinner and overnight at the hotel.
One of the largest and most extensive ruins known in the “New World”, Chichen-Itza once had a population of 100,000 with thousands of stone buildings spread over six square miles. Experts claim it was occupied as late as the 16th-century although its period of greatness was much earlier.
14TH DAY. THURSDAY: CHICHEN-ITZA/MERIDA. Breakfast at hotel. Return to Merida with the remainder of the day free.
15TH DAY. FRIDAY: MERIDA/USA.
Transfer to the airport for your flight home.
