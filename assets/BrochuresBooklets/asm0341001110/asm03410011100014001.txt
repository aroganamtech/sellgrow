Full day tour to Tikal including lunch. Transfer to the airport for an early flight to the Mayan ruins at Tikal located in the heart of the jungle. The Mayan people simply disappeared from Tikal centuries ago leaving pyramids and temples to be over-run by the vegetation. Today these ruins offer a fascinating view of one of America’s ancient civilizations. Return to Guatemala City in the late afternoon. Price including roundtrip air fare to Tikal... $110.
Overnight Tour to Tikal. For those who want a little more time at the ruins and also be able to see a bit of the jungle you may prefer to take the overnight trip. You will fly to Tikal, tour the ruins, have lunch at Tikal and continue in the early evening to Flores. Dinner and overnight will be at Flores. Return from Flores to Guatemala City the next morning.
