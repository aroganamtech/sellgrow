1ST DAY. SUNDAY: USA/GUATEMALA.
Board your Pan Am Jet Clipper for Guatemala. Assistance on arrival and transfer to your hotel. Balance of day at leisure.
2ND DAY. MONDAY: GUATEMALA.
Morning city tour. Afternoon and evening at leisure.
Your morning tour will show you the residential districts, the Arts and Crafts
Market and the new University Complex. Then continuing on you’ll visit Minerva Park, where you’ll see a large relief map of the city; the main plaza with its National Palace and Cathedral; and the new Civic Center.
3RD DAY. TUESDAY: GUATEMALA/TIKAL/FLORES. Full day tour to Mayan ruins at Tikal.
Transfer to the airport for an early flight to Tikal, the Mayan ruins located in the heart of the jungle. The Mayan people simply disappeared from Tikal centuries ago leaving pyramids and temples to be over-run by vegetation. Today these ruins offer a fascinating view of one of America’s ancient civilizations. In the afternoon you will return to Flores.
4TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: FLORES/GUATEMALA.
After breakfast in Flores by air to Guatemala. Balance of day is free.
5TIT DAY. THURSDAY: GUATEMALA/CHICHICASTEN-ANGO/LAKE ATITLAN.
Morning departure by motorcoach. Lunch in Chichicastenango. Dinner and overnight in Lake Atitlan.
Chichicastenango is an Indian-colonial town in the Maya-Quiche highlands.
The colorfully costumed Indians bring their wares to the marketplace which is set up in front of the Dominican Santo Tomás Church, built in 1540. On the steps of the church Indian men burn aromatic copal incense in a unique religious custom which combines worship of pre-Hispanic pagan deities with Catholicism introduced by the Conquistadores. After your visit to the market you will continue to Lake Atitlan for the night.
6TH DAY. FRIDAY:
LAKE ATITLAN/ANTIGUA. Breakfast at the hotel. Boat ride on Lake Atitlan. Lunch en route to Antigua. Dinner and overnight at the hotel.
This morning you’re off on a glorious ride on Lake Atitlan. Lake Atitlan lies at 5,200 feet in a triangle of volcanoes.
The clear blue waters contrast with the dark mountains which rise from its shores.
Lunch en route and on to Antigua, the ancient capital during the Spanish Conquests, and today America’s most impressive monument to Spanish colonial grandeur. Overnight in Antigua.
7TH DAY. SATURDAY: ANTIGUA/GUATEMALA.
Morning tour of Antigua. After lunch return to Guatemala City. Evening at leisure.
This morning you will tour the “Most Noble and Loyal City of St. James of the Knights of Goathemala”. You will see the Plaza Real, Palace of the Captains General, and the Monastery of San Francisco to name just a few of this city’s highlights.
After lunch your motorcoach returns to Guatemala City.
8TH DAY. SUNDAY: GUATEMALA/USA.
Board your Pan Am Jet Clipper flight for home arriving later the same day.
