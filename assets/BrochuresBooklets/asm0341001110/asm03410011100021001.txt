3RD DAY. MONDAY: GUATEMALA/TIKAL/FLORES. Full day tour to Mayan ruins at Tikal. Lunch at Tikal. Dinner and overnight in Flores.
Transfer to the airport for an early flight to Tikal, the Mayan ruins located in the heart of the jungle. The Mayan people simply disappeared from Tikal centuries ago leaving pyramids and temples to be over-run by the vegetation. In the late afternoon continue to Flores.
4TH DAY. TUESDAY: FLORES/GUATEMALA.
After breakfast in Flores return by air to Guatemala. Balance of day is free.
5TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: GUATEMALA/PANAMA.
Transfer to the airport for flight to Panama City. Assistance and transfer to hotel. Remainder of day free.
6TH DAY. THURSDAY:
PANAMA.
Morning sightseeing tour of Panama City and Canal. Balance of day at leisure.
A half-day sightseeing tour will take you to the ornate National Cathedral, the President’s Palace and San Jose, the church whose lovely gold altar was saved from the Pirate Morgan’s attack in 1671. You’ll drive through the old part of town to Las Bovedas—the turreted seawall built by the Spanish to keep out pirates. (It didn’t work.) Then on to the fame of new Panama; the Canal and Locks. Here, ships pass between Atlantic and Pacific.
7TH DAY. FRIDAY: PANAMA/LIMA.
Transfer to airport. Direct flight to Lima. Transfer to hotel. Guided tour in afternoon.
You’ll visit both colonial Lima and the city’s modern residential suburbs. Your first visit will be to the Cathedral where you can see the remains of the conqueror Pizarro, then on to the Court of the Inquisition and the Colonial Palace of Torre Tagle. After seeing colonial Lima your drive takes you through residential districts to the Olive Grove and the country club of San Isidro.
8TH DAY. SATURDAY: LIMA/CUZCO.
Transfer to the airport for early morning flight to Cuzco. Transfer to hotel. Afternoon sightseeing tour of Cuzco.
Capital of the Incan Empire, Cuzco is located at 11,400 feet. Most visitors like to rest a short time to become adjusted to the altitude and avoid any discomfort.
9TH DAY. SUNDAY:
CUZCO.
Sightseeing tour of Urubamba Valley including market town of Pisac. Afternoon visit to Inca Fortress of Ollantaytambo.
Head up the Urubamba Valley in the morning and stop at Pisac for a colorful display of an Indian market town. You can find bargains in alpaca, vicuna, fur and woven articles, inexpensively priced. After lunch you’ll visit the Inca Fortress of Ollantaytambo. Return to Cuzco via scenic Lake Huaipo.
10TH DAY. MONDAY: CUZCO/MACHU PICCHU. Transfer to railway station for scenic ride to Machu Picchu.
The “Lost City of the Incas” (at an altitude of 8,000 feet) is probably the most awe-inspiring sight in South America. A mountain sanctuary used by the Inca rulers in times of distress, it was so well hidden that its existence was not known until its discovery in 1911 by an American named Hiram Bingham.
11TH DAY. TUESDAY:
MACHU PICCHU/CUZCO.
Train to Cuzco and overnight.
12TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: CUZCO/LIMA.
Transfer to airport for early morning flight to Lima. Transfer to hotel. Remainder of day free.
13TH DAY. THURSDAY: LIMA/USA.
Day at leisure. Very late transfer to airport for flight to the USA.
14TH DAY. FRIDAY:
USA.
Arrive back in USA.
