2ND DAY. SATURDAY:
SAN JOSE.
Morning sightseeing tour of San Jose. Balance of day at leisure.
San Jose is a much admired city of broad avenues and spacious parks. Your sightseeing tour will include the University of Costa Rica, residential districts and markets. A visit to the Archaeological Museum will introduce you to the precious collection of gold artifacts and other pre-Columbian Indian relics. You will also see the National Theatre, known throughout Central America for its ornate decorations.
3RD DAY. SUNDAY:
SAN JOSE.
Full day tour to Irazu Volcano and the Orosi Valley including lunch.
Your drive to the volcano is one of the unforgettable experiences in Costa Rica. The drive passes through some of the richest coffee growing regions in the country. The climb continues through dairy land up to the rim of the volcano,
11,264 feet high, where you can actually lean over and look down into the crater. On a clear day, from this peak, you can see both the Atlantic and the Pacific Oceans as well as the Great Lake of Nicaragua. On your return you will stop for lunch at a quaint mountain hotel and then continue on to the enchanted valley of Orosi. You will visit the 400-year-old church of Orosi with its treasure of religious relics. You will also visit the “Ruins of Ujarras”, one of the first Spanish settlements, continuing through lush sugar and coffee plantations to admire the impressive “Cachi Dam”. Late in the afternoon you will return to San Jose.
