You’ll likely want to divide your time between the many pleasures of the beautiful new Hotel Presidente Hyatt and El Salvador’s many attractions. You can take an optional tour of the colonial city of Santa Tecla, or get a thrilling closeup of colorful Lake Ilopango. Or you can visit the pottery market of San Martin, take in San Salvador’s exciting nightlife, or spend a day at an outstanding beach club. Wherever you go, the best place to start is Hotel Presidente Hyatt, nestled in a nine-acre garden setting of flowers, fountains, pools and palm trees. Near all important cultural, artistic, and international attractions and with its own array of pleasures: A superb restaurant. A lakesize swimming pool, largest of any hotel in Central America.
