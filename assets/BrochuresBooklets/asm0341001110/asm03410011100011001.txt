1ST DAY. FRIDAY:
USA/SAN JOSE.
Board your Pan Am Jet Clipper flight for San Jose. Assistance and transfer to your hotel. Balance of day free.
2ND AND 3RD DAYS.
SAN JOSE.
Saturday morning city tour with afternoon and evening free. Sunday free.
Your Saturday sightseeing tour will include the University of Costa Rica, residential districts and markets. A visit to the Archaeological Museum will introduce you to the precious collection of gold artifacts and other pre-Columbian Indian relics. You will also see the National Theatre, known for its ornate decorations. Sunday is yours to explore.
4TH DAY. MONDAY:
SAN JOSE/GUATEMALA.
Transfer to the airport for your flight to Guatemala City. Assistance on arrival and transfer to your hotel. Balance of day free.
View of Costa Rica
5TH AND 6TH DAYS. GUATEMALA.
Tuesday morning sightseeing tour, afternoon at leisure. Wednesday is free to relax or take an optional tour.
Your morning tour will show you the residential districts, the Arts and Crafts Market and the new University Complex. Then continuing on you’ll visit Minerva Park, where you’ll see a large relief map of the city; the main plaza with its National Palace and Cathedral; and the new Civic Center.
7TH DAY. THURSDAY: GUATEMALA.
A full day tour to Chichicastenango and Lake Atitlan. Late afternoon return. Evening free.
Visit Chichicastenango and see the Indians bring their wares to market then continue on to Lake Atitlan. Return to your hotel in the early evening. (Day of tour will vary to coincide with Mayan Market day.)
8TH DAY. FRIDAY: GUATEMALA/USA.
