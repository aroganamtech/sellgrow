2ND DAY. GUATEMALA.
Morning sightseeing tour of Guatemala City. Afternoon at leisure.
Your morning tour will show you the residential districts, the Arts and Crafts Market and the new University Complex. Then continuing on you’ll visit Minerva Park, where you’ll see a large relief map of the city; the main plaza with its National Palace and Cathedral; and the new Civic Center.
