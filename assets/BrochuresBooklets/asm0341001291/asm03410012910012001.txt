EVEN THE FOOD TASTES FINER

*****
It’s when people start waiting on you that you discover the careful planning that Pan American has put into every phase of new-day Jet Clipper service.
Pan American makes a particular point of what it calls its “International Standard of Service.” This is important when you consider that in a very short time few countries of the world will be further than twelve hours apart by Jet Clipper. You want international flavor on board your Jet Clipper — it adds to the adventure, lifts you away from the accustomed routine world; at the same time, you want enough American atmosphere so that you will always feel comfortably “at home.”
Pan American chooses its personnel and plans its passenger service with this in mind. If you want to practice your French before you land in the midst of Paris, you’ll find that Jet Clipper stewardesses are multilinguists. They are also capable advisers to answer questions about your destination.
The Pan Am International Standard is pure delight when it comes to food. In First Class service, your full course dinner gives you a wide and wonderful choice, with favorite dishes of various countries featured. French cooking — by famous Maxim’s — makes the dinner hour perfect. If you travel on Economy Service, you’ll discover just how good sandwich meals can be.
Every minute of your Jet Clipper flight, you are waited on hand and foot by a service team of cabin attendants. Their sole job — to make you happy, to answer your wants without delay.
