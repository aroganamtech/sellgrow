JET CLIPPERS MEAN A NEW CONCEPT OF THE WORLD YOU LIVE IN
Think of the great exciting moments of history that you missed — that happened before you were born. Think of the ones you have seen — the beautiful things, the products of invention, that have touched your life, and made it more wonderful.
Now come with us to meet a new miracle. A miracle with graceful swept-back wings. A great and powerful sky giant—a new kind of air transport that can make all your most wonderful worldly dreams, and many you never thought of, all come true.
It’s the magnificent new Pan American Jet Clipper — the Boeing 707. And very soon, the DC-8 Jet Clipper. Both products of American designing and manufacturing skill, of American progress in the air — introduced by the leader in international air transportation, Pan American.
Jet travel by Pan Am Clipper is no longer a thing of tomorrow. IPs here — now, today. You can set your watch by Westminster’s Big Ben six and one-half hours after leaving the U.S. — or, in seven astonishingly short hours, find yourself sipping an aperitif in a Paris sidewalk cafe. Soon, you can span the Pacific, or float gently down over Rio de Janeiro as casually as though you were going to Chicago, or Denver, or Miami. It’s a truly new world — a world you can grasp—a world that you can make your own. With travel time cut almost in half, you can see the sights you’ve longed for, realize the opportunities — in pleasure, in business — that once seemed too remote. The distant Secret Places are now far easier and far less tiring to reach, than the one you visited on your last automobile trip.
The realities of Jet Clipper speed mean a whole new concept of the world you five in. But there’s another part that’s equally important — the unbelievably wonderful experience of flying in a Jet Clipper.
Let us show you what it is like ... in the next few minutes.
