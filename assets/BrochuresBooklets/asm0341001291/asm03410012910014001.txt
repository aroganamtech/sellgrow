SO SMOOTH, SO STEADY, SO WONDERFULLY RESTFUL
What makes the new Jet Clippers so completely comfortable to fly in? The astonishing quiet — yes. The plane’s smoothness and freedom from vibration. Both these things are perhaps the biggest surprises.
But the joy of your Jet Clipper trip is made up of a great number of things. Its very size makes for more comfort — you feel it the moment you step inside. There’s more space everywhere. Seats, too, are improved — made carefully to man’s measure, and cantilevered in construction so that there’s plenty of footroom. Arm rests are wide, with real elbowroom. Decoration is rich, yet restful. There’s soft soothing music. And there are ever so many more windows.
When night comes, and time for sleep, the domed ceiling changes to restful blue. There’s a special hassock for your feet, the stewardess slips a down-soft pillow under your head, draws up a blanket — and there you are, blissfully slipping from one dream world into another.
