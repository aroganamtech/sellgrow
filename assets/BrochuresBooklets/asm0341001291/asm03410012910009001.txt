WORLD TRAVELER — YEARS IN THE MAKING
The Jet Clipper 707 has more development and testing behind it than any other air transport ever put into commercial service. Typical of Pan American’s leadership in the introduction of improved aircraft, Pan Am engineers and technical experts have had a major hand in making the Jet Clipper the great globe girdler that it is.
Pan American’s initial work in the development of commercial jet aircraft started in 1945. The majestic Jet Clipper which you see here is the final result. This is a plane built for world operation, a plane that cruises effortlessly at 575 miles per hour—the most important giant stride in aviation history.
