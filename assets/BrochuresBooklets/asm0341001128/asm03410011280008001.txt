When You Fly Pan American...You’ll Arrive—
and
READY FOR BUSINESS OR PLEASURE
Sleeper-equipped Clippers provide you with luxury and comfort all the way. A 3,000 mile journey becomes a delightful interlude. You retire to your berth at your regular bedtime hour, enjoy a restful night’s sleep and arrive at your destination the next morning. Your trip is completed with unbelievable ease and the time saved gives you many added days to devote to business and pleasure. There is, of course, an additional charge for a berth. If, however, you do not require a berth, passage may be booked at the regular fare. Ask your travel agent or phone the nearest Pan American office for complete details.
