ladies’ lounge
The Ladies’Lounge is decorated in soft pastels with the deft touch of a queen’s artist. There are comfortable lounge seats, smart vanity tables, spotless wash basins and gleaming mirrors. Such tasteful elegance and convenience adds immeasurably to the pleasure of feminine travelers aboard the new Clippers.
You’ll find plenty of hot and cold water and individual bars of gentle soap. Soft towels, tissues and other convenience items are available. There’s also a germicidal lamp to provide extra sanitation.
Wide, gleaming mirrors and fully equipped vanity tables give you a wonderful feeling of confidence that you’ll be looking your loveliest all during the trip.
Courteous stewardess service adds that friendly, personal touch. If you need assistance in any way-just ask, she’ll be glad to help.
