Assignments:
When you have completed training, you will be assigned to a base where additional indoctrination will be given to you about the equipment, area and routes you will fly. Then you will be released to line flying. You will periodically undergo additional training to keep you abreast of new developments in aviation throughout your career as a Jet Clipper Pilot. Whenever possible, it is Pan Am’s policy to let you know in advance where you will be assigned. It is also Pan Am’s policy to assign you initially to a base of your choice when company requirements permit such placement.
Equipment:
The modern, fully equipped Pan American Jet Clipper Fleet consists of Boeing 720B, 707 and 727 aircraft as well as Douglas DC-8’s. On order are Boeing 747’s, Concordes and American SST’s. Pan Am has traditionally been the industry leader in the introduction of new aircraft to commercial service and in the refinement of existing equipment and techniques for improved safety and utilization.

Salary:	1
• Your initial salary for the first six months as a Probationary Copilot will be $500 (flat rate) per month. During your second six months as a Probationary Copilot you will receive $658 (flat rate) per month. As a Jet Copilot on increment pay during your second year you will receive $842 per month (minimum guarantee) or $1043 for 85 hours, and during your third year $1046 per month (minimum guarantee) or $1295 for 85 hours.
