If assigned to serve as a Flight Engineer your initial salary will be $500.00 (flat rate) per month during the first year. During the first six months after your first productive flight as a Flight Engineer, your monthly salary will be $840.00 (minimum guarantee) or $1020.00 for 85 hours service on B-707 Jet equipment.
Your pay continues to be adjusted in accordance with your longevity and responsibility.
Company Benefits:
Pan American offers excellent benefits to pilots and their families including:
Travel privileges on Pan Am’s international, worldwide routes at minimum fares. Travel discounts on other domestic and international airlines.
Extensive Group Retirement Income Plan funded solely by Pan Am. You pay nothing. This Plan operates as a variable trust and includes a minimum disability factor.
A Group Insurance Plan (all risk) which is one of the best in the industry requiring nominal contribution on your part for maximum coverage.
Group Hospital and Surgical Plan. Paid for by Pan Am except for nominal contributions by you during the first six months of your probationary period.
30 days of vacation with full pay each year for all pilots. Probationary pilots receive 15 days vacation after one year.
