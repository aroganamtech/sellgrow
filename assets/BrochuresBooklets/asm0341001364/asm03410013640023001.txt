PAN AM PRESENTS A DISTINGUISHED SERIES OF AVIATION LITHOGRAPHS: This limited edition collection commemorates ten historic firsts in aviation history. The superbly printed 8-color lithographs are from original paintings by noted aviation artist, John McCoy. Prints measure 20” x 16” and come in a handsome embossed portfolio. Included in each set are the following:
Plate 1. The Fokker F-7 flying from Key West to Havana, January 16, 1928. Captain: Hugh I. Wells.
Plate 2. The Sikorsky S-38 arriving at the Canal Zone in Panama from Miami, February 6, 1929. Captain: Charles A. Lindbergh.
Plate 3. The Sikorsky S-42 Brazilian Clipper arriving in Rio de Janeiro, August 18, 1934. Captain: Wallace Culbertson.
Plate 4. The Martin M-130 Philippine Clipper arriving in Hong Kong Harbor, October 23, 1936. Captain: J. H. Tilton. Plate 5. The Boeing B-314 Dixie Clipper completing the first transatlantic passenger flight, New York to Lisbon, June 29, 1939. Captain: Robert D. Fordyce.
Plate 6. The Douglas DS-4 inaugurating commercial land plane service into the Belgian Congo, January 19, 1946. Captain: Audrey Durst.
Plate 7. The Lockheed Constellation completing the first commercial round-the-world flight in San Francisco, June 29, 1947. Captain: Hugh H. Gordon.
Plate 8. The Boeing B-377 Clipper America on Operation Deep Freeze arriving in McMurdo Sound, Antarctica, October 15, 1957. Captain: Ralph Savory.
Plate 9. The Boeing 707 Clipper America on Operation Deep Freeze; first scheduled jet passenger flight, October 26, 1958. Captain: Samuel H. Miller.
Plate 10. The Boeing 747 Superjet on its maiden commercial flight from JFK Airport to Paris, January 21, 1970. Captain: Robert M. Weeks.
