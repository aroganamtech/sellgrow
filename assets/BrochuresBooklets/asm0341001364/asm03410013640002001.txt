Welcome to Pan Am’s World of Shopping!
Since we opened the world to air travel (starting back in 1929) we’ve carried more people to more places around the world than anyone else.
And, one thing we’ve noticed.
Just about everyone who flies to a foreign land agrees that part of the fun is shopping.
Because this is true, you might say that we’ve taken more people shopping to more places around the world than anyone else.
In addition to all the shops we can take you to browse or buy — the boutiques in Paris, the bazaars of the Middle East, the market places of the Orient — we now can offer you another place to shop. Right in the pages of this book.
In it you’ll find an array of gifts you can order to be shipped anywhere in the world. And as you might expect from Pan Am, this new international mailing service is the first of its kind on any airline.
Whether you select Italian alabaster, or Swedish cookware, something in English leather, a novelty from Japan or any of the other unique and elegant items, we think you’ll be delighted with the gifts listed in this book.
The mailing classification of each item is shown in parentheses () after the price. See the mailing chart on the back cover to determine mailing charges for your purchases. Fill in the enclosed order form and include your check or money order in the currency of your own country. (Prices are shown in U. S. currency.) Or you may charge to the credit cards indicated, including our Pan Am Take-Off Card. Then mail your order, and be assured it will receive our prompt attention.
