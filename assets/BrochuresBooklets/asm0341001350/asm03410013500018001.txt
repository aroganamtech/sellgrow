PUERTO RICO /VIRGIN ISLANDS

Alight refreshed and relaxed from your Clipper in San Juan, Puerto Rico. Your headquarters for the first four days will be the beautiful new San Juan Intercontinental Hotel. In San Juan,.a tour of the city will take you to the Cathedral, the Presidential Palace, the attractive suburbs of Santurce and Rio Piedras and other highlights of this ancient city . . . and there’s another scenic drive along the beautiful tropic coastline to El Yunque Mountain and the Caribbean National Forest. On the fifth day, a 30-minute flight carries you to St. Thomas, free port of the Virgin Islands. In the quaint old town of Charlotte Amalie, the islands’ capital, you’ll find fascinating native bazaars and modern stores selling European China, Danish silver, liquors, cameras and other fabulous imported wares at low “duty-free” prices. You’ll have ample opportunity for whatever activities you prefer, from golf, tennis, boating, fishing and other active sports to lazing on the beautiful white-sand beaches, with nightly entertainment and dancing. And you may leave and return by comfortable Pan Am Clipper any day of the week.

You’ll spend your first night at the Myrtle Bank Hotel in Kingston, Jamaica, the island’s capital, then drive by way of luxuriant Castleton Gardens to the famous North Shore resort town of Ochos Rios. Your headquarters will be the beautiful Tower Isle Hotel — one of the most luxurious hotels in the Caribbean area. Swimming is incomparable, in beach or pool, and you’ll find rowboats, sail and power boats for coastal cruising or deep sea fishing at your whim. On the seventh day, return to Kingston by private car for your homeward flight — direct to New York or via Miami, Florida, at your option.
