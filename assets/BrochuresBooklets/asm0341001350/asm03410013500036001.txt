GENERAL FACTS YOU'LL WANT TO KNOW
TRANSPORTATION: Comfortable, dependable Clipper air transportation starting from and returning to Gateway City you select as your point of departure . . . transfer between air and rail terminals and hotels . . . private car or coach sightseeing tours . . . local air transportation where indicated.
HOTELS: Carefully selected hotel accommodations are arranged in advance and included in the Pan Am Holiday prices.
MEALS: With a few exceptions, such as where hotels specify other than American Plan, meals are included. In some major cities, breakfasts only.
NOT INCLUDED: Laundry and valet services, wines, liquors and other personal items; transportation taxes within the U.S. and nominal taxes levied by other governments are not included.
ALL HOLIDAY PRICES shown are approximate and subject to change without notice and to the conditions described in the tour folders from which these typical tours are selected.
OTHER INFORMATION
PASSPORTS: Many of the tours in this Tour Guide do not require passports of U.S. citizens. Your local Pan Am office or Travel Agent can give you full details about what papers are needed and how to go about getting them.
