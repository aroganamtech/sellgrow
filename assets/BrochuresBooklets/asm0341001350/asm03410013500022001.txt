En route, visit Guatemala’s most picturesque Indian market and the 400-year-old Church of Santo Tomas. Plenty of free time for exploring the many attractions in this quaint town on your own. Then return to Guatemala City for the brief flight to Merida, Yucatan, where you’ll motor to Chichen Itza for a stay at the unique Mayaland Lodge. Chichen Itza, the American continent’s largest archaeological center, is one of the loveliest ancient cities in the Western Hemisphere, and you’ll have two days to visit temples, shrines and palaces . . . to inspect closely the centuries-old Mayan Architecture which has no parallel anywhere. Next you’ll drive through quaint Mayan villages and sisal plantations to the famous ruins at Uxmal — House of the Dwarf, the Palace of Governors, the
Nunnery Quadrangle and other attractions—before your return to Merida. A tour of the city precedes a two-hour flight to gay Havana, where you’ll stop at the famed Hotel Nacional. Next day, you’ll see both old and new Havana . . . the ancient cathedral, Alameda de Paulo, city market, the Capitol, famed Prado and sea-skirting Malecon Drive. An evening tour takes you to Chinatown and the renowned Tropicana Night Club for the floor show and dancing. You may fly home from Havana by any of several interesting routes.
