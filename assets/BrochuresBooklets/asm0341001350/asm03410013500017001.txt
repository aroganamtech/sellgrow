Arriving by Clipper, via San Juan, Puerto Rico, you will enjoy 7 days in St. Thomas, free port of the Virgin Islands, making your headquarters at your choice of the four finest hotels. By auto, you’ll tour the city from French Cha-Cha Town to the heights above Magens Bay. In the quaint town of Charlotte Amalie, the island capital, are fascinating native bazaars and modern stores selling European china, Danish silver, English woolens, liquor, cameras and other fabulous imported wares at low “duty-free” prices. If you seek rest and relaxation, there are innumerable beauty spots and superb white-sand beaches. Or if you prefer the active life, you’ll find golf and tennis, daily schooner cruises to nearby cays and islands, water-skiing, skin-diving, fishing and nightly dancing and
entertainment. The Virgin Islands are ideal for making a vacation whatever you wish it. And you may leave and return by comfortable Pan Am Clipper any day of the week.
