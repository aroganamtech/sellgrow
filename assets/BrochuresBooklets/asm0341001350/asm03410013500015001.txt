America’s Year-Round Playground
As a playground, a vacation-land, a travel adventure “away from it all”... the Caribbean has everything. Right in your own “back yard”— just a few hours away from any point in the United States — you can enjoy all the exotic flavor, the exciting difference of faraway lands and foreign places.
Jamaica and Nassau, for example, are as British as Hyde Park or Big Ben — more so in many ways! While Dutch-speaking Curacao is a miniature Holland — even to its quaint gabled roofs and old bridges. Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Dominican Republic are Spanish by language, by tradition, by preference. In Martinique, you can sample the Creole Culture of “New France.” And in cosmopolitan Trinidad, there’s even a rich oriental influence, complete with Hindu temples, Moslem mosques and women in saris.
No wonder more and more travel-seasoned vacationers head South to the Caribbean when holiday time rolls around! The climate’s always good — with no more than 10-12 degrees average variation between winter and summer. And speaking of summer, there’s better choice of accommodations and much lower rates during that happy season. And the Caribbean’s so easy to reach.
