Begins with Clipper flight to London, where you’ll visit Parliament, St. Paul’s, the Tower, see the Changing of the
Guard ... then motor down to Oxford and “Shakespeare Country” . . . Stratford, Anne Hathaway’s Cottage, Warwick Castle. Then across the Channel for two days in Holland . . . Amsterdam, diamond-center of the world, the quaint old-world costumes at Volendam, the Peace Palace at the Hague. In Belgium you’ll tour its lovely capital, Brussels, and its medieval Guild Houses, Renaissance Town Hall. Down to Paris then, where you’ll have four full days to visit the monuments, the palaces, the churches and quaint sections of Europe’s gayest and most historically interesting capital . . . the Louvre, Montmartre, Sacre Coeur, the Left Bank, Sor-bonne . . . and a special excursion to the Palace Gardens of Versailles. South then by train to Lourdes, where you’ll have a free day to visit the famous shrine, and on to Nice and the French Riviera . . . and by the spectacular “ Grande Corniche” highway to Monaco and Monte Carlo. From Nice, you’ll turn West next — to the Iberian Peninsula ... to Barcelona, bustling capital of Catalonia . . . and on to four wonderful days in Madrid, and its great museums, palaces, fortresses and magnificent hotels. You’ll take a special side trip to Toledo of El Greco fame, and (at your option), to El Escorial, Aranjuez or Sogovia. Your historical tour concludes in Lisbon, Portugal, where you’ll see the Museum of Coaches, the coastal resort, Estoril and other principal sights. Departure for home is from Lisbon by transatlantic Pan Am Clipper.
