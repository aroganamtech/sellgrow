Although 22 U.S. airlines participated to varying degrees in international operations for ATC and NATS, Pan American flew about half of the total air mileage flo^vn by the airlines! During the course of the war Pan American made over 18,000 trans-ocean crossings and flew over 90 million aircraft miles for the U.S. Government. The airline’s contribution to the United States war effort was unmatched! Indeed, the war supplies which the airline flew across the S. Atlantic and across Africa to. Cairo
were credited by General Montgomery with turning the tide, enabling the Allied forces to drive the Axis out of Africa and the Mediterranean, and to save the vital Suez Canal!
In July, 1946, Pres. Harry S. Truman awarded “The Medal for Merit” to Juan Trippe for exceptionally meritorious conduct in the performance of outstanding services to the United States. Juan Trippe was also presented the Harmon Trophy in 1946 for his airline’s contribution to the military success of the Allied powers. . . “at a time and in a manner that could not have been equalled by any other allied agency!”
