Powered by 700 hp P-W Hornets, the S-42 set ten international flying boat records before entering airline service with Pan American on S. American routes.
At the time the S-42s went into service Sikorsky designed and produced still another aircraft, the S-43! This twin-engined amphibian', with 18 passenger capacity, was introduced in 1936 as a replacement for the aging S-38s and the Commodores. Known as the “Baby Clipper,” the S-43 was powered by 750 hp Wasp engines. This plane, too, proved to be a record breaking aircraft!
