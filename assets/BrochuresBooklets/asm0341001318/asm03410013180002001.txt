INTRODUCTION
Having been a student of Pan Ann’s history for several years, the author is convinced that this story is a record of incomparable accomplishments in commercial aviation.
The creation of this concise history of Pan Am was for the purpose of promoting employee awareness of Pan Ann’s past greatness with the hope of creating a greater spirit of pride and loyalty among the airline’s employees. This can only serve to build a stronger, more efficient and more profitable airline with an assured future.
With this booklet I salute the thousands of Pan Am employees whose loyalty and enthusiasm have contributed so much to the survival of our great airline!
