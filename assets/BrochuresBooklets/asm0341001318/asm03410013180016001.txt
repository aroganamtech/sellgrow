Boeing B-377, 1949
The first challenger to Lockheed’s Constellation was Boeing’s B-377, a civil version of the KC-97 tanker-transport developed for the USAF. Pan American had placed an order for 21 B-377s in November, 1945, at a price of $1.5 million each. First deliveries were made early in 1949, and the company opened “Stratocruiser” service from San Francisco to Honolulu on April 1st. Trans-Atlantic service with this type began in June; and Stratocruiser service on the Tokyo run started in October, 1949. The Stratocruiser cut 8 hours off the DC-4 time between San Francisco and Tokyo. Pan Ams Pacific jet stream studies enabled their Boeing 377s to make frequent non-stop flights from Tokyo to Honolulu in the record time of 11 hours, eliminating the fuel stop at Wake Island and cutting 7 hours off the scheduled time.
