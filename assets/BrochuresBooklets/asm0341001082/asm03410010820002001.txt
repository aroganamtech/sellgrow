...Just a Few Short Hours Away
MEXICO
Will cast a rainbow spell around your shoulder, and you'll forget the workaday world as you dine and dance in Mexico City's glamorous night clubs and cosmopolitan restaurants ... Cuisine by top chefs from the world over, including spicy Mexican dishes you'll long remember... The finest race track in America beckons you to the sport of kings. .. and there are the thrills of the bullfights... cockfights... jai-alai. .. boxing .. . wrestling, and many other spectator sports.
For your cultural delight you'll find the breath-taking campus of University City, an endless array of museums and libraries, ancient buildings and pyramids ... a score of theatres. .. movie houses with films from all nations... fascinating market places and every where, music by the mariachis, the famed Mexican troubadors. A short ride away is Xochimilco, the Venice of Mexico, with its floating gardens. All around the city are awe-inspiring drives through mountain scenery dominated by the snow-capped peaks of Popocateptl and Ixtaccihuatl.
