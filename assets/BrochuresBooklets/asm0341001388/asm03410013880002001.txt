
Shell Folders
You’ll find Pan Am offers a choice of shells for many destinations, usually with varying amounts of space available for copy so that you may choose the folder that’s best suited to what you want said in print. Naturally you will want to include all the important facts about your program... dates, air fare, land costs, etc. And you will want to give good display to your agency name, address and phone number... both for image-building reasons and to be sure the prospect calls your agency with questions... and to make reservations.
All available shells are four-color, and a well-designed imprint will make it appear that the finished folder was custom-printed in its.entirety for your agency. Some travel agents make this illusion even more convincing by imprinting in two colors... using the second color for the Travel Agency logo, name and address and for major headlines. Two-color printing, in most areas, is not greatly more expensive than one-color printing.
It’s the initial preparation of four-color artwork that is the major expense.
For example, the cost of producing 3,000 of your own 4-color folders might easily exceed $500 for layouts, art mechanicals and color separations, plus $600 for printing— or a total of 3 7<t apiece.
By comparison, Pan Am shells are priced at only 3.9< each (including all shipping and handling costs), plus your local imprinting charge of under 10<L each. That’s quite a saving for top-quality 4-color folders.
Your Pan Am Sales Representative has an up-to-date Sales Promotion Catalog with illustrations and complete descriptions of all of the currently available shell folders, letterheads, and other promotional materials. Most shell folders are 4" x 9" (to fit easily in racks and #10 envelopes) and are 8-panel. Besides “destination” shell folders, there are also general interest, service and sports shells available for special promotions.
Suggestions:
When you are preparing your own copy and/or layout for an imprint, start with a list of all the vital material that must appear. Check off each item after copy is done to be sure all are included.
Keep copy as brief as practical. Overwriting is a far more common fault than underwriting (and type-setting costs money).
Do not use too many sizes and styles of type (both to save money and to avoid a cluttered, amateurish look). Generally, one typeface for body copy and a maximum of two typefaces or typesizes for
headlines and subheadlines.
Letterheads
As with shell folders, you have a wide choice of illustrated letterheads, some photographic, some with artwork. All have Pan Am identification, but are designed to allow your imprint to have greater prominence.
There are destination letterheads and theme letterheads, and chances are good there’ll be at least one suited to whatever travel promotion you might have in mind.
The choice of letterhead naturally depends on your purpose. And besides specific and general destination letterheads, there are moderate quantities available of some strictly ‘general travel promotion’ letterheads that were initially designed for specific mailings but are suitable for any promotion that can be adapted to the illustration and headline.
LETTERHEADS may also be used as “Flyers” — with the main promotional story on the illustrated side and the necessary ‘nuts and bolts’ data on the other side.
