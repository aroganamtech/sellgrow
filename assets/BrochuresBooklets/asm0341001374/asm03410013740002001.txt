meet the U. S. CUSTOMS:
the rules are simple . . . knowing them can ease you through inspections . . . fast!
Whether you’re homeward bound ... or making your first visit to the United States . . . there’s no need to fear delay or inconvenience at the customs. There are rules, of course — but all of them are sensible — and set up simply to protect the United States, its citizens, and its visitors. But since these rules are enforced without exception, we thought you’d like to look over the ones that affect you . . . plus some hints to speed you through customs.
For instance, it’s faster to pack dutiable items together. It’s easier to list each one as you pack it. It’s safer to check your final list with your sales slips . . . see that everything is listed correctly. Then you have only to copy this list on the U.S. Customs declaration form — which you will be given by your purser aboard the Clipper — with ease and speed.
Of course, traveling by Pan American “Clipper” you’re at your destination before there’s any need to disturb that final packing — and you avoid the havoc which would result from having to live out of your luggage en route.
When you land at the U.S. Port of Entry, there are several questions you’ll have to answer: How long you’ve been gone from the country . . . what you acquired on your trip . . . what you have to declare.
And your answers are important! An unintentional error or omission can cause you considerable inconvenience . . . and misrepresentation can result in loss of your free-entry privileges or in some cases, confiscation of your belongings. We think you’ll find these suggestions an aid to a more pleasant trip. And you can count on their accuracy . . . the United States Government supplied us with its latest regulations.
