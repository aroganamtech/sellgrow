When you’re going to visit the United States, you’ll be wise to list for customs inspection everything you have with you. Ordinarily, wearing apparel, articles of personal adornment, toilet articles, etc., which you owned abroad and which are for your own use are considered exempt from declaration. However, a complete list can serve as protection for you and you may be asked for one.
strictly personal . . .
The personal items described above are exempt from duty and internal revenue taxes, broadly speaking. Other personal effects you may include are hunting and fishing equipment, cameras for non-professional use, wheelchairs, pet and hunting dogs, etc. You should be able to prove that you owned them prior to your departure . . . that they are necessary, appropriate and intended for your own use or wear . . . and that they are not for other persons or for sale. An article brought in for sale or as a gift for another person must be declared in writing.
