tips on tariffs
TO SAVE YOU TIME & MONEY:
Get a sales slip, purchase order or receipt for everything you buy abroad.
Keep a list of articles to be declared.
Pack these articles together, if possible.
Since your written declaration must be made at the first U.S. customs port reached on your return, be sure to get the list into the hands of your Pan American purser well ahead of your arrival. He will supply you with a customs declaration form, and give you any assistance you need in making it out.
