CUSTOMS INSPECTORS
THE
Customs officers are stationed at U.S. ports of entry for your protection. You’ll find them courteous, quiet and efficient . . . interested in clear-ing you and your belongings just as quickly and pleasantly as possible.
You can help them by having your declaration, with sales slips and purchase orders attached, ready for their immediate inspection. It will also help both you and them if your bags are packed so that inspection of their contents will be easy. When the customs inspector asks you to open any piece of luggage, do so without hesitation. Such a request is only reasonable when you consider that his job is to protect you, the honest traveler, and the welfare of the country. He has to be on constant guard against the menace of the narcotic smuggler — and other law violators — who often masquerade as honest citizens.
So do your part! With your cooperation, your customs officer can give you fast and friendly service. And we think you’ll agree that your clearance through customs was an interesting part of your trip.
