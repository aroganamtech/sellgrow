CHINA CLIPPER — One of the famous air line aircraft of all time, it was China Clipper which pioneered over ocean scheduled air transportation.
The Martin M-130, built by the Glenn L. Martin Company had a wing span of 130 feet; weight, 26 tons; speed, 150 miles per hour: range 3,200 miles. Maximum seating capacity was 41, but on long flights requiring maximum fuel load it actually carried ten to twenty.
There were three airplanes of this type, the others, in addition to China Clipper, were Hawaii Clipper and Philippine Clipper.
