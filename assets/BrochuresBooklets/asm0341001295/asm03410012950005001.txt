Aviation’s Dne World Introduction
Stay with us on the pages of this book and we’ll take you on a Flying Clipper* trip around the world.
You’ll meet Captains, First Officers, Navigators, Flight Engineers, Pursers and Stewardesses.
You’ll meet other Pan Am people whose work is performed on the ground, and there are twenty of them for every crew member aloft! You’ll discover why their work is necessary for efficient, dependable and comfortable service.
You’ll try the various types of service, varying in cost and in luxury: economy-class Clipper* Thrift Service, tourist-fare Rainbow, first class President and de luxe President Special.
You’ll see how reservations are made, how Clipper Cargo is handled, how each flight is planned with regard for weather and other considerations, and then how it is flown in accordance with the flight plan but with variations to meet changing conditions.
We’ll discuss some of the problems that have to be solved by the management of an air line, and by certain government agencies . . . landing rights, for example, and cabotage.
We’ll explore some of the history of air transportation, and we’ll examine the maintenance procedures which assure that the airplanes are always airworthy.
When our imaginary circum-air-navigation of the globe is completed, you’ll understand, I think, what Ave mean when we speak of “Aviation’s One World.”
George Gardner, Educational Director Pan American World Airways
*Clipper, Trade Mark Reg. U. S. Pat. Off.
January, 1960
