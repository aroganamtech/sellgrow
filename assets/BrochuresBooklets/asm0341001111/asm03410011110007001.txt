Pan American has helped "break the trail" into this new era in Latin America. With its associated airlines, it has succeeded in surmounting the natural barriers hiding the wealth of the countries. It has woven its schedules firmly into the economic life of the regions it serves.
It has opened up new, undeveloped areas, and has furnished a rapid, efficient means of communication between all key cities and the rich interiors.
By its air mail services alone, Pan American revolutionized Latin American trade methods, and speeded up all forms of commercial communication. It has brought these markets and raw material sources only a few hours away from our own trade centers and factories and has become, in effect, an "aerial ambassador” of American business.
When an ever-increasing number of merchant vessels were diverted from prewar inter-American commerce to feed our military life-lines, Pan American helped to fill the gap by adapting her Flying Clippers to cargo-carrying, and this will continue to be one of their vital functions in the postwar world.
Latin America is experiencing a renaissance—and Pan American World Airways extends to you its facilities that you may participate in the business opportunities afforded by this industrial awakening.
