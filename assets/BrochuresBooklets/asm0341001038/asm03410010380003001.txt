Or Swimming? Secluded, sandy beaches and hidden grottoes make the perfect setting for in-season surf swimming. Some hotels have their own pools.
Or Just Loafing? What’s to match the sheer delight of pure leisure in one place where people know the joy of unhurried living?
How About Bicycling? Cycling over miles of smooth, scenic roads, past oleander-covered fences, exploring hidden byways, picnicking under fragrant cedars, is exhilarating exercise.
Is it Tennis? Whether you’re ranking pro or rank amateur you’ll thrill to watching world-famous stars compete in the international tennis stadium .. .Then try a few fast sets yourself on numerous, perfect courts.
Or Perhaps Golf? If you’ve never golfed on rolling velvety green fairways overlooking blue seas, you’re in for a new thrill in Bermuda. Don’t miss the famous Mid Ocean Club course, one of the world’s best.
Maybe You Like Fishing? Drop your line in these purple-turquoise-emerald waters and hook yourself a tuna or wahoo. Or don goggles and swim trunks and try spearing your fish.
