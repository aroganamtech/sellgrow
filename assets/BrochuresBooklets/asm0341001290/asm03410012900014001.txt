Cruising range was 500 miles, carrying 21 passengers at a speed of 180 miles-an-hour. The DC-3 held 822 gallons of gasoline, and consumed 85 gallons an hour.
Pan Am had 53 DC-3’s flying in 1943, operated them on the overland routes.
First of the Douglas series to join the Clipper fleet was the DOUGLAS DC-3, the “workhorse” of scheduled air transportation. The Douglas Company estimates that more than 500-million passengers have flown in the DC-3, not counting the millions of servicemen carried during the war.
