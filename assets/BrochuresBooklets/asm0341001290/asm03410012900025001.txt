
DOUGLAS DC-7F
This Clipper Cargo Merchantman originally served as a passenger aircraft. When Jet Clippers took over the passenger flying job, the Super 7’s were converted to all-cargo carriers and redesignated from DC-7C to DC-7F (F for freighter). It has a non-stop capability of 3,000 miles with 32,000 pounds
of cargo. Now on order from Boeing are Pan American’s new turbofan Jet Cargo Clippers designated 707-320C. Designed to carry large-volume, transoceanic freight at Jet Age speeds, the 320C will carry over 40 tons of cargo more than 3,400 miles non-stop at a speed of 575 miles per hour.
