SIKORSKY S-38
In 1928 the S-38 opened air travel to Puerto Rico and Central and South America. The 10,480 pound S-38 was powered by two engines delivering 420 horsepower each. The S-38 was over 40 feet long, wingspan was 72 feet. It flew at 105 miles-an-hour. Also in
this year, Pan Am added radio operators and stewards, or flight cabin attendants to the flight crews. The next year, 1929, came the big jump for Pan American — from 251 route miles to 1 1,500.
