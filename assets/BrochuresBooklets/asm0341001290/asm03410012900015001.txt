LOCKHEED L-49 CONSTELLATION
Here is the LOCKHEED L-49 “Constellation”. Pan Am’s version seated 45 passengers; cruising speed was around 280 miles-an-hour, with a maximum cruising range of 4,000 miles. The “Connie” was powered by four Wright Cyclone 2,200-horsepower engines, consuming 350 gallons an hour. The first trans-Atlantic plane with a pressurized cabin, it could go as high as 20,800 feet. Its wing span was 123 feet, and length was 95 feet, 1 inch.
The “Connies”, with a crew of eight, scored a “first” when Pan Am used them in 1947 to fly aviation’s first scheduled round-the-world flight.
