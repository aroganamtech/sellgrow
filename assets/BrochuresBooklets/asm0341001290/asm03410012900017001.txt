DOUGLAS DC-6B
The DC-6B, or “SUPER-6”, is similar in size to the 6A, but has the regular passenger door and is fitted for passenger service. It helped Pan Am to extend world-wide tourist service. Its convertible cabin can be changed in a few hours from an 85 passenger tourist plane to a deluxe 44-passenger plane with a lounge and bar. Wing span is T17V2 feet, length 107V2 feet and gross weight is 107,000 pounds. Four Pratt & Whitney 2,500-horsepower engines give it a cruising speed of 320 miles-an-hour, an operating range of 4,400 miles, and a normal operating altitude of 16,000 to 22,000 feet.
DOUGLAS DC-6A
The DC-6A, all-cargo Clipper, has a larger capacity than any railroad freight car. Its huge loading doors, fore and aft, temperature controlled and pressurized cabin, permit this “warehouse in the sky” to airlift anything from earthmovers and elephants to orchids and baby chicks.
