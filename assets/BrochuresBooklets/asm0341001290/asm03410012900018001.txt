DOUGLAS DC-7B
The DOUGLAS DC-7B, capable of carrying 50 to 72 passengers, mail and baggage 4,000 miles non-stop, with 1,000 miles of fuel reserve as an extra safety margin, joined the Pan Am Clipper fleet in 1955. Notice the new Pan Am symbol and markings appearing in the 7B picture above. It is a clean modern design in blue and white with a simple
blue globe as its central symbol. For a comparison, see the illustration of the Super-7 with the old markings on the page opposite.
