
MARTIN M-130, THE CHINA CLIPPER
The CHINA CLIPPER ... the MARTIN M-130 . . . was the plane chosen to make the pioneering dream come true. The first scheduled air mail flight across the Pacific left California on November 22, 1935. After 59 hours and 48 minutes of flying time (six days’ elapsed time) she sat down in Manila Harbor!
Largest air transport of its time, the M-130 cruised 3,200 miles non-stop at 130 miles-
an-hour, and carried up to 32 passengers, depending on the payload of mail and freight. By present-day standards, the M-130 is small, but at the time, it was immense. It weighed 52,000 pounds, or 26 tons, had a wing span of 130 feet, stood 24 feet high, and was 90 feet, 7 inches long. It had four Pratt and Whitney 900-horsepower engines. There were 3 M-l 30’s — China Clipper, Hawaii Clipper and Philippine Clipper.
