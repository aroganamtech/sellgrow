JL allets to hold cargo, and dollies to move the cargo-laden pallets from the loading area to the Clipper Merchantman are the newest adjuncts to speedy loading of Clipper Cargo.
A pallet is a rugged plywood sheet 88 by 108 inches; a dolly is a platform with wheels. The pallet is placed on the dolly, the boxes and cartons are loaded on the pallet, and secured there by a cargo net. This can be done in factory, warehouse or loading area at the airport. Then, when the airplane is available for loading, an hour or so before departure time, the pallet is moved to the ramp, and lifted into the airplane (Left). At the wide door of the airplane, the pallet slides off the dolly, onto ball transfer plates which permit movement in any direction. (Upper left) Then it moves forward over gliding roller tracks. (Upper right) Having reached its position, it is locked into place with automatic clamps so it can not shift during the flight.
A DC-7F interior has room for seven pallets. The first five go through the main cargo door (see photo on opposite page) then are moved forward and locked into place. The sixth pallet enters through the same door, then moves to the rear of the airplane. The seventh is placed immediately inside the door. Altogether the load adds up to 32,000 pounds.
