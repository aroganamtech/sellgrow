SIKORSKY S-40
Here’s a plane that helped Pan Am stay at the head of the parade of progress. It's the SIKORSKY S-40, put into service in 1931 as the first commercial four-engine flying boat. It had 575-horsepower engines, cruised at 115 miles-an-hour, and carried 40 passengers. Its length was 76 feet, 8 inches. Wing span was 100 feet.
The performance of the S-40 and its sister ships greatly expanded the range of Pan American’s operations. In 1932, it established the world's record for the longest overwater route ... 600 miles... non-stop from Kingston, Jamaica to Barranquilla, Colombia. At the end of the year, Pan Am flew out of 29 land and 26 sea bases.
