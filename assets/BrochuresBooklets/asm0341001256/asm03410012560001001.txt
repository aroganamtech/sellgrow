Pan Am’s business travelers. In a class all by themselves.
When you reserve a seat on a Pan Am flight that offers Clipper® Class service, you’ll travel in a separate section of the aircraft reserved exclusively for Clipper Class passengers. Your seat will be wider than most business seats found in comparable service available elsewhere. And with no middle seats, you’ll never be more than one seat away from the aisle.
An exceptional selection of entrees is served in Clipper Class, as well as complimentary cocktails and a selection of fine wines.
Pan Am’s Clipper Class service is available on all Pan Am 747’s and most of our L-1011 ’s, both domestically and internationally.
Consult your Travel Agent, Corporate Travel Department or Pan Am, and ask for Clipper Class on your next business trip.
