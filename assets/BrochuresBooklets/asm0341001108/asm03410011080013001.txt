Between the Plaza de Armas and Plaza San Martin, you’ll find many shops along Jiron de la Union. Best buys are fluffy alpaca hats, slippers and stoles in their natural colors; and beautiful rugs and wall coverings.
10TH DAY. SUNDAY:
LIMA.
Day free.
20th-century Peru is an advanced country, independent and growing. Join
the stylish Lima residents at Unicorn for dinner or dine in the Hotel Crillon’s Sky Room. From the Incan gold collection at Monterrico’s Mujica Museum to the remains of Pizzaro in a glass coffin in Lima’s cathedral, to the sports cars and haute couture of today’s Peruvians ... truly this country is one not to be missed.
