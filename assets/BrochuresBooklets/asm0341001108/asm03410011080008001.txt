7TH DAY. THURSDAY: LIMA/BUENOS AIRES.
Transfer to airport for flight to “B.A.”. Transfer to hotel on arrival.
Remainder of day free.
Take a touch of the streets of Rome, the sophistication of Copenhagen, the intellectual fervor of Munich and the artistic flair of Florence .. . then put them into a bustling seaport in Argentina called Buenos Aires. Now you have the second largest city in the Western Hemisphere as well as the most important port after New York. The prevailing worldly outlook colors Buenos Aires’ cultural life. International opera is enjoyed at Teatro Colon, whose stage alone will hold 600 performers.
8TH DAY. FRIDAY:
BUENOS AIRES.
Morning sightseeing tour. Afternoon free.
Your tour will take you to all the main points of interest in this cosmopolitan, bustling, elegant city. With more than eight million “Porteños” living in B.A., it is the largest city in the Southern Hemisphere. You will see the Government House, Colon Theatre, Recoleta Cemetery and 9th of July Avenue.
9TH DAY. SATURDAY:
BUENOS AIRES.
Day at leisure.
If you’re a shopper, try Calle Florida. This is one of the world’s smartest shopping streets. Everything made of leather is an excellent buy for quality. Another good buy is art work.
10TH DAY. SUNDAY:
BUENOS AIRES.
Day free.
There are several interesting excursions you can take on your own and your host is ready to help. An all day tour to watch the gauchos bring cattle to market is a fascinating experience. The gauchos in their colorful dress are really doing a day’s work and not acting for the tourist.
11TH DAY. MONDAY:
BUENOS AIRES/RIO DE JANEIRO. Transfer to the airport for jet flight to Rio. Transfer to hotel on arrival. Remainder of day free.
No city is blessed with more natural beauty. At dusk, the last sun rays hit the crown of Sugarloaf, and, in a breathtaking finale, the brilliant sky fades to the color of the purple sea. Lights begin to come on all over Rio, and if you are atop the Corcovado Mountain gazing out over the city, yet another stunning sight of the city awaits you.
12TH DAY. TUESDAY:
RIO DE JANEIRO.
Morning sightseeing tour. Remainder of day free.
You’ll ascend to Corcovado Mountain, location of Rio’s landmark, the 125 foot statue of Christ the Redeemer. After enjoying the exceptional view you’ll proceed through Tijuca Rain Forest to Taunay Waterfall. Your return to Copacabana will be via the scenic route along Niemeyer palisades.
In the afternoon take a look at Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, and you’ll see why the Cariocas (Rio’s people) love to play. These sunny, inviting beaches are full of life. Volley-ball nets spring up. Beach chairs are carefully arranged in the sand to accommodate the lithe brown —steadily browner—bodies along the beach, and the surf receives an onslaught of running, laughing swimmers. A low but pervasive rhythm hums along the beach: the samba! Its sound gradually fills the air . . . and soon someone begins to sing. The samba beat is everywhere in Rio.
13TH DAY. WEDNESDAY:
RIO DE JANEIRO.
Full day free.
Looking for things to do? Your host can arrange a tour to Petropolis, a popular resort with people from Rio. Located at 2,800 feet above sea level. Petropolis has been the summer “escape” from the heat of Rio for the upper classes. The scenery on the ride to Petropolis as well as the old colonial buildings make it a worthwhile trip. Or, you can arrange an excursion to Paqueta Island. Situated in Guanabara Bay, the so-called Honeymoon Island is a picturesque and quiet escape from the world. You can walk, rent a bicycle or a horse drawn carriage.
14TH DAY. THURSDAY:
RIO DE JANEIRO.
Free day.
If shopping is your sport, then Rio is the place for you. From Avenida Copacabana with its smart boutiques to Rua do Ouvidor where semi-precious stones are a bargain, you’ll find good buys in clothes, jewelry and souvenirs. Rio sets the styles in beachwear . . . get your bikinis here!
