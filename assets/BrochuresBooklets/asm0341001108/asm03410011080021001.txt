9TH DAY. SATURDAY: PEULLA/BARILOCHE Continue by motorcoach across the Argentine border to Puerto Pañuelo and on by boat to Bariloche. Lunch en route and dinner at the hotel.
10TH DAY. SUNDAY: BARILOCHE.
Morning sightseeing tour. Remainder of the day at leisure. Lunch and dinner at the hotel.
The morning tour will show you some of the most beautiful scenery Argentina has to offer. See the surrounding area of Bariloche: Liao Liao, Swiss Village, Bahia Lopen and Punto Panorámico.
11TH DAY. MONDAY: BARILOCHE/BUENOS AIRES. Transfer to the airport for your flight to Buenos Aires. Transfer on arrival to your hotel. Remainder of day at leisure.
Buenos Aires is the second largest city in the Western Hemisphere as well as the most important port after New York. But “B.A.” is not just a big city. It’s a city of beautiful parks, a pink Presidential Palace,
the widest avenue in the world, and a fascinating dockside area called La Boca where all the houses are painted different colors.
12TH DAY. TUESDAY:
BUENOS AIRES.
Morning sightseeing tour. Remainder of the day free.
Your tour will take you to all the main points of interest in this cosmopolitan, bustling, elegant city. With more than eight million “Porteños” living in B.A., it is the largest city in the Southern Hemisphere. You will see the Government House, Colon Theatre, Recoleta Cemetery and 9th of July Avenue.
13TH DAY. WEDNESDAY:
BUENOS AIRES.
Day at leisure. Dinner at a local restaurant.
If you’re a shopper try Calle Florida. This is one of the world’s smartest shopping streets. Everything made of leather is an excellent buy for quality. Another good buy is artwork.
