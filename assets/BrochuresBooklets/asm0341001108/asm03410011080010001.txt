No city is blessed with more natural beauty. At dusk, the last sun rays hit the crown of Sugarloaf, and, in a breathtaking finale, the brilliant sky fades to the color of the purple sea. Lights begin to come on all over Rio, and if you are atop the Corcovado Mountain gazing out over the city, yet another stunning sight of the city awaits you.

RIO DE JANEIRO.

You’ll ascend to Corcovado Mountain, location of Rio’s landmark, the 125 foot statue of Christ the Redeemer. After enjoying the exceptional view you’H proceed through Tijuca Rain Forest to Taunay Waterfall. Your return to Copacabana will be via the scenic route along Niemeyer palisades.
5TH DAY. TUESDAY:
RIO DE JANEIRO.
Full day free.
If shopping is your sport, then Rio is the place for you. From Avenida Copacabana with its smart boutiques to Rua do Ouvidor where semi-precious stones are a bargain, you’ll find good buys in clothes, jewelry and souvenirs. Rio sets the styles in beachwear ... get your bikinis here!
6TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: RIO/IGUASSU FALLS.
Transfer to the airport for morning flight to Iguassu Falls. On arrival transfer to your hotel. Remainder of the day at leisure.
If you’ve seen Niagara Falls, then be prepared for a surprise, since it doesn’t come close to Iguassu Falls! Iguassu Falls is actually a series of falls some two and a half miles long set in the lush jungle vegetation on the border of Argentina and Brazil. Spectacular is the only word for this natural wonder.
7TH DAY. THURSDAY: IGUASSU FALLS/BUENOS AIRES.
Transfer by motorcoach to the airport for your flight to Buenos Aires. On arrival you will be met and transferred to your hotel. Remainder of the day is free.
Buenos Aires is the second largest city in the Western Hemisphere as well as the most important port after New York.
