14TH DAY. THURSDAY:
BUENOS AIRES/IGUASSU FALLS. Transfer to airport for flight to Iguassu Falls. On arrival transfer to hotel. Remainder of the day at leisure.
If you’ve seen Niagara Falls, then be prepared for a surprise, since it doesn’t come close to Iguassu Falls! Iguassu Falls is actually a series of falls some two and a half miles long set in the lush jungle vegetation on the border of Argentina and Brazil. Spectacular is the only word for this natural wonder.
15TH DAY. FRIDAY:
IGUASSU FALLS/SAO PAULO. Transfer to the airport for your flight to Sao Paulo. Assistance on arrival and transfer to your hotel. Balance of the day at leisure.
17TH DAY. SUNDAY:
SALVADOR.
Day at leisure.
This is a good time to get acquainted with this charming city. Salvador, the capital of the State of Bahia was the original capital of Brazil. The city is actually built on two levels, each distinctly different. The two levels are connected by an elevator.
While exploring the city you’ll want to try some of the spicy Afro-Portuguese food which is served in Bahia. You’ll find it very different from the food served in other countries in South America.
18TH DAY. MONDAY: SALVADOR.
Morning sightseeing tour. Remainder of the day at leisure.
Your tour will show you the Museum of Sacred Art, the shopping center of 7th of September Avenue and Castro Alves Square. In Terreiro de Jesus, one of the oldest parts of the city, you’ll stop for a look at the cathedral and the St. Francis Church built in 1597. Then on to the Pelourinho, the oldest square in the city.
19TH DAY. TUESDAY: BAHIA/RIO DE JANEIRO.
Transfer to the airport for your flight to Rio. Remainder of the day free.
No city is blessed with more natural beauty. At dusk, the last sun rays hit the crown of Sugarloaf, and in a breath-taking finale, the brilliant sky fades to the color of the purple sea.
20TH DAY. WEDNESDAY:
RIO DE JANEIRO.
Morning sightseeing tour. Remainder of day at leisure.
You’ll ascend to Corcovado Mountain, location of Rio’s landmark, the 125 foot statue of Christ the Redeemer. After enjoying the exceptional view you’ll proceed through Tijuca rain forest to Taunay Waterfall. Your return to Copacabana will be via the scenic route along Niemeyer palisades.
16TH DAY. SATURDAY:
SAO PAULO/SALVADOR (BAHIA). Morning sightseeing tour before transferring to the airport for your flight to Salvador, Bahia. Remainder of the day free.
On your tour of Sao Paulo, the fastest growing city in the world, you’ll see the shopping and financial section, the residential area, soccer stadium, Jockey Club and the Butantan Snake Institute and its museum.
21ST DAY. THURSDAY:
RIO DE JANEIRO.
Day at leisure. Farewell dinner at local restaurant.
One last day to work on that tan or make up your mind about a ring or bracelet for a souvenir.
22ND DAY. FRIDAY:
RIO DE JANEIRO/USA.
Transfer to the airport for your flight home.

USA.
