Combination
8 days from *259 plus air fare
Caracas 3 Nights Macuto 4 Nights 1ST DAY. WED/SAT: USA/CARACAS.
Depart by Pan Am Jet Clipper for Caracas. Assistance on arrival and transfer to your hotel.
2ND DAY. THU/SUN: CARACAS.
Morning sightseeing tour. Remainder of day at leisure.
See the Old City of Caracas, with the Casa Natal (Bolivar’s Birthplace), the beautiful Santa Capilla (Holy Chapel) with its ornate altar, and the National Pantheon where Bolivar is buried. The Plaza Bolivar is the center of the Old City. See San Francisco, the city’s most interesting church.
3RD DAY. FRI/MON: CARACAS.
Full day at leisure.
See the city from the 30th floor of Centro Bolivar’s North Tower. Take the teleférico to Mt. Avila’s summit. Shop in the sophisticated Centro Commercial Chacaito, and on the Isle of Margarita; pearls, handicrafts, gold jewelry.
4TH DAY. SAT/TUE: CARACAS/MACUTO.
Transfer to one of the world’s most beautiful resorts.
Time left in the day for golf or swimming.
5TH THROUGH 7TH DAYS. MACUTO.
Three full days at leisure.
Yours to enjoy as you please. Swim, deep-sea fish, water-ski, snorkel, sail and sun to your heart’s content. And the nightspots are as bright as the sun.
8TH DAY. WED/SAT: MACUTO/USA.
Transfer to the airport for Pan Am Jet Clipper flight home.
Pleasure Break
8 days from *285 plus air fare
Margarita Island 3 Nights Caracas 4 Nights
1ST DAY. WED/SAT: USA/MARGARITA ISLAND.
Depart by Pan Am Jet Clipper for Caracas. That same afternoon connect to your flight to Margarita. Assistance on arrival, transfer to hotel.
2ND AND 3RD DAYS. MARGARITA ISLAND.
Two free days.
Margarita — the Island of Pearls is everyone’s dream of the perfect get-away-from-it atmosphere. Hire a launch, sail through quiet lagoons and bays ... lie in the sun, dive into emerald waters.
4TH DAY. SAT/TUE: MARGARITA / C AR AC AS.
Transfer to airport for flight to Caracas. On arrival transfer to hotel.
5TH DAY. SUN/WED: CARACAS.
Morning sightseeing tour. Remainder of day free.
See the gleaming white Capitol with its gold dome and flower-filled patio; Casa Natal, where Simon Bolivar was born; Colonial Art Museum at Quinta de Anauco; National Pantheon; and the magnificent gardens of the Grand Officer’s Club.
6TH AND 7TH DAYS. CARACAS.
Two full days at leisure.
Take the teleférico to Mt. Avila’s summit. Shop in the sophisticated Centro Commercial Chacaito, or look for a bargain in cochano gold in the shops near Plaza Bolivar.
8TH DAY. WED/SAT: CARACAS/USA.
Transfer to the airport for Pan Am Jet Clipper flight home.
