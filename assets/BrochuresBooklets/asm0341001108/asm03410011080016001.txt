You’ll see the gleaming white Capitol with its gold dome and flower-filled patio; Casa Natal, where Simon Bolivar was born; the Colonial Art Museum at the Quinta de Anauco, a beautiful old Spanish estate; the National Pantheon; and the magnificent gardens of the Grand Officer’s Club.

Five full days to explore the City of Eternal Spring on your own.
Take off to the beaches of Macuto, Naiguata and Catia La Mar. Take a look at the replica of Columbus’s ship, the Santa Maria, at Parque del Este. Zip up to the top of Mt. Avila in a teleférico for the best view of the mountains and the sea. And be sure to shop for jewelry made of cochano gold along Sabana Grande, one of the main shopping streets.

1ST DAY. WED/SAT: USA/CARACAS/MACUTO.
Board your Pan Am Jet Clipper for Caracas. Later that same afternoon, you’ll be met at Caracas airport and transferred to Macuto.
2ND DAY. THU/SUN: MACUTO.
Morning sightseeing tour of Caracas. Remainder of the day free.
See the gleaming white Capitol with its gold dome and flower filled patio; Casa Natal, where Simon Bolivar was born; the Colonial Art Museum at the Quinta de Anauco, a beautiful old Spanish estate; the National Pantheon; and the magnificent gardens of the Grand Officer’s Club.
3RD THROUGH 7TH DAYS. MACUTO.
Free time.
Five sun-filled days in idyllic Macuto to do everything—or absolutely nothing! There are beaches, pools, a marina, an 18-hole championship golf course, tennis courts and a bowling alley. Go snorkeling in the crystal-clear waters, or go deep-sea fishing.
For a change of pace, hop the cable car that shoots up 6,925 feet to the top of Mt. Avila, then drops down to Caracas on the other side. Spend the day browsing through the elegant shops in the Chacaito Shopping Center— Charles Jourdan, Yves St. Laurent’s Rive Gauche and Le Drugstore—just to name a few.
8TH DAY. WED/SAT: MACUTO/USA.
Transfer to airport for Pan Am Jet Clipper flight home.
