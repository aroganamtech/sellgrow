8TH DAY. FRIDAY:
BUENOS AIRES.
Morning sightseeing tour. Remainder of the day at leisure.
Your tour will take you to all the main points of interest in this cosmopolitan, bustling, elegant city. With more than eight million “Porteños” living in B.A., it is the largest city in the Southern Hemisphere. You will see the Government House, Colon Theatre, Recoleta Cemetery and 9th of July Avenue.
9TH DAY. SATURDAY:
BUENOS AIRES.
Day at leisure.
If you’re a shopper try Calle Florida. This is one of the world’s smartest shopping streets. Everything made of leather is an excellent buy for quality. Another good buy is art work.
10TH DAY. SUNDAY:
BUENOS AIRES.
Day free.
There are several interesting excursions you can take on your own and your host is ready to help. An all day tour to watch the gauchos bring cattle to market is a fascinating experience.
11TH DAY. MONDAY:
BUENOS AIRES/LIMA.
Transfer to airport for jet flight to Lima. Transfer to hotel on arrival.
Lima, City of Kings, founded in 1535 by Francisco Pizarro, was once a colonial capital of the Spanish Empire. The historical significance is apparent but Lima is now a modern 20th-century city.
12TH DAY. TUESDAY:
LIMA.
Morning sightseeing tour. Remainder of day free.
You’ll visit both colonial Lima and the city’s modern residential suburbs. Your first visit will be to the Cathedral where you can see the remains of the conqueror Pizarro, then on to the Court of the Inquisition and the Colonial Palace of Torre Tagle. After seeing colonial Lima your drive takes you through residential districts to the Olive Grove and the country club of San Isidro.
13TH DAY. WEDNESDAY: LIMA/CUZCO.
Transfer to the airport for early morning flight to Cuzco. Transfer on arrival to hotel. Afternoon sightseeing tour of Cuzco.
Capital of the Incan Empire, Cuzco is located at 11,400 feet. Most visitors like to rest a short time to become adjusted to the altitude and avoid any discomfort.
14TH DAY. THURSDAY:
CUZCO.
Full day excursion to Machu Picchu including lunch.
Machu Picchu, one of the wonders of the world and if you don’t believe it, wait till you get there! Discovered in 1911 by Hiram Bingham, Machu Picchu consists of over 200 buildings, a self-contained city, virtually inaccessible and unnotice-able from the valley below.
15TH DAY. FRIDAY: CUZCO/LIMA.
Transfer to the airport for an early morning flight to Lima. Transfer to your hotel on arrival. Remainder of the day free.
Between the Plaza de Armas and Plaza San Martin, you’ll find many shops along Jiron de la Union. Best buys are fluffy alpaca hats, slippers, and stoles in their natural colors; and beautiful rugs and wall coverings in brilliant colors.
16TH DAY. SATURDAY: LIMA/USA.
Transfer to the airport for your flight home.
