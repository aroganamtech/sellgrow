2ND DAY. THURSDAY:
RIO DE JANEIRO.
Assistance on arrival. Transfer to your hotel. Remainder of day free.
Deus é Brasileiro! goes an old Brazilian saying: “God is a Brazilian.” If this is true (ignoring theological arguments for the moment), then heaven has got to be in Rio. No city is blessed with more natural beauty. And nowhere do people enjoy their blessings as much as in Rio!
At dusk, the last sun rays hit the crown of Sugarloaf and in a breathtaking finale, the brilliant sky fades to the color of the purple sea. Lights begin to come on all over Rio and if you are atop Corcovado Mountain gazing out over the city, yet another stunning sight awaits you.
3RD DAY. FRIDAY:
RIO DE JANEIRO.
Morning sightseeing tour. Remainder of day at leisure.
You’ll ascend to Corcovado Mountain, location of Rio’s landmark, the 125 foot statue of Christ the Redeemer. After enjoying the exceptional view you’ll proceed through Tijuca Rain Forest to Taunay Waterfall. Your return to Copa-cabana will be via the scenic route along Niemeyer palisades.
In the afternoon take a look at Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, and
you’ll see why the Cariocas (Rio’s people) love to play. These sunny, inviting beaches are full of life. Volley ball nets spring up—beach chairs are carefully arranged in the sand to accommodate the lithe brown—steadily browner—bodies along the beach, and the surf receives an onslaught of running, laughing swimmers. A low but pervasive rhythm hums along the beach: the samba! Its sound gradually fills the air—and soon someone begins to sing. The samba beat is everywhere in Rio.
