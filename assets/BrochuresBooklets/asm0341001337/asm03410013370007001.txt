PUBLICITY help you?
A publicity story or picture can’t make a sale by itself, but a successful publicity campaign that precedes and accompanies your sales campaign can open doors for you . . . make sales easier. You don’t hit a corporation executive completely cold ... if he has read of another executive flying PAN AM. Your neighbor does not feel that international travel is beyond him ... if he reads of friends traveling. A nervous Nellie is not as nervous ... if she sees a picture of a child starting on a trip. The octogenarian won’t feel air travel is just for the young and adventurous ... if he has read of an elderly person’s trip.
Similarly, a cargo shipper won’t Hesitate to air ship anything, large or small, if he knows others are doing it.
BUT . . . neither the executive nor the laborer, the nervous Nellie or the octogenarian will know Clipper travel is for them, nor will the cargo shipper ship by Clipper Cargo IF none of them knows others have tried PAN AM. And if no one tells the newspapers, the magazines, radio, television or newsreels, then nothing about PAN AM will be printed or put on film or broadcast . . . and PAN AM’s competitors will fly the passengers and cargo.
