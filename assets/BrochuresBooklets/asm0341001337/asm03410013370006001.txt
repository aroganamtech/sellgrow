What do you do
with the NEWS?
You pass it on to the one department set up by PAN AM to handle the news tips you acquire — Public Relations. Pubrel’s business is to know what newsmen want. It is our obligation to listen to your story, to evaluate and act upon what you have told us. Whether the tip is good enough for newspapers to splash on front page headlines throughout the country, or its importance merits only a mention on an inside page, or no mention at all, the chain must be linked — tip to Pubrel, Pubrel to newsman.
How you tell Pubrel is not important as long as you tell them while the news is still news. Pass it on over the telephone, by wire or through the mail using a memo, or simply a scrap of paper.
What does Pubrel do with the NEWS?
Pubrel converts the news tip into a PAN AM publicity item and angles it for newspapers, magazines, radio, television, and newsreels.
