What is NEWS?
It’s information about a passenger who is very young or very old, very very rich or very poor ... a passenger rejoining his family after a long absence . . . one who is taking part in an international conference . . . who is being married in a foreign land. It’s every celebrity from a movie star to a murderer, from a potentate to a pushcart dealer, from a student to a sword swallower.
It’s information, too, about a cargo shipment . . . the biggest shipment ever made or the smallest one ... the most expensive one or the most inexpensive . . . it’s a machine that will put a factory back into operation; it’s a drug that will save a life ... It’s a lipstick for a princess, a birthday cake for a GI. It’s potentially any animal that walks, hops or crawls. It’s anything going to anyone who is famous or infamous.
