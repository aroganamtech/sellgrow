Salvador ••• Salvador (formerly Bahia) is the royal capital port and the fourth largest city in Brazil. Here the new Hotel Bahia is the best. The market, native costumes, colonial buildings, fabulous beaches, rainbow-tinted houses make it well worth a visit.
Ouro Preto • • ■ The “Williamsburg” of Brazil is Ouro Preto, preserved as a national monument. Houses of hand-made tile, carved doorways, patios and ornate churches make it utterly fascinating. The Church of Saint Francis of Assisi is one of the most gracious of all, and owes its plan and the sculpture of its facade to the famous Aleijadinho. Ouro Preto has also many ancient fountains: those of Palace of Justice and the prison date only from 1846. Another near Casa dos Contos is a fine piece of baroque art and was finished in 1745.
