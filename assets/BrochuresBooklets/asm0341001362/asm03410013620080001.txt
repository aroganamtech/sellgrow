Commercial IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS IN Holidays PLANNING YOUR BUSINESS Abroad ITINERARY
To the international businessman on a tight schedule, few things can be more exasperating than to arrive in a city on a weekend to discover that all offices are closed for Culture Day (Nov. 3 in Japan), for Heroes Day (Mar. 1 in Paraguay), or Midsummer Day (June 20 in Finland and Sweden). The fact is, of the 366 days in 1972, some 300 of them are commercial holidays in one country or another. And to compound the difficulty, many cities and provinces within a country observe local and regional holidays.
To assist you in planning your overseas business trip, on the following pages are the principal holidays observed by 101 countries. You will note certain holidays are starred. This indicates that the dates change from year to year or, in some cases, they are holidays which are not scheduled a year in advance and did not appear on official calendars at the time this directory went to press.
You can avoid much inconvenience by referring to these pages before you plan your trip. Better still, double-check with Pan Am. And best of all, it’s always advisable to make your appointments in advance. You may save yourself many hours of frustration sitting around hotel lobbies waiting for banks and offices to reopen.
