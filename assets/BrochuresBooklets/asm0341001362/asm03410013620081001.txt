FINLAND
Jan. 1 (New Year’s), Jan. 6 (Epiphany), ‘Mar. 31 (Good Friday), ‘Apr. 3 (Easter Monday), May 1 (May Day), ‘May 11 (Ascension), ‘May 22 (Whit Monday), ‘June 20 (Midsummer’s Day), ‘Nov. 1 (All Saints), Dec. 6 (independence Day), Dec. 25-26 (Christmas). Banks are closed all day Dec. 24.
FRANCE
Jan. 1 (New Year’s), ‘Apr. 3 (Easter Monday), May 1 (Labor Day), ‘May 11 (Ascension), ‘May 22 (Whit or Pentecost Monday), July 14 (Bastille Day), Aug. 15 (Assumption), Nov. 1 (Aii Saints), Nov. 11 (Armistice Day), Dec. 25 (Christmas).
August is regarded in France as a poor month for conducting business, as most business firms are closed for vacation.
