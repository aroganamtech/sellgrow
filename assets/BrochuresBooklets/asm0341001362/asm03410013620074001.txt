Exhibits related Made-in-USA products are displayed for one or two weeks, then replaced by another show of different U.S.-made goods. The eighth U.S. Trade Center, which opens in 1972 in Mexico City, is the newest in a chain of permanent commercial showrooms operating in Frankfurt, London, Milan, Paris, Stockholm, Sydney and Tokyo ... all staffed with American officials who help find agents, distributors and buyers for products displayed. In addition, the Department of Commerce operates U.S. Regional Trade Development Centers in Bangkok and in Buenos Aires (to open in May, 1972).
Exhibitors receive
. An in-depth market study of the opportunities for their products before the showing.
•	A strong promotional campaign to bring in potential buyers.
. Experienced, professional designing assistance for their displays.
•	Reception services, interpreters, conference facilities.
Visitors have the opportunity to -
•	See quality goods in related product fields displayed in an established, conveniently-located facility.
•	Meet with qualified U.S. business executives to discuss recent developments, new technology, new product lines, prices and deliveries.
•	Bridge language barriers, dealing directly with decision-making executives through experienced interpreters.
This listing covers those U.S. Trade Center Exhibits scheduled by the Bureau of International Commerce at the time this Directory went to press. For additional information on Trade Center shows, write Director, Bureau of International Commerce, U.S. Department of Commerce, Washington, D.C. 20230.
