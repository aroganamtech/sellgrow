Something Missing?
Is there an international trade fair, meeting or convention - perhaps one in which you have a professional or business interest - that has not been included in this year’s Directory? With the thousands of meetings and exhibits held each year, it’s inevitable we’ll miss a few.
If you know of a listing that should be in this directory, we’ll be happy to make amends in the 1973 edition.
On the other side of this page, please list the full name of the fair or meeting, the dates, the location, whether it is annual, semi-annual, biennial, etc., and the name and address of the individual or organization to be contacted for additional information. In order to be included in the 1973 Directory, all entries must be received not later than July 15, 1972.
Thanks for your help.
