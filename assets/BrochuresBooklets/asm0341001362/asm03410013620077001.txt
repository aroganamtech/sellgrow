TRADE FAIR TIPS
Twenty Pointers to Make Your Trade Fair Exhibit More Productive
Exhibiting and selling abroad take the same advance planning, market research, attention to detail and hard work that result in successful show-and-sell events at home.
Before you decide to exhibit overseas, consider these valuable tips from experienced trade promotion specialists and seasoned exhibitors:
□	Decide your business objectives before you enter the show.
Why do you want to exhibit? Are you looking for on-the-spot sales or new customers? Do you want to introduce a new product or service? Do you want to find agents or distributors? Are you interested in joint venture or licensing arrangements? Perhaps you want to exhibit in order to gain exposure or study the market.
□	Select a market with care.
Study the market before you enter an overseas trade show. You will find Pan Am’s Marketing Guide series (see page 92) helpful in this respect. Pan Am’s Worldwide Marketing Service is a valuable source of free advice and counsel. Clipper Cargo specialists in 124 international marketing centers can provide you with on-the-spot information that is accurate and up to the minute. Trade associations, chambers of commerce and various government trade-development agencies also can assist you.
□	Exhibit your newest products.
Foreign customers want to see and buy your latest and best products. Don’t show outdated merchandise.
□	Prepare ahead.
Give yourself plenty of time - up to 12 months - to prepare for an overseas trade fair. Remember, an outstanding exhibit cannot be put together overnight. You’ll want ample time too, to contact prospective sales representatives and customers - through direct mail, advertising and personal calls - to invite them to visit your booth.
