The Pan Am Ground It’s a matter of coordination. Increased Handling-to-Air System speed and efficiency in airbound cargo of Coordination deliveries requires greater efficiency on the ground - where it all begins (and ends). So, Pan Am air cargo terminals are getting the full treatment. Modernization. Enlargement. And automation.
Jet-age ground-handling facilities are already working (or soon will be) in New York, Boston, Detroit, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Washington, Chicago and Philadelphia. The same advanced equipment has updated terminals overseas such as Buenos Aires, Frankfurt, Brasilia, Tokyo, Rome, Johannesburg ... as well as Hong Kong, Berlin, New Delhi, Munich, Caracas and Honolulu. In all, there are modernized terminals in more than thirty major marketing centers throughout Pan Am’s six-continent cargo network.
And the Pan Am system of coordination goes a step further. Our own crews expedite the unloading and transferring of your cargo ... in virtually all 124 cities served by Pan Am Cargo. These specially trained Pan Am cargo handlers work fulltime to ensure that shipments move quickly and safely to their destinations.
