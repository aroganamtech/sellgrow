Total Cost Adds Up To Total Profit When You Go To Market By Air
There’s no question that distribution by air can broaden your selling base by making distant markets easily accessible . . . can give you a competitive edge by providing your customers with fast, safe, dependable service.
Yet with all its advantages, AirMarket-ing frequently proves to be less expensive than slower surface transport.
Consider total costs, not just air rates alone. Shipping costs are only a fraction of the real cost of overseas market penetration. AirMarketing eliminates many substantial “hidden” costs which can far exceed the cost of transportation itself.
By Pan Am Cargo your packing and unpacking costs - for labor as well as materials - are considerably lower. Loss and damage claims, and consequently insurance rates, are also far less.
Frozen capital - investment tied up in goods in transit - can be many times less for overnight air deliveries compared to several weeks by sea. Distribution by air can substantially reduce the need for warehousing and inventory and provide greater flexibility in the bargain.
Add to these savings, the elimination or reduction of transfer expenses, wharfage charges, onward charges from seaport to inland destination, and the expenses of complex documentation required for surface shipments.
Many of the costs you may have considered “normal” cease to exist when your products take to the air . . . via fast, safe, economical Pan Am Clipper® Cargo.
