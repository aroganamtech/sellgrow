A NEW CONVENIENCE FOR AIR TRAVELERS
Both of them always "on the go”, First Lady Eleanor Roosevelt and PAA,s Vice President and General Traffic Manager, E. Chenea have a common interest—travel. Here, at the White House, Mrs. Roosevelt receives the first book of Clipper Air Cheques issued.
War brings in its wake many complications for those who have to travel. Among these is the difficulty of carrying money from one country to another.
The answer, it seemed to Pan American, was to develop a new kind of "flying money”. . . And Clipper Air Cheques, created in cooperation with the American Express Co., came into being in March, 1944. The small denominations of the Cheques make it unnecessary, as a rule, to carry the currency of one country into another . . . J hey can be purchased and cashed throughout the world—at any sales office of Pan American World Airways, the American Express Company or various authorized Pan American agents.
Only a single signature, on the book cover, is required at time of purchase—and only a single signature, at time of cashing, on each Cheque.
