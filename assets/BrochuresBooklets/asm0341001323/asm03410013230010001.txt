GNAG - CHINA S LIFELINE TO THE OUTSIDE WORLD
The route which connects China with India "over the hump"—the route which has recently been China’s only lifeline to the outside world—was pioneered before Pearl Harbor by CNAC (China National Aviation Corporation). CNAC is owned jointly by Pan American Airways, Inc. and the Chinese Government. Later, when military transports became available, the Air Transport Command assumed the major portion of the task.
Deliberately courting bad weather to avoid Jap planes, CNAC planes have carried, to and from India, Generalissimo and Madame Chiang Kai-shek and many other important officials . . . After General James Doolittle had bombed Tokyo in 1942, CNAC pilots carried him out of China on his way back to Washington.
