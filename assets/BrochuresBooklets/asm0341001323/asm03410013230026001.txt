Navy Department
OFFICE OF THE CHIEF OF NAVAL OPERATIONS Washington 25, X). C.
1 June 1944
To the Employees of Pan American Airways System:-
You who are flying Naval Air Transport planes in many parts of the world, and those of you who remain at bases helping to keep the Navy's far-flung air transport system in operation, are making a vital contribution to final victory in Europe, and particularly in the Pacific. This is a war that involves serious supply problems, and the service you men and women are rendering the nation in maintaining and operating world-wide air supply lines is of the greatest importance to the men at the fighting front.
Please accept my congratulations on a
job well done.
Rear Admiral, U. S. Navy Assistant Deputy Chief of Naval Operations (Air)
WAR DEPARTMENT
HEADQUARTERS OF THE ARMY AIR FORCES WASHINGTON
May 19, 1944
To The Employees of Pan-American Airways:
This month flight operations of the Army Air Forces Air Transport Command will reach the remarkable total of over 450,000,000 miles flown since its organization as the Ferrying Command 29 May, 1941. This is truly an extraordinary accomplishment when one considers the speed required in the organization of the operations and the extent of the aerial life lines required by world-wide hostilities. This accomplishment would never have been possible had it not been for great contributions made by many individuals and organizations, including the American civil airlines. You of the Pan-American organizations are giving services of great importance to the operations of our men and women in uniform. May you continue to tackle our mutual problems from day to day with all the skill and vigor at your command and so to hasten the day of victory.
Sincerely yours
