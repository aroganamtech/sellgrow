PASSENGER SERVICE
ALL SET” FOR POST-WAR BUSINESS
For years Pan American has employed women as Passenger Service Representatives at airports and as workers in shop and office . . . But over-ocean hops are long—long in hours, as well as in miles.
Only recently, therefore, on shorter flights out of Miami, has Pan American begun to employ stewardesses.
In passenger service in general, Pan American has pioneered such innovations as Airport Passenger Service (including interpreters), meals aloft, sliding scale baggage allowances, credit transportation contracts and ground transportation from airport to city. Many more innovations for postwar passenger convenience are now being planned and key PAA personnel is already being trained.
