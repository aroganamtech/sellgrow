1935
First Across A Major Ocean. Pan American inaugurated trans-Pacific air service, the first air transport service over a major ocean route; introduced long-range weather forecasting for aviation.
1936
Close To Radar. Pan American first experimented with use of ultra high frequency radio; instituted flight fatigue medical research program.
1937
Atlantic Surveys. Pan American completed trans-Atlantic survey flights in preparation for regular air service to Europe; commercial service to Bermuda was organized.
1938
Alaska. Pan American inaugurated scheduled service between Juneau and Fairbanks, Alaska, via Yukon Territory; completed survey of route between United States and Alaska.

First Across The Atlantic. The first U. S. trans-Atlantic service for passengers and mail was inaugurated; aircraft with engines accessible for repair in flight were first utilized.

1940
4-Engined, High Altitude Land Planes. The first successful American 4-engined land transports were put into service; these aircraft first utilized pressurized cabins for high altitude over-weather service.
ALSO 1940
Australasia. Pan American inaugurated air transport service from the U. S. to Australasia, via Canton Island and New Caledonia.
1941
South Atlantic. Pan American Airways System organized a war-time air transport service over the South Atlantic between South America and Europe.
New International Air Gateway. Pan American established international transport service from New Orleans, linking the Mississippi Valley with Latin American countries.
Africa. A commercial air service between the United States and Africa, serving Liberia and the Belgian Congo, was inaugurated by Pan American.
