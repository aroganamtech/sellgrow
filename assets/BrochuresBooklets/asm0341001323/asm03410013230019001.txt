USO HEADLINERS —
ON THEIR WAY TO THE FRONT
Laughs go overseas. (right to left) Clipper passengers Boh Hope, Frances Langford, Wendell Niles, Vera Vague, Jerry Colonna and Tony Romano.
Bound for the fighting front. (Left to right) actress Terry Rio, dancer Marilyn Hale, quizzical and cute Helen Twelvetrees, actor George Church and actor
Pat Moran. ^
The wartime passenger lists of the Clippers read like a "Who’s Who in stardom”. When show business wants to carry a bit of in-person American fun and frolic to our men overseas, they’re likely to Clipper their headliners across.
These actors and actresses are not only helping morale at the front—which is the important thing today—they are also foreshadowing the day when whole shows (including scenery) will be able to close one night in London or San Francisco and open the next in New York or Hawaii.
London will be 10 hours from New York, then, and far-away Chungking, China only 24.
Buenos Aires—two thousand miles farther from NewYork than London—will become only21 hours away. New, giant Clippers will carry passengers "above the weather” in stratosphere comfort.
