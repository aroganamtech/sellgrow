Besides its own operations to, and in, Latin America, Pan American Airways System is proud of the operations of its four associated Latin American airlines, and also of its 50%-owned affiliate on the West Coast—Pan American-Grace Airways, Inc.
From the very beginning of the company—in 1927—Pan American has sought not to control the air (as the German Lufthansa attempted to do), but rather to aid and advise our "Good Neighbors” in the formation of their own national airlines.
Today, in Brazil, there is "Panair”, (Panair do Brasil, S. A.)
In Mexico there is CMA (Compania Mexicana de Aviacion, S. A.)
In Colombia there is "Avianca” (Aerovias Nac-ionales de Colombia), and UMCA_(Uraba, Medellin and Central Airways, Inc.)
