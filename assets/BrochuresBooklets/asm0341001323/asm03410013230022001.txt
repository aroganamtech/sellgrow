In his 1944 letter to Pan American stockholders, President Juan Trippe said, "The System's war contribution has been made possible only by the gallantry, loyalty and teamwork of Pan American employees throughout the world."
Of those employees, ninety have given their lives in performance of their wartime duty. Forty -five are interned in Japanese prison camps.
Many of the System’s personnel have performed deeds of courage which will make history. Among these were Captain W. W. Moss and his crew who rescued 48 survivors of a troop ship sinking, despite heavy seas . . . "Praiseworthy judgment, courage and airmanship”, said the Navy Commendation.
