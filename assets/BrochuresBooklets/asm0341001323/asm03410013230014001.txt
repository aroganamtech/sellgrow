OMA-IMPORTANT LINK IN MEXICO'S ECONOMIC REVIVAL
The passengers and cargo now being carried by CMA (Pan American’s Mexican affiliate—see previous page) are now at an all-time high.
The services of this important airline have made a very real contribution to the country’s recent economic revival and industrial expansion.
Since Pan American now flies to Mexico through five great U. S. gateways—Los Angeles, Nuevo Laredo, Brownsville, New Orleans and Miami (via Cuba)—this airline and the beauties of Mexico will become familiar indeed to American tourists after the war.
