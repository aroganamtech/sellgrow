By Way of Introduction...
This is volume I, number 1, of a new publication specifically for Clipper Club members. In this issue and others that will follow, we intend to review the many privileges and advantages of Clipper Club membership. And, from time to time, call your attention to special offers available only to members.
Membership Extension
No matter when you receive your new membership card, it’s good for a full 12 months. So if your card was delayed, you can use it for a year, dating from the time you received it.
All future membership cards will bear a month/year expiration date. Some cards previously processed were dated ,,1971"5 but will be valid for a full year from time of receipt.

Please carry your membership card when you visit Clipper Clubs. Cards are required for admittance, and will be checked to assure you and other members the exclusivity of the clubs. Your card is not transferable, for the same reason.
