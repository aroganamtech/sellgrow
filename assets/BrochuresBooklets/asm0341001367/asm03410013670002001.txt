Hong Kong/New Territories/ Kowloon Tour.
Side one tours Kowloon and the New Territories by train and car: Lion Rock and Amah Rock, Shatin, PagodaofTenThousand Buddhas, Chinese University of Hong Kong, the market town of Taipo, Sheung, Shui, Lok Ma Chou, walled city of Kam Tin and all the major shopping streets. Side two is devoted to the sights of Hong Kong island: Central District, Peak Tram, Tiger Balm Gardens, Happy Valley, Chaiwan, Repulse Bay, Aberdeen, Man Mo Meal, Ladder Street and the Thieves Market.
Tokyo Walking Tour.
Sound effects are an important part of this tape, which takes you around the Marunouchi Business District and Imperial Palace, to Kitanomaru Park, Akasaka district, OkuraArt Museum, Tokyo Festival Hall, Ueno Zoo, Toshogu Pagoda, Benten Shrine, Nakamise shopping street, Rokku theatre district and the Ginza. Side two proceeds from the Tokyo Tower to Shiba Park, Hama Rikyu Garden, Korakuen Gardens and Stadium, Kanda, Tokyo University, Yushlma Tenjin Shrine, the Seido, Shlbuya district, Yoyogi Sports Center, Meiji Shrine and Tokyo Broadcasting Company (NHK).
El Morro Walking Tour.
Side one takes you around San Juan’s most imposing bastion: Main Plaza, Chapel, Austria Bastion, Tejeda Bastion, Lighthouse, Santa Barbara Battery, Living Quarters, Original Gun Tower and Museum. Side two is a selection of contemporary and traditional Puerto Rican music, including “The Bullfighter,” ‘Mexican Incident,” and ‘Vienessa.”

Puerto Rico Driving Tour.
José Ferrer is your guide on a drive-it-yourself tour of El Yunque Rain Forest, Luquillo Mountain and Beach, La Atalaya Watch Tower, the fishing village of Fajardo, Playa de Humacao, Caguas, Central Highway, Cayey, Barranquitas, Ponce, Arecibo, the Karst Region, Rio Abajo State Forest, Arecibo Ionospheric Observatory, Agua-dilla, Mayaguez, La Parguera, San German, Boqueron Beach and Phosphorescent Bay.
U.S. Tours In 5 Languages.
Visitors can now enjoy tours of the three most popular cities in the United States with a guide-on-tape who speaks their language. U.S. Tours on Tape cassettes are available in English, French, German, Italian and Spanish.
New York City-Five Walking Tours.
Side one is divided into two tours: Downtown covers Trinity Church, Lower Broadway, the Battery, Statue of Liberty and Wall Street. The Greenwich Village tour includes Washington Square, MacDougal Street, Sheridan Square, 8th Street, New York University. Side two includes three tours: Midtown covers the Empire State Building, United Nations, St. Patrick’s Cathedral, Rockefeller Center; Museum tour takes you to the Museum of Modern Art, the Frick, Whitney, Metropolitan and Guggenheim; and the West Side Tour covers Columbus Circle, Lincoln Center and Times Square.
New York City Walking Tour.
Side one is divided into two parts. The first concentrates on the Midtown area—Flower Market, Fur Center, Madison Square Garden, Garment District and Diamond Center. The second part is a stroll through Central Park—Bethesda Fountain, Wollman Rink, the Lake and the Zoo. The second side of the tape includes the New York neighborhoods: Grade Square, Upper East Side, Carl Schütz Park, Gracie Mansion, Fireboats, Yorkville District, Brooklyn Bridge, Lower East Side, City Hall, Chinatown, Little Italy,
West Bowery and Greenwich Village.

Washington, D.C. Walking Tour.
First you'll explore the Capitol Building, Library of Congress, Supreme Court, Federal Mall and Pennsylvania Avenue. Then you’ll walk along Constitution Avenue to the Smithsonian Institution, Washington Monument, Jefferson Memorial, Lincoln Memorial and the White House.
San Francisco Walking Tour.
Side one includes Union Square, Nob Hill, Cable Car Barn, View of the Bay, the Art Institute, Fisherman's Wharf and the Cannery. Side two begins with a Harbor Cruise and continues to Ghirardelli Square, Washington Square, Telegraph Hill, Go-go District, Jackson Square, Chinatown, the Shopping District and Palace Hotel. Plus information on good restaurants and leisure-time entertainments.
