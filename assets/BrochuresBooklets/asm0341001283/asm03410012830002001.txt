ROUND THE WORLD
It’s easy . .. from the United States or Europe . . . since Northwest and Pan American can take you to all six continents. To any of 83 countries, colonies or territories, in fact, around the globe. Distance melts away when you travel by air. And hours aloft on Northwest and Pan American airliners are so spectacularly scenic, so carefree, pleasant and downright comfortable, time passes all too quickly.
And because Northwest and Pan American get you around so quickly and conveniently you don’t have to be gone months to see and enjoy those “far-away places”. Istanbul, for example, the fascinating Middle East, Calcutta and Hong Kong, Tokyo and the mysterious Orient. In hours you can be relaxing in Rio, Buenos Aires and lively South America. Or out in unforgettable Hawaii and the land Down Under where Trade Winds invite you to forget the cares of the world.
Best of all, you’ll be surprised how little it costs. In fact, once you learn, chances are you’ll decide to continue around the world by Pan American and Northwest. For ofttimes it won’t cost much more than returning home the way you came.
