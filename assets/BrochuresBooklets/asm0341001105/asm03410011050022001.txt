Daily Departures via Panagra and PAA
U. S. GATEWAY — Leave by plane for Panama. Arrive at the Canal Zone in the afternoon.
PANAMA CITY — Visit old Panama and the canal facilities. QUITO — Fly to the highland capital of Ecuador where you visit the Colonial Gems of Quito and the famous Equatorial Monument.
LIMA — Day flight to the capital of Peru. Visit the city; excursion by car to Chosica and the Inca ruins of Cajamarquilla.
SANTIAGO — Fly past the famed volcano El Misti, Arequipa, and nitrate fields to the capital of Chile. Visit the city. Excursion to Valparaiso and Vina del Mar.
CHILEAN AND ARGENTINE LAKES — Overland trip by rail, lake steamers, and motor through an incomparable setting of mountains, volcanoes, and lakes, visiting Puerto Varas, Peulla, in Chile, and Puerto Blest, Bariloche, in Argentina from where one crosses the great pampas northbound for Buenos Aires.

BUENOS AIRES — Visit the cosmopolitan capital of Argentina, including an excursion to the picturesque boating resort of El Tigre (in summer) or to the shrine of Lujan (in winter). MONTEVIDEO — One hour morning flight across the Rio de la Plata to the smallest of the South American republics. Visit the capital city and its famous beaches.
SAO PAULO — Wing northbound to the cosmopolitan and progressive center of Brazil's coffee trade. Visit the city and the famous Butantan snake farm. Full day excursion over a thrilling cog-wheel railway to the great port of Santos and its beautiful beaches.
RIO DE JANEIRO — Fly to the capital of Brazil. You will drive around this most beautiful city in the world, thrill to the cable ascent of Sugar Loaf Mountain, ascend Corcovado, and visit the famed Quitandinha at Petropolis.
HOMEWARD BOUND — Wing northward across Brazil and over the West Indies.
