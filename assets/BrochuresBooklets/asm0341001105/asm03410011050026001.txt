U. S. GATEWAY — Leave by plane for Mexico City. MEXICO CITY — Visit the city, the Pyramids of Teotihuacan and Guadalupe, the Floating Gardens at Xochimilco, and excursion to Cuernavaca and Taxco.
GUATEMALA CITY — Morning flight to the capital of Guatemala.
QUICHE-MAYAN HIGHLANDS — By car into the Indian Country visiting beautiful Lake Atitlan, Solola, the famous market scenes at Chichicastenango, returning to Guatemala City via Antigua and Lake Amatitlan.
PANAMA — Wings over Central America and visit old Panama and the canal installations.
CALI — Morn ing flight to this important industrial city in the Cauca Valley. Visit the town and excursion into the suburbs viewing the various haciendas.
BOGOTA — Morning flight to the capital of Colombia. Visit the city and the Quinta Bolivar, nearby Monserrate for the panorama, and Tequendama Falls.
QUITO — Fly via Cali, Colombia, to the capital of Ecuador. Visit the Colonial gems of Quito and the famous Equatorial Monument.
LIMA — Southward by Panagra plane to the capital of Peru. Visit this beautiful city founded by Pizarro, and excursion over the famed Central Highway to Chosica and the Incan ruins of Cajamarquilla.
AREQUIPA — Morning flight to this beautiful Colonial city at the foot of El Misti.
CUZCO — Inland trip by rail, overnighting at Puno on the shores of Lake Titicaca, to the one time capital of an Empire embracing one third of the continent. Your sightseeing in Cuzco will include the city proper, an excursion to the citadel of Sachsahuaman, and a full day drive to Pisac for the Sunday market; the climax will be a full day trip by motor and horseback to the outstanding ruins of Macchu-Picchu.
LAKE TITICACA. By rail to Puno, on the shores of Lake Titicaca which is crossed by steamer.
LA PAZ — Connect by rail to the capital of Bolivia, the highest in the world, at the foot of majestic Illimani. Visit the city, fascinating markets, and the picturesque suburb of Obrajes. SANTIAGO — Leave by plane over the nitrate fields of Chile, to Santiago.
VINA DEL MAR — Visit Santiago, the capital of Chile, in a beautiful setting at the foot of the Andes, and the fashionable ocean resort of Vina del Mar, near Valparaiso.
CHILEAN AND ARGENTINE LAKES—Ten day overland trip by rail, lake steamers, and motor through the incomparable setting of mountains, lakes, and fjords, visiting Puyehue, Puerto Varas, Peulla, in Chile, and Puerto Blest, Llao-Llao, and Bar-iloche, in Argentina from where one crosses the great pampas northbound for Buenos Aires.
