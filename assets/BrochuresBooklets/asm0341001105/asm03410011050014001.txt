MIAMI — By morning plane to the capital of Haiti, set in a beautiful bay.
PORT-AU-PRINCE—Visit the bustling markets, the lovely Cathedral, and the surrounding tropical hills at Petionville and Kenskoff. Afternoon plane to the capital of the Dominican Republic.
CIUDAD TRUJILLO — Time for swimming and visit the city and the Cathedral containing the ashes of Columbus. Afternoon plane to Puerto Rico.
SAN JUAN —Visit the second largest city in the West Indies and excursion to the Caribbean National Forest and El Junque Mountain. Leave by plane in the evening for the island of Trinidad.
PORT OF SPAIN — Drive to the Saddle Back Pass for a beautiful panorama of the island.
CARACAS — Morning flight to La Guaira and leave by car, over the mountains, to Caracas. Visit the capital of Venezuela. BARRANQUILLA — By plane via Maracaibo to this important Colombian seaport.
CARTAGENA—Visit Barranquilla and full day excursion to Cartagena, fascinating walled city of the Spanish Main, with lunch at a famous resort hotel.
KINGSTON — Across the Caribbean to the island of Jamaica. Excursion to the ridge of the Blue Mountains and to the famous botanical Hope Gardens.
