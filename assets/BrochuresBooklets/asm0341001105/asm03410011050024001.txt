36th day
CUZCO — Inland trip by rail, overnighting at Puno on the shores of Lake Titicaca, to the one time capital of an Empire embracing one third of the continent. Your sightseeing in Cuzco will include the city proper, an excursion to the citadel of Sachsahuaman, and a full day drive to Pisac for the Sunday market; the climax will be a full day trip by motor and horseback to the outstanding ruins of Macchu-Picchu. Return by rail to Arequipa.
LIMA — By plane to the capital of Peru and visit this beautiful city founded by Pizarro; also excursion over the famed Central Highway to Chosica and the Incan ruins of Cajamarquilla.
QUITO—Morning flight to the highland capital of Ecuador where you visit the Colonial gems of Quito and the famous Equatorial Monument.
BALBOA — Leave by plane via Cali, Colombia, for the Canal Zone, visiting Panama City and the locks, crossing the Isthmus to Cristobal.
U. 5. GATEWAY — Arrive by afternoon plane.
U. S. TRADE WITH LATIN AMERICA
The pleasure traveler AND THE BUSINESSMAN alike will find EXPRINTER SERVICE ever ready to care for all needs. The pleasure traveler will encounter many opportunities en route for some business angle which would not have occurred to him unless he had taken this trip. If the main purpose of the trip is from a business viewpoint, the need for advance reservations and occasional relaxation will be well met by a prearranged itinerary.
