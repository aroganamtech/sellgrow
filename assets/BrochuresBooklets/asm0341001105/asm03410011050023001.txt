CRUISE 121	36 DAYS
Daily Departures via PAA and Panagra
U. S. GATEWAY — Wing over the West Indies to the Island of Trinidad.
PORT OF SPAIN — Drive to the Saddle Back Pass for a beautiful panorama of the island.
ACROSS THE EQUATOR — Continue over the “line" and across the interior of Brazil to Rio de Janeiro, the capital.
RIO DE JANEIRO — You will drive around this most beautiful city in the world, thrill to the cable ascent of Sugar Loaf Mountain, and ascend Corcovado.
SAO PAULO—Morning flight of the cosmopolitan and progressive center of Brazil's coffee trade. Visit the city and the famous Butantan snake farm. Continue southward by plane to the smallest of the South American republics.
MONTEVIDEO — Visit the capital city and its famous beaches. Afternoon flight across the Rio de la Plata.
BUENOS AIRES — Visit the cosmopolitan capital of Argentina, including an excursion to the picturesque boating resort of El Tigre (in summer) or to the shrine of Lujan (in winter). SANTIAGO —Via old Cordoba and Mendoza, and over the lofty passes of the great Cordillera of the Andes by plane to Santiago.
VINA DEL MAR — Visit Santiago, the capital of Chile, in a beautiful setting at the foot of the Andes, and the fashionable ocean resort of Vina del Mar, near Valparaiso.
AREQUIPA — Leave Santiago by plane for the eight hour flight over the nitrate fields of Chile, to the beautiful Colonial city of Arequipa at the foot of El Misti.
