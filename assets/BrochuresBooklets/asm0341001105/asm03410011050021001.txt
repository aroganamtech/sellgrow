WINGS AROUND THE CONTINENT
PANAMA • PERU • CHILE ARGENTINA • URUGUAY BRAZIL • TRINIDAD PUERTO RICO
CRUISE 119-30 DAYS
Daily Departures via Panagra and PAA
1 st day MIAMI — Leave by plane for Panama.
2nd day PANAMA CITY—Visit old Panama and the canal facilities, crossing the Isthmus to Cristobal.
3rd day to 6th day: LIMA —
Day flight to the capital of Peru. Visit the city. Excursion by car to Chosica and the Inca ruinsof Cajamar-quilla.
SANTIAGO — Day flight past the famed volcano El Misti, Arequipa, and nitrate fields to the capital of Chile. Visit the city and excursion to Valparaiso and Vina del Mar on the Chilean Riviera.
FLIGHT OVER THE ANDES — Past mighty Aconcagua, and via Mendoza and Cordoba to the capital of Argentina. BUENOS AIRES — Visit the city and excursion to Lujan (in winter) or El Tigre (in summer).
MONTEVIDEO — By plane to the capital of Uruguay. Visit the city and the beautiful beaches.
SAO PAULO — Wing northward to the cosmopolitan and progressive center of Brazil's coffee trade. Visit the city and the famous Butantan Snake Farm and excursion to Santos.
RIO DE JANEIRO — Day flight to the capital of Brazil. Visit the city, beaches, Corcovado, Sugar Loaf, and the famed Quitandinha at Petropolis.
PORT OF SPAIN — Flight over the interior of Brazil to the mouth of the mighty Amazon and via the Guianas to Trinidad. SAN JUAN —Visit the second largest city in the West Indies. U. S. GATEWAY — Homeward bound to Miami or New York.
CRUISE 1 19A
A 'Round South America cruise similar to the one above, omitting two stopovers, can be reduced to a duration of 20 days.
