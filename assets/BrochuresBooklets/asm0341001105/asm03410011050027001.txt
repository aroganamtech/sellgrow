BUENOS AIRES—Visit the cosmopolitan capital of Argentina, including an excursion to the picturesque boating resort of El Tigre (in summer) or to the shrine of Lujan (in winter), and a trip to La Plata with a stop en route at an “estancia," a typical Argentine farm.
MONTEVIDEO—Morning flight across the Rio de la Plata to the smallest of the South American republics. Visit the capital city and its famous beaches.
SAO PAULO — Wing northbound to the cosmopolitan and progressive center of Brazil’s coffee trade. Visit the city and the famous Butantan Snake Farm. Full day excursion over a thrilling cog-wheel railway to the great port of Santos and its beautiful beaches.
RIO DE JANEIRO — Fly to the capital of Brazil. You will drive around this most beautiful city in the world, thrill to the cable ascent of Sugar Loaf Mountain, ascend Corcovado, and visit the famed Quitandinha at Petropolis.
PORT OF SPAIN—Wings over the Guianas to the island of Trinidad, and drive to the Saddle Back Pass, for a beautiful panorama, and Maracas Beach.
U. S. GATEWAY — Homeward bound to Miami or New York.
