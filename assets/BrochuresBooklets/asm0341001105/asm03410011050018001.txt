PANAMA • PERU • BOLIVIA CRUISE 112 • 16 DAYS
Daily departures via Panagra and PAA
MIAMI — Leave by plane for Panama.
PANAMA CITY — Visit old Panama and the canal facilities, crossing the Isthmus to Cristobal.
ACROSS THE EQUATOR — Winging southward via Colombia and Ecuador to Lima.
AREQUIPA — Morning flight to the city of Eternal Sunshine at the foot of “El Misti.” Afternoon sightseeing.
CUZCO — Inland trip by rail, overnighting at Puno, to the one time capital of an Empire embracing one third of the continent. Your sightseeing will include the city proper, an excursion to the citadel of Sachsahuaman, and a full day drive to Pisac for the Sunday Market; the climax will be a full day trip by rail-car and horseback to the outstanding ruins of Macchu-Picchu.
LAKE TITICACA — By day train to Puno for the crossing of the highest navigable body of water in the world.
LA PAZ — Visit the capital of Bolivia, the highest in the world at the foot of majestic Illimani. Return in the afternoon of the 1 3th day to Lima.
LIMA — Visit the capital of Peru founded by Pizarro. Excursion by car to Chosica and the Inca ruins of Cajamarquilla. MIAMI — Homeward bound via Balboa.
