BAGGAGE TIPS
Identify your baggage outside and inside. Put name and address on tag or label.
Pack light Lock your bags Buy sturdy luggage Check-in early
Claim your baggage immediately on arrival
If your baggage is missing or damaged, tell our Baggage Service personnel before leaving the Claim Area.
Don’t Check: Important papers, medicines, valuables, cash or jewelry, glass, liquids.
Security Items such as knives, toy guns, scissors and the like should be carried in checked baggage only.
BAGGAGE LIABILITY—Liability for loss, delay, or damage to baggage is limited as follows unless a higher value is declared in advance and additional charges are paid: (1) for travel wholly between U.S. points, to $750 per passenger on most carriers; (2) for most international travel (including domestic portions of international journeys) to approximately $9.07 per pound ($20.00 per kilo) for checked baggage and $400 per passenger for unchecked baggage. Unless otherwise stated on the baggage check, the weight of each piece of baggage shall be deemed by Pan Am to be 70 pounds (32 kilos). This presumption limits our liability to no more than $640 per piece of checked baggage. Special rules may apply to valuables and to fragile or perishable articles. Consult your carrier for further information. RESTRICTED ARTICLES—Acid, matches, lighter fluid and other Items classified by the Department of Transportation as hazardous materials—i.e. explosives, flammable solids and liquids, oxidizing materials, compressed gasses, poisons, etiologic agents, radioactive materials and irritating materials cannot be carried as checked baggage. Firearms may be carried only in checked baggage and an oral declaration must be made to carrier that the firearms are unloaded.
