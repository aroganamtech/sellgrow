How to Organize A Flying Clipper Travel Group
and qualify for Tour Conductor’s free or reduced rate
Summer travel to the countries of the world is back again.
Not since pre-War years has there been such a wealth of global travel opportunities. Never before has it been so convenient to make such trips by Flying Clipper.
A very good way of taking advantage of these world air travel opportunities is to organize a group of congenial persons, with similar interests, and travel together. To the person who takes the initiative in organizing such a group, arranging air reservations, hotel accommodations, sightseeing trips, etc., and who accompanies the group as its tour conductor, Pan American World Airways offers free transportation, provided there are 10 or more full fare paying passengers in the group. With a group of 5 to 9, the tour conductor may travel at half the regular fare.
How do you organize an air travel group?
1. Survey the situation.
What friends and acquaintances would be interested in world air travel? Would they be likely to prefer a short trip to Havana, to Bermuda, to Nassau, to Mexico, to the Caribbean, the West Indies, Guatemala, Hawaii or Alaska? Or would they be prospects for a more extensive journey to Europe, through Central America, around South America, to the South Seas, the Orient, or perhaps around the world by Flying Clipper?
Reach a conclusion in your own mind as to what sort of trip might appeal to your circle of friends, the teachers in your school, your fellow students or any others whom you might approach.
But don’t talk to them yet. Instead go on to Step 2.
Decide whether you wish to make all arrangements yourself, or call upon a travel agent for assistance in planning and in making your Clipper bookings and reservations for hotels and sightseeing.
