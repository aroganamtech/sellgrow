They called it “Operation Way Back Home”
Last Christmas, for the first time, thousands of service men and women stationed in Europe spent their furloughs at home. The effect on their morale was tremendous. Here is how it came about:
1.	Last May, tourist air fares across the Atlantic went into effect. Pan American World Airways had led the campaign for such fares for many years. Winter “Thrift Season” reductions brought still further savings.
2.	Pan American worked out a plan with the American Express Company which enabled service men to “fly now—pay later.” This whole plan was called “Operation Way Back Home.”
3.	The Armed Forces permitted those with 30-day furloughs to spend them at home.
More than a thousand service men and women came home by Pan American alone. Extra flights of The Rainbow—Pan American’s fa-
mous tourist fare service—were made, and every service man and woman was guaranteed a seat.
In many cases, where a service man could not come home, Pan American made arrangements for his family to visit him in Europe.
That’s a pleasant thing about being an international airline. You can bring about so many good things that had not been possible before. Pan American World Airways, New York 17, N.Y.
