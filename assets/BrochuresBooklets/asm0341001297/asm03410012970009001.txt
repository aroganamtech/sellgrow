HOW THEY STOP
In landing, high speed was a drawback for the jet plane since the highly streamlined fuselage offered little resistance to the air. With no propeller to reverse, it tended to keep on rolling when it hit the runway. To solve this problem, all jet airliners are equipped with thrust reversers. These are devices for mechanically deflecting the exhaust gases forward in much the same manner as a garden hose nozzle can be made to squirt water backward by placing one’s hand over the end. This deflection of the exhaust gases acts as a brake and slows the airplane down to a safe landing speed.
