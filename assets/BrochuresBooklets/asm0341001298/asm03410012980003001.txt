The Shrinking World
When a Pan American World Airways jet transport lifts off the runway at New York International Airport on the first scheduled jet flight to Europe a new age of international air transportation under the American flag will be born.
The first American-built jet airliner, a Boeing 707, will open an era which will see the world shrunk by 40 per cent. The Jet Clippers will reduce the New York to Paris flying time from 13 hours to 7 hours; from New York to London from 12 to 6!/^ hours with proportionate reductions in other flight times.
The 575-mile-per-hour cruising speed of the Boeing 707-121’s and, later, the Boeing Intercontinentals and the Douglas DC-8’s, will make Chicago only a little more than seven hours from London. San Francisco will be closer to Honolulu than it now is to Chicago.
Pan American has on order 44 Jet Clippers, 23 from Boeing and 21 from Douglas. Other airlines will follow Pan American into the Jet Age and it is safe to say that by 1961 the major airline routes of the world will be flown by jet transports.
