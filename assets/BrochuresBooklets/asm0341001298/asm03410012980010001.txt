In 1959 Pan American will begin taking delivery on its fleet of 17 Boeing Intercontinentals and 21 Douglas DC-8’s. These aircraft, to be powered by Pratt & Whitney J-75 engines, will have longer range and carry more passengers than the first six Boeing 707’s.
The Boeing Intercontinental, a truly long-range jet transport, cruising at almost 600 miles per hour, will make the flight from Paris to New York nonstop in any season of the year. It will require only one stop on the long San Francisco to Sydney, Australia, route.
The Douglas DC-8, which already has made its first flight, is also powered by the big J-75 engines. With a cruising speed of nearly 600 miles per hour it will carry from 109 to 168 passengers, depending on the class of service in which it is used.
With a range of more than 5,000 miles the DC-8, the Jet Age member of a long line of successful commercial transports, will fly from Moscow to New York nonstop or from Seattle to Tokyo.
Pan American has invested $300,000,000 in its new jet fleet. This investment was made because of Pan American’s faith in the future of air transportation. Pan American believes that the Jet Age will see greater increases in international travel and more friendly contact between the people of all nations than in any age in the history of the world. This could well be the road to peace.
