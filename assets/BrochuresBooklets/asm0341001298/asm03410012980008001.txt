Another comfort factor of a jet transport is the lack of vibration inherent in jet power. Propellers rotating at high speeds, along with the many moving parts of the piston engine, create vibration.
The jet engines which utilize their tremendous power in thrust eliminate much of this vibration. As an example, a glass can be balanced on the arm of a seat at normal cruising speed without spilling its contents. The passenger cabin also will have a noise level lower than most propeller-driven airliners.
