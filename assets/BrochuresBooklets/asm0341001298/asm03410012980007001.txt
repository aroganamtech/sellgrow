Jet Age Comfort
The Jet Clippers will carry more people farther and faster and they will carry them in a new kind of comfort.
Jet transports will operate at altitudes of from 30,000 to 40,000 feet where, for the first time, commercial aircraft will be truly “over the weather.” At these great altitudes there are virtually no cloud formations and seldom any turbulence. Occasional “pillars” of clouds reach up to 40,000 feet but they stand out in thin columns and are easily seen and avoided.
