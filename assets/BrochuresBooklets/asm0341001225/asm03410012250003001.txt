Hawaii is as familiar to most people as Broadway and 42nd Street —and today it’s just as aecessible!
Thanks to modern aircraft and to Pan American’s pioneering of a commercial sky route across the Pacific, the Islands are no longer a remote outpost. To the modern air traveler they lie just over the blue horizon, a few comfortable flying hours away. Yet, despite famili-
arity, or perhaps because of it, Hawaii is more alluring than ever—and with reason.
As you step, rested and refreshed, from your Pan American Clipper at the Municipal Airport four miles from Honolulu on the island of Oahu, you have the mixed emotion that you have never left your home, yet you are at the same time in an exotic new world—a world of soft, tropic air whose very scent and feel is strange and new; a world of intense color... of deep greens, brilliant blues and fiery reds.
Diamond Head and famed Waikiki Beach...sun and sparkling sea... pleasant company...a happy holiday.
