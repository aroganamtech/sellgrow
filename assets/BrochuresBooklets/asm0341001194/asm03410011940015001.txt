HOW TO GET THERE
Fly Pan Am direct to Warsaw, Okecie Airport, from New York via Frankfurt, or Copenhagen. Flying time from New York is 9 hours, Copenhagen lh hours, and Frankfurt Vii hours. (Poland is the only socialist state having Pan Am connections.) Time in Poland: GMT+1.
REQUIREMENTS FOR ENTRY AND CUSTOMS REGULATIONS
Passport and visa required, also smallpox vaccination certificate if coming from South America, Australia, or Africa. No limit to amount of foreign currency taken into Poland, but it must be declared and you may not take out more than you brought in. Polish currency must not be taken into or out of the country. A set of minimum amount of currency must be exchanged for each day of stay (US$12; or US$5 for people of Polish origin visiting relatives, students, and young people 16-26 years of age). Duty-free allowance: 250 cigarettes, 50 cigars, or Yt pound of tobacco; 2 liters wine or liquor; visitors are allowed to bring in two cameras with 10 rolls of film for each camera and one movie camera.
AIRPORT INFORMATION
Okecie Airport is 7 miles from Warsaw. Bus service to city terminal costs 1.5ZL Taxi fare, 40 Z Y to 50ZL There are special unmarked taxis (Polonez car). Drivers carry the badge “Taxi”. There is a fixed rate of 120 ZY to any hotel in Warsaw (waiting time 170ZJ per hour). Polish Airlines’ bus links international and domestic airports, fare 5ZY. Tip luggage porters 10ZJ to 20ZJ per bag. Airport has a duty-free shop, a hotel reservation counter, and a small LUNA hotel.
