Hungary
WHAT’S SPECIAL
In Hungary you can drive in a horse-drawn gig over the Puszta, the great Hungarian plains, go for a swim in Europe’s largest warm-water lake, spend the night in a sumptuous palace where the Esterhazy aristocracy once lived, or dine on magnificent goulash, sipping Bull’s Blood (a local red wine) to the strains of soulful gypsy violins. You can wander through the pleasant caffe-lined streets of the twin cities of Buda and Pest, go on a cruise down the River Danube, or just lie in the sun by Lake Balaton.

Climate: Winters in Hungary are rather harsh and cold, but the snow cover in the mountains lasts only 60-90 days. Summers are warm and pleasant, with temperatures in the upper 70s and low 80s, while the long fall is usually dry and sunny. Best time to go: May-September.
