YUGOSLAVIA
Government: A socialist federal republic including Bosnia Herzegovina, Croatia, Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia (with two autonomous provinces) Kosovo and Vojvodina and Slovenia. English, German, and Italian are fairly widespread.
Language: There are three Slav languages. Although there are two alphabets, Latin (generally in western Yugoslavia) and Cyrillic.
Religion: Orthodox, Roman Catholic, Muslim, Protestant.
Currency: Dinar. 100 paras = 1 dinar.

HOW TO GET THERE
Fly Pan Am direct from New York to Frankfurt. Flying time from Frankfurt to Belgrade is IJ2 hours. Time in Yugoslavia: GMT +1.
REQUIREMENTS FOR ENTRY AND CUSTOMS REGULATIONS
Visitors need a current passport, along with visas obtainable on entry; no limit to the amount of foreign currency that can be brought in. A limit of 1,500D in and 1,500D out per person (in denominations up to 100D). Duty-free allowance: 200 cigarettes; 50 cigars; U pound tobacco; % liter liquor; 1 liter wine; reasonable amount of perfume for personal use; personal effects within reasonable amounts.
AIRPORT INFORMATION
Belgrade Airport is 7)2 miles from the city center; taxi fare is 240D. The bus fare is 20D. Tip porters 15D per bag. Airport departure is 45D for Europe, 80D for intercontinental flights.
