Czechoslovakia
WHAT'S SPECIAL
Czechoslovakia lies across the heart of central Europe and is really two nations—Bohemia and Moravia in the west, and Slovakia in the east. The languages are different, though related. Although at the moment the majority of tourists are from other East European countries, Cedok, the national travel bureau, works hard to organize tourist arrangements for Westerners.
