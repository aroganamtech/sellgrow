HUNGARY
ACCOMMODATIONS
There are over 260 hotels in- Hungary, but even so there is a general shortage of hotel rooms during the summer months (June-September); reservations are highly recommended, particularly if you want a room with private bath. Budapest and Balaton hotels offer off-season reductions from 16 November-1 April. There are also 60 first-class camping sites (details from the Hungarian Camping and Caravanning Club, 1088 Budapest VII, Miizeum utca 11—tel: 141 880), as well as a large number of motels and youth hostels. Delightful holiday cabins with a bathroom and kitchen equipped with a refrigerator can also be rented in Balaton resorts, but if you really want to get to know Hungarian people, there is no better way than by becoming a “paying guest” in someone’s home. Details of accommodations in A/I, A/II, and B category hotels can be had from Danubius (1052 Budapest V, Martinelli t6r 8—tel: 173 652), from HungarHotels (1052 Budapest V, Petofi S&ndor utca 16—-tel: 183 018), and from Pannbnia (1052 Budapest V, Kigy6 utca 4-6—tel: 183 910). Paying-guest rooms can be reserved through any IBUSZ bureau, through the Cooptourist and Volantourist travel agencies, and through the tourist offices.
Rates are in US$ for a double/twin room with bath or shower including breakfast.
