Newspapers: Some imported English-language newspapers and magazines are available in hotels. The local English-language publication on sale at newsstands is the Daily News.
Tipping: Although officially discouraged, it is quite customary. In restaurants, leave about 10-15%. Porters are tipped lOfts per piece of luggage; cab drivers, 5-10fts; hotel porters, lOfts; cloakroom attendants, 1-2fts.
Electricity: 220 volts 50 cycles AC, throughout the country.
Laundry: Dry-cleaning and laundry services in most hotels.
Hairdressing: Over 300 beauty salons in Budapest alone. Shampoo and from 30fts; man’s haircut with wash from 20fts. Tip 20%.
Photography: Bring your own film. A black and white film with cost from 13-20fts. Developing 3-4 days.
Babysitting: In Budapest the cooperative Tempo (Bajcsy Zsilinsky utca 56—tel: 127-051) will arrange.
Rest Rooms: In restaurants, cafés, and bars; some public facilities; women, nok, men, ftrfiak.
Health: Many Hungarian doctors and dentists speak English. Ordinary drugs available. Tap water in Budapest and major towns safe.
TRANSPORTATION
Bus, subway, streetcar, and trolleybus services in Budapest. Taxis can be hailed in the street. If you want an English-speaking driver, call 222-222 or 666-666. Weekly rates for car enting from about US$44. Deposit of about US$100 needed. Roads generally very good.
