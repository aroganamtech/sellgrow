REQUIREMENTS FOR ENTRY AND CUSTOMS REGULATIONS
Any Western visitor to Hungary needs a valid passport and an entry visa, which as a rule is obtainable at Hungarian consulates within 24 hours. Two passport-picture-size photographs must be presented with the visa application forms. The cost of a visa is 90fts, payable in convertible currency. Tourists arriving by car or plane may obtain their visas at border crossing stations or at Ferihegy Airport at a cost of 135fts in convertible currency. No visas are issued at the frontier railway stations.
The general limit for a visa is 30 days, but an extension is possible through the Aliens Registration Office KEOKPI, N6pkozt&rsas&g ut 93, Budapest VI, or through the large Budapest hotels.
Duty-free allowances are 2 liters of wine, 1 liter of spirits, 250 cigarettes or 50 cigars or 250 grams tobacco per person over 16 years of age; gifts to the total value of 5,000 fts on each entry. Travelers’ checks, and cash can be exchanged at travel bureaus, at exchange offices on the border, in hotels, at stations, and at the Hungarian National Bank, the National Savings Bank, and their branch offices. Quite a few stores, restaurants, and hotels in Hungary accept Western credit cards. At a number of stores in Budapest, tourists may do their shopping with American Express, Carte Blanche, Diners Club, or Eurocard credit cards.
AIRPORT INFORMATION
The international airport for Budapest is Ferihegy, 10 miles from the city. There are buses every 20 minutes from 5:30-10:30—the fare, including transportation of luggage, to Vorosmarthy Terminal is lOfts per person. Taxi can be scarce, and the fare to the heart of town is 80-100fts including tip. If you are renting a car during your stay, you can have it waiting for you at the airport. There is a departure tax of 30fts. Ferihegy has a dutyfree shop and hotel reservation counter.
