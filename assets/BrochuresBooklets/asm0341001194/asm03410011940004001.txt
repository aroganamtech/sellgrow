CZECHOSLOVAKIA
AIRPORT INFORMATION
Ruzyne Airport is 7 miles from Prague. Airport bus to city terminal, fare 6Kcs (children up to 10 years, 3Kcs). Prague public transport bus runs at 10-minute intervals during the day, fare lKc. Taxi fare is about 70Kcs plus 10$ tip. Luggage porters expect 2Kcs per bag. There is no departure tax. There is a duty-free shop and a Cedok office.
ACCOMMODATIONS
Visitors can travel anywhere in Czechoslovak; reserve in advance through Cedok, whose subsidiary, Interhotels, runs the majority of hotels. Wide selection of accommodations from deluxe hotels to family boarding houses and mountain cabins. In mountain and spa regions, a small sojourn and spa tax is charged. During July and August visitors can stay inexpensively in students’ hostels in Prague. USEFUL INFORMATION
Banks: Open 8-4 weekdays, until noon Sat. Money and travelers’ checks can be changed at Cedok offices and hotels.
Mail: Stamps can be bought at post offices and tobacconists.
Telephone Tips: Street phone booths are usually red and yellow. You can also telephone from hotels and restaurants. Useful numbers in Prague:

Newspapers: Some foreign newspapers are available at hotels and newspaper stands.
Tipping: Officially discouraged but privately welcomed. Tip waiter 3-5$. Give taxi drivers 5-10$, porters 2Kcs per piece of luggage.
Electricity: 220 or 110 volts 50 cycles AC.
Laundry: Main hotels have laundry services, and dry-cleaning is available (dry-cleaning a suit, from 25Kcs; laundering a shirt, from 5Kcs). Laundromats in main cities.
