HOW TO GET THERE
Fly Pan Am from New York to Frankfurt. Flying time from Frankfurt is 1 hour. Time in Czechoslovakia: GMT + 1.
REQUIREMENTS FOR ENTRY AND CUSTOMS REGULATIONS
Passport and visa required. The latter is obtainable from Czech embassies (submit two photographs); it allows a stay of up to 5 months for overseas visitors, 3 months for Europeans. Children under 15 accompanied by and entered on their parents’ passports require no separate visa. Health certificates not required for entry, except for visitors from Asia and Africa. No import or export of Czech currency allowed. Any amount of foreign currency or travelers’ checks may be brought in, but all transactions must be entered on visa form to be shown to customs on leaving. Special bonus rate of exchange for tourists. Visitors must exchange the equivalent of US$10 for each day of their stay (children half this amount). It is, therefore, advisable to buy prepayment vouchers for hotel accommodations and meals to cover this amount. They can be obtained in advance from Cedok, the national tourist bureau, in New York or major European capitals or through its accredited travel agents. Personal belongings come in duty-free, but duty must be paid on gifts worth more than US$30. Duty-free allowance: 200 cigarettes, 41 cigars, or 7 ounces tobacco; 1 quart liquor plus 2 quarts wine; 3 kilos foodstuffs; }i liter cologne; 3 rolls film, 100 meters of 8mm or 16mm movie film; 1,000 shots for sporting guns and 50 rifle cartridges. Antiques and works of art may be bought only at Tuzex shops or Artia Publishing House. These and other items bought at Tuzex shops are duty-free on showing the invoice. A special permit is required for the importation of firearms. This permit may be obtained at any Czechoslovak Consulate. On leaving the country, surplus korunas (beyond the obligatory minimum to be exchanged daily) can be changed into foreign currency at border crossings and at Prague and Bratislava airports. Certain kinds of food, clothing, and footwear cannot be exported, and a list of such goods is available from customs authorities at all border crossings.
