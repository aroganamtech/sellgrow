The annual Bermuda Game Fishing Tournament, held from April through October, is well known and creates great rivalry among resident and visiting fishermen. A special Bermuda Club and Bone-fish Tournament is held in winter. World record amberjack, bonefish and bonito have been taken from Bermuda waters, and eminently respectable catches of marlin, tuna, rock fish and wahoo are frequent. Boats, complete with tackle and bait, may be chartered for a day’s deep-sea fishing for $50 for parties of six or less. Water skiing equipment may be chartered. Horseback riding (saddle horses for hire). Golf clubs are excellent. Mid-Ocean Club is world famous, open to the public three days a week. Riddell’s Bay Golf and Country Club, Belmont Manor Golf Club, Castle Harbor and St. George courses are open to the public. Swimming in pools or at beaches. Equipment for golf, cycling, fishing may be rented.
TIME . . . Bermuda is an hour later than U.S. Eastern Standard Time. Daylight saving time is voted upon each year.
TRANSPORTATION . . . Bicycles, especially motor-assisted cycles
Exploring Bermuda by bicycle is a leisurely and pleasant way to see the mid-Atlantic Colony’s charming scenery.
Carriages may be hired by the hour or day and are fun for sightseeing about the island or for “taxi” service.
