Instinctively he felt the close link between the Flying Clippers and the ships of a century ago. Many of the same problems of buoyancy, equilibrium and streamlining that had faced the marine architects of the nineteenth century had to be solved on the drawing boards of the designers of Pan American Clippers as well.
Now we are in the midst of a new Clipper era, wrought by the same unique combination of commercial and romantic imagination that spurred the development of the Clipper ships. But, today the names read: JET CLIPPER FLYING CLOUD, JET CLIPPER FLEETWING, JET CLIPPER RESOLUTE, etc., etc. - and they ply many of the same routes, flash high above the same oceans and know many of the same ports of call as their sleek predecessors. Today, all of Pan Am’s passenger aircraft bear the proud name “Clipper.” While the flag painted high atop the tail of the Clippers of the Pan American fleet contains more stars than the colors which whipped above the decks of an earlier day, Jet Clipper passengers like yourself see the name of the aircraft beside the forward entrance ... JET CLIPPER SPLENDID, JET CLIPPER BLUE JACKET, JET CLIPPER CATHAY. As they silently speed across the trackless miles, perhaps these names bring to mind the sounds of creaking oak and spray whipping across the rail, and the steady hiss of foam against a polished black hull.
