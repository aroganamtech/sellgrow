China Clipper Inaugurated Service
Eleven days after the Martin’s arrival in Alameda, this aircraft, christened the China Clipper, the largest transport then existing in the world, took off for Honolulu on November 22, inaugurating commercial air transport service between California and the Hawaiian Islands.
On this momentous occasion, Postmaster James A. Farley came from Washington to witness what he characterized “the beginning of the greatest and most significant achievement in the marvelous and fascinating development of air transportation.”
The Parade of Progress was on but as progress is something .alive and vital which is constantly looking forward, so Pan American World Airways even before this service was inaugurated with the Martin Clippers was-looking ahead anticipating greater requirements and making plans for the development; and construction of even larger aircraft.
