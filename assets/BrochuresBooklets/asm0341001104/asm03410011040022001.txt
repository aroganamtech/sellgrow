CRUISE 211 • 30 DAYS
1st day U. S. GATEWAY — Leave by plane over the West Indies.
2nd day PORT OF SPAIN — Leisurely visit of this picturesque West 3rd day Indies trading center. Excursion over the famed Saddle Back Drive, fora beautiful panorama of the island, and to Maracas Beach.
4th day ACROSS THE EQUATOR — Winging over the great jungles of Brazil to incomparable Rio de Janeiro, you will cross the equator at the mouth of the majestic Amazon River.
RIO DE JANEIRO — Stay at a beach front hotel and enjoy life in the gay capital of Brazil. You will drive around the most beautiful city in the world, past many beaches and handsome buildings, and thrill to the cable ascent of Sugar Loaf Mountain for a fascinating bird's eye view of the bay and surrounding mountains.
9th day SAO PAULO — Your next stop in gigantic Brazil will be its 10th day second largest city, Sao Paulo, center of the coffee trade.
The atmosphere is cosmopolitan and the tempo of life brisk. Drive around the city and into the suburbs to visit the famous Butantan Snake Farm.
