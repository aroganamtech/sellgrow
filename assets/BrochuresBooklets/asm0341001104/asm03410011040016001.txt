U. S. GATEVJ/AY — Wing to the West Indies, spending the night in Puerto Rico.
SAN JUAN—Visit the second largest city in the West Indies, leaving by plane in the afternoon for Brazil.
RIO DE JANEIRO—You will drive around the most beautiful city in the world, thrill to the cable ascent of Sugar Loaf Mountain, ascend Corcovado, and visit the famous Quitan-dinha at Petropolis.
SAO PAULO — Morning flight to the progressive center of Brazil's coffee trade. Visit the city and the famous Butantan Snake Farm. Continue southward by plane.
BUENOS AIRES — Visit the cosmopolitan capital of Argentina, including an excursion to the picturesque boating resort of El Tigre (in summer) or to the shrine of Lujan (in winter). IGUAZU FALLS - By air to these famous falls. Transfer to the hotel on the Argentine side of the magnificent series of cataracts set in the heart of a deep evergreen jungle at the frontier abutment of Argentina, Brazil, and Paraguay. Excursion to the Falls and Devil's Gorges. Return to Buenos Aires. MONTEVIDEO — Morning flight to the smallest of the South American republics. Visit the capital city and its famous beaches. Wing northward across the Amazon to Trinidad. PORT OF SPAIN — Drive to the Saddle Back Pass, for a beautiful panorama of the island, and to Maracas Beach.
U. S. GATEWAY — Winging homeward.
