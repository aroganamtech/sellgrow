U. S. GATEWAY — Wing to the West Indies, spending the night in Puerto Rico.
SAN JUAN —Visit the second largest city in the West Indies and excursion to the Caribbean National Forest and El Yunque Mountain. Dine at Jaqueyes hotel to enjoy panorama. VIRGIN ISLANDS — Short flight to Charlotte Amalie, capital of St. Thomas.
ST. THOMAS — Visit principal sights. Time free for swimming and personal activities.
PORT-AU-PRINCE — Short flight to the capital of Haiti, set in a beautiful bay. Visit the building markets, the lovely Cathedral, and the surrounding tropical hills at Petionville and Kenskoff.
JAMAICA — Fly to Kingston for the scenic drive across-island, via Port Antonio, to the beaches of the Tower Isle Hotel for four days of complete relaxation.
MIAMI — Wing homeward to this U. S. Gateway.
