U. S. GATEWAY — By plane to Mexico City.
MEXICO CITY AND SURROUNDINGS - During your stay in Mexico, a comprehensive sightseeing program will be arranged including:
1.	Mexico City proper.
2.	The Floating Gardens at Xochimilco, and Churubusco.
3.	The Pyramids of Teotihuacan, the Guadalupe Shrine, and Acolman Monastery.
4.	Full day excursion to Cuernavaca and Taxco. GUATEMALA AND QUICHE-MAYAN HIGHLANDS-Visit the city and nearby Indian villages. Excursion by car into the Indian Country visiting beautiful Lake Atitlan, Solola, the famous market scenes at Chichicastenango, returning to Guatemala City via Antigua and Lake Amatitlan. TEGUCIGALPA — Morning flight to the capital of Honduras. Visit the city and excursion to Suyapa and San Juancito.
SAN JOSE — Morning flight to the capital of Costa Rica. Visit the city.
CARTAGO — Excursion to this old city, including an ascent of Irazu volcano. Afternoon flight to the Panama Canal. PANAMA CITY — Visit old Panama and the Canal. CARTAGENA — By plane via Barranquilla to this fascinating walled city of the Spanish Main.
BARRANQUILLA — Afternoon plane to this important Colombian port.
CARACAS — Morning flight and leave by car over the mountains to Caracas. Visit the capital of Venezuela.
CIUDAD TRUJILLO — By plane to the capital of the Dominican Republic, via Curacao. Visit the Cathedral containing the ashes of Columbus.
PORT-AU-PRINCE — Continue by plane to the picturesque capital of Haiti, set in a beautiful bay. Visit the bustling markets, the lovely Cathedral, and the surrounding tropical hills of Petionville and Kenskoff.
MIAMI — By plane to this U. S. Gateway.
