LIMA — Visit "The Beautiful City of the Kings" founded by Pizzaro, one time capital of Spanish America.
SANTIAGO — Day flight past the famed volcano El Misti, Arequipa, and nitrate fields to the capital of Chile. Visit the city in a beautiful setting at the foot of the Andes.
BUENOS AIRES—Visit the cosmopolitan capital of Argentina, the largest city in Latin America, with wide tree-lined boulevards, magnificent buildings, numerous parks, famed restaurants and entertainment showplaces.
MONTEVIDEO — Winging northbound to the great cities of the Atlantic shores of South America, your first stop will be Montevideo for a sightseeing tour of the prosperous, clean, and progressive capital of Uruguay.
RIO DE JANEIRO —Your next stopover is the capital of Brazil with its beautiful harbor dotted with numerous islands in the ultramarine water; the background of grey-green hills, is a sight never to be forgotten. Visit the city proper, drive along the beautiful beaches; then a thrilling cable ride to the peak of the famous granite cone the "Pao de Assucar" or Sugar Loaf Mountain.
U. S. GATEWAY — Homeward bound by overnight flight via New York or Miami.
