BUENOS AIRES — Visit the cosmopolitan capital of Argentina, the largest city in Latin America, with wide tree-lined boulevards, magnificent buildings, numerous parks, famed restaurants and entertainment showplaces.
MONTEVIDEO — Winging northbound to the great cities of the Atlantic shores of South America, your first stop will be Montevideo for a sightseeing tour of the prosperous, clean and progressive capital of Uruguay.
SAO PAULO — Your first stop in gigantic Brazil will be its second largest city, Sao Paulo, center of the coffee trade. The atmosphere is cosmopolitan and the tempo of life brisk. Drive around the city and into the suburbs to visit the famous Butantan Snake Farm.
RIO DE JANEIRO —Your next stop is the capital of Brazil with its beautiful harbor dotted with numerous islands in the ultramarine water; the background of grey-green hills, is a sight never to be forgotten. Visit the city proper, drive along the beautiful beaches; then a thrilling cable ride to the peak of the famous granite cone the "PQo de Assucar" or Sugar Loaf Mountain.
28th day TRINIDAD - Winging over the great jungles of Brazil and 29th day again over the Equator at the mouth of the majestic Amazon River, you alight on the island of Trinidad for a leisurely visit of this picturesque West Indian trading center, another shopping paradise. Drive over the Saddle Back Pass to enjoy the panorama, returning by way of Maracas Bay.
