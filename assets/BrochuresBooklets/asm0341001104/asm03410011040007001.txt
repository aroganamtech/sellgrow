3	THE SPANISH MAIN. Buccaneers and corsairs will come vividly to memory amid these settings of tropical brilliance, palm shaded beaches. Inland, in Venezuela and Colombia, you'll find written in timeless cathedrals, palaces and scenic passes of majestic mountains the epics of past wars for independence.
4	THE ANDES COUNTRIES AND INCA LAND. Now
you reach them easily by air —the scenic, legendary and historical sites of Andean nations guarded for centuries by stupendous natural barriers. Here you'll find personal travelogue treasures.
5	THE BIG RIVERS AND WATERFALL REGIONS. Explore off the beaten track throughout the Guianas, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay and the Argentine —discover majestic jungle waterfalls . . . Iguazu, Kaieteur, Paulo Affonso, Sete Quedas . . . along the Amazon and other mighty rivers and their tributaries.
6 THE SOUTHERN LAKES REGION. More natural scenic wonders than you'd expect to find in any one place —volcanoes, lakes and fjords unsurpassed by any in the world.
THROUGHOUT LATIN AMERICA, at the resorts and cities you visit . . . like Rio, Buenos Aires, Montevideo, Punta del Este, Viña del Mar . . . enjoyment of life is a fine art. You'll dine later and more leisurely, with every meal a gourmet's delight. Supper and night clubs are popular meeting places of local society. Theatres are excellent . . . some even offer special entertainment for those whose language command is shaky. And because of the favorable rate of exchange, your dollars stretch amazingly far to cover many luxuries you might deny yourself at home.
