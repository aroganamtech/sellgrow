MIAMI — Leave by plane for Panama.
PANAMA CITY — Visit old Panama and the canal facilities, crossing the Isthmus to Cristobal.
ACROSS THE EQUATOR — Winging southward via Colombia and Ecuador to Lima.
LIMA—Visit the capital of Peru founded by Pizarro. Excursion by car to Chosica and the Inca ruins of Cajamarquilla. CUZCO — By air to the one time capital of an Empire embracing one third of the continent. Your sightseeing will include the city proper, an excursion to the citadel of Sachsa-huaman, and a full day drive to Pisac for the Sunday Market; the climax will be a full day trip by rail-car and horseback to the outstanding ruins of Macchu-Picchu.
LAKE TITICACA — By day train to Puno for the crossing of the highest navigable body of water in the world.
LA PAZ — Visit the capital of Bolivia, the highest in the world, at the foot of majestic Illimani, and its picturesque suburb of Obrajes.
AREQUIPA — Morning flight to the city of Eternal Sunshine at the foot of "El Misti." Afternoon sightseeing. Homeward bound via Lima.
