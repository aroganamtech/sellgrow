BUENOS AIRES —Visit the cosmopolitan capital of Argentina, the largest city in Latin America, with wide tree-lined boulevards, magnificent buildings, numerous parks, famed restaurants and entertainment showplaces.
ARGENTINE AND CHILEAN LAKES —By plane to Bariloche for an overland trip by lake steamer, and motor through an incomparable setting of mountains, volcanoes, and lakes, visiting Llao-Llao, Puerto Blest, in Argentina, and Peulla, Puerto Varas in Chile. Rail to Santiago.
SANTIAGO — Visit the capital of Chile in a beautiful setting at the foot of the Andes, and excursion by train to the fashionable ocean resort of Vina del Mar, near Valparaiso. Leave Santiago by plane over the nitrate fields of Chile to Lima.
LIMA — Visit "The Beautiful City of the Kings" founded by Pizzaro, one time capital of Spanish America. You will drive through historic squares, past busy markets, visiting the great Cathedral with Pizzaro's remains, the Spanish-Moorish house of the Torre Tagle family, and San Marcos University, claiming to be the oldest in the Americas. After admiring the marvelous collection of'Inca and pre-Inca relics in the National Museum, drive through fashionable residential suburbs.
BALBOA — Leave by plane for the Canal Zone, visiting Panama City and the locks.
