Sleek, Comfortable Aircraft that Cruises at More Than 300 Miles an Hour
Years of intensive research went into the building of the Lockheed Constellation aircraft that Pan American Airways has placed in service on many of its world routes.
The Constellation became the first modern, pressurized transport for high-altitude, over-the-weather flying. Its pressurizing meant that air pressure inside the cabin was regulated to maintain “sea-level” pressure, even at altitudes as high as 22,000 feet. And the Constellation’s advanced design and construction meant that it could carry the heaviest load the longest distance in the shortest time, of any transport in the skies.
Today, the Constellation-type Clipper, with a cruising speed of over 300 miles per hour, is one of the most outstanding of modem aircraft. Its sleek, fishshaped fuselage and distinctive triple-finned tail are the distinguishing marks of the Constellation s exterior. Inside, its luxuriously appointed cabin, and extra-roomy lounge-chair-type seats, provide the utmost flying comfort for passengers.
