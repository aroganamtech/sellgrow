World's Largest Aviation Maintenance Plant
Within Miami's huge International Airport, gateway to all of Latin America, lies PAA's Miami Overhaul Base .... 50 acres of service and maintenance facilities.
Here, where half a million Clipper passengers and more than 1 5 million pounds of Clipper Cargo pass each year, you will find the equipment, the skilled engineers, mechanics, and inspectors that keep the Clippers flying on schedule.
Aircraft from the far-flung, globe-circling routes of Pan American World Airways periodically arrive at this giant maintenance
base for COMPLETE CHECK-UP AND SERVICING... ON FREQUENT, INFLEXIBLE SCHEDULES, with the result that PAA can (and does) assure you utmost dependability every swift air-mile of the way!
Comprehensive, rigidly adhered-to schedules of major overhaul are also programmed for the great aircraft. Engines, wings, control surfaces, landing gear, instruments — all the components — are removed and sent to their special Inspection and Repair Departments. After reassembly the Clippers are better than new . . . having incorporated all the latest improvements
and modifications suggested by operating experience. All overhaul and service schedules are developed through the cooperation of the plane manufacturers, PAA and U. S. A. government agencies.
Check and test — this is the constant program of skilled maintenance work on every Clipper at M. O. B. Truly this largest of all PAA operations at Miami contributes an outstanding share in the achievement of Pan American's goal — to make PAA THE BEST WAY IN THE WORLD TO TRAVEL ANYWHERE IN THE WORLD!
