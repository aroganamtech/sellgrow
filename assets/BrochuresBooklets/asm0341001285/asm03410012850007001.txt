The World’s Most Advanced Medium Range Aircraft
Twin-engined, with 40-passenger capacity, the 300-mile-per-hour Clipper CV-240, built by Consolidated Vultee, operates over PAA’s medium distance routes. This Clipper is a beauty, with “clean” lines and graceful proportions.
The Clipper CV-240 is noted among aircraft for its maneuverability, its fuel economy, and the efficiency of its engine cooling system.
In addition to these points, appreciated by pilots and engineers, here are a few of the features that have made PAA’s Clipper CV-240 a great favorite with passengers over the routes it flies: The extra-big, 16-by-18 inch windows, which allow utmost visibility . . . the roomy, recline-to-your-comfort seats, upholstered in- spun-glass cushioning.
A finger-tip touch adjusts them to any position.
Southward to Latin America and the romantic islands of the Caribbean, PAA Clippers CV-240 soar, serving key points on the main arterial routes of Pan American’s global network!
