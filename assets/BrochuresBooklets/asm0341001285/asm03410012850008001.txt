High Altitude, High Speed Luxury Airliner
The range of the DC-6 makes it a logical choice for transportation on far-flung routes of the Pan American World Airways system. The traveling public likes the DC-6 because of its roomy, finely appointed interior ... the complete air conditioning and pressurizing system . . more than 300-mph cruising speed.
When you board your DC-6 for an overnight trip, you may either relax in one of the comtortable reclining seats forward, or tuck snugly into one of eight over-size berths aft.
For your daytime trip, there are wide lounge chairs arranged just two abreast so that both you and your neighbor have plenty of elbow room. Meals are served on your own individual table — a sturdy metal table that locks securely into the chair arms.
The luxury DC-6 El Inter Americano flight operates DAILY over PAA’s route from Miami to Panama City, thence over Pan American-Grace Airways’ routes along the West Coast of South America to Buenos Aires.
