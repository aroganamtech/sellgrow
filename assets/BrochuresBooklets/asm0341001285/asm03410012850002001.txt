Progress Through Experience
It’s experience that counts! Since 1927 —when Pan American Airways made its inaugural flight carrying U.S. mail from Key West, Florida, to Havana, Cuba — through today, 23 years later, as Pan American girdles the globe on regular daily schedules, PAA has had two decades of experience in maintaining and operating its giant Clipper fleet — the finest in the world!
Throughout the years, practical operating procedures, plus the experience and skill of the leading manufacturers of modern commercial aircraft, have enabled PAA to add to its great fleet the latest, most highly developed passenger-carrying luxury Clippers the world has ever known.
Built according to Pan American’s
own specifications, today’s high-speed, long-range, “above-the-weather” fleet wings tons of Clipper Cargo, mail, and millions of passengers on PAA’s far-flung routes every minute of the day and night, with comfort and dependability beyond comparison!
Pan American has many “famous firsts’’ to its credit in the field of commercial aviation. PAA’s latest “firsts” are the luxurious double-decked Clippers that today are flying between New York and Europe . . . between New York and Bermuda . . . between California an d H awaii, the Philippines and Japan. With these “Queens of the Sky” PAA presents an achievement in luxury travel unequaled by any other form of transportation since the world began!
