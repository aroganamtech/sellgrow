Clipper Edition
Famous Columnists
Walter Lippmann, Henry McLemore, Samuel Grafton, Walter Winchell, Drew Pearson, Westbrook Pegler, Sumner Welles, Harold Ickes, Ernie Hill, Jimmie Fidler, Elizabeth Woodward . . they all write for The Clipper Edition. These columnists offer a cross-section story daily of North America for the English-speaking residents of Latin America.
Popular Comics
The array of comics includes Dick Tracy, Blondie, Abbie & Slats, Jiggs, L’il Abner, Rip Kirby, Jane Arden, Smilin’ Jack, Terry & The Pirates, Moon Mullins, Brenda Starr, Buz Sawyer and a host of other comic panels. Add to this, such features as a daily bridge column, Horse Sense, Dr. Brady’s Health Column and you have two pages of comics and features daily. On Sunday, 16 pages!
Shopping News, Fashions
More and more, the women of Latin America look to The Herald as their shopping guide. The women’s department keeps them posted on what is new, what it costs, and where it can be purchased. Telephone responses to the shopping columns have been received from as far as Guatemala, Central America. This is but one example of the unusual pulling power of The Herald features.
