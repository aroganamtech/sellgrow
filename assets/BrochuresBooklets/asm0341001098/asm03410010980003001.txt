LOCATION . . . Bolivia, near the center of South America, is the only country except Paraguay without a seacoast. While much of the country consists of low plains, which are humid and hot, the western section, where the principal cities are located, are large pleateaus.
CHARACTERISTICS ... La Paz is over 12,000 feet above sea level. The city itself is in the bottom of a deep, narrow gorge entirely surrounded by mountains. It is a city of steep, steep streets with houses built on inclined surfaces. The airport is on a plateau high above the city.
POPULATION. . . Estimated 3,922,000 in 1948, the population is about the same as that of Chicago and Tacoma combined.
SIZE . . . Estimated 500,000 square miles, about the size of Texas and California combined.
CAPITAL... La Paz, with about 400,000 inhabitants, slightly larger than Rochester.
GOVERNMENT ... A republic with a President, Senate and a House of Deputies.
HOW TO GET THERE ... By Pan American and Panagra to La Paz, 15 hours (elapsed time) from Miami; ,21 hours from New York. By Panagra 3 V2 hours from Lima, Peru. Cochabamba is only about an hour’s flying time from La Paz. By ship from New York. 14 days to Mollendo, Peru; then about 46 hours by rail and iake steamer to La Paz.
