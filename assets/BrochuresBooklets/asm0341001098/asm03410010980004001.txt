ACCOMMODATIONS . . . Best hotel is Sucre Palace, where rates start at $3 single, European Plan. It is advisable to get confirmed reservations in advance. More modest are the Austria and the Paris. There are also several pensions, such as the Pension Cuellar and Pension Brecher. Pension Ormachea is new, luxurious, comfortable.
ARTS • • - The Tiahuanaco Museum houses a significant collection of pre-Inca relics and is well worth a visit. Check for hours.
BALLET • • • There is no local ballet company, but occasionally visiting troupes stop here.

CALENDAR OF HOLIDAYS ...New Year’s Day; Carnival, usually in February; April 9, Easter; May 1, Labor Day; Corpus Christi in June; August 5, 6, 7, National Festival; October 12, National Anniversary of the Discovery of America; November 1, 2, All Saints and All Souls Days; Christmas Day; various local holidays.
CIGARETTES, CIGARS, PIPES, TOBACCO . There are American cigarettes available in a few places. The tourist may bring 100 cigarettes or 20 cigars or 2 pipes and 2 tins of tobacco.
CLIMATE A dry, sunny mountain climate extremely pleasant during the dry season. Some foreigners find the high altitude difficult for the first few days. They are advised to walk as little as possible and to hire cabs for any destination. The weather is cool all year around and cold in winter.
CLUBS • • • Anglo-American Club; Club Alemán, Círculo Francés and Círculo Peruano. Club de la Paz, Club Tennis La Paz, Club Tennis Sucre, Club de Caza y Pezca, Yacht Club, Rotary International, Lion’s Club, Automóvil Club, Club 16 de Julio, Club Arabe, Club Social Chileno. Mallasilla and Los Pinos Golf Clubs. Cards available. (Inquire at your hotel.)
COMMUNICATIONS ... All America Cables maintains an office on Calle Socabaya. There is also West Coast Cables office. By CIRBOL you can have long-distance telephone communications.
CURRENCY • • ■ The Boliviano is the unit of exchange and the rate frequently varies. Dollar exchange for tourists is at a free-market rate.
CUSTOMS REGULATIONS AND DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR UNITED STATES CITIZENS • • • Passport necessary, but no visa for tourist visits up to 90 days. Health and vaccination certificates necessary. Personal effects such as cameras and typewriters are allowed, but must be declared when entering. For all other articles, passengers must secure special permission. One bottle of liquor allowed duty free. See also cigarettes.
DRUG STORES • ■ • Almost all have American products.
ELECTRIC CURRENT ...Generally 110 volts, 50 cycles, a.c.; 220 volts, a.c., is also available. Hotels are generally adapted for U. S.-type plugs.
