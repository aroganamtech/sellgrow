TRIP TIPS and GENERAL INFORMATION
What the rates include:
AIR TRANSPORTATION—Between New York and the cities in Europe as indicated in the itineraries, based on Pan American World Airways transatlantic Clipper service.
Included in Tour Price:
SURFACE TRAVEL—Second Class on trains in Europe. First Class on river, lake or Channel steamers with berths reserved in minimum staterooms for night journeys.
HOTEL ACCOMMODATIONS—Consisting of a bed in twin-bedded room, with private bath where available, at carefully selected hotels.
MEALS—Continental (Plain) breakfast only, will be provided while at hotels. (In Great Britain the breakfast will be English style).
SIGHTSEEING—Membership provided in standard local excursions: generally by motorcoach, except in Heidelberg where private automobile sightseeing is provided.
TRANSFERS—By taxi or hotel conveyance between city air terminals, railroad stations, and hotels, or vice-versa.
TAXES AND GRATUITIES—As levied locally by governmental or municipal authorities except in Great Britain where gratuities are to be settled directly.
The rates do not include:
Transportation other than that shown in the itineraries; cost of passport, visas, laundry or other items of personal nature; wines, mineral waters and other beverages such as tea and coffee, when not included in the regular menus; the cost of meals, automobiles, guides, sightseeing and other services not specified in the itinerary; cost of private automobile sightseeing tours substituted for motorcoach sightseeing tours where, due to the season, motorcoach tours are not operated; fees, tips to porters for baggage handling; meals while en route or on sightseeing tours in Europe. Cable charges incurred in securing reservations will be for the client’s account.
