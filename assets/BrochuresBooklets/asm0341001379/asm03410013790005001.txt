If the optional collision damage waiver is not taken, a larger Security Deposit up to the deductible amount of the collision insurance may be required. The deposit, less applicable charges, will be refunded at the termination of the rental. See detailed rate information on page 22.
•	Full refund will be made for cancellations received more than 15 days prior to depature from home city. A $10 cancellation fee will be charged on cancellations received within the 15 day period.
•	No refund will be made on unused days on a one week Bargain Car Rental. For rentals exceeding one week, full refund less a $10 cancellation fee will be made on unused days. Refund days are determined by the difference between the days of actual usage (24 hour units or any portion thereof) and the number of days covered by the prepayment.
