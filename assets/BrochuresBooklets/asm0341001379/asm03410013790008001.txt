European Key City Plan for One-Way Rentals
The Key City Plan for One-Way rentals allows you to pick up a car in one city (and country) and return it to another city (and country) without incurring a drop-off charge provided that—
•	the pick-up and return cities are in the same zone.
•	the rental is at least two weeks duration.
•	the car group is either A, B, C, or D.
Rental charges and tax for the country of pick-up will apply and are those shown on the appropriate country page. All rates are based on Unlimited Free Mileage.
The three separate zones with participating cities are as follows—
ZONE 1 Portugal and Spain (Portugal to Spain only)
Lisbon, Barcelona, Madrid, Malaga
ZONE 2 Germany and Switzerland
Berlin, Bonn, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Hanover, Munich, Stuttgart, Basel, Geneva, Zurich ZONE 3 Belgium, France and Holland
Antwerp, Brussels, Rotterdam, Amsterdam, Bordeaux, Cannes, Lille, Lyons, Marseille, Nice, Paris, Strasbourg, Toulouse
Inter-Country rentals between cities not covered by the Key City Plan or, for rentals of less than two weeks duration or, for cars in groups other than A, B, C, or D would be charged at the normal rate for the country of pick-up plus the applicable drop-off charge. The drop-off charge may be estimated at approximately 20<r per kilometer (1.6 km to 1 mi) based on the distance between the two cities. The actual drop-off charge would be dependent upon the specific cities involved, and the length of the rental. It may be requested from the rental company at the originating city at the time of booking.
