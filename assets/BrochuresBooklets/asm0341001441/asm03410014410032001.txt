November 15, 1945	Pacific Division's wartime contract with Navy terminated.
November 29, 1945	Pacific and Alaska Divisions consolidated.
January-March, 1946	South and Central Pacific routes resurveyed, preparatory to resumption of peacetime schedules.
April 18, 1946	Last of flying boat Clippers retired. Landplane service between San Francisco, Los Angeles and Honolulu inaugurated with Constellation Clippers.
June 1, 1946	Service resumed to New Zealand.
August 4, 1946	CAB grants PAA permission to establish service to Japan, China, Australia, Dutch East Indies, Straits Settlement and India.
September 16, 1946	Service resumed to Manila.
October 21, 1946	DC-4 Clipper leaves San Francisco on 25,000-mile survey flight of new routes.
March 17, 1947	Clipper service to Sydney inaugurated.
June 3, 1947	Service established to Shanghai.
June 17, 1947	Clipper America leaves New York on flight over round-the-world routes of Atlantic and Pacific-Alaska Divisions.
September 24, 1947	Service inaugurated to Tokyo and Hong Kong and thus around the world.
November 24, 1948	Service inaugurated between Portland and Seattle and Honolulu.
January 21, 1949	First Boeing 377 double-decked Strato Clippers delivered. (Regular Strato Clipper service to Honolulu began April 1).
June 30, 1950	PAA is first airline to enter Pacific airlift with start of war in Korea.
