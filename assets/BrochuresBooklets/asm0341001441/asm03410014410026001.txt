Another significant pioneering project during 1952 was the inauguration by PAA of low-cost tourist fares between the Pacific Coast and Hawaii. Pan American had been a pioneer in this idea - President Trippe having proposed such fares as long ago as 1943 - and in 1948 it had been introduced between New York and Puerto Rico.
In the Pacific, success of this economical form of travel was assured from the start and was reflected immediately in increased traffic to and from the Islands. And this success played no small part in the decision of an International Air Transport Association traffic meeting in Honolulu in 1953 to extend these fares throughout the Pacific area.
