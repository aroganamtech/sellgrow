July 12, 1940 December 7, 1941
Pan American Airways establishes Pacific Division, with Colonel Clarence M. Young as manager. Headquarters base established at Alameda, California.
Steamer North Haven leaves San Francisco to establish Pacific island bases.
Pan American Clipper (Sikorsky S-42B) makes exploratory flights to Honolulu, Midway, Wake and Guam.
China Clipper leaves Alameda on first trans-Pacific air mail flight to Honolulu and Manila.
Trans-Pacific air express service started.
Regular trans-Pacific passenger service inaugurated to Hawaii, Guam and Manila.
South Pacific survey flight to New Zealand (via Pago Pago). Air mail and express service extended to China (Hong Kong). First trans-Pacific passenger flight to China.
Pacific Division headquarters moved to Treasure Island in San Francisco Bay.
First of fleet of new Boeing 314 flying boat Clippers delivered to Treasure Island.
Service to New Zealand inaugurated.
Pearl Harbor attacked. PAA goes on wartime basis, under contract with U.S. Navy.
