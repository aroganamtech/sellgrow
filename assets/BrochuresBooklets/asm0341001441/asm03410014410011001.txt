The growing threat to U.S. national security prevented realization of the program which Pan American had built around the Lockheed Constellations. The manufacturer of the Constellations had to defer work on these aircraft in favor of more urgent military requirements, so Pan American relinquished its contract for them to the Army.
The fall of France, the entrance of Italy into the war and the closing of the Mediterranean led to increases in traffic by the latter part of 1940 and early 1941 beyond any normal growth. Traffic between the Far East, the Netherlands Indies and New Zealand, which had formerly moved via India and the Middle East, was now diverted across the Pacific and thence across the United States and the Atlantic.
