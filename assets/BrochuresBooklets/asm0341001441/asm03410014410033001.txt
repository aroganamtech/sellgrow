December 5, 1952	Service inaugurated to Rangoon.
November 18, 1952	Clipper Sovereign of the Skies makes first commercial flight in Pacific jet stream, thus inaugurating regular winter nonstop flights between Tokyo and Honolulu.
December 12, 1952	PAA inaugurates low-cost tourist fares between Pacific Coast terminals and Honolulu.
May 26, 1953	Service inaugurated to Saigon.
April 1, 1954	Rainbow tourist fares extended to all points in Pacific. Pacific-Alaska Division introduces dual President lujcary and Rainbow tourist service on Pacific Clippers.
October 13, 1955	PAA orders fleet of 45 jet Clippers for $269-million - first airline to do so.
