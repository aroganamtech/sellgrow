HISTORIC HIGHLIGHTS IN ALASKA
1931	Colonel Lindbergh surveys air route to Orient via Alaska for PAA.
1932	PAA enters Alaskan field, buying Alaskan Airways and Pacific International Airways.
1933-35	Developmental period: PAA pioneers radio and weather reporting system, constructs airports, develops winter flying aids, establishes basic trunk routes.
1935	Fairbanks-Juneau service inaugurated with twin-engine Lockheed Electras.
1940	Alaska linked with continental U.S. with Sikorsky S-42B flying boat.
1941	First Douglas DC-3 goes into operation of Seattle-Ketchikan-Juneau-Fairbanks route.
1942-44	PAA serves with the U.S. Navy, pushing westward into the Aleutians and north to Pt. Barrow.
1945-46	Returning to full-time civilian operations, PAA institutes special cargo rates for furs from Alaska and fresh foods to the Territory.
1947	Four-engine DC-4s go into operation on PAA's Alaskan routes.
1954	High-speed, high-altitude Super Six (Douglas DC-6B) Clippers placed in service.
1955	Round-trip fares slashed up to 10 per cent.
