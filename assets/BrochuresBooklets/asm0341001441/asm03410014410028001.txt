Sketch of Boeing 707 Jet Transport. Twenty of these 575-mile-an-hour Clippers were ordered by Pan American
The new jet Clippers were to be placed in trans-Pacific, trans-Atlantic, and Latin-American service in the five-month period beginning December 1958.
The investment in jet Clippers was $269-million, Mr. Trippe said, and the new Clippers, when delivered in 1961, would double the capacity of the Pan American fleet.
Mr. Trippe said that the jet Clippers would provide a new high standard in speed and passenger comfort. Vibration and noise caused by propellers would be eliminated, he said. There would be complete absence of all vibration and the sound of the jet turbine engines would be audible only to passengers in the very after part of the cabin. The new aircraft were to be equipped with radar.
Ground noise would be lessened by jet turbine silencers, and would make little more noise on the ground than piston-engined planes.
