By establishing service to 68 nations and territories, Pan American World Airways, preeminent in international air transportation, has for 19 years been serving the cause of international friendship and cooperation. In spanning the seas and oceans of the world this international airline has also helped to span the seas of misunderstanding and the even-wider oceans of ignorance that tend to
separate nations. The wings of the Flying Clippers have become symbols of peace and friendship.
From the 90-mile airline of 1927 spanning the straits between Key West and Havana, Pan American World Airways has vastly expanded its services with the increased demands for dependable international air transportation.
