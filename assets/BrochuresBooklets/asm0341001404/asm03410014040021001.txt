	
	WHY PAN AM? Pan Am carries more passengers to and from the United States than any other airline. How can the exceptional popularity of this particular airline be explained? The frequent schedules to all six continents? The varied routings, alternate gateways, choice of services? The wonderful food? The superb service? The personable and expert personnel? They’re all important. But perhaps even more important is that priceless extra that only Pan Am can offer.. . . the priceless extra of experience. Your group couldn’t be in more experienced hands. Pan Am has carried more than 30,000,000 passengers overseas. Every Pan Am Jet Clipper captain has flown at least a million miles . . . many have flown several million. The fact of the matter is that experience is reflected in everything that Pan Am does. You’ll find it first in the offices of your authorized Pan Am Travel Agent and your Pan Am District Representative when you seek their help in getting your common-interest group trip off the ground, when you call on their resources in setting up your incentive travel program, when you ask their advice in planning your overseas convention. You’ll discover it in the knowhow and cooperation and friendliness of their staffs. Later, you’ll feel it in the expertness and courtesy of Pan Am people on the telephone and in the ticket offices. You’ll see it in the courteous efficiency of the flight attendants who serve you. And, you’ll find Pan Am experience reflected, too, in the meticulously careful maintenance of your Jet Clipper, and in the precisely plotted routings that help make your flight smooth and serene all the way. Experience — what’s why Pan Am. Pan Am is the world’s most experienced airline.
