A PART OF PAN AM
Intercontinental is a Pan Am organization. You can make reservations for your group at any Intercontinental Hotel at any Pan Am office, or through your authorized Pan Am Travel Agent.
There are 19 Intercontinental Hotels currently in operation — on all 6 continents. Others are scheduled to open soon.
No two Intercontinental Hotels are alike. Each is unique, is partially owned and completely staffed by local people, accurately and distinctly reflects its particular geographic setting.
Yet all Intercontinental Hotels offer ultramodern accommodations, a wide variety of recreational facilities, the convenience of multilingual service, close proximity to a notable business and cultural center, the area’s best meeting facilities and most competent and courteous staff.
For more information, see the Pan Am office nearest you or your authorized Pan Am Travel Agent.
WHAT IS A JET CLIPPER
Unlike anything else. Comfortable. Quiet. Smooth. So smooth, you’ll find it hard to believe you’re traveling at speeds up to 600 miles an hour . . . particularly when game pieces don’t move until you move them.
You converse in soft, normal tones, completely relaxed, barely aware of the thrust of powerful jet engines. It’s so different from other modes of travel you may find it hard to believe at first . . . certainly hard to forget.
Sink back in the foam-soft reclining chair. Daydream about your destination. If you want anything . . . press a button. Gracious cabin attendants are standing by. Then a welcome hot meal . . . delicious . . . deftly served . . . and complimentary, of course. (And, if you want to, you can even stroll around.)
You’ll find it easy to imagine you’re relaxing at home, in your most comfortable easy chair. One caution, if you choose to read or drift off to sleep, don’t pick a long book or plan a lengthy nap, for in just a few short hours or less you’ll be stepping off the plane ... as much as half a world away.
