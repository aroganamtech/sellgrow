TRAVEL ABROAD...
THE IDEAL GROUP ACTIVITY
If a trip abroad for your group sounds like too ambitious a project — read on — you won’t think so when you’ve finished these pages.
In fact, today, all over the country, the group travel idea has organizations just like yours on the go. Every year Pan Am takes more groups to more places all over the globe. And no wonder!
After all, most groups are formed for some special purpose. Members have a mutual interest — something they can do more effectively and enjoyably together than separately. The group travel idea simply extends this reasoning to another (and often related) area. Travel, too, is something that you get more out of — and have more fun doing — when you’re with a group of congenial companions.
So, if this is the year your group wants to do something different and exciting . . . something that’s fun yet worthwhile, unusual, yet completely practical . . . something that’s wonderful to look forward to, to experience, and to remember, travel abroad together can be for you, as it has been for so many organizations, an ideal activity.
WHERE CAN WE GO?
... HOW MUCH WILL IT COST?
You can travel almost anywhere in the world by Pan American Jet Clippers. They fly ’round the world — to all six continents — to scores of countries and islands across the globe.
How much it will cost depends on where you go. But your group members will be pleasantly surprised to learn how little group travel can cost.
See the preceding section on Fares and Destinations for more specific details on both these points . . . and see your authorized Travel Agent or nearest Pan American office for additional information. Either will be happy to help you set up a trip abroad that’s well within the limits of your group’s travel budget.
TRAVEL AS A GROUP PROJECT
Part of the enjoyment of a group trip abroad is the fun you’ll have getting ready for it. You can devote meetings to travel films Pan Am will furnish. You can hold classes in the language of any country or countries you plan to visit.
Guest speakers and even members of your group can give talks on the history and background of all the exciting spots you expect to see. You can plan bridge parties, dinners, plays, dances, all sorts of fund-raising activities to raise a general “kitty” to help with your travel expenses. Getting ready for a trip abroad can furnish the reason for all kinds of good times — for months before you go.
