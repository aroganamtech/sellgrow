EUROPE
Qualified groups of as few as 25 people can travel to Europe together, on round-trip Group Fares saving up to $325 each. For example, your group could leave from New York City, arriving London first thing in the morning, enjoy visits to Buckingham Palace and Westminster Abbey, colorful, teeming Soho, the picturesque countryside with its lush greenery and thatch-roofed inns and cottages for a round-trip Jet Clipper fare of only $310 per person, from New York.
Regular Jet Clipper schedules also apply on direct flights to other European cities, including Amsterdam, Barcelona, Brussels, Cologne, Copenhagen, Duesseldorf, Frankfurt, Glasgow, Hamburg, Helsinki, Lisbon, Munich, Nice, Oslo, Paris, Rome, Shannon, Stockholm, Stuttgart, Vienna . . . twenty-two in all. And Pan Am’s Extra Cities Bonus Plan allows visits to any number of places en route to and from your ultimate destination.
Effective dates: to Europe, all year except June 24 to July 7 and weekends during May, June and July. From Europe, all year except August 25 to September 7 and weekends during August, September and October.

Generally speaking, hotel facilities are excellent in major cities. However, if your organization wishes to hold a large convention in Europe •—far-in-advance planning is necessary to be sure of adequate accommodations. Mediumsized or small convention groups are not as apt to have reservations problems. Convention sites well worth considering — with these limitations in mind — include London, Paris, Nice, Cannes, Rome, Madrid, Lisbon, Estoril, Frankfurt, Duesseldorf, Munich, Stuttgart, Vienna, Stockholm, Copenhagen, Amsterdam, Brussels. Geneva, historically the meeting place of diplomats and statesmen, is one of the most sophisticated convention sites in the world.
ACROSS THE PACIFIC
Now qualified groups of 25 or more persons can travel round trip or one way across the Pacific — at substantial savings: of as much as $150 compared to regular Jet economy one way fares ... as much as $340 over usual round trip fares! Fascinating destinations available at drastically reduced round trip or one way fares include Hong Kong, Manila, Taipei, Okinawa, Seoul and Tokyo ... at round trip fares only, Djakarta, Palembang, Singapore, Bangkok, and Saigon . . . via either Hawaii or the direct “Great Circle Route” to Japan.
Effective dates: year ’round, except that travel from West Coast gateway cities may not be begun during the months of March, April, September and October. This time restriction does not apply to return travel.
In addition, groups of 15 or more persons can now travel round trip from West Coast points to Sydney for only $744.80 — per person, Jet economy.
Tokyo is fast becoming one of the world’s most attractive convention sites. As a light manufacturing center of world importance, Hong Kong is of particular interest to trade and commercial groups. All the comforts of home are available in cosmopolitan Sydney and sedate Melbourne. Also worth considering are Rangoon, Bangkok, Saigon, Singapore and Djakarta. New modern hotels exist or are under construction throughout these areas.
