GROUP TRAVEL: MORE FUN, LESS EXPENSE
When you’re a group traveler, you’ve congenial companionship . . . you feel free and easy because you’re traveling with people you know and like. You see all the wonderful sights together . . . enjoy them much more because you see them through many pairs of eyes. You share in the experiences and unexpected adventures of your companions . . . and have so much to talk about. And all you have to do is enjoy yourself.
Today, group travel via Pan Am,
is not only more fun — but much less expensive, too. Pan Am’s new World-Wide Group Fares, for example, offer tremendous savings to many destinations all over the world. And now, for the first time, traveling group substantially lowers the cost of a round-the-world trip, too. These and other Pan Am group travel bargains are good news for travel-minded common-interest groups, overseas convention organizers and incentive program planners everywhere.
