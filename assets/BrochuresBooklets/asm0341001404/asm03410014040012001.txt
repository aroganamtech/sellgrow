WHAT JET CLIPPER TRAVEL MEANS TO YOUR CONVENTION PLANNING ...
Remember when “Convention” automatically meant Atlantic City, Miami, or maybe New York or Chicago? When holding a convention in a spot like Bermuda or Hawaii was practically unheard of?
Times have changed. Today, overseas conventions and sales meetings are commonplace, thanks to fast-moving developments in air travel . . . and dramatic improvement in facilities and accommodations abroad.
Pan American Jet Clipper speed virtually halves
the time necessary to fly to a specific destination. But the corollary is just as important for convention planners : Jet Clipper speed virtually doubles the distance you can travel in a specific time.
Consequently, you find a growing list of organizations taking advantage of the ever-widening circle of accessible destinations... and planning conventions, conferences and sales meetings not only in Puerto Rico, Jamaica or Nassau . . . but in Paris, Rome, Rio or even Tokyo!
