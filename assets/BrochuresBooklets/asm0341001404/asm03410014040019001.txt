9	Personal funds: explain which personal expenses are considered part of the prize, which are not. Facts on travelers’ checks, money-changing, rates of exchange.
10	Shopping: destination bargains. Explain duties, customs, etc.
11	Language: supply phrase books if needed. Although English is understood by practically all hotel people, taxi drivers, waiters, etc.
12	Places of worship: denominations, locations, accessibility, times, etc.
13	List of winners: names, home towns, hotel room numbers when practicable, etc. Names and titles of company officials making the trip. List helps winners get acquainted, makes a fine memento.
