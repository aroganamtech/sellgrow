Maintenance personnel at Pan Am are constantly training and re-training to be kept up to the minute in the rapidly changing technology of the jet age. Pan Am mechanics spend at least one month of every year going to school—either to learn about new equipment and design or to take refresher courses. Each mechanic has an investment of about $700 in his own tools. One of every four Pan Am mechanics holds some type of FAA license .
Another reason why Pan American is known as the world’s most experienced airline.
The motto of all Pan Am maintenance men is: “Passengers and crew stake their lives on your work; don’t let them down.”
A QUARTER-MILLION DOLLARS WORTH OF POWER in this Jet Clipper engine helps carry Pan American passengers swiftly and safely all over the world. Each plane has four such engines. A crew of technical experts at the hangar makes sure these giant powerhouses are humming perfectly.
