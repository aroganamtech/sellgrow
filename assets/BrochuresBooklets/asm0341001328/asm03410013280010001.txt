Pan American’s Hangar 14 is the largest installation at Kennedy International Airport. The 740-foot-long structure is expertly designed for the maintenance of the Jet Clipper Fleet. Eight Clippers can be parked on the spacious floor, while another 14 can be positioned outside.
The hangar’s cantilever roof is supported by 176 cables, each 21A inches thick. This leaves the floor free of pillars. The hangar floor contains radiant heating, with snow-melting coils installed in the doorway tracks.
Incidentally . .. Pan Am is the only airline to have its main base operations at Kennedy.
