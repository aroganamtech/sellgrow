DOUGLAS DC-8
Pan Am operates a large fleet of Douglas DC-8 Jet Clippers, which are powered by the same Pratt & Whitney JT-4 engines as the 707-300 Intercontinental. The dimensions are approximately the same, as the 707-300 aircraft. Maximum takeoff weight of the DC-8 is 315,000 pounds.
BOEING CARGO CLIPPER
Pan Am carries more international air freight than any other airline in the world, most of it in the cavernous interiors of its Boeing 707-321C Cargo Clippers. These aircraft can carry as much as 40 tons of cargo across the Atlantic non-stop. They are powered by the same fan-jet engines which power their passenger-carrying sister ships, the 707-320B’s. The Cargo Clippers are readily convertible from all-cargo to all-passenger or part cargo/part passenger service. The Cargo Clippers, and all intercontinental-range Pan Am Jet Clippers, are at the disposal of the U.S.government in times of emergency.
