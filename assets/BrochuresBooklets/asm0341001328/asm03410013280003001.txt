At Pan American World Airways safety first is the most important rule for every employee, whether a pilot or member of a flight crew, a technical specialist or a mechanic. Pan Am thinks first about its passengers at all times and in many ways . . . their comfort and pleasure, their convenience, and the many other big and small extras that make flying aboard a Jet Clipper the wonderful experience it always is. But before any of these things, Pan American is concerned with safety.
It’s a big job making sure the giant Jet Clippers are always in top shape to carry Pan Am passengers and crew safely all over the world.
The key to safety is aircraft maintenance. Experience has shown that where maintenance rules and performance are strictly enforced, airplanes are safer, more efficient and better able to serve passengers. Pan American, the world’s most experienced airline, spends more than $7,000 every hour of every day to provide the most modern and efficient maintenance for its Clipper Fleet.
