Pan Am has two sprawling hangars at Kennedy, where its maintenance men use some of the most modern mechanical and technical equipment available — equipment worth more than a million dollars—-to help keep the Clippers flying.
More than 500 manuals give Pan Am maintenance men directions for upkeep, repair and testing of every working system and every part —from powerful jet engines to wheels, from seat belts to the rich carpet on the floors —on every one of the Pan Am fleet of well over a hundred jet and piston planes.
More than 45 separate shops operate in the hangars to provide a wide-ranging list of services, such as engine overhaul, metalworking, paint and body repair, plating, fabric and equipment work, radio and electrical testing and repair, wheel and tire replacement, welding, fuel system maintenance, and many others.
A total of 337 vehicles are used to keep things moving at Hangar 14, where eight huge Jet Clippers can be parked on the hangar floor at one time, and at Hangar 16 where Pan Am piston (propeller] aircraft (used mainly for cargo-hauling] are kept in top working order.
