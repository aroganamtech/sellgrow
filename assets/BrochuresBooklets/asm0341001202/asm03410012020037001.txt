ANY MORE QUESTIONS?
Luggage: Keep it simple and not too many pieces. Remember that there will be a great deal of car loading and unloading. Wash-wear clothing is the thing — and if you’re doing much driving your clothing needs are reduced. Take warm things though — Europeans are much less sensitive to cold rooms and drafty hotel lounges than you are. A duffle or catch-all bag is handy to have along, also.
You may want a luggage rack and protective tarpaulin for your car. Auto procurement agencies can supply these (make arragements in advance).
Map Case: It’s a good idea to have some sort of zippered case to hold your “travel library” of guide books, maps, vocabularies, etc. Take it with you — or buy it over there.
Picnic Hardware: You’ll want a few simple accessories to make picnicking easy. Plastic plates and cups, eating utensils, a knife for cutting cheese and bread, napkins, paper towels, a bottle opener and corkscrew, and a simple container for all. Europeans love picnics — you’ll have no trouble finding these things in stores abroad.
