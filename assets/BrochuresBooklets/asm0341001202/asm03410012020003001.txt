INTRODUCTION TO THE EXPERIENCE OF A LIFETIME
This booklet is written to those who have never considered driving in Europe — or who, having considered it, have resisted the idea because of niggling doubts. How would it be in a strange country? Won’t I get lost? What about roads, and language, and currency? What about accommodations? Won’t it be expensive?
These are natural questions. Yet there’s no need to be land-locked to too-familiar places and scenery when all you need is authoritative assurance that the planning of a driving trip abroad, and the doing of it, are as simple as American-style apple pie.
It will interest you to know that this booklet was authored by an American who — without earlier experience — went through all the stages of trip planning, followed by a driving tour, in company with his wife, through seven countries, one Principality, and one Grand Duchy of Western Europe — a distance of 7000 miles. Information gained, combined with the experience of many others, is the basis of this booklet.
It is our purpose in publishing this booklet that you may gain from this experience, that you may be convinced that the combined joy of flying to Europe and driving on the Continent is too wonderful an event in life for you to miss.
It is not intended that this booklet be encyclopedic in its information coverage. There are easy ways of getting detailed facts, as the booklet will explain. But if this booklet does save you initial confusion in thinking about a European driving trip, if it does succeed in lifting you from the ordinary and puts you behind the wheel of a smart little foreign car with the cities, castles and countryside that you’ve known in years of dreaming all around you, then we shall be thankful. And so will you.
