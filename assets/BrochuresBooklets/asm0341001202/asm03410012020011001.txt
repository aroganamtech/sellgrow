MAKING ARRANGEMENTS FOR A CAR
There are a number of plans for procuring an automobile, and many car services that specialize in this business — available through your Travel Agent or local dealer. Whether you buy, lease, or rent a foreign car, do some showroom shopping in this country before coming to a decision on make and model. Besides cost, consideration should be given to size (both in respect to luggage, and your own stature perhaps), car appointments and features. It is possible to obtain certain foreign-made cars which are equipped with American-style shifts, if this is your wish.
