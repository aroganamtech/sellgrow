If your trip begins or ends in England, you should know about car ferries to the Continent. There’s a wide point-to-point choice of drive-on channel ferries — to France, Belgium, and the Netherlands. Most exciting and useful are those that go by air — only 20 minutes from Lydd, England to Le Touquet, France, for instance. You travel with your car — and the fare for both car and passengers is most reasonable.
Two suggestions on advance route planning before you see your Travel Agent: You, like most people, probably have certain places in mind that you would like to see. The accompanying fold-out map is provided so that you may use it as a preliminary rough guide in determining routes and distances and driving time required. It will enable you to get a clearer picture of where you might go.
As a next step, you may want to write to the Touring Service Department of one of the major oil companies*, and ask them for a road map of Western Europe. With this map, which shows exact distances between points, you can further refine your route plans.
Then, with this map (or the one enclosed with this booklet) in hand, see your Travel Agent. He can check your figuring, relate your rough plotting to known terrain, points of interest along the way, and places to stay. You will be surprised how well you have done.
