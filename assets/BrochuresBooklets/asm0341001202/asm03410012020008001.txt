IF YOU LIKE DRIVING at HOME YOU’LL LOVE DRIVING in EUROPE
Accept it as fact, driving in Europe is much like driving at home — only vastly more fun. As you will see, in reading the balance of this booklet, there’s little to adjust to.
Some European cars are smaller, but a great many others now correspond in size to U.S. makes. You’ll find that most any European car will be pleasantly responsive and easy to handle. And its safety features may even be ahead of those in the car you drive at home.
There’s no better way to make a strange country wnstrange than to drive in it. There is an intimacy with the countryside that you’ll find most pleasant. Almost instinctively, by your own wish, you set an easy pace. You feel much less the conspicuous tourist — you are, it seems, a European. But even when you ask for directions or a service in fumbling French and thus reveal yourself, you are treated warmly and with good humor. You get the absurd feeling that people like you better, that there is a special communication between you, just because you have chosen to drive in their country, to know it and them better. And when your trip is done, you come away convinced that you have seen places or things that no other tourist has ever seen. And probably this is so.
