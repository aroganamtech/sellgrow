FLY-DRiVE ON BUSINESS, TOO
Driving in Europe serves other purposes than pleasure. Many businessmen rent cars in Europe and make their business rounds, just as they do at home. It’s convenient, economical — often saves time. In the process they enjoy a living experience, a sort of dividend in the pursuit of business duties that is so enjoyable that many are taking their wives along.
If you want to feel reborn in a hurry, if you’ve finally become convinced that this really is one world and that you should enjoy it, or take advantage of it, make your plans for a Fly-Drive trip to Europe now.
