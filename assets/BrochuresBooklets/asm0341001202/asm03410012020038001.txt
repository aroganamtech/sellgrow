Personal Needs: Take cigarettes, tobacco and cigars up to the customs limit. American brands are expensive and often hard to find. But don’t load yourself down with facial tissues, soaps, toothpaste, aspirin, common drugs, and other such supplies. American brands are available in all centers of any importance in Europe, so that you can stock up and not run short when on the road. Granted such supplies cost more overseas — but who’s going to use that much aspirin?
Hotel toilet tissue is unbelievably bad throughout much of Europe, but don’t cram your luggage with it — you can buy good facial tissue quality in countless drug and variety stores abroad.
If you’re fussy about coffee, and don’t like European style, you may want to take some instant coffee with you. Also, “cream” in Europe is generally heated milk, except in the best hotels and restaurants. So if you want to be sure of cream in your coffee, pack a jar of powdered cream as a more satisfactory substitute.
Electrical current varies throughout Europe — plug-in receptacles are different too. Special adapters combining a transformer and Euro-pean-style plug, are available — take one with you.
Many people, when traveling anywhere, suffer mild internal upset due to change in water and food. For protection, arm yourself with one of several good preparations — available in drug stores both here and abroad, or through your doctor.
Medical Care: Excellent hospitals, reliable English-speaking doctors, are found in all centers of any importance throughout Europe.
Film: If you are shooting pictures in color, take film with you up to Customs limit. Color film, while available in cities, is sometimes not easy to find in small towns (where picturetaking is best). It is also expensive.
