"Signpost" — Guide to Great Britain
— Detailed information on Great Britain, covering the cities and byways in similar fashion to Michelin. $3.50 — available from British Book Center, 112 East 55th Street, New York City.
maps of towns and cities showing location of hotels and points of interest (most useful!). Hotels and restaurants are also given, each rated for quality and price. A foolproof reference, and an absolute must for the traveler by road. Cost: France, $1+. Italy, $3.75. Michelin sightseeing guides for other countries and regions are available. It is recommended that you buy Michelin Guides in the U. S. before leaving, since English editions are hard to find in Europe. Available from French and European Publications, 610 Fifth Avenue, New York City, also some other bookstores.
