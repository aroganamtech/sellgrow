And as you sit at home this morning you know that thousands of miles away there’s a pert (did you almost say petite?) car, with your name on it, only waiting for your arrival.
Pan American makes the waiting short, and sweet. When you board your Clipper® you know the dream has really started. There’s unconcealed excitement all about you — a spiritlifting anticipation that even experienced travelers feel when they sink into their cush-ioney Clipper seat and hear the quiet hum of the jet engines as they start. There’s soft music. The stewardesses and the steward get you settled, see that you’re blissfully comfortable — you might be their first passenger, certainly their most important.
