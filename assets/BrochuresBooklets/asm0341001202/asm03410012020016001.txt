PLANNING
YOUR
DRIVING ROUTE
This part of your planning is, of course, highly variable. It depends upon what you want to see, how much time you have (with Jet Clipper travel, you don’t need much), how much your travel piggy bank has in it.
There are a number of possibilities: (1) Flying by Clipper to one city and making driving tours to surrounding areas, returning to your headquarters hotel each night. (2) Capitalizing on Pan Am’s “Extra City” privilege — flying from point to point within Europe and making driving trips around each stop-over city.
(3) Making a continuous driving tour in a grand or medium-size circle and returning to the same point, or starting in one city or country and ending in another. There are advantages in each of these plans.
“EXTRA CITY” STOP-OVER BONUS PLAN
Through this plan you can make important coverage of Europe, within a short time if this is necessary, and at extremely attractive cost. This is because of the bargain aspect of Pan Am’s “Extra City” Bonus Plan privilege for Clipper travelers. Under this plan you buy a round trip ticket to the furthest point you would like to visit. Without any extra fare whatsoever you can stop off in any of many cities en route, and take as long as you like doing it, up to a year. Couple this with a rented car for circle trips in the area of the cities you choose to visit, and you can see you have the makings of the grandest of Grand Tours.
