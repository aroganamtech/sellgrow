CAR LEASE
If your stay is to be one month or more, or if the mileage driven is to be extensive, Car Leasing (or Purchase-Repurchase) will generally be cheaper than rental.
Under the Lease plan, a car is furnished at a flat published price for varying periods of time. Cost examples for European compacts — approximately $315 to $425 for one month, $375 to $550 for two months, $400 to $675 or more for three months. Most leasing companies include the cost of travel documents and insurance in an overall charge — at slightly higher rates. Separately, cost of travel documents can range from about $25 to $65 — insurance, average $45 one month, $60 for two months, $75 three months. You pay for your own gas and oil . . . no limitation on mileage, no mileage charges.
CAR RENTAL
Car Rental in Europe is on two bases (1) a flat charge per day, plus a charge for each mile or kilometer driven (in some cases a daily minimum is required); (2) a flat daily charge with unlimited mileage. Mileage to be driven governs the choice. All necessary touring documents and insurance are included. In most cases oil and maintenance costs are covered, and in some cases, gasoline. In many instances it is possible to pick up a car in one city and leave it in another, without extra charge. Whenever return charges do apply, they are reasonable.
