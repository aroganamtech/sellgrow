WON’T I GET LOST?
Small chance. European roads are better marked than in the U. S. In most of the Continent, in addition to road signs, each kilometer is marked by a roadside stone, bearing the name of the town you have left and the town you are approaching, with kilometer distance to each. On top of this, you will be armed with maps that are exactly as clear as those you use at home, plus guide books.
Traffic and warning signs follow a common system — would that the rest of the world could adopt it. There’s little to learn about these signs — they do not depend on understanding of language, but do their directing through pictorial designs. A few of these are shown here. Your road maps and guides will give them all, also a dash sticker which you will likely get with your car.
