Make it a must to carry with you good vocabularies of all the countries you plan to visit. Be sure they are two-way vocabularies (ex: French-English, English-French). It’s often as necessary to convert a language into English as the reverse — a thing quickly discovered in shops and restaurants. The basic vocabs that you remember from high school or college language courses are the best.
All else failing, French is the most useful foreign language on the Continent. Even a little of it will get you by in Italy, Spain, Germany and other countries. Make it a point, however, to learn certain words or phrases of each country which are commonly needed by the driver (ex: “Left,” “Right,” “Where is,” “How far is it,” “How much,” “Please,” “Thank you” — plus phrases of instruction or request for car service or personal needs).
