Choice of make of car varies between services and between countries. Your Travel Agent can help you in making a selection and reserve your car prior to departure.
Car rental is the best choice if you use Pan Am’s “Extra City” travel privilege, flying from point to point and making circle trips by car around these cities. For the business traveler car rental is usually the obvious method.
A chauffeur (who also serves as a guide) can be arranged through car rental services.
