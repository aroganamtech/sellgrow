HOW TO USE THIS MAP
This map is not a driving map. It is a pleasure plotting map — to help you in reaching an estimate of how far and how long it is to the places you’ve always dreamed of seeing.
Principal roads and major cities only, are shown. The grid of lines superimposed on the map is on a scale that gives you a preliminary means of measuring distance and driving time. These lines are spaced 100 miles (not kilometers) apart. And 100 miles is the average distance that you’d be likely to want to drive in a day.
TYPICAL TRIPS: Please realize that the routings shown under Typical Trips are given only as examples. Trip plans and routings obviously depend upon such things as the nature of the individual, distance driven per day, desire for stopovers at various points, exact time available.
After you have done your own pleasure plotting, take your map to your Travel Agent. He’ll help you in putting the finishing touches on a trip custom-made for you.
