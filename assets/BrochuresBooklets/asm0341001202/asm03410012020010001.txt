HOW TO GO ABOUT PLANNING YOUR TRIP
In introducing this broad subject, first let it be said that the place to go for detailed advice and direct assistance, is your Travel Agent. He can answer questions that this booklet does not attempt to answer due to the varying needs of each individual. At the same time, it is helpful if you do a fair amount of planning before you see your Travel Agent; having some familiarity with the subject, you will be better able to talk with him, and he will be in a better position to advise you. This booklet will help you in your advance planning.
