SO EASY, TOO, TO BRING IT HOME
Through your Travel Agent or local dealer, you can make all arrangements to ship your car home at the same time you purchase it. They can contact a specialized car shipping agent at one of over 40 cities in 19 European countries. Usually your car can be picked up at the airport of departure or your hotel at your convenience. After shipment, delivery can be made to you at the nearest of 28 major ports on the East Coast, or Gulf, West Coast, or Great Lakes ports. Some car shipping firms can arrange delivery to you on your arrival at John F. Kennedy Airport, New York, if you plan to stay in Europe for several weeks after dropping off your car for shipment home.
Or fly it home. Simply contact any Pan Am office in Europe. In a few short hours your car is back at any of Pan Am’s 16 gateway cities throughout the country . . . unloaded in minutes.
