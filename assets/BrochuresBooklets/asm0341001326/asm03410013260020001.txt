Basic map by Richard Edes Harrison
THE map above is NOT just an artist’s conception of proposed postwar air routes. Quite the contrary! It shows Pan American routes—world air transport routes—established and operated before Pearl Harbor (solid and .broken lines).
Over many of these routes Pan American is now flying regular scheduled commercial services despite the war. In addition, over certain routes shown above (and over others not shown) 3,600,000 miles a month have been flown by Pan American for the armed services.
