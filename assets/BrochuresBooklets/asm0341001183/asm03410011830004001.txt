COMMON COURTESIES AND LOCAL CUSTOMS ... “Thank You” (Tak) is the most often heard phrase in Denmark. You shake hands when you meet people and when you leave. Bring flowers when invited to dinner at a private home or send them ahead.
Time is referred to as half of the next hour rather than half past an hour; for example, 6:30 is called “half-seven.”
