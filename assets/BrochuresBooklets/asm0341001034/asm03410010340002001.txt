SCHEDULE OF EVENTS
FRIDAYS
Tennis tournament finals and the awarding of prizes. Gala College Week Dances — Friday Night will be College night at several of Bermuda’s famous Hotels: Elbow Beach Surf Club, Harmony Hall, Castle Harbour, Belmont Manor, and Princess. Join the round of dancing and entertainment specially planned for your enjoyment. Each of the hotels will offer its own distinctive entertainment program.
ACTIVITIES
SAILING — FISHING — SWIMMING — CYCLING — TENNIS — GOLF VOLLEYBALL — PICNICS — DANCING
rugby week: U. S. University teams will compete against Bermuda,

MONDAYS
“College Day at the Beach.” Bring your box lunch. Volleyball tournaments. Prizes. Afternoon dancing to the famous Talbot Brothers and soft drinks on the house. Your hosts: College Week Committee. WEDNESDAYS
Boat trip. All day steamer excursion: Hamilton, Riddells Bay, Great Sound, along North Shore to St. George’s for shopping or sightseeing. Lunch on board. Your host: Bermuda Trade Development Board.
