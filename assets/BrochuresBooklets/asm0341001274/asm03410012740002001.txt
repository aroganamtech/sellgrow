Experienced world travelers know well the performance-proved dependability of the famous DC-4 Clipper.
Tested in transport service under every possible operating condition — improved, then re-tested all over the world by PAA... this famous4-engined Douglas ship has valiantly earned its position as an important member of the giant Pan American fleet.
And today the DC-4 Clipper adds to its fame by offering a versatile trio of specialized PAA services.
Deluxe Sleeperette service provides supremely comfortable reclining chair-beds with foot and leg rests, adjustable for complete relaxation or deep, restful sleep.
Regular two-abreast seating on most first class flights assures maximum comfort and plenty of elbow room.
Economical tourist service, although amazingly kind to your purse, includes operation by the same experienced crews and smiling attendants as those who serve aboard all PAA Clippers.
