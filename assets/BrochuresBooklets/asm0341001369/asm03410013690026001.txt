Spanish vowels have the following sounds: A, as in father; E, sometimes like e, as in met, sometimes like ay, as in ray, or like ai, as in raiment; I, like ee in beet; O is a sound between the English oh and ou, as in ought; Y when used as a vowel, same as the Spanish I. In no case may one vowel be prolonged into a diphthong. Diphthongs are a rapid sequence of the individual sounds, each, however, must be plainly audible. In the combinations ue and ui, when preceded by q or g, the u is silent.
