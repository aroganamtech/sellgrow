Imagine Taking the Whole Family on a Wonderful Vacation Abroad
You probably hoped you might some day but hardly dreamed you could do it so soon. But Pan Am’s wonderful new Family Fare Plan . . . the greatest innovation in the travel industry since Pan Am pioneered low-cost tourist fares . . . permits entire families to fly abroad during “Thrift Season” for little more than the parents alone would pay during summer.
As a result, men who must travel overseas on business can now turn routine trips into never-to-be-forgotten family holidays. Now wives can go along with their husbands on that trip to Europe and parents who’ve looked forward to a European vacation can enjoy it still more by taking the children along.
You Can Use the Plan Throughout the “Thrift Season”
The FAMILY FARE PLAN will apply on trans-Atlantic travel that starts after October 31st and ends be-
fore April 1st. Naturally half the fun will be traveling back and forth together. But you don’t have to. All that’s required is that you start as a single group and travel together as far as your first stopover.
How the Plan Works
You pay the regular fare, but your wife and your children 12-25 years of age go at the greatly reduced rate explained below. Incidentally the Plan doesn’t apply to children under 12 since they always travel at half fare on Pan American . . . and an infant under 2, of course, pays only 10%.
The Plan applies on both Rainbow and President Services. For example, if you choose The Rainbow, you pay the low Thrift Season Round Trip Tourist fare for yourself and save $200 on the fare of each member of your family who qualifies. On The President, you’d pay the Thrift Season Round Trip First Class fare and save $300 on the fare of each family member. On one-way trips, each Family Fare saving would be $130 on The Rainbow . . . $150 on The President.
