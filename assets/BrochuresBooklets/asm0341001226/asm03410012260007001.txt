Instant hotels
And inexpensive, too. The exotic lands around the Pacific make living a very economical activity. Naturally, if you want, you can spend a great deal of money. But almost everywhere you can get very luxurious accommodations for much less than you would pay at home. And the same goes for food.
It’s Important that you make reservations in advance. That’s where PANAMAC comes into play. In addition to being the electronic heart of Pan Am’s reservations system, PANAMAC keeps tabs on 30 Intercontinental Hotels and most other hotels around the world.
This means you, or your Travel Agent can take care of all your “living arrangements” at the same time that passage is booked. In fact, doing both takes only a matter of seconds. The Pan Am reservations agent pushes a few buttons and your needs are flashed to the central computer. In no time PANAMAC has spelled out the availabilities ... and word is flashed back offering you accommodations. If they’re okay with you, say so, a “confirm button” is pushed and you’re in business.
In the same way you can order all hotel services available at your destination. Transportation from the airport . . . sightseeing tours . . . baby sitters . . . special dietary needs . . . television . . . theatre and festival bookings . . . whatever you want. And of course there’s no charge for this PANAMAC service.
Keep in mind that these reservations can be changed as easily as they are made. You’re never “locked in.” If you decide to linger longer in one city than you planned, or to make a detour... the Pan Am office near you will be glad to change your hotels right along with your flights.
