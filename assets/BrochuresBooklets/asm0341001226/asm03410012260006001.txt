What's the weather like?
You probably already know It’s perfect. Take Hawaii as an example. Always like spring or early fall on the Mainland. The temperature ranges from seventy to eighty.
The ocean serves as a stabilizer, tempering the tropical sun. The same goes for the other lands in the Pacific ... with slight variations depending on how near each is to the equator. Of course there are some things to watch for — the rainy season in the Philippines, for one. But for the most part you won’t have to worry about seasons.
Below the equator, as you know, the seasons are the reverse of ours. This doesn’t really matter much until you get as far south as Australia and New Zealand. Even here there are plenty of places for sun worshipers all year round ... but you can go skiing in July if you have a mind to.
Japan, on the other hand, has seasons about like the middle belt of the United States — though without the extremes. During the winter Tokyo averages in the low forties ... while in July and August temperatures range in the seventies.
Your Travel Agent will give you specific weather data on any place you like — or ask Pan Am.
