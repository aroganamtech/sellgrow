Sales Promotion Materials
The items shown on the following pages are typical of those Pan Am offers for your use. They are constantly being brought up to date and their number added to. They have one aim — to help promote travel under the new World Wide Group Fares.
The Group letter, folders and booklets illustrated on these pages can be used to help awaken interest in destinations, routings and fares, leading to active expression of interest. Posters, card displays are useful reminders of the pleasures of travel to and within many of the world's popular travel destinations.
Colorful Pan Am films offer a powerful appeal at club or organization meetings when membership is in the process of pinpointing favored travel destinations.
Pan Am can also provide personalized items, at modest cost, such as imprinted overnight bags and special souvenir glassware — mementos groups will cherish.
Your Pan Am Office has complete information concerning the material currently available in your area.
