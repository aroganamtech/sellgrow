Foreword
Pan American's successful struggle to bring about World Wide Group Fares now makes it possible for members of clubs and organizations to travel to most parts of the world — or all the way around it — at truly phenomenal savings. As examples, a group of 25 or more people on a New York-London round trip pay only $310 each. 15 or more people can travel Round the World for as little as $924 apiece...and round trip savings to all Europe, the Middle East, Caribbean, Hawaii, Orient and the Pacific are equally dramatic.
Group Fare reductions are now effective on most of Pan Am's world wide routes, aboard regularly-scheduled Jet Clippers, via economy class Rainbow service.
Promoting group travel is a popular and worthwhile idea... more so today than ever before. You're offering adventure people can share, at a price many can afford. And you can now offer trips to a far greater number of destinations than ever before. Furthermore, Pan Am works with you as an organizer * helping you with all the details — for groups of any size. With the advent of the new World Wide Group Fares, a new age of group travel is beginning.
This book shows you, step by step, exactly how to arrange group travel via Pan Am. The following pages include thorough studies of the rules governing travel in groups . . . areas included ... fares ... schedules ... planning and promotion strategy ... how Pan Am helps make your job easier, by pinpointing markets for you . . . providing a variety of exciting promotional materials.
