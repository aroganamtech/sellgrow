IT TAKES A HISTORY OF FIRSTS TO MAKE
A WORLD LEADER IN AIR TRANSPORTATION
From the beginning, there has been no end, no limit, to what mastery of the air could mean to living. Men who understood this, moved by inspiration, have — in a little over thirty years — lifted the airplane from an awkward duckling to a swept-wing sky giant, incredibly fast and entirely world-worthy.
Pan American, and the people of Pan American, have played a key role in the past history that makes today possible. Each Pan Am “first,” each major stage in this airline’s development, has been an advance in the bigger history of world aviation. The following brief review of Pan Am’s growth will show you how far, how fast, Clipper travel has come — will give you a basis for envisioning how promising tomorrow can be.
