Coming — a subsonic giant
The Boeing 747 — giant new subsonic jet transport — will soon see its first commercial service ... under Pan American insignia.
It will cruise at a higher altitude and about 8V2% faster (587 miles per hour) than today's jets... and have a maximum range of about 6000 statute miles.
It will carry 360 passengers in spacious First Class/Economy configuration.
There will be ample space for a luxurious lounge on the upper level... a roomy first class "salon" on the main deck. Twenty-foot wide compartments will
provide unprecedented seat and aisle room in the economy class section.
The 747 Superjet will mark another step in a well-established Pan Am policy — anticipating customers' needs.
As the 747 story illustrates, this policy has meant participating from start to finish in the design and development of aircraft to meet these needs.
Over the years, it has required that Pan Am be the first to acquire these new aircraft...
•	the Sikorsky S-40 — first commercial, 4-engine flying boat
•	the Martin M-130 "China Clipper" that opened up transpacific travel
•	the Boeing B-314 — last and largest of the great seaplanes
•	the Boeing 377 Stratocruiser
•	the Boeing 707
Soon — the 747's. And, due in the 1970's, even faster, supersonic jet transports — the SSTs. After that, who knows?
