To Manila ... with a load of history!
"Historic" sums up the China Clipper's maiden flight from California to Manila Harbor that November 1935. It was the first scheduled air mail flight across the Pacific — and only 3 minutes late after a flight of 59 hours, 48 minutes.
Beside 110,865 letters, she carried 12 crates of turkeys... providing the first traditional Thanksgiving dinners in the history of Midway and Wake Islands. Life aboard the China Clipper was pretty soft. There were roomy double-decker bunks... and a spacious lounge where 4-course luncheon buffets were served on long tables.
Largest air transport of their day, these Clippers (Martin MM30s) weighed 26 tons, were 24 feet high, with a wing span of 130 feet. Length? 90 feet, 7 inches. Four Pratt and Whitney 830-horsepower engines gave the M-130s a 130 MPH speed, a cruising range of 2500 miles nonstop. They could carry as many as 32 passengers.
