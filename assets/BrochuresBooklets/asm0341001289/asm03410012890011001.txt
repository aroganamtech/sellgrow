Playing leapfrog over storms!
This Boeing 307 — first pressurized cabin landplane ever used in regular commercial flying — could quite literally leap above troublesome weather. Powered by 4 Wright Cyclone 1100-horsepower engines, it cruised at 16,000 feet, far above storms and turbulence. The 307 made a comfortable 185 MPH with 33 passengers ... over a maximum distance of 1100 miles. Length? 74 feet 4 inches. Wingspan? 107 feet 3 inches. Take-off weight? 45,000 pounds.
