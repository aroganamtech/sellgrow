Granddaddy of the Flying Clippers
The Sikorsky S-40 joined Pan Am in 1931 — the first commercial four-engine flying boat. First, too, to carry the proud designation "Clipper/' It was also a world record-holder —for the longest over-water flight: 600 miles nonstop from Kingston, Jamaica to Barranquilla, Colombia.
You can imagine how this expanded Pan Am's range of operations! In fact, by year-end, we were flying out of 29 land and 26 sea bases.
With 40 passengers, the S-40 cruised at 100 miles-an-hour, powered by a battery of 575-horsepower engines. Length?
76 feet 8 inches. Wing span? 114 feet.
