Back... after a brilliant war record
The Douglas DC-4 proved its reliability and versatility serving the Armed Forces. In 1945 — it led Pan Am's post-war parade of planes. The DC-4 could cruise at up to 192 MPH with 55 First Class passengers (78, if traveling tourist)... as well as 8000 pounds of cargo. There were Sleeperette® accommodations for 30 passengers. Other key facts: 4 Pratt & Whitney 1450-horsepower engines; 2250-mile operating range; 93-foot 5-inch length; 117V2 foot wing span.
