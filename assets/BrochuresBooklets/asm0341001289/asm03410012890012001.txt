Great and glorious workhorse
The Douglas DC-3 earned its nickname of "workhorse" by carrying more than half a billion passengers, in all, not counting millions of servicemen during the war.
In 1943, Pan Am had 53 of these DC-3s operating on overland routes. Vital statistics: wing span — 95 feet; length 64V2 feet; gross weight — 25,200 pounds; two Wright Cyclone engines, 1000-horsepower each. Cruising range was 500 miles, at 180 MPH, carrying 21 passengers.
