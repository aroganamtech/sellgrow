The Flying Clippers*
Key West to Havana — at 90 MPH!
Pan Am's first plane was this sturdy, three-engined Fokker F-7. Our first route?
A 90 mile haul from Key West, Florida to Havana, Cuba with up to 8 passengers plus mail.
With its three Wright 220-horsepower engines, the F-7 cruised at 90 miles-an-hour— not bad for those days. It had the necessary range. And it could stay afloat at sea, thanks to special compartments built into its plywood wings.
