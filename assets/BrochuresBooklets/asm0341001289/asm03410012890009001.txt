Across the Atlantic... with a bridal suite!
Pan Am's Yankee Clipper not only inaugurated the first scheduled transatlantic service in 1939, it also introduced a new concept of flying luxury. Spacious staterooms even included a bridal suite! And the 70 passengers could relax in overstuffed chairs and sofas.
Weighing 42 tons, the Yankee Clipper (or Boeing B-314) was the largest — as well as the last — of the seaplane fleet. Her four Wright engines — delivering 1550 horsepower each — were the first that were accessible for in-flight repairs. Wing span —152 feet. Length —106 feet.
