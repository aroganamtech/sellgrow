High-flying luxury
The double-decked Stratocruiser of 1949 — the Boeing 377 — cushioned its 95 Economy Class passengers from rough weather with a 15-25,000-foot operating altitude. And it surrounded them with the comfort of a lower deck lounge ... private staterooms and Sleeperette service.
A modified version of the 377 — the Super Stratocruiser — joined Pan Am service in 1954, embodying some major technical improvements. Propeller revo-lutions-per-minute were reduced, without affecting the 3500-horsepower output of each of the four Pratt &
Whitney engines. New turbo-superchargers were added, as well as extra wing-tip fuel tanks, making possible nonstop service from New York to London or Paris, regardless of weather. Wing span —141 feet, 3 inches. Length —110 feet, 4 inches. Weight —147,000 pounds. Cruising speed — 275 MPH. Operating range — 4600 miles.
