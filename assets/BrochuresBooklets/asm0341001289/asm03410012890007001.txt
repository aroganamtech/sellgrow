Master of oceans
By 1934, Pan Am was ready to cross oceans — with this SIKORSKY S-42 ... America's first true transoceanic transport. Built to rigid specifications, it could fly 750 miles with a safe fuel reserve, and operate with one engine out of commission. The S-42 broke all performance records for flying boats. Among many innovations, the S-42 used flush riveting to reduce air drag, wing "flaps" to reduce landing speed, and constant-speed propellers. It weighed 43,000 pounds and could carry 32 passengers. The S-42 was assigned to regular service in the Caribbean and on such routes as New York to Bermuda. As the "Pan American Clip-
per," an S-42 surveyed flying routes across the Pacific and over the Atlantic to Europe.
