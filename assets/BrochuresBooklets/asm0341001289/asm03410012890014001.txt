Record performance under pressure
Pan Am's version of the Lockheed L-49 ''Constellation" was the first transatlantic plane to boast a pressurized cabin, which enabled it to clear 20,800 feet with 45 passengers. Carrying a crew of eight, a "Connie" flew aviation's first scheduled round-the-world flight in 1947. Four Wright Cyclone 2200-horsepower engines pulled the L-49 along at close to 250 miles-an-hour over a maximum range of 3000 miles. Wing span — 123 feet. Length — 95 feet, 1 inch.
