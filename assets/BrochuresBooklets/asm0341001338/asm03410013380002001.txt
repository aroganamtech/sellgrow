career as a
PAN AM STEWARDESS
Wherever in the world you go, people know that a girl in the Pan Am uniform leads an exciting life. Your heart is in the clouds...your chosen career is stimulating-free from workaday monotony and far, far more than “just a job.” You wear your Pan Am Stewardess wings with pride. The whole wide, wonderful world is your “place of business,” and your days are filled with the romance of far-away, exotic lands... the adventure of our ever-changing world... the warmth of human contact with the peoples of all nations. You’ll fly to Africa, Europe and Asia—from Hong Kong to Helsinki...from Rome to Rangoon! Or to the glamorous cities of Latin America. The capitals of the world will be as familiar as your own home town.
You enjoy the prestige of a profession that is widely— and deservedly—acclaimed as glamorous. You meet the most fascinating international celebrities. And you can take keen satisfaction from knowing that when you fly with Pan Am, you’re flying with the “World’s Most Experienced Airline.” You know, too, that a large measure of Pan American’s leadership can be traced to the world-famous hospitality of cabin attendants like yourself.
It Is Pan American’s policy to employ the best qualified people available without regard to race, creed, color or national origin.
