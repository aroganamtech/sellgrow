MARIAN YEAR PILGRIMAGE
30 DAYS VISITING PARIS, LISIEUX, NEVERS, PARAY-LE-MONIAL, ARS, LYONS, TOULOUSE, LOURDES, CARCASSONNE, THE FRENCH RIVIERA, MILAN, PADUA,
VENICE, FLORENCE, LORETO, ASSISI, ROME, LISBON, FATIMA.

1st day NEW YORK CITY—Leave by Pan American World Airways for overnight flight to Europe.
2nd day PARIS — Arrive by air. Visit both banks of the to	great capital of France on the Seine, including
4th day Notre Dame Cathedral, the Sainte Chapelle, the Louvre Museum, the Invalides, and other famous landmarks, such as Sacre Coeur, church where a special Mass will be served; also the Shrine of the Miraculous Medal at Rue de Bac. See the incorrupt body of St. Catherine Laboure, to whom the Blessed Mother appeared to promote the Miraculous Medal devotion. At the Church of the Vincencian Fathers close by, the body of St. Vincent de Paul may be seen.
5th day LISIEUX — Full day excursion by motor into the countryside of Normandy. Special Mass at the international pilgrimage center of the Little Flower, whose basilica is one of the largest in Europe and each year draws hundreds of thousands of pilgrims to intercede with or to thank Ste. Theresa of the Child Jesus, proclaimed by Pope Pius XI “The Child Beloved of all the World.”
6th day NEVERS and PARAY-LE-MONIAL — Start grand motor tour of Europe to visit the principal shrines and famous scenic or historic sights.
Leave Paris via FONTAINEBLEAU, MONTARGIS to NEVERS, where a visit will be made to the Tomb of Ste. Bernadette. Continue via MOULINS to the Shrine of the Sacred Heart at PARAY-LE-MONIAL, which is, next to Lourdes, the most visited shrine in France, and the center of devotion to the Sacred Heart of Jesus, whose confraternity numbers over a million members. You will visit the 12th century basilica of Sacre Coeur and Chapel of the Visitation, site of the famous pilgrimage of Ste. Margaret Mary in 1673. Continue by motor for an overnight stop in the second largest city of France.
7th day LYONS and ARS — Morning departure by motor from LYONS to ARS where Mass will be celebrated at the village church, where St. John Baptist Vian-ney, “The Famed Cure of Ars”, Patron Saint of Priests, spent his life.
8th day TOULOUSE — Motor across the beautiful mountainous central part of France via Le Puy and its incredible churches atop rocky pinnacles to the famed cathedral of Albi and the important city of Toulouse.
9th day LOURDES — Continue towards the lofty Pyre-and nees to Our Lady of LOURDES. Truly the city 10th day of the Blessed Virgin, Lourdes, with its Grotto of Massabielle and miraculous spring has been famous since 1858 when the Blessed Virgin appeared to the little girl, Bernadette Soubirous. It is one of the most venerated of holy places in the Catholic world. Visit the Grotto, the Church of the Rosary, and the Way of the Cross on the mountainside.
llth day CARCASSONNE — Motoring in the foothills of the Pyrenees, via Foix, to this amazing walled city of the middle ages.
12th day MARSEILLES — Via Nimes and its Roman arena to Provencal Arles and the great and bristling seaport of Marseilles, where Mass will be celebrated at the Cathedral of Notre Dame de la Garde.
13th day NICE — Motor along the beautiful French Riviera and via the naval base of Toulon and many summer 14th day and winter resort towns of international fame such as ST. RAPHAEL, CANNES, JUAN-LES-PINS and ANTIBES, to the Queen of France’s famed Mediterranean resorts. Spectacular motor excursion over the Grande Corniche to Menton, returning via Monte Carlo, Monaco, and Villefranche.
15th day MILAN — Continue along the Mediterranean Coast to GENOA. Thence inland to Milan to visit the famous Cathedral, the Scala Opera House and the Church of Santa Maria delle Grazio, to admire the Last Supper fresco of Leonardo da Vinci.

16th day PADUA— Via LAKE GARDA and VERONA to PADUA which, with its magnificent basilica, is the sanctuary of St. Anthony who in 1220 became a follower of Saint Francis of Assisi and started his crusade against heretics with an intense and passionate campaign in Italy and abroad. Continue in the afternoon to VENICE from where you will leave your car to gondola to the hotel.
17th day VENICE — Visit St. Mark’s Square and Basilica, the Doges Palace, Bridge of Sighs, and ride in gondola through the network of canals.
18th day FLORENCE — Resume motor tour via BOLO-and GNA to FLORENCE. Visit this great Tuscan 19th day “City of the Renaissance”, with its many monuments of Medici Fame, the beautiful Cathedral, the Belfry by Giotto, the art galleries of the Pitti and Uifizi Palaces.
20th day HILL TOWNS — By motor into the Apennines to the picturesque Hill Town of PERUGIA and to the Great Shrine of Assisi, steeped in religious history and filled with monuments and relics of the glory of Saint Francis, who preached a life of joyful renunciation seven centuries ago. Continue by motor toward the Adriatic to LORETO.
21st day LORETO — Mass at this important shrine, scene of the miraculous transfer of the House of Mary, devoted to the Madonna of Loreto, patron of Aviators, and renowned for its cures. Continue across the peninsula to ROME.
22nd day ROME — In His Encyclical Letter on the Marian to Year, Pope Pius XII said of Rome: “As all know, 25th day there are many sacred edifices here, in which the Blessed Virgin Mary is exposed to the devotion of the Roman people; but the greatest without doubt is the Basilica of Ste. Mary Major in which the mosaics of Our predecessor of pious memory still glisten, an outstanding monument to the Divine maternity of the Virgin Mary.” During your stay in Rome you will be privileged to receive an Audience by the Holy Father. A comprehensive sightseeing program of Rome and its seven hills will include the Four Major Basilicas, the Vatican, the ancient Roman Forum, the Colosseum, the Catacombs, and the Appian Way.
26th day LISBON — Leave by plane across the Mediter-ond ranean and Spain to PORTUGAL. Visit the beau-27th day tiful capital of Portugal located on the Tagus River estuary; Jeronimoz Monastery and Belem Palace, the Royal Coaches Museum, focal Black Horse Square, and the picturesque Alfama quarter.
28th day FATIMA—Leave by motor along the coast, via the beautiful beach and attractive fishing port of Nazare to Alcobaca and its famous Cistercian Monastery. Lunch at Alcobaca, continuing in the afternoon to the famous Monastery of Batalha, thence to Fatima to visit the important Shrine of Our Lady of Fatima and the sanctuary. After a suitable stopover in Fatima, return to Lisbon via Tomar with its Xllth Century Palace of the Templars, and Santarem with a beautiful panorama of the Tagus.
