GENERAL EMPLOYMENT INFORMATION
Assignments abroad are normally on the managerial or technical level. Few vacancies occur in this field and assignment is normally made to employees who have had several years of Company experience and are well versed in Company policy and procedure. Occasionally, assignments must be made directly from outside of Company ranks due to specific job requirements in the technical fields.
Temporary Employment
The general requirements for temporary work have been in the Secretarial and Typist fields. Assignments are usually for a week or two, or perhaps longer. Sometimes Pan American calls for temporary File Clerks or Receptionists. Applications must be filed with the Personnel Office at the location desired.
Part Time Employment
Occasionally, Pan American requires the services of part time clerks for typing, collating, checking, etc. Those interested in this type of employment should file with the Personnel Office at the location desired. Flours of work depend upon projects covered at time of employment.
U.S. DISTRICT SALES OFFICES AND OTHER AIRPORT FACILITIES
In addition to the employment possibilities mentioned on the preceding pages. Pan Am has similar openings available at its District Sales Offices and Airport Facilities located throughout the United States. Employment in the Sales Offices is mainly limited to Sales Agents, Secretaries and Telephone Operators while both technical and clerical positions may be available at the airports.
Interested candidates should apply directly to the office nearest them :
