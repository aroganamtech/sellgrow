INTERCONTINENTAL HOTELS CORPORATION
A wholly owned subsidiary of Pan American, IHC currently operates many hotels around the world and many more are on the drawing boards. The usual openings in the New York office are Secretarial, Sales Agent, Typist, Accountant, etc. Employment abroad is handled locally by each hotel. Applicants with heavy hotel administration background should make application to the Personnel Office, Pan Am Building, N.Y.C.
BUSINESS JETS DIVISION
This division of Pan American, headquartered in the New York Office, is responsible for selling and servicing business jet aircraft to large corporations located in North America. All employment inquiries should be directed to the Personnel Office, Pan Am Building, N.Y.C.
PAN AMERICAN DIVISIONS AND AFFILIATES
Guided Missiles Range Division (GMRD)
As prime range contractor to the U.S. Air Force, GMRD is responsible for the management, operation and maintenance of the 10,000 mile missile test range, known as the Eastern Test Range, stretching from Cape Kennedy, Florida, down through the Caribbean and into the South Atlantic.
Primarily technical and technically-allied positions are available.
