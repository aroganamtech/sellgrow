Pan American Employment Policy
We feel that our employees are well trained in our methods and procedures and when opportunities for expansion are available, our primary concern is to promote the employee according to his ability and talent. This policy enables Pan American to avail itself of the best qualified personnel.
Although some positions with Pan American require that an applicant have specialized training, it is not our policy to recommend schools where such training can be obtained. Qualified individuals, according to our requirements, receive consideration for positions regardless of the school they have attended.
Every applicant must be a high school graduate and should be interested in a career in air transportation. Additional requirements for positions most frequently available are set forth in this brochure. It is our policy to employ the best qualified people available for work without regard to age, race, color, religion, sex or national origin.
Vacancies in the positions listed in this brochure are sometimes available at Pan American’s offices in New York City or at the Company’s major airport facilities and sales offices located in New York, Miami and San Francisco. Applicants who wish to be considered for employment in other locations may wish to apply directly to Pan American sales offices or other airport facilities as indicated on page 7 of this brochure.
