HONG KONG— Fragrant Streams Since its founding in 1841, Hong Kong has grown from a bare, unhealthy, and unknown spot to one of the leading, as well as one of the most beautiful ports of the world. The excellent international air transportation facilities, the numerous industries and fine buildings, all indicate a busy, modern city. One of the principal reasons for the development of the city lies in its magnificent situation and its excellent harbour.
Within the city are numerous places for recreation and amusement. Transportation is by hired cars, taxi, rickshaw, sedan chair, and tram. Sampans and motor boats are available for harbour trips.
Hotel accomodations are among the best in the Orient; and, although sometimes difficult to obtain under today’s crowded conditions, may be had at very reasonable prices.
For further information about the city, you may contact the Pan American office in Hong Kong.
